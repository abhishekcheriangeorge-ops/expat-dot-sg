International school admissions in Singapore are calendar-driven. Families relocating on EP packages often underestimate how early popular grades fill — especially when housing is still undecided.

## Practical timing

- Start outreach 6–12 months before arrival for peak grades
- Ask about sibling priority and waitlist realism
- Map commute from likely housing belts (north / west vs central)
