**Short answer:** Local banking unlocks salary, GIRO, and PayNow — but the common fail is arriving on an **IPA** with **temporary housing** and no address letter. Line up a serviced-apartment / HR address letter, get a **+65** number, finish **issuance → Singpass**, then open the payroll account. Most personal accounts expect lawful local presence + FIN.

Local banking unlocks salary credit, GIRO (levy, utilities), and digital payments. The recurring Reddit failure mode is arriving with an IPA letter and temporary housing — then discovering the bank wants **proof of local address** you do not have yet. A second failure mode: trying to open a **personal** Singapore account with **no FIN / no local status**.

## Typical document stack

| Document | Why banks ask |
| --- | --- |
| Passport | Identity |
| Work / Dependant Pass (or IPA under some programmes) | Residency / employment basis |
| Proof of address | Local presence — TA, employer letter, or accepted temporary-housing letter |
| Employment confirmation | Salary account rationale |
| Local mobile number | App / OTP onboarding |

Requirements vary by bank and whether you are salaried, a dependant, or incorporating. Always confirm the live checklist on the bank’s site the week you apply.

## Breaking the address chicken-and-egg

Practical sequences that show up repeatedly in r/askSingapore:

1. Ask **serviced apartment / hotel** for a letter confirming your stay address  
2. Ask **HR** for a letter on company letterhead stating your temporary / permanent address  
3. Use a signed **tenancy agreement** or stamped lease once you have one  
4. Ask which **partner bank** payroll already uses — friction is lower when HR introduces you  

Some banks are pickier than others about IPA-only openings. If payroll is days away, escalate through HR rather than arguing with a branch queue alone.

## “I don’t live in Singapore — which bank will open a personal account?”

Short answer from forum consensus and bank KYC practice: **most retail personal accounts expect a lawful local presence** (work/visit long-term status + FIN for many products). Having a Singapore **company** does not automatically unlock a **personal** FIN-based salary account. Premium / Treasures-style pitches for non-residents still run AML/KYC — plan an in-person branch visit, source-of-funds narrative, and a realistic chance of refusal.

Bridge options peers actually use:

- International banks where you already hold a relationship (HSBC / some regional banks) with cross-border introduction  
- MAS-licensed remittance / MPI wallets (e.g. Wise) for FX **while** you wait — not a payroll substitute ([remittances](/guides/remittances-singapore))  
- Corporate account paths for founders (separate, stricter KYC)

Do not fly with a suitcase of seed capital above S$20k without reading [CBNI declaration](/guides/cbni-cash-declaration-singapore).

## Singpass timing

Once you have a **FIN** and the pass is issued (or MOM registration creates Singpass with you), banking and government workflows get dramatically easier. r/askSingapore reports that **IPA-only** Singpass registration often fails until issuance — do not plan week-one digital KYC on IPA alone. Prioritise eligibility after pass issuance; it is not optional busywork.

## High-interest accounts wait for clean payroll

Multiplier-style products need **recognised salary credits**, not just a balance. Open the salary account first; chase [bonus-interest categories](/guides/high-interest-salary-accounts-foreigners) after HR’s payroll description clears the bank’s income rules.

## US persons and FATCA friction

US citizens/green-card holders often face extra forms and slower onboarding. Peer reports sometimes favour banks with smoother US-person processes — but policies change. Budget an extra branch visit and bring tax IDs.

## Founders vs salaried EP

| Profile | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| Salaried EP | Personal salary account is the default path |
| Dependant | May need principal’s employment docs; product eligibility varies |
| Founder / EntrePass | Corporate KYC: constitutions, shareholding, business activity narrative |

Corporate account KYC is stricter than personal salary accounts. Bring a clean ownership chart.

## First-30-days checklist

1. Confirm payroll bank preference with HR before wheels-down  
2. Get temporary address letter lined up  
3. Open personal account → set up GIRO for levy/utilities when relevant  
4. Register **PayNow** (prefer **FIN** over a recycled prepaid mobile) — [PayNow setup](/guides/paynow-setup-foreigners-singapore)  
5. Only then chase multi-currency / investment products  

Banking is infrastructure, not a lifestyle brand choice. Optimise for **payroll on time**, then upgrade products later.
