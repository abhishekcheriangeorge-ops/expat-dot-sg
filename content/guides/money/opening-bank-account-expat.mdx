Local banking unlocks salary credit, GIRO (levy, utilities), and digital payments. The recurring Reddit failure mode is arriving with an IPA letter and temporary housing — then discovering the bank wants **proof of local address** you do not have yet.

## Typical document stack

| Document | Why banks ask |
| --- | --- |
| Passport | Identity |
| Work / Dependant Pass (or IPA under some programmes) | Residency / employment basis |
| Proof of address | Local presence — TA, employer letter, or accepted temporary-housing letter |
| Employment confirmation | Salary account rationale |
| Local mobile number | App / OTP onboarding |

Requirements vary by bank and whether you are salaried, a dependant, or incorporating. Always confirm the live checklist on the bank’s site the week you apply.

## Breaking the address chicken-and-egg

Practical sequences that show up repeatedly in r/askSingapore:

1. Ask **serviced apartment / hotel** for a letter confirming your stay address  
2. Ask **HR** for a letter on company letterhead stating your temporary / permanent address  
3. Use a signed **tenancy agreement** or stamped lease once you have one  
4. Ask which **partner bank** payroll already uses — friction is lower when HR introduces you  

Some banks are pickier than others about IPA-only openings. If payroll is days away, escalate through HR rather than arguing with a branch queue alone.

## Singpass timing

Once you are eligible for Singpass, many banking and government workflows become dramatically easier. Prioritise eligibility after pass issuance — it is not optional busywork.

## US persons and FATCA friction

US citizens/green-card holders often face extra forms and slower onboarding. Peer reports sometimes favour banks with smoother US-person processes — but policies change. Budget an extra branch visit and bring tax IDs.

## Founders vs salaried EP

| Profile | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| Salaried EP | Personal salary account is the default path |
| Dependant | May need principal’s employment docs; product eligibility varies |
| Founder / EntrePass | Corporate KYC: constitutions, shareholding, business activity narrative |

Corporate account KYC is stricter than personal salary accounts. Bring a clean ownership chart.

## First-30-days checklist

1. Confirm payroll bank preference with HR before wheels-down  
2. Get temporary address letter lined up  
3. Open personal account → set up GIRO for levy/utilities when relevant  
4. Only then chase multi-currency / investment products  

Banking is infrastructure, not a lifestyle brand choice. Optimise for **payroll on time**, then upgrade products later.

Credit cards are a **later** problem: foreigner income floors are higher (often ~S$45k at major banks), and issuers want payslips. Sequence: salary account → first payslip → then [credit cards for foreigners](/guides/credit-cards-foreigners-singapore).
