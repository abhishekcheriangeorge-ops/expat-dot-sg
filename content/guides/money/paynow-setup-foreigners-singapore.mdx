**Short answer:** PayNow needs a **Singapore bank or participating MPI wallet** first. After pass issuance, register your **FIN** as the stable proxy (mobile is optional and fragile). Recycled prepaid numbers are how rent deposits vanish to strangers — **read the name** on the confirm screen every time.

r/askSingapore’s PayNow threads usually collapse into three different problems: **no local bank account**, a **prepaid mobile still linked to the previous owner**, and confusing **Wise / MPI QR** with a personal FIN proxy. This page is the setup playbook; wrong-number disasters live in [erroneous transfer recovery](/guides/wrong-paynow-epayment-recovery).

## What PayNow actually is

PayNow moves **SGD** between participating Singapore banks and major payment institutions (MPIs) over FAST using a **proxy** — typically:

| Proxy | Who it suits |
| --- | --- |
| **NRIC / FIN** | Expats with an issued work/dependant pass — stable identity for rent, helper salary, school fees |
| **Mobile number** | Everyday peer transfers — fragile when you swap prepaid SIMs |
| **UEN** | Businesses / entities (PayNow Corporate) |
| **VPA** | MPI e-wallet receive path that can sit **alongside** a bank PayNow registration |

You register inside your **bank or MPI app** — having the bank’s contact number on file is **not** the same as linking a PayNow proxy.

## Foreigner setup sequence that works

1. Open a **local bank salary account** after pass issuance — [opening a bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat)  
2. In the bank app, register **FIN** as a PayNow proxy (and optionally mobile)  
3. On the confirm screen, verify the **registered name** matches you before you publish the proxy to landlords  
4. Send yourself a S$1 test from a second account / partner if amounts will be large  

Tourists **without** a Singapore bank or participating MPI wallet cannot invent peer-to-peer PayNow from a foreign number. Grab QR / NETS-style merchant rails are a different product — if the till uses a personal mobile PayNow, overseas wallets often fail.

Week-one order of operations: [SIM → Singpass → bank map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank).

## The recycled prepaid SIM trap

Singapore prepaid numbers get recycled. Forum pattern: you open DBS, assume mobile PayNow “just works,” then Wise/home remittance to your **new** number credits the **previous** owner’s OCBC link.

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer “succeeds” but your balance does not move | Check which bank owns the mobile proxy; call **your** bank to de-link / re-register |
| Name on confirm screen is not yours | Stop — do not send the large amount |
| You changed SIMs without telling the bank | Report the number change; re-register PayNow on the new mobile |

Prefer **FIN** for anything contractual (rent deposits, helper payroll, tuition). Keep mobile for friends who already know your digits.

## Wise / MPI PayNow QR is not your FIN

Specialist FX providers licensed as MPIs can often generate a **PayNow QR** into an SGD balance. Useful for freelancers and visitors who already hold that wallet — still:

- Reconciliation / reference fields matter more than for a personal FIN link  
- It is **not** a payroll substitute or GIRO account  
- Cross-border home remittances remain a separate FX problem — [remittances](/guides/remittances-singapore)

## Habits that prevent Reddit disasters

- Read the recipient **name** on every confirm screen  
- Prefer FIN over mobile for recurring large pays  
- Batch overseas family support on remittance rails, not PayNow  
- If money goes to the wrong proxy, open an **erroneous transaction** case immediately — [recovery guide](/guides/wrong-paynow-epayment-recovery)

PayNow is infrastructure once the FIN is linked. Treat mobile proxies as disposable labels, not identity.
