Reddit’s r/askSingapore is full of “is S$X enough?” threads. The useful answer is never the headline salary — it is **fixed monthly cash after tax, after rent, after school**, with honest assumptions about whether a spouse can work.

## Foreigners and CPF

Most Employment Pass holders do **not** receive CPF contributions the way Singapore citizens and PRs do. Do not mentally import a ~17% employer CPF top-up into your package unless HR explicitly structures something different and lawful. Deep dive: [CPF and foreigners](/guides/cpf-and-foreigners).

<OfficialSource>
CPF rules for citizens/PRs live on the [CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/). EP eligibility and salary definitions live on [MOM’s Employment Pass pages](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass) — allowances that are not fixed monthly salary may not count toward the EP floor or COMPASS salary factor.
</OfficialSource>

## Fixed monthly salary vs AWS / bonus (MOM definition)

For EP floors and COMPASS, **fixed monthly salary = basic monthly salary + fixed monthly allowances**. It does **not** include AWS (13th month), bonuses, commissions, overtime, variable allowances, reimbursements, in-kind benefits, board fees, or stock/dividends.

| Counts toward EP fixed salary? | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Yes (if truly fixed monthly) | Basic pay; fixed food / housing allowance that does not vary |
| No | AWS / 13th month; performance bonus; commission; overtime; variable transport; equity |

MOM may still reject filings where **fixed allowances are a major share** of the total and should have been basic salary. Get the **fixed monthly salary** figure that will appear on the EP application in writing — not a “CTC including 2 months bonus” slide.

## Model monthly cash (do this before you sign)

1. **Fixed monthly salary** (pass-relevant number)  
2. Subtract **estimated tax cadence** (resident progressive rates once you qualify; non-resident treatment differs — see [IRAS tax residency](/guides/iras-tax-residency-filing))  
3. Subtract **rent + utilities** for the neighbourhood you will actually live in  
4. Subtract **insurance gaps** (outpatient, maternity, dental)  
5. Subtract **school / childcare** if applicable  
6. Remaining = lifestyle + savings + remittances + flights home  

Bonuses and RSUs are real but lumpy. Do not underwrite rent on them. Singapore taxes most share awards at **vest** and options at **exercise** — plus a deemed-exercise trap on exit. Deep dive: [RSU / ESOP tax](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore).

## What forums get right about “comfortable”

Order-of-magnitude patterns that keep showing up in askSingapore threads (always replace with your quotes):

| Household shape | Recurring community signal |
| --- | --- |
| Dual-income couple, no kids | Combined ~S$10k+/month often cited as “comfortable” if rent is not CBD-prestige |
| Single earner ~S$120k/year + spouse seeking work | Frequently called tight once mid-range condo rent (often ~S$4–5k) lands |
| Family with international schools | School fees can dominate; many locals say packages without housing/school support should not bring kids |

These are **peer heuristics**, not official thresholds. Use them as stress tests, then rebuild with [cost of living by household](/guides/cost-of-living-by-household).

## Negotiation levers that move the household

Push on items that change monthly cash or risk — not vanity titles.

| Lever | Why it matters in Singapore |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed salary vs allowance split | Pass thresholds and COMPASS; tax treatment |
| Housing allowance (cash) vs company lease | Cash housing is usually **taxable employment income**; company lease shifts negotiation burden but is not “free rent” for IRAS |
| School fee cap + which children | Waitlists make “cap exists” ≠ “seat exists” |
| Spouse career support | Coaching, agency intros, dual-career policy — see [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) |
| Shipping / temporary housing days | Prevents forced condo decisions in week one |
| Notice / probation / relocation clawback | Household is mid-move; early exit risk is asymmetric |
| Insurance schedule | Private hospital bills without cover ruin “comfortable” math |

## Package negotiation script (specific asks)

Ask HR or the hiring manager, in writing:

1. Confirm the **fixed monthly salary** figure that will appear on the EP application  
2. Itemise housing, school, flights, and shipment as **cash vs reimbursed vs in-kind**  
3. Ask whether school support includes **corporate nomination seats** or only fee reimbursement  
4. Ask for a **benefits PDF** listing outpatient/specialist/hospital caps and dependents  
5. If a spouse relocates without a job: request a **career coaching stipend** or introductions — cheaper for the company than a failed dual-career posting  
6. Clarify **tax equalisation / protection** (common in some MNCs; rare on local packages)

## COMPASS is part of compensation design

Raising fixed salary can be the difference between a clean COMPASS pass and a messy appeal. Treat [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) as an offer-design constraint, not an HR afterthought after you have resigned overseas. Age-banded EP floors (non-FS from **S$5,600**, FS from **S$6,200** at the youngest band in the current tables) belong in the same conversation — see [EP vs S Pass](/guides/ep-vs-s-pass-comparison).

## Job-hop cashflow: IR21 still applies when you stay

Changing Singapore employers without leaving the country still usually means the **leaving** employer files **Form IR21** and **withholds** monies due until IRAS clears tax. Immediate re-employment does not erase that clock — it only opens a path to ask IRAS to continue GIRO once you have the new IPA/contract. Budget rent for the freeze window; ops sequence in [changing jobs on EP](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass) and details in [tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) / [IRAS’s employee tax-clearance FAQs](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employees/scenario-based-faqs-for-working-in-singapore-and-abroad/tax-clearance-for-non-singapore-citizen-employees).
