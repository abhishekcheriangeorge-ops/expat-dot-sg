Singapore can be thrifty or eye-watering depending on **housing belt** and **schooling**. Use ranges as planning scaffolds, then replace every line with quotes. The questions below are the ones that dominate r/askSingapore (“is S$120k enough?”, “is S$12k/month enough with kids?”).

## How to read community salary threads

Peers usually answer a different question than the one asked. They assume:

- No company housing / school support (unless stated)  
- Mid-range condo tastes (unless you say HDB/heartland)  
- Spouse job search friction on Dependant’s Pass  
- Private healthcare gaps if insurance is thin  

Reframe your ask as: **fixed salary → after-tax monthly → rent thesis → school thesis → leftover**.

## Singles

Largest levers: rent location, eating-out frequency, travel home.

| Lifestyle | Rough monthly cash need (planning) |
| --- | --- |
| Hawker + HDB/room share + MRT | Often discussed from ~S$3.5–5k all-in |
| Own 1-bed condo fringe + mixed dining | Often ~S$5–7k+ before savings goals |

River-view CBDs are a choice, not a requirement.

## Couples (no kids)

Dual-income changes the map quickly. Community heuristics frequently cite **~S$10k+/month combined** as “comfortable” if rent stays disciplined — and warn that a single **~S$120k/year** package with a non-working spouse and S$4–5k condo rent feels tight.

| Lever | Typical swing |
| --- | --- |
| Heartland HDB vs central 2-bed condo | Easily S$1.5–3k+/month |
| Cook + hawker vs restaurant-default | S$800–2,000+/month |
| No car vs car (COE reality) | Car ownership is a different socioeconomic tier |

See [heartland living](/guides/heartland-living-for-expats) and [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding).

## Families

International school fees and helper costs often dwarf groceries. Model school fees in the **same spreadsheet** as rent — never separately.

Recurring peer signal: **S$12k/month household income with two kids and international school** is widely called tight-to-impossible without housing/school support; **local school + HDB** changes the equation entirely (access rules apply — see [local schools for expats](/guides/local-schools-for-expats)).

| Line item | Why it dominates |
| --- | --- |
| International school | Often thousands per child per month once levies/bus included |
| Family condo near school belt | Rent stacks on top of fees |
| Helper | Salary + levy + lodging space |
| Healthcare | Private bills without strong insurance |

### Helper all-in (order of magnitude)

r/askSingapore family budgets repeatedly under-count the **helper stack**: monthly salary + levy + food/toiletries + insurance/medical + year-end bonus/flights, plus **S$3–5k** class agency/placement cash and the extra bedroom that bumps rent. Part-time cleaning is a different product — cheaper monthly, no live-in room tax. MDW hiring process stays Home-owned; Money only needs the cashflow line.

## Silent costs people forget

Condo miscellaneous fees passed through, Grab creep, CCA fees, flights at peak term breaks, replacement of moulded storage items, club trials, and the first-year “setup tax” (beds, kitchen kit, strollers).

## Offer acceptance test

1. Run after-tax monthly cash ([IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax) for framework; a tax professional for precision)  
2. Pick a **rent thesis** (heartland vs central) and a **school thesis** (international vs local vs undecided)  
3. Assume spouse income = **S$0** for six months unless a pass + offer already exists  
4. Require leftover ≥ your home-country savings rate — or renegotiate housing/school support  

If the spreadsheet only works with heroic assumptions, the package is the problem — not your budgeting skills.
