Relocation packages feel like free money until IRAS season. Separate **reimbursements with receipts**, **cash allowances**, and **ongoing benefits** (housing, car, school) before you burn the lump sum on furniture.

## Ask HR these five questions

1. Is the relocation cash an allowance (likely taxable) or a receipted reimbursement?
2. Which flights, hotels, and temporary housing are company-paid vs employee-paid?
3. Are you on tax equalisation, tax protection, or neither?
4. Will relocation items appear on IR8A / Appendix 8A?
5. Who owns year-one filing support — Big Four, in-house, or you alone?

## Practical split

| Item | Usually ask |
| --- | --- |
| Lump-sum “settling-in” cash | Taxable unless HR documents otherwise |
| Receipted moving invoices paid by employer | Often cleaner if policy-compliant |
| Ongoing housing allowance | Benefit / income characterisation matters |
| Home-leave flights | Check benefits-in-kind rules |
