Mobile careers create overlapping tax questions: remote work for a foreign employer, equity vesting from a parent company, rental income abroad, or consulting on the side. Forum threads keep mixing **“overseas income is not taxable”** with **“I am physically working in Singapore.”** Those are different facts.

## The distinction that matters

| Situation | Usual IRAS orientation (verify on your facts) |
| --- | --- |
| Employment / services **exercised in Singapore**, even if paid by an overseas company into an overseas account | Generally **Singapore-sourced** — taxable here |
| Genuine **foreign-sourced** income received into a Singapore bank (classic remittance) | Often **not taxable** for individuals from 2004 onward, with listed exceptions |
| Overseas posting where services are rendered **wholly outside** Singapore | May be outside Singapore tax — IRAS FAQ territory |
| Travel overseas that is **incidental** to a Singapore employment | Often still tied to the Singapore employment |

r/askSingapore DP / remote-work threads repeatedly cite the first row: living here on a family pass while billing a foreign employer does **not** magically make the income “overseas” for IRAS — and MOM work-rights rules are a separate compliance layer.

## Remittance is not a free pass — or a trap by itself

Paying yourself via Wise / PayNow / wire does not decide taxability. **Source** (where work is done / where the right to income arises) does. Conversely, leaving money offshore does not erase Singapore-source employment income.

## Equity, directors, and dual filing

- RSUs / options from a foreign parent need characterisation (employment benefit vs capital) — get employer/tax vendor docs early  
- Director’s fees from a **Singapore** company have their own IRAS treatment even if you travel  
- US persons and other worldwide taxpayers still file at home; Singapore treatment ≠ home-country treatment  

## Practical stance

- Keep clean records of foreign income, remittances, and days worked by location  
- Align employer equity reporting before the first vest after arrival  
- Engage a cross-border adviser if you have material foreign assets, dual filing, or “remote for foreign Co while on DP/LTVP” structures
