Year one is for scaffolding, not optimisation cosplay. The expensive mistakes are usually **illiquid status purchases** (condo with 60% ABSD, fresh COE car) made before you understand pass volatility.

## Build first

| Buffer | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **3–6 months** essential expenses in accessible cash | Higher if your pass is volatile or household is single-income EP |
| Insurance deductible clarity | Know inpatient gaps before the first A&E — [health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat) |
| Automatic rent + utilities | Bounce fees and GIRO surprises are avoidable |
| Remittance plan that does not strand you pre-payday | [Remittances](/guides/remittances-singapore) |
| IR21-aware exit cash | Final pay can freeze on tax clearance — [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |

Size the emergency fund off **rent + school + insurance + food**, not off aspirational savings rates from Twitter.

## Defer (year-one FOMO list)

- **Buying residential property** as a non-FTA foreigner at **60% ABSD** — read [buying property / ABSD](/guides/buying-property-foreigner-absd) before any OTP  
- **COE car ownership** unless the package truly funds it — [car vs sharing](/guides/car-ownership-vs-sharing-singapore)  
- Complex offshore structures sold at networking events  
- Maxing SRS before you understand exit tax — [SRS for foreigners](/guides/srs-investing-as-foreigner)  
- Interlocking whole-life products pitched in the first ninety days  

Stability first; sophistication later.

## FX and banking hygiene

Open a local operating account once address proof exists ([bank opening](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat)). Keep a home-currency runway until the first Singapore payroll clears. Do not convert your entire relocation lump sum on day one of a strong SGD week without a plan. Bonus-interest products wait until salary credits are **recognised** — [high-interest salary accounts](/guides/high-interest-salary-accounts-foreigners).

## Pass-risk finance

Your best investment in year one is often **optionality**: diplomatic-clause-friendly lease length, school deposits you can actually forfeit, and a job-search buffer if COMPASS / renewal wobbles. Spreadsheets that assume ten uninterrupted EP years are fiction until renewal history exists.
