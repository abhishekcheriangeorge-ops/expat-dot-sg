Singapore taxes individuals primarily on **Singapore-sourced** income. Tax residency affects rates and reliefs — and it is **not** the same as immigration status (EP/PR). r/askSingapore arrival threads often ask whether to time a July start to “avoid tax”; the answer sits in IRAS’s quantitative tests, not folklore.

## Why residency matters

| Status (simplified) | What people care about |
| --- | --- |
| Tax resident | Progressive resident rates + possible reliefs |
| Non-resident | Different rate treatment for employment income; fewer reliefs |
| Leaving mid-assignment | Employer **IR21 tax clearance** and withholding — see leaving guides |

Common residency pathways IRAS documents include:

- **≥183 days** in a calendar year (presence / employment exercise tests as defined by IRAS)  
- **Continuous employment straddling two calendar years** with total stay of at least **183 days** (the “straddling” concession many mid-year arrivals use)  

Apply the official tests to your dates; do not invent a hybrid from Reddit. If a foreign broker or tax office needs proof you are a Singapore tax resident for treaty relief, that is a separate **Certificate of Residence** application — [COR / DTA](/guides/certificate-of-residence-dta).

## Employer reporting vs your return

Most employers auto-transmit employment income to IRAS. In the simple case you review pre-filled figures, declare anything else taxable, and pay the Notice of Assessment. You still own:

- Benefits-in-kind / housing / school fee packages that need correct characterisation  
- Side income or consulting (pass + contract + tax — three separate questions)  
- Equity / RSUs from a foreign parent  
- Dual-country filing if your home country taxes worldwide income (e.g. US persons)

## Cashflow timing for year one

Singapore personal income tax is largely **paid in arrears**. A September starter can receive a material bill the following year covering months already spent. Forum advice that works in practice:

- Keep a **tax sinking fund** from month one (rough heuristic from package modelling — replace with your adviser’s rate)  
- After assessment, consider **GIRO** instalments if IRAS offers them for your case  
- Do not confuse “no monthly withholding” with “no tax”

## Myths to drop

- “EP means tax resident” — related, not identical  
- “Money sitting overseas is invisible forever” — source and work location matter more than remittance theatre  
- “Timing entry to 1 July always halves tax” — straddling rules and home-country exit tax often dominate
