Once payroll lands, Reddit pivots from “which bank opens for EP?” to “why is my Multiplier / 360 / One still on base rate?” Bonus-interest products are **category engines**, not magic savings accounts. Foreigners are eligible — the failure mode is almost always **unrecognised salary credit** or chasing insurance/investment categories before income clears.

This is orientation, not a rate tip sheet. Live rates, floors, and caps change; verify the bank page the month you care.

## Who can open (example: DBS Multiplier)

DBS lists Singaporeans, PRs, and foreigners on **EP / S Pass / DP / LTVP / Student’s Pass** among Multiplier openers, age **18+**, often via Singpass — or continue without Singpass and upload passport / FIN docs. Opening a Multiplier account is separate from opening the first [salary account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat); many people already bank elsewhere and only move payroll once they understand the rules.

| Prerequisite | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Lawful pass + FIN | KYC; Singpass Face Verification is the fast path |
| SGD deposit account that can receive payroll | Bonus interest sits on the product balance, but **income** is recognised from salary-shaped credits |
| Patience for month-one recognition | First payroll often hits before category logic “sees” you |

## Three products EP holders actually compare

| Product (verify live) | Salary-shaped credit cues | Typical pairing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DBS Multiplier** | GIRO / FAST / PayNow with **SAL** / **PAY** codes or **SALARY** / **PAYROLL** / **COMMISSION** / **BONUS** descriptions | Income **plus** card/PayLah!, loan, insurance, or investment categories above published floors |
| **OCBC 360** | **GIRO-SALARY** (GIRO / PayNow via GIRO) or FAST/PayNow via FAST with **SAL** / **SALARY** / **SALA** / **SAL**; common peer floor **S$1,800**/month | Salary bonus stacked with save/spend categories on OCBC’s live board |
| **UOB One** | GIRO/FAST **SALA** or PayNow **PAYNOW SALA**; common peer floor **S$1,600**/month — **or** **3 GIRO debit** transactions as the published alternative | Eligible UOB card spend (commonly **S$500**/month) plus salary **or** 3× GIRO |

Cheques, cash deposits, ordinary fund transfers, and inbound remittance wallets are the usual exclusions across issuers.

## Categories in plain English (Multiplier-style)

1. **Income** — salary / dividends / annuities credited the way the bank defines “salary”  
2. **Card / wallet retail spend**  
3. **Home-loan instalments** (rare for new EP arrivals)  
4. **Insurance** bought **after** product open (existing policies often ignored)  
5. **Investments** bought **after** product open  

Parking cash alone earns the **base** rate.

## Salary credit — the EP failure mode

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Salary arrives but product shows no income | Ask HR to use the bank’s salary GIRO path (peers cite **GIRO code 22** on some corporate files) or the exact SAL / SALA / GIRO-SALARY description that bank publishes |
| Paid from overseas HQ in FX | Convert/credit as local payroll into the SGD account the product watches — remittance wallets are not salary |
| Freelancer / DP LOC income | OCBC/UOB/DBS may reject non-employer credits; UOB’s **3× GIRO** path is the usual peer workaround when salary codes will not fire |
| Joint-account confusion | Read the issuer’s joint rules; combined income may attribute oddly |
| Insurance not recognised (foreigner) | ID on the policy must match bank records (passport vs FIN mismatches are a known Multiplier trap) |

## Sensible year-one sequence

1. Open a working [salary account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat) and get one clean payslip  
2. Only then open Multiplier / 360 / One at the bank that already sees payroll  
3. Add [card spend](/guides/credit-cards-foreigners-singapore) — not peer transfers  
4. Skip insurance/investment category farming until you understand [exit tax and SRS](/guides/srs-investing-as-foreigner)  
5. Re-check rates when balance approaches the published bonus-interest cap  

High-interest cash is a **payroll hygiene** project. Treat it after rent GIRO and [PayNow](/guides/paynow-setup-foreigners-singapore), not before.
