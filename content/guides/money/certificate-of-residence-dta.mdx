Multi-country households hit the same wall: a broker, payroll team, or foreign tax office asks for proof you are a **Singapore tax resident** so you can claim **Avoidance of Double Taxation Agreement (DTA)** benefits. That letter is an IRAS **Certificate of Residence (COR)**. It is **not** your EP card, FIN, or IR8A.

## What COR is for

IRAS issues a COR to certify Singapore **tax residency** so you can give it to a **foreign** tax authority (or withholding agent) when claiming treaty relief — reduced withholding on dividends/interest/royalties, or other DTA benefits that only **residents** of a DTA partner enjoy.

| You need COR when… | You do **not** need COR when… |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign broker / company demands Singapore residency proof for treaty rate | You only have Singapore-sourced salary and no foreign claim |
| You are reclaiming foreign withholding under a DTA | You are still a **non-resident** under IRAS tests |
| Home-country advisor asks for Singapore residency evidence | You want “proof of address” for a bank KYC form |

Non-residents do **not** get DTA resident benefits. Confirm residency first — [IRAS tax residency](/guides/iras-tax-residency-filing).

## How foreign employees apply

IRAS’s COR page splits paths by profile. **Foreign employees** with Singpass use the online COR application for DTA countries/jurisdictions; without Singpass, IRAS points to the downloadable COR application spreadsheet submitted by email. SC/PR employees have a separate QR / form path. If a foreign tax form needs **IRAS endorsement** on an original overseas form, use the physical application packet and post to Revenue House as published.

**Processing:** IRAS states about **2 to 3 weeks**. Digital-notice users get SMS/email when the letter is ready on myTax Portal; paper-notice users receive mail to the address on IRAS records.

## Expat failure modes

1. **Applying while non-resident** — wasted cycle; fix dates / straddling-year facts first  
2. **Assuming EP = tax resident forever** — residency can be reviewed at **IR21** clearance when you leave  
3. **Mixing COR with foreign-income myths** — Singapore generally does not tax most foreign-sourced income received here by individuals the way new arrivals fear; see [foreign income](/guides/foreign-income-and-tax)  
4. **Leaving RSU / dividend withholding unplanned** — [RSU/ESOP](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore) often needs treaty paperwork **before** the pay date  

Keep a PDF of the COR with your year-end tax folder. It does not replace home-country filing advice.
