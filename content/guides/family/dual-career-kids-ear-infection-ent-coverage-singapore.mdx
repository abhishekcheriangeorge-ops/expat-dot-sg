An ear infection is not “just another MC day.” Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering night pain, swimming bans, and last-minute flight holds while both calendars are still full.

Ordinary sick matrix: [sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Dengue-season fever: [dengue fever coverage](/guides/dual-career-dengue-child-fever-coverage-singapore). This guide is **ear infection / ENT ops**.

## Coverage products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named overnight adult** | Ear pain peaks at 02:00 — not dual muted Slack |
| **Same-day GP path** | Know who leaves work first before pharmacy queues |
| **Swim / flight hold list** | Lessons and Batam weekends fail without a written pause |
| **Preschool return rule** | Follow the letter; fever-free ≠ pain-free |
| **ENT escalation trigger** | Recurrent cases need a dated referral, not forum drops |

<OfficialSource>
Clinical orientation: [HealthHub middle ear infection](https://www.healthhub.sg/a-z/diseases-and-conditions/middle-ear-infection), [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/), and children’s hospital ENT pages such as [KKH](https://www.kkh.com.sg/). Medication and clearance decisions are clinician-led — do not invent “always amoxicillin” folklore.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career playbook

1. Name tonight’s coverage owner before bedtime ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
2. Book GP / paediatric slots early if pain + fever stack ([finding a paediatrician](/guides/finding-pediatrician-singapore)).  
3. Pause swim school and water play until cleared ([water safety](/guides/kids-swimming-water-safety-singapore)).  
4. Rewrite helper rest days if nights are broken ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
5. Keep a short ENT clinic shortlist for recurrence — not WhatsApp randoms.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Sending a swimming child “because fever broke” | Wait for clinician clearance |
| Both parents half-monitoring overnight | One overnight owner |
| Flying with unresolved ear pain | Ask before you book the taxi to Changi |
| Stacking enrichment the next morning | Cancel early; protect sleep |
