r/askSingapore birth-cost threads ([give birth here vs home country](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1l1fe33/important_considerations_whether_to_have_my/)) keep naming the same soft-cost surprise: **28 days of confinement help** can rival a cheap flight home for grandparents. For foreigner households the harder part is not the agency brochure — it is **MOM’s Malaysian-only Work Permit**, the **citizen vs non-citizen baby levy**, and stacking that admin on top of the [newborn pass clock](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore).

Confinement is week-one coverage — the dual-career relay into infant care is [newborn→preschool handoff](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

## What a confinement nanny is (and is not)

| | Confinement nanny (pui yuet / yue sao) | MDW / long-term helper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical window | ~**28 days** postpartum (agency packages); MOM WP max **16 weeks** from birth | Years, with full MDW rules |
| Core job | Mother recovery + newborn nights, meals, early breastfeeding support | Whole-household domestic work |
| Foreign hire path | **Malaysian** Work Permit under confinement-nanny scheme | Separate MDW Work Permit / agency stack |
| After the window | Ends — not a backdoor helper | Continues if you keep the MDW |

If you already have (or are hiring) a helper, decide **roles** before day 0: confinement nanny for fourth-trimester intensity, helper for ongoing household load — see [hiring a domestic helper](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw). Do **not** assume one person can legally cover both titles.

## MOM Work Permit — Malaysian-only gate

Official scheme facts parents skip:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Nanny nationality | **Malaysian** only under this WP |
| Nanny age | At least **23** and below **70** at application |
| Employer | **21+**; must have a newborn under **4 months** |
| Duration | Up to **16 weeks** from the child’s date of birth — applying late **shortens** validity |
| Renewable | **No** |
| Application fee | **S$35** (verify live apply page) |
| Pass format | WP **letter**, not a plastic card (short duration) |

Apply from **4 weeks before EDD** up to **16 weeks after birth**. Beyond that window, MOM points you to a normal **MDW** Work Permit instead.

## Levy — the citizenship tax on sleep

| Newborn status | Monthly levy (MOM) |
| --- | --- |
| **Singapore citizen** child | **S$60** / month |
| **Non-Singapore citizen** child | **S$300** / month |

Dual-foreigner EP/S Pass babies sit on the **S$300** line. SC babies (including mixed-status households where the child is citizen) get the concession band. If you already have a helper on your name, MOM combines levies for GIRO — still budget the confinement band explicitly.

Medical insurance for the nanny is required (MDW-style minimums — confirm live MOM insurance notes before policy start dates). Salary paid to the nanny is **taxable**; IRAS rules apply even for a short stint.

## Package realism (order of magnitude, not a quote)

Agency 28-day stay-in packages commonly land in the **mid four figures SGD** before you add:

- **Ang bao** / first-day gift culture (often hundreds of dollars — confirm agency norms)  
- Extension days after day 28  
- Groceries, confinement herbs, and postnatal massage sold as **add-ons**  
- Transport for the nanny  
- The **levy** and insurance if the agency did **not** bake them in  

Treat “all-in” claims as a checklist, not a vibe. Ask in writing whether MOM application, levy, and insurance sit inside the package. Do **not** confuse confinement WP with the MDW **security bond** story — different scheme.

## Decision frame for foreigner households

| Option | When it wins | When it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Malaysian confinement nanny + MOM WP | You want structured postpartum help in SG and can run WP + levy on top of delivery bills | You leave the WP to the last week of pregnancy |
| SC/PR / local night nurse (no WP) | You can afford higher day rates and skip levy | Supply is thin; English / method fit still varies |
| Family flying in | Culture match + trust; often cheaper than SG packages | Visitor Pass length, housing space, and your partner’s recovery load |
| Give birth abroad then return | Lower clinical + confinement stack in some countries | Dual paperwork; baby still needs SG pass path if you live here — [maternity](/guides/maternity-care-singapore) |
| Helper only from week one | You already have a trained MDW and clear night coverage | Newborn intensity + household chores burn one person out |

Forum heat about “just hire a nanny for 2.8–4k” ignores the **foreign-baby levy** and the parallel [birth registration / DP sprint](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore). Put confinement booking on the same project board as LifeSG registration and embassy passport slots.

## Practical sequence (third trimester → week 6)

1. Decide **SG delivery vs home country** with unsubsidised foreigner billing in mind — [maternity care](/guides/maternity-care-singapore)  
2. If staying in SG: shortlist agencies **and** confirm Malaysian WP eligibility; apply as early as MOM allows  
3. Parallel-path helper / infant-care plan for month 2+ — [preschool & childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore)  
4. After birth: LifeSG registration → passport → employer DP lodge — do not let confinement logistics eclipse the immigration clock  
5. Book paediatric follow-ups on cash-pay terms if needed — [finding a paediatrician](/guides/finding-pediatrician-singapore)
