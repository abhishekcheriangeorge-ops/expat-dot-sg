Mid-year transfers are not a second orientation week with nicer tote bags. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep underestimating **notice periods**, LS handoffs, and the week both parents still have full Slack while uniforms and buses reset.

Orientation ops: [orientation week](/guides/international-school-orientation-week-dual-career-singapore). Waitlists: [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism). This guide is **mid-year transfer ops**.

## Transfer products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Written leave notice** | Deposit / fee rules are school-specific — screenshots first |
| **LS / IEP handoff pack** | Do not arrive cold if support was in progress |
| **Named ops owner** | One adult owns admissions portal + bus forms |
| **Two-week soft landing** | Shorter work blocks while routes and friendships reset |
| **Sibling impact check** | One child’s move can still break the other’s CCA calendar |

<OfficialSource>
International-school admissions and withdrawal norms are school-specific ([MOE international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools); [Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub)). There is no single government “mid-year transfer guarantee.”
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career playbook

1. Decide the go / no-go date with fee forfeiture math on paper.  
2. Put admissions interviews and uniform fittings on **both** calendars ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. If LS was active, transfer the packet early ([LS waitlist ops](/guides/international-school-learning-support-waitlist-ops-dual-career-singapore)).  
4. Reset bus / pickup before day one ([bus/pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore)).  
5. Re-check sibling fee / bursary effects ([sibling fees](/guides/international-school-sibling-fees-bursary-singapore)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Treating mid-year like August orientation | Notice + forfeiture clocks differ |
| Losing the LS paper trail | Hand-carry PDFs |
| Both parents “helping” with no owner | One ops lead |
| Ignoring the child still in the old school | Sibling calendar impact |
