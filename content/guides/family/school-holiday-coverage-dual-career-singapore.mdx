Singapore school calendars do not care that both parents have Q3 closes. r/askSingapore dual-career threads spike every May and November: camps sold out, helper on home leave, and two EP calendars that somehow both “cannot take leave that week.”

Camp product catalogues live in [kids activities & holiday camps](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps). This guide is the **coverage operating system** for EP/DP dual-career households — not another enrichment brochure.

## Map the calendar before you map the camps

| Block | Typical pressure | Book-by heuristic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **March** short break | Light — good dry-run | 4–6 weeks out |
| **June** long holidays | Highest camp demand | 8–12 weeks out |
| **September** break | Mixed IS / MOE | 6 weeks out |
| **November–December** | Flights + camp + family visitors | 10–12 weeks out |

<OfficialSource>
MOE holiday frames: [MOE school calendar](https://www.moe.gov.sg/calendar). International schools publish their own — never assume MOE weeks equal IS weeks. Employment leave baselines: [MOM leave](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave) (contract still wins).
</OfficialSource>

## Coverage stack (pick two, not seven)

| Layer | Best for | Fail mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Split parental leave** | Keystone weeks both want at home | Both save leave for “later” and later never comes |
| **Holiday camps** | Structured days; sibling batches | Waitlisting in May for June |
| **Part-time babysitter / day nanny** | Bridge days camps do not cover | Hiring in week zero — [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore) |
| **Helper overtime / swapped off-days** | Known household adult | Helper home leave colliding with school holidays |
| **Membership-club kids weeks** | One campus logistics | Entrance fees — [membership clubs](/guides/membership-clubs-comparison) |
| **Grandparent visit** | Soft cover | Immigration is not a babysitter product — plan passes separately |

Dual-career role clarity still sits in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Preschool-age wraparound differs from primary — [preschool childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore).

## Six-week ops checklist

1. Overlay **both** employer blackout dates on the school calendar  
2. Mark “one adult must be in Singapore” weeks (visa/medical/SEN)  
3. Submit camp applications before flights  
4. Confirm babysitter/helper cover for camp half-days and pickup cliffs  
5. Pre-write the emergency adult list for camp forms  
6. Protect one recovery weekend — burnout is not a coverage strategy  

SEN transport or shadow-support needs longer lead times — [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support). Secondary exam-year teens may need quieter study weeks more than camps — [IB/A-Level pathways](/guides/secondary-ib-alevel-pathways-singapore).

## Club and campus forks

Junior holiday programmes at [British Club](/entities/british-club) or [Singapore Cricket Club](/entities/singapore-cricket-club) trade money for fewer venue hops. IS-run camps at places like [Dulwich](/entities/dulwich-college-singapore) can simplify sibling logistics if seats exist — still book early.

## What usually fails

- Booking December flights before June camps  
- Assuming the trailing partner “will figure something out” when both are employed  
- Three camps in three corners of the island with 08:00 drop-offs  
- Zero backup adult when a camp cancels for haze  
- Treating every holiday as enrichment instead of rest  

Holiday coverage is a **project plan**. Design the stack when the term starts, not the Friday before break.
