Enrolment is a decision. **Pickup is a product.** Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep learning that a 3:10 dismissal plus a 4:00 client call is not a “figure it out later” problem — it is a weekly ops design.

School choice still starts with the [landscape](/guides/international-schools-landscape). Enrichment load lives in [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps). This guide is **bus + pickup + bridge coverage** for two working adults.

## Pickup products (pick one primary)

| Product | Best when | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **School / contracted bus** | Stable address + predictable end time | Late CCA / early dismissal days orphan the schedule |
| **Helper / driver pickup** | One adult already home-side | Rest day + medical leave = sudden cliff |
| **After-school care / late room** | Both EPs in CBD / travel weeks | Seat waitlists; fever exclusions |
| **Grab / private hire backup** | Occasional overflow | Daily use destroys the COL model |
| **Named adult swap** (condo / colleague) | Rare emergencies | Not a standing plan |

Role clarity still sits in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Bridge hours: [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore). Helper compliance: [hiring MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw).

Local-system student care orientation: [MOE Student Care Centres](https://www.moe.gov.sg/education-in-sg/our-programme/student-care-centres). International-school bus terms are **operator + school contracts** — get routes, cut-off times, and CCA exceptions in writing; LTA framing does not replace your invoice.

## Dual-career ops that survive Tuesday

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Put **bus route + CCA calendar** on a shared household board | Surprises are usually timetable collisions |
| Assign a **primary pickup owner** per weekday | “Whoever finishes first” fails every travel week |
| Pre-buy **10 Grab credits / month** as overflow, not daily default | Overflow fund beats daily FOMO |
| Write **early-dismissal / fever-day** protocol | Centres and buses exclude sick kids; meetings do not |
| Rehearse one **rest-day / leave** alternate | Helpers and grandparents are not infinite |

## Contract questions for the school / bus operator

1. What is the latest boarding time on **CCA days**?  
2. Who do you SMS when the bus is 20 minutes late?  
3. Can a sibling or named adult collect without a same-day email?  
4. What happens on **exam / half-days**?  
5. Is the seat tied to an address — and what is the change lead time?
