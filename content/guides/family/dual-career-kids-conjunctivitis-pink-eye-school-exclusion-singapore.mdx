Pink eye spreads faster than Slack replies. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep debating “sticky eye vs allergy” at 07:10 while the bus is still coming — then learning that **school exclusion is written**, not vibes-based.

Ordinary sick matrix: [sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Outbreak letters: [HFMD coverage](/guides/dual-career-hfmd-school-outbreak-coverage-singapore). Vesicles: [chickenpox exclusion](/guides/dual-career-chickenpox-varicella-school-exclusion-singapore). This guide is **conjunctivitis / pink-eye exclusion ops**.

## Coverage products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named overnight + morning owner** | Eye pain and discharge peak before first Zoom |
| **Written return rule** | Centre letters beat WhatsApp folklore |
| **GP vs eye-clinic fork** | Know when pharmacy drops fail |
| **Swim / water hold in writing** | Pool decks do not want sticky-eye debates |
| **Sibling class check** | Same preschool year can cascade |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Photograph discharge / redness once for the GP chat — then wash hands and stop sharing towels.  
2. Name today’s coverage owner before the first stand-up ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. Book GP / paediatric review early ([finding a paediatrician](/guides/finding-pediatrician-singapore)); escalate if pain, light sensitivity, or vision change suggests more than garden-variety pink eye ([optical & eye care](/entities/optical-eye-care)).  
4. Forward the school/preschool exclusion + return rule into the shared channel; put the clearance date on the calendar.  
5. Pause swim and water CCA until the written hold lifts ([kids swimming & water safety](/guides/kids-swimming-water-safety-singapore)).  
6. Rewrite helper rest-day collisions if exclusion lands on a Sunday ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| “Eyes look better after breakfast” = cleared | Follow the written return rule |
| Both parents “monitoring between meetings” | One coverage owner |
| Sending sticky-eye kids to swim “for the social” | Written water hold |
| Sharing pillows / face towels across siblings | Laundry + separate towels day one |
| Treating every red eye as bacterial drops folklore | Clinician call; allergy and viral paths differ |

## Questions to close tonight

1. Who owns overnight wipe-downs and morning drop decision?  
2. What exact line does the school letter use for return?  
3. Is swim / water enrichment paused in writing?
