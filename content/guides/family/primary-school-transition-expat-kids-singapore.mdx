Preschool exit is not “just pick a brand.” It is a **year-zero operating system**: which system (MOE primary vs international Year 1/2), which language load, which commute, and who covers the 1–3pm cliff. r/askSingapore P1 and “IS vs local primary” threads keep punishing families who treated waitlists as a plan and Mother Tongue as a footnote.

Seat scarcity still lives in [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism). Local-system fee and MTL traps sit in [local schools for expats](/guides/local-schools-for-expats). This guide is the **transition year** playbook.

## Two products, two calendars

| Path | What you are actually buying | First-term shocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MOE primary (P1)** | Assignment-heavy local system; non-citizen fee bands | Interest / registration windows; Mother Tongue; CCA later |
| **International primary (Y1/Y2)** | Curriculum continuity for mobile kids | Application fees + waitlists; bus contracts; enrichment upsell |

Do not mix calendars. An IS deposit does not hold an MOE P1 seat, and a P1 balloting story does not unlock a Tanglin / UWC Year 1 place.

International-student P1 and mainstream admissions: [MOE P1 for international students](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/international-students) and [MOE international admissions](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students/admission). Mother Tongue framing: [MOE MTL](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/curriculum/mother-tongue-languages). Non-citizen fee bands: [MOE school fees](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/fees).

## Twelve months before Day 1

| Window | Ops to finish |
| --- | --- |
| T−12 to T−9 | Shortlist **two** systems; map housing to bus / MRT reality |
| T−9 to T−6 | File IS applications; diary MOE international P1 interest windows |
| T−6 to T−3 | Decide language strategy (MTL / Chinese enrichment / English-only IS) |
| T−3 to T−1 | Lock after-school care rail; trial bus; pack medical / allergy / ADHD files |
| Week 0 | Name one parent ops lead for forms, WhatsApp, and early pickups |

Dual-career homes fail when **both** adults assume the other owns the 1pm call — pair with [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore) and [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore).

## Mother Tongue and Mandarin without panic

Local primary MTL is a **structural** load for many EP/DP kids, not a weekend hobby. If you choose MOE primary with weak Chinese at home, budget tutoring early — see [Mandarin enrichment](/guides/mandarin-enrichment-expat-kids-singapore). International primary does not erase language politics; it just moves them into optional Chinese tracks and playground code-switching.

## After-school cliff (the real burnout trigger)

| Pattern | Why it breaks |
| --- | --- |
| “School ends at 1; we’ll figure it out” | Both parents still in meetings |
| Stacking three enrichment trials in week one | Exhausted kids + Grab maths |
| Helper start date after term start | No bridge care |
| Ignoring heat / haze PE cancellations | Sudden half-days |

Keep enrichment light for the first six weeks — [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps) — and treat care continuity as infrastructure, not a treat.

## SEN and medical handoffs

If your child already has learning support, allergy action plans, or ADHD meds, transfer **local letters** before Day 1 — [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support). Overseas PDFs alone rarely unlock IS learning support or MOE school processes.

## What usually fails

- Paying two IS application fees and treating that as a P1 strategy  
- Choosing a school 45 minutes from the office “because friends go there”  
- Ignoring MTL until the first failed spelling test  
- Starting helper / babysitter search in week two of term  
- Assuming AEIS is a casual backup without reading [AEIS/SAEIS](/guides/aeis-saeis-international-students) timelines  

Primary transition is a **project**, not a brand decision. Lock system, language, commute, and care before you argue about uniforms.

Belonging note: art-studio waiting sofas can be a quieter adult hello rail during primary transition term than another PA coffee — keep showcase politics out of it ([art-studio waiting](/guides/art-studio-waiting-parent-belonging-singapore)).
