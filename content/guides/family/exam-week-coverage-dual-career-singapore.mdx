Holiday camps are a logistics puzzle. **Exam weeks** are a different product: sleep, silence, food, and which adult is on-island when the mock timetable shifts. r/askSingapore dual-career parents keep learning that EP blackout dates and IB/IGCSE calendars do not negotiate.

Pathway choice still lives in [IB vs A-Level](/guides/secondary-ib-alevel-pathways-singapore). Application storytelling lives in [university counselling](/guides/university-counselling-international-schools-singapore). This guide is **exam-week household coverage** for EP/DP dual-career homes.

## Exam season ≠ holiday coverage

| Season | Primary job | Coverage shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School holidays | Supervised daytime activity | Camps / sitters / split leave |
| **Mocks / finals** | Protected sleep + study blocks | Quiet house, food ops, one adult “on call” |
| Results / clearing windows | Rapid adult availability | Named parent for school calls |

Do not recycle the June camp spreadsheet as your November mock plan.

## Dual-career ops that actually work

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Overlay **both** employer blackouts on the school exam PDF | Surprises are usually calendar collisions |
| Assign a **primary on-call parent** per paper week | Two “busy” adults = zero coverage |
| Cap new tuition starts mid-crunch | Adding a fourth tutor in week two is panic, not strategy |
| Pre-cook / helper menu for late papers | Decision fatigue is real |
| Pause non-essential enrichment | [Kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps) can wait a fortnight |

Role clarity still sits in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Younger siblings still need a human — [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore) for after-school cliffs while the exam child owns the dining table.

## Stress and SEN adjacency

If sleep collapses or anxiety spikes, open clinical rails early — [mental health resources](/guides/mental-health-resources) and [teen school counselling](/guides/teen-mental-health-school-counselling-singapore). Access arrangements and learning support need lead time — [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support). Clinic continuity for stress-related illness: [Parkway Shenton](/directory/clinics/parkway-shenton) / [SMG](/directory/clinics/smg) as category frames, not endorsements.

## Soft-start checklist (T−6 weeks)

1. Print the full mock/final timetable including orals  
2. Mark travel bans for both adults  
3. Agree “no new CCA commitments” window  
4. Name the emergency adult if both get pulled into regional flights  
5. Tell grandparents/visitors the house is a study zone — social plans move off-site  

Nationality association study halls can help trailing partners host quiet afternoons — e.g. [Japanese Association](/clubs/japanese-association) peer tips — but they do not replace a named on-call parent.

## What usually fails

- Both parents booking client travel across HL maths week  
- Treating every exam season like another enrichment opportunity  
- Discovering oral dates three days prior  
- Sibling birthday parties in the study room  
- Waiting until meltdown week to cancel football  

Exam-week coverage is a **protected calendar**. Design it when the mock schedule drops, not the night before Paper 1.

Book-week costume blackouts are a separate ops product — do not stack parade call times onto Paper week ([book week / author-visit ops](/guides/international-school-book-week-author-visit-ops-dual-career-singapore)).
