Dual-career Singapore postings often fail quietly: one partner’s EP is fine, the kids’ school is fine, and the **parents** are running on fumes. r/askSingapore “exhausted parents,” helper-gap, and “spouse on DP can’t breathe” threads keep pointing at the same stack — long hours, thin grandparents, paid care that still needs management, and an employer EAP nobody trusts until week 40.

This guide is the **parent-load** layer. Clinical pathways sit in [mental health resources](/guides/mental-health-resources); spouse job-search rails sit in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

## Burnout vs “just busy”

| Signal | Usually means |
| --- | --- |
| Dreading Sunday night for **months** | Load > coping, not a bad week |
| Snapping at kids after every Teams call | Recovery debt, not “bad parenting” |
| Helper / babysitter drama feels existential | Care logistics are the job |
| One partner’s career is “optional” on paper only | Household spreadsheet lies |
| Sleep debt + no friends outside work | Belonging gap compounding clinical risk |

Burnout is a **systems** problem: care hours + commute + school admin + visa household + social vacuum. Fixing only one rail rarely sticks.

## Employer EAP — what foreigners should verify

| Claim you will hear | Reality check |
| --- | --- |
| “EAP is confidential” | Usually true for the vendor; still read whether HR gets utilisation **themes** vs named cases |
| “Unlimited counselling” | Most plans are **3–6 sessions**, then cash or referral |
| “Covers the whole family” | Confirm DP spouse + children ages; many cover employee only |
| “They can prescribe” | EAP is usually talk-first; psychiatry is a separate door |
| “Use it or lose it” | Unused EAP does not roll — but stigma still blocks first calls |

Ask HR (or the EAP portal FAQ) for: session count, dependents, video vs in-person, and the **handoff** when you need a psychiatrist. Pair with the benefits schedule in [health insurance for expats](/guides/health-insurance-expat) — outpatient mental-health carve-outs are common even when EAP exists.

## Clinical doors that work for EP/DP parents

1. **EAP intake this week** — use it for triage and short-term skills even if you plan private therapy later  
2. **GP / panel clinic** — useful when sleep, anxiety meds, or a psychiatrist referral is on the table ([healthcare GP/hospital](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital))  
3. **Private psychologist / counsellor** — match modality (CBT, couples, parenting) to the problem  
4. **Public / charity pathways** — IMH and community counselling are open to foreigners at **unsubsidised** rates; ask fee class before you assume “public = cheap”

## Household ops that reduce load

| Lever | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed **two** protected recovery blocks weekly | Burnout hates vague “self-care someday” |
| Named care rail (helper / [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore) / preschool) | Stops nightly renegotiation |
| One enrichment cut | Kids calendar bloat is a parent tax — [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps) |
| One belonging rail for **each** adult | Work friends ≠ recovery — [find my people](/guides/find-my-people-singapore) / [ANZA/AWA](/guides/anza-awa-associations) |
| Written split of school admin | Forms and teacher emails become one person’s unpaid job otherwise |

Trailing partners: career search stress and parental burnout stack — keep [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore) and this guide open together.

## What usually fails

- Waiting until someone “breaks” before opening EAP  
- Assuming mindline / EAP replaces couples therapy when the fight is structural  
- Adding more enrichment to “tire the kids out” while parents get thinner  
- Treating the helper as emotional labour + childcare + house manager without a backup plan  
- Ignoring DP spouse isolation because the EP’s calendar is full  

Parental burnout is an assignment-risk issue, not a private moral failure. Book the boring support early — EAP, care hours, and one friendship rail — before the posting’s year-two cliff.
