Preschools and many international schools exclude febrile kids. Meetings do not. Dual-career EP/DP threads on r/askSingapore keep failing the same way: both adults “can WFH,” neither is actually free, and the helper’s rest day lands on the worst Tuesday.

Role clarity still lives in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Clinical rails: [finding a paediatrician](/guides/finding-pediatrician-singapore) and [GP/hospital](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital). This guide is the **coverage matrix**.

## Coverage products (pick a primary per weekday)

| Product | Works when | Breaks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Named parent on-call** | Calendar blackout is real | “Flexible WFH” with back-to-backs |
| **Helper at home** | Healthy helper + rest day elsewhere | Rest day / MC / annual leave clash |
| **Backup sitter list (2 names)** | Pre-vetted and paid retainer option | First call on fever morning |
| **Grandparent / visitor** | On-island and willing | Flight schedules and jet lag |
| **Clinic + quiet room at office** | Employer culture allows it | Contagious illness / no quiet room |

Bridge care: [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore). Helper ops: [hiring MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw). Preschool exclusion norms: [preschool & childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore).

Health-seeking orientation: [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/). Early-childhood framing: [ECDA](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/). Centre fever policies are **centre-specific** — keep the PDF, not a WhatsApp paraphrase.
</OfficialSource>

## The weekday matrix (write it down)

| Day | Primary coverage | Backup | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Parent A | Sitter 1 | Avoid helper rest day |
| Tue | Parent B | Helper | |
| Wed | Helper | Parent A | |
| Thu | Parent A | Sitter 2 | |
| Fri | Parent B | Helper | |

Review monthly. Travel weeks need a temporary amendment — same discipline as leave planning.

## Fever-day runbook (first 90 minutes)

1. Temp + symptoms → decide centre drop-off vs stay home using the written policy.  
2. Message the **named owner** for that weekday — not the whole family chat.  
3. Book GP/paeds slot early; bring the vaccination book.  
4. Cancel enrichment; do not “see how the afternoon goes.”  
5. If allergy/anaphylaxis history, follow [EpiPen plan](/guides/kids-allergy-epipen-singapore).

## Dual-career failure modes

| Failure | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Both parents take “half days” and neither covers lunch | One full owner until 15:00 |
| First fever of the year = first time calling a sitter | Quarterly paid trial hours |
| Helper rest day fixed on Sunday only… until it isn’t | Rest-day calendar shared with both EPs |
| Employer sympathy without calendar rights | Put coverage needs in leave tools, not Slack vibes |

Dual-career note: ear pain is a different overnight product from generic fever — name an overnight owner and pause swim school until cleared ([ear infection coverage](/guides/dual-career-kids-ear-infection-ent-coverage-singapore)).

Eczema flares are another overnight product — name a cream-kit owner and pause swim until the hold is written ([eczema-flare coverage](/guides/dual-career-kids-eczema-flare-coverage-singapore)).
