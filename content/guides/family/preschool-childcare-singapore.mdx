Care options range from infant care centres to kindergartens and helper-led home care. For foreigners, the single most expensive misunderstanding is assuming you will see the same **net fees** as Singaporean parents discussing “subsidised childcare” on Facebook.

## Subsidy reality for non-citizens

ECDA preschool subsidies (Basic / Additional Infant & Childcare subsidies, and KiFAS for eligible kindergartens) are framed for **Singapore Citizen children**. Permanent Resident and foreign children enrolled in licensed centres generally pay the centre’s **published gross fee** (minus any employer benefit you negotiate yourself).

You may still need to complete **Form 1** enrolment reporting via LifeSG for regulatory purposes even when you are not subsidy-eligible.

## Fee bands foreigners actually quote (planning only)

| Path | Monthly conversation band (per child) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anchor / large licensed full-day childcare (unsubsidised) | ~S$1,200–2,000+ | Popular districts fill first |
| Private preschool / boutique centres | ~S$1,500–3,500+ | Curriculum branding premium |
| International school early years | Often several thousand | Bundled into school fee architecture |
| Helper-led home care | See MDW all-in cost | Not a licensed preschool substitute for socialisation |

Replace every number with live centre quotes. The directional point: **two unsubsidised preschoolers can rival a mid-tier condo upgrade**.

## Centre types (decision frame)

| Type | Strength | Weakness for expats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ECDA-licensed infant / childcare | Regulation, full-day hours for dual careers | Vacancies; SC priority dynamics in popular sites |
| Anchor Operator centres | Scale, known brands | Still full-fee without SC subsidy |
| Private preschool brands | Flexible programmes, bilingual options | Price; quality variance |
| International school nursery/reception | Continuity into primary pathway | Cost; early waitlists |
| Playgroups / enrichment only | Socialisation without full-day fees | Does not unlock full-time work |

## Waitlist and visit tactics

- Join lists **before** wheels-down if your move date is known  
- Visit at **pickup chaos**, not only the showcase tour  
- Ask explicitly: “What is the **gross monthly fee for a foreign child** on a Dependant’s Pass?”  
- Model backup care for centre closures and child sick days — both parents working without a helper is brittle  
- Short-term / tourist-length childcare is rare; centres want term commitments and deposits  

## Helper + preschool stacks

Many dual-career expat households run **preschool mornings + MDW afternoons** (or the reverse). Budget both lines; do not assume one replaces the other. See [hiring an MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw).

## Path into primary

Preschool choice is not destiny, but geography and friendship networks form early. If you expect international primary, map [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism) and the [international landscape](/guides/international-schools-landscape) in the same quarter you pick infant care.

Childcare is where Singapore’s cost-of-living spreadsheet stops being theoretical. Price the unsubsidised reality first — then decide between centre, helper, or both.
