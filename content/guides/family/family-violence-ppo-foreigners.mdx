On forums, foreign spouses and DP/LTVP holders often delay calling for help because “my pass is under his/her name.” **Safety is not a Singpass form.** Police, NAVH, and Personal Protection Orders exist for people in Singapore regardless of whether your Reddit thread says the sponsor will “cancel everything tomorrow.” Pass strategy comes *after* you are safe — with counsel and, where needed, ICA/MOM advice.

This is crisis orientation, not legal advice.

## Immediate steps

| Situation | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Danger now | **999** (or SMS 70999) |
| Violence / abuse / unsure | **NAVH 1800-777-0000** (24h) or MSF online report |
| Injuries | Medical exam — keep records for PPO |
| Need protection order help | Family Justice Courts **or** a Protection Specialist Centre (PAVE, TRANS SAFE, Care Corner Project StART) |

## What a PPO is (and is not)

A **Personal Protection Order** restrains a family member from committing family violence against you or other family members when the court is satisfied it is necessary. Related orders (often with a protection order) can include expedited orders, domestic exclusion, stay-away, and no-contact orders — see MSF / Family Assist lists.

A PPO is **not** an immigration pass. It does not automatically create a new LTVP. It *does* create a court record and safety tools while you sort housing, kids, and status with specialists and lawyers.

## Pass dependency — plan without freezing

| Status | Typical trap | Next conversation (with counsel / PSC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DP** on spouse’s EP | Sponsor loses job or cancels spitefully | New pass, STVP timing, [between-jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |
| **LTVP** sponsored by SC/PR spouse | Fear of withdrawal / non-renewal | ICA reality + shelter/legal aid path — [LTVP](/guides/ltvp-singapore) |
| Visit pass / pending | Overstay panic | Do not overstay; use published extension / leave rails — [visit-pass extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension) |

Immigration officers and Protection Specialist Centres have seen sponsor-abuse patterns before. AWARE, shelters, and family lawyers routinely help foreign spouses who were told “you will be deported if you report.” Treat that threat as **control**, not gospel — verify with professionals.

Kids and cross-border flight risk sit on the divorce / Hague guide — [divorce & custody](/guides/divorce-custody-foreigners-singapore). Workplace harassment is a different rail — [TAFEP](/guides/workplace-harassment-tafep-ep).

## Neighbours who hear violence

If you are the upstairs/downstairs neighbour: call **999** when someone may be in danger; otherwise NAVH. Do not “resolve” it as MCST noise — [condo noise guide](/guides/condo-noise-mcst-neighbours).

## Practical checklist

1. Save **999**, **1800-777-0000**, and a PSC number offline  
2. Keep passport, FIN/pass card, cash, meds, and kids’ documents accessible  
3. Tell one trusted person your plan  
4. Apply for PPO with PSC support if advised  
5. Only then sequence pass, housing, and divorce filings with counsel
