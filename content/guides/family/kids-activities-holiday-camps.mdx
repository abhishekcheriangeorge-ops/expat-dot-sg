Singapore offers dense enrichment — which can become a **second full-time job** for parents if you let every brochure win.

## Arrival-year advice

Prioritise **one social activity** and **one physical activity**. Let friendships form before stacking resume ornaments. Newly arrived children already carry climate, accent, and curriculum shock — see [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).

| Phase | Focus | Defer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First term | Classmates + one sport/club | Competitive travel teams |
| Second term | One enrichment if energy remains | Triple-stack weekday evenings |
| Holiday peaks | Camps that match sibling logistics | Every “must-do” flyer |

## Holiday camps: book early

June and November–December camps fill first at popular international schools, sports academies, and ActiveSG programmes. Factor:

- Bus timings and pickup windows  
- Sibling split campuses (two Grab routes = real cost)  
- Helper rest days vs camp half-days — see [MDW guide](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw)  
- Heat/haze outdoor cancellations — [haze](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  

School parent WhatsApp groups and early-bird emails matter more than polished websites. Verify refund rules before paying deposits.

## Enrichment without the arms race

Method centres (e.g. [Kumon](/entities/kumon-centres)), swimming, music, and coding all exist islandwide. Commute friction kills consistency faster than brand prestige. Trial a month; exit guilt-free if your child hates it.

Public options via [ActiveSG](https://www.activesgcircle.gov.sg/) can be cheaper than boutique academies once Singpass booking works — see [sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore).

## CCA culture (local vs international)

Local MOE schools treat Co-Curricular Activities as structural; international schools vary by campus. Ask current parents what “optional” really means before promising your child a quiet Tuesday. For preschool-age care stacking, see [preschool & childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore).
