Birth admin is a sprint. **Stable daytime coverage until preschool** is a relay. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep losing months by treating confinement, infant-care waitlists, and return-to-office as three unrelated projects.

Pass paperwork still lives in [newborn pass & birth registration](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore). Daycare product choice lives in [preschool & childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore). This guide is the **handoff timeline** between those rails.

## The relay (not a single hire)

| Phase | Typical window | Coverage product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospital → first weeks | 0–4 weeks | Confinement nanny / family / one parent on leave |
| Bridge | ~1–6 months | Helper, flexible WFH, part-time sitters, or stacked leave |
| Infant care / preschool start | Often 6–18 months depending on seat | Centre seat + backup adult for fever days |
| Stable year-one | After settling | Named fever-day parent + backup sitter |

Do not assume the confinement nanny becomes your preschool plan — different MOM rails and different skills. See [confinement nanny](/guides/confinement-nanny-postpartum-singapore).

## Dual-career ops that actually work

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Put **infant-care applications** on the calendar while pregnant | Popular centres do not care that your EP blackout ends in week six |
| Decide **which parent** owns fever-day calls before return-to-office | Two “busy” calendars = zero pickup |
| Separate **confinement** budget from **MDW / sitter** budget | One quote cannot buy both products |
| Map leave with citizenship gates honestly | [Paternity / shared leave](/guides/paternity-shared-parental-leave-foreigners) is often closed for foreign children |
| Keep a paid backup for the first preschool term | Centres exclude sick kids; meetings do not |

Role clarity: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Bridge hours: [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore). Maternity framing: [maternity care](/guides/maternity-care-singapore).

## Timeline checklist (directional)

1. **T−6 to T−3 months:** shortlist infant care; ask waitlist + foreigner fee bands in writing.  
2. **T−3 to T−1:** book confinement help; confirm newborn DP sequence with HR.  
3. **Birth month:** registration + DP; vaccination card starts; do not freeze centre applications “until life settles.”  
4. **Months 1–4:** decide helper vs sitter vs stacked WFH — write the decision, including rest days.  
5. **First centre start:** named fever-day parent + Grab-budget backup adult for the first six weeks.
