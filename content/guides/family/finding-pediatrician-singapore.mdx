Pick a paediatric clinic near home, not near the CBD office you leave at 19:30. Forum threads from cash-pay foreigners converge on the same trade-off: **private continuity vs polyclinic price**, with the caveat that non-citizens usually **do not get citizen/PR subsidies**.

## Private vs polyclinic for foreign kids

| Path | Best for | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private paediatric clinic | Continuity, after-hours guidance, employer panel rates | Walk-in fees can surprise; confirm cash/card and insurance coding |
| Polyclinic | Vaccinations and routine reviews when queues are tolerable | Foreigner fees are unsubsidised — compare before assuming “cheap” |
| Children’s hospital ED (e.g. KKH / NUH pathways) | True emergencies | Not your well-child clinic |

Many families run a hybrid: polyclinic or panel GP for vaccines and mild URTIs; a named private paediatrician for complex issues and when you need someone who already knows your child. For NIR uploads, diphtheria/measles pass docs, and SC/PR vs foreigner fee bands, use the dedicated [childhood vaccinations for foreigners](/guides/childhood-vaccination-foreigners) guide before you book a polyclinic slot that rejects missing registry history.

## What to check before the first fever

- **Insurance panel status** — in-network rates matter more than brand names  
- Weekend / public-holiday hours and tele-advice practices  
- How vaccination records are stored for mobile kids (ask for a printable history)  
- Distance from home at 2 a.m., not from the office  
- Whether developmental concerns can be referred onward (see [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support))  

Bring previous immunisation records translated if needed. Establish the relationship during a **well visit** — not only in crisis.

## Dependant Pass and vaccines

If children are joining on Dependant Pass formalities, confirm any medical or vaccination requirements with MOM/ICA timing before you assume the school calendar is the only deadline. Keep a digital + paper copy of the immunisation booklet.

## After-hours plan

Write down tonight:

1. Clinic after-hours number (if any)  
2. Nearest 24-hour children’s / family clinic your insurer accepts  
3. Hospital ED you would actually drive to  
4. What “wait until morning” means for your child’s age
