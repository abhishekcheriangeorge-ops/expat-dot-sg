When both adults work, grandparents often look like the “free helper.” Singapore immigration does **not** treat caregiving as a free-form tourist hobby. r/askSingapore threads on parents visiting “for six months to help with the baby” keep colliding with Social Visit Pass limits, LTVP eligibility folklore, and the quiet truth that **grandma is not a Work Permit**.

Paid day care still lives in [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore) and [preschool](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore). This guide is the **grandparent long-stay ops** layer for EP/DP households.

## Pass products (do not invent a fourth)

| Product | Typical use | Caregiving realism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Visit Pass / visit entitlements** | Short stays; tourism framing | Extensions are discretionary — do not promise HR a six-month free nanny |
| **Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP)** | Eligible parent relationships under published ICA categories | Not automatic because you “need childcare”; check current ICA parent/LTVP rules |
| **Dependant’s Pass** | Spouse / unmarried children of EP (usual MOM frame) | Grandparents are **not** a standard DP category |

<OfficialSource>
Start from [ICA Social Visit](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/extend-stay) and [ICA LTVP](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP) / [LTVP apply](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/apply). MOM [Dependant’s Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass) frames who usually rides on an EP — grandparents generally do not. Always verify live ICA checklists before buying non-refundable flights.
</OfficialSource>

## Household ops that keep the peace

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Grandparent jet-lag + newborn nights | Rotating night shifts; do not dump both adults’ overtime on day-one arrival |
| Medical needs mid-stay | Named GP / A&E plan — [healthcare GP/hospital](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital); travel insurance that covers older adults |
| Disagreement on feeding / screen / discipline | Written house rules before landing |
| Dual-career calendar collapse when visit ends | Bridge care booked **before** departure — babysitter / preschool |
| Isolation for the visiting parent | One nationality / association rail — [nationality groups](/guides/nationality-groups-singapore) / [ANZA/AWA](/guides/anza-awa-associations) |

Dual-career households should treat grandparent cover as a **time-boxed project** inside [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore), not a permanent headcount cut.

## Caregiving vs employment myths

- Unpaid family caregiving ≠ a domestic worker Work Permit  
- Paying grandma “under the table” for full-time childcare is a compliance smell — use legal care products instead  
- Condo access cards, school pickup authorisations, and hospital next-of-kin forms need **named paperwork**, not WhatsApp folklore  
- SEN / medical complexity still needs professional rails — [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support)

## Soft-landing week for visiting grandparents

1. Rest day one — no full daycare takeover  
2. Walk the school / preschool pickup route once with a parent  
3. Save Singpass/ICA appointment screenshots and insurance PDFs in a shared folder  
4. Introduce one neighbour or association coffee so the visit is not only unpaid labour  

## What usually fails

- Promising HR “my mum is moving here for a year” on a tourist stamp  
- Cancelling the babysitter the week grandma lands  
- Ignoring the grandparent’s own medical and mobility limits  
- Leaving end-of-visit childcare as a problem for “later”  
- Treating ICA refusal as a rude surprise after the lease is signed  

Grandparent cover is immigration + care logistics. Design both before the airport run.
