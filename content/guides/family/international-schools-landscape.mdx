Singapore’s international school market is deep — and emotionally overheated. Start with constraints, not campus tours. Pair this landscape frame with [waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).

## Choose a curriculum thesis

| Pathway | Continuity strength | Typical next-country fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IB (PYP/MYP/DP) | High for globally mobile families | Many countries recognise DP |
| British / IGCSE / A-Level | Strong UK / Commonwealth pipelines | Good if UK universities are likely |
| American / AP | Strong US college counselling ecosystems | Fits US return or US unis |
| National systems (French, German, Japanese, Swiss…) | Best language/cultural continuity | Ideal if you will repatriate to that system |

Match to **likely next country**, not Instagram sports halls.

## American-pathway shortlist noise (SAS vs Stamford and peers)

r/askSingapore “is Stamford American good?” and “SAS vs OFS” threads usually become proxy wars. Useful planning distinctions:

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Curriculum continuity vs facilities brochure | Boarding, theatre, and pool do not equal college counselling fit |
| Nationality mix / seat availability | Some campuses are easier to enter for certain passports in peak grades |
| Geography | [SAS](/schools/singapore-american-school) Woodlands vs [Stamford](/schools/stamford-american) Lorong Chuan / Woodleigh vs [OFS](/schools/overseas-family-school) Pasir Ris vs [Nexus](/schools/nexus-international) Aljunied vs [XCL](/schools/xcl-world-academy) Yio Chu Kang change the entire rental belt |
| Learning support capacity | Treat SEN places like another waitlist — see [special needs](/guides/special-needs-support) |

**Housing follows school.** Tour with commute maths and year-group availability in writing before you lock a lease on the wrong side of the island. Peer rankings are entertainment; dated admissions emails are plans.

## Campus geography (housing lock-in)

| School cluster (examples) | Housing implication |
| --- | --- |
| Woodlands / north ([SAS](/schools/singapore-american-school)) | North/west living or long drives — decide before you sign a River Valley lease |
| Woodleigh / central-north ([Stamford](/schools/stamford-american)) | Lorong Chuan / Serangoon / Novena belts — do **not** confuse with Woodlands |
| Pasir Ris / east ([OFS](/schools/overseas-family-school)) | East living helps; many OFS families still live town-ish and pay the bus tax |
| Lakeside / west ([CIS](/schools/canadian-international-school)) | Jurong / Clementi / west Bukit Timah gravity |
| Bukit Batok / west ([Dulwich](/schools/dulwich-college-singapore)) | West-side housing; peak-hour CBD reverse commute is a different problem |
| Portsdown / west-central ([Tanglin Trust](/schools/tanglin-trust-school)) | Holland V / one-north / Bukit Timah patterns |
| East ([UWCSEA East](/schools/uwcsea-east) and peers) | East Coast / Marine Parade gravity |
| Dover / west ([UWCSEA Dover](/schools/uwcsea-dover), [GESS](/schools/gess)) | West-central / Dairy Farm / Clementi-adjacent choices |

School first vs house first is a false binary — but **year-group availability** often decides both. See [choosing a neighbourhood](/guides/choosing-neighbourhood-expat).

### School bus is a second rent line

r/askSingapore “SAS Woodlands vs OFS Pasir Ris vs Stamford” threads ([moving with young kids](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1s7j0lb/moving_to_singapore_with_2_young_kids_help_with/)) keep rediscovering the same maths:

| Decision | What to verify in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Live near campus | Door-to-gate at **peak** morning; playground / childcare adjacency |
| Live near office, bus the kids | Operator catchment, one-way minutes, sibling split campuses, term fee |
| Live “in between” | Often the worst of both — long adult MRT **and** long child bus |

Ask admissions for the **current bus vendor contact** and sample route times for your shortlisted condo postal codes before you sign a tenancy. “Singapore is small” is true; a 70-minute each-way kindergarten bus is also true. Bundle bus into the five-year fee stack below and into holiday-camp logistics in [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps).

## Mandarin / bilingual tracks (not MOE Chinese by default)

Families who want Mandarin exposure without an MOE pathway usually confuse three different products:

| Path | What it usually is | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bilingual / immersion tracks inside IS | e.g. [CIS](/schools/canadian-international-school) bilingual options — confirm by grade | Tracks fill early; intensity drops in upper years at some campuses |
| Optional Mandarin CCA / language slots | Common at British/American schools ([Dulwich](/schools/dulwich-college-singapore) and peers) | “Has Mandarin” ≠ immersion; ask weekly contact hours |
| Outside enrichment (Berries, online 1:1, home tutor) | Bridges non-Mandarin homes | Waitlists and package hard-sell — see [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps) |

If your next country needs real Mandarin, shortlist the **track and hours** first, then the sports hall.

## Fee anatomy (ask for the full stack)

| Line item | Why families miss it |
| --- | --- |
| Tuition | The brochure number |
| Enrolment / application | Non-refundable friction |
| Capital / building levy | Multi-year surprise |
| Bus | Geography tax — often rivals a helper month |
| Lunch / uniforms / laptops / CCAs | Recurring drip |
| Sibling discounts | Only if policy still applies |

Compare **five-year totals**, not September invoices.

## Shortlist method

1. Curriculum + language needs  
2. Year-group seat reality (written, dated)  
3. Commute from **two** plausible housing belts  
4. Pastoral / learning-support reputation for *your* child  
5. Only then facilities and cafeteria aesthetics  

## Corporate nomination and “guaranteed seats”

Some employers buy preferential access; it is not universal and it is not free. Ask HR for the **written** policy. Forum folklore about “firms buying spots” is a planning input, not your offer letter.

## Local schools as a parallel track

Some foreign children enter MOE pathways under specific rules — cheaper than international fees, culturally different, availability uncertain. Frame: [local schools for expats](/guides/local-schools-for-expats).
