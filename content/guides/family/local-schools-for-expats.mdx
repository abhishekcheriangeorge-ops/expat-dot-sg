Some foreign children study in MOE mainstream schools. Availability is **not guaranteed**, SC/PR children take priority, and international-student fees are far above citizen rates — still usually cheaper than top international campuses, which is why Reddit budget threads keep asking.

## Why families consider it

- Cost relative to SAS / TTS / UWC-tier fees  
- Local language immersion and PSLE/O-Level pathways  
- Longer-stay or PR ambitions  

## P1 for international students

MOE runs a distinct **P1 registration path for international students (IS)**. You indicate interest in a published window; outcomes arrive later (MOE communicates by email). **Admission is not guaranteed** because vacancies are limited after SC/PR demand.

If P1 fails, families often look at:

- Private / international schools  
- **AEIS** (Admissions Exercise for International Students) for later primary levels  

Popular “brand” primary schools that locals ballot for are effectively closed to foreigners in practice — plan for assignment realism, not wishlists.

<OfficialSource>
Follow [MOE P1 registration for international students](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/international-students) and the broader [studying in Singapore](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students/studying-in-singapore) hub (Student’s Pass rules when DP/LTVP do not cover schooling).
</OfficialSource>

## Fees — not “almost free”

Use MOE’s **school fees checker** for live monthly figures. Non-citizen fees rose across 2024–2026 under MOE’s published schedule. Order-of-magnitude from that press release (school fees component; verify live checker + miscellaneous):

| Level | IS (Non-ASEAN) direction of travel | IS (ASEAN) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Climbing toward ~S$1,000+/month by 2026 | Lower than non-ASEAN but still material |
| Secondary | Materially higher than primary | Still a four-figure monthly band at upper end |

Fees for international students are **GST-inclusive** in MOE’s framing; SC/PR GST treatment differs. Independent schools and specialised institutions set their own fees.

## Culture fit checks

- Class sizes and Mother Tongue expectations  
- CCA load and assessment intensity  
- Whether your child needs EAL / learning support that the assigned school can provide  
- Housing lock-in near the offered school (balloting geography matters less for IS assignment, but commute still does)

## Decision rule

| Household | Lean local MOE | Lean international |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mid package, long horizon, flexible on school brand | Yes — try P1/AEIS with a private backup | Backup only |
| Short assignment, curriculum continuity, waitlist capital | Unlikely | Primary path |
| Special educational needs | Case-by-case with professionals | Often clearer support pathways |
