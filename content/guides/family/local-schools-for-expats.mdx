r/askSingapore keeps asking the same budget question: **can Dependant Pass kids do government school?** Yes — sometimes. Availability is **not guaranteed**, SC/PR children take priority, MOE **assigns** the school, and international-student fees are far above citizen rates (still usually cheaper than SAS / TTS / UWC-tier). Treat local MOE as a **pathway with a private backup**, not a free public school.

## Why families consider it

- Cost relative to premium international campuses  
- Local language immersion and PSLE / O-Level pathways  
- Longer-stay or PR ambitions  
- Sibling logistics when one child already sits in the MOE system  

## P1 for international students (2026 cycle)

MOE runs a distinct **P1 registration path for international students (IS)** — not the citizen Phase 1–3 ballot locals obsess over.

| Step | What MOE publishes (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Indicate interest | Online form + documents in a short May window (2026 example: **19–25 May 2026**, 9am–4.30pm; late submissions are not considered) |
| Outcome | Email by **end October** whether a place is offered |
| If offered | Register at the **designated** school named in the email |
| If not offered | Private / international school, or [AEIS](/guides/aeis-saeis-international-students) for **Primary 2** the following cycle |

Age gate: child aged **6 to 6+** as of 1 January in the year of admission for this P1 IS path. Older children use AEIS / S-AEIS — see MOE’s [admissions hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students/admission).

Follow [MOE P1 registration for international students](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/international-students) and the [studying in Singapore](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students/studying-in-singapore) hub (Student’s Pass rules when DP/LTVP do not cover schooling).
</OfficialSource>

## Assignment realism — not school choice

Forum heat on [local primary for foreign kids](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1houq07/local_primary_schools_for_foreign_kids/) and [DP + government education](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1jfc9e5/dependent_pass_and_government_education/) is blunt:

- Popular “brand” primaries locals ballot for are effectively **closed** to foreigners after SC/PR demand  
- MOE places where **vacancies** exist; residential area is used **where possible**, not as a guarantee of a 1 km walk  
- Families report **cross-island commutes**, taxi carpools, and mid-posting moves when the assigned school sits outside bus catchment  

Passing later AEIS still does **not** mean you pick Raffles-adjacent branding — same vacancy logic.

| Household assumption | Reality check |
| --- | --- |
| “We’ll get the school next to our condo” | Plan for assignment elsewhere + transport cost |
| “EP tax means citizen fees” | Fees follow the **child’s** citizenship / residency, not the parent’s tax status |
| “Native English skips Cambridge gates” | AEIS primary still has a **CEQ** score gate — see [AEIS guide](/guides/aeis-saeis-international-students) |

## Fees — not “almost free”

Use MOE’s **school fees checker** for live monthly figures. Non-citizen fees rose across 2024–2026 under MOE’s published schedule. Order-of-magnitude from that press release (school fees component; verify live checker + miscellaneous):

| Level | IS (Non-ASEAN) direction of travel | IS (ASEAN) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Climbing toward ~S$1,000+/month by 2026 | Lower than non-ASEAN but still material |
| Secondary | Materially higher than primary | Still a four-figure monthly band at upper end |

Fees for international students are **GST-inclusive** in MOE’s framing; SC/PR GST is subsidised. Independent schools and SPED set their own fees. MOE also notes **Child Development Account (Baby Bonus) cannot be used** for mainstream GIRO school-fee payment — do not budget CDA as the school cheque.

Check [MOE school fees](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/fees) and the [2024–2026 non-citizen fee revision](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20231018-revised-school-fees-for-non-citizens-in-government-and-government-aided-schools-for-2024-to-2026).
</OfficialSource>

## Culture fit checks

- Class sizes (~30 early primary; larger later) and assessment intensity  
- **Mother Tongue** expectations — exemptions exist for some approved foreign languages / cases, but plan tutoring if your child has no Chinese / Malay / Tamil base ([MTL hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/curriculum/mother-tongue-languages))  
- CCA load vs what international schools call “optional”  
- Whether the assigned school can staff EAL / learning support your child needs — SEN households should read [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support) before resigning overseas  
- Housing lock-in near the **offered** school, not the shortlist fantasy  

## Decision rule

| Household | Lean local MOE | Lean international |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mid package, long horizon, flexible on school brand | Yes — try P1/AEIS with a private backup | Backup only |
| Short assignment, curriculum continuity, waitlist capital | Unlikely | Primary path |
| Special educational needs | Rarely — capacity + foreigner SPED limits | Often clearer support pathways |
| Need a named campus before signing lease | Unlikely until outcome email | Primary path |

Bridge options while waiting: mid-fee internationals such as [Invictus](/entities/invictus-international), or nationality-system schools like [Lycée Français](/entities/lycee-francais) when curriculum continuity matters more than MOE branding.
