Residential camp week is not “school handles it”. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering that **consent deadlines, medication forms, and 05:30 coach call times** collide with client decks harder than Sports Day.

Sports day ops: [sports day / carnival](/guides/international-school-sports-day-carnival-ops-dual-career-singapore). Holiday camps: [kids activities & camps](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps). Allergy / EpiPen reality: [kids allergy](/guides/kids-allergy-epipen-singapore). This guide is **residential / overnight trip ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Consent / payment deadline calendar** | Portal closes while you are in a board meeting |
| **Medication + allergy packet** | Teachers need written, not WhatsApp folklore |
| **Packing blackout night** | Same-day Decathlon fails in the driveway |
| **Named emergency contact stack** | Dual muted Slack is not an on-call roster |
| **Pickup / early-return owner** | Someone must own the 22:00 fever call |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Pull consent, payment, packing, and medical forms the day the letter drops — not the night before departure.  
2. Put a hard calendar block for the **named emergency contact** ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. Build the medication / allergy packet early; print labels; do not invent doses at 05:00.  
4. Treat packing night as a blackout task — same energy as [sports day](/guides/international-school-sports-day-carnival-ops-dual-career-singapore).  
5. If camp week collides with helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
6. If the child is pulled home sick, switch to the sick matrix ([sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore)) — camp staff are not your weekday coverage plan.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Reading only the trip dates, missing consent close | Calendar the portal deadline |
| Both parents “on call” meaning neither | One named primary + one backup |
| Handing meds in an unlabelled ziplock | Written packet + labelled doses |
| Buying gear the morning of departure | Packing blackout the night before |
| Stacking private holiday camp the same week | Cap one peak overnight |

## Questions to close this week

1. When does consent / payment close?  
2. Who is the named emergency contact?  
3. Is the medication / allergy packet complete?  
4. Who owns early-return pickup if fever hits overnight?
