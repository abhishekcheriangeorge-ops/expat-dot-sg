Report-card week is not PTC week. Dual-career parents on r/askSingapore keep opening the portal at 22:40, arguing about who books the follow-up slot, and discovering the “optional” progress meeting is the only window that explains a grade cliff.

PTC ops: [parent-teacher conference](/guides/parent-teacher-conference-dual-career-singapore). Portal hygiene: [lost property / portal](/guides/international-school-lost-property-admin-portal-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **report card / progress meeting ops**.

## Ops checklist

| When | Move |
| --- | --- |
| T−10 | Note publish date + meeting booking open time on both calendars |
| T−3 | Decide which adult reads first; schedule a 20-minute joint debrief |
| Publish day | Screenshot key comments into the shared channel |
| Booking window | One named booker; protect the slot against client calls |
| After | One follow-up ask max — tutoring FOMO later |

<OfficialSource>
Reporting calendars and meeting formats are school-specific ([MOE international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools); [Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub)). Do not invent “all IS campuses publish Friday 16:00” folklore.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career traps

1. **Both parents skip the debrief** — kids hear two conflicting speeches.  
2. **Booking at the last minute** — progress slots vanish like PTC.  
3. **Weaponising one subject comment** — ask one clarifying question first.  
4. **Ignoring learning-support notes** — route to [special needs](/guides/special-needs-support) / school LS paths, not only enrichment shopping.  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

Exam-week load still applies near finals: [exam-week coverage](/guides/exam-week-coverage-dual-career-singapore). Pathway context for older teens: [IB vs A-Level](/guides/secondary-ib-alevel-pathways-singapore).
