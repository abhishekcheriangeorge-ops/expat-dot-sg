A fever day is a coverage matrix. **An overnight admission** is a different product: one adult in the ward, one adult running the rest of the household, and an employer calendar that does not auto-rewrite itself. r/askSingapore dual-career parents keep learning this at 2am in KK or a private paediatric ward.

Outpatient fever ops: [sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Triage rails: [GP/hospital](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital). This guide is **hospitalisation coverage**.

## Roles that must be named before admission

| Role | Job | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ward parent** | Consent forms, nurse updates, overnight | Both parents “share” and neither sleeps |
| **Home parent** | Sibling school runs, meals, helper oversight | Assumes WFH equals availability |
| **Comms lead** | Employer + school updates (one channel) | Duplicate WhatsApps; missed MC |
| **Logistics lead** | Clothes, chargers, food, Grab | Forgotten passports / FIN cards |

Role clarity still sits in [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

<OfficialSource>
System orientation: [MOH healthcare system](https://www.moh.gov.sg/home/our-healthcare-system). Ward visitor and rooming-in rules are **hospital-specific** — ask admitting staff; do not rely on a forum floor plan.
</OfficialSource>

## First 12 hours checklist

1. Confirm panel / guarantee letter vs cash ([expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat)).  
2. Assign ward vs home parent in writing (text is fine).  
3. Activate sibling coverage — [babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore) / [helper](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw).  
4. Notify school/preschool with expected absence window.  
5. Pack: FIN/passport copies, vaccination book, chargers, change of clothes, snacks that pass ward rules.

## Dual-career employer ops

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Use formal leave tools, not only Slack sympathy | Coverage needs calendar rights |
| Cap “I’ll just take calls from the ward” | Clinical updates need attention |
| Pre-brief a deputy at work | Admissions rarely respect sprint demos |
| Keep one daily update time to both employers | Noise reduction |

## What this is not

- Not clinical advice  
- Not a substitute for emergency 995 pathways ([emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies))  
- Not outpatient fever-day planning — use the sick-child matrix first
