Concert week is not “just an evening show”. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering that **call times, costume blackouts, and volunteer usher slots** collide with client decks harder than Sports Day ever did.

Speech / prize-giving: [speech day ops](/guides/international-school-speech-day-prize-giving-ops-dual-career-singapore). Photo day: [photo day ops](/guides/international-school-photo-day-ops-dual-career-singapore). Adult music rails: [music & arts communities](/guides/music-arts-communities-singapore). This guide is **concert / recital ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Call-time calendar block** | Arrival is earlier than the programme PDF |
| **Costume / instrument blackout** | Same-day Shopee fails in the car park |
| **Named adult in the hall** | Dual muted Slack is not a seat |
| **Hard ticket / donation cap** | Programme ads and raffles stack quietly |
| **Recording / photo rule note** | Phone-up culture vs school policy |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Pull call time, gate, dress code, and recording policy from the portal the day the letter drops — not the afternoon of the show.  
2. Put a hard calendar block for the **named adult in the hall** ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. Set a wallet / donation cap before programme ads and flower stalls open.  
4. Treat costume fittings and instrument checks as blackout tasks — same energy as [photo day](/guides/international-school-photo-day-ops-dual-career-singapore).  
5. If the concert lands on a helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
6. After the show, decide whether external studios stay as belonging rails ([youth performing arts](/guides/youth-performing-arts-communities-singapore)) or get capped for the term ([kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Reading only the 19:00 start, missing 17:45 call | Calendar the call time |
| Both parents “hoping to sneak in for the solo” | One named hall adult; one backup |
| Unlimited PayNow for programmes / flowers | Hard wallet cap |
| Live-streaming against school policy | Follow the written recording rule |
| Stacking private recital + school concert same week | Cap one peak night |

## Questions to close this week

1. What is the exact call time and gate?  
2. Which adult owns the hall seat?  
3. What is the hard wallet cap?  
4. Are phones allowed up for the whole programme?
