Speech day is not sports day and not a casual assembly. Dual-career parents on r/askSingapore keep missing prize callouts because the calendar invite looked optional, then fighting about who leaves the client call.

Sports day: [sports-day ops](/guides/international-school-sports-day-carnival-ops-dual-career-singapore). Photo day: [photo ops](/guides/international-school-photo-day-ops-dual-career-singapore). Report cards: [report-card ops](/guides/international-school-report-card-progress-meeting-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **speech day / prize-giving ops**.

## Ops checklist

| When | Move |
| --- | --- |
| T−14 | Confirm date, dress code, ticket/RSVP rules |
| T−7 | Put a hard work blackout on **one** named adult |
| T−2 | Iron / label formal kit; plan sibling care |
| Day | Arrive early for seating; silence phones; one photographer only |
| After | Short celebrate — then portal / report follow-ups later |

<OfficialSource>
Assembly and prize-giving formats are school-specific ([MOE international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools); [Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub)). Do not invent “all awards are emailed anyway” folklore.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career traps

1. **Both parents RSVP maybe** — one named attendee beats two flaky ones.  
2. **Sibling parked without a plan** — helper rest days collide ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
3. **Live-streaming the whole hall** — follow school photo rules.  
4. **Turning prizes into a grade fight in the car park** — debrief later, once.  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).
