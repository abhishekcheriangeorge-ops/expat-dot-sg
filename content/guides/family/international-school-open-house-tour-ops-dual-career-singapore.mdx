Open house is not a casual Saturday stroll. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering that **slot booking clocks, tour scripts, and sibling coverage** collide with client decks — then paying deposits on vibe instead of waitlist maths.

Waitlist realism: [school waitlists](/guides/school-waitlist-realism). Bus logistics: [IS bus & pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore). Mid-year moves: [midyear transfer](/guides/international-school-midyear-transfer-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **open-house / campus-tour ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Slot booking calendar** | Portals close while you are in a board meeting |
| **Two-adult question script** | LS, bus, fees, and pastoral answers need owners |
| **Sibling coverage plan** | Tours are not free childcare |
| **Deposit / fee realism note** | Brochure tuition ≠ five-year stack |
| **Post-tour debrief block** | Dual muted Slack is not a decision |

School-system orientation: [MOE international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools) and [Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub). Fees, waits, and tour formats are **school-specific** — trust admissions PDFs over forum folklore.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career playbook

1. Book the slot the day registration opens; put a hard calendar block for both adults or one adult + named coverage for siblings ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
2. Split the question script: one owns academics/LS; one owns bus/fees/pastoral.  
3. Pre-write deposit and fee-stack limits before the sales corridor.  
4. If the tour lands on a helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
5. If a child is sick on tour day, switch to the sick matrix ([sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore)) — do not drag a fever into a showroom.  
6. Debrief within 24 hours; update the waitlist tracker ([school waitlists](/guides/school-waitlist-realism)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| “We will just drop by” without a slot | Book; walk-ins fail at peak season |
| Both parents on phones during the LS talk | Named question owners |
| Deposit on vibes before bus/fee maths | Written fee-stack note |
| Bringing three siblings with no coverage plan | Named sibling adult |
| Skipping debrief until “next week” | 24-hour decision block |

## Questions to close this week

1. When does the portal slot close?  
2. Who owns the question script halves?  
3. What is the hard deposit / fee-stack cap?  
4. Who covers siblings during the tour?
