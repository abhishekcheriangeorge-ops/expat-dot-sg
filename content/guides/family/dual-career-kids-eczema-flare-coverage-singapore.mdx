Heat, humidity, and uniform weeks stack fast. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep calling every itch “just dry skin” at 06:15 — then learning that **named coverage owners and written swim/PE holds** beat dual muted Slack.

Ordinary sick matrix: [sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Allergy rails: [kids allergy / EpiPen](/guides/kids-allergy-epipen-singapore). Breathing flares: [asthma-flare coverage](/guides/dual-career-kids-asthma-flare-coverage-singapore). Crusty sores: [impetigo exclusion](/guides/dual-career-kids-impetigo-school-exclusion-singapore). This guide is **eczema-flare coverage ops**.

## Coverage products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named daytime adult** | Moisturiser timing and infection watch are not dual “later” |
| **Home + school cream kit** | Device/location beats memory between meetings |
| **Infection watch card** | Know when weepy sores need a GP same day |
| **PE / swim / heat hold** | Chlorine and midday sun are flare multipliers |
| **Laundry / uniform reset** | Shared towels and scratchy labels cascade siblings |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Put the written flare plan + cream locations in the shared family channel.  
2. Name today’s coverage owner before the first stand-up ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. Book GP / paediatric review early if infection signs appear ([finding a paediatrician](/guides/finding-pediatrician-singapore)).  
4. Pause swim and heat-heavy PE until the written hold lifts ([kids swimming & water safety](/guides/kids-swimming-water-safety-singapore)).  
5. Reset towels, sheets, and uniform labels the same day.  
6. If sores crust and school excludes, switch to [impetigo exclusion](/guides/dual-career-kids-impetigo-school-exclusion-singapore) rules.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Both parents “monitoring between Zooms” | One coverage owner |
| Empty moisturiser tube in the school bag | Kit check the night before |
| Sending a weeping flare to swim “for the social” | Written water hold |
| Treating every itch as “just AC” | Follow the clinician step-up plan |
| Sharing towels across siblings | Laundry reset day one |
