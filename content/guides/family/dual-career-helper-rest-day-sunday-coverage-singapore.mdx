Sunday without a helper is not a “light WFH day.” Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep stacking enrichment, grocery runs, and Slack standups onto the same rest day — then discovering nobody owns lunch.

PH coverage: [PH school-closed](/guides/dual-career-public-holiday-school-closed-coverage-singapore). Holidays: [school-holiday coverage](/guides/school-holiday-coverage-dual-career-singapore). Fever: [sick-child](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). This guide is **helper rest-day / Sunday coverage**.

## Rest-day products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named daytime adult** | One owner per rest-day block — not dual muted Slack |
| **Enrichment cap** | One paid slot max; rest days fail on three venues |
| **Meal plan** | Pre-cook or hawker — do not invent a feast mid-Zoom |
| **Relief backup list** | Agency relief is not same-morning magic |
| **Rest-day pay clarity** | If you ask the helper to work, follow MOM rules — no handshake folklore |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Put the helper rest day on the **shared calendar** every week.  
2. Decide coverage owner by Friday night — not Sunday 08:00.  
3. Cap enrichment; protect one adult deep-work block and one family block.  
4. Keep a short relief / sitter list current ([sitters](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore)).  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Both parents “half working” | One coverage owner |
| Stacking PH + Sunday without rewriting the map | Treat stacks as a different product |
| Last-minute agency relief | Book early or accept a cancelled brunch |
| Treating rest day as free childcare cancel | Rest day is a right — plan around it |
