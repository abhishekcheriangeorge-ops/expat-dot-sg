When P1 registration fails — or your child is already past Primary 1 — Reddit’s fallback chant is “just do AEIS.” That is half-right. AEIS is a real MOE pathway into mainstream schools, but **passing does not mean brand-school placement**, and MOE will **not** issue you an entry permit just to sit the test.

Local-school logistics still need a pickup product and a building hello — [bus/pickup ops](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore) and [condo parent networks](/guides/condo-block-parent-networks-belonging-singapore).

## What AEIS is (and is not)

| | **AEIS** | **S-AEIS** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Admissions Exercise for International Students | Supplementary AEIS |
| Typical levels | Primary **2–5**, Secondary **1–3** | Primary **2–4**, Secondary **1–2** |
| Usual cadence | Apply ~July; tests ~Sep; start **January** next year | Apply ~January; tests ~Feb/Mar; start **Apr/May** same year |
| Guarantee | None — performance + vacancies | None — often fewer seats |

### Live calendar note (verify before you book flights)

| Cycle | Status at last review (2026-09-15) |
| --- | --- |
| **2026 AEIS** | Applications **closed**; tests scheduled in Singapore **1–3 Sep 2026** (MOE AEIS overview) — outcomes still drive **Jan 2027** starts |
| **2027 S-AEIS** | Applications expected **early January 2027**; tests tentatively **end Feb / early Mar 2027**; start **Apr/May 2027** |

If you miss AEIS, S-AEIS is the mid-year catch — with **fewer seats**. Do not treat “we’ll just sit S-AEIS” as a guaranteed backup while cancelling an international-school hold.

P1 for international students is a **separate** registration path — see [local schools for expats](/guides/local-schools-for-expats). AEIS is the mid-stream exam route for ages **7+** as of 1 January in the admission year ([MOE admissions hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students/admission)).

## Eligibility gates parents underestimate

1. **Age band** — MOE’s live checker maps birth date → permitted test / possible placement level. Sitting the “wrong” paper wastes a cycle.  
2. **Primary applicants — Cambridge English Qualifications (CEQ)** — you must hit the published minimum score **before** you submit the AEIS/S-AEIS application. Native English is **not** a waiver; forum threads about “my American kid shouldn’t need B1” collide with MOE’s published gate.  
3. **You must already be able to enter Singapore** — SEAB/MOE state clearly that the entry proof is **not** an immigration pass, and MOE will not arrange entry solely for the test.  
4. **Former MOE students** with fee arrears must clear them before any readmission assessment.

## What the test actually checks

| Track | Content framing (MOE) |
| --- | --- |
| Primary | Mathematics pitched at the **preceding** level (aiming for P3 → know P2 content) |
| Secondary | **English + Mathematics** pitched at the preceding level |

This is not an IQ flex. Children from non-Singapore curricula often fail on **local math method and English exam style**, not raw smarts. Budget months of syllabus familiarisation — not a weekend past-paper binge. Tutoring centres exist; treat them as syllabus translators, not miracle seats.

## Placement realism (the Reddit hard truth)

- Admission is **subject to vacancies**. MOE places where seats exist, using your declared residential area **where possible**.  
- Popular “brand” schools that locals ballot for are rarely the AEIS outcome. Plan for **commute + culture shock + Mother Tongue**, not alumni LinkedIn.  
- International-student **school fees** still apply after placement — check MOE’s fee checker; they are far below top international campuses but **not** citizen rates ([fee context](/guides/local-schools-for-expats)).  
- Student’s Pass / Dependant’s Pass schooling rules still apply — see [Student’s Pass](/guides/student-pass-singapore) if the child’s current pass does not cover mainstream study.  
- SEN profiles that need structured pathway support are usually a poor fit for vacancy-assigned mainstream — see [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support).

## Decision rule for relocating families

| Situation | Lean AEIS / S-AEIS | Lean international school |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long horizon, cost-sensitive, flexible on school brand | Primary path with private backup | Backup only |
| Arrive mid-year with no January start flexibility | Consider **S-AEIS** timing | Often cleaner |
| Short assignment / IB continuity / SEN needs | Unlikely | Primary path — [waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism) |
| Child already struggling in English | Delay a cycle; fix CEQ + language first | Bridge year ([Invictus](/entities/invictus-international) etc.) |

Keep a paid international-school hold until AEIS **outcome** lands — “we’ll cancel after the test” is how families end up with neither seat.

**DSA-Sec note for international-school / home-schooled kids:** Direct School Admission offers to foreign students are typically **conditional** until AEIS requirements are met — talent shortlisting is not a substitute for the admissions exercise. Confirm the live MOE DSA and AEIS pages for your child’s pathway.
