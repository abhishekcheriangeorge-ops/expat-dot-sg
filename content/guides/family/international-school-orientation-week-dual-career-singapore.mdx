Orientation week is not “a light first day.” Dual-career parents on r/askSingapore keep double-booking uniform fittings against board meetings, missing bus trials, and discovering the portal password is still on a sticky note in another country.

Bus ops: [bus/pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore). Portal hygiene: [lost property / portal](/guides/international-school-lost-property-admin-portal-dual-career-singapore). Transitions: [primary transition](/guides/primary-school-transition-expat-kids-singapore). This guide is **orientation week ops**.

## Week-one checklist

| When | Move |
| --- | --- |
| T−14 | Confirm orientation blocks; put them on **both** work calendars |
| T−7 | Uniforms, PE kit, labelled bottles; portal login tested |
| T−2 | Bus trial or walking route dry-run; backup Grab pin saved |
| Day 1–3 | One named drop-off adult; shorter work blocks |
| Day 5 | Lost-property sweep + permission-slip inbox zero |

<OfficialSource>
Orientation schedules and buddy programmes are school-specific ([MOE international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools); [Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub)). Do not invent “all IS campuses do half-days all week” folklore.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career traps

1. **Both parents “might drop by”** — one named attendee per block.  
2. **Portal setup left to the kid** — verify submissions yourself.  
3. **Skipping the bus trial** — first rainy Monday is a bad first lesson.  
4. **Stacking PTA coffee + client dinner on day two** — protect sleep.  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

Waitlist realism still applies if you are mid-transfer: [school waitlists](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).
