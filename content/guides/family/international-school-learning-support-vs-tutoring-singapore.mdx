When grades wobble, r/askSingapore defaults to **“get a tutor.”** International-school learning support is a different product: scheduled pull-outs, classroom strategies, and sometimes an IEP-like plan — not unlimited 1:1 homework rescue. Dual-career households burn cash when they buy both without a written goal.

School shortlists still start with the [landscape](/guides/international-schools-landscape). Deep SEN pathways: [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support). This guide is the **support vs tutoring cash decision**.

## Two products, two invoices

| Product | What you are buying | Typical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-school learning support** | Screening, strategies, small-group / pull-out, teacher collab | Hours capped; waitlists; not a private tutor on payroll |
| **Private tutoring** | 1:1 content rescue, exam technique, confidence | Quality variance; can mask missing school supports |
| **External ed psych / ST/OT** | Diagnosis and therapy | Cash for most EP families; school may or may not act on reports |
| **Enrichment centres** | Subject drills (e.g. maths chains) | Easy to confuse with learning support |

<OfficialSource>
System SEN orientation: [MOE Special educational needs](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs). International-school support policies are **school-specific contracts** — ask for the written learning-support handbook, not a tour anecdote ([MOE international schools overview](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools)).
</OfficialSource>

## Decision table for dual-career parents

| Signal | Prefer school support first | Prefer / add private tutoring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher flags processing / attention in class | Yes — request observation notes in writing | Tutor alone rarely fixes classroom access |
| Content gaps after a curriculum switch | After placement conversation | Short, goal-dated tutoring block |
| Exam technique only (IB/IGCSE year) | Ask for school revision clinics | Targeted subject tutor with an end date |
| Formal diagnosis path | School + qualified clinician | Tutor is not a diagnostician — see ADHD meds continuity guide if relevant |

ADHD medication continuity: [kids ADHD medication](/guides/kids-adhd-medication-singapore). Language enrichment is yet another invoice — [Mandarin enrichment](/guides/mandarin-enrichment-expat-kids-singapore).

## Questions that prevent invoice regret

1. How many **learning-support hours per week** are included in fees?  
2. What triggers an IEP-like plan — and who attends the meeting?  
3. Will the school **read and act** on an external ed-psych report?  
4. What is the tutoring goal in one sentence, and the **stop date**?  
5. Who owns progress review — form tutor, learning-support lead, or parent?

## Cash hygiene

- One tutor per subject maximum unless there is a written exception.  
- No open-ended “until grades improve” retainers.  
- Enrichment CCA load still competes for sleep — [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps).  
- Dual-career households: name which adult owns the weekly progress call ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).

Dual-career note: once the LS referral is filed, switch from “buy another tutor” panic to **waitlist ops** — named chase owner, interim goal cap, review blackout ([LS waitlist ops](/guides/international-schools-landscape)).
