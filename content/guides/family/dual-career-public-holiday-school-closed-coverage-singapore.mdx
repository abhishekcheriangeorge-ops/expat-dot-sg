Public holidays close many schools and leave **both parents still on Slack**. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering that PH weeks are not school-holiday camps and not sick-day matrices — they are calendar collisions with helper rest days and last-minute enrichment FOMO.

School holidays: [holiday coverage](/guides/school-holiday-coverage-dual-career-singapore). Fever: [sick-child](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Haze closures: [haze PSI](/guides/dual-career-haze-psi-school-closure-coverage-singapore). This guide is **PH school-closed coverage**.

## PH products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named daytime adult** | One owner per PH block — not dual “half WFH” folklore |
| **Helper rest-day map** | Many helpers take PH + Sunday; stack those early |
| **Camp / playdate cap** | One paid slot beats three WhatsApp maybes |
| **Travel realism** | Batam / JB day trips fail when passport / pass cards are mid-renewal |
| **Work blackout note** | Put “school closed — coverage” on both calendars a week out |

<OfficialSource>
Singapore public holidays: [MOM public holidays](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/public-holidays). School calendars and PH observance for international schools are school-specific ([MOE Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub); [international schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools)) — do not assume MOE mainstream PH lists equal your campus.
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career playbook

1. Mark the next three PHs on the shared family calendar **this weekend**.  
2. Decide coverage owner before the week before — not the night before.  
3. Shift helper rest days off PH mornings when the contract allows.  
4. Decline the third enrichment FOMO invite; protect sleep.  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Backup: [sitters](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Treating PH like a free work-from-home deep day | Kids still need a named adult |
| Stacking helper PH + Sunday without a plan | Rewrite the rest-day map |
| Booking overseas day trips late | Check passports / entry rules first |
| Both parents taking “just a few calls” | One coverage owner; one work owner |
