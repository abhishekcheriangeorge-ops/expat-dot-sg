Sports day is one calendar hit. **Weekday fixtures** are the recurring product — early kickoffs, kit bags, away buses, and dual EP blackouts that stack all term. r/askSingapore dual-career threads keep treating “just one away game” like optional networking until the bus leaves at 13:40.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Kickoff / bus-call calendar block** | Portals punish late kit and late adults |
| **Named kit-bag owner** | Boots, bottles, and medical forms are not dual “later” |
| **Away-bus / pickup rewrite** | Contracted school buses rarely match fixture ends |
| **Sibling coverage plan** | Fixtures are not free childcare |
| **Haze / rain fork** | Outdoor calls move; calendars must move with them |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Put the fixture and bus-call times on the shared calendar the day the portal posts them ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
2. Name one kit-bag / medical-form owner the night before.  
3. Rewrite pickup if the fixture ends after the contracted bus ([IS bus & pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore)).  
4. Pre-write sibling coverage before you leave the office.  
5. If the fixture lands on helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
6. Haze / PSI weeks: follow the school outdoor call, not WhatsApp optimism ([haze PSI coverage](/guides/dual-career-haze-psi-school-closure-coverage-singapore)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| “Someone will Grab them” without a named adult | Named pickup owner |
| Both parents assuming the other packed boots | Kit checklist night before |
| Ignoring away-bus return clocks | Calendar the real end time |
| Treating every fixture as mandatory adult attendance | Cap midweek presence; rotate |
| Sideline coaching other people’s kids | Belonging ≠ coaching |

## Questions to close this week

1. When is bus call vs kickoff?  
2. Who owns the kit bag and medical form?  
3. Who covers siblings / late pickup?  
4. What is the haze / rain fork in writing?
