CAS / service evening is not optional networking with free pizza. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep treating reflection nights like casual drop-ins — then discovering **slot clocks, evidence logs, and sibling coverage** collide with board packs.

Curriculum nights: [curriculum-evening ops](/guides/international-school-curriculum-evening-ops-dual-career-singapore). Language nights: [language-evening ops](/guides/international-school-language-evening-ops-dual-career-singapore). Volunteer load: [PTA volunteer ops](/guides/international-school-pta-volunteer-ops-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **CAS / service-evening ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Slot / room calendar block** | Portals punish late arrivals |
| **Evidence / reflection owner** | Hours without artefacts fail audits |
| **Sibling coverage plan** | Evenings are not free childcare |
| **Transport rewrite** | Late ends collide with contracted buses |
| **Follow-up email owner** | Dual muted Slack loses the action list |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Book the slot the day registration opens; calendar a hard block for the named adult(s) ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
2. Name who owns the evidence / reflection pack before the evening.  
3. Pre-write sibling coverage or sitter overflow before you leave the office.  
4. If the evening lands on helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
5. Check bus / pickup contracts for late returns ([IS bus & pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore)).  
6. Keep youth volunteering belonging separate from CAS ops paperwork ([youth volunteering](/guides/youth-volunteering-communities-singapore)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| “We will just drop by” without a slot | Book; walk-ins fail at peak season |
| Hours without reflections | Named evidence owner |
| No sibling plan | Named coverage adult |
| Losing the follow-up list in WhatsApp | One email owner within 24 hours |
| Treating CAS night as unlimited PTA recruitment | Cap the ask |

## Questions to close this week

1. When does the portal slot close?  
2. Who owns the evidence / reflection pack?  
3. Who covers siblings?  
4. Who sends the follow-up email?
