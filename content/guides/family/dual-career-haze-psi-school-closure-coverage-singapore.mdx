Haze days are not sick days and not holiday camps. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep discovering that a 10:00 PSI jump can cancel outdoor PE, pull bus routes early, or strand a preschooler while both parents are mid-meeting.

Climate kit: [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness). Ordinary fever: [sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore). Outbreak letters: [HFMD coverage](/guides/dual-career-hfmd-school-outbreak-coverage-singapore). This guide is **PSI / school-ops coverage**.

## Haze-day products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Named daytime adult** | School messages arrive mid-morning — someone must own the phone |
| **Indoor air plan** | Working N95s for travel; home aircon / purifier already serviced |
| **Bus / pickup fork** | Early dismissal without a parent at the gate fails families |
| **Sibling split plan** | One school closes outdoor CCA; another stays open — do not assume symmetry |
| **Helper rest-day shift** | Haze weeks are the wrong weeks for Sunday-only backup |

<OfficialSource>
Live PSI and haze advisories: [NEA](https://www.nea.gov.sg/). School action (indoor PE, dismissal, closure) is **school-specific** — follow the portal / SMS, not WhatsApp folklore ([MOE Parent Hub](https://www.moe.gov.sg/parent-hub); [international schools orientation](https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-schools)).
</OfficialSource>

## Dual-career playbook

1. Put NEA / school alert apps on **both** phones ([apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore)).  
2. Pre-write the “who leaves work first” rule before PSI hits 100.  
3. Keep a labelled haze bag: masks, spare uniform, water, inhaler if prescribed.  
4. Do not invent outdoor sports-day or carnival plans on orange/red days.  
5. Role map: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore). Backup adults: [sitters](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for a government “nationwide school holiday” | Most international schools decide independently |
| Sending kids out “for fresh air” at lunch | Follow the school’s outdoor ban |
| Both parents staying on mute Slack | One named coverage owner per day |
| Ignoring aircon failures mid-haze | Service before the season ([aircon renewals](/guides/aircon-pest-renewals)) |

Bus chaos still applies: [IS bus/pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore).
