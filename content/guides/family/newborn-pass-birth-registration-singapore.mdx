Giving birth in Singapore does **not** make the baby Singaporean unless citizenship rules through a parent apply. For EP/S Pass households the Reddit panic ([newborn DP timing](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/17hkidm/dependent_pass/), [Malaysian newborn docs](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1b0icqt/questions_regarding_malaysian_newborn_baby/)) is the **42-day / 6-week clock**: register the birth, get a passport, and have the **employer** lodge a Dependant’s Pass before the Special Pass runs out.

## Clock you actually have

| Step | Official timing | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register birth | ICA legal window within **42 days**; MOM’s newborn-DP page asks you to register within **14 calendar days** so the passport/DP sprint starts early | [LifeSG](https://www.life.gov.sg/services-tools/birth-registration) |
| ICA Special Pass (stay) | **42 days** from date of birth (advisory note after registration) | Issued with birth registration outcome |
| Employer lodges DP | Within **6 weeks** of birth (MOM newborn path); most outcomes ~**3 weeks** online | myMOM Portal |
| Passport ready | Before DP **issuance** (not always before application) | Your embassy / high commission |
| Digital birth cert download | Within **90 days** of notification | ICA e-Service |

Missing the birth-registration deadline is an offence under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act. Overstaying beyond the Special Pass without a proper immigration pass is an Immigration Act offence.

## Birth registration — what changes for foreigners

- **All** parents, any immigration or marital status, register online via LifeSG (app or website).  
- SC / PR / long-term FIN holders use **Singpass**; others use the LifeSG website path.  
- Fee published at last review: **S$18** (card / PayNow).  
- Physical certificates are **not** issued for births registered from 29 May 2022 — save the digital PDF immediately.  
- Hospital counters no longer run the registration for you; treat discharge as the start of **your** admin sprint.

## Passport before pass issuance

MOM will not **issue** the Dependant’s Pass until the baby’s **passport** is ready. Embassy photo rules (eyes open, plain background) are the silent bottleneck parents underestimate — especially for sleepy newborns. If the passport is still processing when you lodge the DP, upload **proof of passport application** (e.g. embassy letter) and plan a Special Pass extension if the ICA advisory note is within **7 days** of expiry.

| Special Pass situation | What MOM publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Holding ICA advisory note; passport late | Request Special Pass extension only when the note is within **7 days** of expiry |
| Holding a **MOM-issued** newborn Special Pass; DP pending | Auto-extends **28 days**; SMS one day before expiry |
| DP **rejected** | Baby must leave before Special Pass expiry — MOM will **not** extend to “wait and see” |
| Not applying for DP | Ask ICA about a visit pass / Special Pass extension path |

Forum notes also mention a temporary **T-series** identification on the Singapore birth certificate for foreign babies, then a **G-series** FIN once the Dependant’s Pass card issues — useful when clinics ask which number to use for records.

## Salary gate for the DP

The main EP/S Pass holder must still meet MOM’s family-pass salary rules for a Dependant’s Pass. Combined household income myths on Reddit are wrong: it is the **sponsoring pass holder’s** fixed monthly salary thresholds that matter. If a promotion that clears the floor lands mid-pregnancy, confirm with HR **when** the new salary is on the MOM record before assuming the newborn DP is automatic.

## Practical week-by-week sequence

1. **Week 0** — Confirm employer will lodge newborn DP; ask HR for the document checklist in writing.  
2. **Days 1–7** — LifeSG registration; download digital birth certificate when notified.  
3. **Days 1–14** — Book embassy passport appointment; take the hard photo.  
4. **As soon as passport is in hand** — HR submits DP on myMOM; keep ICA advisory note and Special Pass evidence.  
5. **Insurance** — Enrol the baby on day-0 rules your insurer actually writes ([health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat)).  
6. **Capacity** — Helper / infant care waitlists do not wait for the plastic card ([MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw), [preschool](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore)).  
7. **Money schemes** — Baby Bonus / CDA only after SC citizenship gates clear ([Baby Bonus mixed-status](/guides/baby-bonus-cda-mixed-status)).  
8. **Vaccines** — Upload overseas records to NIR before polyclinic catch-up; diphtheria/measles docs matter for long-term passes ([childhood vaccinations](/guides/childhood-vaccination-foreigners)).
