Being born here doesn't make your baby Singaporean. Citizenship runs through a parent, and if neither of you passes it on, what you have on day one is a foreign national with no immigration pass of their own. For EP and S Pass households that turns the first six weeks of parenthood into an admin sprint, which is why the newborn threads on r/askSingapore ([newborn DP timing](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/17hkidm/dependent_pass/), [Malaysian newborn docs](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1b0icqt/questions_regarding_malaysian_newborn_baby/)) all circle the same worry. Register the birth, get a passport out of your embassy, and have the Dependant's Pass lodged before the Special Pass runs out.

That last bit catches people, so it's worth saying plainly: **you can't file the newborn's DP yourself, the employer lodges it**, exactly as they did for your own pass. If nobody in HR knows that, you want to find out in week zero and not in week five.

## The clocks you're actually racing

| Step | Official timing | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register the birth | ICA's legal window is 42 days, but MOM's newborn-DP page asks you to register within 14 calendar days so the passport and DP sprint starts early | [LifeSG](https://www.life.gov.sg/services-tools/birth-registration) |
| ICA Special Pass (stay) | 42 days from the date of birth, on the advisory note you get after registration | Issued with the birth registration outcome |
| Employer lodges the DP | Within 6 weeks of birth on MOM's newborn path; most outcomes land around 3 weeks online | myMOM Portal |
| Passport ready | Before the DP is issued, though not necessarily before you apply | Your embassy or high commission |
| Digital birth certificate download | Within 90 days of notification | ICA e-Service |

Neither of the two hard deadlines is a formality. Missing birth registration is an offence under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, and staying past the Special Pass without a proper immigration pass is an Immigration Act offence. Yes, that means a newborn can technically be an overstayer. It is not a sentence anyone expects to read in their first month as a parent.

## What registration looks like when you're not local

Every parent registers online through LifeSG now, whatever your immigration or marital status, on the app or the website. Citizens, PRs and long-term FIN holders go in with Singpass; everyone else uses the LifeSG website path. The fee at last review was S$18, by card or PayNow.

Two changes still catch new parents out. Physical certificates aren't issued at all for births registered from 29 May 2022, so the digital PDF is the certificate. Download it as soon as you're notified rather than assuming you can come back for it, because that window closes at 90 days. And hospital counters no longer run the registration for you. Discharge used to be roughly the end of the paperwork. Now it's the start of it.

## The passport is the real bottleneck

MOM won't issue the Dependant's Pass until the baby's passport is ready, and this is where timelines slip more than anywhere else. Embassy photo rules don't bend for infants (eyes open, plain background), and a newborn does not cooperate to a schedule. Almost everyone underestimates this step.

If the passport is still in process when you lodge the DP, upload proof of the passport application, an embassy letter for example. And if the ICA advisory note is close to running out, that's when you ask about an extension rather than pre-emptively.

| Where you are | What MOM publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Holding the ICA advisory note, passport running late | Request a Special Pass extension only once the note is within 7 days of expiry |
| Holding a MOM-issued newborn Special Pass, DP pending | Auto-extends by 28 days, with an SMS one day before expiry |
| DP rejected | **The baby has to leave before the Special Pass expires.** MOM won't extend it so you can wait and see |
| Not applying for a DP | Ask ICA about the visit pass or Special Pass extension path |

One small thing that saves an awkward conversation at a front desk. Parents report a temporary T-series identification number appearing on the Singapore birth certificate for foreign babies, replaced by a G-series FIN once the Dependant's Pass card issues. Handy to know when a clinic asks which number to put on the records and all you're holding is the birth certificate.

## The salary gate doesn't soften for a baby

The main EP or S Pass holder still has to meet MOM's family-pass salary rules before a newborn gets a DP. The combined-household-income theory that goes round on Reddit is simply wrong: what's measured is the sponsoring pass holder's own fixed monthly salary, not what the two of you bring in together.

That matters most when a promotion is the thing that gets you over the line. If the raise that clears the floor lands mid-pregnancy, ask HR in writing when the new salary is actually on the MOM record, rather than assuming the newborn DP follows automatically from the offer letter.

## How the weeks actually run

1. Week 0: confirm the employer will lodge the newborn DP, and get the document checklist from HR in writing.
2. Days 1–7: register on LifeSG, and download the digital birth certificate the moment you're notified.
3. Days 1–14: book the embassy passport appointment and take the difficult photo.
4. As soon as the passport is in hand: HR submits the DP on myMOM. Keep the ICA advisory note and your Special Pass evidence somewhere you can grab them.
5. Insurance: enrol the baby under the day-0 rules your insurer actually writes, not the ones you assume they write ([health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat)).
6. Capacity: helper and infant care waitlists don't wait for the plastic card ([MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw), [preschool](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore)).
7. Money schemes: Baby Bonus and the CDA only open once the citizenship gates clear ([Baby Bonus mixed-status](/guides/baby-bonus-cda-mixed-status)).
8. Vaccines: upload overseas records to the NIR before the polyclinic catch-up. Diphtheria and measles documentation matters later for long-term passes ([childhood vaccinations](/guides/childhood-vaccination-foreigners)).

Delivery costs and hospital packages are a separate project, and we've written that one up in [maternity care](/guides/maternity-care-singapore). This page is the immigration project, and it starts the moment the baby exists.
