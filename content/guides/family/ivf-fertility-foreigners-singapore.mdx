r/askSingapore IVF threads mix three populations: **SC couples** on public co-funding, **SC + foreigner** couples arguing about grant percentages, and **EP + EP** couples shocked that KKH/NUH still bill them like private patients. The clinical pathway can be excellent; the subsidy story is citizenship-gated.

## The co-funding gate (read this first)

Government **Assisted Conception Procedure (ACP)** co-funding at **public** Assisted Reproduction centres is built for couples where **at least one spouse is a Singapore Citizen** at the start of the ART cycle. MOH also caps how many co-funded fresh/frozen cycles you may have already used.

| Couple pattern | Typical co-funding story |
| --- | --- |
| SC + SC | Highest published co-funding tier at public AR centres |
| SC + PR | Lower tier than SC–SC — still citizen-linked |
| SC + foreigner (EP/LTVP/etc.) | Lower still — still needs the **SC** spouse |
| PR + PR / EP + EP / two foreigners | **No** MOH ART co-funding — budget cash / insurance reality |

<OfficialSource>
Eligibility language and MediSave withdrawal bands: [MOH — Marriage and Parenthood schemes](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/marriage-and-parenthood-schemes/). Public AR centre framing: [MOH IVF co-funding response](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/response-to-adjournment-motion-on-providing-more-help-for-those-undergoing-ivf/).
</OfficialSource>

Verify live percentages and caps on MOH before you treat any clinic WhatsApp as gospel — schemes have been revised more than once.

## Public vs private AR centres

Public AR centres named in MOH materials include **KKH, NUH, and SGH**. Reddit’s “six-month wait before stims” stories are about **capacity**, not rudeness.

| Path | Upside | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public AR + eligible co-funding | Lowest cash for SC-linked couples | Queues; less “pick your brand” |
| Public AR as foreigner / ineligible | Same clinical brands | Often **unsubsidised** billing |
| Private fertility clinic | Scheduling, continuity marketing | Cash; MediSave only if you actually have MediSave |

Private centres do **not** unlock MOH ART co-funding. They may still accept MediSave for ACPs if the patient (or spouse) has a MediSave account and meets withdrawal rules.

## MediSave — only if you have it

MOH’s published MediSave pattern for ACPs (ART/IUI) is roughly:

- **S$6,000** first cycle / **S$5,000** second / **S$4,000** third and subsequent  
- Lifetime limit around **S$15,000 per patient**  

Employment Pass holders generally **do not** build MediSave the way PRs/citizens do — see [CPF and foreigners](/guides/cpf-and-foreigners). Do not plan an EP–EP IVF budget around “we’ll Medisave it.”

## Insurance and maternity sequencing

Most group hospital plans still **exclude** fertility treatment and often exclude maternity unless you bought the right rider early. Audit before you start stims:

1. Does outpatient / specialist cover investigations only, or also retrieval/transfer?  
2. If you conceive, does the **maternity** waiting period still apply?  
3. Neonatal / NICU limits if you deliver as a foreigner — [maternity care](/guides/maternity-care-singapore)  

## Timeline realism from forum heat

- Pre-IVF investigations alone can take **weeks to months**  
- Public stims/monitoring are appointment-dense — travel and EP leave planning matter  
- Failed cycles are common; price **more than one** attempt if that is your plan  
- Successful pregnancy returns you to the newborn admin clock — [birth registration & DP](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore)

## Practical questions for the clinic finance desk

1. Are we co-funding eligible **today** (citizenship of each spouse on cycle start)?  
2. Fresh vs frozen cycle cash quotes **after** any co-funding / MediSave?  
3. What is included vs add-ons (ICSI, PGT, freezing storage)?  
4. If we upgrade to a named consultant, does co-funding change?  
5. Who bills NICU if we deliver here later as foreigners?

Fertility in Singapore is medically sophisticated. For two work-pass holders it is usually a **cash project** with excellent clinics — not a Marriage & Parenthood subsidy story.
