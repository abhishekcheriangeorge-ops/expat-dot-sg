International-school fees buy a **curriculum**, not a guaranteed Oxbridge/Ivy ops team. r/askSingapore secondary threads keep asking whether HCIS / SJII / Dulwich / UWC counselling will actually file UCAS and Common App on time — especially when the family might move mid-DP.

Curriculum choice still starts in [IB vs A-Level pathways](/guides/secondary-ib-alevel-pathways-singapore). Campus counselling realism lives in [university counselling](/guides/university-counselling-international-schools-singapore). This guide is the **application operating system** for mobile EP/DP households.

## Product map (do not mix portals)

| Destination | Primary rail | Parent ops to own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK | **UCAS** | Predicted grades, reference, personal statement drafts, Oxbridge/medicine early deadlines |
| US / many liberal-arts | **Common App** (+ Coalition where used) | Essays, activities list, counsellor/teacher recs, testing plan |
| Australia / NZ | Often direct / agent portals | Prerequisite subjects; IB translation quirks |
| Singapore local unis | Separate from UCAS | Citizenship/PR pathways dominate — see [MOE post-secondary](https://www.moe.gov.sg/post-secondary/admissions) |

## Calendar that survives a posting

| Window | What must already be true |
| --- | --- |
| DP1 / Year 12 start | Named counsellor contact; subject choices locked for target countries |
| Testing year | SAT/ACT/IELTS dates booked around family travel — not “after mocks” forever |
| Early autumn (UK) | Personal statement drafts **before** Oxbridge/medicine cutoffs |
| Common App season | Activities CV portable across schools; recommenders warned 6+ weeks out |
| Results / clearing | Who is in-country if offers need rapid replies |

Mobile families fail when the **only** person who knows the portal password is a counsellor on leave during a home-leave month.

## Mid-posting school moves

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Predicted grades reset | Ask both schools, in writing, who owns the UCAS reference |
| Teacher recommenders evaporate | Collect two adults who have taught the student **recently** before exit |
| CCA story breaks | Keep a dated activity log parents control — not only the school portal |
| Curriculum switch (IB ↔ A-Level) | Re-validate university prerequisites before resigning a seat |

Treat counselling continuity like a SEN file transfer — same discipline as [school waitlists](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).

## What parents should ask the counselling office

1. Counsellor **caseload** and who writes UK references vs US recs  
2. Last three years’ destinations by **country counts**, not logo walls  
3. Whether predicted grades are committee-based or teacher-only  
4. How mid-year leavers are handled for UCAS / Common App  
5. Testing support (SAT dates, fee codes) vs “figure it out yourself”

## Household load (parents burn out here too)

Application season is a second unpaid job. Dual-career homes should name **one** parent ops lead for portals and one for testing logistics — and budget EAP / sleep when essays collide with EP travel ([parental burnout & EAP](/guides/parental-burnout-eap-singapore)). Teens under acute stress need the clinical map in [mental health resources](/guides/mental-health-resources), not only another essay tutor.

## What usually fails

- Starting UCAS the week the school’s internal deadline hits  
- Assuming IB alone “covers” US testing expectations  
- Letting a mid-contract move orphan the reference  
- Brochure logos without country-level outcome counts  
- Parents rewriting the personal statement until it sounds like LinkedIn  

University applications are a **project plan**. Tour the ops, not only the campus café.
