“Just two hours” is how PTA weeks eat both EPs. Dual-career households on r/askSingapore keep accepting class WhatsApp shifts at 22:40 — then discovering **blackout nights, helper rest days, and wallet caps** were never written down.

Photo day: [photo day ops](/guides/international-school-photo-day-ops-dual-career-singapore). Book week: [book week ops](/guides/international-school-book-week-author-visit-ops-dual-career-singapore). Fair ops: [winter fair](/guides/international-school-winter-fair-bazaar-ops-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **PTA / class-volunteer ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Hard shift cap per term** | Unlimited “quick helps” become a second job |
| **Blackout nights on the shared calendar** | Client decks beat last-minute stall shifts |
| **Named adult vs decline script** | Dual muted Slack is not an RSVP |
| **Wallet / donation cap** | Raffles and class gifts stack quietly |
| **Helper rest-day rewrite** | Sunday bake sales collide with FDW rest |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Set a term shift cap and wallet cap the week the first volunteer form drops.  
2. Put blackout nights on the shared calendar before you accept anything ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
3. Use a one-line decline script when the cap is hit — do not negotiate at midnight.  
4. If a shift lands on helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
5. If a child is sick on your shift day, switch to the sick matrix ([sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore)) — do not guilt-swap into three make-up stalls.  
6. Keep enrichment load capped in heavy volunteer weeks ([kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps)).

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Accepting every “urgent” WhatsApp ask | Term shift cap |
| Both parents “maybe we can both drop by” | One named adult or a clean no |
| Unlimited PayNow for class gifts | Hard wallet cap |
| Baking at 01:00 after a board pack | Blackout nights |
| Trading sick-day shifts into three make-ups | One make-up max, or decline |

## Questions to close this week

1. What is the hard shift cap this term?  
2. Which nights are blackout?  
3. What is the wallet / donation cap?  
4. Does any accepted shift collide with helper rest day?
