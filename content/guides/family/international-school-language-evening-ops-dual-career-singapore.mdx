Language evening is not optional networking with free canapés. Dual-career EP/DP households on r/askSingapore keep treating mother-tongue / language info nights like casual drop-ins — then discovering **slot clocks, pathway rooms, and sibling coverage** collide with board packs.

Curriculum nights: [curriculum-evening ops](/guides/international-school-curriculum-evening-ops-dual-career-singapore). Coffee mornings: [coffee-morning ops](/guides/international-school-parent-coffee-morning-ops-dual-career-singapore). Volunteer load: [PTA volunteer ops](/guides/international-school-pta-volunteer-ops-dual-career-singapore). This guide is **language-evening / mother-tongue info-night ops**.

## Ops products

| Product | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Slot / room calendar block** | Portals and language pathways punish late arrivals |
| **Two-adult question script** | Placement, LS, and pathway answers need owners |
| **Sibling coverage plan** | Evenings are not free childcare |
| **Follow-up email owner** | Dual muted Slack loses the action list |
| **Bus / pickup rewrite** | Late ends collide with contracted routes |

## Dual-career playbook

1. Book the slot the day registration opens; calendar a hard block for the named adult(s) ([dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore)).  
2. Split the question script: one owns placement/pathway; one owns pastoral/logistics.  
3. Pre-write sibling coverage or sitter overflow before you leave the office.  
4. If the evening lands on helper rest day, rewrite coverage early ([rest-day coverage](/guides/dual-career-helper-rest-day-sunday-coverage-singapore)).  
5. Check bus / pickup contracts for late returns ([IS bus & pickup](/guides/international-school-bus-pickup-dual-career-singapore)).  
6. If a child is sick, switch to the sick matrix ([sick-child coverage](/guides/dual-career-sick-child-coverage-singapore)) — do not drag a fever into a packed language lounge.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| “We will just drop by” without a slot | Book; walk-ins fail at peak season |
| Both parents on phones during placement talk | Named question owners |
| No sibling plan | Named coverage adult |
| Losing the follow-up list in WhatsApp | One email owner within 24 hours |
| Ignoring bus cutoffs | Rewrite pickup before the evening |

## Questions to close this week

1. When does the portal slot close?  
2. Who owns the question script halves?  
3. Who covers siblings?  
4. Who sends the follow-up email?
