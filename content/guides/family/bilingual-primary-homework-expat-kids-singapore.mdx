The fight is rarely “is Mandarin important?” — it is **Tuesday at 8pm** when English comprehension, Mother Tongue worksheet, and a science journal all land on the same dining table. r/askSingapore parents in MOE primary and bilingual IS tracks keep describing the same spiral: child melts down, parent Googles tutors at midnight, enrichment brands multiply, sleep disappears.

Enrichment product maps stay in [Mandarin enrichment](/guides/mandarin-enrichment-expat-kids-singapore). Local-system fee/MTL framing stays in [local schools for expats](/guides/local-schools-for-expats). This guide is the **nightly bilingual homework ops** layer.

## Two systems, two homework shapes

| System | Nightly reality | Parent trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MOE primary** | English + MTL + other subjects; spelling/composition cadence | Assuming “school Chinese” equals home fluency |
| **International primary** | English-heavy; optional Chinese homework varies by campus | Buying MTL-intensity tutors as if sitting PSLE |

## Operating rules that survive dual careers

| Rule | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| **One language block per evening** for weak MTL homes | Two-hour bilingual marathons teach avoidance |
| Parent coaches **process**, tutor owns **content** when parents are non-speakers | Shame spirals when a non-Chinese parent “teaches” characters at 9pm |
| Cap enrichment to **one** MTL product per term | Stacking Berries + tutor + apps + worksheets is not a strategy |
| Protect sleep over extra drill before tests | Exhausted kids fail the next day’s English too |
| Named adult for homework nights | Dual-career chaos — [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore) |

Grandparent helpers can sit with readers — but only with house rules from [grandparents long-stay caregiving](/guides/grandparents-long-stay-caregiving-singapore). Babysitters are pickup/logistics, not MTL teachers — [part-time babysitter](/guides/part-time-babysitter-nanny-singapore).

## Tutor vs enrichment vs parent

| Option | Best for | Fail mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School + light home reading | Mild gaps; strong school support | Ignoring MTL until mid-year reports |
| Small-group enrichment | Social + routine | Brand hopping every term |
| 1:1 tutor | Targeted composition / oral | Tutor becomes the only relationship; parents check out |
| Apps only | Drill between human sessions | Fake progress metrics without writing output |

If learning support or attention issues sit under the language fight, open [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support) before buying another worksheet pack.

## Weekly template (weak-MTL home)

1. **Mon/Thu:** 25–40 min MTL block (oral + reading aloud)  
2. **Tue:** English reading / comprehension only  
3. **Wed:** School worksheet catch-up — no new brand  
4. **Fri:** Light oral games; stop before meltdown  
5. **Weekend:** One cultural input (show, library, grandparent call) — not a three-hour tuition double

Keep holiday camps and extras from eating the term plan — [kids activities](/guides/kids-activities-holiday-camps).

## What usually fails

- Treating enrichment logos as a substitute for sleep  
- Non-speaker parents doing character drills after overtime  
- Switching schools mid-year solely to “escape Chinese” without reading the new campus’s Chinese track  
- Parallel English tuition + MTL tuition + piano on school nights  
- Waiting for the first red report before hiring help  

Bilingual homework is a **calendar design** problem. Design the week, then buy the minimum product that fits it.
