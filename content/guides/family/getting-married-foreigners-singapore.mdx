On r/askSingapore the marriage threads split into two failure modes: **EP couples** who assume ROM is a one-click Singpass form, and **citizen + foreigner** couples who skip Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (PMLA) then discover post-wedding housing and pass friction. Civil marriage is doable; the paperwork is the project.

## Who needs whose approval?

| Status | MOM marriage approval before marrying SC/PR? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Employment Pass / EntrePass / S Pass** | Generally **no** for ROM itself | Still need ROM stay/document rules |
| **Current or former Work Permit** marrying SC/PR | **Yes** — Controller of Work Passes approval first | Applies in Singapore **or** overseas; religious/customary marriages included |
| **Tourist / short-term visit** | No MOM WP approval | Strongly encouraged: ICA **PMLA** if marrying SC and planning long-term stay |

<OfficialSource>
MOM: [Work Permit marriage & pregnancy regulations](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/what-are-the-marriage-and-pregnancy-regulations-for-wp-holders-in-singapore). Do not invent “EP needs MOM blessing” from WP threads.
</OfficialSource>

## The 31-day stay rule (foreigner in the marriage)

Where **any** party is not a Singapore citizen or PR, at least **one** party must have been physically present in Singapore for **31 continuous days** (excluding arrival day) before the notice of marriage can be submitted. That stretch need **not** be immediately before the filing — a single past continuous trip can qualify.

<OfficialSource>
Published process overview: [Marriage.gov.sg — Overview of Marriage Process](https://www.marriage.gov.sg/Overview_of_Marriage_Process_Oct_2024.pdf). Statutory framing: [Women’s Charter](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/WC1961?ProvIds=P13-) notice/licence provisions.
</OfficialSource>

## Fees and timing (verify live)

| Item | Published figure (Oct 2024 overview) |
| --- | --- |
| Application fee — at least one SC/PR | **S$42** |
| Application fee — **both foreigners** | **S$380** |
| Notice window | File at least **21 days** before solemnisation; application typically valid up to **6 months** |

You need two witnesses aged **21+** with ID (NRIC or passport). If either party lacks Singpass **or** foreign documents are involved, expect an **in-person declaration** at ROM rather than a fully remote path.

## Documents that stall foreigner couples

- Valid passports / immigration passes for the appointment  
- Divorce / death certificates if previously married  
- **Letter of no impediment / capacity** from home authorities when ROM or your embassy requires it — often with MFA/consulate authentication or apostille sequencing ([apostille guide](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore))  
- Certified English translations for non-English papers  

Home-country recognition of a Singapore marriage certificate is a **separate** embassy/registry step. Ask your mission before you treat the ROM certificate as the final word for visa or surname changes abroad.

## Marrying a Singapore citizen — run PMLA early

If one of you is SC and you intend to live long-term in Singapore after marriage, ICA strongly encourages the **Pre-Marriage Long-Term Visit Pass Assessment** before you solemnise. Passing PMLA does not guarantee a later LTVP, but failing to assess first is a recurring Reddit regret when the foreign spouse’s stay hinges on ICA after the wedding. See [LTVP Singapore](/guides/ltvp-singapore).

## After the certificate

1. Download / store digital + physical copies; order extras while you are still in Singapore  
2. Sequence spouse pass (DP / LTVP) with the main pass holder’s employer or ICA — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore)  
3. Update bank, insurance, and will/CPF nomination language — [wills & CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore)  

ROM is logistics. Women’s Charter domicile and divorce jurisdiction are legal strategy — use Singapore family counsel if either of you may leave soon after marrying here.
