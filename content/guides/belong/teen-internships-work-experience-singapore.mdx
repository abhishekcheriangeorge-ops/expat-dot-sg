A teenager whose week runs from tuition to screens and back doesn't meet many adults outside school, and that is really the problem a holiday internship solves. A placement that's legal, properly supervised and short enough to survive the Singapore holiday calendar can do more for belonging than another term of enrichment.

University application storytelling still belongs in [university counselling](/guides/university-counselling-international-schools-singapore), and curriculum load sits in [IB and A-Level pathways](/guides/secondary-ib-alevel-pathways-singapore). This page is about work experience as a social rail, for EP and DP families.

## What "internship" usually means here

| Format | What it does for belonging | What to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School-brokered work week | Safer framing, and your teen arrives with a peer cohort | Few seats, and competitive to get one |
| Shadow days through a parent's network | The fastest soft landing available | Nepotism optics are real, and MOM age rules still apply |
| Short charity or NGO projects | Purpose, plus a room with mixed ages in it | Overlaps heavily with [volunteering networks](/guides/volunteering-networks-singapore) |
| Startup "unpaid hustle" | Rarely worth the holiday | Vague tasks and a genuine exploitation risk |

<OfficialSource>
Start from [MOM employment of children and young persons](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/young-persons-and-children) before any paid or hours-heavy arrangement. Pass status (DP / Student Pass) and age thresholds matter — do not improvise from home-country labour folklore. Confirm live MOM pages for the teen’s age band and the host’s obligations.
</OfficialSource>

## What actually builds ties, as opposed to résumé theatre

1. The same desk and the same supervisor for at least two weeks beats five one-day shadows. Nobody remembers a visitor.
2. A weekly team lunch does more for a teenager's weak ties than any post about the experience afterwards.
3. Get them a debrief with one adult who isn't a parent, a counsellor or a mentor, after the first week.
4. Line the placement up with a CCA they're already part of, so the social ground isn't starting from zero: [CCAs and belonging at international schools](/guides/international-school-cca-belonging-singapore).

Chambers and alumni networks surface placements that never reach a job board, and [chambers of commerce](/guides/chambers-of-commerce-singapore) is where to start on that. The teen still needs a named person onsite who knows they're arriving, though. Without one, the week dissolves into sitting quietly near a printer.

## Two working parents and the holiday calendar

Internships compete with camps and with family travel, and the loser is usually whoever booked last. Design the placement inside your [school-holiday coverage](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital) rather than stacking both parents' peak travel on top of a Jurong industrial park commute. In dual-career households, one parent needs to own the logistics outright rather than sharing them vaguely: [dual-career spouse](/guides/dual-career-spouse-singapore).

## Before day one

| Step | You're done when |
| --- | --- |
| Check age and pass rules against MOM guidance | You have written notes, not WhatsApp hearsay |
| Get the scope in writing: hours, paid or unpaid, who supervises | The host has emailed it to you |
| Dry-run the commute | The teen can navigate it alone, or there's an escort plan |
| Midpoint check-in | You've adjusted the tasks before week-two resentment sets in |

Careers offices at [ACS International](/schools/acs-international) and [Dulwich](/schools/dulwich-college-singapore) sometimes broker programmes, so ask early rather than once the holidays have started. Club networks like the [British Club](/clubs/british-club) and the [Singapore Cricket Club](/clubs/singapore-cricket-club) throw up shadow days through members rather than through job boards.

## What usually goes wrong

- A full-time unpaid "internship" with no mentor and no learning plan
- Treating the placement purely as raw material for a university essay
- Waving away **MOM age rules** because "it's only shadowing"
- Booking it across the one week the whole family had free together
- Expecting any sense of belonging from a fully remote week of filing PDFs

Treat the whole thing as a supervised experiment in belonging rather than a career move. Keep it legal, keep it short, keep a real human in charge of it, and let the weak ties compound on their own.
