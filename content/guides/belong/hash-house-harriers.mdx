The Hash is a social trail run (or walk) with a stubborn sense of humour. Singapore has multiple kennels with different paces and cultures. r/askSingapore “make friends” and British-culture threads still name hashing beside Meetup sports — a low-fee weekly ritual when country clubs feel absurd.

## Why newcomers like it

- Built-in weekly ritual without entrance fees  
- Mixed nationalities and ages  
- Walkers welcome at many kennels — fitness is optional; consistency is optional; friendliness is required  
- After-trail “circle” doubles as social hour  

It is not for everyone — humour can be crude — and that is fine. Try once as a guest before judging the whole movement.

Cultures differ — try two kennels before declaring a home pack. Check Facebook / kennel sites for the week’s start location (hashes move).

## Guest etiquette

1. Arrive on time; introduce yourself as a virgin / visitor if that is the kennel’s language  
2. Bring cash for the run fee + optional drinks  
3. Wear trail-capable shoes and humidity-sensible kit ([what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate))  
4. Hydrate; Singapore heat is not a European spring 10k  
5. Stay for the circle once — that is where names stick  

Pair hashing with [sports & ActiveSG](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore) and [find my people](/guides/find-my-people-singapore) so one kennel is a hub, not your only social plan. If the humour is not your tone, leave without guilt — Singapore has other weekly sports.
