**Short answer:** Wait 3 working days after your IPA before you try the portal, then book SEV and CSS as early as you can get them and finish the e-Journey inside the published clocks.

The post-IPA panic on r/askSingapore is almost always about the calendar rather than eligibility. The portal won't let you in on day one. SEV slots look empty. Someone in the group chat insists CSS only runs once a month. The stage map and ceremony timing live on [Singapore Citizenship Journey](/guides/singapore-citizenship-journey-scj). What we're covering here is the narrower day-3 login and booking layer, for the 16–60 band who have to complete SCJ.

## The clocks that unblock your booking

The SC Journey Portal opens to you **3 working days after the date on your IPA letter**, which is not the same as the day the letter reaches you. A Friday letter usually means logging in from the following Wednesday. That gap is why so many people decide on the Monday that something has gone wrong with their file, when nothing has. The [sgjourney help](http://sgjourney.gov.sg/help) page is where to check it.

Once you start the e-Journey, the roadmap gives you up to 7 days for particulars and uploads, then up to another 7 days to review, submit and pay. Diary both of those, because they run from the moment you start the step rather than from a fixed date you'd naturally remember.

SEV and CSS you book early, and there's no clever version of this. An inconvenient slot you can actually turn up to beats waiting for a mythical empty month.

If any clock you read here or anywhere else conflicts with your IPA letter, the letter wins. And if you still can't log in after three working days, use the portal or the ICA contact path named in your IPA pack rather than an agent's WhatsApp group.

## Booking hygiene

1. The hour the IPA lands, diary the date 3 working days out. That's your portal day.
2. Work through the e-Journey on the 7 + 7 rhythm, before the deadlines slip quietly out of your head.
3. Book the Singapore Experiential Visit (SEV) and the Community Sharing Session (CSS) the moment the portal shows you slots.
4. Run the foreign-citizenship renunciation documents for the ICA Building registration annex in parallel, not after, if you're 21+ and required to renounce.
5. Model NS separately with [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) and our [citizenship and NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) guide. Sitting through the SCJ sessions doesn't waive that liability literacy.

If your children are already SC by descent or registration, what's ahead of them is [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21), not this CSS booking stack.

## What to hold onto

IPA, then 3 working days, then the portal, then SEV and CSS booked early, then the e-Journey finished on the published clocks. That's the whole shape of it.
