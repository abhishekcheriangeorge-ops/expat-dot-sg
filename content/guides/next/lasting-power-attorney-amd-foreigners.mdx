r/askSingapore “final days / hospital bills / bank freeze” threads keep rediscovering the same split: a **will** moves assets **after death**; a **Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)** lets a chosen donee act **while you are alive but lack mental capacity**. Without an LPA, families often face a slow **deputyship** court path. Expats and new PRs under-plan this because home-country powers of attorney do not automatically unlock Singapore banks or CPF.

This is orientation, not legal advice. Make documents while capacity is clear.

## LPA in one table

| Idea | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Who can be a donor | Age **21+**, with capacity to make the LPA |
| What a donee can cover | **Personal welfare** and/or **property & affairs** (as granted) |
| When it bites | Only when the donor **lacks capacity** (or the donee reasonably believes so) — not a general day-to-day POA for convenience |
| Form 1 | General powers with basic restrictions — most people |
| Form 2 | Customised powers — solicitor-drafted clauses |
| Without an LPA | **Deputyship** via the Family Justice Courts — months, not days |

## Fees: citizens vs PR vs foreigners (from 1 May 2026)

OPG application fees (verify live; Certificate Issuer professional fees are **extra**):

| Donor | Form 1 | Form 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore citizen | Free (Form 1 path as published) | Reduced fee path — check live table |
| **Singapore PR** | **S$30** | **S$30** |
| **Foreigner** (neither SC nor PR) | **S$160** | **S$160** |

The [Mental Capacity (Public Guardian Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2026](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL-Supp/S272-2026/Published/20260430?DocDate=20260430) set the PR **S$30** and foreigner **S$160** application fees with effect from **1 May 2026**. OPG’s [fees PDF](https://www.msf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/opg/lpa_fees_table.pdf) is the citizen-facing summary — no refund after submission even if rejected.

EP/S Pass households with Singapore bank accounts, SRS, or property interests should not wait for PR “because LPA is for locals.” Foreigners can make an LPA; they just pay the foreigner fee band.

## Practical sequence most people skip

1. Decide **who** the donee(s) are and whether they need welfare, property, or both  
2. Book an **LPA Certificate Issuer** (doctor / lawyer / psychiatrist on OPG’s list) — their fee is separate  
3. Complete Form 1 on **OPGO** (Form 2 via solicitor)  
4. Pay OPG application fee when billed  
5. Store access instructions for banks / insurers **with** the will and CPF nomination map — [wills & CPF](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore)  

Activation later usually needs a medical report confirming lack of capacity before banks will act. Reddit’s “just use Singpass” shortcuts fail when the donor cannot authenticate.

## Advance Medical Directive (AMD) is a different document

An **AMD** tells doctors that if you are **terminally ill and unconscious**, you do **not** want extraordinary life-sustaining treatment used only to prolong life. It is **not** an LPA and **not** a will.

- Age **21+**, not mentally disordered  
- Made through a **doctor**; two witnesses at the same sitting (one must be the doctor)  
- Valid only after registration with the Registrar of Advance Medical Directives at MOH  

<OfficialSource>
[MOH — Advance Medical Directive](https://www.moh.gov.sg/seeking-healthcare/advance-medical-directive/)
</OfficialSource>

Pair AMD with Advance Care Planning conversations and your insurer’s hospital LOG habits — [healthcare triage](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital). After a death, next of kin still need [certificates / NEA / repatriation](/guides/death-repatriation-foreigners-singapore).
