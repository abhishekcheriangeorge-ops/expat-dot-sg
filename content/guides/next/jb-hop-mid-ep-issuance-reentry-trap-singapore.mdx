This one sits under [travel before the card](/guides/travel-before-work-pass-card) and [issuance, EPSC and the Notification Letter](/guides/work-pass-issuance-epsc-notification). What it covers is narrower: the short hop across the Causeway while your pass is still only half-issued. Landing-week threads rediscover this one every cycle, usually from the wrong side of the border.

## IPA is not a day-trip stamp

An in-principle approval does two things. It lets you enter, and it lets you finish issuance. That's the whole job. It isn't a finished work pass, and it isn't a promise that you can nip over to Johor Bahru for lunch and walk back in while HR still owes a medical, the issuance fee, a Notification Letter, or your EPSC biometrics.

The awkward part is that leaving is usually easy. Getting back in is where incomplete status bites, and by then you're on the wrong side of a land border with a work start date coming at you. The same logic kills the regional kickoff "just two nights" before the Notification Letter lands, except there you also have an airline doing its own document check at the gate.

People also over-read the Notification Letter. It's temporary evidence, it carries **a validity end date**, and it doesn't settle the question of what ICA or an airline will accept on the way back. And if your EPSC appointment is next week but the trip is this weekend, you've got two clocks colliding: letter validity and biometrics timing. Finish issuance first.

## What to ask HR before you book

1. Which MOM steps are still open — medical, issuance fee, Notification Letter, EPSC?
2. Do I have written clearance to leave and return on the status I'm holding right now?
3. If I'm on an STVP or visit conditions, what happens when I exit?
4. Who picks up the phone if ICA or the airline blocks me on the way back?
5. What I'd actually like: Notification Letter live, HR's written OK, and an issued view in SGWorkPass. Then I'll travel.

## The rule we'd hold to

No finished issuance, no Causeway run. Being hungry for one more weekend is a lot cheaper than being stuck offshore while your EPSC slot slips.
