This is a companion page, not the whole story. For how Fair Consideration works in general, start with [Fair Consideration and MyCareersFuture](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep). COMPASS points are a separate Stage 2 problem and they live in [COMPASS explained](/guides/compass-framework-explained), with renewal firm scores in [COMPASS firm scores](/guides/ep-renewal-compass-firm-scores-singapore). What we're dealing with here is narrower: the 14-day advertising clock, and the way candidates quietly set fire to it by resigning too early.

## What the clock usually requires

Before most EP and S Pass filings, unless the role is genuinely exempt, four things need to have happened. The vacancy has to have sat on MyCareersFuture for at least 14 consecutive days. Candidates have to have been fairly considered. No job offer can be made during that mandatory window. And when the pass application eventually goes in, it has to match the ad on employer, occupation, salary and vacancy count, quoting the Job Advertisement ID.

The third one is where enthusiasm becomes a problem. A hiring manager who tells you on day two that you're their person is being kind, and is also describing something that isn't supposed to have happened yet. "We already picked you" is not a published shortcut, and it's not a reason for you to hand in notice while the ad is still running.

## Exemptions worth getting in writing

Some roles skip the advertising step altogether. The catch is that HR has to be claiming a real, named category rather than a vibe.

| Common exemption (verify live) | Still true |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed monthly salary ≥ S$22,500 | Fair hiring still applies; the ad may be skipped |
| Employer with fewer than 10 employees | Same |
| Vacancy ≤ 1 month / eligible ICT / other published categories | HR must name the actual category, not "we already know you" |

Ask which one applies and get the answer in an email. A mislabelled exemption is how an otherwise clean transfer turns into an FCF problem months later, at which point you're the one holding the pass application.

## What to screenshot before you resign

None of this is paranoia. It's five minutes of admin that protects the only leverage you have if the story changes.

1. The MCF Job Advertisement ID, plus the first and last dates you can see the ad live
2. The title and salary band on the ad, next to the fixed pay in your offer letter
3. Written confirmation of whether HR is relying on an exemption, and which one
4. The intended filing month set against your own resignation and notice calendar — see [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)

Point two matters more than it looks. **If the IPA salary comes in below the band you screenshotted on the ad, get HR to reconcile it before you resign from your current job**, because afterwards you're negotiating from nowhere.

## The rule to hold onto

Clearing the advertising clock is not the same thing as clearing COMPASS. They're different gates and passing one tells you nothing about the other. Get the 14 days done, or get a documented exemption, before you let yourself treat an offer as pass-ready. Then run the SAT and firm criteria as an entirely separate checklist.
