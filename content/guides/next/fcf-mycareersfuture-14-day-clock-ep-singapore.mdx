**Short answer:** Unless exempted, clear **14 consecutive MCF days with no offer during the window** — then treat COMPASS as a **separate** checklist before you resign.

Parent FCF literacy: [Fair Consideration & MyCareersFuture](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep). COMPASS points are a **different** Stage 2 problem — [COMPASS explained](/guides/compass-framework-explained); firm-score renewals: [COMPASS firm scores](/guides/ep-renewal-compass-firm-scores-singapore). This companion is the **14-day advertising clock** candidates burn by resigning too early.

## What the clock usually requires

Before most EP / S Pass filings (unless exempted):

1. Vacancy on **MyCareersFuture** for at least **14 consecutive days**  
2. Fair consideration of candidates  
3. **No job offer** during that mandatory window  
4. Later pass application **matches** the ad (employer, occupation, salary, vacancy count) and quotes the **Job Advertisement ID**

Verbal “we already picked you on day two” is not a published shortcut.

## Exemptions candidates should verify in writing

| Common exemption (verify live) | Still true |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed monthly salary ≥ **S$22,500** | Fair hiring still applies; ad may be skipped |
| Employer with fewer than **10** employees | Same |
| Vacancy ≤ **1 month** / eligible ICT / other published categories | HR must name the real category — not “we know you” |

Mislabelled exemptions are how clean transfers become FCF problems later.

## Candidate screenshot pack

1. MCF **Job Advertisement ID** + first/last visible dates  
2. Title / salary band on the ad vs offer letter fixed pay  
3. Written confirmation whether HR is using an **exemption** and which one  
4. Filing month vs your resign / notice calendar — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)  

If IPA salary sits below the MCF band you screenshotted, ask HR to reconcile **before** you burn the old job.

## Decision rule

**FCF advertising ≠ COMPASS score.** Clear the 14-day (or documented exemption) **before** treating an offer as pass-ready; then run SAT / firm criteria as a separate checklist.
