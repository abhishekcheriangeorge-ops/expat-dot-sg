**Short answer:** For PRs: **mission renames the passport → ICA particulars with that bio-page → Transfer of REP → private chase**.

r/askSingapore’s PR name-change threads skip the hard rule: **ICA will not put a new name on your blue IC until your current foreign passport already shows that name**. The parent stack (deed poll → ICA S$60 particulars → private chase) lives on [change of name on NRIC](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore). This companion is the **passport-first** layer for permanent residents only.

Citizens upload a deed poll (and ICA-listed certificates) without a foreign-passport gate. Do not copy a citizen friend’s sequence.

## Sequence that usually survives ICA review

| Step | Who | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Lawyer **deed poll** (if your fact pattern needs one) | You | Private legal instrument bridging old → new string |
| 2. Update the **foreign passport** name at your mission | You | ICA’s published supporting document for PRs |
| 3. File ICA **IC change of particulars** with the new passport bio-page | You (Singpass) | S$60 published fee; ~7 working days typical |
| 4. **Transfer REP** onto the new travel document | You | New book ≠ auto REP — [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport) |
| 5. Chase banks, insurers, HR, schools, telco with deed poll + new IC | You | Singpass / government rails often follow IC; private counterparties do not |

Skipping step 2 is the rejection peers describe as “ICA asked for passport with new name.”

<OfficialSource>
[ICA — Replace Identity Card (Change of Particulars/Damaged)](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/update_particulars) — footnote: PRs with a name change must first update the name on their current passport and provide that bio-page. [Transfer of REP](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/PR/transfer-REP) after the new book issues.
</OfficialSource>

## Common failure modes

- **Deed poll uploaded, old passport bio-page attached.** ICA’s PR footnote rejects the particulars file.  
- **New passport issued, IC updated, REP left on the cancelled book.** Boards / gates fail on the stale travel document link.  
- **Travel mid-stack** (old IC + new passport name, or reverse). Finish ICA + REP before long-haul trips that assume automated clearance.  
- **Assuming Singpass “pushes” banks.** Bring the deed poll permanently; many schools never reprint certificates.

## Decision rule

For PRs: **mission updates the passport name → ICA particulars with that bio-page → Transfer of REP → private chase**. Parent overview stays on [change of name on NRIC](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore); annual calendar on [renewing life admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually).
