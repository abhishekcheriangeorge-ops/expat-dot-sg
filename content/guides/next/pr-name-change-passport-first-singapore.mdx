The PR name-change threads on r/askSingapore all skip the rule that decides the outcome: **ICA won't put a new name on your blue IC until your current foreign passport already shows that name**. The general stack — deed poll, then the ICA particulars filing at S$60, then the private chase — lives in [change of name on NRIC](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore). This page is the passport-first layer, and it applies to permanent residents only.

Citizens upload a deed poll, along with the certificates ICA lists, with no foreign-passport gate in front of it. Don't copy a citizen friend's sequence. Theirs really is shorter, and it won't work for you.

## The sequence that survives ICA review

1. Get a deed poll done with a lawyer, if your fact pattern needs one. It's the private legal instrument that bridges the old name to the new one.
2. Update the name on your foreign passport at your mission. This is the supporting document ICA publishes for PRs, and everything downstream waits on it.
3. File the ICA change of particulars for your IC through Singpass, attaching the new passport bio-page. The published fee is S$60, and around 7 working days is typical.
4. Transfer your REP onto the new travel document. **A new passport book doesn't carry the REP across by itself**, which is the part people discover at a gate rather than at a desk. See [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport).
5. Chase the private side with the deed poll and the new IC: banks, insurers, HR, schools, telco. Singpass and government rails tend to follow the IC on their own. Private counterparties don't.

Skip step 2 and you get the rejection everyone describes in the same words: ICA asked for a passport with the new name.

[ICA — Replace Identity Card (Change of Particulars/Damaged)](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/update_particulars) — footnote: PRs with a name change must first update the name on their current passport and provide that bio-page. ## Where this goes wrong

Deed poll uploaded, old passport bio-page attached. ICA's PR footnote rejects the particulars filing and you start over.

New passport issued, IC updated, REP left behind on the cancelled book. Boarding and immigration gates fail on the stale travel document link, and they do it at the least convenient moment.

Travelling mid-stack, holding an old IC and a new passport name, or the other way round. Finish the ICA filing and the REP transfer before any long-haul trip that assumes automated clearance.

Assuming Singpass pushes the change out to your banks. It doesn't. Keep the deed poll somewhere permanent, because many schools never reprint certificates and you'll be producing it for years.

## Decision rule

For PRs the order doesn't bend: the mission updates the passport name, then you file ICA particulars with that bio-page, then Transfer of REP, then the private chase.
