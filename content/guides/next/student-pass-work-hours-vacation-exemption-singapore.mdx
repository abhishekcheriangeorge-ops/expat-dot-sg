**Short answer:** **Check institution eligibility + term vs vacation + live hour caps before paid work** — graduation ends STP work folklore.

Parent STP mechanics: [Student’s Pass](/guides/student-pass-singapore). This companion is the **work-hours / vacation** layer Reddit compresses into “students can work 16 hours.”

## Exemption ≠ open labour market

MOM’s [work pass exemption for foreign students](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-pass-exemption-for-foreign-students) is a **narrow** carve-out for listed institutions and conditions. If you sit outside it, you need a proper work pass — freelancing, full-time jobs, or “help at uncle’s shop” are not vibes-based exceptions.

| Situation (verify live) | Typical posture |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible full-time STP during **term** | Hour caps apply (commonly discussed as up to **16 hours/week** — confirm live MOM text for your cohort) |
| Eligible full-time STP during **vacation** | Broader vacation work window for listed schools — still not every employer type |
| **Exchange** students | Generally **barred** from the exemption |
| Internship / industrial attachment | Often needs the school’s approved path / separate MOM product — not a tourist stamp |
| After graduation / STP ends | Exemption dies with the study basis — need EP/S Pass or another lawful stay rail |

## Rails people confuse with STP work rights

- **Training Employment Pass / Work Holiday Pass** — separate MOM products; see [WHP vs TEP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass)  
- **Tuition Grant bond** — MOE employment obligation after graduation, not an STP work licence — [Tuition Grant bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond)  
- **Graduate LTVP** — stay/job-hunt adjacency for eligible IHL grads, not free work — [graduate LTVP edges](/guides/graduate-ltvp-ihl-renewal-edges-singapore)  
- **Employer EP after grad** — FCF / COMPASS still apply — [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore)

## Decision rule

**Check institution eligibility + term vs vacation + live hour caps before you accept paid work.** Graduation ends STP work folklore; sponsor a real pass instead of inventing a bridging job on an expired card.
