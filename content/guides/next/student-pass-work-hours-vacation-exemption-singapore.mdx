**Short answer:** Before you take any paid work, check three things: that your institution is on MOM's list, whether you're in term time or vacation, and what the live hour caps say for your cohort. Once you graduate, none of it applies any more.

The mechanics of the pass itself are in our [Student's Pass guide](/guides/student-pass-singapore). This one is about the layer Reddit flattens into "students can work 16 hours."

## The exemption is not an open labour market

MOM's [work pass exemption for foreign students](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-pass-exemption-for-foreign-students) is narrow on purpose. It covers students at listed institutions under stated conditions, and it was never a general permission to earn money while you study. Sit outside it and you need a proper work pass like anyone else. Freelancing doesn't escape that because it's freelancing, and helping at your uncle's shop doesn't escape it because it's family.

What makes this messy is that students compare notes. Someone on a full-time course at a listed school genuinely can do some paid work. You, on an exchange semester, generally can't. Same campus, different legal position, and the difference only surfaces when something goes wrong.

| Situation (check the live rules) | Where you usually stand |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible full-time STP, term time | Hour caps apply, commonly discussed as up to 16 hours a week, but confirm MOM's current text for your cohort |
| Eligible full-time STP, vacation | A broader vacation window at listed schools, and still not every kind of employer |
| Exchange students | Generally barred from the exemption altogether |
| Internship or industrial attachment | Often needs the school's approved path or a separate MOM product, not just a stamp |
| After graduation, once STP ends | The exemption dies with the study basis, so you need an EP, S Pass, or another lawful stay rail |

## Things people mistake for student work rights

The Training Employment Pass and the Work Holiday Pass are separate MOM products with their own eligibility rules, not upgrades bolted onto your STP. We compare them in [WHP vs TEP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass).

The [MOE Tuition Grant bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond) confuses people in the other direction. It's an employment obligation you take on after graduation in exchange for subsidised fees, not a licence to work during your course.

The [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-ihl-renewal-edges-singapore) gives eligible IHL graduates room to stay and job-hunt. Room to stay is not the same as permission to work.

And an [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) after graduation is an ordinary hire, with FCF and COMPASS applying exactly as they would to any other candidate.

## The rule worth remembering

Check institution eligibility, then term versus vacation, then the live hour caps — in that order, and before you accept the work rather than after. **Once your STP ends, someone has to sponsor a real pass**, because the exemption goes when the study basis goes. A bridging job on an expired card isn't a thing.
