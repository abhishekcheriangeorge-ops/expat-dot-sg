r/askSingapore exit threads forget the helper until levy invoices keep arriving after the family’s flight. Hiring cost stacks live on [hiring an MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw). This Next companion is the **cancel-when-leaving** layer beside your own [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).

You are still the **employer** until MOM cancellation completes — salary, levy, insurance, and bond hygiene do not auto-stop when your EP cancel date is set.

## Sequence that usually hurts less

| Step | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Decide **transfer** to a new employer vs **home-country repatriation** | Transfer timelines and agency paperwork differ from straight cancel |
| 2. Settle **final salary**, unused rest-day pay, and any MOM-required items in writing | Disputes delay bond release folklore and airport scenes |
| 3. Cancel the MDW **Work Permit** in MOM’s channel for the chosen path | Levy should stop only after a successful cancel — verify live |
| 4. Follow MOM’s instructions on **security bond** release / discharge | Do not assume the bond frees the day she flies |
| 5. Keep cancel acknowledgement with your IR21 / lease / shipping file | Exit audits love paper |

Do not book the helper’s repatriation flight on the assumption that your IR21 cash will free the same week.

## Decision rule

Lock transfer-vs-repatriate → settle pay → MOM cancel → confirm levy stop / bond path → then your own flight.
