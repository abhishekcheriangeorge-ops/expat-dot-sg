**Short answer:** Decide transfer or repatriation, settle her pay, cancel the Work Permit through MOM, then confirm the levy has actually stopped. All of that goes before your own flight, because the levy doesn't die with your EP cancellation date.

Exit threads on r/askSingapore tend to forget the helper entirely, right up until levy invoices keep landing after the family has already flown. What hiring costs in the first place is on [hiring an MDW](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw). This guide is the cancel-when-leaving layer that sits beside your own [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).

The thing to hold on to is that you remain **her employer until MOM's cancellation completes**. Salary, levy, insurance and security bond obligations don't quietly switch off on the date your own EP is cancelled. Your HR is handling your pass, not hers.

## A sequence that usually hurts less

1. Decide transfer to a new employer versus home-country repatriation, and decide it first. Transfer timelines and agency paperwork are not the same as a straight cancel, and this one choice sets the shape of everything after it.
2. Settle final salary, unused rest-day pay and anything else MOM requires, in writing, before you cancel. An unresolved pay question is what turns a routine bond conversation into an airport scene.
3. Cancel the MDW Work Permit through MOM's channel for whichever path you chose. The levy should stop only after a successful cancel, so check it live rather than assuming it has followed along.
4. Follow MOM's published instructions on security bond release or discharge. Don't assume the bond frees the day she flies.
5. Keep the cancellation acknowledgement with your IR21, lease and shipping paperwork. Exit audits love paper, and this is the cheapest file you'll ever keep.

## The parallel calendars people mix up

There are four clocks running at once during an exit, and they belong to different people. Confusing them is how the levy keeps billing.

| Calendar | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Your EP and family pass cancel, plus STVP | HR and you. See [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |
| Your IR21 tax clearance and the final-pay freeze | Your employer and IRAS. See [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) |
| The MDW Work Permit cancel and levy stop | You, as her employer. That's this guide |
| Condo lift booking and shipping | You. See [shipping out](/guides/shipping-out-household) |

Which is why you shouldn't book her repatriation flight on the assumption that your IR21 cash will free up in the same week. Different clock, different owner.

## The decision rule

Lock transfer versus repatriate, settle the pay, cancel with MOM, confirm the levy stop and the bond path, and only then book your own flight.
