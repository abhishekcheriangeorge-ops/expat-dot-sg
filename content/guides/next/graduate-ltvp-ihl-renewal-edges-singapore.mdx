Reddit’s “my private uni friend got graduate LTVP” threads skip the gate that actually matters: **is your institute on ICA’s published IHL list?** Fees, no-work rule, and STVP timing live on [graduate LTVP job hunt](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt). This companion covers **eligibility list edges**, **processing vs runway**, and **renewal** traps.

Spouse / child LTVP + PLOC / LOC is a **different** category — [PLOC / LOC](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore). Do not mix LOC folklore into a graduate-seeking-employment file.

## Confirm the IHL list before you pay S$45

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| School appears on ICA’s **List of Institutes of Higher Learning** | Local unis / polys, named offshore campuses, LASALLE / NAFA — not every private programme peers advertise |
| Certificates + transcripts ready (translations per ICA rules) | Incomplete packs stretch past the “within 6 weeks” headline |
| Photo **400 × 514** per ICA guidelines | Soft rejects burn calendar you may not have after Student’s Pass / EP cancel |

If your school is **not** on the list, graduate LTVP is the wrong product — switch early to [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) or the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) rather than filing a doomed application.

## Processing clock vs STVP / Student’s Pass end

| Reality | Operating move |
| --- | --- |
| ICA publishes **within ~6 weeks** when documents are in order; some take longer | Do not assume week-2 approval for a lease or interview trip |
| Non-Singpass apply path is available on the graduate page | Useful when you never held Singpass as a student |
| STVP after EP cancel is **stay**, not work — and not automatic graduate LTVP | Ask HR for the longest STVP **before** cancel — [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |
| Paid work still needs MOM EP / S Pass / WP (or another path that authorises work) | [MOM — work passes for ICA LTVP holders](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-passes-for-holders-of-long-term-visit-passes-issued-by-ica) |

Tuition Grant bond years need **authorised** employment — LTVP days do not invent EP credit. See [Tuition Grant bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond).

## Renewal edges (when your letter allows renew)

ICA’s general LTVP renewal guidance (verify against **your** approval letter and the live page):

- Apply **at least three months** before current LTVP expiry where renewal is available for your category.  
- Renewed validity starts from **issuance** (after issuance fee) — **unused days on the old pass do not carry forward**.  
- Sponsor / Singpass renewal flows on the general LTVP page often assume a sponsor; graduate-seeking-employment filings used the **non-Singpass** path at first issue — confirm the renewal channel ICA names for *your* pass before the clock runs out.  
- Outcome and status still run through ICA e-Service / email — not university “auto renew.”

Treat duration and renewability as **what your letter says**, not forum claims of “automatic one more year.”

## Decision rule

**List → complete pack → file before your stay rail ends → treat ~6 weeks as real → renew early with no carry-forward myth.** Parent stay/job-hunt framing stays on [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt); EP return path on [employment pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore).
