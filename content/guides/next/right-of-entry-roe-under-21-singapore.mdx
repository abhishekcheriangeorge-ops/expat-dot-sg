r/askSingapore’s dual-passport kid threads mix **pink IC**, **Singapore passport**, **foreign passport**, and **ORAL at 21** into one airport panic. ICA publishes a narrower product for under-21 citizens who also hold a foreign passport: **Right of Entry (ROE)** — so the child can clear Singapore immigration on the **foreign** passport.

This is **not** permission to keep dual citizenship past 21. Retention still runs through [ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21). Status letters for schools or foreign missions live on [confirmation of citizenship](/guides/confirmation-singapore-citizenship-status).

## Who ROE is for

| Rule ICA publishes | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who | **Singapore citizen under 21** who also holds a **foreign passport** |
| Fee | **None** |
| Typical outcome clock | About **3 working days** when documents are complete |
| Under 16 | Parent (or parent-appointed proxy) files |
| 16–20 | Child may file as requestor |

ROE is issued **on the foreign passport**. After ICA confirms issuance, that foreign passport can be used for immigration clearance when entering or leaving Singapore through Singapore checkpoints.

## Documents and the name-match trap

Child side (high level):

- Child’s **foreign passport**  
- Deed poll / religious certificate if the name changed  

ICA’s hard rule: the name on the foreign passport must **match ICA’s records** for ROE to be issued on that passport. If the child already holds a Singapore IC and the names diverge, fix ICA particulars first via the IC unit / [name-change path](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore) before expecting ROE.

Requestor side: proxies need a letter of authorisation (and, where listed, the requestor’s Singapore IC).

## New foreign passport → transfer ROE

ROE does **not** auto-migrate when the child renews the foreign passport. ICA’s published instruction: submit a fresh request to **transfer ROE** onto the new travel document. Treat this like the PR [Transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport) habit — passport first, immigration annotation second, long-haul third.

## What ROE is not

| Folklore | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| “ROE means they can keep both citizenships forever” | No — ORAL / adult renunciation rules still apply at 21+ |
| “ROE replaces a Singapore passport” | It facilitates clearance on a **foreign** passport; it is not a substitute SC passport strategy |
| “Embassy can issue ROE overseas like a visa sticker fantasy” | Follow ICA’s online request flow and live instructions — do not invent mission folklore |
| “Confirmation letter = ROE” | Different product — [confirmation letter](/guides/confirmation-singapore-citizenship-status) is a digital status letter (S$20), not checkpoint facilitation |

Male dual-status teens still need [NS / Exit Permit](/guides/ns-exit-permit-pr-families) literacy before travel plans assume endless foreign-passport convenience.

## Decision rule

If an under-21 SC regularly travels on a foreign passport, get **ROE** on that passport (and transfer it on renewal) so checkpoint clearance matches the document in hand. Diary **ORAL at 21** separately — ROE never buys dual citizenship past the published retention window.
