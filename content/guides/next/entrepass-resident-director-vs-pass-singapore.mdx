r/askSingapore founders keep asking “which visa lets me start a company?” as if ACRA and MOM were one form. Incorporation mechanics: [incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore). Founder-pass gates: [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore). This companion is the **vs** layer — resident director ≠ EntrePass ≠ permission to work.

## Two jobs, two agencies

| Job | Agency | What clears it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own shares / register a Pte Ltd | **ACRA** (foreigners via **CSP**) | Name reservation, constitution, ≥1 ordinarily **resident director**, company secretary rules |
| Live in Singapore and **run** the business | **MOM** | EntrePass, employer EP, ONE Pass / PEP (watch PEP shareholder-director ineligibility), or DP business LOC — not “Bizfile = work rights” |

Anyone can own shares. Operating from Singapore without a work pass is a different offence.

## EntrePass when the thesis is founder/innovator

Use EntrePass when you clear **≥30%** + venture/innovative technology **and** at least one published innovator test (funding, incubator, prior exit, registered IP, Singapore research collab). Model the **TBS / local workforce** renewal ladder before relocating a household. Shelf companies older than 12 months are assessed against renewal criteria — do not assume a quiet Pte Ltd unlocks a founder IPA.

If a properly capitalised sponsor can clear a normal EP role, that path is usually cleaner than inventing an EntrePass for ordinary employment.

## Resident director traps

- **Nominee directors are real directors** under the Companies Act — cheap “name only” packs are personal-risk theatre.  
- An **EP holder** as resident director may still need a **secondary directorship LOC** + primary-employer consent for commercial board seats; registration does not legalise moonlighting.  
- **PEP** lists shareholder-directors / freelancers / sole props as ineligible — entrepreneurial work points to EntrePass or ONE Pass, not PEP. Chooser: [PEP / ONE / Tech.Pass](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore).

## Decision rule

**ACRA first for the company; MOM second for the human.** If the pitch deck needs a founder pass, read EntrePass end-to-end before you pay for “guaranteed pass + nominee director” packages.
