The "I moved into a condo and MOM won't take my address" threads on r/askSingapore almost always come down to the same thing: the landlord hasn't made the FWTES declaration. It isn't a mysterious blacklist and it isn't something you did wrong. The full notify mechanics for address, mobile and passport live on [notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport). This page is the narrower layer underneath: the 5-day clock, what FWTES does to it, and why none of this is the same thing as ICA's eCOA if you hold an NRIC.

## The 5-day clock

Move house and you or your employer have **5 days** from the change to file the new residential address, through the EP eService or through OFWAS. Change your mobile number and it's the same 5 days on the same channel. Passport particulars are worded more loosely, but treat them the same way: update through the EP eService after a renewal, before you start relying on automated gates that are still matching an old passport number.

Keep the acknowledgement PDF the eService hands you. "I'll do it after the weekend" is how people blow the window, and there's no partial credit for having meant to.

## Where FWTES bites

FWTES is the Foreign Worker Tenant Enquiry Service, and it is usually the reason a perfectly real address gets bounced. Your landlord has to have declared that the unit allows foreign-employee address registration. A lot of first-time private residential landlords have never heard of it, which is why the rejection feels so arbitrary from your side of the transaction.

| What you're seeing | What's usually behind it | What to do about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The EP eService or OFWAS rejects the unit | The landlord hasn't declared the unit in FWTES | Escalate in writing to the landlord or the agent. First-time private residential landlords miss this constantly |
| You're trying to register a hotel or Airbnb address | Short-let and hotel stays commonly fail lawful residential registration | Use a serviced apartment or a proper lease you can actually declare. Our [serviced apartment bridge](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment) covers the options |
| Still inside the 5 days, but the portal won't accept it | The clock keeps running while FWTES is pending | Don't wait quietly. Push the landlord and keep screenshots of every attempt you made |

## A MOM address update is not an ICA eCOA

Two different agencies, two different clocks. Mixing them up is how people update their address confidently on the wrong portal and walk away thinking they're done.

If you're on an EP or S Pass you have a FIN and no NRIC, so your address goes to MOM through the EP eService or OFWAS. That's this guide, plus [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) for the rest of the mechanics.

If you're a citizen or PR with an NRIC, your address goes to ICA through eCOA instead, which runs on a 28-day report clock rather than a 5-day one. That's [eCOA NRIC address change](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore).

## The order that works

On move-in day, confirm with the landlord that the unit is declared in FWTES. Then file the address and mobile update with MOM inside **5 days** and save the acknowledgement. Passport renewals are a separate notification, and worth doing before you travel rather than after a gate has already refused you. The parent notify map stays on [EP notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport); if you hold an NRIC, you're on [eCOA](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore) instead.
