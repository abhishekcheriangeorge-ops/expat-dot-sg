**Short answer:** Move-in day → confirm landlord **FWTES** → file MOM address/mobile inside **5 days** — this is not ICA eCOA.

r/askSingapore’s “I moved into a condo and MOM won’t take my address” threads are usually **FWTES / landlord declaration** blockers, not mysterious blacklists. Full notify mechanics (address, mobile, passport) live on [notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport). This companion is the **5-day clock + FWTES** layer — and the hard line vs ICA **eCOA** for NRIC holders.

## The 5-day clock

| Change | Who updates | Clock MOM publishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential **address** | Pass holder or employer via EP eService / OFWAS | Within **5 days** of the change |
| **Mobile** number | Same channel | Within **5 days** |
| **Passport** particulars | EP eService after renewal | Before you rely on automated gates |

Keep the eService acknowledgement PDF. “I’ll do it after the weekend” is how people blow the window.

## FWTES landlord traps

| Symptom | Likely cause | Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EP eService / OFWAS rejects the unit | Landlord has not declared the unit allows foreign-employee address registration in **FWTES** | Escalate in writing to landlord / agent; first-time private residential landlords often miss this |
| Hotel / Airbnb “address” | Short-let / hotel stays commonly fail lawful residential registration | Use a serviced apartment or proper lease you can declare — [serviced apartment bridge](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment) |
| Still inside 5 days but portal blocked | Clock still runs while FWTES is pending | Do not wait silently — push landlord + keep screenshots |

## MOM address ≠ ICA eCOA

| Holder | Agency | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EP / S Pass (no NRIC) | **MOM** EP eService / OFWAS | This guide + [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) |
| SC / PR with **NRIC** | **ICA** eCOA (28-day report clock) | [eCOA NRIC address change](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore) |

Mixing the two agencies is how people “update address” on the wrong portal and think they are done.

## Decision rule

Move-in day → confirm landlord **FWTES** → file MOM address/mobile inside **5 days** → keep acknowledgement. Passport renewals are a separate notify before travel. Parent notify map stays on [EP notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport); NRIC holders use [eCOA](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore).
