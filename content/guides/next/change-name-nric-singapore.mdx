r/askSingapore’s name-change threads treat ICA as a one-click Singpass toggle. It is not. For adults, the usual stack is **lawyer deed poll → ICA Identity Card replacement (change of particulars) → chase every private institution yourself**. Government rails often follow the IC; banks and schools generally do not. **PRs:** the passport-first gate gets its own companion — [PR name change passport first](/guides/pr-name-change-passport-first-singapore).

This is **not** the [lost / stolen IC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) path (different fee and trigger). Damaged cards share the same ICA change-of-particulars page and must be replaced within **14 days** of damage.

## Adult name change — practical order

1. Instruct a Singapore lawyer for a **deed poll** (fees are private-market; forums quote roughly low hundreds of dollars — verify with the firm).  
2. **Citizens:** upload the deed poll (and other ICA-listed docs) in the IC change-of-particulars e-Service.  
3. **Permanent Residents:** ICA’s published rule is stricter — **update the name on your current foreign passport first**, then use that passport bio-page as supporting evidence for the Singapore IC change. Skipping the passport step is a common rejection — deep dive: [PR passport-first](/guides/pr-name-change-passport-first-singapore).  
4. Pay ICA’s published **S$60** fee; processing about **seven working days** (longer in peaks).  
5. Collect the new IC within **three months** of approval (SMS/email notify).  
6. Walk a chase list: banks, insurers, employer HR / MOM pass records if still on a work pass world, clubs, schools, telco, CPF/IRAS if anything still shows the old string.

<OfficialSource>
IC replacement for particulars / damage: [ICA — Replace Identity Card (Change of Particulars/Damaged)](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/update_particulars).
</OfficialSource>

## What ICA asks for (high level)

| Applicant | Documents ICA commonly lists |
| --- | --- |
| Singapore citizen | Deed poll (name change); marriage certificate if adding married name; baptism/religious certificate rules if adding a religious name; recent digital photo |
| Singapore PR | Foreign passport pages showing the **new** name; marriage certificate if adding married name; recent digital photo |
| Minor name change on IC | **Both** birth parents’ consent; if only one consents, an **Order of Court** empowering unilateral change (plus ICA’s declaration form path) |

Translations must follow ICA’s embassy / notary patterns. ICA does not endorse private translation shops.

## Babies under one — different ICA product

If the child was **born in Singapore**, is **under one year old**, and the name has **never** been altered, parents may use ICA’s **Alteration of Child's Name on Birth Certificate** e-Service instead of waiting for an IC. Published fees: **S$33** application + **S$18** new digital birth certificate; about **three working days** when documents are complete. After age one, birth-certificate alteration closes and the **deed poll + notify ICA** path becomes the practical stack for schools and future IC registration.

<OfficialSource>
[ICA — Alteration of Child's Name on Birth Certificate](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/birth/alter-child-name).
</OfficialSource>

## Myths

- **“Singpass updates everything.”** Government systems often follow the IC; **private** counterparties need your deed poll or new IC in person / upload. There is no universal one-click.  
- **“Deed poll alone is enough.”** Without ICA (and, for PRs, passport) updates, you hold a private instrument that does not match your identity card.  
- **“Schools will reprint certificates.”** Many will not; keep the deed poll permanently to bridge old and new names on transcripts.  
- **“This is the same as lost-IC fees.”** Loss replacement is **S$100 / S$300**; particulars replacement is **S$60** on the live ICA page.

## Decision rule

Deed poll first, **passport-before-IC for PRs**, ICA S$60 particulars e-Service second, private chase list third. Treat the deed poll as a lifelong bridge document even after the new blue or pink card is in your wallet.
