r/askSingapore's name-change threads tend to treat ICA as a one-click Singpass toggle. It isn't. For adults the usual stack is a lawyer's deed poll, then an ICA Identity Card replacement under change of particulars, then chasing every private institution yourself. Government rails mostly follow the IC once it's updated; banks and schools generally don't, and that tail is the part nobody warns you about. If you're a PR there's a passport-first gate before any of this, which gets its own companion: [PR name change passport first](/guides/pr-name-change-passport-first-singapore).

To be clear about what this isn't: it's not the [lost or stolen IC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) path, which has a different trigger and a different fee. Damaged cards do share the same ICA change-of-particulars page, and they must be replaced **within 14 days** of the damage.

## The adult name change, in order

1. Instruct a Singapore lawyer for a deed poll. Fees are private-market, and forums quote roughly low hundreds of dollars, but verify with the firm rather than with the thread.
2. If you're a citizen, upload the deed poll and the other documents ICA lists in the IC change-of-particulars e-Service.
3. If you're a Permanent Resident, ICA's published rule is stricter: **update the name on your current foreign passport first**, then use that passport bio-page as supporting evidence for the Singapore IC change. Skipping the passport step is a common rejection, and an avoidable one. Deep dive on [PR passport-first](/guides/pr-name-change-passport-first-singapore).
4. Pay ICA's published S$60 fee. Processing runs about seven working days, longer in peak periods.
5. Collect the new IC within three months of approval. ICA notifies you by SMS or email.
6. Then walk the chase list yourself: banks, insurers, employer HR and MOM pass records if you're still in the work-pass world, clubs, schools, telco, and CPF or IRAS if anything there still shows the old string.

## What ICA usually asks for

A citizen changing their name is generally asked for the deed poll, a marriage certificate if a married name is being added, and a recent digital photo. Adding a religious name brings in its own baptism or religious certificate rules.

A PR is asked for the foreign passport pages showing the new name, which is why the ordering above matters, plus the marriage certificate where a married name is involved and the same recent digital photo.

Changing a minor's name on the IC needs both birth parents' consent. Where only one parent consents, you need an Order of Court empowering the unilateral change, along with ICA's declaration form path.

Translations have to follow ICA's embassy or notary patterns, and ICA does not endorse private translation shops.

## Babies under one: a different ICA product altogether

If the child was born in Singapore, is under one year old, and the name has never been altered, parents can use ICA's Alteration of Child's Name on Birth Certificate e-Service instead of waiting for an IC. Published fees are S$33 for the application plus S$18 for the new digital birth certificate, and it takes about three working days when the documents are complete.

After the first birthday that door closes. Birth-certificate alteration is no longer available, and you're back to the deed poll plus notifying ICA, which becomes the practical stack for schools and for future IC registration.

## Four things people get wrong

Singpass does not update everything. Government systems often follow the IC, but private counterparties want to see the deed poll or the new IC themselves, in person or uploaded. There is no universal one-click.

The deed poll on its own isn't enough either. Without the ICA update, and the passport update if you're a PR, you're holding a private instrument that doesn't match your identity card. That mismatch is exactly what causes trouble at counters.

Schools will not necessarily reprint certificates. Many won't, so keep the deed poll permanently: it's the document that bridges your old and new names on transcripts, possibly decades from now.

And this is not the lost-IC fee. Loss replacement is **S$100 / S$300**; particulars replacement is S$60 on the live ICA page.

## The short version

Deed poll first. Passport before IC if you're a PR. Then ICA's S$60 particulars e-Service, then the private chase list, which is the long part. Treat the deed poll as a lifelong bridge document rather than a step you file away once the new card is in your wallet.
