People renounce PR to relocate permanently, end a roots experiment, or — in the loudest Reddit threads — try to exit NS mid-stream. ICA’s process is bureaucratic; MINDEF’s consequences are the part forums under-warn about.

## When renunciation is (and is not) the path

ICA: a PR may renounce if intending to **relocate or leave Singapore permanently**.

- If the **main applicant** renounces, **dependants** (spouse and children under 21) who got PR under that sponsorship generally **must renounce together**  
- Children 21+ who got PR under a parent need their **own** application  
- If you already **lost** PR by staying overseas **without a valid REP**, ICA says you are **not required** to file renunciation  

Government may **reject or withhold** renunciation when you have unresolved agency matters or **outstanding NS obligations**.

## What to prepare

Mandatory uploads (PDF/JPEG/PNG, **≤1MB** each) typically include:

- **Annex A** — Letter of Request stating reasons  
- **Annex B** — MINDEF Acknowledgement Form where applicable (NS-liable male dependants / Foreign Student Scheme PRs, and sponsors as the table requires)  
- Passport biodata + signature page  
- Name-change proofs, divorce/custody papers, death certificates if relevant  
- Loss-of-IC police report / statutory declaration if the blue IC is missing  
- **Clearance letter from MINDEF / SPF / SCDF** — compulsory for **serving NSFs**  

Apply via ICA’s online portal (~20 minutes). Incomplete packs stall. Processing often up to **~4 weeks**; longer if clarifications are needed.

### While you wait

Applicants **in Singapore** should remain until completion. On success you receive a **30-day Visit Pass** and a renunciation letter by email (check spam). Return the **blue IC** within **14 days** via Overseas Mission or registered mail to ICA’s published address — or file a statutory declaration / police report if lost.

## NS-liable males — read this before you romanticise Reddit “guides”

ICA’s PR page: renouncing or losing PR **without serving or completing full-time NS** has a **serious adverse impact** on later applications to **work or study**, and on **long-term immigration facilities** — including for **family members’** sponsorship, REP renewals, and citizenship bids.

r/NationalServiceSG threads that walk through “tell PC → MINDEF interview → ICA renounce → leave” describe **process**, not **consequence-free outcomes**. Serving NSFs need the clearance letter; approval is discretionary. Do **not** AWOL while paperwork is pending.

## Money and estate after renunciation

- **CPF:** full account closure / withdrawal for non-citizens happens only after you are **no longer** SC/PR — renounce or lose status first, then close ([CPF leaving Singapore](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/cpf-asset-management/on-leaving-singapore); myth-busting in [CPF and foreigners](/guides/cpf-and-foreigners))  
- **Wills / nominations:** update once status and accounts change ([wills & probate](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore))  
- **REP:** irrelevant once you are no longer PR; do not confuse renunciation with letting REP lapse into accidental loss ([REP guide](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas))
