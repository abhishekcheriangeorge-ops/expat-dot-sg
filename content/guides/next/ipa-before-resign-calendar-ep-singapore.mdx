**Short answer:** **No new IPA → no resign letter** — verbal offers do not start the clock; old passes do not auto-die.

MOM’s line is short: the **new employer files a fresh pass**; keep the old job until you hold the **new IPA**. Parent overview: [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore). Bond clawbacks on the way out: [resign after IPA on a training bond](/guides/training-bond-resign-after-ipa-ep). This companion is the **calendar** — where Reddit “I resigned on a verbal” threads die.

## Week-shaped sequence (orientation, not a promise)

| Phase | Do | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet search | Interview while the current EP/S Pass is live | Announce resign dates to the market |
| Offer diligence | Confirm FCF advertising / COMPASS or S Pass gates with new HR — [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) | Treat “we’ll sort the pass later” as a start date |
| **New IPA in hand** | Only then resign / serve notice | Resign on a verbal or “IPA next week” |
| Notice / garden leave | Align last day with IPA validity and new start; MC still counts toward notice — [leave & notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep) | Assume garden leave pauses bond or non-compete clocks without reading the clause |
| Cancel → issue | Old employer cancels after employment ends; new pass issues | Assume the old pass auto-dies when the new one is approved |

Subsidiary moves usually still need a **fresh** application. Employers should not refuse lawful cancellation after you resign with proper notice.

## Collisions people miss

- **IPA expiry vs long notice** — if contractual notice is longer than IPA validity / preferred start, ask the new employer about issuance timing **before** you resign, not after week six of notice.  
- **Leave offset myths** — encashment vs bringing the last day forward are different deals — [AL encashment / notice offset](/guides/annual-leave-encashment-notice-offset-ep-singapore).  
- **Family passes** — DP / LTVP usually track the main pass; budget card re-issuance and school term risk if the new IPA fails.  
- **Retrenchment mid-hunt** — notice while still employed is your legal work window; STVP after cancel is stay, not work — [retrenchment](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits) · [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## Decision rule

**No new IPA → no resign letter.** Everything else (bonds, leave encashment, IR21, family cards) is a parallel checklist once that gate clears.
