Put a recurring calendar event: **Singapore life admin week**. Forum panic about passport appointments, EP renewals, and school re-enrolment usually means someone skipped this.

## Review annually (or every September)

| Domain | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Work / family passes | Expiry dates for EP/S Pass/DP/LTVP; COMPASS / salary thresholds — [EP renewal](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy) |
| Passports | 6–12 months validity for schools and airlines; embassy lead times — [passports & documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas) |
| Insurance | Beneficiaries, outpatient caps, maternity waiting periods — [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat) |
| Housing | Lease renewal / diplomatic clause window — [deposits & diplomatic clause](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) |
| School | Re-enrolment fees, bus contracts, camp deposits |
| Estate | Will / guardianship recognising cross-border reality |
| Condo / HDB | Emergency contacts at security and school |
| Banking | FIN, PayNow proxies, standing instructions after phone changes |
| Exit readiness | IR21 awareness even if you are staying — [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) |

## Passports and embassies

Your Singapore pass is **not** your passport. Missions often need appointments weeks out; some require separate submit and collect trips. Start from [MFA overseas missions](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-Missions) for your nationality’s Singapore embassy pages.

## Boring systems are kindness

Shared password vaults, scanned marriage/birth certificates, and a one-page household “in case of emergency” sheet prevent 2 a.m. chaos. Families stay kinder under stress when the admin is already done.
