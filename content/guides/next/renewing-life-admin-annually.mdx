## Passports and embassies

Your Singapore pass or NRIC is **not** your passport. Missions often need appointments weeks out; some require separate submit and collect trips. Start from [MFA overseas missions](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-Missions) for your nationality’s Singapore embassy pages.

## PR-specific traps the September review catches

- **“PR renew” folklore** — you renew the **REP**, not permanent residence itself.  
- **Overseas address on the IC** — legal to report, but expect harder REP conversations if Singapore ties look thin.  
- **Lost IC while overseas** — replace on return; do not wait for a club night or enlistment letter to force the issue.

## Boring systems are kindness

Shared password vaults, scanned marriage/birth certificates, and a one-page household “in case of emergency” sheet prevent 2 a.m. chaos. Families stay kinder under stress when the admin is already done.
