## Pass ≠ placement you wanted

AEIS / S-AEIS measures performance **plus vacancies**. MOE assigns where seats exist — not the brand primary locals ballot for. Crossing the score line still leaves:

| Outcome | Household move |
| --- | --- |
| Place offered | Register at the **designated** school; model commute before celebrating |
| Pass, no place / weak options | International hold or another cycle — fee already gone |
| Fail | Same — non-refundable test fee |

## Calendar forks that kill holds

| Path | Typical start | Hold strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AEIS** | **January** next year | Keep international seat through outcome email if cash allows |
| **S-AEIS** | **Apr/May** same year | Fewer seats — worst backup if you already cancelled the private hold |
| **P1 IS** | Separate May interest window | Not AEIS — age **6 to 6+** on 1 Jan of admission year |

Do not cancel a paid international hold on “we’ll just do S-AEIS” folklore.

## Decision rule

**Budget AEIS as a lottery ticket with a dated start — keep a private hold until the offer letter names a school you can actually commute to.** Assignment beats preference; international fees beat a cross-island Year 3 surprise only if you planned the buffer.
