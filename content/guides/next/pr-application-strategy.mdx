Permanent Residence is discretionary. There is **no** public points calculator that guarantees approval — despite what LinkedIn consultants imply.

## What ICA actually publishes

ICA states that it takes into account factors such as:

- Family ties to Singaporeans  
- Economic contributions  
- Qualifications  
- Age  
- Family profile  
- Length of residency  

…to assess ability to contribute, integrate, and commit to sinking roots. EP and S Pass holders may apply via ICA e-Service with Singpass. Published processing guidance is **within 6 months** when documents are in order; some cases take longer.

Fees (as published on ICA’s PR page at last review): **S$100** per application at submission, then formalities fees (Entry Permit, Re-Entry Permit, Identity Card) on success. All submission fees are non-refundable.

<OfficialSource>
Use [ICA — Becoming a Permanent Resident](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/PR) for eligibility categories, documents, fees, and Singpass requirements. ICA states it has **no affiliation** with commercial “PR specialists.”
</OfficialSource>

## Eligible categories (high level)

Per ICA, foreigners may apply if they are, among other categories:

- Spouse of a citizen or PR (sponsor logs in with Singpass)  
- Unmarried child under 21 of a citizen/PR (within marriage or legal adoption rules)  
- Aged parent of a citizen aged 21+  
- Holder of an Employment Pass or S Pass  
- Student who has passed at least one national exam (PSLE / GCE N/O/A) or is in the Integrated Programme  
- Foreign investor via EDB’s Global Investor Programme (separate track)

## How to read peer anecdotes (carefully)

Singapore Expats Forum and Reddit threads are full of “approved on try 2 after salary jumped” stories. Treat them as **illustrations of uncertainty**, not policy:

| Anecdote pattern you will hear | Safer interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| “Wait 2–3 years then apply” | Tenure helps tell an integration story; it is not a published rule |
| “Need S$X salary” | ICA publishes no salary floor for PR; higher economic contribution is one factor among many |
| “Kids in local school helped” | Consistent with “integration / roots” language; not a checklist item ICA publishes |
| “Agency guaranteed approval” | Ignore — ICA explicitly disclaims commercial agents |
| “Rejected then approved after promotion” | Profiles change; re-applying without new substance rarely helps |

Never invent a private scoring system. If a consultant claims insider quotas, walk away.

## Timing that is actually a strategy

- Build **stable employment**, clean tax filings, and a coherent family plan before you apply  
- Align PR timing with **schooling and housing** decisions you can reverse if refused  
- Avoid anxiety applications every six months with identical documents  
- Model **NS liability** for male children **before** you celebrate an in-principle approval  

## National Service — non-optional reading

Under the Enlistment Act, male citizens and PRs are generally liable for National Service unless exempted. ICA’s PR page summarises registration/enlistment timing and warns that renouncing or losing PR **without** completing full-time NS has serious adverse effects on future work, study, and long-term immigration facilities — including for family members.

<OfficialSource>
Read [CMPB NS information](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) and the NS section on [ICA’s PR page](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/PR) before any family application that includes sons.
</OfficialSource>

## After approval — money and mobility change

- CPF contributions typically begin for employed PRs — remodel take-home pay ([CPF and foreigners](/guides/cpf-and-foreigners))  
- Housing and school fee bands can change; verify live HDB/MOE rules  
- Re-Entry Permits matter if you travel; do not treat PR as a souvenir stamp  

PR is a roots decision, not a coupon code for cheaper international school. If you are still undecided on a five-year horizon, read [long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit) first.
