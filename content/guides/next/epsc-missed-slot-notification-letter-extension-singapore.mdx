**Short answer:** Put the letter's end date and the EPSC deadline in your calendar the hour that email lands. If the biometrics slot slips, the fix is HR rebooking it or asking for a letter extension, not a hop over the causeway while you work out what to do.

The full issuance sequence is in [work pass issuance, EPSC and the Notification Letter](/guides/work-pass-issuance-epsc-notification), and if you're a step further back — still refused, or holding an IPA and nothing else — start with [EP rejection and IPA](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance). This page covers one specific way it goes wrong: the letter has arrived, and the appointment calendar won't cooperate.

## Two clocks start the day the pass is issued

Once your employer issues the pass, MOM emails a Notification Letter. For an EP, orientation is about 1 month of proof you can work and travel on while the physical card is being printed. If your letter says registration is required, you then have about 2 weeks from issuance to get fingerprints and photo done at EPSC. Both are orientation figures, so read the real dates off your own copy of the letter.

Here's the part people get wrong. The two clocks aren't linked, and **missing your appointment doesn't quietly extend either one**. The deadline stays where it was; you've just moved closer to it with one fewer option.

| Slip | First move |
| --- | --- |
| Next EPSC slot falls after the 2-week window | Ask HR to rebook, and get them to confirm whether the letter still requires registration at all |
| EPSC falls after the Notification Letter end date | Ask your employer to request a letter extension before it expires, not after |
| Letter sitting in HR's inbox only | Get it forwarded to you the same day — you work and travel on that PDF, so you need to be holding it |
| “I’ll sort EPSC out after JB” | Finish issuance first, then read [travel before the card](/guides/travel-before-work-pass-card) |

## What to ask HR the day the letter lands

1. What's the validity end date on my copy of the Notification Letter?
2. Does this letter require EPSC, and by when?
3. Who books the slot, me or the agent, and what do we do if the next one available is past the deadline?
4. Where is the card being delivered, and who's authorised to collect it?
5. If the letter needs extending, what's the plan, in writing?

Question three earns its place. “The earliest slot is three weeks out” is a very different problem on day one than on day twelve, so ask while you can still act on the answer.

## The rule to hold onto

Diary the letter end date and the EPSC deadline the hour the email arrives. After that, a slot that slips is your employer's extension request to make. It doesn't quietly become a tourist trip while you figure things out.
