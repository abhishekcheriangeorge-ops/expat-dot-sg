## Two clocks after issuance

Once the employer **issues** the pass, MOM emails a **Notification Letter** (EP orientation: about **1 month** of work/travel proof while the card prints). If the letter requires registration, finish **EPSC** fingerprints/photo within about **2 weeks** of issuance (verify live). Missing the slot does not silently extend either clock.

| Slip | First move |
| --- | --- |
| EPSC appointment after the 2-week window | Ask HR to rebook **and** confirm whether the letter still requires registration |
| EPSC after the Notification Letter end date | Ask employer to **request a letter extension** before it expires |
| Letter in HR inbox only | Get it forwarded to **you** the same day — you travel/work on that PDF |
| “I’ll EPSC after JB” | Finish issuance first — [travel before card](/guides/travel-before-work-pass-card) |

## HR ask list the day the letter lands

1. Validity end date on **my** Notification Letter copy  
2. Does this letter require EPSC? By when?  
3. Who books EPSC — me or the agent — and what if the next slot is past the clock?  
4. Card delivery address / authorised collector  
5. Written plan if letter extension is needed

## Decision rule

**Diary letter end date and EPSC deadline the hour the email arrives.** Slot slips are an HR extension problem — not a tourist hop while you “figure it out.”
