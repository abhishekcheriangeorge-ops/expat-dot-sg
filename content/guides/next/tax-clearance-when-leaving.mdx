When certain foreign employees cease employment in Singapore, go on an overseas posting, or plan to leave Singapore for more than three months, employers generally must seek **tax clearance** from IRAS before releasing final pay.

This applies to work pass holders, including categories such as ONE Pass and PEP holders, under IRAS rules.

## What employers must do

Per IRAS guidance:

1. Notify IRAS via **Form IR21** at least **one month** before cessation / qualifying departure  
2. **Withhold all monies due** to the employee from the date the employer knows of the impending cessation or departure  
3. Follow IRAS directions before releasing withheld amounts  
4. File additional/amended IR21 if further income becomes payable later  

If one month’s notice is impossible, employers must still file and provide reasons. Late filing without valid reason can attract employer penalties (IRAS cites fines up to S$5,000).

## When clearance may not be required

IRAS lists exceptions — including Singapore citizens, certain SPR situations with a Letter of Undertaking (not leaving permanently), short work periods under defined income thresholds, and some intra-group transfers. **Do not self-exempt** from a Reddit comment; have HR confirm against the live IRAS page.

## What you should do as the employee

- Tell HR your departure date **early** — withholding often starts at resignation notice, not only in the final month  
- Keep payslips, bonus letters, and benefits records tidy  
- Ask which components will be withheld and when IR21 will be filed  
- Keep a **cash buffer** — see [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs)  
- Do not book non-changeable flights that assume same-week clearance  
- After leaving, keep Singpass/myTaxPortal access where possible to confirm no outstanding tax  

## Garden leave and short notice

IRAS expects clearance at the **start of gardening leave** when applicable. Short resignations still require IR21; immigration stay windows after pass cancellation are a separate MOM/ICA problem — coordinate both calendars.

## Unvested RSUs and options

Foreign employees often face IRAS **deemed exercise** on unexercised options / unvested share awards at clearance. Ask HR whether the company uses an approved **tracking** arrangement before you resign into a large vest cliff. Detail: [RSU / ESOP tax](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore).
