## LOC is narrow; own pass is the labour-market door

From **1 May 2021**, ordinary employer jobs for DP holders generally need a separate **EP / S Pass / Work Permit**. Letter of Consent is mainly for eligible **DP business owners**, not “any job with a consent letter.”

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “DP + LOC = open labour market” | Ordinary employment usually needs **own EP/S Pass/WP** |
| “LOC renews if the business still exists” | Renewals commonly need a **local hire on CPF** at Local Qualifying Salary — solo shops fail here |
| “ONE Pass spouse LOC rules copy onto EP DP” | Wrong playbook — see [ONE Pass spouse LOC depth](/guides/one-pass-spouse-loc-depth-singapore) |
| “If principal EP renews late, LOC keeps me working” | LOC dies with the **DP**; DP usually dies with the principal pass |

## Chooser checklist

1. Is the trailing partner taking an **employer job**? Push for sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) / [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore) — not LOC folklore.  
2. Is the plan a **Singapore business** as owner/director with published shareholding? Stress-test LOC eligibility **and** renewal local-hire rules before quitting abroad.  
3. Is work **overseas-only remote** with no Singapore entity/clients? Read [side income / freelance](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) instead of forcing LOC.  
4. Sequence principal renewal IPA before betting on continuous DP/LOC work.  
5. Budget the household as **single-income** until the second product is issued.

## Decision rule

**If the trailing partner needs ordinary Singapore employment, choose own EP/S Pass — treat DP business LOC as a narrow owner path with renewal traps, not a general work pass.**
