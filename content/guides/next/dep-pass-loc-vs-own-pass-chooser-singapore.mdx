If you've come here from the [Dependant's Pass guide](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) or from [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore), this is the narrower question: which work-rights product does the trailing partner actually need? ONE Pass households are running a different product, and that one has its own guide in [ONE Pass spouse LOC depth](/guides/one-pass-spouse-loc-depth-singapore). What follows is the chooser, written for couples who arrive believing a DP Letter of Consent is a general Singapore work visa. It isn't, and it hasn't been for years.

## LOC is narrow. An own pass is the real door.

Since **1 May 2021**, a DP holder taking an ordinary employer job generally needs their own EP, S Pass or Work Permit. The Letter of Consent still exists, but it's aimed at eligible DP business owners rather than at "any job, with a consent letter attached". That one distinction explains most of the disappointment in this area.

| What people believe | What's actually true |
| --- | --- |
| "DP plus LOC means the open labour market" | Ordinary employment usually needs your own EP, S Pass or Work Permit |
| "The LOC renews as long as the business still exists" | Renewal commonly needs a local hire on CPF at the Local Qualifying Salary, which is where solo shops fall over |
| "ONE Pass spouse LOC rules carry across to an EP household's DP" | Different product, different playbook. See [ONE Pass spouse LOC depth](/guides/one-pass-spouse-loc-depth-singapore) |
| "If the principal's EP renewal runs late, the LOC keeps me working" | The LOC dies with the DP, and the DP usually dies with the principal pass |

## Working out which one you need

1. If the trailing partner is taking an ordinary employer job, push for a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) or [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore) from the outset. An employer who offers to "sort out an LOC" is working from a pre-2021 memory, and it's better to correct that at offer stage than at start date.
2. If the plan is a Singapore business, as owner or director with the published shareholding, stress-test LOC eligibility and the renewal local-hire rules before anybody resigns a job abroad. Those are two different tests and the second is much the harder one.
3. If the work is overseas-only remote, with no Singapore entity and no Singapore clients, that's a different question altogether. Read [side income and freelancing](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) rather than trying to force an LOC to fit a shape it was never cut for.
4. Whichever applies, sequence the principal's renewal IPA before betting on continuous DP or LOC work.
5. Budget the household as single-income until the second product has actually been issued. Not approved in principle. Issued.

## The rule

**If the trailing partner needs ordinary Singapore employment, get them their own EP or S Pass.** Treat the DP business LOC as what it really is, a narrow owner path with genuine renewal traps, and not a general work pass.
