The "my company cut my package, does my spouse's DP die?" threads on r/askSingapore all rest on the same assumption: that a payroll change is a private matter between you and your employer. MOM doesn't see it that way. The employer-side notify mechanics (salary, occupation, moves between related companies) are on [EP salary & occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes). What we're covering here is what happens downstream of the money, in your household.

## The cut is an immigration event

Here's the published shape of it. Check the live pages before you rely on any of it, because these rules move.

Your employer notifies MOM, not you. That's worth sitting with for a second: the thing protecting your family's right to stay here is somebody else's admin task, and you can't file it yourself if they forget. The notify has to go in **at least 1 month before** the date the lower salary takes effect.

The revised fixed monthly salary then has to still qualify for the EP on its own terms. That's what the Self-Assessment Tool is for. Put the new number in and see what comes back. If the pass no longer qualifies at that figure, the EP gets cancelled and the person applies for whatever pass they can actually hold at the new salary.

Then the part that gives this page its name. If the revised salary no longer clears MOM's criteria for DP or LTVP privileges, **MOM revokes the DP or LTVP tied to that work pass**. The existing ones, not just the next renewal. A handshake pay cut with no notify to MOM is how families find out mid-school-term that their dependant passes are gone.

## Questions to send HR before you sign the cut

Send these in writing and keep the replies. If HR is handling it properly, the answers cost them ten minutes. If they can't answer, you've learned something useful before you signed rather than after.

1. Will you file the MOM salary-reduction notify at least 1 month before the effective date?
2. Will you run SAT on the revised fixed monthly salary and send me the screenshots?
3. Do my spouse's and children's DP or LTVP privileges still clear at the new number?
4. If they don't, what's the plan: restore the pay, cancel and reapply, or a family exit timeline?
5. Is this a related-company transfer in disguise? That one needs a new EP rather than a salary notify, and we cover it on [changing jobs](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass).

## Raise vs cut: don't mix up the folklore

The two directions aren't symmetrical, and forum advice tends to blur them into one rule.

| Change | Immediate MOM notify? |
| --- | --- |
| Salary cut | Yes, filed by the employer, at least 1 month before |
| Raise alone | Usually no; you declare it at EP renewal |
| Raise, then adding dependants | Notify before you apply for the dependant privileges |
| Occupation or title rewrite | A separate employer occupation-update request, roughly 3 weeks |

What actually counts as "fixed monthly salary" is its own rabbit hole, and it matters here because the wrong reading of your package can make a cut look survivable when it isn't. We've unpicked it on [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding). Renewal-year COMPASS firm scores are a different theatre entirely, and they live on [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy).

## When you can call the cut safe

Three things, in writing: the MOM notify, the SAT result on the revised number, and a straight answer that the DP or LTVP privileges still clear. Two out of three isn't a yes. Until you have all three, don't treat the cut as settled for the household, however settled it feels for your job.
