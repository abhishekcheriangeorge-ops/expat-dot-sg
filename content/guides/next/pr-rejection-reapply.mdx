r/askSingapore’s PR threads cluster around three moments: **pending forever**, **rejected with no reasons**, and **what to change before try #2**. ICA does not publish a scorecard. What it does publish is how to appeal, how to check status, and that a fresh application only makes sense when your circumstances have actually changed.

## Pending is not approval (or rejection)

ICA’s published guidance: applications are generally processed **within 6 months** when documents are in order; **some take longer**. Forum waits of 12–27 months are common anecdotes — longer waiting is **not** a positive signal.

| Status / UI quirk people panic about | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Pending” for >6 months | Still undecided; email ICA with the reference if you need to confirm no missing request |
| Upload button disappears | Often means outcome is near — still not a guarantee either way |
| PRE-… vs PRS-… reference change | Usually an ICA-side refile / reconsideration path; treat as “still live,” not approved |

Check status via **MyICA** (Singpass) or the e-Service with reference ID + FIN/UIN. Outcomes arrive by **email** (or post for older submissions before 26 June 2024).

## Material changes while pending

Salary jump, new employer, marriage, birth, property purchase, completed degree — Reddit’s advice to “just email ICA_PR@…” is folklore. Prefer the **upload channel inside the live application** when ICA has opened it; otherwise write to ICA with your **application reference** and attach evidence. Do not invent a second concurrent PR application.

Changing jobs mid-pending is a common anxiety. Update ICA when the change is real; do not stall a career solely for superstition — but do not ghost the file either.

## If you are rejected

ICA does **not** explain individual refusals in public FAQs. Published assessment themes still apply on appeal and re-apply: family ties to Singaporeans, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, length of residency, ability to integrate, and commitment to sinking roots.

### Official appeal path (e-Service only)

You may appeal a **rejected** SC / PR / LTVP application through ICA’s dedicated e-Service.

- **Who submits:** the original **sponsor** with Singpass; if there was no sponsor, the **main applicant**  
- Non-Singpass original applications: use the **same email** as the first filing  
- ICA states it does **not** accept appeals **in person, by email, or by post**  
- Pending applications: wait for outcome — do not use the appeal e-Service as a status nudge  

Each appeal is assessed on its own merits; processing times vary; outcome by email (and online status).

### Fresh re-application

ask.gov.sg’s ICA answer: if unsuccessful, **consider a fresh online application when circumstances change**. Community folklore about a hard **six-month cooldown** is common; verify any cooling-off language on your rejection letter / live ICA pages — do not treat Reddit as the clock.

Re-apply only when something **material** moved:

- Sustained higher fixed salary / clearer economic contribution  
- Longer residency or stronger family profile (marriage length, SC/PR children, local schooling)  
- Credentials completed (degree conferred, not “almost done”)  
- Documented community roots that were missing the first time  

Identical documents six weeks later waste fees (non-refundable **S$100** per application at submission) and calendar years of anxiety. Agencies cannot buy an outcome — ICA explicitly disclaims commercial “PR specialists.”

## Spouse / local-sponsorship refusals

Local-sponsor threads (citizen or PR sponsoring a spouse) often cite short marriage length, thin shared roots, or sponsor income stories. Length of marriage and household stability show up constantly in peer anecdotes; they are **not** published pass/fail thresholds. Plan a re-file around real life changes (second child, property, years of cohabitation), not calendar superstition about “apply in January.”

## NS still belongs in the decision

Rejection does not erase the need to model **National Service** before the next try that includes sons.
