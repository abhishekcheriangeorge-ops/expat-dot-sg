This is a companion to the main [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) guide and the [renewal ladder](/guides/entrepass-renewal-evaluation-ladder-singapore). It covers the exit most founders eventually face: moving off the founder pass onto an employer-sponsored EP. The usual trigger is a raise or a self-hire that people assume morphs the pass automatically. It doesn't.

## A founder pass is not an employee pass

EntrePass is a venture and innovator product, judged at renewal against TBS and local-workforce bars. An Employment Pass is a different animal: a sponsored role that has to clear the Stage 1 salary floor plus [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) unless it's exempt, and usually [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) on top of that. Your own Pte Ltd can sponsor your EP, but only as a credible employer making a real filing. ACRA showing your name twice is not that.

Four pieces of founder folklore do most of the damage here.

The first is that you'll just convert EntrePass to EP in the eService. There's no one-click morph. You file a new EP while the EntrePass is still valid, and the two applications live separate lives.

The second is that 30% shareholding guarantees you an EP for yourself. Shareholding is an EntrePass gate. It is not an EP exemption, and it buys you nothing on the EP side of the wall.

The third is that founding the company gets you out of FCF. A self-hire still faces the live fair-consideration and advertising rules unless MOM has published an exemption that covers *your* case.

The fourth is the expensive one: missed the TBS bar, so the EP will paper over renewal. These are different products assessed on different things. Stage the EP **before** the EntrePass dies, because the moment it lapses you are arguing your case from no pass at all.

## How to run the exit

1. Model the next EntrePass renewal against the TBS and LWF bars. If you can already see you'll miss it, start the EP calendar early rather than at renewal week.
2. Confirm your fixed monthly salary against the live EP age-band floor, along with the COMPASS inputs, with whoever is actually doing the filing.
3. Keep the EntrePass valid until the EP is **issued**, not merely IPA'd, unless MOM instructs otherwise in writing.
4. Keep resident-director and ACRA hygiene separate from the pass story. They get conflated constantly: [resident director vs pass](/guides/entrepass-resident-director-vs-pass-singapore).
5. If what you actually want is top-talent status or a spouse LOC, compare the [ONE / PEP chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore) before you force an EP self-hire.

## The rule of thumb

Leave EntrePass for an EP when there is a genuine sponsored role that clears COMPASS and FCF. Not because incorporation folklore promised you a conversion button.
