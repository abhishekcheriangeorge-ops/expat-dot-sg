r/askSingapore’s “convert my spouse” threads collapse three products into one word — **marriage**, **LTVP**, and **PR**. ICA publishes a clear eligibility row: a **Singapore citizen or PR** can sponsor a **foreign spouse** for Permanent Residence via ICA e-Service. Sponsorship creates a right to **apply**, not a right to **approval**.

This guide is the **spouse** row only. Child sponsorship is [PR for a newborn / child](/guides/pr-for-child-newborn-singapore); EP/S Pass self-applications stay on [PR application strategy](/guides/pr-application-strategy).

## What ICA actually lists

| Path | Who logs in | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spouse of **SC or PR** | The **local spouse** sponsors with Singpass | Not automatic on ROM day |
| Unmarried child under 21 | Parent sponsors | Separate form path |
| Aged parent of an **SC** aged 21+ | Citizen child sponsors | **PR children cannot** use the aged-parent PR row |

Every person added as an applicant must review the form (Singpass or ICA-issued credentials) before submit. ICA recommends starting only after eligible applicants already have Singpass — account approval can take **2–5 working days**.

## Marriage ≠ PR (and LTVP is often the bridge)

A solemnisation or overseas marriage certificate does **not** mint a blue IC. Forum “apply the week after ROM” stories usually describe **rejections**, not strategy. Many SC–foreigner couples first stabilise stay on a **Long-Term Visit Pass** (and, where eligible, Letter of Consent for work) before a PR file — see ICA’s [LTVP page](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP) and the PMLA / processing clocks there for citizen spouses.

Practical stack peers actually run:

1. Legal marriage recognised for ICA filings (ROM or registered overseas marriage + certified translations)  
2. Stable **sponsor** employment / housing story  
3. Foreign spouse’s identity docs, prior immigration history, and any children on the same household plan  
4. Optional but common: months/years of LTVP residence, tax filings if working, coherent family profile  
5. Then the spouse-sponsored PR application  

ICA’s published assessment themes still apply: family ties, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, length of residency, ability to integrate, and commitment to sinking roots. “We have been married X years” is one narrative input — not a published points table.

## Fees and clocks ICA publishes (verify live)

| Stage | Published fee / clock |
| --- | --- |
| At submission | **S$100** per application (non-refundable) |
| At completion of formalities | **S$20** Entry Permit + **S$50** for a 5-year Re-Entry Permit + **S$50** Singapore Identity Card (when issued) |
| Processing | About **6 months** when documents are in order; some files take longer |
| Outcome channel | Email / MyICA (older postal outcomes for pre-26 June 2024 submissions) |

Payment rails ICA lists: Visa/MasterCard, AMEX, selected Singapore internet banking, or PayNow.

## Documents — follow the checklist, not the agency binder

Upload **certified true copies** plus official English translations where needed. ICA accepts embassy translations, Singapore/overseas notary translations, and privately translated docs attested by the embassy or a notary — it does **not** endorse commercial PR agencies.

Reddit fights about **recommendation letters, MP letters, and volunteering certificates** that ICA did not ask for. Safer rule: submit what the live checklist and form request. Extra unsolicited bundles do not create a published scoring bonus; incomplete required docs can stall or bounce the file.

If circumstances change while pending (new job, birth, property, salary jump), prefer the **in-application upload** path when open; otherwise write ICA with the **application reference** — see [PR rejection / reapply](/guides/pr-rejection-reapply). Do not open a second concurrent PR application out of anxiety.

## After IPA — same formalities project as any PR

In-principle approval is not the finish line. Book biometrics, complete medical / MediShield steps the IPA names, print Entry Permit + REP, collect the IC, and re-register Singpass on the new NRIC — full day-one stack on [after getting PR](/guides/after-getting-pr-checklist). Travel on “almost PR” folklore while formalities are open is how people earn secondary inspection.

If the foreign spouse later sponsors children, fold [child PR](/guides/pr-for-child-newborn-singapore) and NS literacy into the same household plan. Divorce / pass-collapse anxiety sits on [Family’s divorce guide](/guides/divorce-custody-foreigners-singapore) — do not treat PR as a substitute for counsel when sponsorship is failing.

## Rejections and try #2

Spouse sponsorship refusals are common when marriage length is short, the household profile is thin, or nothing material changed since the last file. ICA does not publish individual refusal reasons. Use the official **appeal e-Service** when you have grounds; otherwise wait until circumstances actually change before paying another S$100 — [appeal / reapply playbook](/guides/pr-rejection-reapply).

## Decision rule

Sponsor spouse PR when the **relationship + household roots story** is coherent and documents match ICA’s checklist — not the week after ROM because a group chat said so. Use LTVP / PLOC for stay-and-work bridging when that is the published path, treat agents as form-fillers at best, and budget six months of patience after you click submit.
