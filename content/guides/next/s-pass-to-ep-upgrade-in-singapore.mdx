**Short answer:** Going from an S Pass to an EP means a fresh MOM application, not a switch HR flips after your raise comes through. Stay in status until you have either an IPA or a written Plan B.

The parent guides are [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore) and [EP vs S Pass](/guides/ep-vs-s-pass-comparison). This one is the in-country upgrade calendar, the bit Reddit collapses into "HR will convert me after the raise."

## A raise isn't a conversion

An S Pass doesn't quietly become an Employment Pass the moment payroll clears the EP age-band floors. The EP is a new application in its own right: Stage 1 salary, [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) unless you're exempt, and [Fair Consideration](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) advertising unless an exemption applies.

The good news is that you can usually stay in Singapore and keep working while that file is with MOM, as long as your S Pass itself is still valid. That's continuity of stay. It is not a guaranteed IPA, and the difference between those two things is where people get hurt.

Here are the four things you'll hear that aren't true.

| What you'll hear | What's actually going on |
| --- | --- |
| "There's no EP quota, so I'm stuck on S Pass forever" | The EP has no foreign-worker quota at all. Ask whether HR really means COMPASS risk, salary budget, or the S Pass DRC |
| "The promotion auto-files the EP" | Someone still has to advertise where required, score COMPASS, and actually submit |
| "If the EP is refused I have to leave tomorrow" | Not while the S Pass is valid. Keep it that way until the gap plan is in writing |
| "The family's DPs upgrade with me" | They don't. Dependants still need the right principal and salary story: [Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) |

## What to check with your employer before you celebrate

Work through these with HR while the mood is still good, because the answers are much harder to get once a file has been submitted badly.

1. Your fixed monthly salary against the live EP age-band floor. Not "total package", and not last year's number.
2. The MyCareersFuture and FCF clock, unless you're exempt. Screenshot the advertising window while it's up.
3. The COMPASS firm-level and candidate-level inputs. Don't let anyone invent points from an old offer letter.
4. A written plan for a refusal: stay on the S Pass, retitle the role, or [pivot](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) to another sponsor.
5. If you're changing employer at the same time, stop treating this as an upgrade and treat it as a fresh EP hire, with all the sequencing that implies: [IPA before you resign](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore).

## The rule to hold onto

**S Pass to EP is a new MOM file, not a payroll toggle.** Stay employed and in status until the IPA lands, or until you've deliberately chosen a Plan B and have it in writing.
