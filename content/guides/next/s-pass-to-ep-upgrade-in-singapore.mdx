## Raise ≠ conversion

An S Pass does **not** morph into an Employment Pass when payroll clears EP age-band floors. EP is a **new application**: Stage 1 salary, [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) (unless exempt), and [Fair Consideration](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) advertising unless an exemption applies. You can usually stay in Singapore on a **valid S Pass** while the EP files — that is not the same as a guaranteed IPA.

| Claim | Reality check |
| --- | --- |
| “No EP quota, so stuck on S Pass forever” | EP has **no** foreign-worker quota — ask whether HR means COMPASS risk, salary budget, or S Pass DRC |
| “Promotion auto-files EP” | Someone still has to advertise (if required), score COMPASS, and submit |
| “Refuse EP and I must leave tomorrow” | Keep the S Pass valid until you have a written gap plan — do not cancel early |
| “Family DP upgrades automatically” | Dependants still need the right principal + salary story — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) |

## Employer checklist before you celebrate

1. Fixed monthly salary vs **live** EP age-band floor (not “total package”).  
2. MyCareersFuture / FCF clock unless exempt — screenshot the ad window.  
3. COMPASS firm + candidate inputs — do not invent points from old offers.  
4. Written plan if EP is refused: remain on S Pass, retitle, or [career pivot](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) to another sponsor.  
5. If you are also changing employer, treat it as a fresh EP hire calendar — [IPA-before-resign](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore).

## Decision rule

**S Pass → EP is a new MOM file, not a payroll toggle.** Stay employed and in status until IPA (or a deliberate Plan B) is in writing.
