r/askSingapore’s “can I keep Mum’s address for vouchers / voting?” threads treat NRIC address as optional cosmetics. For SC and PR **Identity Card** holders it is a **legal report** — including when the new home is overseas.

## Who must update, and by when

| Rule ICA publishes | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who | All **IC holders** (SC and PR), including those residing **overseas** |
| Clock | Report within **28 days** of moving into the new local **or overseas** residence |
| Kids without IC | SC/PR children **not yet issued** an IC do **not** use this e-Service for address |

This is **not** the MOM work-pass address update path, and not [loss replacement](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) or [30/55 re-registration](/guides/nric-reregistration-30-55-singapore).

<OfficialSource>
[ICA — Change of Residential Address on Identity Card](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/update_residential_address). Citizens who move overseas and need electoral rolls: [Elections Department](https://www.eld.gov.sg/) Local Contact Address (LCA).
</OfficialSource>

## How eCOA actually runs

1. Log in with **Singpass**.  
2. File for yourself, or as a **group** (you + up to **four** sub-applicants). Sub-applicants get an SMS from `gov.sg` and must **consent within 5 days** on the e-Service.  
3. ICA posts a **PIN mailer** to the **new** address for the main applicant to verify online.  
4. After success, ICA mails **address stickers** — paste on the **back** of the IC (ICA publishes a language-selectable how-to video on the same page).  
5. **Fee:** none for the address registration itself.

If you cannot complete the e-Service, ICA’s page says to contact them for help — not to “just wait for the next IC.”

### Mid-replacement / re-registration overlap

If you already filed a **replacement** or **30/55 re-registration** and put the new address on that form, you still get the PIN mailer. The IC application proceeds only after PIN verification — and you receive a **new IC with the address printed**, not a sticker on the old card.

## Overseas address, REP, and voting

Reporting an overseas residential address on the IC is a published path. It is **not** a clever REP hack — thin Singapore ties plus an overseas IC address is how peers walk into harder [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) conversations.

**Citizens** who change to an overseas residential address on the IC must register a **Local Contact Address (LCA)** with the **Elections Department** if they want their name entered or retained on the Registers of Electors. That is an ELD Singpass flow, not an ICA sticker.

## Why “don’t update” is a bad strategy

Peers skip updates for voucher shopping, old-constituency voting folklore, or landlord drama. ICA has publicly prosecuted failure to report a change of residential address under the National Registration Act — treat the **28-day** clock as real. There is **no** published fee for doing it correctly.

## Decision rule

Move in → start eCOA inside **28 days** → verify the PIN → stick the label (or finish the mid-IC application). Use group filing for the household, register ELD LCA if you are an SC overseas, and never invent a “keep old address for REP / vouchers” exception ICA does not publish.
