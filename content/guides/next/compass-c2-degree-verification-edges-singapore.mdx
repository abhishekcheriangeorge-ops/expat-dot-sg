## Brand ≠ awarding body

C2 scores degree-equivalent credibility against MOM’s published bands (top-tier list vs other recognised degree-equivalent vs none). The **awarding body printed on the certificate** matters more than the campus brand on LinkedIn.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Same university brand overseas = 20 points” | Only if the awarding body matches the live top-tier list — campus speculation is risky |
| “Professional diploma equals degree points” | Many diplomas score **0** C2 — verify before resigning |
| “HR can fix C2 after IPA query” | Fix the pack **before** filing; queries delay clocks |
| “SAT green means C2 is locked forever” | SAT is a planning tool — MOM can still reassess documents |

## Verification checklist

1. Read the certificate’s awarding body line — not the marketing campus name.  
2. Cross-check against MOM’s live top-tier / recognised lists via employer SAT.  
3. Prepare attestation / translation early if the document is not English.  
4. If C2 is weak, rebuild points from C1 fixed salary or firm attributes — [C1 packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore).  
5. Do not resign on “my uni is famous” folklore.

## Decision rule

**Score C2 on the awarding body MOM can verify — campus brand folklore is not a 20-point guarantee.**
