The parent map is [Notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport). This companion is the edge depth: what happens when the five-day address and mobile clock, a landlord dragging their feet on FWTES, and the passport auto-gate window all land in the same week as a flight.

## The clock doesn't pause for your trip

MOM wants your residential address or mobile number updated within **5 days**. Passport particulars go into the EP eService, and the automated gates then typically need about 4 working days to catch up afterwards. Those two windows run independently of each other, which is the part people miss. Put a house move, a passport renewed overseas and a long-haul flight into the same week and you've produced the classic "surely MOM should just know by now" post.

| Edge | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| Renew passport overseas, fly home next day | Update eService immediately; expect manual lanes until the auto-gate window clears |
| Move into a condo while landlord stalls FWTES | The five-day clock still runs on you, so escalate owner consent early |
| DP / family address "should sync" | Dependant files don't inherit EP address updates; check each pass rail |
| Hotel / Airbnb as temporary EP address | Often refused, so use a declare-able stay ([serviced apartment](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment)) |

## The order that keeps you out of trouble

Do the address and passport updates before the flight that needs automated gates, not after it. Then save or print the EP eService acknowledgements, because banks and landlords still ask for them and digging one out months later is a chore you don't need.

If FWTES is holding up the address because the landlord hasn't given consent, the five-day clock is still running against you rather than against them. That's unfair and it's also how it works, so escalate in writing early and keep the trail. Written escalation is what lets you show you stayed inside the five-day story when the delay wasn't yours.

A lost or stolen card is a different rail entirely: [lost / replace work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card). And keep the address your bank holds for KYC consistent with the one MOM holds, which is easier to do at the time of the move than to unpick later. See [banking](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat) and the [relocation checklist](/guides/relocation-checklist-7-30-90).

## The rule to remember

Update address and mobile inside five days, and sort the passport before you need the auto-gates. Travel, a slow landlord and family passes that "should sync" are not exemptions from EP eService.
