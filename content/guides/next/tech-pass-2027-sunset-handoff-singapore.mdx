If you've come from [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore) or the [PEP vs ONE vs Tech.Pass chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore), this is the companion piece: the 2027 sunset handoff calendar. It's written for holders who've quietly started treating Tech.Pass as a permanent arrangement, which is an easy thing to do when the pass has worked well for years.

## The sunset is a planning problem, not a vibe

EDB and MOM communications around Tech.Pass set a finite application and renewal horizon into 2027. The date most commonly discussed is renewals winding down toward **28 Jan 2027**, and you should verify that on the live EDB and MOM pages rather than trusting this page or a forum post from two years ago.

Here's why it bites harder than a date usually does. The failure mode isn't missing a deadline by a week. It's reaching the final quarter and finding that every alternative needs months of runway you no longer have: ONE Pass salary floors, PEP keep rules and sponsored-EP COMPASS scoring are all things you build toward over time, not switches you flip when the old pass runs out.

## How to sequence the handoff

1. Put the live Tech.Pass application and renewal cut-off in your calendar, taken from the official pages rather than from what you remember someone saying.
2. Score yourself honestly against ONE Pass, PEP and a sponsored EP. The [chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore) exists so you can do that without flattering yourself.
3. If an employer-sponsored EP is Plan B, start the COMPASS and FCF conversations months out, because your prospective employer needs lead time too ([COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained)).
4. Don't time a resignation to the sunset month unless the next pass has actually been issued.
5. Keep the family-pass knock-ons in the same calendar as your own handoff. Dependants move when you move, and they move slower.

## The decision rule

Treat 2027 as a hard planning fence. Pick the successor product while Tech.Pass is still renewable, not after the window has become a screenshot of a dead FAQ.
