**Short answer:** **Treat 2027 as a hard fence.** Pick ONE, PEP, or sponsored EP while Tech.Pass is still renewable — not after the FAQ dies.

Parents: [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore) · [PEP vs ONE vs Tech.Pass chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore). This companion is the **2027 sunset handoff** calendar — where holders treat Tech.Pass like a forever card until the programme window is gone.

## Sunset is a planning problem, not a vibe

EDB/MOM communications around Tech.Pass include a **finite application / renewal horizon into 2027** (commonly discussed as renewals winding down toward **28 Jan 2027** — **verify live** on EDB/MOM pages). The failure mode is waiting until the last quarter, then discovering ONE Pass salary floors, PEP keep rules, or sponsored EP COMPASS need months of runway.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “They’ll extend Tech.Pass again” | Plan as if the published sunset holds |
| “I’ll just flip to PEP in a weekend” | PEP has its own high-earner bar + [keep-rule evidence](/guides/pep-keep-rule-income-evidence-singapore) |
| “ONE Pass is automatic for Tech.Pass holders” | ONE Pass has its own salary / achievements tests — [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) |
| “Sponsored EP is a demotion, ignore it” | Employer-sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) is often the realistic bridge |

## Handoff sequencing (orientation)

1. Diary the **live** Tech.Pass application/renewal cut-off — not forum memory.  
2. Score yourself honestly against ONE Pass vs PEP vs sponsored EP — use the [chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore).  
3. If employer-sponsored EP is Plan B, start COMPASS / FCF conversations **months** out — [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained).  
4. Do not time a resignation to the sunset month without an issued next pass.  
5. Keep family-pass knock-ons in the same calendar as the principal handoff.

## Decision rule

**Treat 2027 as a hard planning fence.** Pick the successor product while Tech.Pass is still renewable — not after the window is a screenshot of a dead FAQ.
