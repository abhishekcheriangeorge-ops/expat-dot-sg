The "I paid S$35 then changed my mind" and "ICA says originals not received" threads on r/askSingapore all sit after the eligibility decision has already been made. Who can renounce, the NS gates, and how ORAL differs from renunciation are on [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship). This page is the layer underneath that: the portal, the cooling-off choice, and the originals.

## What you upload vs what you must post back

ICA splits the pack in two, and the split is where most of the trouble starts. Some documents are digital uploads. Others are **originals that must physically reach ICA before processing starts**, which means the clock you think began when you paid hasn't begun at all until an envelope lands.

The certified true copies you upload are the foreign citizenship certificate, a valid foreign passport if you've obtained one, and any deed poll, name-change or marriage documents if the name changed along the way.

Then there are the mandatory forms, which you upload and then return in original: the notarised Statutory Declaration of Renunciation, the Advisory Note on Renunciation Implication, and, for males, the MINDEF Advisory Note.

The originals to return are the Singapore IC, a valid Singapore passport, the Singapore Citizenship Certificate where that applies, and the original mandatory forms above.

Then there's the portal itself. ICA's user guide says the online form takes around 30 minutes and has **no save function**. You can't come back to it tomorrow. So assemble the certified copies and the notarised forms before you click start, because the alternative is doing the whole thing again from the top.

## Cooling-off: an optional hold with hard edges

You can select a cooling-off period of up to 3 months so that processing doesn't start immediately. It's a real feature and it's worth using if you're not certain. The edges are firmer than people expect.

During the hold, the application simply sits. The processing clock starts after the hold, and after the originals have arrived.

There is no extension. You pick the hold once, and that's the decision made.

Withdrawing before processing is possible, which in practice means during cooling-off or before processing has otherwise begun. The fee is generally not refunded even then.

Withdrawing after processing is a different matter. ICA's position is that a withdrawal request will not succeed once the renunciation has already been processed. That is the sentence worth reading twice before you pay anything.

Cooling-off, in other words, is a rethink buffer and not a way to park your passport indefinitely. If the foreign naturalisation proof is still shaky, fix it before you pay rather than during the hold. ask.gov.sg still requires documents showing that you are, or are about to become, a citizen somewhere else.

## Fee and processing clocks

The fee is S$35, collected online at application by card or PayNow. General processing runs about 3 months from receipt of all required documents, and "all" is carrying real weight in that sentence. If you selected a cooling-off period, processing takes longer by exactly the hold you chose.

Keep your postage evidence. ICA may ask for tracking or delivery status for originals you've mailed, and "I posted it" is not a delivery status.

Overseas applicants use the same portal. Missions can notarise, and may accept originals for onward transmission, but that adds courier and travel time on top of the three-month orientation rather than absorbing any of it.

## Myths this companion kills

Uploading the PDFs is not enough and the originals are not optional. Processing waits on originals received, full stop.

Cooling-off is not an automatic 3 months. You select it, and the default is not a free hold.

You generally do not get the S$35 back if you withdraw, so treat it as spent the moment it leaves your account.

This is not the same as renouncing PR. That's a different product on a different rail — [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore).

And missing ORAL is not the same paperwork. Automatic SC loss at 22 is a different thing from a registered renunciation — [ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21).

## The short version

Notarise and assemble the full pack before you open the no-save form. Decide on cooling-off with a calendar in front of you, allowing for the foreign citizenship proof and the travel or courier time the originals need. Send the originals with tracking. Treat the S$35 as spent once paid.
