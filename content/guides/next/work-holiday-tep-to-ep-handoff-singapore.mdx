Parent map: [Work Holiday vs Training EP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass). This companion is the **handoff to a full work pass** — where interns treat WHP/TEP tenure as a stealth EP conversion clock.

## Short runway ≠ conversion product

**Work Holiday Programme** and **Training Employment Pass** are time-boxed rails. An Employment Pass is a **new sponsored file** (Stage 1 salary + [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) unless exempt, usually [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep)). TEP is commonly **non-renewable as TEP** — convert or leave.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “WHP months auto-count toward EP” | No auto-convert — start a fresh EP/S Pass calendar |
| “TEP extends if the manager likes me” | TEP is short and generally **not** renewable as TEP — verify live MOM key facts |
| “Skip FCF because I already train here” | Training history ≠ published FCF exemption |
| “I’ll finish on STVP and file later” | Visit-pass work is illegal folklore — stage the EP **before** WHP/TEP dies |

## Handoff checklist

1. Put the WHP/TEP end date on a shared calendar with whoever files the EP — leave buffer for FCF’s [14-day clock](/guides/fcf-mycareersfuture-14-day-clock-ep-singapore) unless exempt.  
2. Confirm fixed monthly salary vs live EP age-band floor and COMPASS inputs — training stipend folklore is not Stage 1.  
3. Keep WHP/TEP valid until EP is **issued** (not merely IPA’d) unless MOM instructs otherwise in writing.  
4. If EP will miss, map [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore) or exit / [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) — do not invent a visit-pass internship.  
5. Student’s Pass holders: do not mix holiday/work rails — [Student’s Pass](/guides/student-pass-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Treat WHP/TEP as a runway to a full sponsored file — not as a conversion button MOM forgot to publish.**
