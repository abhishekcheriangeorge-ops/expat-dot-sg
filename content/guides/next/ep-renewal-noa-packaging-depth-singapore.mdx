## MOM is reading three stories at once

An NOA request usually means MOM is cross-checking **what HR declared on the EP**, **what payroll paid as fixed monthly**, and **what IRAS assessed**. Uploading a crisp PDF does not fix a broken story.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “They just want any tax PDF” | Prefer the **NOA** years named in the request — IR8A alone is often not enough |
| “Bonus-heavy years look stronger” | Stage-1 / COMPASS care about **fixed monthly**; bonus spikes can widen the gap |
| “Partial-year join explains everything” | Only if HR’s cover note matches join date, pro-rata pay, and IRAS figures |
| “Cut salary quietly; renew later” | Un-notified cuts are the trap — [notify / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes) |

## Packaging checklist before HR hits submit

1. Pull the exact NOA year(s) named in MOM’s letter — not “whatever is in my inbox.”  
2. Line up **fixed monthly salary on the EP** vs payroll letters vs IRAS assessable income.  
3. Separate AWS / variable bonus / RSUs from fixed monthly — [C1 packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore) and [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding).  
4. If you took a cut mid-term, confirm whether MOM was notified before you invent a reconciliation paragraph.  
5. Let **employer / EA** upload the reply in myMOM — you supply documents; you do not casework MOM directly.

## Decision rule

**If EP fixed monthly, payroll, and NOA cannot be explained in one factual paragraph, fix the salary / notify path first — do not “package” your way past a Stage-1 floor problem.**
