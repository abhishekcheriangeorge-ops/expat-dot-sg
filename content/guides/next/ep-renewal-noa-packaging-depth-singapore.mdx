Start with the parent map, [EP renewal NOA documents](/guides/ep-renewal-noa-documents), and the layer above it, [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy). What follows is the reconciliation depth: the bit for when MOM asks for an NOA and everyone in the chain treats it as a scan-quality problem, when what's really being asked is a question about your salary story.

## MOM is reading three stories at once

An NOA request usually means MOM is holding three versions of your pay up against each other. What HR declared on the EP. What payroll actually paid as fixed monthly. What IRAS assessed. Uploading a crisper PDF does nothing to make those three agree, and if they don't agree, the disagreement is the whole point of the request.

| What people assume | What's safer to assume |
| --- | --- |
| "They just want any tax PDF" | Send the NOA years the request names. IR8A on its own is often not enough |
| "A bonus-heavy year looks stronger" | Stage-1 and COMPASS care about fixed monthly. A bonus spike can widen the gap rather than close it |
| "A partial-year join explains everything" | Only if HR's cover note lines up with the join date, the pro-rata pay and the IRAS figures |
| "Cut the salary quietly, renew later" | An un-notified cut is the real trap — [notify / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes) |

## What to line up before HR hits submit

1. Pull the exact NOA year or years named in MOM's letter, rather than whatever tax document happens to be in your inbox
2. Put the fixed monthly salary declared on the EP next to the payroll letters and the IRAS assessable income, and see for yourself whether they tell the same story
3. Separate AWS, variable bonus and RSUs out from fixed monthly, because they aren't read the same way ([C1 packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore), [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding))
4. If you took a cut mid-term, find out whether MOM was actually notified before you draft a reconciliation paragraph that quietly assumes it was
5. Let the employer or the EA file the reply in myMOM. You supply the documents; you don't casework your own file with MOM

## The short version

**If the fixed monthly on your EP, your payroll and your NOA can't be explained in one factual paragraph, you don't have a packaging problem.** Fix the salary or the notification path first. Nobody has ever formatted their way past a Stage-1 floor.
