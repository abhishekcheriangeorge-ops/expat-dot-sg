## Cancel clock ≠ civil clawback

Lawful resignation with notice stops HR from trapping your pass by “rejecting” the letter. It does **not** delete a signed clawback. Unilateral cuts from final salary for “bond” still have to fit **Employment Act allowable deductions** / required written agreement — contested amounts are a civil / [TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom) fight, not a MOM IPA feature.

| HR move | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “We’ll deduct the bond from final pay” | Ask for the **allowable deduction** category and written consent trail |
| “No cancel until you pay” | MOM cancel follows employment end / employer process — not a private debt collection switch |
| “Remaining 18 months’ salary is training cost” | Scarecrow penalties ≠ named invoice-backed training spend — [clause patterns](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep) |
| “New IPA means old bond dies” | Pass sequencing ≠ civil waiver — get waivers in writing |

## Deduction-edge checklist

1. Demand itemised training invoices vs a lump “bond” number before last payday.  
2. Put deduction objections in writing; keep the full final-pay slip.  
3. Sequence resign around [IPA-before-resign](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore) — do not trade pass panic for an illegal cut.  
4. If salary is short-paid, use [TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom) rails for unpaid wages separately from civil bond counsel.  
5. After cancel, [STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) is stay only — not a work bridge while you fight invoices.

## Decision rule

**Treat final-pay bond cuts as an Employment Act / civil problem — MOM cancel and a new IPA do not authorise payroll self-help.**
