**Short answer:** Renew parent LTVP **≥3 months** early, expect **no leftover-day carry**, and **notify ICA** early if the sponsor cannot continue — backup SC/PR needs approval.

r/askSingapore’s “Mum’s LTVP expires next month” threads confuse **first issue**, **renewal**, and **what happens if the sponsoring child emigrates**. Product chooser (SC-only aged-parent PR vs SC/PR parent LTVP) lives on [aged parents PR vs LTVP](/guides/aged-parents-pr-ltvp-singapore). This companion is the **renewal + sponsor-change** layer for **parent LTVP**.

Parents-in-law are still **not** on the parent LTVP row — do not file under “parent” folklore.

## Renewal clocks ICA publishes

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When to file | At least **3 months** before current LTVP expiry |
| New validity | Starts from **issuance** (after issuance fee) |
| Leftover days | **Not** carried forward onto the renewed pass |
| Fees (verify live) | Same family as first issue: **S$45** submission · **S$60** issuance · **S$30** MJV if applicable |
| Processing | Most within **~6 weeks** when documents are in order; some longer |

Sponsor login: if the SC/PR child is **21+**, that child sponsors via Singpass. If the child is **under 21**, a local SC/PR sponsor aged 21+ is required — same shape as first issue on ICA’s parent LTVP page.

## Sponsor withdraws / wants out

ICA’s published LTVP terms (verify live PDF) are clearer than forum panic:

- The LTVP is issued on the basis of a **local sponsor**.  
- If the sponsor intends to **withdraw sponsorship of renewals**, you must **notify ICA**.  
- Another **SC or PR** may sponsor, **subject to ICA’s approval**.  
- A sponsor generally **cannot unilaterally cancel** the LTVP without the holder’s consent — ICA expects **both** consents to cancel — **and** ICA may still cancel under the Immigration Regulations at its discretion.

Do **not** assume Mum’s pass silently survives after the sponsoring child leaves Singapore, renounces PR, or dies. Treat sponsor loss as a **same-week** ICA contact + backup-sponsor conversation, not a “finish the year then renew” problem.

## Medical / housing realism still applies

Renewal does not create subsidised citizen healthcare or HDB purchase rights. Re-check insurance and caregiver capacity before you treat another year of LTVP as free — [health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat). If the household’s plan is exit, run the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) for the parent’s stay rail too.

Aged-parent **PR** (SC sponsor only) remains a separate discretionary file — see the parent chooser guide. Rejections and appeals sit on ICA’s appeal rails, not agent WhatsApp.

## Decision rule

**Diary renewal ≥3 months out → no leftover-validity myth → notify ICA early if the sponsor cannot continue → line up another SC/PR only with ICA approval.** Parent product chooser stays on [aged parents PR vs LTVP](/guides/aged-parents-pr-ltvp-singapore).
