## Encashment day ≠ clearance folklore

Unused annual leave **encashment**, taking AL during notice, and mutual **offset** of notice are three different deals. IR21 tax clearance for foreign employees is a **fourth** calendar — employers often need clearance before releasing final pay. “We’ll encash leave after you fly” is how people miss both MOM salary timing and IRAS clearance hygiene.

| Edge | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “AL offset auto-moves my last day for IR21” | Offset needs **mutual** agreement — get the last day in writing |
| “Encashment can wait until next payroll cycle abroad” | Align encashment with final pay / clearance — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) |
| “MC extends notice so IR21 waits” | For covered employees, MC during notice usually **counts toward** notice — verify live MOM text |
| “Underpaid encashment is an immigration issue” | It is a salary / [TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom) fight, not a pass IPA feature |

## Exit checklist

1. Screenshot AL balance **before** you resign; ask HR which pattern they mean: encash / AL-during-notice / offset.  
2. Put the **last day** and encashment amount in the same email as the IR21 timeline.  
3. Do not trade a clean last day for verbal “pay leave later.”  
4. Keep pass cancel / [STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) separate from leave math.  
5. Job-change exits still want IPA before resign — [IPA-before-resign calendar](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Lock encashment, last day, and IR21 in writing together — leave folklore does not move tax clearance by itself.**
