## New IPA does not erase IR21

Leaving an employer usually triggers **Form IR21** and final-pay withholding until IRAS clears tax — even if your next EP IPA is already in hand. “I’m not leaving Singapore” is not a free pass out of clearance maths.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Same country job hop = no IR21” | Employer change still commonly means clearance / withhold clocks |
| “New HR can unlock my old final pay” | Old employer + IRAS own the clearance; budget a cash bridge |
| “Cancel old EP the day new IPA arrives” | Sequence cancel / STVP / issuance carefully — [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |
| “Dependants are fine through the gap” | DPs ride the principal — plan household STVP asks in writing |

## Resign-week checklist

1. Get **new IPA** (or written start constraints) before burning notice when possible.  
2. Ask old HR for the **IR21 / withhold** timeline in email — not Slack vibes.  
3. Buffer rent/school fees for the freeze window; RSUs/bonuses need their own line items.  
4. Align cancel day with household STVP requests for every DP.  
5. After issuance, reset address/passport hygiene — [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport).

## Decision rule

**Treat IR21 withholding as a cash-flow problem parallel to the new EP file — never assume a same-city job hop skips clearance or keeps dependants covered without a written STVP plan.**
