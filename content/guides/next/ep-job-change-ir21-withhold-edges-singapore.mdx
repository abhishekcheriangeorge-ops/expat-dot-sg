What follows is the IR21 withholding detail for the case people assume is the easy one: a job hop that stays inside Singapore.

## A new IPA doesn’t erase IR21

Leaving an employer usually triggers **Form IR21** and the withholding of your final pay until IRAS clears your tax. It does that even when the IPA for your next EP is already sitting in your inbox. “But I’m not leaving Singapore” feels like it ought to matter, and it doesn’t, because what triggers clearance is the end of your employment with that employer rather than your departure from the country.

| What people believe | The safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Same country job hop, so no IR21” | Changing employer still commonly means clearance and withholding clocks |
| “My new HR can unlock the old final pay” | It isn’t theirs to unlock. Your old employer and IRAS own the clearance, so budget a cash bridge |
| “Cancel the old EP the day the new IPA arrives” | Sequence the cancellation, any STVP, and the issuance carefully — [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) |
| “Dependants are fine through the gap” | DPs ride on the principal pass. Plan the household STVP asks, in writing |

## What to do in resign week

1. Get the new IPA in hand, or at least written start constraints, before you burn your notice, where the timing lets you.
2. Ask your old HR for the IR21 and withholding timeline **in email**, not as Slack vibes you can’t refer back to in six weeks.
3. Buffer rent and school fees for the freeze window. RSUs and bonuses need their own line items, because they don’t behave like salary.
4. Line up your cancel day with the household STVP requests for every DP, not just your own.
5. Once the new pass issues, reset your address and passport hygiene; [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) has the details.

## The decision rule

Treat IR21 withholding as a cash-flow problem running in parallel with the new EP file, rather than something the new job quietly absorbs. A job hop across town doesn’t skip clearance, and it doesn’t keep your dependants covered unless somebody has actually written the STVP plan down.
