**Short answer:** Work out what's actually blocking your household — the salary band, whether the pass renews, whether your spouse can work, or the Tech.Pass sunset in 2027 — and pick the product that solves that one thing. If your EP renewals look clean, the answer is usually to stay where you are.

The "just get PEP" advice you'll see on r/askSingapore quietly treats three quite different products as interchangeable. They aren't. We've written each one up properly: [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep), [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore), and [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore). This page is just the chooser that sits above them.

## How they actually differ (verify the live numbers)

| Gate | PEP | ONE Pass | Tech.Pass (until 2027) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical salary track | ≥ S$22,500/mo fixed, with a keep-rule around S$270k/year | ≥ S$30,000/mo from one employer, or the outstanding-achievements path | ≥ S$22,500/mo plus leading-role and company-scale / VC tests |
| Tied to one employer? | No, it's personalised | No, it's personalised | No. Multi-activity by design |
| Renewable? | Generally one-shot | Yes, commonly discussed as 5-year terms | Renewals stay open until the 28 Jan 2027 sunset |
| Spouse work LOC? | Not the EP-style LOC story people are hoping for | Yes: DP spouse plus LOC, one LOC at a time | Check the live EDB and MOM family rules rather than importing EP LOC folklore |
| Best reason to want it | A job-hop or layoff buffer while you still clear the high-earner floors | Top talent with a dual-career household that needs the LOC | Tech founder or multi-hat operator who can evidence company scale |

If you're on an ordinary mid-level EP and renewals look clean, the boring answer is the right one: stay on the EP. PEP is generally one-shot, so spending it on flexibility you don't currently need leaves you with nothing to renew on the day you do.

## Decision shortcuts

If the real problem is a dual-career spouse who needs employer work rights, stress-test ONE Pass honestly before you build a plan on it. Not every senior engineer clears S$30k a month, and the outstanding-achievements route is a genuine assessment rather than a formality.

If you're a high earner who moves between employers and has a PR thesis inside roughly 3 years, PEP is the product that fits. Plan the exit to EP, PR, or ONE Pass early, though, because there is no second PEP waiting behind the first.

If you're in a leading tech role with large-company or VC proof and no local sponsor lined up, Tech.Pass is worth filing while EDB still accepts it, with a ONE Pass (AI & Tech) handoff planned before the 2027 cutoff rather than after it.

If you're in an ordinary sponsored role and renewals are fine, stay on the [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) and spend the energy elsewhere.

And if you're a founder or innovator with ≥30% plus funding, IP, or an incubator behind you, that's [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) territory. Shareholder-directors are PEP-ineligible, so PEP was never on the table for you.

## Getting the order right

**Don't cancel the live EP, Tech.Pass, or PEP until the next IPA is ready to issue.** A gap between two products isn't a sabbatical, whatever it feels like from the inside. It's unemployment, and it carries all the pass consequences that come with that, which we've laid out in [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## Decision rule

Match the product to the household blocker, whether that's the salary band, renewability, the spouse LOC, or the 2027 sunset. Then read the parent guide end to end before you resign on the strength of "we'll put you on ONE Pass next month." That sentence is easy for a hiring manager to say and much harder for them to deliver.
