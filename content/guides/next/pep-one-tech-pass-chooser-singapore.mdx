**Short answer:** **Match the product to the household blocker** — salary band, renewability, spouse LOC, and the Tech.Pass 2027 sunset — then stay on EP if renewals are fine.

r/askSingapore “just get PEP” threads collapse three **different** personalised products. Parent deep-dives: [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep), [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore), [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore). This companion is the **chooser**.

## Side-by-side (verify live)

| Gate | PEP | ONE Pass | Tech.Pass (until 2027) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical salary track | ≥ **S$22,500**/mo fixed; keep-rule ~**S$270k**/year | ≥ **S$30,000**/mo from **one** employer (or outstanding-achievements path) | ≥ **S$22,500**/mo **plus** leading-role + company-scale / VC tests |
| Tied to one employer? | No (personalised) | No (personalised) | No (multi-activity by design) |
| Renewable? | Generally **one-shot** | **Yes** (commonly discussed as 5-year terms) | Renewals open until **28 Jan 2027** sunset |
| Spouse work LOC? | Not the EP-style LOC story peers want | **Yes** — DP spouse + LOC (one LOC at a time) | Check live EDB/MOM family rules — do not invent EP LOC folklore |
| Best “why” | Job-hop / layoff buffer while you still clear high-earner floors | Top-talent + dual-career LOC household | Tech founder / multi-hat operator with company-scale proof |

Ordinary mid-level EP renewals that look clean should usually **stay on EP** — burning PEP for vibes is how people strand without a renew path.

## Decision shortcuts

| Your real problem | Lean toward |
| --- | --- |
| Dual-career spouse needs employer work rights | Stress-test **ONE Pass** salary / company / achievements honestly — not every senior engineer clears S$30k |
| High earner, multi-employer hops, PR thesis inside ~3 years | **PEP** — then plan exit to EP/PR/ONE early |
| Leading tech role + large-company / VC proof, no local sponsor yet | **Tech.Pass** while EDB still accepts filings; plan **ONE Pass (AI & Tech)** handoff before the 2027 cutoff |
| Ordinary sponsored role, renewals fine | Stay on [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) |
| Founder / innovator with ≥30% + funding / IP / incubator | [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) — not PEP (shareholder-directors are PEP-ineligible) |

## Sequencing hygiene

Do **not** cancel the live EP/Tech.Pass/PEP until the next IPA is ready to issue. Conversion gaps are unemployment, not a sabbatical — [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Match the product to the household blocker** (salary band, renewability, spouse LOC, 2027 sunset) — then read the parent guide end-to-end before you resign on “we’ll put you on ONE Pass next month.”
