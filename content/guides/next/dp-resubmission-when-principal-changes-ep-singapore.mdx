## Why DP does not auto-follow the new IPA

A Dependant’s Pass is tied to the **principal’s** pass and employer sponsorship story. A new EP is a **fresh** principal file — not a transfer stamp. When the old EP cancels, sponsored DPs / LTVPs typically fall with it unless the **new** employer has already re-filed and issued family passes (or you hold another lawful stay rail).

| Step | Principal | Family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New EP IPA in hand | Resign / serve notice | Do **not** assume old DP survives old cancel |
| New EP issued | Old employer cancels | New employer should already be filing / issuing DP/LTVP |
| Gap risk | STVP ask on cancel | Same-week family STVP / re-sponsor — get it **in writing** |

## Written asks for both HRs

1. New HR: exact **family re-sponsor** timeline vs my new EP issuance date  
2. Old HR: cancel date + **STVP length** requested for principal **and** dependants  
3. Whether any DP work LOC / school enrolment letters need re-issue after card change  
4. IR21 / final-pay freeze on the old employer — still fires when you stay in Singapore — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving)

FIN usually **persists**; cards and employer linkage do **not**.

## Household knock-ons

School term letters, tenancy diplomatic clauses, helper passes, and trailing-partner job search all assume continuous lawful stay — budget a bridge, not a same-day magic transfer. Spouse career rails: [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore). Stay-only after cancel: [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## Decision rule

**New principal IPA → family re-sponsor plan in writing → then burn the old EP.** Do not treat “FIN unchanged” as “DP auto-migrates.”
