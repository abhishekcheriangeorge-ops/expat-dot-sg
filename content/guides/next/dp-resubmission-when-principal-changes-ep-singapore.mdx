**Short answer:** Get the new EP's In-Principle Approval first, get the family re-sponsor plan in writing second, and only then let the old EP be cancelled. Keeping the same FIN does not mean your family's passes come along for the ride.

The parent guide for a job move is [changing jobs on an EP](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass), and the wider timing picture sits in [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore). This one is the layer underneath both: what happens to the family's passes while you move. It's the part that gets waved away on Reddit with "the FIN stays the same anyway."

## Why the DP doesn't just follow your new IPA

A Dependant's Pass hangs off the principal's pass and the sponsoring employer. That's the whole mechanism, and it's why nothing transfers. A new EP is a fresh principal file at a new company, not a transfer stamp on the old one. So when the old EP cancels, the DPs and LTVPs sponsored underneath it generally fall with it. The exceptions are narrow: the new employer has already re-filed and had the family passes issued, or someone in the household holds a lawful stay rail of their own.

| Step | Principal | Family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New EP IPA in hand | Resign, serve notice | Don't assume the old DP survives the old cancel |
| New EP issued | Old employer cancels | New employer should already be filing or issuing the DP/LTVP |
| Gap risk | Ask for an STVP at cancel | Same-week family STVP or re-sponsor, and get it in writing |

## What to ask both HR teams, in writing

Four questions, and you want them answered by email rather than in a corridor, because the answers are what you'll be planning the household around.

1. New HR: what's the family re-sponsor timeline, measured against your own EP issuance date?
2. Old HR: what's the cancel date, and what STVP length are they requesting — for you and for your dependants?
3. Does any DP work Letter of Consent or school enrolment letter need re-issuing once the cards change?
4. What's the IR21 and final-pay position at the old employer? It still fires even though you're staying in Singapore, which surprises people every time. See [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving).

Your FIN will usually persist through all of this. The cards and the employer linkage behind them will not. Treat an unchanged FIN as continuity of identity, not continuity of pass, because immigration and HR systems are reading the linkage, not the number.

## What else is quietly depending on this

School term letters, the diplomatic clause in your tenancy, the helper's pass, your partner's job search — all of it assumes uninterrupted lawful stay for everyone in the household. None of those calendars flex easily, so budget for a bridge rather than a same-day handover that has to go perfectly. Spouse-side options are in [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore), and the stay-only situation after a cancel is covered in [between jobs and the STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## The rule to hold onto

New principal IPA, then a written family re-sponsor plan, and **only then** let the old EP go. "FIN unchanged" and "DP auto-migrates" are not the same sentence, however often they get typed as one.
