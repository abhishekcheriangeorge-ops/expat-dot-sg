**Short answer:** **Not SC/PR first** → then CPF close → Singapore bank disbursement (~**12 weeks**) — REP expiry is not a cash-out button.

Parent myths (EP never had ordinary CPF; Aussie-super pipe folklore): [CPF withdrawal when leaving](/guides/cpf-withdrawal-leaving-singapore). This companion is the **after renunciation** closure checklist — status change first, Board process second.

## Eligibility gate

| Status | Closure path? |
| --- | --- |
| Still SC or PR (even living overseas) | **No** full cash-out closure — ordinary member rules only |
| Ex-PR / ex-SC after ICA renunciation / loss completed | Eligible to **close** and transfer to a Board-registered bank account |
| EP / S Pass only (never PR) | Usually **nothing to close** — you were outside ordinary CPF |

REP expiry is immigration mobility, not a CPF button — [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).

## Closure sequence (orientation)

1. Finish **ICA** renunciation / loss of PR or citizenship  
2. Prefer applying **before** you leave Singapore (Singpass + bank letter path is cleaner)  
3. Ensure a **Singapore bank account** is registered with CPF Board for disbursement  
4. Apply online (or Form CPF-CA / mission certification if already abroad)  
5. Budget about **~12 weeks** average processing — verify live; thorough checks before disbursement  
6. After funds hit the bank, move money under **receiving-country** tax / retirement rules yourself — no CPF→super / 401k direct pipe  

## Before you renounce for the cash

Model MediShield / housing / future return options that vanish with PR. Returning ex-SC/PR can face **refund-with-interest** obligations for amounts previously withdrawn — read live Important Notes on the closure form. Nomination / estate while the account still exists: [wills & CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore). Exit cash map: [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs).

## Decision rule

**Not SC/PR → then close → then bank → then foreign retirement product (optional).** Do not renounce on Reddit timelines without modelling the non-cash losses of PR.
