The parent guide deals with the myths, chiefly that your EP was quietly paying into ordinary CPF, and the folklore about a pipe running straight from CPF into Australian super: [CPF withdrawal when leaving](/guides/cpf-withdrawal-leaving-singapore). This companion picks up afterwards, once you've actually renounced. The order matters more than people expect. Your status changes first, and only then does the Board's process start.

## Who can actually close an account

| Status | Closure path? |
| --- | --- |
| Still SC or PR, even if you're living overseas | No. There's no full cash-out closure, and you stay on ordinary member rules |
| Ex-PR or ex-SC, once ICA renunciation or loss has completed | You're eligible to close the account and have the balance transferred to a bank account registered with the Board |
| EP or S Pass only, and never PR | Usually nothing to close, because you were outside ordinary CPF the whole time |

The confusion worth naming here: an expired REP is an immigration question about your ability to return. It has no effect on your CPF account and it is not a cash-out button. More on that in [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).

## How the closure actually runs

1. Finish the ICA side first: renunciation, or loss of PR or citizenship.
2. Apply before you leave Singapore if you possibly can. The Singpass plus bank-letter route is a great deal cleaner than certifying paperwork from another country.
3. Make sure a Singapore bank account is registered with CPF Board, because that's where the money has to go.
4. Apply online, or use Form CPF-CA with mission certification if you're already abroad.
5. Budget for roughly **12 weeks** of processing on average, and verify that against the live page. The Board runs thorough checks before it disburses.
6. Once the funds land, moving them into a foreign retirement product is your own job, under your receiving country's tax and retirement rules. There is no CPF-to-super or CPF-to-401k direct pipe.

## Before you renounce just to get at the balance

Model what disappears with PR: MediShield cover, housing options, whatever future return you might one day want.

Then there's the sting in the tail. Returning ex-SCs and ex-PRs can face **refund-with-interest** obligations on amounts they withdrew earlier, so money you take out now may follow you back. Read the live Important Notes on the closure form rather than trusting a summary, including this one.

While the account still exists, nomination and estate questions belong here: [wills & CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore). The wider exit cash picture is in [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs).

## The order that actually works

Stop being SC or PR, then close, then have the bank account ready, then buy a foreign retirement product if you want one. Don't renounce on a Reddit timeline without first pricing the things PR was quietly giving you.
