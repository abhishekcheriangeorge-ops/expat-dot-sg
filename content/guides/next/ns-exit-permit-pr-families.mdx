**Short answer:** NS-liable **PR sons** often need CMPB **Exit Permits** for longer overseas study/stays. University deferment is generally **not** granted; NSmen have separate ~12-month overseas rules — use OneNS, not Reddit.

Families who take PR for sons often discover **Exit Permit** rules years later — usually when a boarding school, overseas degree, or parent secondment collides with enlistment age. CMPB/MINDEF rules differ for **pre-enlistees** and **NSmen**. Reddit mixes both; your letter age band is what matters.

This is orientation, not personalised NS advice. Write CMPB / use OneNS for your file.

## Who this hits

Under the Enlistment Act, male Singapore citizens and permanent residents (unless exempted) face NS obligations. ICA’s PR page is explicit: males granted PR as a **foreign student** or under **parents’ sponsorship** are generally **liable** — register around **16½**, enlist from **18**, and university deferment is **not** granted.

Exit Permit requirements exist so NS-liable males who live or study overseas still return to serve. CMPB says letters go out when boys turn **13**.

## Pre-enlistment (ages 13 → enlistment)

CMPB’s published table (verify live — bond amounts move):

| Age band | Overseas duration | What CMPB requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **13 to 16.5** | 3 months to &lt; 2 years | Exit Permit |
| **13 to 16.5** | **2 years or longer** | Exit Permit **plus bond**: Banker’s Guarantee of **S$75,000** or **50% of both parents’ combined preceding-year gross income**, whichever is **higher** |
| **Above 16.5 to enlistment** | **3 months or longer** | Exit Permit (see live CMPB page for bond / docs in your case) |

Bond paperwork needs overseas study proof plus parents’ employment / income tax assessments. The Exit Permit issues **after** the bond process completes. Apply, renew, extend, cancel, or check status via **OneNS**.

If you cannot return before expiry, apply for an **extension** — case-by-case. Staying out without a valid permit risks **bond forfeiture** and offences. CMPB cites fines up to **S$2,000** (13–16.5 band, including parent/guardian liability) and up to **S$10,000** and/or **3 years’ imprisonment** for liable males **above 16.5** who remain overseas without a valid Exit Permit.

Course changes (institution, place of study, programme) after a study Exit Permit is granted need a CMPB/OneNS update — do not assume the old permit silently covers a transfer.

## After ORD — NSmen overseas

Operationally Ready National Servicemen face a **different** clock than teenagers:

- Exit Permit required for overseas trips of **12 months or longer**  
- Trips of **6 to under 12 months**: Exit Permit generally **not** required, but **inform your unit** and keep contact details current  
- If call-ups (ICT etc.) fall inside an Exit Permit period, seek **deferment** approval with supporting docs  
- MINDEF Reserve still needs Exit Permits for ≥12-month trips until statutory age / exemption  

Study Exit Permits typically need school acceptance letters (eCOE / CAS / I-20 style docs) and, if the letter is stale, newer proof (student visa, recent fees). Work/study Exit Permits that people file late often get approved only from the **submission date** — forums warn that gaps can leave you exposed to IPPT / defaulter warnings. Apply **before** departure; permits are not a backdated souvenir.

## Do not confuse Exit Permit with Re-Entry Permit

| Instrument | Owner | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Re-Entry Permit (REP)** | ICA | Keep **PR immigration status** while travelling |
| **Exit Permit** | CMPB / MINDEF | NS liability / overseas travel compliance |

You can need **both**. Losing PR without a valid REP is an ICA problem; skipping Exit Permit is an Enlistment Act problem. See [REP guide](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).

## Family planning questions this answers

- **“Can my PR son finish a four-year overseas degree before NS?”** Pre-tertiary deferment only; university studies are disrupted for enlistment once liable. Exit Permit / bond may still be needed for earlier overseas schooling.  
- **“We got PR under PTS — does my adult male applicant serve?”** Scheme and grant letter matter; do not generalise from “first-gen exemption” memes — read [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications).  
- **“If we renounce PR before enlistment, can he return on EP later?”** ICA/MINDEF warn of **serious adverse impact** on later work, study, and family long-term facilities. Get written CMPB advice before treating renunciation as a loophole — [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore).

## Practical sequence for a parent secondment

1. Confirm each son’s **NS liability letter** and age band  
2. Diary Exit Permit lead times against school term / visa starts  
3. Budget bond cash / Banker’s Guarantee capacity for long pre-enlistment stays  
4. Keep REP and Exit Permit calendars separate but both green  
5. If the household is choosing exit over roots, read [long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit) and [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) before you trip into default  

NS admin is not optional fine print on a PR celebration email. Treat CMPB as the source of truth.
