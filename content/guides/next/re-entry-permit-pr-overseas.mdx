Permanent Residence is not a souvenir stamp. A valid **Re-Entry Permit (REP)** is what lets you leave Singapore and still be treated as a PR when you return. Reddit’s “I forgot to renew and almost lost PR at Changi” threads are not urban legends — they are the product.

## What the REP is for

- Issued when you take up PR; renewed for fixed validity periods.  
- Required whenever a PR **travels out** of Singapore and wants to retain status.  
- Leaving or remaining overseas **without** a valid REP risks loss of PR; you are then assessed as an ordinary visitor on return.

### Visa-free entry ≠ keep PR

r/askSingapore periodically invents a loophole: *My passport is visa-free for Singapore tourism, so an expired REP does not matter — I will just enter as a visitor and still be PR.* Wrong. ICA’s framing is blunt: leave or remain overseas **without a valid REP** and you risk **losing PR**. Tourist entry (30/90-day visit pass folklore) is not PR restoration. Visa-free Chinese / EU / other passports do not override REP conditions.

If PR is already lost, you re-enter the immigration ladder like any other foreigner (work pass → fresh PR application). Prior NS completion or SC parents can help a **story**; they are not a reinstatement button. See [PR application strategy](/guides/pr-application-strategy) and [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore) if you are choosing a deliberate exit instead of silent REP death.

## How to renew (normal path)

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Window | Online via MyICA / e-Service within **3 months** before expiry |
| Login | Singpass (overseas without Singpass → nearest Singapore Overseas Mission, ideally ≥2 months before expiry) |
| Travel document | Valid passport with ≥ **3 months** remaining |
| Documents | Usually **none** unless ICA asks |
| Processing | Often within **~1 week**; some cases longer |

New passport of the **same nationality**? Transfer / update travel-document details promptly. ICA may only clear you as a PR if the REP matches the passport you present.

## From 1 December 2025 — overseas without a valid REP

ICA / MHA’s revised process (effective **1 Dec 2025**) replaces the old “lose PR immediately + ~1-month reinstatement folklore”:

| Clock | Published rule |
| --- | --- |
| Departed **without** a valid REP | Apply for an REP within **180 days** from the date of departure |
| Left with a valid REP that **expired overseas** | Apply within **180 days** from the **REP expiry** date |
| Applied inside the 180 days | You generally **remain a PR** until the outcome |
| Application **refused** | PR lost the **day after** the rejection is sent |
| Never applied in the window | PR lost the **day after** the 180-day period ends — even if you have since returned |
| Re-entry during the window | Subject to checkpoint assessment, ICA may issue a **PR’s Single-Entry Pass (PRSEP)** so you can re-enter while regularising |

REP issuance remains **discretionary**. Meeting the deadline is not an approval. MHA’s framing: once PR is lost under this process, there is **no reinstatement pathway** — only a **fresh PR application** if you are eligible ([PR strategy](/guides/pr-application-strategy) / [rejection & reapply](/guides/pr-rejection-reapply)).

Do not treat PRSEP as a multi-year travel plan. Renew or obtain a proper REP before the next departure.

## Unemployed / short REP renewals (forum heat)

r/askSingapore’s “I got only **1 year**” threads are usually about **REP validity length**, not instant PR cancellation. Patterns that match ICA’s commitment language (anecdotes, not a published scorecard):

- Unemployed or thin CPF / tax story at renewal → peers often report **1-year** REPs instead of the familiar multi-year grant.  
- Long overseas study with only short Singapore touchpoints → same short-REP pattern.  
- Return to Singapore employment / clearer ties → later renewals often lengthen again.  
- Staying **physically in Singapore** without travelling → expired REP is still a problem the day you try to leave; renew before you fly.

Do **not** lie on the occupation / income fields. If you are between jobs, say so accurately; inventing employment to chase a 5-year stamp is how files get messy. If you are about to resign, peers often renew **while still employed** when the 3-month window allows — still not a guarantee.

If ICA refuses renewal while you are overseas in the new 180-day regime, model loss of PR + fresh application, not “appeal reinstatement.”

## Long overseas stints — renewal realism

Forum consensus is not policy, but the pattern matches ICA’s “commitment” language:

- Short overseas study / secondment with clear return intent → renewals often continue.  
- Multi-year residence abroad with thin Singapore ties → expect **shorter** REPs or non-renewal.  
- CPF top-ups alone do **not** replace physical ties, employment, family, or tax residency stories.

If you are choosing between keeping PR as a safety net and relocating for a decade, model the exit path deliberately ([long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit)).

## CPF: you cannot “cash out” while still PR

Full CPF account closure / withdrawal for non-citizens happens **after** you are no longer SC or PR (renunciation / loss completed). Until then, ordinary contribution and scheme rules apply. Closing early and leaving Singapore without finishing ICA steps creates messy overseas certification.

<OfficialSource>
CPF Board: [Closing your account when you leave Singapore](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/cpf-asset-management/on-leaving-singapore). Prefer closing and transferring to a bank account **before** you leave, while Singpass still works.
</OfficialSource>

## Conduct conditions

From **1 Dec 2025**, ICA emphasises Entry Permit / REP good-conduct conditions (including not interfering in domestic politics or undermining social harmony, and criminal offences). Breaches can trigger review and revocation. Read the live conditions page, not group-chat paraphrases.

NS liability for male PRs does not disappear because you hold an REP overseas — see [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) before any family PR strategy ([PR application](/guides/pr-application-strategy)). Family REP renewals can also be affected if an NS-liable relative renounces or loses PR without completing obligations — ICA’s published notes say as much on the conditions page.
