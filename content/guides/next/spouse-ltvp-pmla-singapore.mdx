The r/askSingapore threads that start “we just ROM’d, how long for LTVP?” usually collapse four separate things into one imagined clock: PMLA, the Letter of Eligibility, the spouse LTVP itself, and spouse PR. They aren’t stages of a single process. They’re distinct products with their own forms, their own timelines, and in one case their own agency.

This guide covers the ICA spouse LTVP lane, where an SC or PR sponsors you. Renewals and the LLE window are in [spouse LTVP renewal and PMLA](/guides/spouse-ltvp-renewal-pmla-lle-window-singapore). Permanent residence is a different conversation entirely, over on [PR for spouse](/guides/pr-for-spouse-singapore). Work rights once you hold the LTVP are on [PLOC and LOC](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore). And if you’re arriving at this from a cancelled EP, start with [EP job loss to spouse LTVP bridge](/guides/ep-job-loss-spouse-ltvp-bridge-singapore), because the sequencing there is tighter.

## The SC versus PR fork that people miss

| Sponsor | Is PMLA available? | Published processing orientation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore citizen | Yes, a free Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment before you marry | Up to 6 months without PMLA, or around 6 weeks if PMLA was completed before the marriage |
| Singapore PR | No published PMLA path | Most within 6 weeks when the documents are in order, some longer |

PMLA is built for a citizen and a non-resident. A PR sponsor can’t “do PMLA to speed things up,” because ICA’s PMLA pages are written for citizen sponsors and there’s no equivalent door. It sounds like a technicality, right up until you go looking for the form that does not exist. If what you’re actually confused about is MOM versus ICA pass categories on arrival, that’s handled in our [LTVP overview](/guides/ltvp-singapore).

## What PMLA actually does for you

ICA’s Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment is free, which by itself makes it worth doing if you’re eligible. It does three useful things for a citizen and non-resident couple.

It tells you whether the intended foreign spouse is likely to be eligible for an LTVP at all, which is the question nobody wants to ask out loud. Marriage does not guarantee anyone the right to stay here, and finding that out before the wedding is better than finding it out after. It also shortens the later LTVP file to roughly 6 weeks rather than up to 6 months. And it forces the awkward conversations about income, housing, and immigration history to happen before the ROM rather than in the middle of an application.

Here’s what ICA publishes about how it runs.

| Topic | What ICA says |
| --- | --- |
| Who logs in | The SC, with Singpass. The non-resident supplies passport and particulars |
| How long a draft lives | Submit within 7 days of starting the draft, or it’s automatically removed |
| Processing | Within 1 month when the information is in order, some longer |
| A positive outcome | A Letter of Eligibility, valid for 1 year, to support a later LTVP filing |
| What the LLE isn’t | Not an LTVP, not work rights, not a promise of PR |

ICA also warns that in a small number of cases the PMLA result and the later LTVP outcome can diverge, if circumstances or the particulars you submit change between the two filings. So treat a positive PMLA as planning clarity rather than a voucher you can cash later.

There’s a separate trap for anyone who holds, or recently held, a Work Permit, including a Training Work Permit. If you want to marry an SC or PR, whether in Singapore or overseas, and whether the ceremony is civil, religious, or customary, **you need approval from MOM’s Controller of Work Passes first**. Skipping that step can cost you your work privileges and your ability to enter the country. It’s the kind of rule people only discover afterwards, when it’s hard to fix.

## After the marriage: filing the actual LTVP

The local spouse sponsors the application on the ICA e-Service using Singpass, and every applicant has to review the declarations, using Singpass or ICA-issued credentials. ICA suggests only starting once the people who are eligible for Singpass already have it, because account approval can take 2–5 working days and you don’t want that sitting in the middle of your timeline.

These are the fees ICA publishes for LTVP. They’re non-refundable, and worth verifying live.

| Stage | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| At submission | S$45 per application |
| At formalities | S$60 for LTVP issuance, plus S$30 for a Multiple Journey Visa if it applies |

One renewal detail catches almost everyone: **renew at least 3 months before expiry**, because the renewed validity runs from issuance and any leftover validity on the old pass is not carried forward. Renew late and you’ve paid for time you’ll never use. The full version is in [spouse LTVP renewal and the LLE window](/guides/spouse-ltvp-renewal-pmla-lle-window-singapore).

If your household is currently on an EP, S Pass, or DP and switching across to a spouse LTVP after marrying an SC or PR, sequence it deliberately so that cancel day doesn’t open a gap in anyone’s lawful stay. [Between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) covers the timing, and [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) covers the EP-family side of it.

## An LTVP is not PR, and it isn’t work either

Spouse LTVP is a stay product. It doesn’t produce a blue IC, and it doesn’t quietly turn into one. Plenty of couples do spend months or years on LTVP before filing for [spouse-sponsored PR](/guides/pr-for-spouse-singapore), and that’s a reasonable household strategy, but it isn’t a published waiting period you’re required to serve.

The LTVP on its own also doesn’t let you work here. Eligible spouses, and unmarried children under 21, of an SC or PR can ask for a PLOC with the LTVP file or afterwards, or work under an employer-held LOC. The [PLOC and LOC playbook](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore) explains which one fits your situation.

## What we’d actually do

If you’re a Singapore citizen marrying a foreigner and your partner’s ability to stay matters, run PMLA before the ROM, then keep an eye on the LLE’s 1-year window and file the spouse LTVP inside it with documents that tell one consistent story. If you’re a PR sponsor, ignore the PMLA folklore entirely and plan around the published 6-week lane, accepting that some take longer.

Two last things. Agents are form-fillers at best. And an LTVP IPA is not a PR approval, however similar the email looks on your phone.
