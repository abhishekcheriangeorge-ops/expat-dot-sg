r/askSingapore’s “I nominated but it failed” threads are almost always **witness hygiene**, not mysterious CPF Board hostility. Estate rails (Singapore will vs probate vs CPF) live on [wills, probate & CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore). This companion is the **Singpass witness + amend** layer for PRs who already contribute.

EP/S Pass holders usually have **no** CPF — but a PR spouse in the same household often does. Do not skip this because “we’re expats.”

## What must be true online

| Rule | Published orientation (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Nominees | Up to **15** online |
| Witnesses | **Two** individuals aged **21+** with Singpass |
| Witness conflict | Witnesses **cannot** be nominees on that nomination |
| Confirm clock | Each witness must confirm within **7 days** of your submission |
| Outcome if a witness misses the clock | Nomination typically **fails / expires** — you restart |

Ask witnesses **before** you click submit. A spouse who is also a nominee cannot double as a witness on the same online nomination.

## Online vs paper (when peers panic)

| Path | When people use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Online (Singpass)** | Default for residents with two cooperating Singpass witnesses |
| **Paper / counter** | Witnesses abroad without Singpass, complex share splits, or Board-directed edge cases |

Paper is slower and easier to mis-complete. Prefer online unless you have a concrete reason you cannot.

## Life events that should trigger a review

| Event | Why re-check nomination |
| --- | --- |
| Marriage / divorce / remarriage | Old nominees may no longer match intent; a will change does **not** rewrite CPF |
| Birth / adoption | Add children as nominees if that is the plan |
| Nominee dies or loses capacity | Stale shares can send slices to Public Trustee paths |
| Renouncing PR / leaving for good | Full cash-out rules sit on CPF leaving pages — nomination hygiene still matters until the account closes |

Capacity planning while alive (who can act if you cannot) is [LPA / AMD](/guides/lasting-power-attorney-amd-foreigners) — a different rail from CPF nomination.

## No nomination → Public Trustee friction

Without a valid nomination, CPF Board channels monies through the **Public Trustee** under statutory distribution rules. Peers underestimate fees and months of delay while Singapore banks are already frozen waiting for probate on non-CPF assets. Nomination is free; intestacy admin is not.

## Decision rule

Treat CPF nomination like annual life-admin: two pre-briefed Singpass witnesses → submit → both confirm inside **7 days** → screenshot success → revisit after marriage/divorce/kids. Parent estate map stays on [wills & probate](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore); leaving / PR exit cash-out sits on [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).
