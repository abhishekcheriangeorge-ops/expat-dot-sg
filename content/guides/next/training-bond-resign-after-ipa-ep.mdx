r/askSingapore's "I got a new IPA but my old EP has a 24-month bond" threads roll three separate problems into one panic: pass sequencing, notice, and civil clawback. They run on different clocks and they're decided by different people. Clause anatomy — penalty versus liquidated damages, sign-on clawbacks — lives on [training bonds & clawbacks](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep). This page is the resign-and-IPA-timing layer on top of it.

Treat it as orientation, not as a view on whether *your* clause is enforceable. Once the numbers are genuinely contested, you want counsel.

## Three moments, three risk shapes

Where you are in the timeline changes the shape of the risk more than the wording of the bond does.

Start with the earliest one: before you begin, or before the pass is issued. You have an offer and an IPA, and you can still walk away from the new role. What you may not be able to walk away from is the signed offer or bond, which can already create civil exposure on its own. Read the trigger dates before you assume otherwise, because "commencement", "confirmation" and "12 months from joining" are three different dates and contracts use them carelessly.

The second moment is the one people actually post about: you're in the job, mid-bond, with a new IPA in hand. On the pass side, MOM's guidance is to **get the new employer's IPA before you resign** from the old EP when changing jobs. On the bond side, serve your contractual notice, or pay in lieu if the contract allows for it. Bond repayment is a separate civil question, and it doesn't quietly resolve itself just because the pass side went smoothly.

The third is an employer-initiated exit, where the pass cancellation follows the employer's action rather than yours. That's the moment to negotiate a written waiver, and retrenchment or other no-fault exits are the usual carve-out to ask for.

MOM's published line is that an employer cannot reject a resignation given with proper notice. That's genuinely useful and narrower than people want it to be. It stops HR trapping your pass by refusing to accept your letter. It does not delete a signed clawback.

## Deduction or invoice, after your last day

Four HR moves come up here, and they don't get the same answer.

If they cut the bond straight out of your final salary, remember that Employment Act allowable deductions are narrow, and consent or a written agreement matters. Push back in writing. If it turns into an unpaid-salary fight, [TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom) is the route.

If they invoice you or send a demand letter after you've gone, that's the civil recovery path rather than a payroll question. Ask for a calculation tied to named training costs or to the signed schedule, not a round number someone arrived at by feel.

If the demand is "pay the remaining 18 months' salary," that tends to look like a penalty rather than a genuine pre-estimate of loss. That's an argument worth taking to counsel rather than acting on yourself, because ignoring a formal demand is its own risk.

And if anyone holds your passport or refuses to cancel the pass, treat that as a separate problem with its own hygiene. Keep the immigration trail clean, including [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) and the IR21 paperwork, whatever is happening with the money.

Scholarship and **MOE Tuition Grant** bonds are a different statutory animal, covered in [TG bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond). Don't reason from those rules across to a private SME foreigner training bond, or the other way round.

## A sequence that usually hurts less

1. Land the new IPA first ([career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)).
2. Read the bond's trigger dates, and any employer-initiated carve-outs, before you resign.
3. Resign with contractual notice, and keep proof of delivery.
4. Ask HR in writing whether they intend to invoice or to deduct, and ask for the cost schedule.
5. Don't trade a clean pass cancellation for a WhatsApp promise to waive it later.

## The short version

Run the two calendars in parallel rather than trying to merge them. The EP job-change one goes IPA, then notice, then cancel. The bond one you negotiate or contest on paper at its own pace, and neither one waits for the other. Clause hygiene stays on [training bonds & clawbacks](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep), and collisions with leave and notice sit on [Employment Act leave & notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep).
