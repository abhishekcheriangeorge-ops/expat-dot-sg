**Short answer:** Mission **replacement / ETD first**, ICA **REP transfer second**, Changi **third** — a new document number without transfer breaks the PR clearance script.

r/askSingapore’s “passport stolen in Bangkok, am I still PR at Changi?” threads mix **mission replacement**, **emergency travel documents (ETD)**, and **ICA REP transfer** into one panic. Routine renewals live on [transfer REP to a new passport](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport). This companion is the **loss / ETD / mid-trip** layer.

Blue NRIC does not travel with a passport number. Your **Re-Entry Permit** does. A new book — including many temporary / emergency documents your mission issues — is still a **new travel document** for ICA purposes until you transfer remaining REP validity onto it.

## Sequence that usually hurts less

| Step | Who | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Report loss / theft as your mission and local police require | You | Replacement / ETD eligibility |
| 2. Obtain the **replacement passport or ETD** | Home mission | ICA transfer needs a travel-document number to attach to |
| 3. Run ICA **Transfer of REP / Update Travel Document** (Singpass) | You | Moves remaining REP validity onto the new document |
| 4. Print updated REP; carry with the new document | You | Secondary inspection script |
| 5. Only then assume PR clearance on return | You + ICA | Visa-free entry ≠ PR status |

## ETD vs full replacement book

| Document | Typical use | REP transfer posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full replacement passport** | Lost / expired book replaced by mission | Transfer onto the **new passport number** before you expect PR return clearance |
| **Emergency / temporary travel document** | One-way or short validity home / to Singapore | Ask the mission **and** check whether ICA’s transfer e-Service accepts that document type; if transfer is unavailable, expect officer discretion / secondary inspection — do not invent “NRIC selfie is enough” folklore |
| **Same passport number, new pages** | Rare extension stickers | Confirm whether ICA still wants an update — most PR cases are **new numbers** |

ICA’s published transfer fee for a new travel document is typically **free**, with processing commonly cited around **three working days** (verify live). Stolen-passport chaos does not shrink that clock — start the transfer as soon as the new document exists.

## Transfer ≠ renew ≠ lost NRIC

| Situation | Path |
| --- | --- |
| New passport / ETD number, REP still valid | **Transfer REP** (this guide + [routine transfer](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport)) |
| REP itself expiring / expired risk | **Renew REP** — [REP overseas](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) |
| Blue NRIC lost | ICA NRIC replacement — [lost NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) — different rail |

## Decision rule

Mission replacement / ETD **first**, ICA transfer **second**, Changi arrival **third**. Routine passport renewals stay on [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport); wider overseas REP discipline sits on [REP overseas](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).
