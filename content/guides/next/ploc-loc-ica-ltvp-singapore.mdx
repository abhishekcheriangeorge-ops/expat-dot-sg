“Can my LTVP spouse work?” is one of those r/askSingapore questions where every answer is partly right, because the thread inevitably mixes up PLOC, the employer-applied LOC, the post-2021 Dependant’s Pass rules, and graduate LTVP. Only some ICA LTVP categories get MOM’s spouse and child work rails at all, which is the fact that makes the confusion so durable.

This guide covers one lane: you hold an ICA-issued LTVP as the spouse, or the unmarried child under 21, of a Singapore citizen or PR. If you already have a PLOC and you’re heading towards renewal, the sequencing is in [renewing PLOC and LOC, LTVP first](/guides/ploc-renewal-ltvp-sequencing-singapore).

## Which product fits you

| Product | Who it’s for | Do you need a job offer first? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLOC, the Pre-approved Letter of Consent | ICA LTVP or LTVP+ holders who are spouses or unmarried children under 21 of an SC or PR | No. You can hold it before you find work, though it’s still assessed on its merits |
| LOC, the Letter of Consent | The same ICA LTVP spouse and child population, and the fallback when PLOC fails or the job is restricted | Yes. The employer applies once you have an offer |
| Graduate-seeking-employment LTVP | An IHL graduate staying on to job-hunt | Not a PLOC category at all, see [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt) |
| Parent LTVP | Parents of an SC or PR | Not PLOC either. MOM points parents towards Work Permit and other rails, whatever the folklore says |
| EP Dependant’s Pass | Trailing partners of EP and S Pass holders | A different story since 2021, covered in [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) and [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) |

The thing to hold on to is that **an LTVP by itself is permission to stay, not permission to earn**. Taking paid local work without a PLOC, an LOC, or another MOM work pass isn’t rescued by calling it remote work for an overseas HQ, which is the loophole people reliably invent for themselves in the comments. The actual rules are in [side income and freelancing](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## Getting a PLOC

The easy way is to ask for it with the LTVP itself. When eligible foreigners apply for or renew an ICA LTVP or LTVP+, the flow offers a PLOC option. Tick it if dual income is any part of your household plan, even a hypothetical part. It is far less hassle than adding it afterwards.

If you did skip it, you’re not stuck. The LTVP sponsor or holder can request a PLOC 2 days after the LTVP card arrives.

The timing trap is renewal. PLOC validity is tied to the LTVP or LTVP+ underneath it, so if your LTVP is nearly expired, the PLOC you get will be correspondingly thin. **Renew the LTVP first, then ask for the PLOC.** Doing it the other way round means paying attention to two clocks and getting the short end of both.

MOM publishes that each PLOC is assessed on its own merits, and the outcome email usually turns up within a week, so check your junk folder before you conclude something has gone wrong. Note also that employers and employment agents cannot apply for a PLOC on your behalf. It isn’t an EP sponsorship in a different wrapper; the request comes from your side of the table.

One more restriction that ends a lot of plans: MOM’s key facts state that a PLOC does not allow the holder to be self-employed. If your idea was a solo consultancy, that needs a different pass structure entirely. PLOC is not a freelance licence, however much the name suggests a general permission.

## Starting a job, and leaving one

Show your employer the PLOC together with your valid LTVP or LTVP+. From there the obligation sits with them, not you: the employer has to notify MOM before you start work, and again within 7 days of your last day.

Both notifications are the employer’s duty rather than yours, which is worth knowing if a former employer turns out to be slow about paperwork. Profession-specific licensing carries on applying as well, for things like massage establishments and security work. A PLOC is permission to work under MOM’s conditions, not a waiver of whatever licence your sector demands.

## Restricted occupations, where you need an employer LOC

PLOC holders can’t take MOM’s restricted occupations. In practice that means media and religion-related roles: journalist, editor, sub-editor, producer, religious occupations, and you should check the live list rather than assume your job title isn’t on it. For those roles, the prospective employer applies for an LOC instead.

The same fallback covers you if a PLOC was refused, or if you never asked for one. Find an employer willing to file an LOC, and the route reopens.

## Renewals, cancellations, and the things that quietly lapse

Renew the ICA LTVP at least 3 months before it expires, and request the PLOC again as part of that renewal if you still want work rights. The full sequencing is in [PLOC renewal sequencing](/guides/ploc-renewal-ltvp-sequencing-singapore).

When the LTVP or LTVP+ expires or is cancelled, the PLOC dies with it. There’s no independent life to it, which is why the renewal order matters so much.

Going the other way is simpler than people fear. You don’t have to cancel a PLOC because you’ve stopped working. Just leave PLOC off the next LTVP renewal if you no longer want the work rights, and it lapses on its own.

And if an employer eventually sponsors you for a full EP, S Pass, or Work Permit, follow MOM’s published sequencing FAQs for the switch rather than running two contradictory stories about your status at once.

## What we’d actually do

Ask for the PLOC when you file the spouse or child LTVP, as long as dual income is a real possibility and the work you have in mind isn’t on MOM’s restricted list. Use an employer LOC for restricted occupations, or after a PLOC refusal.

Two things not to do. Don’t tell a hiring manager you “have LOC” when what you hold is a graduate LTVP; they’re different products and HR will find out at the worst moment. And don’t treat a PLOC as a self-employment pass, because it explicitly isn’t one. If the answer is that you need your own principal EP rather than working under someone else’s status, we’ve mapped that move in [LTVP and PLOC to EP conversion](/guides/ltvp-ploc-to-ep-conversion-singapore).

If you’re in a ONE Pass household looking at spouse work rights, that’s a different product again: [ONE Pass spouse LOC in depth](/guides/one-pass-spouse-loc-depth-singapore).
