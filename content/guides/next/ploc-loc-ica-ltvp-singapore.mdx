r/askSingapore’s “can my LTVP spouse work?” threads mix **PLOC**, **employer LOC**, **Dependant’s Pass work rules**, and **graduate LTVP**. Only some ICA LTVP categories get MOM’s spouse/child work rails. This guide is the **ICA-issued LTVP spouse / unmarried child under 21 of an SC or PR** lane. Renewal sequencing: [PLOC / LOC renew — LTVP first](/guides/ploc-renewal-ltvp-sequencing-singapore).

## Product chooser

| Product | Who it is for | Job offer needed first? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PLOC** (Pre-approved Letter of Consent) | ICA **LTVP / LTVP+** holders who are spouses or unmarried children **under 21** of an **SC or PR** | **No** — work without finding a job first (still assessed on merits) |
| **LOC** (Letter of Consent) | Same ICA LTVP spouse/child population (and the fallback if PLOC fails / restricted job) | **Yes** — employer applies after you have an offer |
| Graduate-seeking-employment LTVP | IHL graduate stay-to-hunt | **Not** a PLOC category — see [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt) |
| Parent LTVP | Parents of SC/PR | **Not** PLOC — MOM points parents at **Work Permit** / other rails, not spouse PLOC folklore |
| EP Dependant’s Pass | Trailing partners of EP/S Pass holders | Different post-2021 story — [DP](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) / [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) |

LTVP alone is still **stay only**. Starting paid local work without PLOC / LOC / another MOM work pass is not a “remote for overseas HQ” loophole you invent on Reddit — [side income rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## How to get a PLOC

1. **With the LTVP file** — when eligible foreigners apply for or renew ICA LTVP / LTVP+, ICA’s flow offers a PLOC option. Tick it if dual-income is part of the household plan.  
2. **Ad-hoc later** — if you skipped it, the LTVP sponsor/holder can request PLOC **2 days after** receiving the LTVP card.  
3. **Expiring soon?** — renew the LTVP **first**. PLOC validity is **tied to** the current LTVP / LTVP+; a short residual LTVP makes a thin PLOC.  

MOM publishes that each PLOC is assessed on its own merits; outcome email is usually within a **week** (check junk). **Employers / EAs cannot apply for PLOC** on your behalf — it is not an EP Sponsorship clone.

MOM key facts also state PLOC does **not** allow the holder to be **self-employed**. Solo consultancy dreams need a different pass structure, not “PLOC = freelance licence.”

## Starting and leaving a job on PLOC

Show the employer your **PLOC + valid LTVP/LTVP+**. The **employer** must notify MOM:

- **Before** the PLOC holder starts work  
- Within **7 days** of the last day of work  

Profession-specific licences (e.g. massage establishments, security) still apply. PLOC is permission to work under MOM’s conditions — not a waiver of sector licensing.

## Restricted occupations → use employer LOC

PLOC holders **cannot** work in MOM’s restricted occupations (media and religion-related roles such as journalist, editor, sub-editor, producer, or religious occupations — verify the live list). For those jobs, the prospective employer must apply for an **LOC** instead.

If PLOC is refused or you never requested one, the same fallback applies: find an employer who will file **LOC**.

## Renewals and cancels

- Renew ICA LTVP at least **3 months** before expiry; request PLOC again on that renewal if you still want it — deep dive: [PLOC renewal sequencing](/guides/ploc-renewal-ltvp-sequencing-singapore).  
- When LTVP / LTVP+ **expires or is cancelled**, PLOC dies with it.  
- You do **not** need to cancel PLOC just because you stop working — simply skip PLOC on the next LTVP renewal if you no longer want work rights.  
- If a prospective employer later sponsors a full **EP / S Pass / Work Permit**, follow MOM’s published sequencing FAQs rather than holding two contradictory stories.

## Decision rule

Ask for **PLOC with the spouse/child LTVP** when dual income is real and the job is not on MOM’s restricted list. Use **employer LOC** for restricted occupations or PLOC refusals. Never tell HR you “have LOC” on a **graduate** LTVP, and never treat PLOC as a self-employment pass.
