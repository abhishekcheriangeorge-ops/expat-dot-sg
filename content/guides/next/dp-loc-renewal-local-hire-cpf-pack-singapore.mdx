The parents to this one are [Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) and [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore). This companion is the evidence layer, the part solo consultancies only meet at renewal, a year after a first LOC that went through without a murmur.

## First LOC ≠ renewal rules

A Letter of Consent for an eligible DP business owner — an ACRA sole proprietor, partner or director who meets the shareholding tests — is time-boxed from the start. That much people read. What they don't read is that renewal asks for something the first application never did.

Which is why the forum heat is almost never really about MOM hating freelancers. It's about year two: twelve months of invoicing, no Local Qualifying Salary hire, and no CPF trail to point at.

Four things need to be true when you submit a renewal.

You need at least one Singaporean or PR on payroll, at or above the live Local Qualifying Salary.

You need **three consecutive months of CPF contributions** for that hire, already paid, before you submit. This is the one that ends renewals, because it can't be fixed in the week you notice it.

The business still has to qualify, which means ACRA status plus the shareholding or partnership facts still matching the product you were approved under.

And the DP underneath has to still be alive, because the LOC dies with it. If the principal's pass is up for renewal around the same time, sequence that first.

## If the pack is thin

There are a few honest moves here, and one that isn't.

Pause the Singapore-client work before the LOC expires. Invoicing through the gap and hoping nobody lines the dates up is the option that isn't honest, and it's the one people reach for first.

Hire to the published standard early enough that three CPF months genuinely exist by your submission date. Count backwards from the renewal, not forwards from today.

Or move onto an employer-sponsored or self-sponsored [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) through a properly capitalised vehicle; that route runs through [incorporating](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore).

If your clients are clearly overseas-only, the DP overseas FAQ is a different question, and we cover it in [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass). It still isn't a licence for Singapore clients.

## Decision rule

Renewal is a hiring and CPF story, not a shareholding story. A first-year LOC letter and a tidy ACRA profile don't waive the local-hire bar, however often the forums wish they did.
