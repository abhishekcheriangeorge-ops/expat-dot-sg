## First LOC ≠ renewal rules

A Letter of Consent for eligible **DP business owners** (ACRA sole prop / partner / director meeting shareholding tests) is time-boxed. Forum heat is almost never “MOM hates freelancers” — it is **renewal** after a year of invoicing with **no** Local Qualifying Salary hire and **no** three-month CPF trail.

| Renewal ask (orientation) | What to gather |
| --- | --- |
| Local employee | At least one Singaporean/PR on payroll at/above live **Local Qualifying Salary** |
| CPF trail | **Three consecutive months** of CPF contributions for that hire **before** you submit |
| Business still qualifies | ACRA status + shareholding / partnership facts that still match the LOC product |
| DP still live | LOC dies if the underlying DP dies — sequence principal renewal first |

## If the pack is thin

1. **Pause Singapore-client work** before the LOC expires — do not “invoice through the gap.”  
2. Hire to the published standard **early enough** for three CPF months, or  
3. Migrate onto an employer / self-sponsored [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) via a properly capitalised vehicle — [incorporate](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore), or  
4. Keep clearly **overseas-only** clients under the DP overseas FAQ — still not a Singapore-client licence — [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## Decision rule

**Renewal is a hiring + CPF story.** Shareholding and a first-year LOC letter do not waive the local-hire bar Reddit keeps wishing away.
