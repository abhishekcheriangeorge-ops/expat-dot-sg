**Short answer:** Adult **SC renunciation** needs **21+**, another (or imminent) citizenship, and returned IC/passport — **not** the PR form and **not** silent ORAL loss. NS gates can block males.

r/askSingapore and r/NationalServiceSG conflate three different exits: **miss ORAL and lose SC at 22**, **renounce PR**, and **formally renounce Singapore citizenship as an adult**. Only the last one is this guide. If you want to **keep** SC after dual nationality as a minor by descent/registration, read [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21). If you hold PR (not SC), read [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore).

## Who ICA says can renounce

Under the published Constitution / ICA framing, an SC may renounce only if they are:

| Gate | Published rule |
| --- | --- |
| Age | **21+** |
| Capacity | Of **sound mind** |
| Other nationality | Has **acquired** another citizenship — or can show they are **about to become** a citizen of another country |

You generally **cannot** renounce into statelessness. ask.gov.sg’s ICA answer: documents must show you are a citizen elsewhere **or about to become** one. Plan the foreign naturalisation / certificate timeline before you open the Singapore portal.

## NS — the male-applicant gate

ICA: the Government **may reject** (or withhold) renunciation by a male citizen who has enjoyed citizenship privileges — including possession or use of a Singapore passport — and still has **outstanding NS obligations**. Forum “renounce mid-NSF and walk” plans collide with Enlistment Act / MINDEF advisory notes attached to the renunciation pack.

Practical sequence that matches agency language:

1. Clarify **full-time / ORNS liability** in writing with [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) / unit HR before you pay the S$35 fee.  
2. Males upload the **MINDEF Advisory Note** with the online pack.  
3. Do not assume tourist-entry anecdotes after default equal a clean future EP — see [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications).

## What you file and return

Online application (desktop strongly preferred; ICA warns there is **no save** mid-form). Certified true copies typically include:

- **Statutory Declaration of Renunciation** (notarised / JP / Commissioner for Oaths / Singapore consular officer)  
- **Advisory Note on Renunciation Implication** (acknowledge: no reinstatement; no assurance you can stay/study/work as a foreigner or PR after)  
- **MINDEF Advisory Note** (males)  
- Proof of **other** (or imminent) citizenship  

Originals ICA expects back before processing completes:

- Singapore **IC**  
- Valid Singapore **passport**  
- Singapore **Citizenship Certificate** where applicable (descent / registration cohorts)

Lost IC or passport? Declare it in the application and follow ICA’s replacement / loss instructions — do not invent a courier workaround.

| Admin fact | Published detail |
| --- | --- |
| Fee | **S$35** online (card / PayNow) |
| Cooling-off | Optional hold up to **3 months** before ICA processes (no extension; withdrawal before processing — fee generally not refunded) |
| Processing | About **3 months** after all documents received (longer with cooling-off or incomplete packs) |
| Reinstatement | ICA advisory: **none** once registered |

## Overseas applicants

Missions and high commissions point back to the **same online portal**. Statutory declarations may be notarised at a Singapore overseas mission. Originals still need to reach ICA (registered post / mission instructions). Living abroad without a nearby mission usually means travel for notarisation or return of documents — budget that into the three-month clock.

## How this differs from silent ORAL loss

| Path | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Take ORAL, keep SC | Renounce the **foreign** citizenship; swear allegiance — [ORAL guide](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) |
| Miss ORAL by 22nd birthday | **Automatic** loss of SC (descent/registration minors) — not the same as a registered renunciation file |
| Adult SC renunciation | Deliberate ICA registration + document return; NS gate for males |

Silent ORAL expiry and formal renunciation both end SC, but only the formal path creates a clean paper trail for foreign naturalisation authorities — and both can still leave NS-default problems if you walked away from liability.

## Decision rule

Treat SC renunciation as **irreversible status change**, not a travel-document tidy-up. Get the foreign citizenship proof and NS clearance story straight first; then run ICA’s pack once — incomplete uploads and missing originals are what stretch “three months” into forum horror stories.
