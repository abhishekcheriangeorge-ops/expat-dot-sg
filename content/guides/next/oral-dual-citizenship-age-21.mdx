r/askSingapore’s dual-citizenship threads mix three clocks into one panic: **home-country renunciation**, **Singapore’s Oath of Renunciation, Allegiance and Loyalty (ORAL)**, and **NS for sons**. ICA’s published rule for minors who obtained citizenship by **descent or registration** is blunt — take the ORAL after you turn **21** and **before your 22nd birthday**, or you **automatically lose** Singapore citizenship on that 22nd birthday.

This guide is the **age-21 retention** layer. Adult PR → SC filing lives in [Singapore citizenship application](/guides/singapore-citizenship-application). Adults who want to **drop** SC deliberately use [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship). NS liability modelling lives in [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications).

## Who this applies to

| Situation | ORAL published rule |
| --- | --- |
| SC by **descent** or **registration** as a minor (often overseas-born, registered at a mission, or registered as a child) | Must take ORAL in the **12 months after turning 21** |
| Adult who just completed PR → SC formalities | Different track — follow your IPA / Citizenship Journey instructions, not this minor-oath page |
| “I never used the Singapore passport” | Still check with ICA — retention is about citizenship acquisition path, not forum folklore about benefits |

If you are unsure whether you hold SC by descent/registration, treat the ICA letter that arrives around your 21st birthday (or the minor-oath page) as the starting document — not Reddit timelines.

## What “failure to take the oath” means

ICA’s published consequence: miss the ORAL within **12 months of turning 21** and you **lose Singapore citizenship automatically on your 22nd birthday**. There is **no guarantee** you can then stay, study, or work on a Student’s Pass, work pass, visit pass, or as a PR. Peers who “ignore the letter and keep both passports” are choosing silent loss of SC — not clever dual status.

Male citizens still face **NS** modelling before any plan to drop SC. Losing status without discharging NS obligations carries the same adverse-impact language family as [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore). Confirm personal liability with [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/), not group chat.

## Documents ICA lists

Bring **originals** plus copies ICA can retain. Non-English documents need official translations (embassy of the issuing place, Singapore/foreign notary, or privately created translations attested/notarised as ICA accepts). ICA does **not** endorse private translation shops.

Published requirements to take the ORAL:

- **Identity Card** (NRIC)
- **Foreign Citizenship Renunciation Certificate** (proof you have renounced the other nationality under that country’s process)

The hard part is often **home-country law**, not the ICA counter. Some embassies will not process renunciation until you are 21 and/or until ICA has issued its summons letter. Start that embassy queue early; university-visa passport-validity traps are common in Reddit because foreign student visas need long passport validity while Singapore may limit renewals until ORAL is done.

## Where and how

| Path | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| In Singapore | ICA Services Centre, **2 Crawford Street**, in person |
| Hours (published) | Mon–Fri **8.00am–4.30pm**; closed weekends and public holidays |
| Peaks | Mondays, Fridays, and days around public holidays — expect longer waits |
| Overseas | Nearest **Singapore Overseas Mission** |
| Fee | **None** for taking the ORAL |
| Processing | ICA: completed the **same day** when documents are in order |

Forum requests for **early ORAL before 21** (university visas, passport validity) usually fail — majority age is the legal hinge. If an officer invites a written request with supporting docs, treat that as discretionary correspondence, not a published pathway.

## Sequence that actually works

1. Confirm you are on the **minor by descent/registration** track (letter / ICA).  
2. Map **home-country renunciation** lead time (often the long pole).  
3. Diary the **21st birthday → 22nd birthday** ORAL window.  
4. Book the ICA or mission appointment with renunciation proof + NRIC ready.  
5. Only then assume Singapore passport renewals and long overseas visas will clear cleanly.

If you intend to **keep the foreign citizenship and drop SC**, prefer ICA’s deliberate [adult renunciation](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship) process over silent expiry on the 22nd birthday — automatic loss can complicate later EP / LTVP / PR sponsorship stories for family, and NS-default risk still needs CMPB clarity.

## Decision rule

Treat ORAL as a **hard calendar**, not a soft admin chore. Dual-nationality lifestyle advice that ignores the published 21–22 window is unsafe for anyone who still wants Singapore citizenship (or clean future passes after losing it).
