Expat admin fails in the same place Reddit describes: someone quotes **S$2–3k** for overseas notarisation + apostille + translation of a thick marriage file, while another family pays embassy rates measured in tens of dollars per page. Singapore’s pipeline is usually **Notary Public → Singapore Academy of Law (SAL) Apostille** for documents that need to travel abroad — plus a different path when **ICA/MOM** need home-country proofs for LTVP.

## Two directions of paper

| Direction | Typical path |
| --- | --- |
| **Singapore-issued** docs for use overseas (birth/marriage certs, court papers, notarised affidavits) | SAL legalisation / Apostille (public docs) or Notary → SAL authentication (private docs) |
| **Foreign** docs for use in Singapore (LTVP common-law evidence, school forms, home-country affidavits) | Often notarise / apostille **in the issuing country**, or embassy letter + English translation — SAL cannot “apostille” a foreign original as if it were Singapore-issued |

## SAL Apostille (Singapore documents)

Since **16 September 2021**, Singapore implements the Apostille Convention. SAL is the competent authority: it affixes an Apostille so receiving countries that are Convention parties accept the origin of the document without embassy legalisation chains.

Practical rules from SAL’s published guidance:

- Prefer **originals**; laminated originals may need a certified true copy / extract from the issuing agency  
- Digitally issued government docs usually need to be **printed** for counter legalisation  
- Proxy submission is allowed; appointment is generally **not** required for authentication  
- Public-document legalisation fee is published around **S$10.70** per document (incl. GST) — verify live

## Private documents: notarise first

Private papers (affidavits, true copies of passports, commercial packs) generally need a **Singapore Notary Public**. SAL then authenticates the notarial certificate with an Apostille. Fees typically paid at the notary’s office include:

- Notarisation fee (capped under Notaries Public Rules — ask for the schedule)  
- SAL authentication fee published around **S$87.20** incl. GST (verify live)  

Find practitioners via the [SAL Notary Public directory](https://legalisation.sal.sg/Directory). US university forms and US litigation signatures often work with a Singapore notary — confirm whether the receiving party also wants embassy consularisation.

## LTVP / ICA document panic (common-law and translations)

For MOM LTVP common-law spouses, [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/documents-required) accept **one of**:

- Common-law marriage certificate, **or**  
- Affidavit by both partners that the relationship is recognised under home-country law — **notarised in the home country/region**, **or**  
- Embassy letter acknowledging the common-law relationship under that country’s law  

Non-English documents need the **original + English translation as one upload**. Forum consensus: ask your **embassy in Singapore** for an approved translator list before paying overseas character-based quotes. Pair with the [LTVP guide](/guides/ltvp-singapore).

## Cost-control checklist

1. Ask the **receiving authority** (school, court, ICA/MOM, overseas university) exactly what they want: Apostille, embassy stamp, certified translation, or all three.  
2. Prefer **Singapore notary + SAL** for Singapore-origin docs — do not ship originals overseas for a second apostille.  
3. For foreign-origin LTVP packs, price **embassy vs home-country notary** before accepting a S$2k translation quote.  
4. Keep PDFs of every stamped page; renewals and PR files love repeat paperwork.
