The harassment threads on r/askSingapore tend to freeze on a single sentence: if I report this, they'll cancel my EP. The channels and the evidence habits are covered in [workplace harassment on an EP or S Pass](/guides/workplace-harassment-tafep-ep). This companion is about the pass clock specifically, because that fear is what stops people acting, and it's usually pointed at the wrong thing.

## Safety first, then the paper trail

The order matters more than the speed. Get out of the room, build the record, then decide who to tell.

| Priority | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate danger | Leave the space and call 999 ([emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies)) |
| Same day | Dated notes, plus lawful exports of the relevant chats and email |
| Written report | HR or the whistleblowing channel, or an alternate channel if the harasser *is* HR |
| External | TAFEP, once HR stalls, is conflicted, or has no policy to work from ([contact](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/contact-us)) |
| Running in parallel | A quiet job search and IPA sequencing ([career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)) |

Here's the part people get wrong. **Calling TAFEP does not cancel your pass.** What starts the cancellation clock is the employment ending, whether that's you resigning, the company dismissing you, or a retrenchment. The report itself isn't the trigger. It's worth sitting with that distinction before you decide to say nothing for another six months.

## Planning around the pass, scenario by scenario

| Where you are | How to operate |
| --- | --- |
| You can stay, and the culture could actually change | Put the request to stop in writing, take it to HR, escalate to TAFEP if that goes nowhere, and keep working while the file moves |
| You need to leave fast for your own safety | Notice and salary in lieu still apply ([leave and notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep)). Harassment is not a free walk out of a contract, however unfair that feels in the moment |
| The best path is a new employer | Get the IPA in hand before you resign, wherever the timing lets you |
| You were dismissed after reporting in good faith | That's the wrongful-dismissal route through TADM ([salary and TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom), [filing clocks](/guides/tadm-salary-claim-ep-filing-clocks-singapore)) |
| The pass is ending anyway | STVP and exit choreography ([between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)) |

One more thing that gets retold badly on forums. If MOM tells someone to pay short notice, that's a ruling about contract notice and nothing more. It is not a finding that the harassment was fine. The two questions run on separate tracks, and losing the first doesn't settle the second.

## The short version

Safety first. Then the written trail. Then TAFEP, if the internal route has stalled. Then plan the IPA, the notice period and any TADM filing on purpose, rather than deciding all three in a panic on a Friday afternoon. The parent channel map stays on [workplace harassment](/guides/workplace-harassment-tafep-ep).
