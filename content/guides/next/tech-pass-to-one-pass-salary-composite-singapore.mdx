## Cash floor ≠ composite floor

EDB Tech.Pass has long centred a **S$22,500** last-drawn fixed monthly salary plus company-scale experience. MOM’s March 2026 factsheet points ONE Pass (AI and Tech) toward combining fixed cash (**≥ S$22.5k**) with **vested non-cash** components toward a **S$30k**/month composite, plus company-scale / experience tests. Forum math that ignores vesting clocks is how late-2026 filings miss.

## Pre-sunset checklist

1. Confirm whether you need **new / renewal Tech.Pass** before **28 January 2027** — after that date EDB stops those filings.  
2. Build a composite worksheet: fixed cash + vested equity / other published components — do not invent categories from Slack.  
3. Keep a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) Plan B if the employer will file COMPASS.  
4. If outstanding achievements are the real story, package that track separately — do not blend with composite folklore.  
5. Start the next pass while Tech.Pass is still live; do not wait for a courtesy conversion letter.

## Decision rule

**Treat Tech.Pass S$22.5k cash and ONE Pass composite as different products — stage the handoff on live MOM/EDB text before EDB closes renewals, not on forum auto-convert myths.**
