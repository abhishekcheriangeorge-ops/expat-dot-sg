## A cash floor is not a composite floor

EDB Tech.Pass has long centred on a **S$22,500** last-drawn fixed monthly salary plus company-scale experience. MOM’s March 2026 factsheet points ONE Pass (AI and Tech) somewhere different: fixed cash of at least S$22.5k combined with vested non-cash components, working toward a S$30k/month composite, alongside company-scale and experience tests.

The two numbers sit close enough together that people assume they're the same test. They aren't, and the difference is filled by vesting clocks — which is precisely what the forum arithmetic leaves out, and why late-2026 filings miss.

Four pieces of folklore worth retiring before you plan around them.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “My Tech.Pass S$22.5k auto-converts to ONE Pass” | There is no automatic switch; file the next pass while Tech.Pass is still valid |
| “RSUs count the day the grant letter lands” | The composite direction usually cares about vested value, so verify the live MOM text |
| “Outstanding achievements is the same as composite” | Different story altogether: see [achievements evidence](/guides/one-pass-outstanding-achievements-evidence-singapore) |
| “PEP is always easier than ONE Pass composite” | PEP is generally one-shot; compare with the [chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore) |

## What to do before the sunset

Start by working out whether you need a new or renewal Tech.Pass at all. If you do, it has to be filed before **28 January 2027**, because after that date EDB stops accepting those filings.

Then build yourself a composite worksheet: fixed cash, plus vested equity, plus whatever other components are actually published. Don't invent categories from a Slack thread. A worksheet that quietly includes something MOM doesn't count is worse than no worksheet, because it tells you that you're comfortably clear when you aren't.

Keep a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) as your Plan B if your employer is willing to file COMPASS. And if outstanding achievements are genuinely your story, package that track separately rather than blending it into composite folklore.

The last one matters most: start the next pass while your Tech.Pass is still live. Nobody sends a courtesy conversion letter.

## The rule to hold on to

Treat Tech.Pass S$22.5k cash and the ONE Pass composite as different products. Stage the handoff against live MOM and EDB text before EDB closes renewals, rather than against forum auto-convert myths.
