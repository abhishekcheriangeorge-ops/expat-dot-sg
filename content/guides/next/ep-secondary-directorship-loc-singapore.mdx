This one is about the board seat you get offered at a second company while you're an EP employee somewhere else. Reddit collapses that into "I'll just add myself as a director of the side Pte Ltd." It doesn't work like that, and the gap between the ACRA filing and your actual authorisation is where people get into trouble.

## The LOC is the thing, not the ACRA filing

MOM's secondary-directorship path covers one specific situation: an EP holder being appointed to the board of a related company, with the primary employer's consent and a Letter of Consent behind it. Registering the appointment at ACRA is a filing. It records who sits on the board; it says nothing about whether you're allowed to work for that entity. **ACRA registration on its own does not authorise you to work the second company.**

A few bits of folklore worth putting down. Owning shares doesn't let you run the company, because shareholding isn't work authorisation on an EP — those are two separate questions that happen to involve the same business. "Unpaid advisory is always fine" isn't true either; unpaid charity boards sit under a different FAQ, and a paid commercial seat needs the LOC path. "Related" has a narrow meaning too: the relatedness has to be visible in ACRA shareholding or group records that MOM can verify, not in the fact that you all know each other from the same industry WhatsApp group. And a nod from HR on Slack isn't consent. You want it written down, because the written consent is what goes into the LOC file.

## What to have in hand before you accept the seat

1. Confirm on ACRA that the appointing company is genuinely related to your employer, through group structure or shareholding, and not just a friend's startup.
2. Get your primary employer's consent in writing, covering who you'd be, the role, whether it's paid, and the meeting load.
3. Have the LOC before you start acting as a director in Singapore, not after the first board meeting.
4. Track the LOC's validity against the EP underneath it. If that EP is cancelled, the LOC story goes with it.
5. Don't invoice Singapore clients through the side company as "director fees" until you've read [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## The short version

Primary EP, a related ACRA entity, written consent, and the LOC itself. Miss any one of those and the board seat is blocked, not pending. Treating it as pending paperwork is how people end up sitting on a board they have no cover to sit on.
