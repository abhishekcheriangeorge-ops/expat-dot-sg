This companion covers the layer people skip once they've been approved: what you have to keep proving afterwards. A lot of holders treat PEP as a permanent high-earner club card. It isn't one.

## The bar to get in isn't the bar to stay in

PEP is personalised and not tied to an employer, which is exactly what makes it feel like freedom and exactly why holders stop paying attention to it. MOM publishes ongoing income expectations for the people holding one. The figure usually discussed is keeping roughly **S$22,500** a month in fixed salary, or annualised evidence amounting to the same thing, and you should verify that live rather than trusting a number in a guide. Fall below it and you discover that PEP behaves much more like a one-shot high-earner path than a renewable EP.

| What people believe | What holds up |
| --- | --- |
| "PEP never actually gets checked" | Keep your evidence as though MOM will ask tomorrow. IRAS and payroll trails carry weight; a screenshot of your LinkedIn title doesn't |
| "Side consulting tops up the number" | PEP is hostile to casual freelancing, and the top-up story is where people talk themselves into trouble. See [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) |
| "I'll just renew it like an EP" | PEP generally isn't the renewable EP product, so plan your exit path early rather than at expiry |
| "A few gap months after a layoff won't matter" | Job-hop and unpaid gaps puncture the keep story, which is why you model [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) before you need to |

## The evidence pack, kept current

1. Fixed monthly salary letters or contracts covering each spell with each employer
2. IRAS NOA and IR8A trails that match the income story you're telling
3. Bank salary credits that reconcile to fixed pay rather than to one-off bonuses
4. A calendar of your employment gaps, because unexplained months are the weak point

Write down your Plan B while the income is still healthy: back to a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore), across to a [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore), or out. Doing that thinking before the dip is much easier than doing it during one.

## The decision rule

**If you can't document the keep threshold with payroll and IRAS trails, treat your PEP as expiring**, not as quietly renewable. That single assumption is what separates the people who plan a move from the people who get surprised by one.
