r/NationalServiceSG and r/askSingapore still sell "just renounce PR before enlistment" as a clean exit. It isn't one. The paperwork itself is covered in our process guide, [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore); this page is the trap map that sits next to it — who actually gets hurt, which clock you're on, and why letting your REP quietly die isn't the shortcut it looks like.

## Work out which clock you're on before you touch a form

What renunciation does to you depends almost entirely on where the NS-liable male in the household sits on the NS timeline. Find your row before you read anything else.

| Where he is now | What renunciation actually looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-enlistment (registered or lettered, not yet NSF) | ICA may withhold renunciation while NS obligations are unresolved. Parents who treat it as an NS loophole still carry Exit Permit and bond exposure until the status genuinely ends — see [Exit Permit](/guides/ns-exit-permit-pr-families). |
| Serving NSF | ICA's pack requires a clearance letter from MINDEF, SPF, or SCDF. Going AWOL and calling it "paperwork pending" is how people end up with a criminal problem and an immigration problem at the same time. |
| After ORD (NSman) | Usually an immigration or life decision rather than an ORNS escape hatch. You still need [NSman Exit Permit](/guides/ns-exit-permit-nsman-edge-cases-singapore) literacy until status ends, and ICA's future-facilities warning still applies if obligations were skipped earlier. |
| PR already lost through an expired REP overseas | ICA says formal renunciation isn't required once PR is already gone. NS liability history doesn't get wiped along with it. If the loss is recent, read the [PRSEP / 180-day FAQ](/guides/prsep-180-day-rep-faq-singapore). |

The government can reject or withhold renunciation outright when NS or other agency matters are outstanding. That is published discretion, not a Reddit rumour, and it's the assumption most plans quietly skip over.

## The traps that hit the whole household

1. The sponsor cascade. When the main applicant renounces, dependants who got PR under that sponsorship generally have to renounce too. So "Dad renounces so the son dodges NS" can **pull the mother's and siblings' status out in the same file**. Families rarely plan for that, because they're thinking about one person's problem.
2. The adverse-impact warning is real. ICA's PR page warns that renouncing or losing PR without serving or completing full-time NS has a serious adverse impact on later work and study applications, and on long-term immigration facilities, including for family members. The "my friend got an EP anyway" story on every forum thread is one data point, not a policy override.
3. Annex B and the MINDEF acknowledgement. NS-liable male dependants and Foreign Student Scheme cases need the MINDEF forms ICA lists. Incomplete packs stall, and forged or empty reasons invite exactly the scrutiny you were hoping to avoid.
4. A lapsed REP is not a planned renunciation. Letting your REP die while you're overseas can destroy PR without ever producing a clean renunciation letter, a sequenced CPF closure, or any Visit Pass runway. If you've decided to go, file renunciation on purpose and work through the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).
5. The wrong pack entirely. Singapore citizens don't use the PR e-Service; they use [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship). And dual-status confusion at 21 is an [ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) question, not a PR form question.

## What "serious adverse impact" means in practice

ICA doesn't publish a points table, so nobody can quote you a price. What families should actually model is harder scrutiny on any future Employment Pass, Student's Pass, or LTVP for the man who exited liability early, spillover risk when the same household later sponsors a spouse, child, or parent, and no expectation that a short overseas degree somewhere resets the file.

Before you conclude that renunciation is cheaper than serving, get written CMPB or MINDEF advice against your own dates. [Citizenship and NS implications](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) is the strategic twin to this list of traps.

## The rule to hold on to

If NS liability is live, renunciation is a MINDEF and ICA project, not an ICA-only PDF. Choose deliberate renunciation over silent REP death. Finish NSF clearance before you file if you're serving. And budget for household-wide status loss and harder future passes when someone exits without completing full-time NS.
