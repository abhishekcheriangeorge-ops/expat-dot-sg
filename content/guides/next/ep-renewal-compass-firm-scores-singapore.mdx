**Short answer:** Get **SAT screenshots that include firm criteria** before you assume last year’s salary clears this renewal — firm mix can move without you.

r/askSingapore renewal threads often blame the candidate’s **age-band salary** when the real wobble is **firm COMPASS criteria** that moved after a hiring spree. Age floors, SAT choreography, and S Pass contingency live on [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy). Candidate-facing COMPASS literacy lives on [COMPASS explained](/guides/compass-framework-explained). This companion is the **firm-score on renewal** layer.

Unless exempted, renewals still need **40 COMPASS points**. Your C1 salary percentile is **not** the Stage 1 qualifying-salary floor — and firm criteria (diversity / local PMET share) can drop even when your personal pay cleared last year.

## What “firm score” means in renewal English

| Lens | Candidate control | What drifts between cycles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stage 1 qualifying salary** | Negotiate fixed monthly pay | Your age band at filing / expiry (confirm with SAT) |
| **C1 salary points** | Pay vs sector/age benchmarks | Benchmark tables and your fixed-pay definition |
| **Firm criteria (e.g. diversity / local PMET)** | Low — ask HR | Headcount mix after new EP/S Pass hires or local exits |
| **Individual criteria (qualifications / skills)** | Degree proofs, shortage skills | Rarely the surprise on a same-role renewal |

HR owns the firm screens. You own the questions **before** they file.

## Questions to send HR in writing

1. Please run the **Self-Assessment Tool** on my renewal profile for the intended filing month and send screenshots.  
2. Are we still clearing **40 COMPASS points** after recent foreign / local PMET headcount changes?  
3. Which **firm criteria** are close to the edge (diversity nationality share, local PMET share)?  
4. Am I in any **COMPASS exemption** band (verify live thresholds — do not rely on Slack folklore)?  
5. If firm scores are soft, is the plan raise / delay filing / role rewrite / S Pass contingency?

| Symptom | Likely lane |
| --- | --- |

Do not answer a firm-score problem by uploading a random IR8A pack.
