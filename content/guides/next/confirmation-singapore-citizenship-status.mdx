**Short answer:** Need proof you’re Singaporean for a school/mission? Buy ICA’s **S$20 digital confirmation letter** — no stamp/QR. It is **not** ROE and **not** ORAL.

r/askSingapore’s “school / foreign mission wants proof I’m Singaporean” threads reach for birth certificates, pink ICs, and random PDF scans. ICA publishes a dedicated product: a **digital Confirmation of Singapore Citizenship Status** letter — fee **S$20**, typically **3 working days** when documents are complete.

## Who can apply

ICA’s eligibility line is broad:

> Any person who is a **current or former** NRIC/FIN holder, or who has a **Singapore birth certificate**, may apply.

That includes people clarifying status for foreign universities, other citizenship processes, or “am I still SC after missing ORAL?” conversations — the letter reports status; it does not rewrite the [ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) clock.

## What you get (and what you do not)

| Published fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Format | **Digital only** |
| Presentation | ICA states the letter has **no** signatures, stamps, or QR codes |
| Fee | **S$20** per application (card / PayNow) |
| Processing | About **3 working days** when required docs are in |

Do not expect a wet-ink “fancy certificate.” If a foreign authority rejects “unsigned PDFs,” ask them what format they will accept **before** paying S$20 twice — [apostille / notary](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore) is a different stack when they need attested copies of other documents.

## Documents and proxies

Typical uploads ICA lists:

- Applicant’s foreign passport / birth certificate / Singapore citizenship certificate / Singapore IC  

Proxy rules:

- Appointed proxy → authorisation letter + requestor’s Singapore IC  
- Children **under 16** → parent or parent-appointed proxy  
- Age **16+** → can request as themselves  

Follow the live form’s document list; incomplete files delay the three-day clock.

## When families actually need this

| Use case | Better product |
| --- | --- |
| Prove SC status to a foreign school / mission | Confirmation letter (this guide) |
| Under-21 SC clearing SG checkpoints on a **foreign** passport | [ROE](/guides/right-of-entry-roe-under-21-singapore) |
| Keep SC past 21 when dual by descent/registration | [ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) |
| Adult PR → SC application / IPA project | [Citizenship application](/guides/singapore-citizenship-application) |

Former citizens clarifying “what does ICA still say I am?” can still use this letter — it does not reopen renunciation.

## Decision rule

Buy the confirmation letter when a **third party** needs ICA’s status wording in writing. Use ROE for checkpoint facilitation on a foreign passport, and ORAL for the age-21 retention cliff — do not treat one PDF as a substitute for the other two clocks.
