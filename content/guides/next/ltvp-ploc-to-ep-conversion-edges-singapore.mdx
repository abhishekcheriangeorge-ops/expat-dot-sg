The parent map is [LTVP and PLOC to EP](/guides/ltvp-ploc-to-ep-conversion-singapore), with [PLOC vs LOC](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore) alongside it. This companion goes into the edges: what happens when a household starts an EP application while the LTVP or sponsor story is still moving underneath it.

## A stable household isn't the same as a stable conversion

PLOC lets eligible ICA LTVP holders work, which is why it feels settled. But an employer EP is a new principal pass in its own right, with FCF and COMPASS unless you're exempt. The two things rest on completely different foundations. So when the SC or PR marriage, the sponsor's own employment, or the LTVP expiry date starts to wobble halfway through an IPA, the cheerful Reddit answer of "just finish the conversion" stops being useful advice.

| What can shift mid-process | What it actually means |
| --- | --- |
| The sponsor's EP or PR story changes | LTVP validity can follow the household, so get the ICA and MOM calendars into email before you resign from PLOC work |
| The employer pauses FCF advertising | "They are already on PLOC" is not a published FCF exemption. See [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) |
| The LTVP expires before the EP is issued | Plan for the LTVP to stay valid until the EP is **issued**, not merely approved in principle |
| Divorce, or a child ageing out | Treat it as leaving the LTVP category entirely, and stage the EP before the visit pass dies |

## The checklist for the edges

1. Get the LTVP expiry and the sponsor's pass status in writing before the employer starts the FCF clock. Verbal reassurance from HR is not a date.
2. Don't cancel PLOC work rights on the strength of an IPA. Wait for actual EP issuance, unless ICA or MOM tell you otherwise in writing.
3. Keep the COMPASS documents ready even if there are months of PLOC tenure behind you. Tenure isn't an automatic points story, and nobody at MOM is going to infer it for you.
4. If the household is leaving Singapore altogether, map [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) as its own exercise, separate from the EP conversion.
5. For the wider picture on what this does to a career, we've written that up in [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

## The rule to hold onto

Convert on a stable LTVP and a real FCF calendar. A sponsor shock mid-process is an ICA and MOM problem to be solved on their terms, and it's not a reason to start treating PLOC tenure as a stealth EP. It never was one.
