## Stable household ≠ stable conversion

PLOC lets eligible ICA LTVP holders work. An employer EP is a **new principal pass** (FCF + COMPASS unless exempt). When the SC/PR marriage, sponsor employment, or LTVP expiry wobbles mid-IPA, Reddit’s “just finish the conversion” advice collapses.

| Shock mid-process | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsor EP / PR story changes | LTVP validity can follow the household — keep ICA/MOM calendars in email before resigning PLOC work |
| Employer pauses FCF advertising | “Already on PLOC” is not a published FCF exemption — [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) |
| LTVP expires before EP issuance | Plan so LTVP stays valid until EP is **issued**, not merely IPA’d |
| Divorce / child ageing out | Treat as leaving the LTVP category — stage EP **before** the visit pass dies |

## Edge checklist

1. Get **written** LTVP expiry + sponsor-pass status before the employer starts FCF.  
2. Do not cancel PLOC work rights on IPA vibes — wait for EP **issuance** unless ICA/MOM instruct otherwise in writing.  
3. Keep COMPASS docs ready even if you have months of PLOC tenure — tenure is not an automatic points story.  
4. If the household is exiting Singapore entirely, map [between-jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) separately from the EP conversion.  
5. Trailing-partner framing: [trailing partner career](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Convert on a stable LTVP + FCF calendar — mid-process sponsor shocks are ICA/MOM problems, not a reason to treat PLOC tenure as a stealth EP.**
