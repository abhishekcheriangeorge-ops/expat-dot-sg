**Short answer:** Missions **cannot** print NRICs — diary **one year after return** (or ICA-accepted early apply if abroad the whole window) and **collect in Singapore**.

r/askSingapore’s “I’m in London for my 30th — can the High Commission print my new IC?” threads die on one fact: **collection needs identity authentication in Singapore**. Fees, digital notice, and the 10-year replacement carve-out live on [NRIC re-registration at 30 / 55](/guides/nric-reregistration-30-55-singapore). This companion is the **overseas / return** layer.

Lost / stolen IC while abroad is a different rail — [lost / stolen NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) (overseas return notes live on that parent).

## Two overseas clocks ICA / regs publish

| Situation | Operating rule |
| --- | --- |
| You live abroad during the re-registration period | Re-register **within one year of returning to Singapore** (ICA page + National Registration Regulations) |
| You can prove you will be **resident outside Singapore for the whole** specified age window | Regs allow applying in the **year before** turning 30 / 55 — confirm with ICA; do not invent a DIY early file without their acceptance |
| Digital notice already landed while you are abroad | Subsidised **S$10 / S$50** window is still **one year from notice** when you are eligible — if you cannot collect in Singapore inside the approval’s **three-month** collection window, plan the trip before you pay |

Embassies do **not** hand over NRICs. Book ICA (or SingPost if offered on your notice) after approval.

## Trip plan that usually works

1. Confirm you are **not** inside the 10-year replacement carve-out (recent loss / particulars card may skip the age ritual).  
2. Keep **REP** valid if you are a PR — re-registration does not replace REP discipline — [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).  
3. File online (Singpass) when eligible; upload a compliant photo and PR passport bio-page if required.  
4. Fly for **collection** inside three months of approval — fingerprints / facial matching are Singapore-side.  
5. If renaming mid-file, PRs still need the **passport-first** name on the foreign book — [name change](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Do not expect mission collection → diary one year after return (or early-apply only if ICA accepts your whole-period abroad proof) → collect in Singapore.** Parent fees and notice flow stay on [re-registration at 30 / 55](/guides/nric-reregistration-30-55-singapore).
