## Approval ≠ sustainable renewal

EntrePass is renewable, but MOM’s **Total Business Spending (TBS)** and **local workforce** expectations ramp by cycle. First renewal is often easier than second/third. WhatsApp screenshots of old tables are not the live ladder.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “We got EntrePass once — renewals are automatic” | Later cycles demand higher TBS / more locals on CPF payroll — **verify live** |
| “Contractors count as local hires” | Locals usually need to be on the **company’s direct payroll with CPF** for the published months |
| “Shelf company + 30% shares is enough forever” | Ongoing venture / innovative activity + financials still matter |
| “Family passes will be fine if mine renews late” | Dependants ride the founder’s renewal risk — model household clocks early |

## Cycle planning checklist

1. Diary renewal from **~3 months** before expiry — not the week the card dies.  
2. Map **this cycle’s** TBS / local-hire bar vs your CPF payroll reality (not aspirational headcount slides).  
3. Keep shareholding / Startup SG Network / innovative-activity evidence current.  
4. If the ladder is about to fail, plan a sponsored [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) exit while the EntrePass is still valid — not after cancel.  
5. If household work rights were the real reason you founded, stress-test [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) / [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) honestly instead of forcing a fake startup story.

## Decision rule

**If you cannot clear the next published TBS / local-hire rung with real CPF payroll and financials, treat EntrePass as a bridge — and stage the EP (or exit) before the renewal window closes.**
