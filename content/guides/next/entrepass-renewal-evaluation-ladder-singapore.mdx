This companion is about the renewal evaluation ladder, which is where founders who treated year-one approval as proof the pass renews forever come unstuck.

## Getting approved is not the same as staying approved

EntrePass is renewable. What catches people out is that MOM's Total Business Spending and local workforce expectations ramp by cycle, so the bar you cleared on the way in is not the bar you'll be measured against next time. The first renewal is often the easy one. It's the second and third where the ladder bites. And the WhatsApp screenshot of an old table that a well-meaning founder forwards you is not the live ladder.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| "We got EntrePass once, renewals are automatic" | Later cycles want higher TBS and more locals on CPF payroll, so verify the live numbers |
| "Contractors count as local hires" | Locals usually need to be on the company's direct payroll with CPF for the published months |
| "Shelf company plus 30% shares is enough forever" | Ongoing venture activity, innovative activity and the financials all still matter |
| "Family passes will be fine if mine renews late" | Dependants ride on the founder's renewal risk, so model the household clocks early |

## Planning the cycle

Diary the renewal from around 3 months before expiry, not the week the card dies. Almost every fix available to you, hiring included, takes longer than a week to show up as evidence.

Then map this cycle's TBS and local-hire bar against your real CPF payroll rather than the headcount slide you show investors. Those two numbers drift apart quietly, and only one of them is what MOM sees.

Keep the shareholding, Startup SG Network and innovative-activity evidence current as you go along. Assembling it retrospectively inside the renewal window is how founders find out what they can't actually prove.

If the ladder is clearly about to fail, stage a sponsored [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) exit **while the EntrePass is still valid**. Once it's cancelled you're negotiating from a weaker position on a much shorter clock.

And if household work rights were the real reason you incorporated in the first place, it's worth being honest about that. Stress-test [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) or the [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) route properly instead of maintaining a startup story that the renewal evaluation is eventually going to test.

## The decision rule

If you can't clear the next published TBS and local-hire rung with real CPF payroll and real financials, treat EntrePass as a bridge rather than a home, and **stage the EP or the exit before the renewal window closes**.
