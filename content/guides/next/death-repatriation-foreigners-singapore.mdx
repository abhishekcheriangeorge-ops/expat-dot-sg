**Short answer:** In SG: doctor cert → **digital death certificate** (download fast) → **NEA bury/cremate permit**. Overseas PR/pass deaths: foreign cert then **ICA report**. Not the same as probate.

r/askSingapore threads on “what happens when a foreigner dies here?” and “reporting death of a foreign spouse” mix three clocks: **medical/ICA registration**, **NEA burial or cremation / repatriation logistics**, and **bank probate**. They are not the same job. This guide is orientation for next of kin — not grief counselling and not legal advice.

## Death in Singapore: registration is mostly automatic now

From **29 May 2022**, NEA’s citizen-facing guidance is that the public generally does **not** queue at a physical counter to register a death. Once a doctor certifies the death online, registration proceeds and a **digital death certificate** is issued (retrievable via My Legacy / the path ICA points NOK to). Next of kin should download it as soon as they receive the certificate number — NEA notes a **30-day** download window.

With the digital certificate (or, where that is unavailable, alternatives such as a manual confirmation of death / hospital printout), NOK or an authorised funeral director applies for a **Permit to Bury/Cremate** on NEA’s ePortal before government or private cremation/burial bookings.

| Step | Who / where |
| --- | --- |
| Certification | Doctor / hospital |
| Digital death certificate | Download promptly (30-day window per NEA) |
| Permit to bury/cremate | NEA ePortal (or authorised funeral director) |
| Embassy / mission | Notify for passport cancellation, consular help, home-country paperwork |
| Pass / FIN admin | Employer / ICA as relevant; do not invent timelines on Reddit |
| Estate / banks | Singapore probate / LA — [wills & probate](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore) |

## Death overseas of a Singapore PR / long-term pass holder

If a Singapore citizen, **PR**, or **long-term pass holder** dies **outside** Singapore, ICA’s published sequence is:

1. Register the death with the **foreign authority** and obtain that country’s death certificate  
2. Report the death to **ICA** (FormSG path on ICA’s overseas-death page) with identity docs, the foreign death certificate, and the deceased’s Singapore ID / long-term pass card when available  

ICA states **no fee** for the overseas-death report and a general processing time of about **three working days** after complete documents (complex cases longer). NEA’s overseas-death pages cover bringing remains or ashes into Singapore separately from ICA’s immigration registry step.

[ICA — Report death that occurred overseas](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/death/death_overseas) · [NEA overseas death](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/after-death/post-death-matters/when-death-occurs/overseas)
</OfficialSource>

## Repatriation vs local cremation

- **Local cremation / burial** — NEA permit first; funeral directors handle facility bookings.  
- **Repatriation of remains** — airline + destination-country rules, coffin permits, and often embassy letters. Budget and timelines vary wildly; start the mission and a funeral director the same day.  
- **Ashes** — usually lighter paperwork than a coffin, but still expect death + cremation certificates and airline screening rules. Destination customs are the hard gate, not “Singapore bans ashes.”

Keep passport biodata pages, marriage/birth certificates, and pass cards in one folder. Apostille / notarisation for foreign courts sits in [document apostille](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore).

## Pass-dependent survivors

When the main EP holder dies, **Dependant’s Pass / LTVP** status does not magically convert to a right to stay and work. Contact **ICA** early about Special Pass / LTVP options while you sort schools, lease, and employment — see [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) and [LTVP](/guides/ltvp-singapore). Forum anecdotes are not approvals.

## Do not confuse this with probate

Banks freeze Singapore accounts until they see a Singapore grant (or a properly resealed foreign grant). CPF (for PRs) follows **nomination**, not the will. Build that map while the person is alive — [wills, probate, CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore) and [LPA / AMD](/guides/lasting-power-attorney-amd-foreigners).
