**Short answer:** A new foreign passport doesn't carry your Re-Entry Permit across by itself. Run ICA's Transfer of REP / Update Travel Document e-Service, which usually takes about 3 working days, print the updated REP, and travel after that rather than before. **Transfer is not the same thing as renewal.**

The "I just got a new passport" threads for PRs on r/askSingapore tend to cover the mission appointment and stop there, which leaves the immigration half of the chore undone. Your blue NRIC isn't tied to a passport number. Your Re-Entry Permit is. ICA's published rule is that when you obtain a new travel document, you transfer the remaining REP validity onto it before you expect PR clearance on the way back in.

Worth separating this from its neighbours while we're here. It isn't an REP renewal — renewal extends validity, while a transfer moves the same validity onto a new book. It also isn't a [name change on NRIC](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore), which is a particulars replacement, or a [lost NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) replacement. Different forms, and mixing them up costs you a cycle.

## What ICA asks you to do

Renew or replace the foreign passport with your home country's mission first, since the transfer needs the new document's details to exist. Then log into ICA's e-Service with Singpass and run Transfer of Re-Entry Permit / Update Travel Document. Print the updated REP once it comes through. ICA advises carrying the printout when you travel, and a sheet of paper is cheap next to being the person at the counter explaining themselves.

Only then book the long-haul trip that assumes you'll re-enter as a PR. The order matters far more here than the effort does.

## What it costs and how long it takes

| Item | What ICA publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Fee | Nothing. There is no fee for transferring to a new travel document |
| Processing | Generally within three working days, excluding the day you submit, though some files run longer |
| Once it's done | Print the REP and carry it with the new passport |
| If you arrive without the update | ICA may only clear you as a PR if the REP shows the updated passport details; otherwise tell the officer you are a PR and follow instructions |

The folklore that your NRIC plus a screenshot of the old REP will do at Changi is how people end up in secondary inspection. The Dec 2025 Entry and REP conditions page restates the electronic transfer duty when you get a new travel document, so this isn't an old rule that people are over-reading. For the wider REP discipline, see [REP overseas](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).

## Transfer, renew, or something else entirely

| Situation | The ICA path |
| --- | --- |
| Same nationality, new passport number, REP still valid | Transfer REP, which is this guide |
| REP expiring within about 3 months, or already at risk | Renew REP instead: [the REP guide](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) |
| Legal name change on the passport and the IC | Passport first if you're a PR, then IC particulars: [change of name](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore) |
| Passport lost while you're overseas | Mission replacement or an ETD, then the transfer: [lost passport and ETD companion](/guides/rep-transfer-lost-passport-etd-singapore) |

## Putting it in the household calendar

Passport renewals belong in the same pile as the rest of your [annual life admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually) and [passports and documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas). Once the PR formalities are behind you, treat that first foreign-passport renewal as a paired project rather than a photo sitting: the mission appointment and the ICA transfer, held together in one task.

If your dependants hold their own REPs, each of them needs their own transfer when their passport changes. A parent's transfer doesn't reach a child's document, and that is not a thing you want to discover at a counter with a tired child beside you.

## The decision rule

New passport book, then the ICA transfer e-Service (free, around 3 working days), then print the REP, then travel. Renewing the foreign passport and stopping there is unfinished immigration homework.
