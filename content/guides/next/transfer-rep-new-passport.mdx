**Short answer:** A new foreign passport does **not** auto-move your **Re-Entry Permit**. Run ICA’s **Transfer of REP / Update Travel Document** e-Service (often ~3 working days), print the updated REP, then travel. Transfer ≠ renewal.

r/askSingapore’s “I just got a new passport” threads for PRs skip the immigration half of the chore. Your **blue NRIC** does not travel with a passport number. Your **Re-Entry Permit** does. ICA’s published rule: when you obtain a **new travel document**, transfer the remaining REP validity to it **before** you expect PR clearance on the way back.

This is **not** an REP renewal. Renewal extends validity; transfer moves the same stamp onto a new passport. Do not confuse it with [name change on NRIC](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore) (particulars replacement) or [lost NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore).

## What ICA asks you to do

1. Renew / replace your **foreign passport** with your home mission first.  
2. Log into ICA’s e-Service with Singpass and run **Transfer of Re-Entry Permit / Update Travel Document**.  
3. Print the updated REP for your records — ICA advises carrying the print-out when travelling.  
4. Only then book long-haul trips that assume you will re-enter as a PR.

## Fees and clocks ICA publishes

| Item | Published rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fee | **No fee** for transfer to a new travel document |
| Processing | Generally within **three working days** (excluding submission day); some files longer |
| After success | Print the REP; carry it with the new passport |
| If you arrive without an updated REP | ICA may only clear you as a PR if the REP shows the **updated passport details** — otherwise tell the officer you are a PR and follow instructions |

Forum folklore that “NRIC + old REP screenshot is enough at Changi” is how people earn secondary inspection. The Dec 2025 Entry/REP conditions page restates the electronic transfer duty when you get a new travel document — see [REP overseas](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) for the wider REP discipline.

## Transfer vs renew vs name change

| Situation | ICA path |
| --- | --- |
| Same nationality, **new passport number**, REP still valid | **Transfer REP** (this guide) |
| REP expiring within ~3 months / expired risk | **Renew REP** — [REP guide](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) |
| Legal **name** change on passport and IC | Passport first (PRs), then IC particulars — [change of name](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore) |
| Passport lost while overseas | Mission replacement, then transfer REP once the new book exists |

## Household calendar

Fold passport renewals into [annual life-admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually) and [passports & documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas). After PR formalities, treat the first foreign-passport renewal as a **paired** project: mission appointment + ICA transfer, not just a photo sitting.

Dependants with their own REPs need their own transfers when **their** passports change — do not assume a parent’s transfer covers a child’s document.

## Decision rule

New passport book → ICA transfer e-Service (free, ~3 working days) → print REP → then travel. Renewing the foreign passport without the transfer is unfinished immigration homework.
