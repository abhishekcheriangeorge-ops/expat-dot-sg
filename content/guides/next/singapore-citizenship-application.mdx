r/askSingapore’s citizenship threads ask the same cluster of questions: **Am I eligible after one year of PR?** **How long does ICA take?** **Do agencies help?** **What about dual nationality?** ICA publishes eligibility categories, fees, and a **within 12 months** processing orientation — not approval odds.

This guide is the **application process** layer. National Service liability for sons and ex-PR EP risk live in [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications). Adults who later leave SC entirely need [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship) — not this apply page.

## Eligibility ICA actually lists

You may apply if you fit one of ICA’s published paths (high level):

| Path | Published floor |
| --- | --- |
| Adult PR (apply with PR spouse + unmarried children under 21 in marriage / legal adoption) | PR ≥ **2 years**, aged **21+** |
| PR married to a Singapore citizen | PR ≥ **2 years** **and** marriage ≥ **2 years** (SC spouse sponsors with Singpass) |
| Unmarried child under 21 of an SC (marriage / legal adoption rules) | SC parent sponsors |
| PR student track | Residing in Singapore **>3 years** (of which ≥**1 year** as PR) **and** passed at least one national exam (PSLE / GCE N/O/A) or in IP |
| Aged parent of an SC aged 21+ | SC child sponsors |

ICA assesses family ties to Singaporeans, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, and length of residency — ability to contribute, integrate, and sink roots. **Eligibility ≠ approval.**

Forum myths to ignore:

- “The e-Service let me click through at year one, so the 2-year rule is dead” → UI openness is not a grant; published floors still matter for assessment  
- “Agencies know the racial quota” → ICA explicitly disclaims commercial “citizenship specialists”  
- “Longer pending = higher chance” → ICA’s published orientation is **within 12 months**; some cases take longer; delay is not a score

## Fees and processing (published)

| Stage | Adult PR / most children paths (as published) |
| --- | --- |
| At submission | **S$100** per application (non-refundable) |
| At completion of formalities | **S$70** Citizenship Certificate + **S$10** Identity Card (new citizens aged 15+) |
| Processing | Generally **within 12 months**; overseas-born children of SCs often **within 1 month** when documents are in order |
| Outcome | Email + MyICA / e-Service status |

Singpass: every applicant on the form must review and declare. ICA recommends finishing Singpass registration (**2–5 working days**) for all eligible applicants **before** you start the family filing.

## After IPA — Singapore Citizenship Journey and formalities

Successful applicants get an **In-Principle Approval**. You are **not** a citizen until formalities finish. For applicants aged **16–60**, ICA requires the mandatory **Singapore Citizenship Journey (SCJ)** programme before completion.

Treat SCJ + completion as a project (same discipline as [PR AIP formalities](/guides/after-getting-pr-checklist)):

1. Diary SCJ modules / sessions as soon as IPA lands — stalling delays pink IC and passport.  
2. Complete any document / renunciation steps your IPA checklist names **before** the completion appointment.  
3. Pay published completion fees (**S$70** certificate + **S$10** IC for new citizens aged 15+ as listed above).  
4. After the pink IC, re-check Singpass, banks, and employer records — identity rails change again.  
5. Unlock SC-gated schemes only after status is live (e.g. [Baby Bonus / CDA](/guides/baby-bonus-cda-mixed-status) for SC children).

Children who already hold SC by **descent or registration** face a **separate** adult-retention clock at 21 — the [ORAL guide](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) — not this SCJ checklist.

## Dual nationality and home-country law

Singapore’s adult citizenship model is **not** a casual dual-passport lifestyle for most applicants. Many new citizens must **renounce** a prior nationality to complete SC formalities; others face home-country rules that forbid dual status past a certain age. Reddit “keep both forever” stories are usually incomplete or jurisdiction-specific.

Plan with counsel in **both** countries before you file:

1. What must you renounce, and by when?  
2. Does renunciation affect inheritance, property, or return visas at home?  
3. Are sons’ NS obligations modelled ([citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications), [Exit Permit](/guides/ns-exit-permit-pr-families))?  
4. If a child already holds SC by descent/registration, who owns the **ORAL at 21** calendar ([ORAL](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21))?

Male applicants who later try to renounce SC without discharging NS liabilities can have renunciation **withheld**. Losing SC/PR without completing full-time NS carries serious adverse impact language from ICA/MINDEF for future work, study, and family sponsorship — same trap family as [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore).

## Agencies and “profile building”

Paid immigration consultancies cannot buy an outcome. ICA states it has **no affiliation** with commercial specialists and does not endorse them. Spend money on certified translations, accurate documents, and (if needed) dual-nationality counsel — not on a cover letter that invents volunteer hours.

If a prior citizenship application was refused, treat re-file like [PR rejection / reapply](/guides/pr-rejection-reapply): change **material** circumstances, or wait. Identical packages months later waste fees and years of anxiety.

## Decision rule

File citizenship when your household is ready for **permanent political membership** — including NS for liable males — not because a Reddit timeline says “year three is lucky.” If you are still modelling exit optionality, stay on [long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit) and keep [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) discipline while PR.
