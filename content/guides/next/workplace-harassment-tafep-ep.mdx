Sexual remarks in a group chat. “Jokes” about where you’re from. A subordinate who doesn’t stop after a clear no. The r/askSingapore threads about all of these share one assumption, and it is simply wrong: that foreigners on an EP or S Pass have no channel. Your pass status doesn’t delete workplace harassment protections. What it *does* change is the fear calculus, because the pass is employer-tied and everyone in the room knows it. Treat that fear as a planning constraint, not a legal veto. The pass-clock version of the plan is in [harassment and pass fear on an EP](/guides/workplace-harassment-pass-fear-plan-ep-singapore).

This is orientation, not legal advice. And to be clear about the top end of the scale: threats, stalking, unwanted touching, or assault are **police** problems first, before they are HR problems.

## What actually counts as harassment

TAFEP describes workplace harassment as behaviour that harasses, alarms, or distresses someone at work. That covers sexualised talk, humiliation, intimidation, and harassment in work channels online, which catches a lot of the behaviour people assume is too informal to count. The [Tripartite Advisory on Managing Workplace Harassment](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/employment-practices/guidelines/tripartite-advisory-on-managing-workplace-harassment.pdf) expects employers to prevent and manage it, and Protection from Harassment Act (PoHA) remedies sit alongside that advisory rather than replacing it.

| Channel | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Internal HR, grievance or whistleblowing line | Your first written report, unless the harasser *is* that channel |
| Regional or HQ HR | The local boss or the owner is the problem |
| TAFEP | You want advice, employer engagement, or someone to look at the policy gap |
| Police (999 or a report) | Assault, threats, stalking, anything where you fear for your safety |
| Protection orders and civil PoHA | Lawyer and courts, after the criminal triage |
| TADM wrongful dismissal | You get fired after reporting |

## Evidence that survives “he said, she said”

Most of these cases come down to whose account is more documented, so start documenting early, even if you never intend to escalate:

1. Dated notes covering who, what, where, and who else was in the room
2. Screenshots of chats and email, exported off company devices where you can lawfully do that
3. Names of the people who have noticed the same pattern
4. Any “please stop” or “keep it professional” messages you have already sent
5. Medical notes, if anxiety or injury is part of the picture

Don’t confront anyone alone in a closed room. A colleague with you and a written follow-up afterwards beat every revenge fantasy on Reddit, and they are the only version of events that survives being questioned six months later.

## Pass anxiety: plan the clock, don’t freeze

Your EP or S Pass is cancelled when your employment ends. Not when you call TAFEP. That distinction is the whole of it, because the thing people are most afraid of doesn’t work the way they imagine.

If you resign early to get out, you still owe notice or salary in lieu under the Employment Act, and that bill lands on you ([leave and notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep)). If the company dismisses you after a good-faith report, TAFEP’s own list points at the wrongful dismissal path through TADM, and we cover the mechanics in [salary and dispute rails](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom). Where you can manage it, secure a new IPA before you burn the old job rather than after; [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) has the sequencing, and the deeper version lives in the [pass-fear plan](/guides/workplace-harassment-pass-fear-plan-ep-singapore).

You will also see people quote MOM telling someone to “pay short notice to leave.” That is an answer about contract notice. It is not a finding that the harassment was fine. Those are different questions, and they get conflated constantly in forum threads.

## The practical sequence most people skip

1. Safety first. Leave the space, and call **999** if you are in danger
2. A written report to HR, or to the alternate channel, with the dates in it
3. Contact TAFEP if HR stalls, is conflicted, or turns out to have no policy at all
4. A parallel job search, if the culture is not going to change
5. Mental-health support, through [resources](/guides/mental-health-resources) and [mindline and emergency lines](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies)
6. Counsel, once PoHA orders, a settlement, or a dismissal claim are genuinely live; that is family and employment counsel territory

Violence at home runs on an entirely different rail, PPO and NAVH rather than TAFEP or your HR team. That one is [family violence for foreigners](/guides/family-violence-ppo-foreigners).
