r/askSingapore threads about sexual remarks in group chats, “jokes” about nationality, or a subordinate who will not stop after a clear no all share one myth: **foreigners on EP/S Pass have no channel**. Pass status does not delete workplace harassment protections. What it *does* change is the fear calculus — people stay quiet because the pass is employer-tied. Treat that fear as a planning constraint, not a legal veto — pass-clock plan: [harassment pass fear on EP](/guides/workplace-harassment-pass-fear-plan-ep-singapore).

This is orientation, not legal advice. Threats, stalking, unwanted touching, or assault are **police** problems first.

## What counts

TAFEP describes workplace harassment as behaviour that harasses, alarms, or distresses someone at work — including sexualised talk, humiliation, intimidation, and online harassment in work channels. The [Tripartite Advisory on Managing Workplace Harassment](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/employment-practices/guidelines/tripartite-advisory-on-managing-workplace-harassment.pdf) expects employers to prevent and manage it; Protection from Harassment Act (PoHA) remedies sit alongside that advisory.

| Channel | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Internal HR / grievance / whistleblowing | First written report, unless the harasser *is* that channel |
| Regional / HQ HR | Local boss or owner is the problem |
| **TAFEP** | Advice, employer engagement, policy gaps |
| **Police (999 / report)** | Assault, threats, stalking, fear for safety |
| Protection orders / civil PoHA | Lawyer / courts after criminal triage |
| **TADM** wrongful dismissal | You are fired after reporting |

## Evidence that survives “he said / she said”

1. Dated notes: who, what, where, witnesses  
2. Screenshots of chats / email — export off company devices if you can do so lawfully  
3. Names of people who heard the same pattern  
4. Written “please stop / keep it professional” messages you already sent  
5. Medical notes if anxiety or injury is involved  

Do not confront alone in a closed room. Buddy systems and written follow-ups beat Reddit revenge fantasies.

## Pass anxiety — plan the clock, do not freeze

EP/S Pass cancellation follows **employment end**, not a TAFEP call. If you resign early to escape, you still owe notice or salary in lieu under the Employment Act (see [leave & notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep)). If the company dismisses you after a good-faith report, TAFEP’s own list points to a **wrongful dismissal** path via TADM — [salary / dispute rails](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom). Secure a new IPA before you burn the old job when you can — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore). Deep dive: [pass-fear plan](/guides/workplace-harassment-pass-fear-plan-ep-singapore).

MOM telling someone “pay short notice to leave” is about **contract notice**, not a finding that harassment is fine. Those are different questions.

## Practical sequence most people skip

1. Safety first — leave the space; call **999** if in danger  
2. Written report to HR (or alternate channel) with dates  
3. Contact **TAFEP** if HR stalls, is conflicted, or has no policy  
4. Parallel job search if the culture will not change  
5. Mental-health support — [resources](/guides/mental-health-resources) and [mindline / emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies)  
6. Counsel if PoHA orders, settlement, or dismissal claims are live — entity: family/employment counsel category  

Family violence at home is a different rail (PPO / NAVH) — [family violence for foreigners](/guides/family-violence-ppo-foreigners).
