**Short answer:** Run **IR21 (cash freeze)** and **EP cancel → STVP (stay-only)** as **parallel calendars** — STVP days do not unfreeze pay, and clearance does not extend stay.

Leaving threads mix **IRAS cashflow** with **MOM stay rights**. Parent clearance map: [tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving). Final-pay / RSU freeze detail: [IR21 final pay & RSU withholding](/guides/ir21-final-pay-rsu-withholding-leaving). Stay after cancel: [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). This companion is the **sequence**.

## Two calendars that refuse to merge

| Calendar | Owner | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IR21** | Employer → IRAS | Withhold final pay / leave / bonuses from the date HR knows you are leaving; release only after IRAS direction |
| **Pass cancel → STVP** | Employer → MOM | When you may **stay** (often ask for up to **90** days; silence gets the short default) — **not** work rights |

An STVP day does **not** unfreeze withheld salary. IRAS clearance does **not** extend your immigration stay.

## Week-shaped orientation

| Phase | IR21 posture | Pass posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Written resign / known leave date | HR should plan IR21; withholding often **starts now** | EP still live while employed (including many garden-leave setups) |
| ≥ **1 month** before cessation | IRAS expects IR21 filing (or reasons if impossible) | Align last day with family / lease / school exits |
| Garden leave start | Clearance often expected at **garden-leave start** when applicable | Still employed for pass purposes until cancel |
| Employment end / cancel | Withholding continues until IRAS releases | Ask HR **in writing** for longest STVP; DP/LTVP knock-ons — [STVP myths](/guides/stvp-after-ep-cancel-special-pass-myths-singapore) |
| After cancel | Chase IRAS direction + release; keep Singpass/myTaxPortal access | **No work** on STVP; fly before the stamp dies |

## Cash + share-plan checklist (in writing to HR)

1. Cessation date for IR21 and which pay components will freeze  
2. Tracking arrangement vs deemed exercise on unvested awards  
3. Expected IRAS release timing vs your flight  
4. STVP length requested on cancel  
5. Whether IR21 covers ONE Pass / PEP cohorts the same way your HR assumes — verify live IRAS text  

Budget runway on [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs); ops checklist on [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook). Annual stay/exit planning: [stay/exit review calendar](/guides/annual-stay-exit-review-calendar-singapore).

## Decision rule

**File IR21 early → freeze is expected → STVP is stay-only → release is a separate IRAS event.** Never book non-changeable flights that assume same-week clearance **and** a long STVP without written HR confirmation of both.
