Threads about leaving Singapore tend to mash two separate things together: what IRAS does to your cash, and what MOM does to your right to be here. They run on different clocks, they're owned by different parts of your employer, and neither one waits for the other. The parent map for clearance is [tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving). The detail on frozen final pay and RSUs sits in [IR21 final pay and RSU withholding](/guides/ir21-final-pay-rsu-withholding-leaving). What happens to your stay once the pass is cancelled is in [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). This page is about the order you do them in.

## Two calendars that refuse to merge

| Calendar | Who runs it | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IR21 | Your employer, filing to IRAS | Withholding of final pay, leave and bonuses from the date HR knows you're leaving. Released only once IRAS gives the direction |
| Pass cancellation and STVP | Your employer, filing to MOM | How long you may stay. HR can often ask for up to 90 days, and silence gets you the short default. It is not a work right |

An STVP day doesn't unfreeze a dollar of withheld salary, and IRAS clearance doesn't buy you a single extra day of stay. That sounds obvious written down. In practice people assume one unlocks the other, book a flight on that assumption, and end up with neither the money nor the days.

## Roughly how the weeks fall

| Phase | Where IR21 stands | Where your pass stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You resign in writing, or a leaving date is known | HR should be planning the IR21. Withholding often starts here | EP still live while you're employed, including most garden-leave setups |
| At least 1 month before cessation | IRAS expects the IR21 filed, or reasons why it couldn't be | Line your last day up with family, lease and school exits |
| Garden leave begins | Where it applies, clearance is often expected at garden-leave start | Still employed for pass purposes until the cancellation goes in |
| Employment ends, pass cancelled | Withholding carries on until IRAS releases it | Ask HR **in writing** for the longest STVP, and watch the DP and LTVP knock-ons: [STVP myths](/guides/stvp-after-ep-cancel-special-pass-myths-singapore) |
| After cancellation | Chase the IRAS direction and then the release. Keep your Singpass and myTaxPortal access alive | No work on an STVP. Fly before the stamp dies |

## Five things to get from HR in writing

1. Your cessation date for IR21 purposes, and exactly which pay components will freeze.
2. Whether unvested awards go onto a tracking arrangement or get hit with deemed exercise.
3. Expected IRAS release timing, set honestly against your flight date rather than against a vague "a few weeks".
4. How long an STVP they will actually request when they cancel the pass.
5. Whether IR21 treats ONE Pass and PEP cohorts the way your HR assumes it does. Check that against live IRAS text, not internal lore.

Runway budgeting is on [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs), and the operational side is on the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook). If you'd rather not be caught out by any of this again, the [stay and exit review calendar](/guides/annual-stay-exit-review-calendar-singapore) turns it into something you look at once a year.

## The rule to hold on to

File the IR21 early. Expect the freeze. Understand that an STVP buys you stay and nothing else, and that the release of your money is a separate event on IRAS's timetable. Which leads to the one thing worth memorising: **don't book non-changeable flights** on the assumption of same-week clearance and a long STVP unless HR has confirmed both of those in writing.

If you're changing jobs inside Singapore rather than leaving it, the withholding edges of that same-city hop are in [EP job-change IR21 withhold](/guides/ep-job-change-ir21-withhold-edges-singapore).
