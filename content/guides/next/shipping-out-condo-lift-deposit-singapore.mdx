r/askSingapore exit threads stall on the same afternoon: movers arrive, the **service lift is not booked**, and the MCST wants a **refundable deposit** you forgot to float while IR21 is freezing final pay. Sell-vs-ship volume choices live on [shipping out household](/guides/shipping-out-household). This companion is the **condo / deposit / pack-out clock** layer.

## Book the building before the crate

| Item | Typical condo / MCST ask (verify your house rules) |
| --- | --- |
| Service / cargo lift booking | Often **3–7+ working days** ahead; weekends fill first |
| Loading bay / visitor parking | Time-slot limited; overtime fees common |
| Move-out / padding deposit | Refundable cash or cheque held until inspection |
| Protective padding | Often mandatory for lobbies and lift interiors |
| Quiet hours | Evening / Sunday bans are common |

HDB blocks have their own lift and common-area rules — still book early; do not assume “we’re leaving Singapore so rules flex.”

## Cash sequencing with IR21

Outbound moves collide with [IR21 withholding](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving): final salary may be frozen while movers, deposits, and flights still need cash.

| Workstream | Safer order |
| --- | --- |
| MCST / condo deposit | Pay **before** resignation-week cash crunch |
| Mover deposit / balance | Align sail date with destination keys + storage buffer |
| Landlord security deposit | Separate rail — [diplomatic clause / deposits](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) |
| Survive suitcase | Keep 2 weeks of clothes / docs **out** of the crate |

Budget line items sit on [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs).

## Pack-out day checklist

1. Confirm lift + bay booking in writing (email / condo portal screenshot).  
2. Pay / receipt the MCST deposit; photograph pre-existing lobby / lift damage.  
3. Stage cartons in a dry area — tropical rain + paper cartons is a known tragedy.  
4. Keep passports, work-pass cards, and IR21 paperwork on your person — never in the container.  
5. Do a final unit walkthrough with the landlord **after** movers leave, not during the chaos.
