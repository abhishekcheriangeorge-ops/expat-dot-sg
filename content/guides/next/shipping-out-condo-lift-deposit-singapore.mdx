The movers turn up, **the service lift was never booked**, and the MCST wants a refundable deposit nobody floated because IR21 has already frozen the final pay. Sell-versus-ship volume decisions live on [shipping out household](/guides/shipping-out-household). This one is the layer underneath it: the condo, the deposit, and the pack-out clock.

## Book the building before you book the crate

The building is usually the binding constraint, not the shipping line. Every condo runs its own house rules so check yours, but the asks cluster in a fairly predictable way.

| Item | Typical condo or MCST ask (verify against your own house rules) |
| --- | --- |
| Service or cargo lift booking | Often **3–7+ working days** ahead, and weekends fill first |
| Loading bay or visitor parking | Time-slot limited, with overtime fees if you run long |
| Move-out or padding deposit | Refundable cash or cheque, held until they inspect |
| Protective padding | Usually mandatory for lobbies and lift interiors |
| Quiet hours | Evening and Sunday bans are common |

HDB blocks have their own lift and common-area rules, and they aren't any looser. Book early either way. “We're leaving Singapore” is not a reason anyone's rules flex.

## The IR21 cash squeeze nobody plans for

Outbound moves run straight into [IR21 withholding](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving). Your final salary can sit frozen while the movers, the deposits and the flights all still want paying, which makes for an uncomfortable fortnight if you haven't seen it coming.

| Workstream | Safer order |
| --- | --- |
| MCST or condo deposit | Pay it before the resignation-week cash crunch, not during |
| Mover deposit and balance | Line the sail date up against your destination keys, with a storage buffer |
| Landlord security deposit | A separate rail entirely: [diplomatic clause and deposits](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) |
| Survive suitcase | Keep 2 weeks of clothes and documents out of the crate |

The line-by-line budget sits on [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs).

## Pack-out day

1. Get the lift and bay booking confirmed in writing — an email, or a screenshot of the condo portal. A verbal yes from the guardhouse is worth nothing on the day.
2. Pay the MCST deposit and take the receipt, then photograph any pre-existing lobby and lift damage before a single carton moves.
3. Stage cartons somewhere dry. Tropical rain plus paper cartons is a well-documented tragedy.
4. Keep passports, work-pass cards and IR21 paperwork on your person. Never in the container.
5. Do the final unit walkthrough with the landlord **after** the movers have gone, not while they're still working around you.

## The rule of thumb

No lift booking, no MCST deposit floated, and an IR21 freeze week landing on top: that combination is how a move becomes a same-day disaster, and all three are fixable weeks in advance.
