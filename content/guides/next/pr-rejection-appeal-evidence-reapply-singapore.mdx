The post-refusal threads on r/askSingapore blur three different moves into one conversation: appealing, updating a file that's still pending, and starting again from scratch. They aren't interchangeable, and the people arguing in the comments are usually answering a different question from the one you're asking. Pending updates and the wider refusal map live on [PR rejected: appeal & reapply](/guides/pr-rejection-reapply). This page is the narrow bit: what belongs on an appeal, and what should wait for a fresh filing.

Accept one thing before you start. ICA doesn't publish your individual refusal reasons, and it isn't going to. Working out "what they dinged me on" by comparing your profile to a stranger's approved one is archaeology at a site with no artefacts. You're guessing, and so is everyone advising you.

## Three doors, and you only get to pick one

| Where you actually are | The right door |
| --- | --- |
| Application still pending | Wait, or upload material updates inside the live file. Not the appeal e-Service |
| Rejected, and you want the same filing looked at again with extra context | The appeal e-Service |
| Rejection stands, the appeal failed, or your life has genuinely moved on | A fresh PR application, once circumstances have changed |

Whatever else you do, **don't open a second PR file while one is still pending or under appeal**.

## Appeal e-Service rules people skip

| What ICA publishes | The detail |
| --- | --- |
| Channel | The dedicated appeal e-Service, and only that |
| Not accepted | In person, by email, or by post, per ICA's appeal page |
| Who files | The original sponsor, with their Singpass. No sponsor, and it falls to the main applicant |
| Originals filed without Singpass | Use the same email address as the first filing |
| Outcome | Email plus online status; timelines vary |

The sponsor rule is the one that catches households out. If someone sponsored your application, they're the one who has to sit down with Singpass and file the appeal, not you. Work out who that is and whether they're actually available before the window is half gone.

## What belongs on an appeal, and what belongs on a refile

| Evidence type | Better on appeal | Better on a fresh reapply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documents missing or corrupted on the first file | Yes, supply the corrected pack | Only if you've missed the appeal window |
| Clarifying letter on facts you already gave (job scope, family timeline) | Sometimes | If the appeal is closed |
| A new sustained salary band, or a new employer | Rarely something the same file can absorb | Yes, this is material change |
| Marriage length, a new child, local schooling years that didn't exist before | Weak if nothing has actually changed | Yes, when the change is real |
| The identical PDF pack, six weeks later | No | No, and it wastes the non-refundable fee |

The pattern underneath the table is simple enough. An appeal asks ICA to look again at the file you already gave them, so it works when something was missing or unclear. A fresh application asks them to look at a different set of facts, so it needs the facts to be different.

ask.gov.sg's ICA framing is worth reading literally: after an unsuccessful outcome, consider a fresh online application when circumstances change. The operative word is change. The community's "hard six-month cooldown" lore isn't a rule and it's no substitute for your own rejection letter and ICA's live pages.

## The rule to hold onto

Still pending, update the live file. Rejected with fixable document gaps, use the appeal e-Service. Rejected but with a genuinely stronger profile behind you now, reapply properly.
