**Short answer:** **STVP = stay. Group medical ≠ STVP length. Travel cover ≠ work rights.** Bridge medical before cancel, and do not fly on STVP hoping re-entry is automatic.

Parent STVP rail: [between jobs on EP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). Plan audit: [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat). This companion is the **travel + medical gap** layer people discover mid-STVP.

## Three products that are not the same

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **STVP after EP cancel** | Lawful **stay** — up to **90 days** if HR asks; **no work** |
| **Group medical** | Usually ends on **last employment day** — not when the STVP stamp ends |
| **Travel / visitor medical** | Optional cash product — may cover accidents while visiting/staying; read exclusions for “job seeker” / pre-existing / outpatient |

Leaving Singapore on STVP can end your ability to return without a fresh visa or new IPA — treat outbound trips as **high-risk**, not a JB reset.

## Bridge checklist before cancel day

1. HR: longest STVP request **in writing**  
2. HR/broker: exact **group medical end date** for you and dependants  
3. Personal / travel medical start date that overlaps the gap (waiting periods, LOG, deposit cash)  
4. Do **not** schedule elective care into an uninsured week  
5. IR21 freeze is a separate cash event — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving)

IPA-before-resign still beats STVP optimisation — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

## Decision rule

**STVP = stay. Travel insurance ≠ work rights. Group medical ≠ STVP length.** Bridge medical cover before cancel, and do not fly on STVP hoping re-entry is automatic.
