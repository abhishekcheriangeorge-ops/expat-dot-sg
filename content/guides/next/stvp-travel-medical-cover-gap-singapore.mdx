The stay mechanics themselves live on [between jobs on EP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore), and if you want to audit the policy you actually hold, that's [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat). This page covers the bit people tend to discover halfway through the window: your medical cover has probably already stopped, and a quick trip out of the country could mean you can't come back.

## Three products, three different clocks

An STVP after an EP cancel buys you lawful stay and nothing else. Up to 90 days if HR asks for it, and **no work of any kind** while you hold it. It is a permission to be here, not a permission to earn.

Group medical is the one that catches people. It usually ends on **your last employment day, not when the STVP stamp ends**. Those two dates are set by different people for different reasons, and nobody is going to volunteer the gap between them to you.

Travel or visitor medical is the third thing, and it's a cash product you buy yourself. It may cover an accident while you're visiting or staying here. Before you assume it covers you properly, read the exclusions for "job seeker" status, for pre-existing conditions, and for outpatient care, because those are the three that quietly are not there.

Leaving Singapore while you're on an STVP can end your ability to return without a fresh visa or a new IPA. Treat an outbound trip as high-risk. It is not a JB reset, whatever the group chat says.

## What to sort out before cancel day

1. Ask HR in writing for the longest STVP they will request. Silence gets you the short default.
2. Ask HR or the broker for the exact group medical end date, for you and for your dependants. You want the date, not a reassurance that "you're covered for a while".
3. Line up personal or travel medical cover with a start date that actually overlaps the gap. The details that bite are waiting periods, the LOG, and the deposit cash a hospital wants up front.
4. Do not schedule elective care into an uninsured week. This is the mistake that turns an annoying month into an expensive one.
5. Remember that the IR21 freeze is a separate cash event running alongside all of this — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) has the mechanics.

None of this beats having an IPA in hand before you resign. Bridging is damage control; it is not a plan. [Career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) covers the sequencing that avoids the whole problem.

## The rule worth remembering

STVP gives you stay, not work. Travel insurance gives you cover, not work rights. And your group medical ends with your employment, not with your STVP. Bridge the medical cover before cancel day, and do not fly out on an STVP hoping that re-entry is automatic.
