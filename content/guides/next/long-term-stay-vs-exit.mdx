**Short answer:** Sit down once a year and decide on purpose whether you're staying another cycle or planning the exit, and do it before school re-enrolment deposits and EP renewal paperwork decide for you. Score career, partner work, schooling, family abroad, PR and NS honesty, and your cash buffer, then commit to one path for the next 12–24 months. The calendar and the lock-in dates are in [annual stay/exit review](/guides/annual-stay-exit-review-calendar-singapore).

By year three, inertia does most of the deciding. You renew because renewing is simply easier than not renewing, and another two years go by. So make it a deliberate call **once a year**, ideally before the school deposit and the EP paperwork arrive in the same fortnight and quietly sign you up for the next cycle.

## Score it on paper, not in your head

| Dimension | Stay signals | Exit signals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Career | Compelling next role / PEP–ONE optionality | Stalled COMPASS / renewal risk, no Plan B employer |
| Partner work | Real LOC / career traction | Chronic underemployment or DP limits |
| Schooling | Stable seat; costs still fit | Waitlist chaos or SEN capacity failure |
| Family abroad | Manageable visit cadence | Caregiving demand that remote money cannot solve |
| Identity / PR | Honest desire for deeper roots | Using PR only as a “maybe” without NS literacy |
| Cashflow | Buffer survives rent + school + insurance | One shock away from credit-card float |

Couples who only ever have this conversation out loud tend to discover, usually late, that they had been carrying different silent deadlines the whole time. Numbers are what surface that. Put the school fees, the rent, the therapy and the flights home next to the feelings and the gap becomes visible.

## PR and citizenship aren't free options

If staying long-term means applying for PR, read [PR strategy](/guides/pr-application-strategy) and [citizenship and NS implications](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) before you start treating the blue IC as a lifestyle upgrade. Male children can inherit NS liability pathways, which changes what your family owes rather than just what it's allowed to do. Verify that with CMPB rather than with the parent WhatsApp group, because the folklore in those chats is confidently wrong about the edge cases.

## If you stay

Line the EP renewal up with the school year rather than against it, which is the whole point of [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy). Then re-price the household at year-three prices, because the budget you built on arrival has drifted further than it feels like it has; the [year-one finance lessons](/guides/financial-planning-year-one) are worth rerunning with today's rent. And if SEN or therapy support is part of your family's picture, reconfirm those costs deliberately instead of rolling last year's figure forward — see [special needs support](/guides/special-needs-support).

## If you leave

Start the cashflow calendar early. The diplomatic clause, deposit recovery, IR21 withholding, shipping and school notice periods all run on their own lead times, and they don't queue politely behind each other. Work through [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs), the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook), and [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) while you still have an HR department that answers your emails.

<OfficialSource>
Employers and employees should follow [IRAS IR21 tax clearance](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/tax-clearance-for-employees) timelines — do not discover withholding on your final payslip by accident.
</OfficialSource>

The goal was never certainty. It's a shared decision with a date on it, one you can revisit next year without either of you having to rewrite the history of how you got here.
