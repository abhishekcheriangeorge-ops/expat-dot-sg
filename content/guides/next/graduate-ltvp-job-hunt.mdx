Reddit’s graduate threads mix three different LTVPs into one word. The pass ICA issues so a **foreign graduate of an eligible Singapore institute of higher learning** can stay and **seek employment** is a **stay** facility — not a work pass, and not the spouse/parent LTVP that sometimes pairs with a Letter of Consent.

## What this LTVP is for

| Topic | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Remain in Singapore after graduation (or between jobs, if ICA accepts your fact pattern) while you hunt for sponsored work |
| Work | **No.** Starting paid work needs an employer-sponsored **EP / S Pass / Work Permit** (or another MOM path that actually authorises work) |
| LOC confusion | Spouse / unmarried-child LTVPs of SC/PR may use **LOC** in published MOM categories. **Graduate-seeking-employment LTVP is a different category** — do not tell F&B managers you “have LOC” because a poly friend said so |
| Tuition Grant | Completing a bond still needs **authorised** work years — LTVP days do not count as EP employment |

Peers report ~**one year** of stay when approved; treat duration and renewability as **what your approval letter says**, not forum folklore about “automatic conversion.”

## Fees and processing (published)

| Stage | As published on ICA’s graduate LTVP page |
| --- | --- |
| At submission | **S$45** per application (non-refundable) |
| At completion | **S$60** LTVP issuance + **S$30** Multiple Journey Visa if applicable |
| Processing | Generally **within 6 weeks** when documents are in order; some take longer |
| Apply | Non-Singpass option available on ICA’s page |
| Documents | Recent photo (400×514), travel document, educational certificates and transcripts (+ certified translations if needed) |

Outcome by email; status via ICA e-Service.

## Timing — Student’s Pass end vs EP cancel

### Fresh graduates

Apply when your Student’s Pass / graduation paperwork supports the ICA checklist. Do not assume HR or the university “auto-converts” you — file and watch the clock so you do not overstay into a Special Pass mess.

### After losing an EP (local-grad path)

r/askSingapore’s recurring question: *Can I apply for graduate LTVP while still on EP notice, or only after STVP?*

Published ICA pages do not give a neat calendar for every ex-EP graduate. Anecdotes include ICA advising **case-by-case** timing and asking for a **termination / explanation letter**. Safer operating procedure:

1. Ask **HR in writing** to request the longest **STVP** available on EP cancel (up to **90 days** per MOM cancel guidance) — they cannot usually add it after the fact. See [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) and [retrenchment](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits).  
2. Call or write **ICA** with your graduation year, Tuition Grant status, and EP cancel date — get the filing window in writing for *your* file.  
3. Do **not** work on STVP or LTVP while “waiting for IPA.” IPA ≠ issued pass.  

## Job hunt realism

LTVP helps you stay for **in-person interviews**. It does **not**:

- Remove COMPASS / qualifying-salary screens for EP  
- Replace employer sponsorship  
- Let you freelancing-bridge rent ([side-income rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) are Move-pillar; graduate LTVP still is not a freelance licence)

If cash runway is short, switch early to the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) rather than burning deposits while hoping for a miracle week-12 offer.

## Decision rule

Use graduate LTVP as a **bridge**, not a career plan. Confirm work rights from MOM’s live pages for *your* LTVP category before you accept any paid shift — and treat [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) as the path back onto an EP.
