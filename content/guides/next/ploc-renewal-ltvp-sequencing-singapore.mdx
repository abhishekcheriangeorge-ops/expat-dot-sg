"My PLOC expires next month" threads on r/askSingapore nearly always describe the same situation: someone is trying to renew the work letter while the LTVP card underneath is already thin. That's the wrong end to start from. Which product you're even on, whether that's a PLOC, an employer LOC, or one of the graduate myths, we sort out on [PLOC vs LOC](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore). What follows is the renewal sequencing layer.

## PLOC: renew the LTVP first, every time

A PLOC's validity is tied to your current ICA LTVP or LTVP+. It's not an independent permission with its own expiry logic, which is why a short residual LTVP can only ever produce a thin PLOC. So if the stay pass is close to expiring, the order is fixed: **renew the LTVP with ICA first**, on the same at-least-3-months-before-expiry orientation ICA uses generally, and only then deal with the work letter.

When you renew the LTVP or LTVP+, tick the PLOC box if you're still eligible. That's the cheap path, and it saves you a second submission. Miss it and you're not stuck, but the fallback has a catch: the ad-hoc request goes in 2 days after you receive the renewed LTVP card, through FormSG, and the form runs on the **holder's own Singpass**, not the employer's and not an EA's. Your HR can't do this one for you, however willing they are.

Stopping work is simpler than most people expect. There's nothing to cancel. You simply don't tick PLOC at the next LTVP renewal, and it lapses with the pass.

The outcome email usually arrives within about a week, and it has a habit of landing in junk, so look there before you assume silence means trouble. Each PLOC is also assessed on its own merits, which means a renewal isn't a rubber stamp just because the last one sailed through.

## Employer LOC runs on a different rail

| Topic | Published orientation |
| --- | --- |
| Who renews | Your employer, or an appointed EA, through EP eService. Not you. |
| When | Up to 6 months before the LOC expires |
| Prerequisite | The LTVP or LTVP+ has to be valid for at least 3 months. If it's not, renew the ICA pass first. |
| Cost and clock | Free, and about 1 week, though some run longer |
| Cancelling | Within 1 week after the last day of notice, and you can cancel in advance up to 14 days |

Restricted occupations, and cases where a PLOC has been refused, still run down this LOC path; the parent guide covers who lands where. And if the LOC itself goes missing, it's the employer who reports the loss and arranges the replacement, not you.

## The short version

Diary the ICA LTVP renewal at least 3 months out, renew the stay first, and only then handle the work letter, whether that turns out to be a ticked PLOC box, an ad-hoc FormSG request, or an employer LOC renewal.
