"Lost my EP card in Bangkok, can I still fly home?" is a recurring r/askSingapore panic, and the replies reliably mix up three separate things: what the airline desk needs, what SGWorkPass proves, and when MOM will issue a re-entry letter. We cover the fees, the 1-week replacement clock and the in-Singapore playbook on [lost / damaged work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card). This is the overseas version.

Start with the reassuring part. Your pass status sits against your passport in MOM's records, so the plastic isn't the only proof that you hold one. That doesn't make replacement optional, and it doesn't do much for a check-in agent in a third country who wants something printable in front of them.

## What to do the same day

1. Open SGWorkPass, or your Singpass digital work pass, before anything else. That's your counter proof of validity, and it's the fastest thing you have.
2. Message HR the same day, with a photo of the police report if the card was stolen. This one matters because only your employer or their appointed EA can file most replacements in EP eService; you can't do it yourself from a hotel room.
3. File a local police report if it was stolen. It's basic replacement hygiene as far as MOM is concerned.
4. Ask HR to inform MOM immediately, and to request a re-entry letter if you have to fly before a new card can physically exist. That's the overseas-loss path in MOM's replace-card guidance, and it's the piece most people don't know to ask for.
5. Still complete the physical card replacement **within 1 week** of the loss. The digital pass doesn't waive the plastic clock, however smoothly it works at the gate.

## What actually goes wrong at the airport

The desk asks for a "visa" or for the FIN card. Show SGWorkPass alongside your passport, and have HR reachable on WhatsApp while you're standing there.

You lost the card and renewed your passport overseas. That's two problems rather than one, because the travel-document particulars need updating in EP eService and a broken passport link is a trap in its own right.

You assume the digital pass means you can skip the replacement. It doesn't. The 1-week employer filing clock keeps running whether or not the app looks fine.

You lost a blue PR NRIC rather than a work pass. Wrong agency entirely: that one is ICA, not MOM, and it sits on [lost NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore).

## What it costs, roughly (verify live on the EP card page)

| Case | Published band |
| --- | --- |
| Damaged card | About S$65.40 including GST |
| First loss or theft | About S$109 |
| Subsequent losses | About S$327 |
| Collection | Often around 4 working days after approval, at EPSC |

The escalation across those rows is the point of the table, and repeated losses can also earn you a MOM interview. If the old card turns up later, return it the way MOM instructs rather than leaving it in a drawer.

## The short version

An overseas loss means HR the same day, SGWorkPass for the flight, and a MOM re-entry letter if the plastic won't exist before you board. Then finish the card replacement inside 1 week regardless.
