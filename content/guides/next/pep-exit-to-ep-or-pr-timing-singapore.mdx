This guide sits on top of two others. [Personalised Employment Pass](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) covers the pass itself, and [PEP keep-rule evidence](/guides/pep-keep-rule-income-evidence-singapore) covers the income paperwork you're expected to be able to produce. If you're still choosing between products rather than exiting one, start at the [PEP vs ONE vs Tech.Pass](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore) chooser instead. What follows is the exit-timing depth, which exists because so many holders treat PEP like a renewable EP right up until the runway is already gone.

## PEP is a runway, not a forever card

PEP is personalised and not tied to an employer, which is exactly what makes it feel permanent. It isn't. It's generally a finite high-earner path with keep-rule expectations attached, and it doesn't behave like an EP you renew as a matter of course. The failure mode is always the same shape: you leave it until the last quarter to shop for an employer EP or open a PR file, and by then every remaining option needs more runway than you have left.

Four bits of folklore are worth taking apart before they cost you a year.

"I'll just renew PEP quietly." Plan an exit product early instead, whether that's a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) or a PR strategy. A renewal is not the thing you should be steering towards.

"Keep-rule dips are fine this close to expiry." An income dip can force an earlier exit rather than being quietly absorbed by the clock, which is why the [keep-rule evidence](/guides/pep-keep-rule-income-evidence-singapore) needs to stay current the whole way through.

"Unemployment months don't count." Model them against the published unemployment limit, and know what an [STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) can and cannot bridge before you need one.

"ONE Pass is a last-month flip." [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) has bars of its own, and they aren't the kind you clear in your final month.

## When to start, and in what order

1. Put PEP expiry in the diary **9–12 months** out, not the month the card dies.
2. If an employer-sponsored EP is Plan A, start the COMPASS and FCF conversations while your PEP is still healthy rather than when it's nearly spent.
3. Keep your keep-rule evidence current. Treat a dip as a signal to accelerate the exit, not as something to hope your way through.
4. Don't cancel PEP until the next IPA or issuance path is real and in hand.
5. If PR is the thesis, run it in parallel. It is not a substitute for holding a lawful stay product in the meantime.

## The decision rule

Stage the next lawful product while your PEP is still valid, whether that's an employer EP or another personalised path. What you shouldn't do is invent a quiet PEP renewal that was never on offer, or bank the household on flipping to ONE Pass in the final month.
