r/askSingapore’s “HR said my annual leave eats my notice” threads mix **three** MOM rails: unused leave **encashment**, taking AL **during** notice (with approval), and a separate **mutual** deal to bring the last day forward. Floor entitlements live on [Employment Act leave & notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep). This companion is the **exit math** layer.

## Three exit patterns (do not collapse them)

| Pattern | What it usually means | Who must agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Encash unused AL** | Pay out accrued, unused statutory/contract leave on termination (misconduct carve-outs aside) | Employer pays; you do not “spend” those days on the calendar |
| **Take AL during notice** | You are still serving notice while on approved leave; last day stays put | Employer approval for *when* you take AL |
| **Offset notice with AL** | Bring the **last day forward** so leave days replace calendar notice days | **Mutual** agreement — not a unilateral HR power move |

MOM’s termination-with-notice page is the primary text. Screenshot balances **before** you resign; WhatsApp “we’ll sort leave later” is how encashment fights start.

## MC during notice — still part of the clock

If you are covered by the Employment Act, paid **or unpaid** sick leave during notice **counts toward** the notice period. The employer **cannot** extend notice or claim short-notice damages for those MC days. Contracts that say otherwise are fighting published MOM guidance — keep the MC and the MOM link in the same email thread.

Unpaid leave during notice is different: the employer may extend notice **only with your agreement**.

## Decision rule

**Encash ≠ offset ≠ AL-during-notice.** Ask HR in writing which of the three they mean, keep balances + the MOM page in the thread, and do not trade a clean last day for a verbal “we’ll pay leave later.”
