## Three clocks still do not merge

Probation resignation ends **work for that employer**. MOM cancellation ends the **pass**. A new IPA is permission to **issue** another pass — not a bridge that lets you resign on a WhatsApp “IPA tomorrow.”

| Overlap folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Probation exit is same-day cancel” | Contract end ≠ MOM cancel — get the cancel date in writing |
| “Pending IPA is enough to resign on probation” | Best practice remains **IPA in hand** before notice — [calendar](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore) |
| “Gardening leave freezes cancel” | Gardening leave is contractual, not a MOM status |
| “DP stays until my new pass issues” | Family passes usually fall with the principal cancel — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) |

## Overlap checklist

1. Do not hand in probation notice on a verbal IPA — wait for the letter / portal IPA.  
2. Align last day, cancel date, and new start with **both** HR teams in email.  
3. If cancel lands before new issuance, map [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) — stay ≠ work.  
4. Inventory DP / LOC / lease / school deposits before you resign on probation.  
5. Training-bond clawbacks are a separate civil track — [resign after IPA on a bond](/guides/training-bond-resign-after-ipa-ep).

## Decision rule

**Serve probation notice only against written IPA + cancel calendars — overlapping clocks are planning problems, not MOM auto-merge magic.**
