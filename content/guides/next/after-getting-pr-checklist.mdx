r/askSingapore’s “I just got PR — what now?” threads are not lifestyle fluff. The first fortnight is an **identity swap**: FIN → NRIC, EP-world insurance → MediShield rails, and take-home pay that suddenly includes CPF. Miss the admin and you get bounced Singpass logins, unpaid premiums, and an HR payroll that still thinks you are on EP.

## Before the blue card — AIP formalities

“Approved in principle” is not the finish line. Peers routinely burn **weeks** between IPA email, medical upload, ICA biometrics appointment, and SingPost IC collection. Treat the portal checklist as a project:

1. **Book the ICA appointment immediately** — slots can sit **3–5+ weeks** out.  
2. Complete the **medical** and any MediSave / MediShield enrolment steps ICA lists for your IPA.  
3. Bring the documents the appointment letter names (passport, existing pass card, etc.).  
4. After biometrics, download / print **Entry Permit + REP**, then book **IC collection**.  
5. On collection day, **re-register Singpass on the new NRIC** — it does **not** always auto-migrate from FIN.  
6. Answer REP occupation / income fields **truthfully** — unemployed or between jobs at take-up can mean a **shorter first REP**; lying is worse than a 1-year stamp. See [REP](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas).  
7. You are **not** a PR for travel until formalities complete — while waiting, exit/re-enter on your **existing work pass** rules, not “almost-PR” folklore.

FIN → NRIC is a **new identifier** for banks and KYC in practice even when forum pedants argue about “same number forever.” Update every institution that still holds a G/F FIN.

## Week-one priority stack

| Order | Action | Why it bites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tell **HR / payroll** with Entry Permit / temporary IC | CPF cannot start until the employer knows your SPR conversion date |
| 2 | Complete **Singpass** re-registration on the new NRIC | Most e-services still keyed to the old FIN will fail |
| 3 | Update **banks, insurers, brokers, telco** | Cards and KYC freeze when FIN ≠ NRIC |
| 4 | Expect **MediShield Life** mail / e-notice | Auto-cover for SC/PR; premiums from MediSave |
| 5 | Read **CareShield Life** enrolment rules for your birth year / PR grant date | Foreigners who become PR from 1 Oct 2020 face mandatory rules that older cohorts may treat as optional |
| 6 | Watch for **DPS** (Great Eastern) enrolment after first CPF contribution | Term cover; premiums from OA — opt-out is deliberate, not default |
| 7 | Keep **Re-Entry Permit** valid before any overseas trip | PR is not a souvenir stamp — see [REP guide](/guides/re-entry-permit-pr-overseas) |

## CPF — remodel take-home before you celebrate

Employed PRs enter CPF. Default **graduated** rates apply in the first two SPR years; full citizen-equivalent rates from the third year (age/wage bands differ — use the live tables).

Directional framing for many private-sector SPRs under age 55 on graduated (G/G) rates (verify the [1 Jan 2026 PDF tables](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/content/dam/web/employer/employer-obligations/documents/CPFcontributionratesfrom1Jan2026.pdf)):

| SPR year | Typical story people quote | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Lower employee + employer % than full rates | Cashflow shock is milder; MediSave may still be thin for first MediShield premium |
| 2nd | Steps up | Recalculate mortgage / school fee capacity |
| 3rd+ | Full rates (e.g. 20% employee / 17% employer bands for many under-55 wage cases) | This is the “my take-home collapsed” Reddit moment |

You and your employer can **jointly apply** to contribute at higher rates earlier (full/full or full-employer / graduated-employee). Do it in writing via CPF Board processes — not a handshake.

## MediShield Life ≠ your old corporate plan

- **Automatic** for all SC/PR, including pre-existing conditions.  
- Benefits are sized for **subsidised B2/C** public-hospital care. Private / Class A bills still leave a large cash or MediSave gap.  
- Premiums auto-deduct from **MediSave**; top up if the first months of graded CPF leave MA short.  

Most new PRs then price an **Integrated Shield Plan (ISP)** on top of MediShield if they want private-hospital / higher-ward cover — compare on MOH’s official table, not an agent’s WhatsApp screenshot.

<OfficialSource>
[MOH — Compare Integrated Shield Plans](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/medishield-life/comparison-of-integrated-shield-plans/). Context for the EP years you are leaving: [health insurance for expats](/guides/health-insurance-expat).
</OfficialSource>

## CareShield Life and DPS — the letters you will ignore at your peril

| Scheme | What it is | New-PR gotcha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CareShield Life** | Long-term care / severe disability payouts | Foreigners granted SC/PR from **1 Oct 2020** generally have **mandatory** participation (disability rules differ by grant date) — do not assume the “optional for older cohorts” Reddit advice applies to you |
| **DPS** | Term life (death / TI / TPD), administered by Great Eastern | Auto-extended on first valid **working CPF contribution** for SC/PR aged 21–65; premiums from CPF OA; you may opt out deliberately |

<OfficialSource>
[CPF — Dependants’ Protection Scheme](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/providing-for-your-loved-ones/insuring-to-protect-your-dependants) and [CareShield Life](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/careshield-life).
</OfficialSource>

## Identity hygiene people skip

- Laminate / store the **Entry Permit**; print / attach **REP** discipline before travel.  
- Update **driving licence** particulars at Traffic Police when prompted (FIN → NRIC is not always automatic).  
- File a **CPF nomination** once contributions start — Public Trustee default is slow ([wills & nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore)).  
- Soft perks: NLB membership and NHB museum free-admission rules shift for PR — nice, not the reason you applied ([arts & culture](/guides/arts-culture-singapore)).  
- Housing: PR does not magically unlock every HDB path — read [HDB for PR / mixed status](/guides/hdb-for-pr-and-mixed-status).  
- **SkillsFuture:** **SkillsFuture Credit** (the dollar wallet) is for **Singapore Citizens** — PRs do **not** get that credit. Many course-fee **subsidies** on MySkillsFuture still list PRs (and sometimes LTVP+) at lower bands than SC mid-career enhancements — check each course’s funding table before you assume “PR = free upskilling.”  

<OfficialSource>
[SkillsFuture Credit eligibility](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/SkillsFuture_Credit.html) and [funding for individuals](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/SkillsFuture_Funding.html).
</OfficialSource>

## Decision rule

Treat PR formalities week as a **project**, not a celebration dinner. If HR has not confirmed the SPR conversion date in payroll within one cycle, escalate — every delayed month is delayed MediSave depth and wrong contribution tables.
