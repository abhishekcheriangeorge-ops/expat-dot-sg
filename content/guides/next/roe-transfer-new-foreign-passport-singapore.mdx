The "new US passport, old ROE" threads treat the endorsement as permanent ink. It isn't. ICA's published rule is that when the child gets a new travel document, you submit a fresh request to transfer the ROE onto that book. Who ROE is for, and the name-match trap sitting underneath it, live on [Right of Entry under 21](/guides/right-of-entry-roe-under-21-singapore). This companion is the FormSG and transfer layer.

## Filing it, and transferring it later

First, confirm the child is actually in scope: a Singapore citizen under 21 holding a foreign passport.

Then fix the name match, before anything else, if the IC and ICA records have drifted apart. This is the step people skip and the one that stalls everything downstream, so don't expect ROE on the foreign book while the records disagree. It's an IC unit job, or a [name change](/guides/change-name-nric-singapore) if it has gone further than a spelling.

Then submit ICA's FormSG ROE request with Singpass. If the child holds more than one foreign passport, that's a separate request for each one.

Then wait for ICA's confirmation, which runs about 3 working days when the documents are complete. There's no fee.

And then put it in the diary, because **when a new foreign passport is issued the ROE does not migrate on its own**. You file a transfer request against the new book.

On who files: under 16, it's a parent or a parent-appointed proxy. From 16 to 20, the child can file as the requestor themselves.

## What ROE still is not

Four pieces of folklore worth putting down.

Transferring an ROE once does not lock in dual citizenship forever. Retention is governed by [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21), which runs on its own clock and doesn't care what the passport endorsement says.

A confirmation letter is not an ROE. Different product, different job: [confirmation of citizenship](/guides/confirmation-singapore-citizenship-status).

A mission does not print an ROE the way it would a visa sticker. Follow ICA's online request and whatever live instructions come with it.

And one ROE does not cover every foreign passport the child holds. FormSG wants a separate request per passport, which matters if there are two books in the drawer and you've only ever thought about one of them.

## Decision rule

Name match first, then FormSG, one request per foreign book. Transfer on every passport renewal, and diary ORAL separately, because nothing in this process touches it. The parent overview stays on [ROE under 21](/guides/right-of-entry-roe-under-21-singapore).
