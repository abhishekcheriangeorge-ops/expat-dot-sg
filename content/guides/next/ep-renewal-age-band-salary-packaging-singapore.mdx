This companion goes deep on one thing only, which is age-band salary packaging. It's where renewals quietly fall over, because last year's fixed monthly no longer clears this year's floor for your age.

## The floor moves with you

Stage 1 is an absolute qualifying-salary gate, and it rises with age. It's also higher in financial services. So clearing it comfortably at 29 tells you very little about clearing it at 35, and that's the trap: the first application went through without drama, so everyone treats the renewal as paperwork.

New applications and renewals also move on different published calendars. Your renewal may not be sitting on the same table HR screenshotted last year, so check the live one for the month they'll actually file in.

## What to sort out before HR files

1. Confirm your age at filing and your sector band against the live MOM table, not the one from last cycle.
2. Separate fixed monthly from AWS, variable pay, and allowances. [Salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding) shows where the line falls.
3. Re-run the SAT with the figure HR will actually type into the form, rather than the recruiter's version of your package.
4. If you need a raise to clear the band, get the notified fixed monthly in place **before** the renewal is submitted. There's no fixing it once the file is in.
5. Treat firm-score risk as a parallel track, not a substitute for this one: [COMPASS firm scores](/guides/ep-renewal-compass-firm-scores-singapore).

## The rule, in one sentence

If your fixed monthly at your renewal age doesn't clear the live Stage-1 band, stop the filing and raise or restructure first. COMPASS points won't paper over a floor miss, because Stage 1 is a gate rather than a score.
