## The floor moves with you

Stage 1 is an absolute qualifying-salary gate that **rises with age** (and is higher in financial services). Clearing it at 29 does not guarantee clearance at 35. **New applications** and **renewals** also move on different published calendars — do not assume your renewal still sits on the table HR screenshotted last year.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “We renewed at this number last cycle” | Re-check the live age-band table for **this** filing month |
| “Total package clears the floor” | MOM cares about **fixed monthly** — [C1 packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore) |
| “Bonus / AWS will cover the gap” | Variable pay usually does **not** fix a Stage-1 miss |
| “HR can file first and raise later” | If the filed fixed monthly is under the floor, raise **before** submit — [salary / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes) |

## Packaging checklist before renewal file

1. Confirm your **age at filing** and sector band against the live MOM table.  
2. Separate fixed monthly vs AWS / variable / allowances — [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding).  
3. Re-run SAT with the figure HR will actually type — not recruiter “package” maths.  
4. If you need a raise to clear the band, get the notified fixed monthly in place **before** renewal submit.  
5. Firm-score risk is a parallel track — [COMPASS firm scores](/guides/ep-renewal-compass-firm-scores-singapore) — not a substitute for Stage 1.

## Decision rule

**If fixed monthly at your renewal age does not clear the live Stage-1 band, stop the file — raise or restructure first; do not hope COMPASS points paper over a floor miss.**
