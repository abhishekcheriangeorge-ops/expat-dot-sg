r/askSingapore death-admin threads stall at the same counter: **DBS/UOB/OCBC will not release Singapore balances on a foreign grant alone**. Estate rails (will vs CPF vs repatriation) live on [wills, probate & CPF nomination](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore). This companion is the **foreign grant → Singapore bank** layer.

This is orientation, not estate advice — contested estates need Singapore counsel.

## Why the freeze happens

| Asset | What banks / agencies usually want |
| --- | --- |
| Singapore bank / brokerage | Singapore **grant of probate** or **letters of administration**, or a **resealed** qualifying foreign grant |
| CPF (if PR contributions exist) | Valid **CPF nomination** — never the will alone |
| Condo / restricted residential interest | Estate counsel + possible Residential Property Act disposal clocks |

A Malaysian, UK, or Australian grant that worked at home often still needs a Singapore court step before local banks move.

## Reseal vs fresh Singapore grant

| Path | When peers use it | Reality check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reseal** a Commonwealth / qualifying foreign grant | Foreign grant already issued; assets in Singapore are secondary | Only certain grants reseal under the Probate and Administration Act framework — counsel confirms eligibility |
| **Fresh** Singapore grant / LA | No resealable grant, or domicile / form issues | Expect foreign-law evidence when the deceased died domiciled outside Singapore |
| “Just show the will” | Never | Banks want a court grant, not a PDF of the will |

Electronic foreign grants and sealed originals have specific filing / verification rules in the Family Justice Courts practice directions — do not invent a Reddit shortcut.

## First bag for next of kin

1. Death certificate + passport bio pages of deceased and executor / next of kin.  
2. Singapore bank / SRS / brokerage statements and account numbers.  
3. Original will (or sealed foreign grant) + certified copies.  
4. Marriage / birth certificates tying the applicant to the deceased.  
5. CPF nomination screenshot if the deceased was/is a PR contributor — [wills & CPF](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore).

Capacity planning while alive (LPA) is a different rail — [LPA / AMD](/guides/lasting-power-attorney-amd-foreigners). Certificates and repatriation logistics sit on [death & repatriation](/guides/death-repatriation-foreigners-singapore).

## Decision rule

Singapore cash without a Singapore grant (or reseal) stays frozen. Start counsel early; do not wait for “the foreign probate to finish everything.” Parent estate map stays on [wills, probate & CPF](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore).
