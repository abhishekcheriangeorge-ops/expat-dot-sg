The post-IPA threads on r/askSingapore panic about the same three things: SEV calendars that are already full, a CSS that only runs about once a month, and whether citizenship is simply "done" once the approval email lands. It isn't. In-Principle Approval starts a project rather than ending one. If you're aged 16–60 you have to complete the mandatory Singapore Citizenship Journey (SCJ) before ICA will finish your citizenship. For portal day-3 timing and booking hygiene in detail, we wrote that up separately: [SCJ SEV / CSS booking](/guides/scj-sev-css-booking-after-ipa-singapore).

Eligibility floors, fees, and the whole "should I even apply" question live on [citizenship application](/guides/singapore-citizenship-application). This one is the after-IPA lane.

## What the roadmap actually looks like

ICA's citizenship roadmap frames the post-IPA work as roughly a 2-month orientation. Treat that as a shape rather than a promise — if your IPA letter says something different, your IPA letter wins.

| Stage | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| IPA lands | Download the IPA letter. You are **not** a citizen yet |
| SC Journey Portal | Log in 3 days after IPA issuance |
| e-Journey + docs | Biodata, family, qualifications, employment, residency; upload the supporting documents |
| Sessions | Book your Singapore Experiential Visit (SEV) and Community Sharing Session (CSS) |
| Renunciation prep | If you are 21+ and required to: start renouncing your other citizenship with that country |
| Registration | Report to ICA Building on the scheduled appointment with the annex documents |
| Ceremony | Usually 3–6 months after registration; a GRC/SMC invite, and the certificate is presented there |

## The portal clocks people miss

The e-Journey isn't one long open window. Once you start the particulars step you have up to 7 days to fill everything in and upload your documents, and then up to another 7 days to check it for accuracy, submit, and pay. Budget around 90 minutes of portal work per individual application while you're at it. That figure multiplies when you're filing for a whole family, and it's the part people underestimate on a weeknight after dinner.

Portal login opens 3 working days after IPA, and the deep dive on that is in [SCJ booking after IPA](/guides/scj-sev-css-booking-after-ipa-singapore). The main thing is not to sit and wait for "more SEV slots next month" with no backup plan. What people who have cleared a packed calendar tend to do is book the inconvenient slot first, then use the portal's change flow if something better opens up, or go through the SCJ contact path named in their own IPA pack. **Where your IPA letter and a forum thread disagree, follow the letter.**

## Renunciation and NS don't go away

The SCJ sessions waive nothing. If you're 21+ and required to give up a prior nationality, start that process with the foreign authority early enough that the paperwork is actually in hand for the registration annex. Foreign bureaucracies move at their own pace and your ICA appointment does not wait for them, which is why dual-status folklore tends to die quietly at the counter.

Male applicants should read [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) before treating pink IC day as the finish line.

And if this is about a child who already holds citizenship by descent or registration, that's a different clock entirely — [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21), not SCJ or CSS booking.

## After you register

You become a citizen when ICA completes the registration formalities, not when a constituency gets round to scheduling a welcome event. So start the admin at that point: Singpass, banks, employer HR, and [eCOA](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore) if the address on the pink IC isn't where you actually live. The ceremony is the civic marker, and it's usually where the certificate is presented. Missing the party doesn't un-citizen you, but keep the invite.

## What to do the day the IPA arrives

Put portal day +3 in the diary. Book SEV and CSS before the calendar fills rather than after it has. Run the e-Journey inside those two 7-day windows instead of assuming you can come back to it. And get the foreign renunciation paperwork moving in parallel, so it's ready for the ICA Building appointment rather than chasing it. Anyone selling an "SCJ fast-track" is selling noise, because ICA already published the roadmap and there is no faster version of it.
