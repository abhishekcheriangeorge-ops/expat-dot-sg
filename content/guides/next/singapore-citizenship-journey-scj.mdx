r/askSingapore’s post-IPA threads panic about **full SEV calendars**, **CSS once a month**, and whether citizenship is “done” when the email lands. It is not. **In-Principle Approval** starts a project. Applicants aged **16–60** must finish the mandatory **Singapore Citizenship Journey (SCJ)** before ICA completes citizenship. Portal day-3 and booking hygiene: [SCJ SEV / CSS booking](/guides/scj-sev-css-booking-after-ipa-singapore).

Eligibility floors, fees, and “should I apply” live on [citizenship application](/guides/singapore-citizenship-application). This guide is the **after IPA** lane.

## Stage map (ICA roadmap)

ICA’s citizenship roadmap treats post-IPA work as roughly a **~2-month** orientation (your IPA letter still wins if clocks differ):

| Stage | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| IPA lands | Download the IPA letter; you are **not** yet a citizen |
| SC Journey Portal | Log in **3 days after** IPA issuance |
| e-Journey + docs | Biodata / family / qualifications / employment / residency; upload supporting docs |
| Sessions | Book **Singapore Experiential Visit (SEV)** and **Community Sharing Session (CSS)** |
| Renunciation prep | If **21+** and required: prepare foreign-citizenship renunciation with the other country |
| Registration | Report to **ICA Building** on the scheduled appointment with the annex documents |
| Ceremony | Usually **3–6 months after** registration — GRC/SMC invite; certificate presented there |

<OfficialSource>
Mandatory SCJ for ages 16–60: [ICA — Becoming a Singapore Citizen](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/citizenship/apply). Checklist orientation: [Your Singapore Citizenship Roadmap (PDF)](https://www.ica.gov.sg/docs/default-source/ica/files/esc-roadmap.pdf).
</OfficialSource>

## Portal clocks peers miss

ICA’s roadmap publishes tight e-Journey hygiene:

1. Up to **7 days** to fill particulars and upload documents after you start that step.  
2. Then up to another **7 days** to review accuracy, submit, and pay.  
3. Budget ~**90 minutes** per individual application for the portal work — family filings multiply.

Portal login is **3 working days after** IPA — deep dive: [SCJ booking after IPA](/guides/scj-sev-css-booking-after-ipa-singapore). Do **not** wait for “more SEV slots next month” without a backup plan. Peers with packed calendars book the inconvenient slot first, then use the portal’s change flow if a better session appears — or use ICA’s SCJ contact path named in your IPA pack. **Your IPA validity / instructions override Reddit.**

## Renunciation and NS — still on the critical path

SCJ sessions do **not** waive home-country renunciation or NS literacy:

- Adults (**21+**) who must drop a prior nationality should start foreign-authority processes early enough to meet the registration annex — dual-status folklore dies at the ICA counter.  
- Male applicants: read [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) before you treat pink IC day as the finish line.  
- Minors who already hold SC by descent/registration face a **different** adult clock — [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21) — not SCJ CSS booking.

## After registration

You are a citizen when ICA completes registration formalities — not when the constituency welcome event is scheduled. Still update Singpass, banks, employer HR, and [eCOA](/guides/ecoa-nric-address-change-singapore) if the pink IC address differs. The ceremony is the civic marker where the certificate is typically presented; missing the party does not un-citizen you, but keep the invite.

## Decision rule

The day IPA arrives, diary **portal day +3**, book **SEV + CSS** before the calendar fills, run e-Journey inside the published 7+7-day hygiene, and parallel-path foreign renunciation docs for the ICA Building appointment. Treat agencies selling “SCJ fast-track” as noise — ICA already published the roadmap.
