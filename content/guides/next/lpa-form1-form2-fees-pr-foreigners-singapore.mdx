r/askSingapore’s “do foreigners even qualify for LPA?” and “Form 1 or Form 2?” threads stop at fee folklore. The orientation guide is [LPA & AMD for PR / foreigners](/guides/lasting-power-attorney-amd-foreigners). This companion is the **Form 1 vs Form 2 + May 2026 fee table** layer.

## Form chooser

| Form | Who it is for | Drafting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Form 1** | Grant donee(s) **general** personal-welfare and/or property powers with **basic restrictions** | Most donors — complete via **OPGO** |
| **Form 2** | Grant **customised** powers beyond the standard Form 1 menu | Clauses must be drafted by a lawyer qualified to practise Singapore law |

Pick Form 2 when you need tailored limits (business assets, specific gift restrictions, complex donee panels). Everyone else should default to Form 1 unless counsel says otherwise.

Form definitions: [OPG — What is an LPA](https://www.msf.gov.sg/what-we-do/opg/lasting-power-of-attorney/what-is-a-lasting-power-of-attorney). Apply path: [MyLegacy LPA](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/find-a-service/lpa) / OPGO.

## OPG application fees (from 1 May 2026)

MSF’s citizen-facing [LPA fees PDF](https://www.msf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/opg/lpa_fees_table.pdf) and the [2026 fee regulations](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL-Supp/S272-2026/Published/20260430?DocDate=20260430) (in force **1 May 2026**) publish:

| Donor status | Form 1 | Form 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore **citizen** | **Free** (Form 1 waiver from 1 Apr 2026 as MSF announces) | **S$30** |
| Singapore **PR** | **S$30** | **S$30** |
| **Foreigner** (neither SC nor PR) | **S$160** | **S$160** |

Also published: cancellation of registration of an LPA — **S$28**. Pay on OPGO. **No refund** after submission to OPG, including rejected applications.

### Fees this table does **not** include

- **LPA Certificate Issuer** professional fee (doctor / lawyer / psychiatrist on OPG’s list) — separate, not set by OPG  
- Solicitor drafting fees for **Form 2** annexes  
- Later medical reports when a donee needs to prove lack of capacity to a bank  

## Myths to kill

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Only citizens can make an LPA” | PRs and foreigners can — they pay the PR / foreigner band |
| “Home-country POA unlocks DBS when I lose capacity” | Singapore banks want a Singapore-registered LPA (or deputyship) |
| “Form 2 is always safer” | Form 2 is for **custom** powers; most households waste money there |
| “LPA replaces a will / CPF nomination” | Different rails — [wills & CPF](/guides/wills-probate-cpf-nomination-singapore) |
| “AMD is the medical half of an LPA” | AMD is a separate MOH document — see the parent [LPA / AMD guide](/guides/lasting-power-attorney-amd-foreigners) |

## Decision rule

If you hold Singapore bank accounts, SRS, or property interests — EP or PR — make an LPA **while capacity is clear**. Default to **Form 1**, budget **S$30** (PR) or **S$160** (foreigner) for OPG plus Certificate Issuer fees, and store donee instructions next to your will and CPF nomination map. Deputyship after a crisis is the slow, expensive alternative this fee table is trying to help you avoid.
