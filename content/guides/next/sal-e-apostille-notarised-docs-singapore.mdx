**Short answer:** Confirm the recipient accepts **e-Apostille**, then choose **ACRA direct (~S$10.70)** vs **notary e-Apostille (~S$87)** — paper still wins when they demand physical form.

r/askSingapore’s “do I still queue at Adelphi for an Apostille?” threads lag SAL’s **e-Apostille** rails. Direction-of-travel basics (Singapore docs overseas vs foreign docs for ICA/MOM) live on [notary & apostille](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore). This companion is the **e-Apostille chooser**.

Confirm the **receiving authority** accepts electronic Apostilles before you pay — some courts and universities still want wet-ink / paper.

## Two e-Apostille products peers mix up

| Product | What it covers | Fee orientation (verify live) | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **e-Apostille for notarised documents** | After a participating Singapore **Notary Public** creates an **e-Notarial Certificate** | About **S$87.20** incl. GST per Apostille (same band as paper authentication) | Request at the **notary’s office** — not a pure DIY portal for the client as of SAL’s Oct 2025 FAQ posture |
| **Direct e-Apostille for eligible ACRA documents** | Selected Accounting & Corporate Regulatory Authority business profile / extracts via Open Attestation / trustBar verification | About **S$10.70** incl. GST per document | Upload via [SAL Legalisation Portal](https://legalisation.sal.sg/) — digital certificate emailed |

Paper Apostilles remain available when the overseas recipient rejects electronic form.

## When e-Apostille is the wrong first move

| Situation | Better path |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign-issued marriage / degree / police cert for **Singapore** ICA/MOM | Apostille / notarise in the **issuing country** (or embassy letter) — SAL does not “apostille” foreign originals as Singapore public docs |
| Destination is **not** an Apostille Convention party | SAL Apostille **plus** foreign embassy legalisation in Singapore may still be required |
| Recipient explicitly demands **physical** Apostille | Paper queue / collection — do not assume PDF is enough |
| Private affidavit / true copy | Singapore **Notary** first → SAL Apostille (e- or paper) on the notarial certificate |

Singapore has affixed Apostilles for outbound docs since the Convention entered into force here (**16 Sep 2021**). MFA no longer legalises outbound Singapore public documents — SAL is the competent authority.

## Cost-control checklist

1. Email the overseas school / court / registry: **e-Apostille OK?**  
2. If the doc is an **ACRA** extract on the eligible list, price the **S$10.70** direct e-Apostille before paying notary rates.  
3. For private packs, pick a notary on the [SAL directory](https://legalisation.sal.sg/Directory) who participates in the **e-Apostille for notarised documents** pilot if you want digital delivery.  
4. Keep the verification link / PDF with the underlying document — renewals love repeat stamps.

## Decision rule

Ask the recipient format first, then choose **ACRA direct e-Apostille**, **notary e-Apostille**, or **paper**.
