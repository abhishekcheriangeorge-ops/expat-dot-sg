## Salary track ≠ achievements track

ONE Pass commonly offers a **high fixed-monthly salary** path **or** an outstanding-achievements path. Mixing them in forum advice (“I’m almost S$30k plus a TED talk”) is how IPA disappointments start. Spouse LOC dreams sit on top of a real principal approval — [spouse LOC depth](/guides/one-pass-spouse-loc-depth-singapore).

## Evidence pack checklist

1. Map yourself to **salary track** or **achievements track** — pick one primary story.  
2. Prefer third-party artefacts (patents, peer-reviewed work, major competition wins, funded company exits) over self-authored bios.  
3. Date-stamp everything; stale glory from a decade ago rarely carries a live filing.  
4. Keep fixed-monthly / employer letters ready anyway if you sit near the salary band.  
5. Do not resign on “achievements IPA vibes” without a sponsored [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) Plan B.

## Decision rule

**If you cannot document outstanding achievements with dated, third-party proof, stay on the salary track or an employer EP — do not invent a ONE Pass achievements story from LinkedIn folklore.**
