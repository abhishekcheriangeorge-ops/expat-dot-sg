## A renewal is not a photocopy of the last IPA

The S Pass comes with two things the Employment Pass doesn't have: an employer quota, the Dependency Ratio Ceiling, and a monthly levy the employer pays every month you're on it. That's why your S Pass renewal is partly a question about your employer's headcount rather than purely about you.

So when HR says there's "no EP quota," it's worth asking what they actually mean. Sometimes it's COMPASS risk. Sometimes it's the salary budget. Often enough it's an S Pass headcount limit restated as though it applied to the EP. Then go and check the live levy and quota tables yourself instead of taking the summary.

A few pieces of folklore worth clearing up while you're in there. **The EP has no DRC quota; the S Pass does** — they aren't two versions of the same rule. The levy isn't yours to pay, but calling it "the employer's problem" underrates it, because it's the cost that quietly shapes how many "we can only do S Pass" offers you get handed. Renewals don't simply inherit last year's salary floor either: new applications and renewals often sit on different clocks, so verify the live figures for your own case. And clearing the S Pass salary bar doesn't unlock a Dependant's Pass on its own, because family privileges run off separate MOM tables ([Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore)).

## Questions to force before you resign or renew

1. Does the company actually have S Pass quota headroom in your sector's DRC right now?
2. Which levy tier will you sit on, and does that change the economics of the offer?
3. Is this a new application or a renewal, and which salary-step date applies?
4. If your fixed pay already clears EP floors and the role is professional-grade, why is the S Pass the only story on the table? ([Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore))
5. What's the household plan if the renewal slips? ([between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore))

## The short version

An S Pass renewal is a quota, levy and stepped-salary problem, and most of it sits with your employer rather than you. That doesn't make it safe to leave with them. Don't accept "no EP quota" as a substitute for checking the live MOM tables against your own fixed monthly pay.
