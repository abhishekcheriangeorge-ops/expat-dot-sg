## Renewal is not a photocopy of last IPA

S Pass carries **employer quota (DRC)** and a **monthly levy**. Employment Pass does **not**. When HR says “no EP quota,” ask whether they mean COMPASS risk, salary budget, or a mis-stated S Pass headcount limit — then verify the live levy/quota tables.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “EP has a foreign-worker quota too” | **EP has no DRC quota** — S Pass does |
| “Levy is the employee’s problem” | Levy is an employer cost that still shapes “we can only do S Pass” offers |
| “Renewal uses last year’s salary floor” | New-app and renewal steps often land on **different clocks** — verify live |
| “Clearing S Pass salary unlocks DP automatically” | Family privileges use separate MOM tables — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) |

## Questions to force before you resign or renew

1. Does the company have **S Pass quota headroom** in your sector DRC right now?  
2. Which **levy tier** will you sit on, and does that change the offer economics?  
3. Is this a **new application** or a **renewal**, and which salary-step date applies?  
4. If your fixed pay already clears EP floors and the role is professional-grade, why is S Pass the only story — [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore)?  
5. What is the household plan if renewal slips — [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)?

## Decision rule

**Treat S Pass renewal as a quota + levy + stepped-salary problem for the employer — and do not accept “no EP quota” as a substitute for checking live MOM tables against your fixed monthly pay.**
