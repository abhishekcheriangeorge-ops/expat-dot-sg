## Two different salary tests

Stage 1 **qualifying salary** (age-banded floor) is a hard gate. COMPASS **C1** scores how your **fixed monthly salary** sits versus peers in the same occupation. Clearing Stage 1 does **not** automatically max C1 — and boosting C1 with allowance folklore does not invent a Stage 1 pass.

| Packaging claim | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Total package S$12k” | MOM cares about **fixed monthly** components first |
| “Guaranteed bonus every year” | Variable / discretionary pay usually sits outside fixed monthly |
| “Housing allowance is basically salary” | Allowances often fail the fixed-monthly test unless structured as such |
| “SAT said 20 points so we’re fine” | SAT is orientation — the filed fixed monthly figure still has to match reality |

## Candidate / HR alignment checklist

1. Separate **fixed monthly** vs variable / allowances / RSUs in the offer letter.  
2. Confirm the figure HR will type into EP Online / COMPASS C1.  
3. Re-run SAT only after that figure is locked — not on recruiter verbal package maths.  
4. On renewal, salary cuts or title drift can move C1 and firm scores — [salary / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes).  
5. Keep screenshots of the SAT inputs you actually used.

## Decision rule

**Fixed monthly salary is the COMPASS C1 input — not total package folklore.** Align the contract line that payroll will pay every month before you resign overseas.
