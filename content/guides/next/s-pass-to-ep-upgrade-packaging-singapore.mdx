## A raise letter isn't an EP file

An in-country upgrade is a new EP application, assessed the way any other one would be. Your years on the S Pass are context, not credit. The packaging failures nearly always sit in the same three buckets: **fixed monthly** salary confused with the total package, FCF advertising proof that nobody kept, and COMPASS points carried across from the old S Pass offer that don't apply to the new one.

| Packaging miss | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| Offer shows OTE / allowances only | Stage 1 and C1 care about fixed monthly — [C1 packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore) |
| "We already employed them on S Pass" | FCF clocks still apply unless a published exemption fits |
| Degree assumed from LinkedIn | C2 needs verifiable awarding-body proof — [C2 edges](/guides/compass-c2-degree-verification-edges-singapore) |
| SAT screenshot from last year | Re-run it against this salary, this title, and this firm mix |

## What actually goes in the pack

1. A one-page fixed monthly salary confirmation that matches the EP form. Not the package, not OTE.
2. MyCareersFuture and FCF ad window screenshots with their dates, unless a published exemption fits.
3. A degree or equivalent pack in which the awarding body names match what's printed on the certificate.
4. A fresh COMPASS / SAT run using current firm diversity inputs, rather than a screenshot from the last hire.
5. A written Plan B that has you staying on the valid S Pass if the EP is refused.

That last item earns its place. Because the upgrade is a separate application rather than a switch being flipped, cancelling the S Pass early to keep the file tidy is how someone ends up with nothing at all when the EP doesn't land. Keep the existing pass alive until the new one is issued.

## The rule to remember

Package the EP the way you'd package a new hire. A raise on the S Pass is evidence for your own HR team; at MOM it isn't a conversion toggle.
