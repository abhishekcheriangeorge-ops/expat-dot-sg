Parent LTVP map: [Long-Term Visit Pass](/guides/ltvp-singapore). This companion is the **common-law evidence** layer Reddit collapses into “we share a lease.”

## Who this path is for

MOM’s common-law spouse LTVP sits under **EP / S Pass** family categories (published fixed monthly salary floor commonly **S$6,000** for that relationship row — verify live). It is **not** ICA’s SC/PR spouse LTVP / PMLA lane — [spouse LTVP + PMLA](/guides/spouse-ltvp-pmla-singapore). Legally married EP spouses usually belong on [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore), not this pack.

## MOM’s three acceptable proofs (any one)

Per [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/documents-required):

| Proof | What to get right |
| --- | --- |
| **Common-law marriage certificate** | Issued where that status is recognised — not a Singapore ROM substitute |
| **Joint affidavit** | Both partners declare a common-law relationship **recognised under home-country/region law**, **notarised in that home country/region** |
| **Embassy letter** | Home mission acknowledges the relationship under that country’s law |

Also upload passport biodata pages. Non-English docs: **original + English translation as one file**. Translation / notary pricing: [apostille & notary](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore).

## What does **not** clear the bar alone

Shared flat, joint bank account, WhatsApp timelines, or a Singapore statutory declaration that ignores home-country recognition. Employer (or EA) still files; published fees/timelines live on MOM’s apply page.

## After IPA — work and healthcare

LTVP alone is **not** work rights. Overseas-remote / local-client rules: [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass). Unsubsidised healthcare shock: [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat).

## Decision rule

**Pick one MOM-listed proof → notarised / embassy-correct → translate as one file → then file.** Do not build the household on “lease + screenshots.”
