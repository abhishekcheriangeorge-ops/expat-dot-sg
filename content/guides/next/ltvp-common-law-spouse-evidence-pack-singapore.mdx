The parent map for all of this is [Long-Term Visit Pass](/guides/ltvp-singapore). This companion covers the one layer Reddit tends to flatten into "well, we share a lease": what MOM will actually accept as proof that you are common-law spouses.

## Who this path is for

MOM's common-law spouse LTVP sits under the EP / S Pass family categories, and there's a published fixed monthly salary floor for that relationship row, commonly S$6,000. Verify the live number before you plan around it. This is not ICA's SC/PR spouse LTVP or PMLA lane, which is its own thing: [spouse LTVP + PMLA](/guides/spouse-ltvp-pmla-singapore). And if you're legally married to an EP holder, you usually belong on a [Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) rather than assembling this pack at all.

## The three proofs MOM accepts

You need one of these, per MOM's [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/documents-required) page. Any one of them is enough.

| Proof | What to get right |
| --- | --- |
| Common-law marriage certificate | Issued somewhere that status is recognised. A Singapore ROM certificate is not a substitute |
| Joint affidavit | Both of you declare a common-law relationship recognised under your home country or region's law, notarised in that home country or region |
| Embassy letter | Your home mission acknowledges the relationship under that country's law |

The thread running through all three is home-country recognition. MOM isn't asking whether you live together; it's asking whether the place you come from treats you as spouses. Upload the passport biodata pages too, and if a document isn't in English, **the original and the English translation go up as one file**. Translation and notary pricing is in [apostille & notary](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore).

## What won't get you there on its own

A shared flat, a joint bank account, a WhatsApp timeline going back years, or a Singapore statutory declaration that skips past the home-country recognition question. None of that stands in for one of the three proofs. Your employer (or an EA) still does the filing, and the published fees and timelines sit on MOM's apply page.

## After IPA: work and healthcare

An LTVP on its own is not work rights. If there's overseas remote work or local clients in the picture, start with [side income](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass). And plan for unsubsidised healthcare, which is the bill that catches couples out in the first year: [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat).

## What to actually do

Pick one MOM-listed proof, get it notarised or embassy-correct in the right country, translate it into a single combined file, then file. Building the household plan on "lease plus screenshots" is how this ends up rejected.
