Renewals fail quietly when everyone assumes last year’s approval guarantees this year’s. Qualifying salary floors move, COMPASS firm scores drift with headcount mix, and admin blockers (stale address, incomplete docs) create last-minute HR panic.

## Start the day the pass is issued

- Diary the renewal window in your personal calendar **and** HR’s  
- Keep employment history, degree certificates, and job scope descriptions ready  
- Track your cash salary vs prevailing qualifying salary for your age band — allowances packaging games are a renewal risk  

## COMPASS-aware living

Firm scores change as local/foreigner mix and diversity metrics change. Your personal competitiveness also moves with market benchmarks. Renewal is a **joint project** with the employer — not a personal Singpass click.

Ask HR annually:

- Are we still clearing COMPASS comfortably for my role?  
- Is my job title/scope still coherent with the EP application?  
- If criteria tighten, what is the contingency (role redesign, localisation, exit timeline)?

## Address and tenancy blockers

MOM expects a valid residential address. Reddit threads document EP holders stuck when landlords will not enable **FWTES** “allow pass holder declarations,” or when short grey stays cannot be registered. Fix address hygiene **months** before renewal — not during IPA-adjacent chaos.

Travel itself rarely “cancels” an EP if the employer keeps sponsoring; tax residency and life-admin still care how many days you are physically here.

## Contingency if renewal looks soft

1. Document achievements and scarcity skills for HR’s narrative  
2. Model household runway if the pass ends (see [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook))  
3. Avoid signing a fresh 24-month lease without a diplomatic clause if renewal risk is material
