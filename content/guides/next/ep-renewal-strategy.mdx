**Short answer:** Start planning the renewal the day your pass is issued. You have to clear the age-banded salary floor and COMPASS all over again, because last year's approval guarantees nothing, and ask HR to run the SAT before they file rather than after.

Renewals fail quietly. Nobody gets a dramatic rejection out of a clear sky; what happens is that everyone assumed last year's approval carried forward, and it didn't. The qualifying salary floor moves with your age, so you can be earning exactly the same money and still fall behind it simply by having a birthday. COMPASS firm scores drift as your employer's headcount mix changes, which is entirely outside your control. Then there are the dull blockers, a stale address or missing documents, that turn into HR panic in the last fortnight. Read the 2024–2026 renewal threads on r/askSingapore and it's almost all age-band salary, S Pass downgrades and slower processing. Not mysterious "blacklists."

## Start the day the pass is issued

Put the renewal window in your own calendar as well as HR's. MOM typically allows renewal from about 6 months before expiry, though confirm that live. The reason to diarise it the moment the pass lands is that if something looks short you want months of runway to fix it, not weeks.

Keep your employment history, degree certificates, verification proofs and job-scope descriptions somewhere you can actually find them. Track your fixed monthly salary against the qualifying salary for your age at renewal, not your age when the pass was first approved. Allowance packaging games are a real renewal risk here, and the edges of that are in [COMPASS C1 fixed-salary packaging](/guides/compass-c1-fixed-salary-packaging-singapore). Then ask HR to run the Self-Assessment Tool on your actual profile before they file anything. The SAT costs nothing and it tells you early whether this is a comfortable year or a difficult one.

## Stage 1: the age-banded salary floor

You have to clear Stage 1 before COMPASS points matter at all. MOM benchmarks the qualifying salary to the top third of local PMET salaries by age, which is why the number climbs as you get older, and financial services sits on its own higher set of floors.

Published orientation at last review, and do verify the live MOM tables because they update:

| Timing | All sectors except financial services (age 23 floor → age 45+ cap) | Financial services (age 23 floor → age 45+ cap) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New apps before 1 Jan 2027 / renewals for passes expiring before 1 Jan 2028 | S$5,600 → S$10,700 | S$6,200 → S$11,800 |
| New apps from 1 Jan 2027 / renewals for passes expiring from 1 Jan 2028 | S$6,000 → S$11,500 | S$6,600 → S$12,700 |

The rows people argue about (non-finance, current band, and again confirm your own live age row):

| Age | Approx qualifying salary (current band) |
| --- | --- |
| 23 or below | S$5,600 |
| 28 | S$6,759 |
| 35 | S$8,382 |
| 40 | S$9,541 |
| 45+ | S$10,700 |

Two questions come up every time. The first is whether your age is taken at the application date or at pass expiry, and the honest answer is that it's a question for HR and the SAT rather than a forum tie-break. Get the SAT screenshot for the exact month you'll be filing in.

The second is bonuses, 13th-month payments included. Stage 1 looks at fixed monthly salary and nothing else. **Don't assume an AWS or a variable bonus closes a S$600 gap** — it doesn't, and by the time HR works that out you've lost the months you needed to negotiate a real raise.

## Stage 2: COMPASS still applies on renewal

Unless you're exempted, renewals still need about 40 COMPASS points. The awkward part is that the firm-level criteria, diversity and local PMET share, move when your company hires or loses foreigners. Your score can slip while you personally do nothing differently. And your C1 salary percentile is not the same number as the Stage 1 floor, which is worth untangling before you try to reason about either.

Three things to ask HR once a year. Are we still clearing COMPASS comfortably for my role and nationality share? Is my job title and scope still coherent with what's on the EP file, especially if Shortage Occupation List bonus points were part of how you got there? And if the criteria tighten, what's the contingency: a raise, a role redesign, an S Pass with its quota and levy, localisation, or an exit timeline?

Firm diversity and local-PMET drift, plus the specific SAT questions worth sending HR in writing, are in [COMPASS firm scores on renewal](/guides/ep-renewal-compass-firm-scores-singapore).

Exemptions exist at a high level: a fixed monthly salary at or above the FCF advertising exemption threshold, published at S$22,500 at last review, overseas intra-corporate transferees, or roles of ≤1 month. Confirm all of those live.

Fair Consideration Framework advertising still applies to many new EP filings, and renewals follow MOM's published FCF rules for renewals. Have HR confirm which applies to your filing rather than assuming a renewal gets a free pass.

## When the renewal looks soft

The usual fork when age-band salary is short is a raise, an S Pass conversion, or leaving. Be realistic about the middle one: S Pass has its own quota, levy and eligibility rules, and **it is not a courtesy downgrade every employer will offer**. Model the household runway on the assumption the pass ends, using the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook), [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore), and [retrenchment benefits](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits).

Four things worth doing while you still have time to do them:

1. Document your achievements and scarce skills so HR has a narrative to argue with.
2. Get SAT outcomes in writing, screenshot and all, before anything becomes a panic negotiation.
3. Don't sign a fresh 24-month lease without a diplomatic clause while renewal risk is material.
4. If a refusal does come, the employer-led appeal and refile clocks are short. Treat immigration counsel as a timed option rather than something to mull over for a fortnight.

## Address and tenancy blockers

MOM expects a valid residential address, and this stops more renewals than it has any right to. Reddit threads are full of EP holders stuck because a landlord won't enable FWTES "allow pass holder declarations", or because a short informal stay can't be registered at all. Sort the address hygiene out months before renewal, not in the middle of IPA-adjacent chaos.

Travel itself rarely cancels an EP as long as the employer keeps sponsoring you. Tax residency and the rest of your life admin do still care how many days you're physically here.

## Extra document requests (NOA / IR8A)

MOM's renew page doesn't list a Notice of Assessment as a default attachment. Case-by-case emails asking for an NOA, sometimes confused with the employer's IR8A, are still genuinely turning up in 2025–2026 threads, so treat that as its own lane: [EP renewal NOA & documents](/guides/ep-renewal-noa-documents).

The related discipline is getting your declared fixed monthly salary to match payroll before HR files rather than after. Cuts get notified under [MOM change rules](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes), and the mechanics are in [salary / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes).

## How long it actually takes

Anecdotes of 4–8+ weeks, plus extra document requests, have been common since COMPASS matured. Build that buffer in before lease renewals, international school fee deadlines, and any family travel that quietly assumes a stamped card in your hand.
