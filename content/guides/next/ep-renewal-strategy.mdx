**Short answer:** Start renewal planning the day the pass is issued. Clear the **age-banded salary floor** and COMPASS again — last year’s approval is not a guarantee. Ask HR to run the **SAT** before they file.

Renewals fail quietly when everyone assumes last year’s approval guarantees this year’s. Qualifying salary floors move **by age**, COMPASS firm scores drift with headcount mix, and admin blockers (stale address, incomplete docs) create last-minute HR panic. r/askSingapore’s 2024–2026 renewal threads are mostly about **age-band salary**, **S Pass downgrades**, and **longer processing** — not mysterious “blacklists.”

## Start the day the pass is issued

- Diary the renewal window in your personal calendar **and** HR’s (MOM typically allows renewal from **6 months** before expiry — confirm live)  
- Keep employment history, degree certificates, verification proofs, and job-scope descriptions ready  
- Track **fixed monthly salary** vs the qualifying salary for **your age at renewal** — allowance packaging games are a renewal risk  
- Ask HR to run the **Self-Assessment Tool (SAT)** on your profile before they file

## Stage 1 — age-banded qualifying salary (the Reddit panic)

EP candidates must clear Stage 1 **before** COMPASS points matter. MOM benchmarks qualifying salary to the top third of local PMET salaries **by age**. Floors differ for **financial services**.

Published orientation at last review (verify live MOM tables — they update):

| Timing | All sectors except financial services (age 23 floor → age 45+ cap) | Financial services (age 23 floor → age 45+ cap) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New apps **before 1 Jan 2027** / renewals for passes **expiring before 1 Jan 2028** | **S$5,600** → **S$10,700** | **S$6,200** → **S$11,800** |
| New apps **from 1 Jan 2027** / renewals for passes **expiring from 1 Jan 2028** | **S$6,000** → **S$11,500** | **S$6,600** → **S$12,700** |

Examples people argue about (non-finance, current band — confirm the live age row):

| Age | Approx qualifying salary (current band) |
| --- | --- |
| 23 or below | S$5,600 |
| 28 | S$6,759 |
| 35 | S$8,382 |
| 40 | S$9,541 |
| 45+ | S$10,700 |

**Birthday vs expiry:** forum threads ask whether age is taken at application date or pass expiry. Treat it as a question for **HR + SAT**, not a Reddit tie-break — get the SAT screenshot for the exact filing month.

**13th-month / bonuses:** Stage 1 looks at **fixed monthly salary**. Do not assume AWS or variable bonus closes a S$600 gap.

## Stage 2 — COMPASS on renewal

Unless exempted, renewals still need **40 COMPASS points**. Firm criteria (diversity, local PMET share) move when the company hires or exits foreigners. Your C1 salary percentile is **not** the same number as the Stage 1 floor.

Ask HR annually:

- Are we still clearing COMPASS comfortably for my role / nationality share?  
- Is my job title / scope still coherent with the EP file (especially if you needed **Shortage Occupation List** bonus points)?  
- If criteria tighten, what is the contingency — raise, role redesign, **S Pass** (quota + levy), localisation, or exit timeline?  

Exemptions (high-level): fixed monthly salary at/above the FCF advertising exemption threshold (published **S$22,500** at last review), overseas intra-corporate transferees, or roles ≤1 month — confirm live.

Fair Consideration Framework advertising still applies to many **new** EP filings; renewals follow MOM’s published FCF rules for renewals — have HR confirm rather than assuming a free pass.

## When renewal looks soft — S Pass and exit clocks

Forum pattern when age-band salary is short: raise, **S Pass conversion**, or leave. S Pass has its own quota, levy, and eligibility — it is not a courtesy downgrade every employer will offer. Model household runway if the pass ends ([leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook), [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore), [retrenchment benefits](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits)).

1. Document achievements and scarcity skills for HR’s narrative  
2. Get SAT outcomes in writing (screenshot) before panic negotiations  
3. Avoid signing a fresh 24-month lease without a diplomatic clause if renewal risk is material  
4. If refusal comes, employer-led appeal / refile clocks are short — treat immigration counsel as a timed option, not a vibe  

## Address and tenancy blockers

MOM expects a valid residential address. Reddit threads document EP holders stuck when landlords will not enable **FWTES** “allow pass holder declarations,” or when short grey stays cannot be registered. Fix address hygiene **months** before renewal — not during IPA-adjacent chaos.

Travel itself rarely “cancels” an EP if the employer keeps sponsoring; tax residency and life-admin still care how many days you are physically here.

## Extra document requests (NOA / IR8A)

MOM’s renew page does **not** list a Notice of Assessment as a default attachment — but case-by-case emails asking for **NOA** (sometimes confused with employer **IR8A**) are real in 2025–2026 threads. Treat that lane as its own playbook: [EP renewal NOA & documents](/guides/ep-renewal-noa-documents). Align declared fixed monthly salary with payroll **before** HR files; notify cuts under [MOM change rules](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) via [salary / occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes).

## Processing realism

Anecdotes of **4–8+ weeks** and extra document requests are common since COMPASS matured. Build buffer before lease renewals, international school fee deadlines, and family travel that assumes a stamped card.
