This companion is the **relist / ad-match** depth — where a pulled MCF ad or drifted salary band silently restarts risk while candidates resign.

## Restarted clock ≠ green light

Most EP / S Pass filings still need a MyCareersFuture vacancy for **14 consecutive days**, fair consideration, **no offer during the window**, and an EP file that **matches** the ad (employer, occupation, salary, vacancy count) with the **Job Advertisement ID**. Pulling and reposting can break consecutive-day folklore; title / fixed-pay drift between ad and IPA is a classic query magnet.

| Edge | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “We advertised last month, then edited salary” | Treat material edits / relists as a **new clock** until HR proves otherwise |
| “Offer during day 10 is fine privately” | Published rule: no job offer during the mandatory window (unless exempt) |
| “IPA salary can sit under the MCF band” | Match rules care — reconcile **before** you resign |
| “We’re exempt so match doesn’t matter” | Exemptions skip advertising, not fair-hiring hygiene — [exemption edges](/guides/fcf-advertising-exemption-edges-ep-singapore) |

## Relist / match checklist

1. Screenshot Job Advertisement ID + first/last visible dates **and** any relist.  
2. Diff ad title / fixed pay vs offer letter before you treat IPA as resign-ready — [IPA-before-resign](/guides/ipa-before-resign-calendar-ep-singapore).  
3. If HR claims exemption, get the published basis in writing.  
4. Keep COMPASS / SAT as a **separate** checklist — FCF clear ≠ COMPASS clear.  
5. Probation exits still need cancel calendars — [probation × IPA overlap](/guides/ep-probation-resign-new-ipa-overlap-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Do not resign on a drifted or relisted MCF ad — consecutive days and ad-match are live filing facts, not HR vibes.**
