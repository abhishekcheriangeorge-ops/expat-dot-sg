r/askSingapore’s “I’ll file TADM after I land home” threads miss the **left-employment clock**. Levy kickbacks and false declared-salary patterns live on [salary disputes / TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom). This companion is the **filing-clock / EP adjacency** layer.

## Clocks TADM publishes (verify live)

| Situation | Filing orientation |
| --- | --- |
| **Still employed** | Salary-related claim: within **1 year** after the dispute arose |
| **Left employment** | Within **6 months** from last date of employment (and claims generally cannot reach further back than **1 year** from filing — read the live eServices text) |
| Wrongful dismissal (general) | Often within **1 month** of last day — parent guide |
| Wrongful dismissal tied to pregnancy / maternity | Within **2 months** from date of confinement — parent guide |

EP / S Pass holders under a contract of service are in the usual TADM population (exclusions include domestic workers, seafarers, public servants — verify live).

## Caps and path

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Advisory | “Ask TADM” / appointment — MOM redirects salary/wrongful-dismissal help here |
| Mediation | Online or in person; lawyers/friends generally **not** in the room under the Employment Claims Act framing |
| Settlement / ECT | Unresolved → **claim referral certificate** → Employment Claims Tribunals |
| Caps (verify live) | Often up to **S$20,000** (or **S$30,000** with recognised union / TMF assistance); combined salary + wrongful dismissal caps can be higher on the live page |

## Pass fear — do not invent overstay

TADM does **not** extend your EP. Align:

1. Document arrears **now**  
2. File while still in Singapore if the left-employment clock is tight  
3. HR cancel / **STVP** choreography — [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)  
4. New IPA before you burn the old job when you can — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)  
5. IR21 / exit tax if leaving — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving)

## Decision rule

**Diary the still-employed vs left-employment clocks → mediate at TADM → ECT only with a referral certificate.** Parent patterns (levy kickbacks, fake declared salary) stay on [salary disputes](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom).
