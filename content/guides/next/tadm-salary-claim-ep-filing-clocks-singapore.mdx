The "I'll file TADM after I land home" threads on r/askSingapore all miss the same thing. The clock changes the moment you stop being an employee, and it gets a lot shorter. The underlying patterns, like levy kickbacks and a declared salary that never matched what you were paid, are covered in [salary disputes and TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom). This companion is about the filing clocks and how they collide with your pass.

## The clocks TADM publishes

Verify these live before you rely on them.

| Situation | Filing orientation |
| --- | --- |
| Still employed | A salary-related claim goes in within 1 year after the dispute arose |
| Left employment | Within 6 months from your last date of employment, and claims generally cannot reach further back than 1 year from filing — read the live eServices text |
| Wrongful dismissal, general | Often within 1 month of your last day; see the parent guide |
| Wrongful dismissal tied to pregnancy or maternity | Within 2 months from the date of confinement; see the parent guide |

The gap between those first two rows is the whole problem. **While you are still employed you have a year; once you have left, the window collapses to 6 months.** People who wait until they have flown home, unpacked, and felt ready to deal with it regularly find the window shut behind them.

If you're on an EP or S Pass under a contract of service, you're in the ordinary TADM population. The exclusions are the ones you'd expect — domestic workers, seafarers, public servants — and they're worth verifying live rather than assuming.

## How the process runs, and what it can award

You start with advisory. "Ask TADM" or an appointment is where MOM redirects salary and wrongful-dismissal help, and it's also where you find out whether what you have is a claim or a grievance.

Mediation comes next, online or in person. Expect to be in the room on your own, because lawyers and friends generally aren't allowed in under the Employment Claims Act framing. That catches out anyone who has quietly budgeted for representation.

If mediation doesn't resolve it, you're issued a claim referral certificate, and that certificate is what opens the Employment Claims Tribunals. There's no route to the ECT that skips the mediation step.

On quantum, the caps are often up to S$20,000, or S$30,000 where you have recognised union or TMF assistance. Combined salary and wrongful-dismissal caps can sit higher on the live page, so read it rather than assuming the lower figure is your ceiling.

## TADM does not extend your pass

Filing a claim buys you no immigration time at all. The two tracks run in parallel, and the pass one moves faster.

1. Document the arrears now, not once things calm down
2. File while you're still in Singapore if the left-employment clock is tight
3. Sort the HR cancellation and STVP choreography: [between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)
4. Get a new IPA before you burn the old job, where that's possible: [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)
5. Start IR21 and exit tax if you're leaving: [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving)

## The rule worth remembering

Work out today which clock applies to you, the still-employed one or the left-employment one, and diary it. Mediate at TADM. The ECT only opens with a referral certificate. The underlying patterns, levy kickbacks and fake declared salary, stay with [salary disputes](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom).
