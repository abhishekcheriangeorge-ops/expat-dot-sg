Salary threads on r/askSingapore fall into the same three shapes over and over. Final pay gets withheld after a resignation. An S Pass or Work Permit shows one salary while the bank account receives a smaller one. Or someone is quietly collecting the levy back in cash. What they all have in common is weeks spent arguing with HR over WhatsApp first, and HR WhatsApp is not a dispute process. TADM and MOM are. If you're on an EP or S Pass and want the filing deadlines in one place, we've set them out in [TADM salary claim clocks](/guides/tadm-salary-claim-ep-filing-clocks-singapore).

## Start at TADM, not at MOM

The Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management, or TADM, is the published first stop for most salary and wrongful-dismissal claims. MOM's own dispute page says requests for assistance get redirected there, so emailing a generic MOM mailbox usually costs you a couple of weeks and ends with you being pointed at TADM anyway.

| Claim type | Filing clock (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Salary / unauthorised deduction | File as early as possible; MOM FAQ frames eligibility around Employment Act coverage and filing within **1 year** of non-payment |
| Wrongful dismissal (general) | Often within **1 month** of last day of work |
| Wrongful dismissal tied to pregnancy / maternity benefits | Within **2 months** from date of confinement |

Those clocks are the reason to act before you feel ready. They run from the event, not from the day you finally accept that HR isn't going to fix it on its own.

Bring the contract, your IPA or pass details, payslips, bank credits, CPF statements if there are any, and the message trails. Mediation can fail, and when it does TADM can issue a claim referral certificate that moves you to the Employment Claims Tribunals. That route is still cheaper and more predictable than inventing a legal strategy from LinkedIn posts.

## Levy kickbacks aren't "industry practice"

Employers must not pass the foreign worker levy on to the worker, and the same applies to other employment costs: pass renewal, the security bond, compulsory medical insurance. MOM's levy FAQ answers the question in one word. No.

So if someone starts collecting a "levy share" in cash or over PayNow, be clear about what that is. It isn't a grey-area arrangement that everybody quietly does. It's the exact behaviour MOM says it will act on.

## When the declared salary isn't the paid salary

The recurring S Pass version goes like this: the pass says S$X, the bank receives S$Y. That gap is usually illegal recovery of levy or agency fees, or a false declaration used to clear MOM's salary floors, and fairly often it's both at once.

Here's why people sit on it. False statements in a work-pass application are offences under the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act with published fine and jail ceilings, so reporting can put your own **pass** at risk as well as the employer. That hesitation is completely rational. It just isn't a plan: a salary story that was never true doesn't get safer the longer you live inside it. Use MOM's infringement reporting channels, and get advice first if the figures on your own IPA were the ones that were wrong.

<OfficialSource>
Penalty framing: [Employment of Foreign Manpower Act](https://www.mom.gov.sg/legislation/employment-of-foreign-manpower-act).
</OfficialSource>

## What TADM won't fix

TADM deals with the money. It doesn't change how fast your pass gets cancelled once you resign, which runs on its own clock and is covered in [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) and [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). It has no bearing on whether your next employer clears COMPASS or the S Pass quota either; that assessment is entirely separate from the arrears. And it says nothing about whether side income is legal, which is its own question: [side income on a work pass](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## A sensible order to do things in

1. Document the underpayment now, not later. Screenshots age badly.
2. Ask HR in writing for itemised arrears, and keep the tone factual.
3. Open a TADM advisory or claim path before you leave Singapore if you can. The deadlines are in [TADM filing clocks](/guides/tadm-salary-claim-ep-filing-clocks-singapore).
4. If levy recovery or a fake declared salary is involved, treat it as an EFMA issue rather than only a civil debt. Different channel, different consequences.
5. Line up tax clearance and pass cancellation so that "they still owe me" doesn't turn into "I overstayed".

Salary disputes are solvable when the evidence is clean. They get expensive when someone waits until the flight home is booked and only then discovers what the SCT and TADM attendance rules require of them.
