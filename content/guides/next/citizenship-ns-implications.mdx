Singapore citizenship is a profound commitment. For families with sons, **National Service (NS)** obligations are a central planning fact — not a footnote buried under passport aesthetics.

Forum threads (Singapore Expats Forum and Reddit) mix accurate NS warnings with folklore about “first-gen PR exemption.” Status, scheme, and age matter. Treat CMPB/MINDEF as authoritative; treat group chats as anecdotes.

## Citizenship vs PR (planning stack)

| Status | What changes for families |
| --- | --- |
| EP / other passes | Exit optionality higher; school/housing rules tighter |
| PR | Deeper roots; NS liability questions for male PRs / sons intensify |
| Citizenship | Full political membership; home-country dual-nationality rules may force renunciation |

ICA assesses citizenship applications on its own criteria. PR first is the common path but not a guarantee of later citizenship.

## NS — ask the right office early

Before you pursue PR or citizenship for a household with boys:

- Read CMPB guidance relevant to **PRs, citizens, and registration ages**  
- Clarify how **second-generation** male PRs are treated versus other schemes — do not rely on a single Reddit comment about PTS  
- Model life disruption: ORNS cycles, pre-enlistment, overseas exit permit rules if applicable  

Families sometimes delay PR precisely to keep exit optionality for teenage sons. That is a values trade-off, not a moral failing — but make it consciously.

<OfficialSource>
Use [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) and official MINDEF channels for NS liability questions tied to your exact status.
</OfficialSource>

## Home-country nationality law

Some countries forbid dual citizenship; others allow it with reporting duties. Renunciation, military service conflicts, and inheritance rules sit outside ICA. Speak to counsel in **both** jurisdictions if the asset or identity stakes are high.

This is not a DIY internet-forum decision. Get personalised immigration + NS advice before you file.
