**Short answer:** An EP does **not** erase the Employment Act. After ~**3 months**, statutory annual leave and paid sick leave floors usually apply; MC during notice **does not** extend your last day.

EP status does **not** erase the Employment Act. Since 2019, core leave and salary protections cover almost all private-sector employees under a contract of service — including most managers and executives — with classic carve-outs (domestic workers, seafarers, public officers). Reddit’s “foreigners have no leave rights” line is folklore. Work-injury MC that is also a **WICA** claim needs a parallel paper trail — [WICA for EP holders](/guides/wica-work-injury-compensation-ep).

## What you get after 3 months (statutory floor)

| Entitlement | Employment Act floor (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Paid annual leave | Starts after **3 months**; year-one minimum is **7 days**, rising with service years; pro-rate if you leave mid-year |
| Paid outpatient sick leave | Up to **14 days** / year after 3 months (pro-rated earlier in the first year) |
| Paid hospitalisation leave | Up to **60 days** / year inclusive of the outpatient days |
| Public holidays | Paid PH under the Act’s rules |
| Notice | Contractual length (must be equal both ways) or MOM’s default table if the contract is silent |

Your contract or handbook can be **more** generous. It cannot undercut the Act’s floor for covered employees. Approval of *when* you take annual leave is still an employer decision — vanishing approved balances or “no leave in year one” policies that drop you below the floor are the fights that escalate to TADM.

## Notice period — resignation is not a special EP ritual

If the contract sets notice, you serve it or pay **salary in lieu**. Notice must be in writing. MOM’s published counting rules include the day notice is given plus weekends/PHs.

**Sick leave during notice:** if you are covered by the Employment Act, paid **or unpaid** sick leave counts as part of the notice period. The employer **cannot** extend notice or claim short-notice damages for those MC days. Contracts that say otherwise are fighting published MOM guidance — document the MC and keep the link.

**Annual leave during notice:** allowed with approval; unused leave is generally encashed on termination (except misconduct paths). “Offset notice with AL” (bring last day forward) is a different mutual agreement — read MOM’s distinction carefully before you assume you can start the next job mid-notice.

**Unpaid leave during notice:** employer may extend notice **only with your agreement**.

## Part IV overtime — why many EP Reddit threads talk past each other

Hours-of-work / OT / rest-day rules in **Part IV** still use salary bands (workmen vs non-workmen ceilings). A typical EP salary sits **above** those bands, so “no OT” can be lawful even while annual leave and sick leave still apply. S Pass kitchen/service roles closer to the Part IV ceilings are a different story — see [salary disputes / TADM](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom).

## Pass cancellation is a separate clock

Leave rights end when employment ends. Your employer cancels the EP/S Pass and usually requests a Short-Term Visit Pass — default often **30 days**, ask for up to **90** during cancellation. That is immigration, not leave law — [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) and [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

## Adjacent rails people mix up

- **WFH / hybrid** is a formal Flexible Work Arrangement request under TG-FWAR (from 1 Dec 2024), not annual leave — [FWA guide](/guides/flexible-work-arrangement-request)  
- **Harassment** that pushes you to resign early is a TAFEP / PoHA / safety problem first — [workplace harassment](/guides/workplace-harassment-tafep-ep) — then notice math  

## Practical checklist

1. Confirm you are covered by the Employment Act (almost always yes on EP/S Pass private contracts)  
2. Screenshot leave balances and the clause that sets notice **before** you resign  
3. If HR says “MC extends notice,” reply with MOM’s termination-with-notice page and keep it factual  
4. Align IR21 / final pay with notice end — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving)  
5. Salary underpayment during notice is still a TADM problem, not a “foreigner exception”

Company culture can be stingy. The statute is not optional theatre for locals only.
