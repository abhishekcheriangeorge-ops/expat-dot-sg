**Short answer:** An NOA ask on EP renewal is **case-by-case evidence**, usually to reconcile **EP-declared salary vs IRAS**. Supply the NOA through HR/EA — panic about PDF formatting misses the real risk.

r/askSingapore’s 2026 renewal threads increasingly include a second email after filing: **MOM wants your NOA**. That request is not a published default checklist item on the renew page — it is a **case-by-case** ask. The panic is almost never about PDF formatting. It is about whether the **salary declared on the EP** still matches what IRAS assessed.

Age-band floors, COMPASS, and SAT choreography live on [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy). This guide is the **extra-documents** lane.

## What MOM publishes about renewal

Employers (or appointed EAs) renew via **myMOM Portal**. Published orientation at last review:

- Renew from **6 months** before expiry (EP Sponsorship: **3 months**)  
- Candidate must still clear **qualifying salary** + same-employer managerial/executive/specialised work + **COMPASS** (unless exempt)  
- Renewals are **not guaranteed**  
- Processing: most myMOM renewals processed or updated within **10 business days**; EP Sponsorship often within **6 weeks**  
- Successful renewals still need IPA issuance before IPA or current pass expiry  

MOM does **not** publish “always attach last year’s NOA.” Forum consensus that “MOM never asks” is outdated the moment your file gets the email.

## NOA vs IR8A (stop swapping the acronyms)

| Document | Who issues it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IR8A** | Employer (via IRAS Auto-Inclusion / forms) | Employment income for a tax year — input to filing |
| **NOA** (Notice of Assessment) | **IRAS** | IRAS’s assessed income / tax payable for that year |

When MOM says **NOA**, do not upload a random payslip pack and call it done. Pull the Notice of Assessment from myTax Portal for the year(s) requested. If MOM names a different artifact (employment contract, CPF history, bank salary credits), send **that** artifact.

## Why the mismatch email hurts

MOM’s EP story is built on **fixed monthly salary** declared for the pass. IRAS assesses **actual** taxable employment income. Common failure modes:

- Employer declared a higher EP salary than payroll / IR8A reality (“top-up later” folklore)  
- Shareholder-directors **adjust** salary for business reasons without [notifying MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) when pay falls for ≥1 month  
- Allowances / benefits packaged for Stage 1 optics that never appear as fixed monthly salary  
- Job-hop year where prior employer IR8A / NOA does not match the narrative HR filed  

If MOM asked for the NOA because numbers diverge, treat it as an **integrity** problem for HR and counsel — not a Reddit debate about whether MOM “usually” asks. Fake declarations and under-reporting are not “packaging tips.”

Safer operating rule **before** renewal season:

1. Run SAT on the **true** fixed monthly salary  
2. Confirm mid-year cuts / occupation changes were notified — [EP salary & occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes)  
3. Keep NOA PDFs for recent assessment years in the same vault as degree proofs  
4. Align bonus / AWS stories with [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding) — Stage 1 is not total cash  

## How to respond when the email lands

1. Read the request letter for **exact years and document names**  
2. Download NOA(s) from IRAS; rename cleanly; upload through the channel MOM names (usually employer / EA portal)  
3. If figures differ, prepare a **factual** explanation with HR (role change date, notified salary cut, partial-year employment) — do not invent a second set of books  
4. If the true salary no longer clears the age-band floor, model raise / **S Pass** / exit clocks early — [renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy), [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)  
5. Keep personal copies; tax residency / COR questions are adjacent but Money-owned — [Certificate of Residence](/guides/certificate-of-residence-dta)  

Physical presence in Singapore is rarely the blocker for digital renewals; incomplete or inconsistent **documents** are. Build buffer before lease and school fee deadlines when an extra-docs round is open.

## Decision rule

Treat a MOM NOA ask as a **reconciliation** task between EP declarations and IRAS assessments — not a random bureaucracy tax. Keep NOAs handy every renewal season, notify salary cuts when required, and never “fix” Stage 1 with numbers your tax file will contradict.
