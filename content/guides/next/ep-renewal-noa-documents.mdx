**Short answer:** An NOA request during EP renewal is case-by-case evidence, not a verdict. MOM is almost always reconciling the salary your employer declared for the pass against what IRAS assessed. Hand the NOA to HR or your employment agent, and spend your worry on the numbers rather than on how the PDF looks.

The 2026 renewal threads on r/askSingapore keep surfacing the same second email. The file goes in, everyone relaxes, and then MOM comes back asking for your Notice of Assessment. It isn't on the published checklist for renewals, which is exactly why it feels ominous when it lands. It's a case-by-case ask. And the thing people panic about — scanned versus downloaded, filename tidy or not — is almost never the thing that matters. What matters is whether the salary declared on your EP still lines up with what IRAS assessed.

Age-band floors, COMPASS, and the SAT choreography are covered on [EP renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy). This guide is the extra-documents lane.

## What MOM actually publishes about renewal

Your employer, or an appointed employment agent, files through the myMOM Portal. The published orientation at our last review:

- You can renew from 6 months before expiry, or 3 months out if you're on EP Sponsorship
- The candidate still has to clear qualifying salary, still be doing managerial, executive, or specialised work for the same employer, and still clear COMPASS unless exempt
- Renewals are **not guaranteed**
- Most myMOM renewals are processed or updated within 10 business days; EP Sponsorship often runs to about 6 weeks
- Even a successful renewal needs the IPA issued before the IPA or the current pass expires

Nowhere does MOM say "always attach last year's NOA." Which is true, and also completely useless to you the moment your file gets the email. Forum consensus that MOM never asks stops being relevant when MOM has asked.

## NOA and IR8A are not the same thing

People swap these two constantly, HR included, and then send the wrong one.

| Document | Who issues it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IR8A | Your employer, via IRAS Auto-Inclusion or forms | Your employment income for a tax year, which feeds into filing |
| NOA (Notice of Assessment) | IRAS | What IRAS assessed as your income and tax payable for that year |

So when MOM says NOA, it means the Notice of Assessment. Pull it from myTax Portal for the year or years named in the request. Don't upload a pack of payslips and call it done, and don't substitute the IR8A because it was easier to find in your inbox. If the letter names a different artifact altogether — an employment contract, CPF contribution history, bank records of salary credits — send that one.

## Why the mismatch email hurts

MOM's whole picture of you is built on the fixed monthly salary declared for the pass. IRAS assesses your actual taxable employment income. The two are meant to tell the same story, and when they don't, it's usually one of these:

- The employer declared a higher EP salary than payroll and the IR8A ever reflected, on the "we'll top it up later" theory
- A shareholder-director adjusted their own salary for business reasons and nobody [notified MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) when the pay fell for a month or more
- Allowances and benefits were packaged to make Stage 1 look good but never appeared as fixed monthly salary
- It was a job-hop year, and the previous employer's IR8A or NOA doesn't match the narrative HR filed

If MOM asked for the NOA because the numbers diverge, this stops being an admin chore and becomes an integrity problem for HR and, where it's serious, for counsel. It is not a Reddit debate about whether MOM "usually" asks. Fake declarations and under-reporting aren't packaging tips, whatever the thread calls them.

Better to sort this out before renewal season than during it:

1. Run the SAT on your true fixed monthly salary, not the aspirational one
2. Check that any mid-year cut or occupation change was genuinely notified: [EP salary and occupation changes](/guides/ep-salary-occupation-changes)
3. Keep NOA PDFs for recent assessment years in the same vault as your degree proofs, because you tend to need both in the same week
4. Make sure the bonus and AWS story matches what's on [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding). Stage 1 is not total cash

## How to respond when the email lands

1. Read the request letter properly. Note the exact years and the exact document names it asks for
2. Download the NOAs from IRAS, rename them so a stranger can tell what they are, and upload through whichever channel MOM names, which is usually the employer or EA portal rather than you directly
3. If the figures genuinely differ, build a factual explanation with HR before anyone replies: the role change date, the notified salary cut, the partial year of employment. **Do not invent a second set of books.**
4. If the true salary no longer clears the age-band floor, model the alternatives early rather than after a rejection — a raise, an S Pass, or exit clocks. See [renewal strategy](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy) and [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)
5. Keep your own copies. Tax residency and Certificate of Residence questions sit next door and are Money-owned, so start from [Certificate of Residence](/guides/certificate-of-residence-dta)

Being physically in Singapore is rarely what blocks a digital renewal. Incomplete or inconsistent documents are. So when an extra-documents round is open, build buffer before lease and school fee deadlines, because you don't control how long that round stays open.

## The rule worth keeping

Treat a MOM NOA request as a reconciliation job between what was declared for the pass and what IRAS assessed. Not a random bureaucracy tax. Keep the NOAs handy every renewal season, notify salary cuts when the rules require it, and never try to fix a Stage 1 problem with a number your tax file will contradict.
