r/askSingapore’s “HR froze my last three months of pay” threads are usually **IR21 tax clearance**, not a personal grudge. The base rail (who needs clearance, one-month notice, exceptions) lives on [IRAS tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving). This companion is the **final-pay freeze + share-plan** layer.

## What gets withheld

| Item | Typical IR21 posture (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Final salary / unused leave pay | Withheld from the date HR knows you are leaving until IRAS directs release |
| Bonuses / commissions still due | Often swept into the same withholding pot |
| Unvested RSUs / unexercised options | May trigger **deemed exercise** income at clearance unless an approved tracking arrangement applies |
| Direction to release | After IRAS processes IR21 — not on your preferred flight date |

Give HR written leave dates **early**. Withholding often starts at resignation notice, not only in the final payroll cycle.

## Clocks peers underestimate

| Clock | Orientation |
| --- | --- |
| Employer IR21 filing | Generally **≥ 1 month** before cessation / qualifying departure (>3 months overseas) |
| Garden leave | Clearance often expected at the **start** of garden leave when applicable |
| Short notice resignations | Still require IR21 — thin runway is your problem, not IRAS’s |
| Pass cancel vs tax | MOM/ICA stay windows after pass cancel are a **separate** calendar — coordinate both |

Do not book non-changeable flights that assume same-week clearance.

## RSU / option questions for HR (in writing)

1. Will you file IR21 for my cessation date, and which pay components will be withheld?  
2. Do we have an IRAS-approved **tracking** arrangement for unvested awards?  
3. What deemed-exercise income will you report if tracking does not apply?  
4. When do you expect IRAS direction to release withheld monies?  
5. Will I keep Singpass / myTaxPortal access after the pass cancels?

Share-plan tax mechanics beyond clearance sit on [RSU / ESOP tax](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore).

## Decision rule

Treat IR21 as a **cashflow event**: early written notice → ask about share-plan tracking → budget [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs) while pay is frozen → confirm release after IRAS direction. Parent clearance map stays on [tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving); ops checklist on [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).
