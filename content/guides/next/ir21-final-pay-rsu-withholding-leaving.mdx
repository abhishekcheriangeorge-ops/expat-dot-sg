The "HR froze my last three months of pay" threads on r/askSingapore are almost never about a personal grudge. They're IR21 tax clearance doing exactly what it is designed to do. The base rail, meaning who needs clearance, the one-month notice and the exceptions, sits on [IRAS tax clearance when leaving](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving). This guide is the layer on top of it: what happens to your final pay, and what happens to your share plan.

## What gets withheld

| Item | Typical IR21 posture (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Final salary / unused leave pay | Withheld from the date HR knows you are leaving until IRAS directs release |
| Bonuses / commissions still due | Often swept into the same withholding pot |
| Unvested RSUs / unexercised options | May trigger **deemed exercise** income at clearance unless an approved tracking arrangement applies |
| Direction to release | After IRAS processes the IR21, which is not the same thing as your preferred flight date |

Give HR your leave dates in writing early, and earlier than feels necessary. **Withholding often starts at resignation notice**, not only in the final payroll cycle, so the gap between telling them and being paid can run a great deal longer than one month.

## The clocks people underestimate

Your employer is generally expected to file the IR21 at least **1 month** before your cessation or qualifying departure (more than 3 months overseas). That lead time belongs to them, but you are the one who feels it when they leave it late.

Garden leave catches people out. Where it applies, clearance is often expected at the start of the garden leave rather than at the end, so the clock has already been running for weeks by the time you think of yourself as leaving.

Resigning on short notice doesn't get you out of IR21 either. It just compresses everything, and a thin runway is your problem rather than IRAS's.

Then keep the pass in a separate column of your head. MOM and ICA stay windows after a pass is cancelled run on their own calendar and don't sync themselves to the tax one, so you have to coordinate the two yourself. Which is the long way of saying: don't book non-changeable flights that assume clearance lands the same week.

## Questions to put to HR in writing

Email these rather than raising them in a corridor. You want the answers in a form you can point back to.

1. Will you file the IR21 for my cessation date, and which pay components will be withheld?
2. Do we have an IRAS-approved tracking arrangement for unvested awards?
3. If tracking doesn't apply, what deemed-exercise income will you report for me?
4. When do you expect IRAS to direct the release of the withheld money?
5. Will I keep Singpass and myTaxPortal access after the pass is cancelled?

The share-plan mechanics beyond clearance itself are on [RSU / ESOP tax](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore).

## How to think about it

Treat IR21 as a cashflow event rather than a paperwork one. Written notice of your dates goes in early, the share-plan tracking question gets asked while there is still time to answer it, and you budget [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs) on the assumption that your pay is frozen for the duration. Then you confirm the release once IRAS has actually directed it, instead of assuming that it happened.
