The parent map for all of this is [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore), and the wider household work-rights picture sits on [trailing partner career](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) and [PLOC vs LOC (ICA LTVP)](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore). What we're adding here is the layer underneath the dual-career threads that treat a ONE Pass as an automatic work visa for your partner. It isn't one.

## An LOC is a second product, not a vibe

ONE Pass did reopen a real Letter of Consent path for spouses, and that's genuinely worth something, because ordinary EP Dependant's Pass households largely lost that route after 2021. But reopening a path isn't the same as handing out work rights. The LOC is its own filing with its own eligibility, and the order runs principal ONE Pass, then the eligible family pass, then the LOC. You can't compress that because a job offer is in a hurry.

The first thing households get wrong is timing. "My spouse can work the day the ONE Pass issues" isn't how it plays out. A legally married spouse normally needs the Dependant's Pass in hand first, and the LOC only after that.

The second is stacking. Plenty of couples plan on a job plus a side company and assume that means two LOCs. MOM's FAQ is clear that it's **one LOC at a time**, and that your own company counts as the employer for this purpose. The side venture isn't a bonus lane, then. It's a choice made against the day job.

The third is partnership status. A common-law partner isn't treated the same way as a married spouse. The published route there is typically an LTVP plus the LOC path that goes with it, which is workable, but again not automatic.

The fourth is contagion from friends. Households on an ordinary EP or S Pass Dependant's Pass can't copy any of this, and the DP work-LOC folklore they may still be repeating is the wrong playbook entirely. What actually applies to them is on [Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore).

## Sequencing for dual-career households

1. Confirm the principal genuinely clears the ONE Pass salary, company and outstanding-achievements tests before anyone builds a plan on top of it. Our [chooser](/guides/pep-one-tech-pass-chooser-singapore) walks through those.
2. Get the spouse's DP issued or renewed, or the applicable LTVP if you're on the common-law path, with enough validity left before you file for the LOC. The figure commonly discussed is at least 3 months of card validity, but verify it live rather than trusting a forum on it.
3. Decide between an employment LOC and a business-as-employer LOC. You can't stack both, so make that call deliberately instead of discovering it at filing.
4. If the "spouse plan" is really overseas remote work only, read [side income and freelance](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) rather than forcing an LOC story onto something that might not need one.
5. Don't resign the trailing partner from their current job on the strength of "the ONE Pass IPA has been submitted." Wait for the LOC outcome that matches the actual offer.

## The rule to hold onto

Treat the spouse LOC as a separate filing with its own eligibility and its own one-employer limit. **It is not a free add-on**, and it is not something ordinary EP households can copy off you.
