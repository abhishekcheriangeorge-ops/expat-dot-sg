The "new passport, old EP file" threads on r/askSingapore tend to end the same way: at an automated gate, or at an airline desk, with someone being told their pass doesn't match their travel document. The pass hasn't been cancelled. MOM is simply still holding the old passport number, and the systems reading your pass have no idea the new book exists. Full notify mechanics for address, mobile, and passport live on [notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport). This companion is only the passport-before-travel layer.

If you're a PR rather than a pass holder, transferring your Re-Entry Permit onto a new foreign passport is a different ICA rail entirely: [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport).

## The sequence that usually works

1. Renew or replace the foreign passport at your mission. You come away with a new bio-page and, in most cases, a new number.
2. Update the passport particulars in MOM's EP eService. Depending on what MOM's channel allows, that's either you or HR, so agree early which of you owns it.
3. Confirm SGWorkPass shows the updated particulars. That's your counter and gate proof when a human does ask.
4. Only then book the long-haul trips that assume smooth clearance, because stale passport numbers are what fail the automated flows.

**Don't wait until Changi to discover the mismatch.** The gate is a bad place to learn this.

## Passport update isn't address, and neither is a lost card

Three rails get confused constantly, usually by someone trying to solve all of them with one form. Here's which is which.

| Change | Rail |
| --- | --- |
| New passport number / bio-page | This guide. MOM EP eService particulars |
| New residential address / mobile | MOM 5-day notify: [EP notify](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) |
| Lost plastic work-pass card | MOM card replacement: [lost work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card) |
| PR blue NRIC plus new foreign passport | ICA Transfer of REP, not MOM: [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport) |

The embassy side of this, meaning appointments, photo specs, and courier arrangements, sits on [passports & overseas documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas).

## The rule worth keeping

Mission renews the book, MOM updates the pass file, then you travel. In that order. The parent notify map stays on [EP notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport), and the annual calendar for this kind of admin is on [renewing life admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually).
