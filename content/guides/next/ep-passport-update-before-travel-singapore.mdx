r/askSingapore’s “new passport, old EP file” threads die at the **automated gate** or the airline desk — not because the pass cancelled, but because MOM still holds the **old passport number**. Full notify mechanics (address, mobile, passport) live on [notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport). This companion is the **passport-before-travel** layer.

PR Re-Entry Permit transfer after a new foreign passport is a **different** ICA rail — [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport).

## Sequence that usually works

| Step | Who | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Renew / replace the **foreign passport** at your mission | You | New bio-page / number |
| 2. Update **passport particulars** in MOM EP eService | You or HR (as MOM’s channel allows) | Links the work pass to the new travel document |
| 3. Confirm **SGWorkPass** shows the updated particulars | You | Counter / gate proof |
| 4. Only then book long-haul trips that assume smooth clearance | You | Stale passport numbers fail automated flows |

Do **not** wait until Changi to discover the mismatch.

## Passport update ≠ address / mobile ≠ lost card

| Change | Rail |
| --- | --- |
| New passport number / bio-page | This guide — MOM EP eService particulars |
| New residential address / mobile | MOM 5-day notify — [EP notify](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) |
| Lost **plastic** work-pass card | MOM card replacement — [lost work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card) |
| PR blue NRIC + new foreign passport | ICA **Transfer of REP**, not MOM — [transfer REP](/guides/transfer-rep-new-passport) |

Embassy renewal logistics (appointments, photos, courier) sit on [passports & overseas documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas).

## Decision rule

Mission renews the book → MOM updates the pass file → then travel. Parent notify map stays on [EP notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport); annual calendar on [renewing life admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually).
