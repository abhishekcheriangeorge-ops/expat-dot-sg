**Short answer:** **Name the exemption or run the 14-day clock** — ad skips do not waive fair hiring or EP-file substance.

Parents: [Fair Consideration Framework](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) · [FCF 14-day clock](/guides/fcf-mycareersfuture-14-day-clock-ep-singapore). This companion is the **exemption edge** layer — where Reddit hears “we’re exempt” and stops asking questions.

## Exempt from advertising ≠ exempt from fairness

Published FCF exemptions can skip the public MyCareersFuture **ad clock**. They do **not** waive fair hiring, COMPASS/salary substance, or matching the role you were actually offered.

| Claimed exemption (orientation) | Still ask HR |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed monthly salary ≥ **S$22,500** | Is that the **fixed** monthly figure on the EP file — not total package folklore? |
| Employer with **fewer than 10** employees | Headcount on which date / definition? |
| Vacancy **≤ 1 month** | Is this a true short cover, or a permanent role labelled short? |
| Intra-company transfer / other published ICT path | Which **exact** MOM exemption category is on the filing? |

## Candidate checks when HR says “exempt”

1. Get the exemption category **in writing** (not Slack vibes).  
2. If an ad **did** run: screenshot Job Advertisement ID, dates, title, salary band.  
3. Align contract **fixed** pay with EP Online / COMPASS C1 — bait-and-switch ads are audit smell.  
4. “We already picked you on day two” is **not** a published shortcut past a mandatory ad window when no exemption applies.  
5. If IPA fails on FCF/COMPASS hygiene, use the [rejection playbook](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-candidate-playbook-singapore).

## Decision rule

**Name the exemption or run the 14-day clock.** Fair hiring still applies either way — exemptions only change the advertising theatre, not the substance bar.
