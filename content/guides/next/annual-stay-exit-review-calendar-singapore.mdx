The r/askSingapore threads that open with "we'll decide after Chinese New Year" tend to end with somebody realising the decision was already made months earlier, by school deposits and EP renewal paperwork. Nobody chose. The calendar chose. The scorecard dimensions themselves live on [long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit), so this companion is the calendar and lock-in layer: when to run the review, and what quietly commits you before you've decided anything.

## When to run the review

Four moments are worth putting in the diary, and the first is the one people consistently miss.

| Trigger | Why it locks the next cycle |
| --- | --- |
| **~8–12 weeks before** school re-enrolment and deposit deadlines | Deposits and notice periods are expensive to unwind once paid |
| Before the EP or pass renewal file opens in earnest | Renewal and COMPASS work quietly assume another year of staying ([EP renewal](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy)) |
| After a major shock: retrenchment, an SEN capacity failure, caregiving needed abroad | Don't wait for the annual slot when the facts have already changed |
| Alongside your [annual life admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually) | Same season as REP, IC and insurance renewals, so you're in the paperwork anyway |

Write the outcome down as a 12–24 month primary path: stay another cycle, bridge toward an exit, or exit now. A path with a date attached survives a stressful quarter. A feeling about it does not.

## Lock-ins that fake a soft maybe

A soft maybe is often a hard yes that nobody has noticed yet, and these are usually the things doing the deciding on your behalf.

| Lock-in | Force-multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| A multi-year lease with no usable diplomatic clause | Turns an exit into a cashflow spike |
| A school year already paid for or bonded | The academic calendar outranks whatever timeline you drew on Reddit |
| A PR file in flight without [NS literacy](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) | Roots deepen before the household has actually agreed to them |
| A partner's career just getting going on a PLOC or LOC | Bridge years start to feel almost settled, which is how they become permanent |
| A single-shock cash buffer | IR21 withholding and deposits can land in the same month; see [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) |

If the path is exit, start on [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs) and the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) in the week you decide, not the week the pass ends. The gap between those two weeks is where most of the avoidable money goes.

## The decision rule

Diary the review before school deposits and EP renewal lock you in, score the six dimensions honestly, then pick one dated path and tell the household what it is. The parent scorecard stays on [stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit).
