r/askSingapore’s “we’ll decide after Chinese New Year” threads discover the decision was already made by **school deposits** and **EP renewal paperwork**. Scorecard dimensions live on [long-term stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit). This companion is the **calendar / lock-in** layer.

## When to run the review

| Trigger | Why it locks the next cycle |
| --- | --- |
| **~8–12 weeks before** school re-enrolment / deposit deadlines | Deposits and notice periods are expensive to unwind |
| **Before** EP / pass renewal file opens in earnest | Renewal + COMPASS work assumes another year of stay — [EP renewal](/guides/ep-renewal-strategy) |
| After a major shock (retrenchment, SEN capacity fail, caregiving abroad) | Do not wait for the “annual” slot |
| Alongside [annual life-admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually) | Same season as REP / IC / insurance renewals |

Write the outcome as a **12–24 month primary path** — stay another cycle, bridge to exit, or exit now — not a vibe.

## Lock-ins that fake a soft maybe

| Lock-in | Force-multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-year lease without a usable diplomatic clause | Exit cashflow spike |
| School year already paid / bonded | Academic calendar > Reddit timeline |
| PR file in flight without [NS literacy](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) | Roots deepen before the household agrees |
| Partner career just started on PLOC / LOC | Bridge years feel “almost settled” |
| One-shock cash buffer | IR21 withhold + deposits can coincide — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) |

If the path is **exit**, start [leaving costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs) / [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) the week you decide — not the week the pass ends.

## Decision rule

**Diary the review before school deposits and EP renewal lock you in → score the six dimensions → pick one dated path.** Parent scorecard stays on [stay vs exit](/guides/long-term-stay-vs-exit).
