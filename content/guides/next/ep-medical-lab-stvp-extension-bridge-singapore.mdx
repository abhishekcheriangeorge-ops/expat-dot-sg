## Two clocks, one body

IPA can require a Singapore medical before your pass is issued. The awkward part is that labs and X-ray reports run on their own schedule, and that schedule has no idea when your visit pass ends. If you tip over into overstay, "HR said the clinic was slow" is not a defence anyone at ICA is obliged to accept.

| Clock | Owner | What goes wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPA medical requirement | You and the clinic | Wrong form (the Work Permit PDF), incomplete panel, delayed pickup |
| Visit / STVP expiry | You and whoever at HR files it | You miss MOM's extension window and you're overstaying |
| Issuance after results | Employer | Results sit in your inbox while the fee and Notification Letter catch up |

## The extension bridge

MOM's apply guidance lets your employer request an STVP extension roughly 7–14 days before expiry when you're waiting on medical results, and they'll normally want a doctor's memo stating the collection date. Work it backwards from your own dates: if your STVP ends 17 Feb, the window to ask lands somewhere around 4–10 Feb. Check that against the live guidance rather than trusting the arithmetic. Ask late and a perfectly clean IPA file quietly becomes an ICA problem instead, which is a different agency and a much worse conversation.

What to hand HR so they can file without chasing you:

1. The doctor's memo with a named collection date on it, not a vague "in about a week"
2. The clinic invoice or appointment confirmation
3. Your passport bio page, plus the current visit stamp or STVP details
4. A written line confirming you are **not** travelling mid-bridge. The [JB hop trap](/guides/jb-hop-mid-ep-issuance-reentry-trap-singapore) explains why a quick trip over the causeway can undo all of this

## What to actually do

Diary the STVP end date the day you land, before jet lag and onboarding swallow the week. If there's any realistic chance the labs miss that date, HR files the extension **inside** the published window rather than on the morning after it has already gone.
