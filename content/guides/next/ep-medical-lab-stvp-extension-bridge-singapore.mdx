**Short answer:** **Diary STVP end date on landing day.** If labs can miss it, HR files the extension inside the published window with a doctor memo — not the morning after expiry.

Parents: [EP medical for issuance](/guides/ep-medical-examination-issuance) · [STVP extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension). This companion is the **labs-still-pending / visit-pass-expiring** bridge Reddit discovers on day 28 of a 30-day stamp.

## Two clocks, one body

IPA can require a Singapore medical before issuance. Labs and X-ray reports do not respect your visit-pass end date. Overstay fines are not cured by “HR said the clinic is slow.”

| Clock | Owner | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPA medical requirement | You + clinic | Wrong form (WP PDF), incomplete panel, delayed pickup |
| Visit / STVP expiry | You + HR/employer filing | Miss MOM’s extension window → overstay |
| Issuance after results | Employer | Results sit in your email while fee / Notification Letter wait |

## Extension bridge (orientation)

MOM’s apply guidance lets employers request an **STVP extension about 7–14 days before expiry** when candidates are waiting on medical results — typically with a **doctor’s memo** stating the collection date. Example shape: if STVP ends 17 Feb, the ask window is roughly **4–10 Feb** (verify live). Late asks are how clean IPA files become ICA problems.

Pack for HR:

1. Doctor memo with **named collection date**  
2. Clinic invoice / appointment proof  
3. Passport bio page + current visit stamp / STVP details  
4. Written note you are **not** travelling mid-bridge — [JB hop trap](/guides/jb-hop-mid-ep-issuance-reentry-trap-singapore)

## Decision rule

**Diary the STVP end date the day you land.** If labs can miss that date, HR files the extension **inside** the published window — not the morning after expiry.
