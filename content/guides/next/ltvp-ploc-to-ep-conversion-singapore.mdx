**Short answer:** **PLOC = consent to work on LTVP; EP = new principal pass** — convert for mobility or employer policy, not forum “upgrade” folklore.

Parent work-rights map: [PLOC vs LOC for ICA LTVP](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore). LTVP overview: [LTVP](/guides/ltvp-singapore). This companion is the **“should I get my own EP?”** fork Reddit collapses into “just convert the LTVP.”

## PLOC is work consent — not an EP

PLOC / employer LOC lets eligible **ICA** spouse/child LTVP holders take approved employment. It does **not** turn the LTVP into an Employment Pass, remove COMPASS, or skip Fair Consideration when the employer later wants a standalone EP.

| Goal | Typical rail |
| --- | --- |
| Keep working under spouse/parent SC/PR LTVP | Stay on **PLOC / LOC** while LTVP stays valid |
| Own career mobility / leave the sponsor household story | New employer files **EP / S Pass** (FCF + COMPASS unless exempt) |
| Graduate job-hunt stay only | [Graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt) — still **not** work rights |

## When EP usually wins over staying on PLOC

1. Employer wants a **principal** pass for headcount / benefits / travel policy  
2. You need mobility if the SC/PR marriage / sponsor story changes  
3. Role or salary is cleaner as a normal EP than as a restricted PLOC occupation  
4. You are leaving the LTVP category entirely (divorce, child ageing out) — plan EP **before** LTVP dies  

FCF advertising and COMPASS still apply to most EP filings — [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) · [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained). Do not treat “already working on PLOC” as an exemption category unless MOM’s live pages say so for *your* case.

## Sequencing hygiene

Keep the LTVP valid until the new EP is **issued** (not merely IPA’d) unless ICA/MOM instruct otherwise in writing. Cancel/re-issue order is employer + household specific — get both HRs’ calendars in email. Career framing: [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) · [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

## Decision rule

**PLOC = consent to work on LTVP. EP = new principal pass.** Convert when mobility or employer policy needs a principal file — not because forums say “upgrade.” Mid-process sponsor / LTVP shocks: [PLOC→EP conversion edges](/guides/ltvp-ploc-to-ep-conversion-edges-singapore).
