**Short answer:** A PLOC is consent to work on the LTVP you already hold. An EP is a new principal pass in your own name. You convert because you want mobility or because your employer's policy needs it, not because the LTVP is somehow ripening into an EP. It isn't.

The parent guide on work rights is [PLOC vs LOC for ICA LTVP](/guides/ploc-loc-ica-ltvp-singapore), and the pass itself is covered in [LTVP](/guides/ltvp-singapore). This one is the fork the forum threads collapse: should you actually go and get your own EP, or stay where you are?

## A PLOC is consent, not a pass

PLOC, or an employer-sponsored LOC, lets an eligible spouse or child holding an ICA-issued LTVP take approved employment. That is all it does. It doesn't quietly turn the LTVP into an Employment Pass, it doesn't make COMPASS go away, and it doesn't spare your employer the Fair Consideration advertising when they later decide they want you on a standalone EP. The assumption that trips people up is that years worked on PLOC bank some kind of credit towards an EP file. They don't.

| Goal | Typical rail |
| --- | --- |
| Keep working under spouse/parent SC/PR LTVP | Stay on PLOC / LOC while the LTVP stays valid |
| Own career mobility / leave the sponsor household story | New employer files EP / S Pass (FCF + COMPASS unless exempt) |
| Graduate job-hunt stay only | [Graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt) — still **not** work rights |

## When your own EP is worth the paperwork

Four situations where it usually is. The first is your employer wanting you on a principal pass at all, because headcount, benefits, or the travel policy are built around one and an LOC holder doesn't fit the box. The second is mobility: your right to work currently sits on someone else's status, and if that story changes you lose the job with it.

The third is the shape of the role itself. Some jobs and salaries sit more naturally on an ordinary EP than inside a restricted PLOC occupation. The fourth is the one to watch the calendar on. If you're leaving the LTVP category entirely, whether through divorce or a child ageing out, you want the EP planned and moving before the LTVP dies, not after.

None of this exempts you from the usual machinery. Fair Consideration advertising and COMPASS still apply to most EP filings — see [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) and [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained). "I'm already working here on a PLOC" is not an exemption category. If you think one applies to you, check MOM's live pages for your specific situation rather than taking a forum's word for it.

## Don't let the LTVP lapse in the gap

Keep the LTVP valid until the new EP is actually **issued**, not merely IPA'd. An IPA is approval to issue a pass, not the pass itself, and the window between the two is exactly where people find themselves holding nothing. Unless ICA or MOM tell you otherwise in writing, don't cancel early to be tidy.

The cancel and re-issue order depends on your employer and your household, so get both HRs to put their calendars in an email. For the wider career picture, see [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) and [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

## The rule of thumb

PLOC is consent to work on an LTVP. An EP is a new principal pass. Convert when mobility or employer policy genuinely calls for a principal file, and not because someone on the internet called it an upgrade. If the sponsor or the LTVP falls over mid-process, that mess has its own guide: [PLOC to EP conversion edges](/guides/ltvp-ploc-to-ep-conversion-edges-singapore).
