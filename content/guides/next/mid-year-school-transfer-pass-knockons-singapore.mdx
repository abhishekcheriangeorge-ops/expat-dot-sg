The parent guides here are [international schools landscape](/guides/international-schools-landscape) for the map and [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism) for how seats actually move. This one is the layer underneath: what a mid-year transfer does to your seats, your fees, and your pass calendars. Families tend to treat it as a single WhatsApp decision, and it isn't one.

## Changing schools isn't an immigration event on its own

Moving a child to a different campus mid-year **does not by itself reissue an EP, DP, or LTVP**. Nobody at MOM needs to hear about the school. Immigration risk turns up only when the transfer drags something else along with it.

| What the move drags along | Why it bites |
| --- | --- |
| A new commute or neighbourhood | Lease break, school-bus bond, and address notification clocks all start at once |
| A principal job change bundled with the school move | A new EP IPA means re-sponsoring the family, on its own calendar rather than the school's |
| An exit, or a spell between jobs | Your STVP and cancellation week now collide with term withdrawal penalties |
| A pivot to AEIS or a local school | A different set of eligibility tests entirely — [AEIS / S-AEIS](/guides/aeis-saeis-international-students) |

## Where the money goes mid-year

Withdrawal notice and term forfeiture are governed by the handbook you signed, not by what other parents report on Facebook, and the two differ more often than you'd like. Read the handbook.

Bus, device, and CCA bonds get billed on exit surprisingly often, even when the new school is down the road and you're keeping the same routine. Sibling priority is the trap people genuinely don't see coming: pulling one child out can reshuffle your waitlist leverage at the old campus for the others still there. And if your employer nominates or reimburses, check the policy before you commit, because some will only fund one mid-year move. Get finance to confirm that in writing.

Neighbourhood choice still drives bus time far more than school brand does: [choosing a neighbourhood](/guides/choosing-neighbourhood-expat).

## The pass checks to do before you sign

1. Confirm the principal pass is valid through the new school's next billed term, not just through the acceptance date.
2. Check the DP and LTVP card expiries against the start date. There is no version of this where a school letter extends a pass.
3. If someone is job-hopping in the same quarter, sequence it properly: IPA first, family re-sponsor second, then hard school deadlines.
4. If you're leaving Singapore rather than switching schools, align the withdrawal notice with the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) and [between jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## The rule to take away

Treat a mid-year transfer as a contract and logistics problem first. It becomes an immigration problem only when the address, the sponsor, or a cancellation calendar moves with it. And don't assume a school acceptance letter repairs a short pass.
