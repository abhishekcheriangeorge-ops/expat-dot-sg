**Short answer:** **Treat mid-year transfer as contract + logistics first; immigration only if address, sponsor, or cancel calendars move.** A school letter does not repair a short pass.

Parent school map: [international schools landscape](/guides/international-schools-landscape). Waitlists: [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism). This companion is the **mid-year transfer** layer — seats, fees, and pass calendars that Reddit treats as one WhatsApp decision.

## School move ≠ pass event — until the household moves

Changing campuses mid-year does **not** by itself reissue an EP, DP, or LTVP. Immigration risk appears when the transfer forces:

| Knock-on | Why it bites |
| --- | --- |
| New commute / neighbourhood | Lease break, school-bus bond, address notify clocks |
| Principal job change bundled with school | New EP IPA → family re-sponsor — separate from school admin |
| Exit / between-jobs | STVP / cancel week vs term withdrawal penalties |
| AEIS / local-school pivot | Different eligibility tests — [AEIS / S-AEIS](/guides/aeis-saeis-international-students) |

## Fee and contract traps mid-year

1. **Withdrawal notice / term forfeiture** — handbooks beat parent Facebook lore  
2. **Bus / device / CCA bonds** — often billed on exit even when the new school is down the road  
3. **Sibling priority** — leaving one child can reshuffle waitlist leverage at the old campus  
4. **Corporate nomination** — some employers only reimburse one mid-year move; get finance in writing  

Neighbourhood choice still drives bus time more than brand: [choosing a neighbourhood](/guides/choosing-neighbourhood-expat).

## Pass checklist before you sign the transfer

1. Principal pass validity through the **new** school’s next billed term  
2. DP / LTVP cards’ expiry vs start date — no “school letter extends pass” myth  
3. If job-hopping the same quarter, sequence **IPA → family re-sponsor → then** hard school deadlines  
4. If leaving Singapore, align withdrawal notice with [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) / [between jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)

## Decision rule

**Treat mid-year transfer as a contract + logistics problem first; immigration only if address, sponsor, or cancel calendars move.** Do not assume a school acceptance letter repairs a short pass.
