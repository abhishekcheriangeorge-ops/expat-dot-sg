**Short answer:** When your EP is cancelled, ask HR in writing for a Short-Term Visit Pass of up to 90 days, because plenty of HR teams default to something much shorter unless somebody asks. It buys you **stay, not work**. Family passes tied to your EP usually fall in the same week, and any trip out of the country is high-risk while you're on it. It is also not a Work Permit Special Pass, whatever a forum thread calls it: [STVP vs Special Pass myths](/guides/stvp-after-ep-cancel-special-pass-myths-singapore). Tick the gap on the [between-jobs journey](/journeys/between-jobs).

The recurring panic on Reddit is some version of "I got laid off, do I only have 30 days?" The real mechanic is kinder than the folklore. When an EP is cancelled, or expires while you're still in the country, your employer can request a Short-Term Visit Pass so your stay remains lawful, and MOM's cancel page says that can run up to 90 days from the application date. What people actually get is often shorter, for the unglamorous reason that nobody asked for more.

If you're married to a Singapore citizen or PR, the STVP is only the short buffer and there's a better lane sitting behind it: [EP job loss to spouse LTVP bridge](/guides/ep-job-loss-spouse-ltvp-bridge-singapore). The money side of a retrenchment, contract terms against TAMEM norms and TADM, is a separate guide: [retrenchment on EP](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits).

One thing to keep straight before you start reading around. This is not the same workflow as extending a tourist or social visit pass while you wait for a first IPA or a Student's Pass appointment. That one is ICA's rail, and it's covered in [short-term visit pass extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension).

## What an STVP is, and what it isn't

| Topic | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Who requests it | Your employer or their agent, through the EP cancellation, or promptly after expiry |
| Stay length | Up to 90 days per MOM's cancel guidance. Confirm what HR actually selected |
| Work | **Illegal.** Cancellation ends your work rights immediately |
| Re-entry | Leaving Singapore on an STVP can end your ability to come back without a fresh visa. Treat travel as high-risk |
| Family | DPs and LTVPs issued under your EP are typically cancelled with the main pass, so plan their status the same week |

## What happens to the rest of the family

The "my spouse still has an LOC, so we're fine" threads skip the dependency graph. A Dependant's Pass, and many family LTVPs tied to your work pass, **generally falls when the sponsoring EP is cancelled**. Work rights that were riding on that family pass don't float in mid-air once the pass underneath them is gone. Households tend to find this out late, because everyone's attention is on the person who lost the job.

Things to sort out in the same week:

1. Ask HR or MOM what happens to each dependant's pass on your cancel date. Don't assume a silent 90-day grace covers the whole family.
2. If your spouse was working on an LOC attached to their DP, treat that work right as ending with the DP unless MOM confirms otherwise in writing.
3. Tell the school or childcare admin about the pass change straight away. Fee and enrolment rules very often assume a live long-term pass.
4. Go through the lease, the helper's paperwork, the bank and Singpass e-services, and work out who is named as the main pass holder on each one.
5. If the household is heading for the door, run the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) for everyone rather than just the EP holder.
6. If the plan is to stay and hunt, the clean path is still a new EP IPA and then fresh DP applications, before the cancel where that's possible. See [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

One category confusion worth heading off early: an ICA LTVP held by the spouse or unmarried child of a citizen or PR, with a PLOC, is a different animal from an EP-tied DP. Start at the [LTVP overview](/guides/ltvp-singapore), and don't mix either of them up with [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt).

## The best outcome is never entering the gap

If you're changing jobs rather than losing one, MOM's published sequence hasn't changed: the new employer applies, the IPA comes through, and only then do you time the resignation and the cancellation. The deep version is in [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore). A clean IPA before you resign beats any amount of clever STVP optimisation, because it removes the gap rather than managing it.

## If the gap is unavoidable

1. **Ask HR in writing** to request the longest STVP available when they cancel. People consistently report having to ask for the 90 days; silence gets you the short default.
2. Get IR21 tax clearance moving early. Employers are expected to engage IRAS roughly one month before cessation, and your final pay may be withheld until it clears, which matters a great deal when you're budgeting a gap. See [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving).
3. Inventory the lease, schools, helper, bank and dependants on the day the notice lands, using the family list above. Not the week after.
4. Interview hard while the old EP is still live, if you have notice left to work. An STVP is for packing and interviewing, not for freelancing. The insurance hole that opens in the gap is covered in [STVP travel/medical cover](/guides/stvp-travel-medical-cover-gap-singapore).
5. If you hold a PEP or a ONE Pass you're on entirely different unemployment clocks (a PEP is cancelled if you're unemployed for more than 6 months). Compare [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) and [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore).

## Graduate LTVP, a narrow rescue

Foreign graduates of eligible Singapore IHLs sometimes look at ICA's LTVP while seeking employment. Published fees start at S$45 to submit and S$60 on issuance, with processing often within 6 weeks. Be clear about what it is, though: a stay bridge, not work rights, and not the spouse LTVP plus LOC path. The "apply while still on EP with a termination letter" anecdotes on Reddit are real, but ICA decides the timing case by case, so don't build your plan around someone else's outcome. Full depth in [graduate LTVP job hunt](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt).

## Exit or hunt

If the market is dead and your cash runway is short, switch to the [leaving Singapore playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) earlier than feels comfortable. Deposits, shipping and IR21 all run to their own timetables, and none of them care that you might get an offer next week.

Two edge cases have their own pages. Same-city EP hops carry IR21 wrinkles worth reading first ([EP job-change IR21 withhold](/guides/ep-job-change-ir21-withhold-edges-singapore)), and a PEP running out raises its own timing question ([PEP exit timing](/guides/pep-exit-to-ep-or-pr-timing-singapore)).
