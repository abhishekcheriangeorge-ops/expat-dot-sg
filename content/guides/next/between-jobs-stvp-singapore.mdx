Reddit’s recurring panic question: “I got laid off / I resigned — do I only have 30 days?” The official mechanic is clearer than the folklore. When an EP is cancelled (or expires while you remain in Singapore), your employer can request a **Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP)** so you have **valid stay** — MOM’s cancel page states this can run **up to 90 days** from the application date. Default HR behaviour is often shorter unless someone asks. Married to an **SC or PR**? STVP is only the short buffer — see [EP job loss → spouse LTVP bridge](/guides/ep-job-loss-spouse-ltvp-bridge-singapore). Retrenchment **money** (contract vs TAMEM norms, TADM) is a separate guide: [retrenchment on EP](/guides/retrenchment-ep-benefits).

This is **not** the same workflow as extending a tourist / social **visit pass** while you wait for a first IPA or Student’s Pass appointment — that is ICA’s [short-term visit pass extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension) rail.

## What STVP is (and is not)

| Topic | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Who requests it | Employer / agent via EP cancellation (or promptly after expiry) |
| Stay length | Up to **90 days** per MOM cancel guidance — confirm what HR actually selected |
| Work | **Illegal.** Cancellation ends work rights immediately |
| Re-entry | Leaving Singapore on STVP can end your ability to return without a fresh visa — treat travel as high-risk |
| Family | Related DPs / LTVPs issued under your EP are typically cancelled with the main pass — plan their status the **same week** |

<OfficialSource>
Primary procedure: [Cancel an Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass). Overstaying after the STVP ends attracts fines and worse — do not invent “I’ll hop to JB to reset.”
</OfficialSource>

## Family cascade — DP / LTVP when the main EP dies

Reddit’s “my spouse still has LOC, so we are fine” threads skip the dependency graph. A Dependant’s Pass (and many family LTVPs tied to your work pass) generally **falls when the sponsoring EP is cancelled**. Work rights that rode on that family pass (LOC / employer arrangements) do not float in mid-air.

Same-week checklist for households:

1. Confirm with HR / MOM what happens to **each** dependant’s pass on your cancel date — do not assume silent 90-day grace for the whole family.  
2. If a spouse was working via **LOC on DP**, treat that work right as ending with the DP unless MOM confirms otherwise in writing.  
3. School / childcare: tell admin the pass change immediately; fee and enrolment rules often assume a live long-term pass.  
4. Lease / MDW / Singpass e-services: inventory who is the “main pass holder” on every contract.  
5. If the household’s plan is **exit**, run the [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) for everyone — not just the EP holder.  
6. If the plan is **hunt**, the clean path is still new EP IPA (then fresh DP applications) before cancel when possible — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).

Spouse / unmarried-child **ICA LTVP of an SC/PR** with **PLOC** is a different category from EP-tied DP — see [LTVP overview](/guides/ltvp-singapore) and do not confuse it with [graduate LTVP](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt).

## Ideal path: never enter the gap

If you are **changing jobs**, MOM’s published sequence is still: new employer applies → IPA → then resign / cancel timing. Deep dive: [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore). A clean IPA-before-resign beats any STVP optimisation.

## If the gap is unavoidable

1. **Ask HR in writing** to request the longest STVP available when they cancel (peers often report needing to ask for 90 days; silence gets you the short default).  
2. Start **IR21 tax clearance** early — employers must engage IRAS roughly **one month** before cessation; final pay may be withheld. See [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving).  
3. Inventory **lease, schools, helper, bank**, and dependants the same day the notice lands — use the family cascade list above.  
4. Interview aggressively **while the old EP is still live** if you still have notice — STVP is for packing and interviewing, not for freelancing.  
5. If you hold a **PEP** or **ONE Pass**, different unemployment clocks apply (PEP: cancel if unemployed >6 months). Compare [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) and [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore).

## Graduate LTVP (narrow rescue)

Foreign graduates of eligible Singapore IHLs sometimes explore an ICA **LTVP while seeking employment** — published fees start at **S$45** submission / **S$60** issuance, processing often **within 6 weeks**. It is a **stay** bridge, **not** work rights, and **not** the spouse LTVP+LOC path. Reddit’s “apply while still on EP with a termination letter” anecdotes exist; **ICA decides** timing case-by-case. Full depth: [graduate LTVP job hunt](/guides/graduate-ltvp-job-hunt).

<OfficialSource>
Start from ICA’s [LTVP — graduate seeking employment](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/apply/graduate-from-an-institute-of-higher-learning-seeking-employment-in-singapore) page and call ICA if your fact pattern is odd.
</OfficialSource>

## Exit vs hunt

If the job market is dead and cash runway is short, switch to the [leaving Singapore playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook) early — deposits, shipping, and IR21 do not care that you “might get an offer next week.”
