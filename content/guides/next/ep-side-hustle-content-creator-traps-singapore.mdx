The parent overview is [side income and freelancing on work passes](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass). This companion covers the creator and weekend-gig layer specifically, because that's the part Reddit keeps re-litigating on the grounds that it isn't a real job.

## Your EP or S Pass names one employer, and means it

MOM's FAQ doesn't carve out influencers, photographers, tutors or Grab drivers. If you're being paid for activity in Singapore, for a Singapore client or brand, that's work for someone other than your designated employer. Generally speaking, that isn't allowed, and the fact that it arrived as a DM rather than a contract doesn't change the analysis.

The costumes people put on it are pretty consistent.

| What it gets called | How it reads on an EP or S Pass |
| --- | --- |
| A paid Instagram or TikTok deal for a Singapore brand | Income earned in Singapore. No |
| "Just one" wedding or corporate shoot | Still a Singapore client gig. No |
| Tutoring locals for cash | Same answer |
| Weekend Grab or food delivery | Same answer |
| Unpaid open source, or learning a craft | Usually fine, right up until invoices, brand deals or local customers appear |
| A Pte Ltd on the side | Holding shares isn't moonlighting clearance. See [incorporating](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore) |

## DP and LTVP: overseas remote work is a genuinely different question

The Dependant's Pass FAQ is where the confusion starts, because it does say no work pass is needed for a clearly overseas employer or client, with no Singapore-entity linkage and no Singapore clients, while you're physically here. That's a narrow set of conditions and all of them have to hold. Flip any one of them and you need the right pass, or a [business-owner LOC](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/loc-for-dependants-pass-business-owners/eligibility), **before you send the first invoice** rather than after. Tax residency and remittance run on their own track at IRAS: [foreign income](/guides/foreign-income-and-tax).

What you can't do is take the DP overseas-remote reasoning and apply it to an EP. Different pass, different rule, and people do get this wrong in both directions.

## The personalised passes aren't a freelance licence either

PEP generally excludes freelancers, sole proprietors and shareholder-directors. EntrePass exists for a venture that meets the published innovator rules, not for assorted gigs. Tech.Pass and ONE Pass do allow multiple activities, but only the activities described in the programme text, which is a much narrower thing than the folklore suggests.

## The rule to hold onto

Where the client sits, and what pass you hold, beat whatever you write on the invoice. On an EP or S Pass, assume zero Singapore side income. On a DP, settle the overseas-versus-Singapore question before you bill anyone. And tax is a separate conversation from immigration, running in parallel, which is why answering one of them well doesn't get you out of the other.
