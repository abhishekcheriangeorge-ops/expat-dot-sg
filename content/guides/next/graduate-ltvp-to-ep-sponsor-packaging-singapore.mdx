## Stay bridge ≠ labour-market pass

Graduate LTVP (IHL seeking employment) is a **stay** facility. Paid work still needs employer-sponsored **EP / S Pass / Work Permit** (or another MOM path that actually authorises work). COMPASS and FCF still apply to most EP files — LTVP months do not invent exemptions.

| Folklore | Safer reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Graduate LTVP includes LOC / work rights” | Wrong category — spouse/child LTVP LOC playbooks do not copy across |
| “HR can skip FCF because I’m already here” | Presence ≠ published advertising exemption |
| “IPA while on LTVP lets me start Monday” | IPA ≠ issued pass — do not work until the EP is live |
| “LTVP renews forever until I get EP” | Treat duration as **what your approval letter says** — [renewal edges](/guides/graduate-ltvp-ihl-renewal-edges-singapore) |

## Packaging checklist

1. Keep ICA LTVP / STVP validity written down beside the employer’s FCF + COMPASS calendar.  
2. Package fixed monthly salary and awarding-body docs like any other EP hire — [S Pass→EP packaging](/guides/s-pass-to-ep-upgrade-packaging-singapore) habits still help.  
3. Do not resign a student / prior pass story on IPA vibes — wait for **issuance** unless MOM/ICA instruct otherwise in writing.  
4. If the EP will miss before LTVP ends, map exit or [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) early — do not freelance-bridge on LTVP folklore.  
5. Tuition Grant bond years still need **authorised** work — LTVP days do not count as EP employment.

## Decision rule

**Package the EP as a new sponsored file while LTVP only buys interview runway — stay rights never become work rights by Reddit consensus.**
