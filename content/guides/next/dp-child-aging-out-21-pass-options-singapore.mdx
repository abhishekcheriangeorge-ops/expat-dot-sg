The parent map for all of this is our [Dependant's Pass guide](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore). This page is about the one cliff families keep walking off: the child's 21st birthday, which forums tend to wave away with "MOM will just renew for another year."

## The hard gate

MOM's Dependant's Pass lane for children is for those who are unmarried and under 21. The birthday isn't a soft reminder that starts a conversation. It's the eligibility cliff itself, and a plastic card that still has ink on it doesn't override the published age rule once your child has aged out of it.

Four beliefs are worth killing early. The first is that you'll simply renew the DP to cover uni year 1. Renewal still requires the child to clear the under-21 and unmarried tests at the moment MOM assesses the application, so the pass can't carry them past the birthday just because you asked nicely.

The second is that a Student's Pass follows automatically from a DP. It doesn't. An ICA Student's Pass is a separate filing with its own school and course gates, and someone has to actually make it — see [Student's Pass](/guides/student-pass-singapore).

The third is that LTVP is the catch-all when nothing else works. It isn't a default lane for children who've aged out; the relationship and sponsor rules still have to be satisfied on their own terms ([LTVP](/guides/ltvp-singapore)).

The fourth is the dangerous one: that they can job-hunt here on an expired DP. Overstaying, or working without a pass, is the failure mode that turns an admin problem into an immigration one. **Get the next status lined up before the current pass is cancelled**, not after.

## Give yourself six months

1. Work out which clock runs out first, the birthday or the pass expiry. They're rarely the same date, and the earlier one is the one that governs.
2. If your child is staying in qualifying full-time study, start the ICA Student's Pass paperwork with the institution early. Schools move at school speed.
3. If the plan is work, that means their own [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) or [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore), which needs a real employer, a salary that clears the floor, and for EP, COMPASS and FCF. It isn't a parental upgrade button.
4. Think about household risk too. If the principal EP dies mid-transition, every DP cascades with it, and an STVP buys you time to stay rather than a bridge to a career ([between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)).
5. Don't book non-refundable university housing abroad until the Singapore status for the gap months is written down somewhere.

## Decision rule

Under-21 is the entire product. Everything after it, whether that's a Student's Pass, their own work pass, or LTVP, is a fresh application with its own evidence and its own timeline. Start it while the DP is unambiguously valid, not once the candles have been blown out.
