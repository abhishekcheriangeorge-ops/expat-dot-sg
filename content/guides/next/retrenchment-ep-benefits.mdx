r/askSingapore “I got retrenched on EP” threads conflate three questions: **(1)** what money am I owed, **(2)** how long can I stay, **(3)** can I keep working while I hunt. Only (2)/(3) are mostly immigration mechanics — covered in [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). This guide is the **money + responsible-retrenchment** rail.

## Retrenchment benefit is usually contractual — not a statutory cheque

MOM’s [responsible retrenchment](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/retrenchment/responsible-retrenchment) page is blunt:

- Retrenchment benefit depends on what is in your **employment contract** or **collective agreement**  
- If there is **no** provision, the quantum is **negotiated**  
- Tripartite **norm** (advisory, not a hard statutory entitlement): about **2 weeks to 1 month of salary per year of service**, depending on company finances and industry  
- Employees with **2 or more years’ service** are treated as eligible for retrenchment benefit under the advisory; those with **under 2 years** may get **ex-gratia** only  
- **No CPF** on retrenchment benefit (for those in CPF)  
- If a cut happened just before the exercise, use **pre-cut salary** to compute  

The Employment Act does **not** invent a universal retrenchment payout the way some home countries do. Read your contract the night you get the letter.

## What you can still demand on the last day

Regardless of retrenchment-benefit negotiation, MOM expects employers to pay **all salaries due** on the last day — including **unused annual leave** and **notice pay** (or salary in lieu) per contract / Employment Act coverage. See [leave & notice](/guides/employment-act-leave-notice-ep).

| Claim type | Typical path |
| --- | --- |
| Contractual retrenchment benefit unpaid | **TADM** within **6 months** of last day |
| No contractual provision; employer below advisory norm | Negotiate; non-unionised staff can seek **TAFEP** engagement per MOM FAQ |
| Unpaid salary / leave / notice | **TADM** — [salary disputes](/guides/salary-dispute-tadm-mom) |
| Discriminatory selection | TAFEP / counsel — different from pure quantum fights |

## Pass clock: notice is your hunting window

1. While you are still employed (including garden leave / notice), the **EP remains the work pass** — use that time to interview.  
2. On cancellation, ask HR **in writing** to request the longest **STVP** MOM allows (peers often cite needing to ask for up to **90 days**; silence gets the short default).  
3. **You cannot work on STVP.** IPA-before-start still rules — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).  
4. Start **IR21** conversations early — [tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving).  
5. Map lease, schools, helper, and DP/LTVP knock-ons the same week.

Deep stay mechanics: [between jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). If the runway is dead, switch to [leaving playbook](/guides/leaving-singapore-playbook).

## Negotiation notes that are not “entitlement”

- Ask for **outplacement**, extended notice, or paid garden leave even when cash quantum is stuck — time is often worth more than a week of salary on EP.  
- Get the package **in writing** before you sign a release that waives claims.  
- Retrenchment is not the same as **misconduct dismissal**; do not let HR re-label if the facts are redundancy. Counsel helps when the letter smells wrong — entity: employment counsel category.
