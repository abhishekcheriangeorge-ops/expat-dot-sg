Exits fail when people treat them as the reverse of arrivals. Dependencies differ — and IRAS tax clearance alone can freeze your final pay at the worst possible moment. r/askSingapore resignation threads add three recurring landmines: **vacation on notice**, **STVP length after cancellation**, and **cashflow while salary is withheld**.

For a cash-flow model of the same process, see [leaving Singapore costs](/guides/leaving-singapore-costs).

## Sequence (typical foreign employee)

1. Confirm **last day**, notice, and **pass cancellation** timing with HR  
2. Trigger **diplomatic clause** / lease end / assignment of tenancy early enough for viewings  
3. Give **school withdrawal** notices per each school’s handbook  
4. Start **tax clearance (Form IR21)** workflow — employer files; you prepare for withheld monies  
5. Cancel or transfer **MDW Work Permit** if you employ a helper  
6. Book **shipping / storage** and declutter ruthlessly  
7. Shut **GIRO**, telco, condo MCST auto-debits, and subscriptions in a controlled order — settle any IRAS GIRO **before** the employer pays clearance tax if you need a different arrangement  
8. Export **medical / school records**; photograph inventory for claims  
9. Confirm immigration status after cancellation — ask HR for the longest available **STVP** (MOM allows up to **90 days**; default ask is often 30); details in [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)  

## Tax clearance is on the critical path

For non-citizen employees, employers generally must notify IRAS **at least one month** before cessation / long departure and **withhold monies due** from the date they know you are leaving. This is not optional HR folklore — it is IRAS process. Withholding can exceed one month’s pay depending on income — budget runway before you resign into a thin bank balance.

## Notice period, leave, and travel

While the EP is **still valid**, leave and overseas trips are employer-policy questions — immigration usually cares that the pass remains live. After cancellation, an **STVP** is not a re-entry visa: leaving Singapore can end your ability to return without a fresh visa. Ask HR explicitly for STVP duration when they cancel; do not assume 90 days.

Encashment vs taking leave is managerial. Do not plan non-refundable flights that assume same-week IR21 release.

## Housing and deposits

- Serve notice per tenancy agreement; diplomatic clauses have notice formats that must be exact  
- Budget for cleaning, aircon servicing, and disputed deductions — photograph everything  
- Do not assume deposit return lands before your flight  

## If you are also ending PR

Renouncing PR is a separate ICA track from EP cancellation — see [renouncing PR](/guides/renouncing-pr-singapore). CPF cash-out only after you are no longer SC/PR.

## Emotional offboarding

Goodbyes need calendar space. Children especially need a narrative, not just a flight. Leave buffer days after the last working day if immigration status allows — rushing IR21 + movers + school farewells in 72 hours is how people miss documents.
