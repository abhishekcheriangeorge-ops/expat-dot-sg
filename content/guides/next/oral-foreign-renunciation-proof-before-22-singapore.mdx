The ORAL panic you see on r/askSingapore is almost never about the ICA counter. It's about waiting months for a foreign renunciation certificate while the clock between the 21st and 22nd birthday quietly runs down. The retention rules themselves, including automatic loss of citizenship on the 22nd birthday, are covered in [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21). This guide is the layer underneath: getting the foreign proof in hand and getting the oath booked.

One thing to separate out first. Adults who want to deliberately give up Singapore citizenship are looking at [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship), which is a different ICA product entirely from minor ORAL retention. They get conflated constantly, and the paperwork has nothing in common.

## What ICA asks for, and what the missions ask for

| Source | Documents commonly listed |
| --- | --- |
| ICA minor-oath page (Singapore) | Identity Card, plus the Foreign Citizenship Renunciation Certificate |
| Singapore Overseas Mission pages (examples) | Copy of the IC, the original Singapore Citizenship Certificate, a certified true copy of the other country's renunciation certificate, and the completed ORAL / oath form |
| Overseas notary path | Some mission pages allow the oath before an authorised notary with Registrar permission. Confirm it with your own mission rather than arranging a notary and hoping |

Mission checklists are location-specific, and they differ more than you'd expect. Email or call the mission that will actually administer the oath before you courier original documents anywhere.

## The order that actually works

Everything here is arranged around one fact: the foreign renunciation is the long pole, and nothing else can start until it's moving.

1. Confirm which track you're on — descent, or registration as a minor. Get this wrong and you'll be preparing for the wrong product from the start.
2. Begin the home-country renunciation as early as that country's law allows. Many require the applicant to be 21, or want a letter from ICA first, so "as early as allowed" often isn't as early as you'd like. This is the step that decides your whole timeline.
3. Put the window in the diary: the 21st birthday through to the day before the 22nd. Miss it and Singapore citizenship is lost automatically. There's no grace period to appeal to.
4. Book the ICA Services Centre if you're in Singapore, or the nearest Singapore mission if you're not, with the renunciation pack already assembled. ICA can generally complete it the same day when the documents are in order. Missions often quote several weeks of admin on top, which is exactly the kind of delay that eats a 12-month window.
5. Only after all that should you assume Singapore passport renewals and long foreign student visas will clear cleanly. Passport-validity problems are common in the months while dual status is ending, and they tend to surface at the worst time.

Taking the oath itself costs nothing, according to ICA's page. And the peers who talk about "skipping ORAL and keeping both passports" aren't being clever about dual status — they're choosing to lose Singapore citizenship silently. Sons still need to model National Service into the decision, via [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) and our [citizenship and NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications) guide.

## Where this goes wrong, over and over

The embassy won't process the foreign renunciation until the applicant turns 21, or until an ICA summons arrives, so families sit on their hands. Get into the queue the week the letter lands instead.

People book the ICA appointment before the foreign certificate is physically in hand, then have to rebook. People also assume the under-21 dual-passport convenience carries on past 21, which it doesn't — [Right of Entry](/guides/right-of-entry-roe-under-21-singapore) is a separate product with its own rules. And when a school asks for proof of status, the thing to send is a [confirmation of citizenship status](/guides/confirmation-singapore-citizenship-status), not something improvised out of ORAL paperwork.

## The rule to hold onto

**The foreign renunciation proof is the critical path.** Get that moving first, then take the oath at ICA or a mission inside the 12-month window. The retention framing lives on [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21), and the travel-document logistics are in [passports and documents overseas](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas).
