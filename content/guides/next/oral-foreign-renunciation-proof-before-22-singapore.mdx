**Short answer:** Start **foreign renunciation proof** first — it is the critical path — then book **ICA or a mission ORAL** inside the **21 → day-before-22** window.

r/askSingapore’s ORAL panic usually is not the ICA counter — it is **waiting months for a foreign renunciation certificate** while the **21 → 22** clock runs. Parent retention rules (automatic loss on the 22nd birthday) live on [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21). This companion is the **foreign-proof + mission booking** layer.

Adults who want to **drop** Singapore citizenship deliberately use [renouncing Singapore citizenship](/guides/renouncing-singapore-citizenship) — a different ICA product from minor ORAL retention.

## What ICA vs missions ask for

| Source | Documents commonly listed |
| --- | --- |
| ICA minor-oath page (Singapore) | **Identity Card** + **Foreign Citizenship Renunciation Certificate** |
| Singapore **Overseas Mission** pages (examples) | Copy of IC · original **Singapore Citizenship Certificate** · **certified true copy** of the other country’s renunciation certificate · completed ORAL / oath form |
| Overseas notary path | Some mission pages allow ORAL before an authorised notary **with Registrar permission** — confirm with *your* mission; do not invent a DIY notary |

Treat mission checklists as location-specific. Email / call the mission that will administer the oath before you courier originals.

## Sequence that survives the long pole

| Step | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Confirm you are on the **descent / registration as minor** track | Wrong track → wrong product |
| 2. Start **home-country renunciation** as early as that country’s law allows (often needs age 21 and/or ICA letter) | Longest lead time |
| 3. Diary **21st birthday → day before 22nd** | Miss it → automatic loss of SC |
| 4. Book **ICA Services Centre** (if in Singapore) or the nearest **Singapore mission** with the renunciation pack ready | Same-day complete when docs are in order (ICA); missions may quote multi-week admin |
| 5. Only then assume Singapore passport renewals and long foreign student visas clear cleanly | Passport-validity traps are common while dual status ends |

Fee for taking ORAL: **none** on ICA’s page. Peers who “skip ORAL and keep both passports” are choosing silent loss of SC — not clever dual status. Sons still model **NS** with [CMPB](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/) / [citizenship & NS](/guides/citizenship-ns-implications).

## Failure modes forums keep repeating

- Embassy will not process foreign renunciation until 21 / until ICA summons arrives — start the queue the week the letter lands.  
- Booking ICA without the foreign certificate in hand.  
- Assuming ROE / under-21 dual-passport convenience extends past 21 — [ROE](/guides/right-of-entry-roe-under-21-singapore) is a different product.  
- Needing a status PDF for a school — use [confirmation of citizenship](/guides/confirmation-singapore-citizenship-status), not ORAL folklore.

## Decision rule

**Foreign renunciation proof is the critical path → then ICA or mission ORAL inside the 12-month window.** Parent retention framing stays on [ORAL at 21](/guides/oral-dual-citizenship-age-21); travel-document logistics on [passports & overseas documents](/guides/passports-and-documents-overseas).
