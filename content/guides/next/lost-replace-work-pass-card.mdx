**Short answer:** Tell HR the same day, open SGWorkPass, and get MOM a card replacement **within 1 week** — file a police report first if it was stolen. The digital pass helps you at counters, but it doesn't waive the plastic. And if what you lost is a blue PR NRIC, that is ICA's process, not MOM's.

Losing a wallet on a JB weekend is a whole r/askSingapore genre. The reassuring part is that your pass status lives with MOM and ICA against your passport, so the card was never the only proof you exist here. The less reassuring part is that you still have to replace a lost, stolen or damaged card on MOM's timeline, and that timeline is short.

One agency check before you do anything else. If you already hold a blue PR NRIC, or a pink citizen card, ICA runs that replacement and MOM has nothing to do with it. Head to [lost or stolen NRIC](/guides/lost-replace-nric-pr-singapore) instead.

## What to do today

1. Open SGWorkPass, and set it up if you never got round to it. The digital work pass is what you show a counter that wants proof your status is valid.
2. Message HR the same day. In most cases only your employer, or an appointed employment agent, can submit the replacement in EP eService, so nothing at all moves until they know.
3. If it was stolen rather than mislaid, file a police report before the replacement goes in.
4. If you lost it overseas, get HR to inform MOM immediately. MOM can issue a letter so you can re-enter Singapore.

## The replacement rules (EP as the worked example, verify live)

| Item | MOM position (EP card page) |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | The employer must apply within 1 week of loss, damage or theft |
| Who applies | Your employer, or an appointed employment agent |
| Damaged card fee | S$65.40, GST inclusive and non-refundable |
| First lost or stolen card | S$109 |
| Subsequent losses | S$327 |
| Approval | Often immediate; collect at EPSC about 4 working days after approval |
| Digital pass | Free, set it up immediately, and it does not waive the physical replacement |

The fee ladder is the part worth noticing. It climbs steeply after the first loss, and that is deliberate. Repeated losses can also trigger an interview, which is not a meeting anyone enjoys scheduling around work. If a card you had already written off turns up later, post it back to MOM as instructed rather than leaving it in a drawer.

## Travelling without the plastic

The forum consensus here matches MOM's push toward the digital pass, with a couple of caveats worth knowing before you're standing at a desk.

Immigration gantries usually read your passport-linked status, and plenty of people re-enter without ever showing the card. The friction tends to come earlier, at the airline check-in counter, where staff want proof you can actually enter Singapore before they will board you. Have SGWorkPass and Singpass open on your phone, and an HR contact who will answer.

Two things break the passport link. Changing passports without updating MOM and ICA is the common one, so update your travel-document details in EP eService whenever you renew. The other is losing the card while you're already abroad, where you may need a MOM re-entry letter before the flight; we walk through that case in [lost work pass overseas and the re-entry letter](/guides/lost-work-pass-overseas-reentry-letter).

## Who files for which pass

| Pass type | Typical path |
| --- | --- |
| EP / S Pass, plus dependants, with an employer | Employer files through EP eService |
| PEP / ONE Pass / EntrePass family with no current employer path | EA appointment or EPSC appointment flows — follow MOM's “replace a pass card” notes for your specific pass |
| 3+ losses, blocked online | The “lost card interview” appointment path on MOM's appointment service |

## Making it not happen again

Install SGWorkPass on day one; it is on our list of [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore). Keep a photo of the front and back of the card in a password vault, not in a group chat. Don't laminate the card or punch a hole in it. And add "pass card lives in the travel pouch" to your [annual life-admin](/guides/renewing-life-admin-annually) sweep.

Once PR formalities are done, stop treating the old FIN card as your identity. Update wallets and travel pouches when you collect the blue IC, which is on the [after PR checklist](/guides/after-getting-pr-checklist).

Plastic is replaceable. The one-week clock is the part that isn't.
