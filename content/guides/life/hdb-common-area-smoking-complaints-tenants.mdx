HDB corridor smoke threads are not abstract public-health essays. They are 11pm WhatsApp fights about **who owns the shared air** outside your door. This guide is the **common-area smoking complaint** rail — corridors, stairwells, void-deck benches — not a deep dive into smoke drifting from inside the next flat, and not a vape-law primer ([vaping & tobacco laws](/guides/vaping-tobacco-laws-foreigners)).

## Common-area vs unit-to-unit smoke

| Pattern | Primary rail |
| --- | --- |
| Smoker on the **corridor / staircase / void-deck** | OneService / Town Council common-area path |
| Smoke entering your flat from a **neighbour’s windows / doors** | Neighbour dispute / landlord / quiet enjoyment writing |
| Vapes / illegal products | Tobacco / vape law enforcement — different offence class |
| Condo common property | MCST / by-laws — not HDB Town Council |

## Evidence pack that actually routes

1. **Time + location** — block, floor, staircase or void-deck bay  
2. **Photo / short video** showing the act in the common area (not a doxx collage of faces for Telegram)  
3. **Frequency log** — three nights beats one angry message  
4. **OneService submission** with pin drop; keep the reference number  
5. **Optional:** polite block WhatsApp once — then stop debating  

Same reporting muscle as dengue breeding pins — [dengue / OneService ops](/guides/dengue-cluster-living-renters). Install the app in week one with the rest of the stack — [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## What not to do

- Corridor confrontations that become assault or property damage  
- Withholding rent because the staircase smells — [quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment) is written notice, not a rent strike  
- Fake “HDB ban” posters printed from a meme page  
- Assuming condo MCST rules apply verbatim to HDB corridors  

## Estate texture matters

Mature northeast stacks around [Kovan](/neighbourhoods/kovan), [Bartley](/neighbourhoods/bartley), and [Lorong Chuan](/neighbourhoods/lorong-chuan) mix older corridors with condo-edge living — walk the staircase at night before you LOI if smoke sensitivity is a hard constraint. For urgent safety (fire, violence), use [995 / 999](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies) — OneService is not an emergency line.
