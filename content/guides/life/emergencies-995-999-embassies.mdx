**Short answer:** Dial **995** for fire / ambulance / medical emergencies (SCDF) and **999** for police. Use **1799** for scam triage and **1800-777-0000** for family violence help. Embassies help with passports and welfare — they do **not** pay bills or cancel fines.

After the ambulance, dual-career households still need a ward-vs-home roster — [child hospitalisation coverage](/guides/dual-career-child-hospitalisation-coverage-singapore).

## Numbers to know cold

| Number | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **995** | Fire / ambulance / civil defence medical emergencies (SCDF) |
| **999** | Police emergencies |
| **1799** | ScamShield helpline (24/7) — [scams & Singpass](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats) |
| **1800-777-0000** | National Anti-Violence and Sexual Harassment Helpline (NAVH) — [family violence](/guides/family-violence-ppo-foreigners) |

Confirm current guidance for your situation; teach children and helpers the **block address** in clear English (unit + street + postal). Condo security can guide responders — still know your own door.

## Dengue and NEA home inspections

Singapore runs active **dengue cluster** mapping. If your estate is yellow/red, NEA officers may knock to inspect bathrooms, kitchens, plant trays, and other stagnant-water spots — **HDB and condo alike**. Reddit threads panic about fines; the official frame is vector control under the Control of Vectors and Pesticides Act.

Practical habits:

1. Check [NEA dengue clusters](https://www.nea.gov.sg/dengue-zika/dengue/dengue-clusters) and myENV alerts when neighbours mention fogging  
2. Empty plant saucers, pails, and AC drip trays — see also [aircon / pest renewals](/guides/aircon-pest-renewals)  
3. Verify officer ID before entry; helpers should know your rule if you are at work  
4. If larvae are sampled, do **not** pour them away — officers collect vials for lab confirmation; fines (if any) arrive by mail, not on-the-spot cash  

Living ops beyond the knock on the door — cluster colours, OneService breeding reports, HDB chute fogging: [dengue cluster living](/guides/dengue-cluster-living-renters).

## Embassies

Consular services help with lost passports and welfare — they do **not** override Singapore law, cancel NEA fines, or pay your medical bills. Save your mission’s contact alongside insurers and your company’s emergency line. After a death in-country or overseas, next of kin still need certificates, NEA permits, and embassy repatriation sequencing — [death & repatriation](/guides/death-repatriation-foreigners-singapore).

See [SCDF](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/) and [Singapore Police Force](https://www.police.gov.sg/) for official emergency information.

## Frequently asked questions

### 995 or 999 — which do I dial?

**995** = medical / fire / SCDF. **999** = police. If you are unsure and someone is badly hurt, **995** first; operators can cross-route.

### Can the embassy fix a dengue fine or hospital deposit?

**No.** They can advise and help with documents. Money and legal outcomes stay with you, your insurer, and Singapore agencies.

### What if a “police officer” calls about Singpass?

Hang up. Real enforcement does not ask you to read out OTP codes. Call **1799** or verify via official apps — [scam playbook](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats).
