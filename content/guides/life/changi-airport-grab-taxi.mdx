**Short answer:** After you clear Changi, **compare Grab’s upfront fare** with a **metered taxi + LTA Changi location surcharge** (**S$8** from **5pm–11:59pm**, else **S$6**). Both run overnight. Use the **MRT** only with light luggage and time to spare. You do **not** need a wad of SGD cash if you already have a card in Grab or a card-friendly taxi.

Jet-lagged Reddit threads still argue “always Grab” vs “always taxi queue.” The useful rule is a **30-second fare compare** at the kerb — not loyalty to a brand.

## Decision table (first ride)

| Situation | Prefer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy luggage / kids / rain / hard check-in time | Taxi queue or Grab | Door-to-door; less platform transfer |
| Late night (midnight–6am) | Taxi or Grab | MRT thinner; taxis add **50%** late-night hiring on the meter (LTA) |
| Light backpack, daytime, CBD / East-connected hotel | MRT + short walk/bus | Cheapest; practice [SimplyGo habits](/guides/mrt-bus-transport-literacy) |
| App shows surge 1.8× and taxi queue is short | Street-hail taxi | Fixed location surcharge beats opaque surge |
| Taxi queue snakes past 20+ minutes | Ride-hail | Pay for time, not ideology |

## Taxi: what the meter actually includes

Street-hail from Changi is still the classic path: follow **Taxi** signs after baggage claim.

1. Join the official taxi queue — not a lobby tout.  
2. Prefer standard liveries over unmarked “premium black” unless you mean to pay premium flag-down.  
3. Ask **“Card OK?”** before bags go in if you have no SGD cash.  
4. Expect **flag-down + distance/waiting** plus the **Changi location surcharge**, and possibly **peak (25%)** or **late-night (50%)** hiring on the metered portion.

| Changi taxi location surcharge (LTA) | When |
| --- | --- |
| **S$8** | 5pm to before midnight, any day |
| **S$6** | All other times |

## Grab / ride-hail: upfront fare, your pickup pin

Download **Grab** (and optionally Gojek / TADA) **before** you fly. Add a foreign card at home Wi-Fi so OTP fights happen on the sofa, not in Arrivals.

1. Clear immigration + bags first.  
2. Open the app on airport Wi-Fi or your new [prepaid +65 SIM](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank).  
3. Select the **exact pickup pin** the app shows for your terminal / Jewel door.  
4. Read the **upfront total** — surge and temporary operator fees are baked into what you confirm.  
5. Walk to the pin; do not accept kerbside “I can take Grab cash” strangers.

Grab’s Changi transfer pages emphasise **upfront fares** and private-hire options so you are not bargaining with touts — treat the in-app number as the contract: [Grab — Changi Airport transfers](https://www.grab.com/global/airport-rides/changi-airport/).

## MRT: when it is the adult choice

If you have one roller and daylight, public transport is fine. Tap with foreign contactless (SimplyGo **S$0.60/day** foreign-card admin fee) or buy a stored-value card — full habits in [MRT & bus literacy](/guides/mrt-bus-transport-literacy). Budget an extra transfer or short Grab for the last kilometre to a walk-up condo.
