Court threads are about peak-hour release. Camp threads are about **who booked the June holiday programme before the waitlist opened**. This guide is **ActiveSG holiday / school-break camp booking** for foreigners — distinct from casual [pool / lane hire](/guides/activesg-swimming-pools-singapore), [swimming lessons](/guides/activesg-swimming-lessons-booking-singapore), gym entry, and court sports, and narrower than the climate overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)).

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **camp / holiday programme** sessions — not court or lane hire  
4. Learn the **release week**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, complete medical / pickup forms  
6. Cancel early if travel plans change  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Camp-week etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the drop-off window — late entry is often refused  
- Label water bottles; pack for outdoor heat — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Follow allergy and medication instructions exactly  
- Use authorised pickup adults only  
- Camps beat ad-hoc babysitter weeks for belonging rhythm — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “kids club” math

A condo function room “holiday club” is not a SportSG programme. If you need structured multi-day coverage, ActiveSG Standard Rate camps usually beat WhatsApp parent co-ops that collapse mid-week.

Catchments around [Boon Keng](/neighbourhoods/boon-keng), [Sennett](/neighbourhoods/sennett), and [Geylang East](/neighbourhoods/geylang-east) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community centres — watch the holiday release calendar before you assume June still has places.
