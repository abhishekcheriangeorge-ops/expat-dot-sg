## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **skating / rink / skate-park** sessions — not court hire  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, pack PPE / hire money  
6. Cancel early if you bail  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Rink etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late entry is often refused  
- Follow direction-of-travel and speed rules  
- Dress for indoor AC after outdoor heat — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Family skate mornings beat solitary hotel-gym loops for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “roller zone” math

A condo multipurpose court is not a supervised ActiveSG rink. If you want session structure and hire skates, Standard Rate slots usually beat brochure “play court” photos.

Catchments around [Lavender South](/neighbourhoods/lavender-south), [Kallang Wave](/neighbourhoods/kallang-wave), and [Rochor Fringe](/neighbourhoods/rochor-fringe) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday daytime session before you assume weekend capacity exists.
