**Short answer:** Use **haze.gov.sg / myENV**, not random AQI widgets. **1-hour PM2.5** = decide now; **24-hour PSI** = plan tomorrow and follow MOH bands. Stock N95s + a bedroom purifier if anyone is sensitive — and treat **heat** as the year-round risk, not only haze weeks.

Transboundary haze is intermittent but memorable. **Heat stress is annual.** Flash floods are the third climate surprise renters under-check — see [flood / rental site checks](/guides/flash-floods-singapore-renters). Dengue-cluster weeks are the fourth — [cluster / fogging ops](/guides/dengue-cluster-living-renters). New arrivals often confuse third-party AQI apps with Singapore’s official **PSI** and **1-hour PM2.5** readings — then over- or under-react.

## Two numbers, two jobs

| Metric | Use it for | Orientation bands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1-hr PM2.5** (µg/m³) | Decide **now** (run, CCA, outdoor lunch) | Normal 0–55 · Elevated 56–150 · High 151–250 · Very high ≥251 |
| **24-hr PSI** (and haze-season forecast) | Plan **tomorrow** / school and training decisions | Good 0–50 · Moderate 51–100 · Unhealthy 101–200 · Very unhealthy 201–300 · Hazardous &gt;300 |

Scientific health evidence is largely built on **24-hour** exposure. That is why MOH advisories key off PSI bands even when you also watch the hourly PM2.5 chart.

## What to do when PSI rises

Orientation from MOH tables (verify live):

- **Healthy adults:** reduce prolonged/strenuous outdoor exertion from **Unhealthy (101–200)**; avoid it at **Very unhealthy**; minimise outdoor activity when **Hazardous**
- **Children, elderly, pregnant people:** tighten earlier — minimise strenuous outdoor activity from Unhealthy
- **Chronic heart/lung disease:** avoid strenuous outdoor exertion from Unhealthy; avoid outdoor activity at higher bands

N95-style masks help when you must be outdoors for longer stretches during haze; they are usually unnecessary indoors with windows closed and filtration running. People with heart/lung conditions should ask a doctor before prolonged masked exertion.

## Household kit

- Correctly sized N95-style masks for each family member  
- At least one decent purifier for sleeping rooms if anyone is haze-sensitive or asthmatic  
- Hydration norms for kids’ outdoor CCAs in heat — haze or not  
- myENV / haze.gov.sg bookmarked; do not rely solely on foreign AQI widgets  

When PSI spikes, reschedule outdoor sport without drama. It is ordinary regional weather politics meeting biology — not a personal failure of “tropical toughness.”
