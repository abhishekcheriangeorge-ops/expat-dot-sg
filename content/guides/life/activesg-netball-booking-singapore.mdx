Badminton threads are about peak release. Netball threads are about **which listing is actually netball, not a multipurpose court with hopeful bibs**. This guide is **ActiveSG netball booking** for foreigners — distinct from [pickleball](/guides/activesg-pickleball-booking-singapore), [fencing](/guides/activesg-fencing-booking-singapore), [badminton](/guides/activesg-badminton-court-booking-singapore), [tennis](/guides/activesg-tennis-court-booking-singapore), bowling, skating, climbing, archery, camps, and gym entry, and narrower than the climate overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)).

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **netball** — not pickleball, badminton, or fencing  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check bib rules  
6. Cancel early if your seventh drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Court etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose court time  
- Return shared bibs; keep drinks off the court  
- Dress for indoor AC after outdoor heat — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Drop-in cohorts beat solitary mall walks for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “multipurpose court” math

A condo badminton court with temporary posts is not a maintained ActiveSG netball SKU. If you want lined courts and programme pathways, Standard Rate sessions usually beat brochure “games court” photos.

Catchments around [Mattar Fringe](/neighbourhoods/mattar-fringe), [Tai Seng North](/neighbourhoods/tai-seng-north), and [Ubi West](/neighbourhoods/ubi-west) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume weekend capacity exists.
