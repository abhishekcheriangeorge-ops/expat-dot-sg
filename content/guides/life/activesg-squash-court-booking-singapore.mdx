Squash threads are rarely about grip. They are about **who refreshed MyActiveSG+ at release** and whether the condo court still has glass that isn’t cracked. This guide is **ActiveSG squash court booking** for foreigners — distinct from tennis, football/futsal cages, stadium tracks, and pool lanes, and narrower than the climate/gym overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)).

## What you are booking

| SKU flavour | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- |
| Singles court | Peak lunch / evening scarcity |
| Doubles / larger court (where offered) | Eyewear + etiquette; confirm court type in the app |
| Peak indoor slot | Release-time competition; no-show culture burns goodwill |

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **squash** (not tennis / badminton) at your centre  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, WhatsApp your hitting partner  
6. Cancel early if you bail  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one app stack: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Court etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Non-marking indoor shoes; no black-sole drama  
- Protective eyewear when the centre or your partner expects it  
- Arrive inside the grace window — courts turn over hard at peak  
- Heat / haze still matter for the walk to the centre — [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  
- Social ladders beat solitary boasts for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo court math

Many central condos sell squash in the brochure and deliver a locked door or a court used as furniture storage. Run a four-week experiment: ActiveSG Standard Rate vs the rent premium you paid for “facilities.” A local hit can also replace a restless weekend hop — [weekend getaways](/guides/weekend-getaways-from-singapore) — when Causeway timing looks ugly.

Catchments around [Lavender](/neighbourhoods/lavender), [Bugis](/neighbourhoods/bugis), and [Farrer Park](/neighbourhoods/farrer-park) sit near a dense band of ActiveSG / school indoor courts — test a midweek lunch slot before you assume the condo glass box is real.
