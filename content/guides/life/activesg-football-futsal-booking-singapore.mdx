Gym turnstiles and lap pools get the brochure space. The WhatsApp that actually fills is **“who booked the cage?”** This guide is **ActiveSG football pitches and futsal courts** for foreigners — a different product from climate/gym overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)), pool lanes, or stadium track bookings.

## What you are booking

| Facility | Typical use | Friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Football pitch / field | 11-a-side or smaller formats | Group hire + peak scarcity |
| Futsal / indoor cage | 5-a-side, after-work leagues | Release-time races |
| Outdoor hard court overlays | Casual kickabouts | Surface / light rules |

## Foreigner playbook

1. Get Singpass, then MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm you see **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Learn **your** centre’s booking release time; set a phone alarm  
4. One lead booker; split costs in the group chat after confirmation  
5. Screenshot QR / booking ID before anyone commutes  

Before Singpass, call the centre — walk-up rules vary.

## Peak etiquette

- No-shows burn slots; cancel early if the group collapses  
- Arrive inside the grace window with non-marking shoes where required  
- Heat / haze still apply outdoors — [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  
- Injuries: know [995 vs 999](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies) before you PR a sprint in 32°C
