## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **gateball** or **petanque** — not lawn bowls, cricket nets, or stadium court  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check kit rules  
6. Cancel early if your pair drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Court / piste etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose ends  
- Keep sticks, balls, and boules inside the marked playing area  
- Dress for outdoor heat and sudden showers — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Shared greens beat solitary gym loops for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “void deck bowls” math

A condo lawn or void deck is not a maintained ActiveSG gateball / petanque SKU. If you want marked courts, programme pathways, and Standard Rate booking rails, ActiveSG listings usually beat brochure “multi-purpose lawn” photos.

Catchments around [Pasir Ris West Fringe](/neighbourhoods/pasir-ris-west-fringe), [Loyang Edge](/neighbourhoods/loyang-edge), and [Tampines North Edge](/neighbourhoods/tampines-north-edge) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume evening capacity exists.
