Singapore’s grid is stable until the afternoon your lift dies, the freezer beeps, and Telegram fills with “is it just my block?” Power cuts here are usually **short** and **localised** — but high-floor renters still need a playbook.

## First five minutes

1. Check whether **corridor lights / neighbour doors** are dark — single-flat vs block  
2. Flip your **main switch / RCD** only if you know what you are doing; otherwise leave it  
3. Open the **SP Group** outage channels / app notices (save the contact page before you need it)  
4. If you smell **burning**, see sparks, or hear breaker chatter with smoke — leave and call **995** ([emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies))  
5. Text your **landlord / MA** once you know the blast radius

## High-floor and lift realism

- Treat lifts as **unavailable** until confirmed — stair fitness matters on level 30  
- Keep a small **torch / power bank** where you can find it in the dark  
- Medical devices / mobility needs: pre-plan building staff assistance; do not wait for the first outage to ask  
- Car-park barriers and garage doors may strand vehicles — walk before you rage-queue at the boom  

## Food, heat, and work-from-home

Singapore heat makes fridge/freezer clocks matter. Keep freezer doors closed; decide early whether to cook-and-eat thawing food. WFH renters: know the nearest **mall / library / office** with power before a deadline day. Aircon death in a sealed bedroom is a health issue — ventilate safely ([climate / haze habits](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)).

## Condo vs HDB wiring myths

- Block feeder faults are **SP Group** (or the building’s electrical contractor coordinating with SP)  
- Inside-unit trips after you plugged five adaptors into one socket are **your** problem — landlord electrician  
- Open Electricity Market retailer switches change **billing**, not who digs up the feeder ([utilities setup](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup))  

## When it overlaps other crises

Flash-flood evenings sometimes coincide with trips and pump failures — [flood renter checks](/guides/flash-floods-singapore-renters). Haze weeks still need ventilation discipline when the aircon dies. If a cut follows renovation works next door, document for your landlord; do not assume the contractor “owes you” grid restoration.

## Neighbour etiquette

Share SP updates in the chat group once; do not spam. Offer stair help to neighbours who need it. Candle use is a fire risk in HDB corridors — prefer torches.
