Hawker centres are civic infrastructure. They are also how you stop spending S$28 on mediocre salads out of fear.

## Payment reality (foreigners)

r/askSingapore tourist and newcomer threads agree: **mall cards work; many hawker stalls want cash or local QR**. PayNow / NETS QR usually needs a Singapore bank proxy — visitors without local banking cannot assume phone-wallet magic. Practical stack:

1. Withdraw modest cash in **S$10** notes (hawkers dislike huge S$50s for a S$4 meal)  
2. Once you have a local bank + FIN, register PayNow — see [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore)  
3. Keep a card for coffee chains and kopitiams that take contactless  

WeChat Pay is **not** a Singapore default outside China-facing tourist pockets.

## First plate strategy

Pick one nearby centre and learn **five stalls** well. Consistency beats endless novelty for weeknight sanity. Old Airport Road, Maxwell, Tekka, and neighbourhood centres near HDB estates often beat tourist-queue Orchard food courts on price and quality.

## Ordering tips

- Queue etiquette matters — join the end, not the side  
- “Less spice / no chilli / no pork” are normal requests  
- Pointing at photos is fine; mangling Mandarin is fine  
- **Tray return** is part of the social contract (centralised return points)  
- Peak lunch (12:00–13:30) means longer queues and sold-out favourites  

## Beyond the starter pack

Once chicken rice and laksa are on autopilot, rotate Indian-Muslim, Teochew porridge, vegetarian economy rice, and Malay/Indonesian stalls. Hawker meals are still one of the strongest cost-of-living levers for singles and DINKs — compare against [grocery cooking](/guides/shopping-essentials-singapore) and [household budgets](/guides/cost-of-living-by-household).
