r/askSingapore “caught with a K-pod / vape on DP” threads are not hypothetical trivia. **Possession and use of e-vaporisers is illegal in Singapore** for citizens, PRs, pass holders, and tourists. For foreigners, the immigration knock-on is the part forums under-weight: composition fine first, then **re-entry bans** and **pass revocation** on repeat or etomidate cases.

This is not a how-to for concealment. It is a pass-risk briefing.

## What the public guidance says (verify live)

gov.sg’s [Stop Vaping — higher penalties](https://www.gov.sg/stopvaping-penalties/) page (updated into 2026) summarises the Tobacco and Vaporisers Control Act (**TVCA**) framework and foreigner consequences:

| Situation | Published consequence theme |
| --- | --- |
| Foreigner found with an e-vaporiser | Device seized + **Notice of Composition** |
| **STVP** holder who **re-offends** | Ban from re-entering Singapore on departure |
| Long-term pass (EP, S Pass, WP, Student’s Pass, LTVP, DP) — **third** ordinary vaping offence | Pass / immigration facility may be **revoked**; deportation + re-entry ban possible |
| **Etomidate** vaporisers (K-pods) or positive etomidate test | Pass may be revoked; deportation + re-entry ban — appeals case-by-case |
| Import / supply | Far harsher criminal penalties under TVCA / related regimes |

Do not treat “first offence = just S$500” as a lifestyle cost. Composition amounts and escalation rules change; the pass risk is the career-ending part.

## Myths that get people deported in forum comments

- **“Tourists are exempt.”** No. Visitors must follow the same prohibition.  
- **“It’s only CBD / nicotine-free.”** Prohibited products are not saved by marketing labels.  
- **“My kid’s DP won’t affect my EP.”** Household outcomes are fact-specific; do not gamble. Student’s Pass / DP holders face school discipline **plus** national enforcement.  
- **“I’ll just pay and keep the device.”** Devices are seized.  
- **“Medication import rules are the same.”** Bringing approved meds under HSA is a different rail — [HSA medication](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa). Vapes are not “personal medication.”

## If you already use nicotine

Quit before you fly, or switch to **legal** tobacco products sold under Singapore’s tobacco controls (age limits, duty-paid retail, smoking-room rules). Do not mail vaporisers to yourself. Do not ask friends to “bring a couple for the flight.”

Quit support pointers on the gov.sg page historically included HPB QuitLine (**1800 438 2000**) — confirm live programmes; QuitVape programme status changed in 2026 per that page.

## Nightlife and share-culture

Bars and clubs do not create a legal bubble. Peer pressure on a night out is how Student’s Pass and young DP holders end up in the threads above — [nightlife](/guides/nightlife-social-scene) is social, not a waiver.

If you are already in police / HSA process, get counsel and notify your employer/school as required — pass cancellation timing interacts with [STVP between jobs](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) only after employment ends for other reasons; do not invent a “vape STVP.”
