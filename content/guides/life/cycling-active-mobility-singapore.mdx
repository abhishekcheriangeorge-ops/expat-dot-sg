**Short answer:** Pedal bikes need **no licence** but road helmets/lights matter. **PABs** must be LTA-registered with a theory test — grey-import e-bikes and footpath riding get people fined.

r/askSingapore cycling threads from tourists and new EP holders usually ask the same things: Do I need a licence? Can I ride the road? Why are e-bikes so restricted? Singapore’s **Active Mobility Act** answers those with path rules, device standards, and real fines — not “just be careful” etiquette.

## Ordinary bicycles

| Rule | Practical takeaway |
| --- | --- |
| No driving licence for pedal bikes | Still obey traffic signals on roads |
| **Helmet compulsory on roads** | Encouraged on paths |
| Front white + rear red lights / reflectors at night | Mandatory when riding on roads |
| Use cycling paths / PCNs where provided | Give way to slower path users; keep left |
| No expressways / tunnels | Plan Park Connector Network (PCN) routes instead |

East Coast Park and major PCNs are the forgiving practice grounds. Footpath etiquette still matters where shared use is allowed for pedal bikes — treat pedestrians as the priority user.

## Power-Assisted Bicycles (PABs / “e-bikes”)

Grey-import e-bikes that exceed LTA specs are a classic fine magnet. Legal PABs must typically:

- Meet **EN15194**, ≤ **250 W** continuous assist, assist cuts off by **25 km/h**  
- Weigh ≤ **20 kg** (published technical criteria)  
- Be **type-approved, sealed, and registered** at an LTA-authorised inspection centre with a visible registration plate  
- Be ridden by riders **≥ 16** who have passed the **mandatory PAB theory test**  
- Stay on **roads and cycling paths** — **not** footpaths

First-time penalties for riding without a valid theory certificate or using a non-compliant PAB are published in four-figure / jail ranges — do not treat Carousell “EU bike” listings as compliant.

## Personal Mobility Devices (PMDs)

Motorised PMDs (e-scooters and kin) have been heavily restricted relative to the mid-2010s free-for-all. Assume **public-path bans** and insurance/registration regimes unless you have verified the **current** LTA device class rules for the exact model. Do not import a tourist scooter and invent legality from 2018 forum posts.

## Commute realism for expats

- Heat + afternoon storms beat heroics; combine bike + MRT for longer hops ([MRT literacy](/guides/mrt-bus-transport-literacy)).  
- Condo bike rooms and HDB yellow boxes fill up — check estate rules before buying a cargo bike.  
- Own-car economics still lose for most households ([car vs sharing](/guides/car-ownership-vs-sharing-singapore)); a legal bike is a lifestyle tool, not a COE substitute.

Ride the published rules, not the Facebook group’s “never been stopped.”
