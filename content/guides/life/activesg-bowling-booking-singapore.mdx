## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **bowling / lane** sessions — not court hire  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, arrive for shoe hire  
6. Cancel early if the group shrinks  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Lane etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose the lane  
- Return house balls; keep drinks off the approach  
- Dress for indoor AC after outdoor heat — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Group bowls beat solitary mall walks for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “games room” math

A condo multipurpose hall is not a maintained ActiveSG lane. If you want scoring screens and shoe hire, Standard Rate sessions usually beat brochure “recreation room” photos.

Catchments around [Jalan Besar Fringe](/neighbourhoods/jalan-besar-fringe), [Farrer Park South](/neighbourhoods/farrer-park-south), and [Bendemeer West](/neighbourhoods/bendemeer-west) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday afternoon lane before you assume weekend capacity exists.
