**Short answer:** Install for jobs, not vibes. **Singpass first**, then ScamShield, one bank app, and transit. Prefer **PayNow → FIN**. Trust **haze.gov.sg / myENV**, not random AQI widgets. Everything else can wait until week two.

## Singpass notes foreigners hit

- SMS 2FA wants a **Singapore** number — set up the app properly before you travel with only a foreign SIM  
- Face verification saves you when SMS fails overseas  
- Keep recovery codes / device hygiene serious; lockouts are painful mid-renewal  
- Treat unexpected Singpass “verify device” prompts and bank WhatsApp threads as scams until proven otherwise via official apps  

<OfficialSource>
Use the official [Singpass](https://www.singpass.gov.sg/) site and app stores — not third-party “helpers.” Install [ScamShield](https://www.scamshield.gov.sg/) in week one.
</OfficialSource>

## PayNow: the FIN chicken-and-egg

Reddit’s “how do foreigners use PayNow?” threads share one answer: **you need a Singapore bank (or eligible MPI) account**. Tourists without local banking cannot magically peer-to-peer to someone’s personal mobile proxy.

Once you have an account:

1. Update your **FIN** (and Singapore mobile) in the bank’s records  
2. Register PayNow proxies — mobile and/or **NRIC/FIN** where your bank supports FIN  
3. Verify the recipient name before every transfer  

Some banks support FIN registration (e.g. DBS/POSB documents the FIN path); others may push you to a mobile-number proxy only. ABS summarises the network on the [PayNow page](https://www.abs.org.sg/e-payments/pay-now). Until PayNow works, carry a card + some cash for hawkers and small vendors — see [opening a bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat). Sent to the wrong proxy? Call the bank — do not peer-refund strangers — [wrong PayNow recovery](/guides/wrong-paynow-epayment-recovery).

## Delete ruthlessly

Food delivery, hawker discovery, and lifestyle apps are curiosity tools. If an app’s notifications cost more attention than they save, remove it after week two. Notification debt is a real cost of living.

Haze season: keep [NEA haze guidance](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/pollution-control/air-pollution/haze) bookmarked — see [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness).

Family note: haze weeks and early-dismissal SMS threads punish parents who only installed Grab — keep NEA / school portal / ActiveSG apps signed in on **both** phones.
