Install for jobs, not vibes. The only app that repeatedly blocks bank, telco, and government onboarding is **Singpass** — everything else is optional until week two.

## Week-one stack

| Need | App / service theme | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | **Singpass** (+ Face Verification) | Banks, MOM, ICA, many telcos |
| Number | Your telco app after SIM is live | Top-ups, eSIM, bills — see [utilities & telecom](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup) |
| Payments | Bank app + **PayNow** proxy once FIN links | Rent, helpers, hawker QR |
| Transit | Maps + LTA / rail operator tools you will actually open | Bus timing beats heroics |
| Gaps | Grab (or equivalent) | Late luggage, rain, airport runs |
| Air | NEA haze / PSI during burning season | School outdoor-play decisions |

## Singpass notes foreigners hit

- SMS 2FA wants a **Singapore** number — set up the app properly before you travel with only a foreign SIM  
- Face verification saves you when SMS fails overseas  
- Keep recovery codes / device hygiene serious; lockouts are painful mid-renewal  

<OfficialSource>
Use the official [Singpass](https://www.singpass.gov.sg/) site and app stores — not third-party “helpers.”
</OfficialSource>

## Delete ruthlessly

Food delivery, hawker discovery, and lifestyle apps are curiosity tools. If an app’s notifications cost more attention than they save, remove it after week two. Notification debt is a real cost of living.

Haze season: keep [NEA haze guidance](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/pollution-control/air-pollution/haze) bookmarked — see [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness).
