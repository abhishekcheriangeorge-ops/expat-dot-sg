Tennis threads on r/askSingapore are rarely about grip size. They are about **who hit refresh at release time**. This guide is **ActiveSG tennis court booking** for foreigners — distinct from the climate/gym overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)), football/futsal cages, stadium tracks, and pool lanes.

## What you are booking

| SKU flavour | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- |
| Outdoor hard court | Heat, haze, sudden storms |
| Indoor / sheltered court | Non-marking shoes, peak scarcity |
| Night slot with lights | Light surcharge on some centres |

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Learn your centre’s **release time**; set an alarm  
4. Book, screenshot confirmation, then WhatsApp your hitting partner  
5. Cancel early if you bail — no-shows burn community goodwill  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment.

## Court etiquette that keeps you unbanned

- Non-marking shoes; no black-sole drama  
- Arrive inside the grace window  
- Heat / haze strategy still applies outdoors — [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  
- Social hits beat solitary drills for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo court math

Many east-central condos sell tennis in the brochure and deliver a locked gate. Run a four-week experiment: ActiveSG Standard Rate vs the rent premium you paid for “facilities.” Week-one apps unlock booking — [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

Catchments around [Mountbatten](/neighbourhoods/mountbatten), [Nicoll Highway](/neighbourhoods/nicoll-highway), and [Aljunied](/neighbourhoods/aljunied) sit near a dense band of ActiveSG / school courts — test a midweek 19:30 slot before you assume the condo court is real.
