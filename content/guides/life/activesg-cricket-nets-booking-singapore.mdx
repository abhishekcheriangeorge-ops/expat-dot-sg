## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **cricket nets** — not lawn bowls, cycling circuit, or stadium court  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check kit rules  
6. Cancel early if your pair drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Net etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose overs  
- Bowl only from the posted end; clear the net between turns  
- Dress for outdoor heat and sudden showers — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Shared nets beat solitary gym loops for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “void deck cricket” math

A condo void deck or grass patch is not a maintained ActiveSG cricket-net SKU. If you want nets, bowling machines, and programme pathways, Standard Rate bookings usually beat brochure “multi-purpose court” photos.

Catchments around [Bedok Reservoir Fringe](/neighbourhoods/bedok-reservoir-fringe), [Tampines West Edge](/neighbourhoods/tampines-west-edge), and [Simei North](/neighbourhoods/simei-north) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume evening capacity exists.
