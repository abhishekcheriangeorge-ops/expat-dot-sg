**Short answer:** Daily pet life is **lease + licence + logistics**. HDB/AVS ownership limits are not landlord permission; PALS is not condo approval; public transport is mostly off-limits — plan GrabPet, PCNs, or a car.

Import paperwork gets you through Changi. Daily life is **lease + licence + logistics**. r/askSingapore pet threads after arrival cluster on three surprises: HDB rules changed for cats, condo MCSTs still say no, and public transport is not a pet bus.

For quarantine schedules and AQC booking, stay on [importing pets](/guides/importing-pets-singapore). This guide is the living stack.

## Housing reality check (write it in the TA)

| Housing type | AVS / HDB ownership limits (verify live) | Lease friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HDB** | Up to **2 cats** + **1 approved small-breed dog** per flat under current HDB Animals Rules / Cat Management Framework; no Specified (restricted) dog breeds | Landlord still decides; many refuse pets even when rules allow |
| **Private condo / landed** | Up to **3** cats/dogs combined per premises (one Specified dog rules apply); more needs AVS case-by-case approval | **MCST by-laws** + landlord clause — a PALS licence is **not** permission to keep the animal at that address |

AVS is explicit: a cat/dog licence does **not** approve the premises. Disclose breed, weight, and count in the LOI. Forum pattern: pet households often pay an extra month’s deposit or lose viewings — budget time, not just rent.

## Licences and household ops

1. Complete the AVS **Pet Ownership Course**, then apply via **PALS**  
2. Microchip cats (framework requirement) and keep licence renewals calendarised  
3. Mesh windows / balconies — fall-from-height is a real licensing and welfare risk in high-rises  
4. Update PALS address when you move flats — FIN/pass address changes and pet address changes are different admin tracks  

## Getting around with pets

Singapore public transport is generally **not** pet-friendly for dogs/cats outside approved assistance animals. Forum consensus:

| Option | When it works |
| --- | --- |
| **GrabPet / pet taxi** | Ideal on paper; drivers often scarce — book early or message confirming acceptance |
| Regular Grab / taxi + carrier | Small pets in secure carriers; always ask the driver before they start the trip |
| Own / shared car | Park-connector households often justify a car for vet + boarding runs — [car ownership math](/guides/car-ownership-vs-sharing-singapore) |
| Walk + park connectors | East Coast, West Coast, and inland PCNs are the daily exercise grid |

Vets, boarding, and grooming cluster near family belts — East Coast and west-coast corridors show up repeatedly in expat shortlists. See [East Coast](/entities/east-coast) and [Clementi / West Coast](/entities/clementi-west-coast).

## Neighbour diplomacy

Noise complaints and corridor “accidents” end leases faster than AVS fines. Keep litter sealed, leash in common areas, and learn your condo’s pet lift / pet path rules on day one. Heartland living etiquette still applies — [heartland living](/guides/heartland-living-for-expats).
