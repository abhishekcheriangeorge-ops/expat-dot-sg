## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **athletics / track** — not stadium court or field hire  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check spike rules  
6. Cancel early if your interval partner drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Lane etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose lane time  
- Keep slower traffic outside and clear the finish promptly  
- Dress for outdoor heat and sudden showers — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Group intervals beat solitary condo treadmill myths for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “jogging path” math

A condo perimeter path is not a maintained ActiveSG athletics SKU. If you want lined lanes and programme pathways, Standard Rate sessions usually beat brochure “fitness corner” photos.

Catchments around [Kembangan Fringe](/neighbourhoods/kembangan-fringe), [Bedok North West](/neighbourhoods/bedok-north-west), and [Guillemard East](/neighbourhoods/guillemard-east) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume evening capacity exists.
