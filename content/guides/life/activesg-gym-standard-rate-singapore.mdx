Gym threads on r/askSingapore are rarely about programming. They are about **Standard Rate vs membership screenshots** and whether the condo gym still has working cables. This guide is **ActiveSG gym Standard Rate** for foreigners — distinct from badminton, table tennis, squash, tennis, football/futsal, stadium tracks, and pool lanes, and narrower than the climate overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)).

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Pick a centre you can reach without a taxi every session — [MRT & bus literacy](/guides/mrt-bus-transport-literacy)  
4. Train off-peak when you can; screenshot confirmation if the centre requires booking windows  
5. Cancel early if you bail  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one app stack: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Session etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Wipe equipment; re-rack plates  
- Dress for the AC–outdoor swing on the walk over — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Heat / haze still matter if you run outdoors after — [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  
- Do not treat ActiveSG as a free commercial franchise — Standard Rate is public infrastructure  

## Condo gym math

Many south-west and harbourfront condos sell a “fully equipped gym” and deliver two treadmills and a broken cable stack. Run a four-week experiment: ActiveSG Standard Rate vs the rent premium you paid for “facilities.”

Catchments around [Redhill](/neighbourhoods/redhill), [Telok Blangah](/neighbourhoods/telok-blangah), and [HarbourFront](/neighbourhoods/harbourfront) sit near a dense band of ActiveSG centres — test a midweek lunch session before you assume the condo gym is real.
