## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **cycling circuit** — not athletics track or stadium court  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check helmet/bike rules  
6. Cancel early if your paceline drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Circuit etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose lap time  
- Ride the posted direction; call before overtaking  
- Dress for outdoor heat and sudden showers — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Group laps beat solitary mall walks for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “loop road” math

A condo internal road is not a maintained ActiveSG cycling-circuit SKU. If you want marshalled sessions and programme pathways, Standard Rate bookings usually beat brochure “jogging track” photos.

Catchments around [Siglap Fringe](/neighbourhoods/siglap-fringe), [Frankel East](/neighbourhoods/frankel-east), and [Opera Estate West](/neighbourhoods/opera-estate-west) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume evening capacity exists.
