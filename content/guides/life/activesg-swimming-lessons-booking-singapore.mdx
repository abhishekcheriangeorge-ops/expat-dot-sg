This guide is **ActiveSG swimming lesson / programme booking** for foreigners — distinct from casual [pool / lane hire](/guides/activesg-swimming-pools-singapore), gym entry, court sports, and studio classes, and narrower than the climate overview ([sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore)).

## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **swimming lesson / programme** sessions — not lane hire  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, pack goggles (+ cap)  
6. Cancel early if you bail  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment and intake. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Deck etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the grace window — late wet entry is often refused  
- Follow lane / teaching-area instructions from the instructor  
- Dress for the outdoor–AC swing after class — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Know where the nearest AED / guard desk is; emergencies still start at **995** — [emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies)  
- Lesson cohorts beat solitary hotel-pool laps for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo pool math

A condo lap pool is not a coaching programme. If you need strokes fixed, ActiveSG Standard Rate lessons usually beat private condo “coach WhatsApp” chaos — and you still need a separate plan for casual lane days.

Catchments around [Balestier](/neighbourhoods/balestier), [Thomson](/neighbourhoods/thomson), and [Braddell](/neighbourhoods/braddell) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community pools — test a weekday evening beginner slot before you assume weekend capacity exists.
