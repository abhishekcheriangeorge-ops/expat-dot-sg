**Short answer:** Government, banks and CPF will never ask you to transfer money, share an OTP or Singpass password, or install a sideloaded app. Install ScamShield, learn what the gov.sg SMS sender looks like, and call **1799** when something feels off. Then call your bank on the number from your card or app, not the one in the message.

New EP holders are unusually good targets, and it isn't bad luck. Your FIN card is fresh, your Singpass is new, you don't yet know what your bank's normal looks like, and recruiters are already asking you for passport scans as a matter of routine, so one more request for documents doesn't stand out. The recurring r/askSingapore pattern barely varies: a WhatsApp message about bank fraud, or a fake police officer, or a job ad that wants photos of your NRIC or EP, and then a Singpass login that drains the accounts.

## The list worth memorising

Government officials, banks and CPF Board will never ask you, over a call or a chat, to:

- Transfer money to a "safe" account
- Share a bank login, an OTP, or your Singpass password
- Install an app from outside the official stores
- Hand over cash, gold or jewellery for an "investigation"
- Approve a Singpass transaction you didn't start

There is no genuine version of any of those. If one of them is being asked for, you already know what you're dealing with, and you can stop reading the message. SPF's own banner points at the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline on 1799, and it exists precisely for the moment you're not certain.

## What to set up in your first week

| What to do | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Install ScamShield and grant the call and SMS filter permissions | Blocks numbers SPF has flagged, and filters suspicious SMS from unknown or overseas senders |
| Learn what the gov.sg SMS sender ID looks like | Agency SMS consolidates under the government sender, so anything outside it deserves a second look — [sms.gov.sg](https://sms.gov.sg/) |
| Give Singpass a strong unique password, and be deliberate about face verification | A compromised Singpass unlocks banks, CPF and IRAS in one go |
| Set transaction limits in your bank app, plus Money Lock or similar if your bank offers it | Limits the blast radius if an OTP does get phished |
| Never send front and back of your EP plus a selfie holding your FIN to "HR on Telegram" | Classic mule and loan-shark intake |

## If you've already clicked or shared

1. Hang up, or stop the chat. Don't "cooperate to clear your name" — that line is part of the script.
2. Call **1799** and your bank's official hotline, using the number on your card or in the app rather than one from the SMS.
3. Reset Singpass through the official helpdesk or security flow, then review your registered devices and recent transactions.
4. File an SPF i-Witness or Police e-services report with the numbers, links and account details you still have.
5. If a cracked APK was installed, factory-reset the phone once your accounts are secured.

Photos of an EP on their own usually aren't enough to empty a bank account overnight. Combined with OTP phishing or a Singpass device transfer, they are. Move fast; the time you spend being embarrassed is the time that actually mattered.

## Money-mule and "easy side job" traps

Ads that pay you to receive and forward money, register companies, or open accounts for "overseas clients" are how foreigners end up as the arrested middle layer rather than the victim anyone feels sorry for. Side income on a work pass already has MOM limits worth understanding, which is what the [side income guide](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) covers, and mule activity isn't a grey-area gig. It's criminal.
