## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **lawn bowls** — not indoor bowling, cycling circuit, or athletics track  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check shoe/bowl rules  
6. Cancel early if your rink partner drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Green etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose rink time  
- Wear flat shoes; keep drinks off the green  
- Dress for outdoor heat and sudden showers — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Programme cohorts beat solitary mall walks for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “games court” math

A condo multipurpose court is not a maintained ActiveSG lawn-bowls green. If you want lined rinks and programme pathways, Standard Rate sessions usually beat brochure “recreation deck” photos.

Catchments around [Upper East Coast Fringe](/neighbourhoods/upper-east-coast-fringe), [Lagoon View West](/neighbourhoods/lagoon-view-west), and [Mandarin Gardens East](/neighbourhoods/mandarin-gardens-east) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume weekend capacity exists.
