## Foreigner playbook

1. Singpass → MyActiveSG+  
2. Confirm **Standard Rate**, not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Filter for **sepak takraw** — not netball, pickleball, or badminton  
4. Learn the **release time**; set an alarm  
5. Book, screenshot confirmation, check ball rules  
6. Cancel early if your third drops  

Before Singpass, call the centre about counter payment. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore).

## Court etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Arrive inside the session window — late parties lose court time  
- Return shared balls; keep drinks off the court  
- Dress for indoor AC after outdoor heat — [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-singapore-climate)  
- Drop-in cohorts beat solitary mall walks for belonging — [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore)  

## Condo “multipurpose court” math

A condo badminton court with a borrowed net is not a maintained ActiveSG takraw SKU. If you want lined courts and programme pathways, Standard Rate sessions usually beat brochure “games court” photos.

Catchments around [Kaki Bukit Fringe](/neighbourhoods/kaki-bukit-fringe), [Eunos North](/neighbourhoods/eunos-north), and [Paya Lebar West](/neighbourhoods/paya-lebar-west) sit near a useful band of ActiveSG / community venues — test a weekday slot before you assume weekend capacity exists.
