**Short answer:** Singapore prices cars as a scarce privilege. **COE + taxes + parking** often beat “I spend S$1k on Grab” folklore. Prefer **MRT + Grab/GetGo** unless school-runs and weekly mileage clearly justify ownership — and convert your licence before the foreign-licence clock ends.

r/askSingapore’s car threads rhyme: someone spends **S$800–1,200+/month on Grab**, wonders if a car is cheaper, and locals reply that **COE alone** often prices ownership into a different socioeconomic tier. Singapore designs cars as a scarce privilege — not a default adult milestone. If you do drive, learn the accident report clocks before you need them — [traffic accidents & motor claims](/guides/traffic-accident-motor-claims-singapore).

## What a COE actually is

A **Certificate of Entitlement** is the right to register and use a vehicle on public roads, typically for **10 years**. You bid in LTA open exercises (usually twice a month). Quota premiums move; treat any “Cat A is X this week” WhatsApp as perishable.

At year ten you **deregister** or pay **Prevailing Quota Premium (PQP)** to renew. Unused COE time can yield rebates if you deregister early — still not a reason to buy casually on a three-year EP.

## Rough all-in stack (orientation)

| Cost bucket | Why it hurts |
| --- | --- |
| COE quota premium | Often the largest single line — category A/B/E prices swing |
| ARF / OMV taxes | Scales with vehicle value |
| Insurance + road tax | Annual, non-optional |
| Parking (home + office) | Condo lots and CBD season parking add up fast |
| ERP + petrol/charging | Variable; city-centre days punish casual driving |
| Maintenance / PARF exit | Resale depends on remaining COE years |

Forum heuristics often land **S$1,500–2,500+/month** economic cost for an ordinary car once you amortise COE — before lifestyle parking. Grab at S$400–800/month still loses on **convenience**, not on spreadsheet math.

## Decision table for expats

| Pattern | Usually better |
| --- | --- |
| CBD / MRT-heavy commute, few kids | MRT + occasional Grab — see [transport literacy](/guides/mrt-bus-transport-literacy) |
| Occasional IKEA / airport / weekend haul | **GetGo / BlueSG**-style hourly share or short rental |
| Heavy school-run + off-peak offices | Ownership or long-term hire — budget honestly |
| JB / Desaru weekends only | Cross-border hire or Grab to checkpoint; owning for weekend-only is expensive |
| Assignment &lt; 3 years left | Avoid fresh 10-year COE unless package includes a car |

## Car-sharing traps peers mention

- Photograph interior **and** exterior before/after; disputed damage bills are a recurring GetGo anxiety thread  
- P-plate / new-licence restrictions and higher excesses are common  
- Fleet condition varies — tyre wear and dirty cabins are user-reported, not urban legends  
- After a scrape, follow SPF’s mandatory-report criteria and the Motor Claims Framework — [accident playbook](/guides/traffic-accident-motor-claims-singapore) — not only the fleet assessor’s WhatsApp

Car-share is a **tool**, not free ownership.

## Condo visitor parking and CashCard reality

r/askSingapore visitor-parking threads ([do condos charge?](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/119y05w/do_condos_charge_visitors_for_parking/), [label / clamp culture](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1al3clm/condo_council_enforcing_car_label_dedicated/)) share one answer: **every MCST is different**. Guardhouse registration, time limits, paid visitor lots, and clamp theatres are by-laws — ask before your in-laws land at Changi with a rental car.

Driving itself still needs an **IU + CashCard / NETS FlashPay** stack for ERP and most gantry carparks. Foreign contactless bank cards that work on SimplyGo MRT do **not** magically open condo or mall parking. Buy/top up CashCard at 7-Eleven or petrol stations; keep balance before exit. Pets add another mobility tax — [pets daily life](/guides/pets-daily-life-singapore) for GrabPet vs car math.

## Licence first

You need a valid right to drive before any of this matters. Convert on the Traffic Police clock — do not invent a JB “weekend licence” shortcut. Full guide: [converting a foreign driving licence](/guides/converting-foreign-driving-licence).

## Package negotiation angle

If a employer dangles a car, ask whether they cover **COE / ARF / parking / insurance** or only a cash allowance. A S$1,200 “car allowance” rarely clears true ownership cost in a COE up-cycle.
