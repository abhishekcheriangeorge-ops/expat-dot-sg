ActiveSG is not only the gym turnstile. Expat threads that start “where can I run a 5k without dying?” or “how do foreigners book badminton?” are asking about **stadium tracks, indoor sports halls, and courts** — a different product from pool lanes and MyActiveGYM folklore. Pair this with the climate / gym overview in [sports & fitness](/guides/sports-fitness-singapore).

## What you are actually booking

| Facility type | Typical use | Booking friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stadium / running track | Laps, intervals, walk groups | Entry session vs field hire — read the SKU |
| Indoor sports hall | Badminton, volleyball, pickleball overlays | Peak slots vanish at release |
| Outdoor / indoor courts | Tennis, basketball | Surface rules + light surcharges |
| Field / pitch hire | Football groups | Group booking + deposit norms |

## Foreigner playbook (Standard Rate + Singpass)

1. Create / verify the **MyActiveSG+** account once you have Singpass  
2. Confirm you see the **Standard Rate** column — not a friend’s SC/PR screenshot  
3. Add a payment method the centre accepts (cash is often refused)  
4. Learn **your** centre’s booking release time; set a phone reminder  
5. Screenshot confirmation QR / booking ID before you commute  

Before Singpass, call the specific stadium or hall. Walk-up rules vary; do not trust a Facebook comment from 2019.

## Peak-hour etiquette that keeps you unbanned

- Arrive inside the grace window; no-shows burn slots other people refreshed for  
- Non-marking shoes on indoor courts — black-sole drama is real  
- Share courts when the booking type expects it; do not annex three nets for two players  
- Heat strategy still applies outdoors: dawn / late evening, haze checks via [haze preparedness](/guides/haze-climate-preparedness)  
- Injuries: know [995 vs 999](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies) before you PR a sprint session in 32°C

## Condo court vs ActiveSG math

Condo marketing sells tennis and badminton. After move-in, many blocks have **ghost booking systems** or courts closed for months of waterproofing. Run a four-week experiment: log ActiveSG Standard Rate visits vs the rent premium you paid for “facilities.” Heartland households without condo courts lean on ActiveSG hardest — see [heartland living](/guides/heartland-living-for-expats).
