The gym bag ate the fob; the boom gate rejects the spare; the MA wants an owner letter before cutting a new card. This guide is **condo resident access-card / fob replacement ops** for tenants — not [visitor parking / guest access cards](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants), not [intercom / boom-gate / visitor QR](/guides/condo-intercom-boom-gate-visitor-qr-tenants), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resident access card / RFID fob | Issued via MA; often inventoried to unit | Report loss; pay replacement if TA says so |
| Lift / wing-door tag | May be a second serial | Confirm which doors each serial opens |
| Visitor parking / guest QR | Guest-flow product | See visitor-parking guide |
| Owner authorisation letter | Landlord / agent | Request same day as the loss report |
| Deactivation proof | MA desk / email | Keep the written confirmation |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How many **resident cards / fobs / lift tags** does the unit inventory list?  
2. What is the MA **replacement fee** and turnaround?  
3. Does the MA need an **owner-signed** authorisation for new credentials?  
4. Are **barrier, lift, and wing doors** the same serial or separate profiles?  
5. Who pays replacement fees under the **TA**?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights over landlord delay still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- File a written loss report the same day; ask for deactivation by serial  
- Email the landlord / agent for owner authorisation without waiting for a site visit  
- Photograph the inventory schedule before you pay cash at the MA desk  
- Test barrier, lift, and wing doors after issue — do not assume one tag opens all  
- Pair with guest-flow literacy only when visitors need temporary access — [visitor parking](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants) + [visitor QR](/guides/condo-intercom-boom-gate-visitor-qr-tenants)  

Catchments around [Potong Pasir Fringe](/neighbourhoods/potong-pasir-fringe), [Kallang Bend](/neighbourhoods/kallang-bend), and [Lavender North](/neighbourhoods/lavender-north) mix older and newer credential stacks — assume MA forms differ by estate, not by MRT stop.
