Singapore’s private rental market moves quickly. Good units can collect multiple Letters of Intent in a weekend. Understanding the paper trail protects you more than haggling over a symbolic S$50 in rent. Tenant-side fees and renewal commissions are a separate contract from the TA — [CEA agent commission](/guides/tenant-agent-commission-cea).

## From viewing to LOI

After viewings, interested tenants submit a **Letter of Intent (LOI)** with proposed rent, lease length, move-in date, diplomatic/break clauses, and a good-faith booking deposit (commonly half a month’s rent, later applied to the security deposit).

LOIs are often treated as binding in practice once accepted — read before you WhatsApp “confirm.”

For **HDB**, add two LOI asks that private-condo hunters skip:

1. Copy of **HDB approval** to rent out the flat or bedroom (with your name to follow on registration)  
2. Whole-flat vs bedroom clarity — owners who “just visit on Sundays” fail continuous-occupation rules on bedroom rentals ([HDB bedroom playbook](/guides/hdb-bedroom-rental-rules-tenants))  

Also run the fibre TP availability check before the holding deposit clears — [fibre / NetLink](/guides/fibre-tp-netlink-renters).

## Tenancy Agreement (TA)

The TA is the full contract. Check:

- Inventory list completeness (especially furnished units)
- Diplomatic clause wording and notice period — deep dive: [security deposits and diplomatic clause](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause)
- Repair responsibilities and aircon servicing schedules
- Guest and pet rules (must match condo by-laws)
- Early termination penalties — if you lack a qualifying break clause, read [breaking a lease early](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants) before you sign
- **Landlord entry / quiet enjoyment** notice for inspections and end-of-lease viewings — [entry playbook](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment)
- **Sale / landlord-break** wording if you are in HDB (mid-lease sales are a recurring forum shock — [landlord selling](/guides/hdb-landlord-selling-tenants))
- Condition that HDB approval remains valid for the term (bedroom and whole-flat)

CEA publishes non-compulsory [tenancy agreement templates](https://www.cea.gov.sg/real-estate-professionals/agreements-and-checklists/) for HDB and private residential — use them as a clause checklist, then negotiate.
</OfficialSource>

## Stamp duty (lease duty) — the Reddit red flag

Agents who say “no stamp duty on this landed studio / room” are a classic scam or illegal-sublet tell. IRAS lease duty applies to residential tenancy documents; an unstamped TA is hard to enforce if the deposit fight starts.

| IRAS lease-duty rule (verify live) | Practical number |
| --- | --- |
| Average annual rent (AAR) ≤ S$1,000 | Exempt |
| Lease ≤ 4 years, AAR &gt; S$1,000 | **0.4% of total rent** for the lease period |
| Lease &gt; 4 years / indefinite | 0.4% of **4 × AAR** |
| Rounding | Rounded down; minimum duty S$1 |
| Who pays if the TA is silent | **Tenant / lessee** per Stamp Duties Act defaults |

Example: S$4,500/month × 24 months = S$108,000 total rent → roughly **S$432** lease duty (0.4%).

AAR includes many “other considerations” (furniture, service, advertising charges, etc.) excluding GST — read the IRAS renting page before you accept a suspiciously cheap quote.

<OfficialSource>
Rates and stamping: [IRAS — Renting a property](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/renting-a-property). Liability defaults: [Who should pay stamp duty](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/basics-of-stamp-duty-for-property/who-should-pay-stamp-duty). Stamp via myTax Portal → Stamp Lease/Tenancy Documents; keep the **Stamp Certificate**.

Negotiate who pays in the TA if you want the landlord to cover it — silence defaults to you. ## Deposit norms

Security deposits of one to two months are common for annual leases, depending on furnishings and landlord preference. Document every scratch on move-in day with dated photos — humidity mould fights start with move-in evidence, not WhatsApp arguments in month 23.

Family note: LOI timing should respect orientation calendars — do not schedule key collection on the same morning as bus trials and uniform fittings.
