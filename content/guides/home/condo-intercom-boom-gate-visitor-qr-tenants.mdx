Grab is at the boom; the intercom rings the landlord’s old number; the guard wants a QR you never generated. This guide is **condo intercom / boom-gate / visitor QR ops** for tenants — not [visitor parking / access-card deposits](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants), not lift padding, and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit intercom / app push | MA + occupying mobile | Register day-one numbers |
| Visitor QR / PIN | Hosting tenant via MA app | Issue timed codes; do not recycle |
| Boom-gate camera / LPR | MA / security vendor | Know drop-off vs overnight rules |
| Guard desk override | Security SOPs | Keep TA proof of occupancy handy |
| Access card / fob deposit | Separate product | Do not confuse with QR entry |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Which **app** issues visitor QR / PIN, and who can admin the unit?  
2. Does the **intercom** ring mobile, wall handset, or both?  
3. Is **boom-gate** LPR tied to resident IU only, or timed visitor codes?  
4. What is the **max visitor dwell** before a bay booking is required?  
5. Will MA staff update numbers when your **+65** changes?  

Put answers in email before your first grocery drop-off. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore). Fake “boom-gate fine” SMS still circulates — [scams & Singpass phishing](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats).

## Ops playbook

- Register every adult occupant’s mobile with the MA on handover  
- Test intercom + a sample visitor QR the same evening  
- Issue **timed** codes; screenshot confirmation for the guest  
- Separate overnight parking into the [visitor parking](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants) workflow  
- Escalate dead intercoms in writing — do not bribe the boom with “just this once”  

Catchments around [Balestier](/neighbourhoods/balestier), [Thomson](/neighbourhoods/thomson), and [Braddell](/neighbourhoods/braddell) mix older walk-up stock and newer condo towers — assume QR culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
