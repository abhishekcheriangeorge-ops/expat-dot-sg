You promised a Saturday BBQ; the MA app says **no slots for six weeks**, and the function-room form wants a **cleaning deposit** you never budgeted. This guide is **condo BBQ / function-room booking** for tenants — deposits, guest caps, and MA etiquette — not renovation works, not move-in lift padding, and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BBQ pit / pavilion slot | MA app / lobby tablet | Book early; peak weekends sell out |
| Function / multi-purpose room | MA + cleaning bond | Read guest cap and end-time rules |
| Facility deposit | Booker (often tenant) | Photograph the room before and after |
| Visitor parking for guests | Separate visitor rules | Register IUs — [visitor parking](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants) |

## Pre-booking checklist (email, not WhatsApp folklore)

1. Who may book — tenant card, owner approval, or both?  
2. **Guest cap**, end time, and quiet-hour rules  
3. **Deposit amount**, payment channel, and refund trigger  
4. Whether **music / alcohol / pets** are allowed in the pavilion  
5. Clean-up checklist the MA photographs at handover  

Put answers in writing before you invite twenty people. Brochure “resort living” is not a confirmed pit number.

## Ops playbook for the party

- Screenshot the booking confirmation into the household chat  
- Photograph the pit / room **before** guests arrive and after clean-up  
- Keep guest count inside the MA cap — overage is a by-law fight  
- Route visitor cars through the proper IU flow, not the loading bay  
- End on time; overtime cleaning eats deposits fast  

Social nights still need a plan B when the pit is booked out — [nightlife](/guides/nightlife-social-scene) and [dating & social](/guides/dating-social-singapore).

## Escalation without drama

1. MA ticket with booking ID, timestamps, and photos  
2. CC landlord/agent if access or deposit is wrongly withheld  
3. Do **not** withhold rent or force a locked pavilion door  

Catchments around [Chinatown](/neighbourhoods/chinatown), [Outram](/neighbourhoods/outram), and [Tanjong Pagar](/neighbourhoods/tanjong-pagar) mix older walk-ups and dense condo stock — assume MA facility rules vary by estate, not by MRT stop.
