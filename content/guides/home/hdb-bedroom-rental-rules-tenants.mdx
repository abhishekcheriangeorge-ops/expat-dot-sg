Heartland room rents look cheap until the setup is an **unauthorised bedroom rental**, a **partitioned living room**, or an “owner” who actually lives in JB and visits on Sundays. HDB’s rules are stricter than private-condo roommate culture — and tenants get caught in the cleanup when HDB revokes approval.

## Whole flat vs bedroom (tenant checklist)

| Question | Whole-flat rental | Bedroom rental |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who may rent out? | Generally **Singapore Citizen** owners after MOP (PR owners cannot rent out the **entire** flat) | SC **or** PR owners of **3-room+** flats (verify live; Plus/Prime nuances exist) |
| Owner must live in? | No — they rent out the whole unit with HDB approval | **Yes** — owners **and** authorised occupiers must be in **physical, continuous occupation** with you |
| Your pass | EP / S Pass / WP / Student’s / DP / LTVP etc. with enough remaining validity (commonly **≥6 months** at application — confirm live) | Same eligibility stack |
| Non-citizen quota | Neighbourhood/block **quota** can block non-Malaysian foreigners on whole-flat rentals | Quota **does not** apply the same way to bedroom rentals |
| Min lease | Typically **≥6 months**; short-lets are not a loophole | Same minimum-stay culture — no “Airbnb month” |

## The continuous-occupation trap

HDB is explicit: locking a room “for the owner” while living elsewhere (Malaysia, another flat, hotel) is **not** continuous occupation. If owners stop living in the flat, bedroom-rental approval can be **automatically revoked** and the arrangement treated as **unauthorised whole-flat rental**.

Forum heat ([owner stays but visits weekly](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1mzsjy6/inquiry_about_renting_hdb_with_owner_stay/)): ask, in writing, where the owner sleeps **most nights**. A Sunday drop-in is a red flag, not a quirk.

## Illegal partitions and “creative” rooms

Living-room plywood partitions rented as bedrooms are a recurring r/askSingapore nightmare ([partition room legality](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1kaidl9/isit_ever_legal_in_singapore_to_rent_out_a_hdb/)). Treat as high risk:

- Fire / escape and HDB alteration rules often make the setup unlawful  
- No proper room key / landlord enters freely → quiet-enjoyment and safety issues  
- Contracts purporting to lease an illegal room can fail hard when disputes reach SCT or higher courts — illegality arguments cut both ways; get advice for large deposits  

Practical pre-LOI tells: missing window, no real door/lock, kitchenette carved out of the living room, more unrelated adults than the flat type allows.

## What to demand before you pay

1. **HDB approval / registration letter** naming **you** as authorised tenant (owners can apply online; outcome is often immediate for bedrooms)  
2. TA clause: tenancy is **conditional on valid HDB approval** for the whole term  
3. Written owner-occupation statement for bedroom rentals  
4. Pass copies with enough validity; do not invent a “tourist bridge” — see [short-term vs long-term](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental)  
5. Stamp the TA — “no stamp duty on rooms” is a classic scam tell ([renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits))

HDB also notes tenants may check whether owners listed them as authorised occupants after approval — use that if the agent stalls.

## If you already moved into a bad setup

1. Photograph the layout (partition, locks, who else sleeps there)  
2. Ask the owner for the **current** HDB approval PDF  
3. If approval is missing / owner absents / partition is the “bedroom”: seek written mutual termination + full deposit return  
4. CEA complaint if a salesperson marketed an unlawful arrangement  
5. Deposit refusal → [SCT / diplomatic-clause guide](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) after you have docs — not WhatsApp threats  

This is not legal advice. Unauthorised subletting can also end with HDB telling occupants to leave on short clocks — budget a lawful backup stay early.
