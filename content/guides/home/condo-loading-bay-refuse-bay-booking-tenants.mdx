The mover WhatsApps “we’re 20 minutes early”; the loading bay still holds yesterday’s mattresses; the MA wants a deposit screenshot before the boom opens. This guide is **condo loading-bay / refuse-bay booking ops** for tenants — not [move-in/out lift booking](/guides/condo-move-in-move-out-lift-booking-tenants), not [visitor parking / access cards](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants), and not the full [waste / recycling / bulky disposal](/guides/condo-waste-recycling-bulky-disposal-tenants) playbook.

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loading bay slot | MA booking calendar | Book window; pay deposit |
| Refuse / bulky bay | Often same desk, sometimes separate | Confirm which calendar |
| Move-in/out lift booking | Separate estate product | See lift-booking guide |
| Visitor parking | Guest cars only | Do not stage trucks here |
| Bay overrun / no-show fees | By-law schedule | Photo + written reply |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How far ahead must you book the **loading bay**?  
2. Is the **refuse / bulky bay** the same calendar?  
3. What is the **deposit** and what fails the refund inspection?  
4. Can box trucks enter without a booked bay?  
5. How do bay slots interact with **lift booking** windows?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Intra-island hops: [moving within Singapore](/guides/moving-within-singapore).

## Ops playbook

- Book bay and lift as **two** products; align windows so the truck is not idling on the ramp  
- Screenshot confirmation + deposit receipt before movers leave the depot  
- Keep visitor lots clear of commercial vehicles — [visitor parking](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants)  
- Photograph bay condition at start and end for deposit return  
- Pair bulky rules with [waste / recycling / bulky disposal](/guides/condo-waste-recycling-bulky-disposal-tenants)  

Catchments around [Woodleigh Fringe](/neighbourhoods/woodleigh-fringe), [Boon Keng North](/neighbourhoods/boon-keng-north), and [Geylang Bahru West](/neighbourhoods/geylang-bahru-west) pack mid-age and newer estates with different bay calendars — assume MA forms differ by condo, not by MRT stop. By-law orientation: [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).
