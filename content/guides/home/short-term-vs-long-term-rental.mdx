Singapore tightly regulates short-term accommodation in **private residential properties**. That “condo Airbnb for six weeks while we house-hunt” listing is not a clever expat hack — under URA rules it is generally **illegal**.

## The three-month rule (private residential)

URA defines short-term accommodation as stays of **less than three consecutive months** in private residential properties (condos, landed, apartments, etc.). Owners and tenants must not rent those homes out as hotels, hostels, B&Bs, or home-shares.

| Lawful landing options | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Hotels | Any length |
| Licensed **serviced apartments** | URA notes a **minimum stay of seven days** for serviced apartments |
| Proper tenancy (≥3 consecutive months for private residential) | Living mode |

Penalties under the Planning Act can start with fines; URA also warns visitors they may be pulled into investigations if found in non-compliant stays.

<OfficialSource>
Read [URA short-term accommodation](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/short-term-accommodation/) and [URA renting property](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renting-property/) before you book a “monthly condo.”
</OfficialSource>

## HDB is a different regime

Open-market HDB rentals have their own eligibility (pass type, minimum lease lengths, registration). Tourists cannot rent HDB. Do not import private-condo folklore into HDB — check HDB’s pages for your pass.

<OfficialSource>
Confirm [HDB open-market rental eligibility](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/renting-a-flat/renting-from-the-open-market/eligibility).
</OfficialSource>

## Address / pass admin while you bridge

Employment Pass holders must keep a residential address updated with MOM. One-month grey stays create real friction when landlords will not enable **FWTES** declarations or when HDB minimum lease lengths block registration. Plan a lawful address you can actually declare — serviced residence or ≥3-month private lease — rather than improvising after the five-day clock starts.

## Decision rule

- **IPA soft / school undecided / ≤8 weeks** → pay the hotel or serviced premium  
- **Stable role + known school corridor** → run a proper 12–24 month search; see [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits)  
- **Never** use illegal short lets as “temporary address” for banks or MOM
