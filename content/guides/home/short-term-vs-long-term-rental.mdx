Singapore regulates short-term stays in private residential property tightly, and it enforces it. That "condo Airbnb for six weeks while we house-hunt" listing isn't a clever expat workaround everyone else has somehow missed. Under URA rules it's generally **illegal**.

## The three-month rule for private homes

URA treats any stay of **less than three consecutive months** in a private residential property (condos, landed, apartments and so on) as short-term accommodation. Owners and tenants aren't allowed to run those homes as hotels, hostels, B&Bs or home-shares. The rule keys off the length of the stay, so how the listing describes itself makes no difference.

| Lawful landing options | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Hotels | Any length |
| Licensed serviced apartments | URA notes a minimum stay of seven days for serviced apartments |
| Proper tenancy (≥3 consecutive months for private residential) | Living mode |

Penalties under the Planning Act can start with fines, and URA also warns that visitors staying somewhere non-compliant may find themselves pulled into the investigation. So it isn't purely the owner's problem to worry about. It's also a miserable thing to be dealing with in your first month in a new country.

## HDB plays by different rules

Open-market HDB rentals run on their own eligibility: pass type, minimum lease lengths, registration. Tourists can't rent an HDB flat at all. The trap is carrying private-condo folklore across into HDB, because the two regimes genuinely differ. Check HDB's own pages for your pass type rather than what worked for a friend in a condo.

## The address problem while you bridge

On an Employment Pass you have to keep a residential address updated with MOM, and that's where a month of grey-area staying starts to bite. Landlords in those arrangements won't enable FWTES declarations, and HDB minimum lease lengths can block registration outright. Line up an address you can lawfully declare — a serviced residence, or a private lease of ≥3 consecutive months — instead of improvising once the five-day clock has already started running.

## How to decide

If your IPA is still soft, the school is undecided and you're looking at 8 weeks or less, pay the hotel or serviced-apartment premium and stop trying to optimise it. If the role is stable and you know which school corridor you're aiming at, run a proper 12–24 month search; [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits) walks through how that actually goes. What you never want to do is use an illegal short let as the "temporary address" you give a bank or MOM.
