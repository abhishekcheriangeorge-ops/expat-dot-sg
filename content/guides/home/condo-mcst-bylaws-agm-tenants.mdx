Condo marketing sells the pool. Month three is when the **MCST by-laws** show up: renovation blackouts, visitor-parking caps, pet vetoes, and AGM notices your landlord forgets to forward. This guide is the **tenant orientation** to strata living — not a full noise playbook ([condo noise / MCST neighbours](/guides/condo-noise-mcst-neighbours)) and not the HDB vs condo eligibility compare ([condo vs HDB](/guides/condo-vs-hdb-expat)).

## By-laws vs tenancy agreement

| Document | What it controls for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Tenancy agreement** | Rent, deposit, landlord access, who pays utilities |
| **MCST by-laws / house rules** | Common property use, pets, renovation hours, moving-in bookings, BBQ / facility booking |
| **Managing agent circulars** | Short-term ops (tank cleaning, lift works, AGM date) |

If the TA and by-laws conflict on pets or renovations, assume the **stricter rule wins in practice** until the owner sorts it with council.

## Pre-LOI checklist (cheap)

1. Request the **current by-law / house-rules PDF**  
2. Confirm **pet**, **renovation**, **moving-in / lift booking**, and **visitor parking** clauses in writing  
3. Ask who the **managing agent** is and how residents submit tickets  
4. Photograph lobby notice boards on viewing day — upcoming works beat brochure copy  
5. Put “tenant will receive AGM / circular forwards” as an expectation to the landlord/agent  

## AGMs without a vote

Annual General Meetings are where budgets, by-law changes, and council elections happen. As a tenant you usually **do not vote**. Practical moves:

- Ask the landlord for the AGM pack if a motion hits pets, EV chargers, or major works above your unit  
- If you need to speak, request a **written proxy / attendance note** from the owner  
- Keep circulars — deposit fights love “you never told me about the façade project dust” claims ([deposit return](/guides/deposit-return-handover-disputes))

## Escalation ladder

1. MA ticket with photos and dates  
2. Landlord/agent in writing the same day  
3. Ask owner to raise as SP to council / at the next meeting  
4. Quiet-enjoyment trail if the breach is ongoing — [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment)  

Do not withhold rent to protest an MA silence.

East-central condo/HDB-edge belts like [Eunos](/neighbourhoods/eunos), [Guillemard](/neighbourhoods/guillemard), and [Dakota](/neighbourhoods/dakota) mix older MCSTs with newer inserts — by-law quality varies block to block. Walk the notice board before you LOI.
