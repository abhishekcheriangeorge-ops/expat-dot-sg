Keys are ready, the mover is on the CTE — and the guard asks for a **service-lift booking** you never made. This guide is **condo move-in / move-out lift ops** for tenants: padding deposits, loading bays, and MA checklists. It is **not** renovation / hacking deposits, and it is not the full [security deposit](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) fight.

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service / padded lift slot | MA booking system | Book early; peak month-ends sell out |
| Lift / lobby padding deposit | Booker (often tenant / mover) | Photograph padded routes before and after |
| Loading bay / IU registration | MA + guard house | Send plate + arrival window the day before |
| Unit inventory / make-good | TA + photos | Separate from MA common-area bond — [inventory](/guides/rental-inventory-fair-wear-tear) |

## Pre-move checklist (email, not WhatsApp folklore)

1. Exact **move date / time window** the MA will pad the lift  
2. **Deposit amount**, payment channel, and refund trigger  
3. Mover **COI / public liability** docs the MA demands  
4. Vehicle **plate numbers** and loading-bay rules  
5. Whether **carton-only** hand-carries still need a booking  
6. Who attends the **post-move MA inspection** of common areas  

If you are only ferrying suitcase loads, ask whether passenger lifts are allowed after-hours — rules vary by estate. Soft-goods strategy after keys: [furnishing a rental](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat). Cross-island timing: [moving within Singapore](/guides/moving-within-singapore).

## Ops playbook on the day

- Keep the MA confirmation + deposit receipt in the household chat  
- Photograph lift interiors, lobby corners, and refuse rooms **before** padding comes off  
- Do not let movers “borrow” neighbour lobbies or overnight in visitor lots without MA blessing  
- If the boom rejects the truck, reschedule — do not argue past the guard  

## Exit stacking

Move-out lift deposits and your **security deposit** are different pots. Clear the MA common-area inspection first, then run the unit checkout — [deposit return after handover](/guides/deposit-return-handover-disputes). Early exits still need lift slots before SCT rhetoric — [early lease termination](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants).

Catchments around [Little India](/neighbourhoods/little-india), [Rochor](/neighbourhoods/rochor), and [Jalan Besar](/neighbourhoods/jalan-besar) mix older walk-ups and condo inserts — assume MA rules vary by estate, not by MRT stop.
