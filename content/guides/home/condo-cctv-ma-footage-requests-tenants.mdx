A Grab package vanishes from the lift lobby; the MA says “fill the form”; WhatsApp wants the clip tonight. This guide is **condo common-area CCTV / MA footage request ops** for tenants — not [intercom / boom-gate / visitor QR](/guides/condo-intercom-boom-gate-visitor-qr-tenants), not [visitor parking deposits](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lobby / lift / carpark CCTV | MCST / MA / vendor | Request in writing fast |
| Retention window | Estate policy | Ask the same day as the incident |
| PDPA release rules | MA + PDPA | Expect ID checks; no casual forwards |
| In-unit landlord camera | Landlord / TA | Separate quiet-enjoyment path |
| DIY doorbell facing corridor | Often restricted | Get written MA approval first |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Where are **common CCTV** cameras, and is there a resident notice?  
2. What is the **retention** window before overwrite?  
3. What **form / email** starts a footage request, and who signs off?  
4. Does the MA require a **police report** for release?  
5. Are **doorbell / unit-door** cameras allowed in writing?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Real emergencies still start at **995/999** — [emergencies & embassies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies).

## Ops playbook

- Email the MA with unit, date, time window, camera location, and incident summary  
- CC the landlord if the TA requires owner nomination for requests  
- File a police report when theft / assault is involved — do not wait for the clip first  
- Never pay a random “CCTV retrieval fee” WhatsApp — [scams & Singpass phishing](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats)  
- Keep in-unit camera fights on the [quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment) track  

Catchments around [Boon Keng](/neighbourhoods/boon-keng), [Sennett](/neighbourhoods/sennett), and [Geylang East](/neighbourhoods/geylang-east) mix older and denser stock — assume footage culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
