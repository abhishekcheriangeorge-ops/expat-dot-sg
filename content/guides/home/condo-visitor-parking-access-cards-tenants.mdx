The first weekend you host friends, the lobby tablet asks for an **IU number** you never registered, and the guard points at a “visitor parking full” LED. Condo living is half facilities, half **access choreography**. This guide is visitor parking + access cards for tenants — not the full by-law/AGM orientation ([MCST by-laws & AGMs](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants)) and not HDB eligibility ([condo vs HDB](/guides/condo-vs-hdb-expat)).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resident access card / fob | Issued via MA; deposit often with landlord | Photograph serials at keys; return on handover |
| Visitor IU registration | MA app / lobby kiosk / guard | Register guests before they queue at the boom |
| Season parking lot | MCST allocation + TA | Confirm in writing before LOI |
| Lost card fee | Often tenant under TA | Keep MA receipt for deposit fights |

## Pre-LOI questions that save Saturday fights

1. How many **resident cards** and **visitor quotas** come with the unit?  
2. Is **season parking** included, waitlisted, or excluded?  
3. What is the **lost card** fee and who pays?  
4. Which app / QR registers visitor IUs, and what is the daily cap?  
5. Are **Grab / delivery** bays separate from overnight visitor lots?  

Put answers in email — brochure “ample parking” is not a lot number.

## Ops playbook

- Register your own IU (if you have a car) within the MA’s window — see also [car ownership vs sharing](/guides/car-ownership-vs-sharing-singapore)  
- Keep a screenshot of visitor rules in the household chat  
- For moves, book lift + padding + card deposits early — [moving within Singapore](/guides/moving-within-singapore)  
- At exit, return every fob and photograph the counter receipt — [deposit return](/guides/deposit-return-handover-disputes)  

## Escalation without drama

1. MA ticket with unit, timestamps, photos of the boom LED / clamp notice  
2. CC landlord/agent the same day  
3. Ask owner to escalate as subsidiary proprietor if access is wrongly cut  
4. Do **not** withhold rent or force the boom  

East-central condo belts around [Mountbatten](/neighbourhoods/mountbatten), [Nicoll Highway](/neighbourhoods/nicoll-highway), and [Aljunied](/neighbourhoods/aljunied) mix older MCSTs with newer inserts — visitor rules vary wildly. Walk the lobby tablet on viewing day before you promise guests free parking.
