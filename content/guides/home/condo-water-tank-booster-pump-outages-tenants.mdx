The MA posts “booster pump servicing 9am–1pm”; upper floors go dry; someone blames PUB. This guide is **condo water-tank / booster-pump outage ops** for tenants — not island [PUB pipe works / street interruptions](/guides/pub-water-disruption-pipe-works-renters), not [utilities account setup](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rooftop / intermediate tanks | MCST / MA / vendor | Read cleaning notices; stock water |
| Booster pump sets | MCST / MA | Expect upper-floor pressure loss windows |
| PUB street / network cut | PUB | Separate advisory — check address match |
| In-unit stopcock / leaks | Landlord / tenant TA | Quiet-enjoyment / repair path |
| Emergency dry-out | Household | Keep a 24h bottled buffer |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often does the estate clean **tanks** or service **boosters**?  
2. Where are outage notices posted (app, lobby, email)?  
3. Do upper floors lose pressure first during works?  
4. Is there a temporary **water point** during long windows?  
5. Who owns follow-up if cuts overrun the published slot?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). True emergencies still start at **995/999** — [emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies).

## Ops playbook

- Screenshot MA notices before the window starts  
- Fill bottles / kettle the night before planned works  
- Distinguish estate plant jobs from PUB street advisories for your block  
- Log overrun times in writing to the MA, then landlord if habitability is hit  
- After works, run taps; escalate persistent discoloration  

Catchments around [Lavender South](/neighbourhoods/lavender-south), [Kallang Wave](/neighbourhoods/kallang-wave), and [Rochor Fringe](/neighbourhoods/rochor-fringe) mix mid-rise and high-rise stock — assume tank/booster culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
