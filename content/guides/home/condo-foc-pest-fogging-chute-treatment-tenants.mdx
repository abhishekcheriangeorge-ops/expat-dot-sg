The MA posts “FOC fogging Thursday 10–12”; WhatsApp panics about pets; someone props the chute door open “for air.” This guide is **condo FOC / pest fogging / chute treatment ops** for tenants — not [unit aircon & pest renewals](/guides/aircon-pest-renewals), not [waste / bulky disposal](/guides/condo-waste-recycling-bulky-disposal-tenants), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estate FOC / pest contract | MCST / MA | Read notice windows; seal as advised |
| Refuse-chute treatment | FOC vendor | Keep chute doors closed; bag waste ahead |
| Common corridor fogging | FOC vendor | Bring laundry in; note pet/asthma needs |
| In-unit pest visit | Landlord / tenant contract | Separate booking + notice path |
| Dengue breeding on balconies | Occupying tenant | Clear trays; report clusters |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often does the estate run **FOC fogging / chute treatment**?  
2. Where are notices posted (app, lobby, email)?  
3. Does treatment ever require **unit access**?  
4. What is the protocol for **pets / asthma** on fogging days?  
5. Who owns **in-unit** cockroach contracts — landlord or tenant?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment still governs surprise entry — [landlord entry](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Treat MA schedules as real; do not “skip this month” by wedging chute doors  
- Bring corridor laundry in before fogging windows  
- Email medical/pet constraints **before** the slot, not during it  
- Keep unit pest contracts separate from estate FOC — [aircon & pest renewals](/guides/aircon-pest-renewals)  
- Pair with dengue hygiene — [dengue living](/guides/dengue-cluster-living-renters)  

Catchments around [Bugis South](/neighbourhoods/bugis-south), [Kallang Bahru](/neighbourhoods/kallang-bahru), and [Bendemeer East](/neighbourhoods/bendemeer-east) mix older and denser stock — assume FOC culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
