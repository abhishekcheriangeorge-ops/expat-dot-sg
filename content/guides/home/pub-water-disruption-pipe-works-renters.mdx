Singapore water is reliable until the morning your shower coughs air and the WhatsApp group fills with “no water again?” **Power cuts** are a different utility rail. **Flash floods** are drainage weather. This guide is **PUB pipe works and supply disruption** for renters.

## Planned works vs surprise dry taps

| Situation | First check | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift notice / SMS about pipe replacement | Dates, floors, temporary supply points | Stock jugs; shift showers; tell WFH clients |
| Only your flat is dry; neighbours fine | Internal stopcock / recent DIY / renovation | Landlord plumber — not PUB mains desk |
| Whole stack / block dry, no notice | Neighbours + PUB outage channels | Photograph notice boards; file via official contacts |
| Brown / yellow water after works restart | Run cold taps; skip laundry | Escalate if it does not clear |
| Ceiling wet during mains works | Photos + landlord | [Ceiling leak playbook](/guides/hdb-ceiling-leak-tenants) |

## Tenant kit for pipe-replacement week

1. Fill **clean bottles / pots** the night before a published shut-off  
2. Time showers and dishwasher runs around the window  
3. Keep a **bucket toilet** plan if the outage spans hours  
4. Screenshot PUB / town notices for your landlord file  
5. Do **not** open the main stopcock randomly during contractor isolation  

## Yellow water, laundry, and filters

After mains reconnection, sediment can tint cold water. Run taps to drain; postpone white laundry. Pitcher filters help taste, not a burst main. If water looks wrong for hours, use PUB quality contacts — not Facebook chlorine folklore.

## Landlord vs PUB vs MCST

- **PUB street / riser works:** utility authority notices  
- **Inside your leased unit:** landlord / agent plumber  
- **Condo common pump / tank:** MCST / MA — still loop the landlord  
- **Meter / billing confusion:** SP Group account path from [utilities setup](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup)  

Quiet-enjoyment talks need dates in writing ([landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment)). Rent strikes remain a bad idea ([early exit](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants)).

## Flood weeks are not the same problem

Heavy rain and PUB flash-flood maps are covered in [flash floods for renters](/guides/flash-floods-singapore-renters). A dry tap during pipe works is supply isolation, not a flooded driveway.

Central-east and north-east estates on older mains — think [Kallang](/neighbourhoods/kallang), [Bendemeer](/neighbourhoods/bendemeer), [Potong Pasir](/neighbourhoods/potong-pasir), [Toa Payoh](/neighbourhoods/toa-payoh) — see frequent forum chatter when replacement programmes roll through. Walk the lobby notices before you LOI a “quiet” week.
