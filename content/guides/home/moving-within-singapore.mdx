An island move looks “local” until elevator bookings, MCST deposits, and school pickup collide. Reddit’s condo-move threads are remarkably consistent: **management rules vary by development**, and assuming HDB-style freedom is how you lose a Saturday.

## Timeline that survives tropical rain

1. Secure the new tenancy agreement with a realistic start date  
2. Email **both** managing agents / MCSTs for move-in and move-out rules  
3. Book loading bay + lift slots (weekday mornings are often mandatory)  
4. Get mover quotes that include **trolley distance** and padding duties  
5. Decide what sells on Carousell vs what moves — see [furnishing](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat)  
6. Overlap leases by a few days if budget allows — same-day heroics fail in storms  
7. Update address with banks, telco, IRAS where relevant, schools, insurers, and MOM/ICA records when required  

## Condo friction: lift padding and deposits

Forum ranges you should expect (each MCST sets its own by-laws):

| Item | Typical pattern | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refundable damage deposit | Often **S$500–1,000** per building | Cheque or card hold; release after inspection |
| Lift padding fee | **S$0–200**, sometimes non-refundable | Ask who installs: management, movers, or you |
| Move windows | Weekdays / morning-only common | Weekend refusals are normal, not personal |
| Loading distance | Movers may surcharge “long push” | Photograph the path from bay to lift |

Document corridor and lift condition **before and after**. If padding is “collect and hang yourself,” confirm hooks exist and return the pads the same day.

HDB moves are usually lighter on bureaucracy; condo moves price in CCTV, by-laws, and neighbour complaints. Tell movers the **floor, lift size, and walking distance** up front — surprise surcharges are how quotes become arguments.

## Agent fee myth when the landlord is overseas

CEA rules are blunt: a salesperson may represent the **landlord or the tenant**, not both, even with consent. Dual representation is an offence.

If the listing agent says “landlord is overseas, so you pay my fee,” ask in writing whom they represent. A landlord’s agent helping with paperwork is not the same as you appointing them. Agree any tenant-side commission in a CEA prescribed estate agency agreement — pay the **agency**, not cash to an individual.

## People logistics

- Stage one bedroom first so kids (and adults) have a calm base  
- If you employ an MDW, plan rest days and packing help within [legal rest-day rules](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw)  
- Keep aircon service invoices and meter photos for both ends — deposit fights love ambiguity  

## Address and pass admin

Treat the move as an admin project: bank KYC address, telco bills, school bus routes, insurance panels, and any MOM/ICA address updates your pass type requires. Incomplete address trails cause FWTES and banking friction later — see [short-term vs long-term rental](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental) if you are bridging illegally with hotels.
