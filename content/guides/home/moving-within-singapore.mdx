An intra-island move looks like the easy version right up until lift bookings, MCST deposits and the school run all land on the same Saturday. The condo-move threads on Reddit are consistent about one thing: management rules vary by development, and assuming you’ll get HDB-style freedom is how you lose a weekend. Two things to do before anything else. Re-check what your tenant agent wants for a renewal before you appoint them again, which is what [CEA commission](/guides/tenant-agent-commission-cea) is for. And if you’re moving because an HDB landlord sold mid-lease, build in more calendar buffer than feels necessary ([landlord selling playbook](/guides/hdb-landlord-selling-tenants)).

A new estate also means a new CC and a new playground map. Rebuild that side of things early, via [CC and PA parent belonging](/guides/find-my-people-singapore), rather than after you’ve booked the first birthday venue back in the old neighbourhood.

## A timeline that survives tropical rain

1. Secure the new tenancy agreement with a start date you actually believe in
2. Email both managing agents or MCSTs, or both Town Councils, for the move-in and move-out rules
3. Book the loading bay and the lift slots, because weekday mornings are often mandatory
4. Get mover quotes that price in trolley distance and padding duties, not just volume
5. Decide what sells on Carousell and what genuinely travels — see [furnishing](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat)
6. Overlap the leases by a few days if the budget stretches, because same-day heroics fail in storms
7. Update your address with banks, telco, IRAS where relevant, schools, insurers, and MOM/ICA records where required

## Condo friction: lift padding and deposits

Here’s the shape of what people report, with the standing caveat that every MCST writes its own by-laws and yours may be stricter than the table.

| Item | Typical pattern | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refundable damage deposit | Often S$500–1,000 per building | Cheque or card hold; release after inspection |
| Lift padding fee | S$0–200, sometimes non-refundable | Ask who installs: management, movers, or you |
| Move windows | Weekdays / morning-only common | Weekend refusals are normal, not personal |
| Loading distance | Movers may surcharge “long push” | Photograph the path from bay to lift |

Photograph the corridor and lift condition before and after. If the padding is a collect-it-and-hang-it-yourself arrangement, check that the hooks actually exist before move day, and return the pads the same evening, because **the deposit only comes back after the inspection**.

## HDB moves: lighter paperwork, still book the lift

HDB is usually lighter than the condo deposit routine, but Town Councils still care about padded lifts, protected lobbies and where things get dumped. Use the estate’s stated channel to book lift padding at both ends, and don’t assume void-deck parking is free for a 10-tonne lorry. Bag the rubbish properly on exit day too. Bulky items are not a chute problem ([shopping and bulky waste](/guides/shopping-essentials-singapore)).

Tell the movers the floor, the lift size and the walking distance up front. Surprise surcharges are how a quote turns into an argument on move day, when you have no leverage and a lorry blocking the bay. If the new block faces a live BTO site, put earplugs in the week-one kit ([construction noise](/guides/construction-site-noise-renters)).

## The agent fee myth when the landlord is overseas

CEA is blunt about this. A salesperson may represent the landlord or the tenant, not both, even where everyone consents. Dual representation is an offence.

So when the listing agent tells you “the landlord is overseas, so you pay my fee,” ask in writing whom they represent. An agent helping a landlord with paperwork is not the same thing as you appointing that agent, and the fee follows the appointment. If there genuinely is a tenant-side commission, agree it in a CEA prescribed estate agency agreement and **pay the agency, never cash to an individual**.

## The people part

Stage one bedroom first, so the kids — and, frankly, you — have one calm room to retreat into on night one. If you employ an MDW, plan rest days and packing help within the [legal rest-day rules](/guides/hiring-domestic-helper-mdw) rather than treating move week as an exception to them. And keep the aircon service invoices and the meter photos for both ends. Deposit fights run on ambiguity, and photographs are the cheapest way to remove it.

## Address and pass admin

Treat the move as an admin project in its own right: bank KYC address, telco bills, school bus routes, insurance panels, and whatever MOM or ICA address updates your pass type requires. An incomplete address trail causes FWTES and banking friction months later, long after you’ve forgotten which update you skipped. If you’re bridging the gap between leases with hotels or an informal short let, read [short-term vs long-term rental](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental) first, because not everything advertised as short-term is legal here.
