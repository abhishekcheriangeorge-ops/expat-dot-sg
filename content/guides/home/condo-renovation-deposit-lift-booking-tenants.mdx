Your contractor is ready Monday; the managing agent wants a **renovation deposit**, contractor indemnity, and a **padded-lift slot** you never budgeted. This guide is **your-unit renovation ops** for condo tenants — deposits, lift booking, and make-good — not the [neighbour drilling playbook](/guides/condo-unit-renovation-adjacent-tenants) and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renovation application / by-law hours | MA + MCST | Ask landlord for the stamped approval PDF before dumpsters arrive |
| Renovation deposit (common-property bond) | Usually SP / landlord | Confirm who pays and who chases refund |
| Lift padding + loading bay booking | MA booking system | Book early; peak move weeks sell out |
| Unit inventory / make-good | TA + photos | Treat reno as a mini handover — [inventory & fair wear](/guides/rental-inventory-fair-wear-tear) |

## Pre-works checklist (email, not WhatsApp folklore)

1. Who is the **applicant** on the renovation form — owner or authorised tenant?  
2. Exact **work hours** and quiet-day rules in this estate’s by-laws  
3. **Deposit amount**, payment channel, and refund trigger  
4. Contractor **insurance / work-pass** pack the MA demands  
5. **Lift padding** dates for inbound materials and outbound debris  
6. Whether you remain in occupation — and what that does to quiet enjoyment  

If you are only furnishing (IKEA runs, no hacking), you may still need a **move-in lift booking** without a full reno deposit — clarify with the MA before the truck idles at the boom. Soft goods strategy: [furnishing a rental](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat).

## Ops playbook during works

- Keep the MA approval + lift confirmation in the household chat  
- Photograph lift interiors, lobby corners, and refuse rooms **before** padding comes off  
- Route complaints about after-hours drilling through MA tickets with timestamps — same muscle as [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment)  
- Do not let contractors “borrow” neighbour lobbies or park overnight in visitor lots without MA blessing  

## Exit and deposit stacking

Renovation deposits and your **security deposit** are different pots. Clear the MA common-area inspection first, then run the unit checkout — [deposit return after handover](/guides/deposit-return-handover-disputes). If works spill into a move to another flat, align lift slots with [moving within Singapore](/guides/moving-within-singapore).

Catchments around [Lavender](/neighbourhoods/lavender), [Bugis](/neighbourhoods/bugis), and [Farrer Park](/neighbourhoods/farrer-park) are dense with older condo and walk-up stock — assume MA rules vary by estate, not by MRT stop.
