Reddit’s “my SP bill exploded” threads are rarely about a haunted meter. They are about **who holds the account**, whether the month was an **estimate vs actual read**, and whether a dehumidifier / second aircon / OEM plan step quietly changed the math. This guide is the **billing dispute** rail — not blackout response, and not PUB street pipe shut-offs.

## First triage (same day)

| Signal | Likely cause | First move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bill ~2× after a quiet quarter | Actual read after estimates | Ask SP for read type history; photograph meter |
| Water line spikes, electricity flat | Leak / running cistern / dripping heater | [PUB leak tips PDF](https://www.pub.gov.sg/-/media/Images/Feature/Content-Pages/Resources/Publications/Pdfs/Tips_on_Finding_Leaks.pdf) + landlord |
| Electricity spikes in monsoon | Dehumidifier / aircon runtime | Log RH and hours; see [mould playbook](/guides/mould-humidity-rental-tenants) |
| Price per kWh jumped, usage flat | OEM retailer plan / promo end | Pull plan PDF from retailer portal |
| Only your unit vs whole stack | Internal fault or account mix-up | Compare with neighbour WhatsApp; call SP |

## Landlord account vs tenant account

**Landlord keeps SP, recharges you:** demand the **itemised SP / retailer PDF** each month. Round-number PayNow captions (“utilities $280”) are how disputed months become deposit fights. Put a clause expectation in writing at LOI if the TA is vague.

**Account in your name:** you open/close on key dates, you dispute, you own final-read timing. Photograph meters on **move-in day one** and **move-out handover** — same habit as [inventory / fair wear](/guides/rental-inventory-fair-wear-tear).

**Dual-account chaos during a sale or mid-lease transfer:** confirm whose name the live account is under before you pay anyone “to keep the lights on.” Mid-sale cut-off folklore belongs in writing to agent + landlord, not in a rent strike.

## Estimated reads and true-ups

Estimated bills smooth the graph until an actual read lands. Forum spikes after travel months or locked gates (meter reader could not access) are classic. Ask SP:

1. Which months were **estimated** vs **actual**?
2. What was the previous actual read date?
3. Can they schedule / confirm the next actual read?

Do not pay a “friend who knows someone at SP” to “fix” the meter. Use the official app / hotline trail so you have ticket numbers if CASE or Small Claims becomes relevant later.

## OEM plan shock

If you switched retailers under the Open Electricity Market, the bill can move because **the plan changed**, not because metering failed. Re-check live offers on [OEM price comparison](https://compare.openelectricitymarket.sg/#/home). Watch for:

- Promo / discount periods ending
- Peak / off-peak products that punish all-day WFH cooling
- Bundled “free months” that shift cost into later periods
- Exit fees if you chase another retailer mid-contract

SP still handles the network / metering side for many journeys — but the **unit rate argument** is with your retailer contract.

## Evidence pack for a dispute

1. Bill PDFs for the disputed month + prior three months  
2. Phone photo of the meter (digits + meter ID) on the bill date if possible  
3. Short usage diary (guests, dehumidifier, aircon chemical wash week)  
4. TA clause on who pays utilities + any WhatsApp recharge history  
5. Ticket / chat reference from SP or the retailer  

Then ask for a **re-read**, leak check, or plan clarification — in that order. [Aircon servicing weeks](/guides/aircon-pest-renewals) sometimes coincide with “mystery” spikes when a second unit was left on after chemical wash.

## What not to do

- **Withhold rent** to protest a utility line ([early exit / breach risk](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants))  
- Pay cash to a “meter agent” who messaged you on Telegram  
- Ignore a final bill after handover — closing reads chase FIN holders  
- Confuse a **PUB street shut-off** with an SP billing dispute
