The MA posts a “cat-ladder / roof access permit”; someone assumes it is LPS testing; someone else treats the roof as a party deck. This guide is **condo cat-ladder / roof-access permit notice ops** for tenants — not [LPS / earth-pit inspections](/guides/condo-lightning-protection-earth-pit-inspection-notices-tenants), not [facade / gondola access](/guides/condo-facade-gondola-access-notices-tenants), and not [standby generator / ATS testing](/guides/condo-standby-generator-ats-testing-notices-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cat-ladder / roof permit | MCST common property | Read notice; keep clear of locked cages |
| Hatch keys / escort rules | MA / contractor | Do not “borrow” access |
| LPS / earth-pit inspection | Separate electrical product | See LPS guide |
| Facade / gondola programme | Separate common-property product | See facade guide |
| Generator / ATS test | Separate electrical product | See generator/ATS guide |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often do **cat-ladder / roof permits** appear on the estate calendar?  
2. Are roof decks ever open to residents?  
3. Does any notice require **ledge or corridor** clearance near hatches?  
4. How are **overruns** into quiet hours reported?  
5. How do notices interact with **WFH / helper rest-day** calendars?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Treat circulars as hard calendars — stay off locked ladders and hatches  
- Photograph corridor/ledge condition if temporary clearance is named  
- Do not confuse roof crews with LPS, gondola, or genset teams  
- Write the MA the same day if access windows overrun  
- Pair by-law questions with [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants)  

Catchments around [Upper East Coast Fringe](/neighbourhoods/upper-east-coast-fringe), [Lagoon View West](/neighbourhoods/lagoon-view-west), and [Mandarin Gardens East](/neighbourhoods/mandarin-gardens-east) mix mid-rise and high-rise stock with different roof-access cultures — assume MA forms differ by estate, not by bus stop.
