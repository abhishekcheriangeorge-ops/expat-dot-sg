The MA posts a “smoke-stop / staircase pressurisation fan test”; someone bags the smoke detector; someone else clears a hydrant landing that was never in scope. This guide is **condo smoke-stop and staircase pressurisation fan test notice ops** for tenants — not [sprinkler drain-down / fire-shutter tests](/guides/condo-sprinkler-drain-down-fire-shutter-test-notices-tenants), not [hydrant / hose-reel clearance](/guides/condo-fire-hose-landing-hydrant-clearance-tenants), not [fire-alarm / smoke-detector drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills), not [LPS / earth-pit inspections](/guides/condo-lightning-protection-earth-pit-inspection-notices-tenants), not [standby generator / ATS testing](/guides/condo-standby-generator-ats-testing-notices-tenants), and not [cat-ladder / roof-access permits](/guides/condo-cat-ladder-roof-access-permit-notices-tenants).

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often does the estate run **smoke-stop** or **staircase pressurisation fan** tests?  
2. Do notices ever close **staircores or smoke-stop lobbies** for a morning?  
3. How loud are **fan plant rooms** beside upper-floor units?  
4. Who do you write if staircore noise overruns into quiet hours?  
5. How do notices interact with **WFH / helper rest-day** calendars?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Treat circulars as hard calendars — clear only what the notice names  
- Photograph named lobbies or corridor edges if temporary clearance is required  
- Do not confuse fan / smoke-stop crews with sprinkler, shutter, LPS, genset, or roof-permit teams  
- Pair siren confusion with [fire-alarm drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills) only when tones are involved  
- Write the MA the same day if windows overrun  

Catchments around [Pasir Ris West Fringe](/neighbourhoods/pasir-ris-west-fringe), [Loyang Edge](/neighbourhoods/loyang-edge), and [Tampines North Edge](/neighbourhoods/tampines-north-edge) mix mid-rise and tower stock with different smoke-control calendars — assume MA forms differ by estate, not by MRT stop. By-law orientation: [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).
