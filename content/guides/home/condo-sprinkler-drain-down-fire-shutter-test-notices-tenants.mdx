The MA posts a “sprinkler drain-down / fire-shutter test”; someone bags the smoke detector; someone else clears a hydrant landing that was never in scope. This guide is **condo sprinkler drain-down and fire-shutter test notice ops** for tenants — not [hydrant / hose-reel clearance](/guides/condo-fire-hose-landing-hydrant-clearance-tenants), not [fire-alarm / smoke-detector drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills), not [LPS / earth-pit inspections](/guides/condo-lightning-protection-earth-pit-inspection-notices-tenants), not [standby generator / ATS testing](/guides/condo-standby-generator-ats-testing-notices-tenants), not [facade / gondola access](/guides/condo-facade-gondola-access-notices-tenants), and not [cat-ladder / roof-access permits](/guides/condo-cat-ladder-roof-access-permit-notices-tenants).

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often does the estate run **sprinkler drain-downs** or **fire-shutter travel tests**?  
2. Do notices ever name **riser cupboards, floor drains, or unit-adjacent valves**?  
3. How are **corridor / lobby shutter paths** closed and reopened?  
4. Who do you write if wet work or shutter noise overruns into quiet hours?  
5. How do notices interact with **WFH / helper rest-day** calendars?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Treat circulars as hard calendars — clear only what the notice names  
- Photograph named cupboards, drains, or shutter paths before crews arrive  
- Do not confuse drain-down crews with LPS, genset, gondola, or roof-permit teams  
- Pair siren confusion with [fire-alarm drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills) only when tones are involved  
- Write the MA the same day if windows overrun  

Catchments around [Bedok Reservoir Fringe](/neighbourhoods/bedok-reservoir-fringe), [Tampines West Edge](/neighbourhoods/tampines-west-edge), and [Simei North](/neighbourhoods/simei-north) mix mid-rise and tower stock with different fire-protection calendars — assume MA forms differ by estate, not by MRT stop. By-law orientation: [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).
