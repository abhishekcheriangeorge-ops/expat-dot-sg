Many expat families employ a migrant domestic worker (MDW) under MOM’s Work Permit framework. This is a regulated employment relationship — not informal cash help — and the all-in monthly cost is easy to undercount if you only budget salary.

Employer peer tips help, but belonging and compliance are different jobs — [workplace parent & helper-community belonging](/guides/workplace-parent-helper-community-belonging-singapore).

## The cost stack (plan with live MOM rates)

| Cost line | Typical planning band | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic monthly salary | ~S$600–1,200+ | Peer reports cluster ~S$650–900 for experienced transfer helpers; infant care / multi-kid roles run higher |
| Foreign worker levy | S$300 / S$450 / S$60 | Normal first helper **S$300**; subsequent **S$450**; concessionary **S$60** only if you qualify |
| Food, transport, phone, toiletries | S$200–400+ | Household-dependent |
| Medical insurance + security bond setup | Front-loaded | MOM-required insurance and bond arrangements |
| Agency / placement fees | One-off | Highest for first-time overseas hires; lower for transfers |
| Rest-day compensation | Variable | If she works a rest day, pay **at least one extra day’s salary** |

Reddit and Singapore Expats Forum threads repeatedly understate levy + food. Treat **S$1,100–1,800+/month** as a realistic all-in conversation for a live-in helper before agency amortisation — then replace with your quotes.

## The expat levy trap most packages miss

Concessionary levy (**S$60/month**) is **not** automatic for every household with kids. MOM’s concession criteria centre on an eligible person in the household — for a child, that means a **Singapore citizen child below 16**. Elderly and disability pathways also have citizen/PR rules.

A typical EP + DP family with foreign children usually pays the **normal S$300** first-helper levy. That alone is **S$2,880/year** more than concession households. Model it before you treat “helper included” as cheap childcare.

<OfficialSource>
Read [MOM levy concession eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession) before assuming the S$60 rate.
</OfficialSource>

## Rest days (non-negotiable)

Your MDW is entitled to **one rest day per week**. You must agree which day. If she works that day, compensate with **at least one day’s salary** (on top of basic pay). At least **one rest day per month cannot be compensated away**.

Update rest-day arrangements in MOM’s FDW eService after you agree them. Enforcement risk sits with the employer.

<OfficialSource>
Follow [MOM rest days and well-being guidance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide/rest-days-and-well-being).
</OfficialSource>

## Agency hire vs transfer

| Path | Speed | Cost pattern | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First-time overseas hire via agency | Weeks to ~2 months (nationality-dependent) | Higher placement fees | First employers, specific language/infant skills |
| Transfer from another Singapore employer | Often faster | Lower agency fee; higher salary expectations | Experienced helpers who know local routines |
| Direct transfer (employer-to-employer) | Requires both sides’ MOM compliance | Fee-light but process-heavy | When you already know the helper |

Interview for deal-breakers early: pets, overnight newborn care, cooking style, English level, willingness to travel on family holidays. Do not outsource those questions to the agency brochure.

## Insurance floors (cannot pass cost to the helper)

Before she arrives you must buy **medical insurance** and **personal accident insurance**. You **cannot** recover those premiums from her salary.

| Product | Published minimum (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Medical insurance | Inpatient + day surgery; annual claim limit ≥ **S$60,000**; enhanced MI stages since **1 Jul 2023** (co-pay rules above S$15k) with further standardisation from **1 Jul 2025** |
| Personal accident | Sum assured ≥ **S$60,000**; sudden permanent disability / death; payout to helper or her beneficiaries |

Reddit “basic maid insurance” shopping often under-insures catastrophic bills — employers remain on the hook for shortfalls. Dental is frequently **excluded**; budget goodwill separately.

<OfficialSource>
[MOM — MDW insurance requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/insurance-requirements).
</OfficialSource>

## Settling-In Programme (first-time MDWs)

First-time helpers must attend the **1-day Settling-In Programme within 7 days of arrival** (excluding Sunday and public holidays). She **cannot start work** before SIP. Miss it → Work Permit not issued and she may be sent home. Employers pay the published SIP fee (~S$76–93 depending on provider). Register early; same-day-as-arrival training is not allowed.

Bring full IPA set, passport, education certificate copy, and ICA digital arrival card to the centre.

<OfficialSource>
[MOM — Settling-in Programme (SIP)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip).
</OfficialSource>

## Live-in MDW vs part-time cleaners

MOM has pointed households with lighter chore needs toward **part-time housekeeping** (e.g. Household Services Scheme / commercial cleaners) as an alternative to full MDW employment. Many Reddit threads now compare:

- **Live-in MDW** — childcare overlap, evening coverage, higher fixed monthly cost, employer compliance burden  
- **Part-time cleaners** — flexible hours, no levy/bond, no childcare by default, weaker when a toddler is sick at 06:30  

If both parents work full-time with infants, part-time cleaners rarely replace a helper; if you need deep cleans twice a week and school-age kids, they often do.

## Condo and HDB constraints

Confirm helper room / occupant norms **before** signing a lease that assumes a live-in. Some condos are practical; some layouts force creative sleeping arrangements that burn out everyone. See [condo vs HDB](/guides/condo-vs-hdb-expat) and [heartland living](/guides/heartland-living-for-expats).

## Employer obligations (checklist)

- Valid Work Permit sponsorship and required medical insurance  
- Timely levy payment (GIRO failure triggers penalties)  
- Adequate housing, food, and safe working conditions  
- Lawful salary practices and rest-day compliance  
- Notify MOM of address / household changes as required  

## Timeline realism for arriving families

1. Shortlist agency or transfer route before wheels-down if possible  
2. Align lease start with helper arrival — first weeks without care are brutal with infants  
3. Budget a buffer month for mismatch / rehire (common, not a moral failure)  
4. Keep preschool waitlists running in parallel — see [preschool and childcare](/guides/preschool-childcare-singapore)  

Hiring an MDW can unlock dual careers in Singapore. Treat it as regulated employment with a five-line cost model — not a soft lifestyle upgrade.
