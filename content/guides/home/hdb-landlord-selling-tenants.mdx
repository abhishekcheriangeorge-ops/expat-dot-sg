r/askSingapore’s angriest HDB-tenant threads start the same way: you signed a **2-year whole-flat TA three months ago**, then the agent WhatsApps that the owner is “selling and you may need to move.” Private-condo folklore about **sale with tenancy** does **not** transplant cleanly to HDB resale.

## The core mismatch (HDB vs private)

| Rail | What usually happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Private condo / landed** | Buyer may take the unit **with existing tenancy** if contracts allow; your TA can survive a change of landlord |
| **HDB resale** | Sellers must generally deliver **vacant possession** at [resale completion](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/selling-a-flat/process-for-selling-a-flat/resale-flat-completion) — buyers expect empty keys after the HDB appointment |
| **Your TA** | Still a private contract with the **seller-landlord**. Sale alone does not magic away rent, deposits, or early-exit clauses — but HDB process pressure often forces a commercial unwind |

Forum consensus ([owner wants to sell mid-lease](https://www.reddit.com/r/askSingapore/comments/1lidib5/renting_a_hdb_with_2yrs_contract_but_owner_wants/)): do **not** assume MOP rules on the *buyer* automatically cancel your lease tomorrow. Do assume the seller needs you gone by completion unless they negotiate something unusual (and HDB-compliant) with the buyer.

<OfficialSource>
HDB’s resale completion guidance: sellers must vacate before the completion appointment so buyers can take possession. Temporary extension-of-stay after completion is a **seller–buyer** arrangement for sellers/authorised occupiers — not a free pass for the seller’s former tenants ([extension of stay](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/selling-a-flat/process-for-selling-a-flat/resale-flat-application/request-for-temporary-extension-of-stay)).

## Read the TA before you panic-post

1. **Sale / landlord-break clause** — some TAs let the landlord terminate on sale with X months’ notice + compensation; many drafts omit this entirely  
2. **Quiet enjoyment + viewing access** — reasonable access for viewings is common; “agent with keys whenever” is not automatic  
3. **Diplomatic / tenant break** — usually **your** early-exit tool, not a sale shield  
4. **Agent commission reimbursement clauses** — often protect agencies, **not** your moving costs  

If the TA is silent on sale, you are in **commercial negotiation** territory: notice length, moving costs, temporary hotel, overlapping rent, and deposit return timing. Large sums → written advice, not Reddit bravado.

## Viewing access while the flat is listed

Sale viewings are the second flashpoint after “when do I leave?”

| Reasonable | Push back |
| --- | --- |
| Written notice (often 24–48h) for daytime viewings | Same-day surprise walk-ins |
| Limited slots / capped frequency in writing | Open-house every evening |
| You or a trusted adult present | Keys left with listing agent “for convenience” |
| Photos of common areas already agreed | Photographing bedrooms without consent |

Document every visit (date, duration, who entered). Harassment, lock changes, or utility cut-offs to force an exit are not “marketing” — escalate via written notice, CEA if an agent is involved, and police if safety is at risk. Deposit fights after a forced move still follow [security deposit / SCT](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) rules.

## Negotiation playbook that survives completion week

1. Ask for the **OTP / expected completion window** in writing (HDB timelines are often ~weeks after acceptance — verify live)  
2. Propose a **mutual termination letter**: move-out date, rent pro-rate, deposit return clock, who pays movers  
3. Price your ask: overlapping rent on the next flat, mover invoice, temporary stay, school-bus disruption — not a vibes-based “penalty”  
4. Keep paying rent until a written deal says otherwise — stopping rent escalates leverage *against* you  
5. Photograph inventory on exit day; do not “leave keys with agent and fly” if you can avoid it  

## What not to believe

- “Sale with tenancy always applies to HDB” — treat as private-market folklore until a lawyer/agent shows an HDB-compatible structure  
- “MOP means the buyer cannot make us leave” — MOP constrains *buyers’* occupation/rental plans; it does not freeze your landlord’s need for vacant possession at completion  
- “Agent fee clauses in the TA refund my moving costs” — usually they don’t  

Pair with [moving within Singapore](/guides/moving-within-singapore) for lift/MCST logistics on the next unit, and [HDB bedroom rental rules](/guides/hdb-bedroom-rental-rules-tenants) if you were in a room rental (approval + owner-occupation risks differ).

Family note: a landlord sale notice during orientation week is a dual-career nightmare — keep the tenancy paper trail ready before term-one fittings, not during them.
