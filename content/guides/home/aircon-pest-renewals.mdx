Humidity is not a vibe; it is a maintenance schedule. Neglect shows up as mould, blocked condensate drains, landlord disputes, and Reddit threads titled “am I getting scammed by aircon servicing?”

## What “basic servicing” should include

Forum consensus (and property-manager advice) for a normal clean:

- Filters cleaned
- Blower / fan coil properly washed (often removed and rinsed — not foam-sprayed for 90 seconds)
- Drain tray cleaned and drain pipe vacuumed
- Outdoor condenser checked when accessible

Ballpark community pricing for honest **general cleaning** often clusters around **S$25–35 per indoor unit** (minimum job sizes vary). **Chemical / deep washes** are occasional (think annually or when mould is visible), not every visit. Steam washes sit between basic and full chemical for some vendors.

| Red flag | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Every visit needs “gas top-up” | Refrigerant should not vanish without a leak — demand manifold gauge readings |
| “Chemical wash” that never opens the unit | You paid for theatre |
| Hard-sell annual chemical contracts on day one | Common upsell; optional for many homes |
| No invoice / cash-only with no company details | Deposit fights later |

Keep invoices. Many tenancy agreements assign **regular servicing to the tenant** and **major repairs / compressor failure to the landlord** — but wording varies. Photograph serial numbers and note unit counts at move-in.

## How often?

Heavy nightly use in humid months: every **3–4 months** is common. Lighter use in a clean flat: **6 months** can work. Dust near construction or highways shortens the interval. If the unit smells musty or drips indoors, service now — do not wait for the calendar.

## Pests (especially cockroaches)

Tropical pests are ordinary, not a moral failing. Confirm whether the condo has a contracted pest schedule and what you escalate via the managing agent vs a private technician. Dengue season and stagnant water on balconies are household hygiene, not just “estate problem.” Document infestations early; end-of-lease arguments get worse with silence.

r/askSingapore cockroach threads converge on the same renter tactics:

| Tactic | Why forums recommend it |
| --- | --- |
| Seal the **rubbish chute** edges (tape / chute cover) during estate fogging | Fogging drives roaches **up** shared chutes into kitchens |
| Bait stations over panic spray | Spray scatters; bait hits the nest |
| Photograph entry gaps (pipes, balcony doors, chute) at move-in | End-of-lease “you caused the infestation” fights |
| Escalate shared-chute infestations via managing agent | Unit boundaries do not stop tropical pests |

Mould on fan coils and bathroom silicone is a humidity ops problem — pair servicing cadence with a [dehumidifier strategy](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat) and the full [mould / landlord remediation playbook](/guides/mould-humidity-rental-tenants), not another chemical-wash upsell.

## Renewals

Start renewal talks **2–3 months** out. Bring comparable rents, your payment reliability, and repair goodwill (you kept aircons healthy). If you will leave, use the [diplomatic clause calendar](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) deliberately — not emotionally.
