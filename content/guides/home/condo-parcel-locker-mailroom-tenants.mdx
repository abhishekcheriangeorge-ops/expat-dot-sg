Lazada says **delivered**; the lobby tablet wants a **PIN you never received**; the guard points at a wall of unlabelled cartons. This guide is **condo parcel locker / mailroom ops** for tenants — not visitor parking, not move-in lift padding, and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parcel locker PIN / QR | MA app / SMS | Register your mobile on day one |
| Mailroom oversized hold | Guard / MA desk | Confirm max days and fees |
| Locker card / fob deposit | Booker (often tenant) | Photograph condition at issue and return |
| Failed delivery liability | Courier + estate rules | Keep tracking screenshots |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Does the estate use **lockers**, a **manned mailroom**, or both?  
2. Which app / phone number receives **PIN SMS**?  
3. What is the **oversized** fee and hold window?  
4. Will guards accept **COD** or perishables?  
5. Is there a **locker card deposit**, and who refunds it at exit?  

Put answers in email before your first grocery stack lands. Week-one apps: [apps that matter](/guides/apps-that-matter-singapore). Phishing SMS that pretends to be “parcel holds” still exists — [scams & Singpass phishing](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats).

## Ops playbook

- Register every adult occupant’s mobile with the MA  
- Screenshot locker PINs into the household chat  
- Collect within the hold window — abandoned cartons get cleared  
- For moves, book lift padding separately — do not block the mailroom corridor  

Catchments around [Orchard](/neighbourhoods/orchard), [Somerset](/neighbourhoods/somerset), and [Dhoby Ghaut](/neighbourhoods/dhoby-ghaut) are dense with older and newer condo stock — assume locker culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
