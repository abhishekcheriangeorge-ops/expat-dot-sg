Most newly arrived professionals default to condos. Many long-stay families later discover well-located HDB estates offer space and value — if the rules fit. For the lifestyle and budget playbook, see [heartland living for expats](/guides/heartland-living-for-expats).

## Condominium rentals

- Facilities (pool, gym, security) baked into maintenance culture
- Often closer to international school corridors (Bukit Timah, East Coast, etc.)
- Higher rent per square foot; agency norms familiar to expats
- Pet policy is **MCST + landlord** — not a national default; see [pets daily life](/guides/pets-daily-life-singapore)

## HDB rentals

HDB owners may rent out bedrooms or whole flats subject to HDB eligibility rules, minimum occupation periods, and nationality restrictions that change over time. Budget for heartland ops: Town Council bulky waste, chute fogging pest waves, and the classic upstairs shower → your ceiling drip — [HDB ceiling leaks for tenants](/guides/hdb-ceiling-leak-tenants).

Pets: HDB now allows limited cats + one approved small dog under current Animals Rules / Cat Management Framework — still subject to landlord consent and AVS licensing.

<OfficialSource>
Verify live eligibility on [HDB’s open-market renting eligibility page](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/renting-a-flat/renting-from-the-open-market/eligibility) before you fall in love with a listing. Pet limits: [HDB keeping pets](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/community/caring-for-your-neighbours/being-a-good-neighbour/tips-on-neighbourliness/keeping-pets).

## Lifestyle trade-offs

| Factor | Condo | HDB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space per dollar | Lower | Higher |
| Facilities | Strong | Usually limited |
| Neighbour density | Moderate | Higher |
| Expat peer density | Higher in core belts | Mixed |
| Leak / slab disputes | MCST / private contractor path | Owner joint repair + possible HDB GRA |
| Pets | By-laws lottery | Clearer national limits, landlord veto still wins |

Choose for commute and schools first; finishes second. If pets or humidity maintenance will dominate year one, treat those as hard filters — not week-four surprises.

If you are tempted to **buy** instead of rent, read [buying property as a foreigner (ABSD)](/guides/buying-property-foreigner-absd) before any option to purchase — rent-vs-buy math collapses under 60% ABSD for most nationalities.
