A pram parks across the hose cabinet; the MA posts a fine; someone argues “it’s only for five minutes.” This guide is **condo fire-hose / landing-hydrant clearance ops** for tenants — not [fire-alarm / smoke-detector drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills), not [waste / bulky staging](/guides/condo-waste-recycling-bulky-disposal-tenants), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing hydrant / hose reel | MCST common property | Keep face clear; no storage |
| Corridor shoe racks / plants | Occupying units | Pull inside the unit line |
| MA obstruction fines | By-law schedule | Photo + written reply |
| Fire-alarm drills / detectors | Separate estate product | See fire-alarm guide |
| Real fire response | SCDF | Call **995** — do not “manage” the reel yourself |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Where are the **hose reels / hydrants** on your floor?  
2. What does the estate fine for **corridor obstruction**?  
3. Are **shoe cabinets** outside the unit door allowed at all?  
4. How does the MA want obstruction reports filed?  
5. Who pays common-area fines under the **TA**?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Real fires still start at **995** — [emergencies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies).

## Ops playbook

- Keep a clear arc in front of every hose cabinet and hydrant face  
- Move parcels, prams, and plants inside the unit line the same day  
- Photograph and email the MA for repeat neighbour blockages  
- Do not “borrow” hose equipment for cleaning or parties  
- Pair with drill literacy — [fire-alarm drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-tenant-drills)  

Catchments around [Jalan Besar Fringe](/neighbourhoods/jalan-besar-fringe), [Farrer Park South](/neighbourhoods/farrer-park-south), and [Bendemeer West](/neighbourhoods/bendemeer-west) pack older and denser landings — assume clearance culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
