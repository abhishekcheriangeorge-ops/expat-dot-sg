Cardboard wedged in the chute; a mattress in the loading bay; the MA WhatsApp blaming “unit XX.” This guide is **condo waste / recycling / bulky-item disposal ops** for tenants — not [move-in lift padding](/guides/condo-move-in-move-out-lift-booking-tenants), not [CCTV footage requests](/guides/condo-cctv-ma-footage-requests-tenants), and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic refuse chute / bins | MCST / cleaner roster | Bag wet waste; never force oversized items |
| Recycling blue bins | Estate SOP + NEA Recycle Right | Rinse; flatten cardboard |
| Bulky / renovation debris | Booked collector / MA slot | Book before staging in common areas |
| E-waste / lighting | Designated drop-off | Keep out of chutes |
| Fines for dumping | By-law schedule | Pay/appeal with photos + booking proof |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Where is the **refuse room**, and what hours is it open?  
2. How do residents **book bulky** collection, and what is the fee?  
3. What belongs in **recycling** vs general waste in this estate?  
4. Where is the **e-waste / lighting** drop-off?  
5. Will move-out bulky clearance be on the tenant or landlord?  

Put answers in email before your first Lazada sofa arrives — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Furnishing churn: [furnishing a rental](/guides/furnishing-rental-flat).

## Ops playbook

- Flatten boxes; never force them down the chute  
- Rinse recyclables; contamination triggers estate crackdowns  
- Book bulky pickup before staging items in corridors  
- Keep e-waste out of wet waste — chute fires are the horror story  
- Photograph MA fine notices for deposit disputes — [deposit return](/guides/deposit-return-handover-disputes)  

Catchments around [Mattar](/neighbourhoods/mattar), [City Hall](/neighbourhoods/city-hall), and [Bras Basah](/neighbourhoods/bras-basah) mix older and denser stock — assume refuse culture varies by estate, not by MRT stop.
