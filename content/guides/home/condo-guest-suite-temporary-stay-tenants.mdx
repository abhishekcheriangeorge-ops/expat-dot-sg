Relatives land Friday night; the MA asks for overnight registration; someone assumes the “guest suite” is free like a hotel annex. This guide is **condo guest-suite / temporary-stay ops** for tenants — not [visitor parking / access cards](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants), not [access-card / fob replacement](/guides/condo-access-card-fob-replacement-tenants), and not [intercom / boom-gate / visitor QR](/guides/condo-intercom-boom-gate-visitor-qr-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overnight visitor cap | MCST by-laws | Register before the night limit |
| Estate guest suite | MA booking calendar | Book + deposit if offered |
| Visitor parking | Guest-car product | Separate from overnight stay |
| Access card / fob | Credential product | See fob-replacement guide |
| Short-stay platform listing | Usually prohibited | Do not list without written leave |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. What is the estate **overnight visitor** cap?  
2. Does the MA require **registration** after a set hour?  
3. Is there a bookable **guest suite**, and who may book it?  
4. Do guest stays need **owner authorisation**?  
5. How do stay rules interact with the **TA** sub-letting clause?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Register overnight guests before the by-law cut-off; keep the confirmation  
- Book estate guest suites as a separate product from visitor parking  
- Do not treat short-stay platforms as a workaround — by-laws and URA framing usually block it  
- Pair guest arrival with visitor QR / boom-gate literacy — [visitor QR](/guides/condo-intercom-boom-gate-visitor-qr-tenants)  
- Pair credential gaps with [access-card / fob replacement](/guides/condo-access-card-fob-replacement-tenants) only when cards are lost  

Catchments around [Mattar Fringe](/neighbourhoods/mattar-fringe), [Tai Seng North](/neighbourhoods/tai-seng-north), and [Ubi West](/neighbourhoods/ubi-west) mix mid-age and newer estates with different overnight caps — assume MA forms differ by condo, not by MRT stop. By-law orientation: [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).
