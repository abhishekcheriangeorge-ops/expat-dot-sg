Open Electricity Market threads on r/askSingapore split into two piles: people hunting a cheaper kWh, and people shocked when the promo cliff or exit fee arrives mid-lease. This guide is the **retailer switch playbook** — not a blackout response, and not a “my bill doubled” dispute after an estimated read.

## SP vs retailer (who does what)

| Role | What they own |
| --- | --- |
| **SP Group** | Network, metering, open/close account, many bill presentment journeys |
| **OEM retailer** | Your electricity **price plan**, promo terms, exit fees, retailer customer service |
| **Landlord** | Only if the account stays in their name — then *they* choose the plan |

## Before you click “switch”

1. Confirm **whose name** is on the live SP / utility account  
2. Pull the last **three months of kWh** (not just dollar totals)  
3. Open the official comparison tool — ignore Telegram “guaranteed save” agents  
4. Read **contract length, promo end date, exit fee, cool-off**  
5. Screenshot the acceptance page and email confirmation  

If the landlord holds the account, push for PDF bills and a written recharge formula instead of a tenant-side retailer hop you cannot unwind at handover.

## Switch timeline (typical)

| Step | Renter move |
| --- | --- |
| Compare + choose | Official OEM tool; match plan type to WFH / dehumidifier load |
| Apply with retailer | Account number / address / ID as they request |
| Cool-off window | Use it if you mis-read peak bands |
| Switch completes | Check first bill for unit rate + any one-off fees |
| Mid-lease exit | Read exit fee before you sign a new LOI elsewhere |

Pair move-in sequencing with [utilities & telecom setup](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup) so the account exists before you shop retailers. Fibre delays are a different rail — [NetLink TP guide](/guides/fibre-tp-netlink-renters).

## Failure modes expats hit

- **Promo cliff:** months 1–3 look cheap; month 4 matches or beats the old rate  
- **Peak / off-peak products:** all-day aircon destroys the spreadsheet  
- **Exit fee on early lease break:** stacks with landlord claims — see [early exit](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants)  
- **Dual-account chaos during a sale:** confirm the live account before paying anyone “to keep power on”  
- **Telegram switch agents:** use official channels; keep ticket IDs  

## When staying put wins

Stay on the current plan if you move in under six months, the landlord owns the account, or the exit fee exceeds a year of “savings.” Quiet-enjoyment fights over meter access are a landlord/agent problem — [landlord entry](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment) — not an OEM discount problem.
