The lift lobby notice says “renovation works — Unit ## — until [date].” Then the angle grinder starts at 9:05am under your desk. Adjacent **BTO / MRT sites** are an [NEA construction rail](/guides/construction-site-noise-renters). General condo neighbour behaviour sits on [MCST / CMC / CDRT](/guides/condo-noise-mcst-neighbours). **This guide is the unit-renovation notice slice** — what the paper on the wall actually buys you as a tenant.

## What the notice is for

MCST / managing agents circulate renovation notices so stacks know **who**, **when**, and **which contractor** is hacking. Forum folklore that “the notice means unlimited drilling” is wrong. Typical estate house rules (verify your by-laws and circulars):

| Item | Practical read |
| --- | --- |
| Approved hours | Often weekday daytime windows; Saturday light works only if your estate allows |
| Hacking / demolition | Often tighter than general fitting works — ask MA which days hacking is permitted |
| Sundays / PH | Usually no reno works |
| After published cut-off | Strongest complaint with timestamped video |
| Notice end date passed | Ask MA whether an extension exists before assuming a breach |

## Tenant playbook that moves the MA

1. Photograph the **lobby / stack notice** (unit, dates, contractor phone if listed)  
2. Film from inside your flat with **visible clock / phone timestamp** after hours or on banned days  
3. Email **MA + MCST council** (keep it factual: dates, times, hacking vs light fitting)  
4. Copy your **landlord / agent** — habitability leverage needs a paper trail ([quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment))  
5. Ask whether **protective works** (lift padding, corridor covers) were approved — dust and debris fights are inventory issues too ([fair wear](/guides/rental-inventory-fair-wear-tear))  
6. Do **not** stop rent as protest — that stacks a second breach ([early exit](/guides/early-lease-termination-tenants))  

## Dust, vibration, and water shut-offs

Reno next door is not only noise. Expect corridor dust, toilet / water interruptions for stack works, and vibration that rattles glass. Ask MA for the contractor’s method statement timeline. Photograph your unit before peak hacking if you fear crack / dust claims at handover.

## Smoke and smell adjacency

Some “reno” complaints are actually smoking on the scaffold / balcony during breaks. That is a neighbour-behaviour rail — see [secondhand smoke between neighbours](/guides/secondhand-smoke-neighbours-singapore) — not an NEA construction ticket.

## Lease realism

Approved daytime condo reno rarely voids a tenancy. Use the file to negotiate a short hotel stipend or rent abatement with the landlord if WFH is impossible, time renewals away from known hacking weeks, and decide early-exit on **contract terms**, not lobby folklore.

Central stacks in [River Valley](/neighbourhoods/river-valley) and lifestyle pockets like [Tiong Bahru](/neighbourhoods/tiong-bahru) see frequent unit flips; west / northwest family towns such as [Bukit Panjang](/neighbourhoods/bukit-panjang) and [Boon Lay](/neighbourhoods/boon-lay) still get renovation seasons when owners refresh before reletting. Walk the block on a **weekday morning** before you LOI.
