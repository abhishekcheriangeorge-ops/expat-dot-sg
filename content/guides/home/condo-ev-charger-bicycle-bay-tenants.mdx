You bought an EV because the listing said **EV-ready**; the MA app has a **18-month charger waitlist** and the bicycle rack is full of untagged frames. This guide is **condo EV charger + bicycle / PMD bay ops** for tenants — not visitor parking IU folklore, not renovation deposits, and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EV charger allocation | MCST / MA waitlist | Confirm type, fee, IU link before LOI |
| Charging usage fee | Resident who plugs in | Keep MA / app receipts |
| Bicycle / PMD bay | MA tagging / app | Register early; photo the bay |
| Visitor parking | Separate visitor rules | Do not mix with charger slots — [visitor parking](/guides/condo-visitor-parking-access-cards-tenants) |

## Pre-LOI questions that save Saturday fights

1. How long is the **EV charger waitlist**, and what connector types exist?  
2. Is charging billed per kWh, flat monthly, or “free until further notice”?  
3. Does the TA include a **season parking** lot, or only motorcycle / none?  
4. How many **bicycle / PMD** bays come with the unit, and are e-devices allowed indoors?  
5. Which app registers tags / IUs, and what is the fine for untagged bikes?  

Put answers in email — brochure “EV-ready” is not a charger ID. Ownership vs sharing math still applies: [car ownership vs sharing](/guides/car-ownership-vs-sharing-singapore). Active mobility rules for street riding: [cycling & active mobility](/guides/cycling-active-mobility-singapore).

## Ops playbook

- Register your IU / bike tag within the MA’s window  
- Screenshot charger booking rules into the household chat  
- Do not hog a charger overnight past estate limits — expect wheel clamps / MA tickets  
- At exit, return tags and photograph empty bays for deposit fights  

Catchments around [Redhill](/neighbourhoods/redhill), [Telok Blangah](/neighbourhoods/telok-blangah), and [HarbourFront](/neighbourhoods/harbourfront) mix older walk-ups and denser condo stock — assume MA EV and rack rules vary by estate, not by MRT stop.
