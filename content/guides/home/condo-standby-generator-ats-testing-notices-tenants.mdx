The MA posts a two-hour “genset / ATS load test”; Zoom dies; someone argues it is a fire drill. This guide is **condo standby generator / ATS testing notice ops** for tenants — not [fire-alarm / smoke-detector drills](/guides/condo-fire-alarm-smoke-detector-drills-tenants), not [facade / gondola access notices](/guides/condo-facade-gondola-access-notices-tenants), and not [water-tank / booster outages](/guides/condo-water-tank-booster-pump-outages-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standby generator / ATS test | MCST common property | Read notice; plan WFH around window |
| Brief essential-load transfer | Estate electrical programme | Expect flicker or short drop |
| Fire-alarm drill | Separate estate product | See fire-alarm guide |
| Facade / gondola works | Separate common-property product | See facade guide |
| Overrun / spoiled-goods claim | MA written path | Photo + same-day email |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. How often does the estate run **generator / ATS** tests?  
2. Does the notice promise a **live transfer** or load-bank only?  
3. Which hours are used for noisy tests?  
4. How do you report **overruns** or equipment trips?  
5. How do notices interact with **WFH / helper rest-day** calendars?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Quiet-enjoyment fights still sit in [landlord entry / quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment).

## Ops playbook

- Treat circulars as hard calendars — move deep-work blocks off the window  
- Save the PDF/photo of the notice before the test starts  
- Do not confuse genset roar with fire-alarm drills or gondola motors  
- Pair tank/booster surprises with [water-tank / booster outages](/guides/condo-water-tank-booster-pump-outages-tenants) only when the notice says so  
- Write the MA the same day if the window overruns  

Catchments around [Kembangan Fringe](/neighbourhoods/kembangan-fringe), [Bedok North West](/neighbourhoods/bedok-north-west), and [Guillemard East](/neighbourhoods/guillemard-east) mix mid-rise and high-rise stock with different genset calendars — assume MA forms differ by estate, not by MRT stop. By-law orientation: [MCST by-laws / AGM](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).
