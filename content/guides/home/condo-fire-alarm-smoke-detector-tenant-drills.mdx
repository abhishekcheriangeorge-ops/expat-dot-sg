The detector chirps at 2am. A neighbour's wok sets off the corridor panel. The MA WhatsApp group settles on blaming "unit XXX." This guide is about condo fire-alarm and smoke-detector ops and drills for tenants, which is a narrower thing than it sounds: not parcel lockers, not BBQ pit bookings, and not the full [MCST by-law and AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants), which lives in its own guide.

## Who owns what

| Item | Who usually owns it | What you do about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit smoke / heat detector | Landlord, plus you as the occupying tenant | Test it monthly; never disable it |
| Common corridor / stair alarm | MCST / MA | Treat every activation as real until someone clears it |
| Fire extinguisher / hose reel | Common property | Know where they are; they are not doorstops |
| Drill / assembly point | MA notice | Walk the route once, in week one |
| False-alarm recovery | By-laws and the fee schedule | Keep your cooking ventilation honest |

## Ask these before you sign

1. Where are the unit detectors, and who pays for battery changes?
2. What is the estate's rule for recovering false-alarm or SCDF call-out costs?
3. Where is the assembly point, and do the lifts lock down during an alarm?
4. Will MA staff enter the unit for detector faults, and with how much notice ([quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment))?
5. Are there recent drill dates or MA circulars you can read before you commit?

Capture the answers in writing before you LOI, for the same reason you pin down everything else at that stage — see [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). None of this replaces a real emergency, which still starts at **995** ([emergencies and embassies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies)).

## Living with it

Test the detectors after you move in, and photograph the serial numbers and placement into the inventory pack. That takes two minutes and settles any later argument about what was working on day one.

When one starts chirping, change the battery, or escalate to the MA or landlord if it's a hard-wired fault. What you must not do is **tape, bag, or remove a detector head to stop the noise**. Disabling a detector or covering the head is a serious fire-safety breach and it can bring MCST or landlord action down on you, which is a poor trade for one quiet night.

Ventilate heavy cooking properly, because oil fires and corridor smoke are the usual nuisance activations. When the panel does sound, treat it as real: stairs rather than lifts, get to the assembly point, wait for the MA or SCDF all-clear. If the false alarm came from your unit, write to the MA and the landlord the same day with the facts, while you still remember them accurately and before the version in the group chat hardens.

Catchments around [Novena](/neighbourhoods/novena), [Newton](/neighbourhoods/newton) and [Toa Payoh Central](/neighbourhoods/toa-payoh-central) mix older and newer condo stock with HDB-adjacent blocks, so assume detector culture and MA recovery rules vary by estate rather than by MRT stop. The building matters far more than the postcode.
