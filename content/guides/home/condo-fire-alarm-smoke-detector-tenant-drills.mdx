The detector chirps at 2am; a neighbour’s wok sets off the corridor panel; the MA WhatsApp blames “unit XXX.” This guide is **condo fire-alarm / smoke-detector ops and drills** for tenants — not parcel lockers, not BBQ booking, and not the full [MCST by-law / AGM orientation](/guides/condo-mcst-bylaws-agm-tenants).

## What usually sits where

| Item | Typical owner | Tenant move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit smoke / heat detector | Landlord + occupying tenant | Test monthly; never disable |
| Common corridor / stair alarm | MCST / MA | Treat activations as real until cleared |
| Fire extinguisher / hose reel | Common property | Know locations; do not use as doorstops |
| Drill / assembly point | MA notice | Walk it once in week one |
| False-alarm recovery | By-laws / fee schedule | Keep cooking ventilation honest |

## Pre-move / pre-LOI questions

1. Where are **unit detectors**, and who owns **battery** changes?  
2. What is the estate’s **false-alarm / SCDF call-out** recovery rule?  
3. Where is the **assembly point**, and do lifts lock during alarms?  
4. Will MA staff enter for **detector faults** with notice — [quiet enjoyment](/guides/landlord-entry-quiet-enjoyment)?  
5. Are there **recent drill** dates or MA circulars to read?  

Capture answers before you LOI — same discipline as [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits). Real emergencies still start at **995** — [emergencies & embassies](/guides/emergencies-995-999-embassies).

## Ops playbook

- Test detectors after move-in; photograph serial / placement into the inventory pack  
- Never tape, bag, or remove heads to stop chirps — fix batteries or escalate  
- Ventilate heavy cooking; oil fires and corridor smoke are the usual nuisance activations  
- When the panel sounds, treat it as real: exit via stairs, assemble, wait for MA / SCDF all-clear  
- After a unit-caused false alarm, write the MA and landlord the same day with facts  

Catchments around [Novena](/neighbourhoods/novena), [Newton](/neighbourhoods/newton), and [Toa Payoh Central](/neighbourhoods/toa-payoh-central) mix older and newer condo / HDB-adjacent stock — assume detector culture and MA recovery rules vary by estate, not by MRT stop.
