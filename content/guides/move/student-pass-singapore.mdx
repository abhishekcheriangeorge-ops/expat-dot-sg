**Short answer:** Full-time international students usually need an ICA Student's Pass, and the school starts it for you through SOLAR or ICA e-services. An offer letter is not status. DP and LTVP holders may be exempt while that pass stays valid, work is only allowed inside MOM's student exemption limits, and **graduating does not turn into an EP**.

If you're on an approved full-time course, you'll generally need a Student's Pass from ICA. The school usually kicks the process off, you complete the online forms (normally via SOLAR or ICA e-services, whichever your institution tells you to use), and you finish biometrics and formalities once you're physically here.

## Who doesn't need one

ICA lists exemptions for people who already hold a valid Dependant's Pass, Long-Term Visit Pass, or Immigration Exemption Order, provided the study is full-time and non-religious and sits inside that pass's validity.

The part worth thinking about in advance is what happens if the underlying pass is cancelled mid-course. You then generally have to apply for a Student's Pass under whatever rules prevail at that point, in the middle of a semester, with no warning. Short-term visit pass holders do have narrow short-course exemptions, but those are not a substitute for a full degree programme.

## The address chicken-and-egg

ICA guidance tells applicants to apply well before term starts, and institutions often cite around 2 months. Meanwhile the form wants a Singapore residential address, and the housing portals only list rooms that are "available immediately" and that you can't sign for from overseas anyway. Everyone hits this. It isn't a sign you've skipped a step.

The pattern university OneStop guides and forum replies converge on:

1. Apply on time, using a temporary, hotel, or short-stay address if the portal allows blanks or placeholders. Follow your own school's written SOLAR notes here, not generic advice off TikTok.
2. Update the address once you've signed a real lease.
3. Don't hold the whole Student's Pass hostage to the possibility that the perfect Master Room appears 10 days before orientation.

Fees and timelines move around, and plenty of students report roughly 2+ weeks for an outcome, sometimes longer. Leave buffer in front of the compulsory medicals, the vaccination paperwork, and the on-campus enrolment windows, because those have hard dates and your application doesn't.

## IPA, then Visit Pass, then card

A successful application produces an In-Principle Approval. You have to be in Singapore on a valid immigration pass to complete formalities, and if you're a visa national you'll often find the visa bundled into the IPA letter itself. Formalities run by appointment, either at an ICA Services Centre or at your school's offsite biometrics session.

Bring every single thing the IPA lists. A missing medical report is the standard reason a card sits unissued while term has already started.

## Working while you study

A Student's Pass is **not** a free work visa. MOM's [work pass exemption for foreign students](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-pass-exemption-for-foreign-students) covers only listed institutions and specific conditions: vacation work, capped hours during term, approved attachments. Exchange students are generally shut out altogether. Once studies end and the pass expires, you need a sponsored work pass, because graduation doesn't mint an EP for anyone. For the hour caps and vacation depth, see [Student's Pass work hours and vacation exemption](/guides/student-pass-work-hours-vacation-exemption-singapore).

Short overseas internships and working holidays run on entirely different MOM rails, the Work Holiday Pass and the Training Employment Pass, rather than on a tourist stamp. We compare the two in [WHP vs TEP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass).

## The Tuition Grant is a separate contract

This one tends to surprise people in year three rather than year one. If you take MOE's Tuition Grant as an international student or PR, you generally owe a 3-year Singapore employment bond after graduation, with liquidated damages if you can't serve it. None of that is ICA Student's Pass fine print. It's a separate MOE agreement, complete with sureties. Read [MOE Tuition Grant bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond) before you sign in Year 1 to save on fees, not three years later when the bond is the reason you can't take the job you want.

## Families and school choice

Younger dependants already on a parent's DP may not need a Student's Pass for school at all, so confirm which path applies to your child before you pay international-school deposits. Check what the contract says about fee refunds if ICA refuses the pass, too. That belongs in writing rather than in hope. Foreign-born children under 12 still need diphtheria and measles documentation for many Student's Pass paths, which is covered in [childhood vaccinations](/guides/childhood-vaccination-foreigners).
