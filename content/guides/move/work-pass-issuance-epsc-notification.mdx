**Short answer:** An IPA **is not permission to work**. Your employer has to issue the pass while you're physically in Singapore, and only then does MOM email the Notification Letter. That letter is the thing that lets you start work and travel while the plastic card prints; for an EP it's valid for 1 month. If your letter says biometrics are required, get EPSC fingerprints and photo done within **2 weeks**, set up SGWorkPass, and expect card delivery about 5 working days later.

Three different PDFs land in your inbox during your first fortnight, and week-one Slack threads treat them as interchangeable. They're not. IPA, Notification Letter and card are a sequence, and each one does exactly one job.

## What each document actually proves

| Document | What it proves | What it doesn't prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPA | You're pre-approved to enter, and you get 6 months to have the pass issued. It may also require a medical. | That you can start work, or that a card exists anywhere |
| Notification Letter | The pass is issued. You can work and travel for as long as the letter is valid, while the card is being printed. | That your biometrics are done. Only the letter tells you that. |
| Physical or digital work pass | Your ongoing status, for everyday checks and for the bank and telco counters that ask | That any of the steps above were optional |

<Callout title="Do not fly the family on ‘application submitted’">
MOM’s apply page is explicit: bring candidates only when the pass is **approved**. Visit-pass extensions are not a safety net for premature arrivals. See [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats) for the landing declaration, then push HR on **issuance**, not more IPA screenshots.
</Callout>

## What happens, in order, once you land

1. Do the medical first if your IPA asks for one. Use the form attached to your IPA or the one HR sends you, not a Work Permit PDF you found in a search result, and keep the results somewhere you can upload them at short notice. We walk through the detail in [EP medical for issuance](/guides/ep-medical-examination-issuance).
2. Your employer issues the pass, and you have to be in Singapore on the day they do it. They or their EA use EP eService, pay S$225 per EP plus S$30 MJV where it applies, and upload your arrival stamp page, the medical or declaration forms, and a residential address that meets the housing rules.
3. Read the Notification Letter the day it arrives, not the week after. You're looking for three things: when it expires, whether EPSC registration is required, and where the card is going plus who is allowed to sign for it. If the only EPSC slots you can get fall outside the one-month window, ask HR to request an extension of the letter rather than letting it quietly lapse.
4. Book EPSC if the letter tells you to, within 2 weeks of issuance for an EP. Bring your original passport, the appointment letter, the Notification Letter, and a phone with a +65 number and both Singpass and SGWorkPass already installed.
5. Once you've registered Singpass at EPSC, or once HR confirms you can skip the centre, open SGWorkPass with Singpass and check that the status and expiry match what you've been told.
6. The card should arrive about 5 working days after registration and document verification. If the courier fails twice, you collect it at EPSC after 3 working days and you don't need an appointment.

The rest of your life admin hangs off this. Issuance is what makes your FIN recognisable to the people you're trying to open accounts with, so the [SIM to Singpass to bank map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank) only really unlocks once the pass is issued rather than merely approved.

## Reading your own letter

| If the Notification Letter says… | What to do | The clock (verify live on MOM) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fingerprints and photo are required | Book EPSC now. Don't wait for the plastic card to make it feel official. | Within 2 weeks of issuance, for an EP |
| Registration isn't required | Set up SGWorkPass once HR confirms, then wait for delivery after the document check | Card about 5 working days after verification |
| A validity date that falls before your EPSC slot | Ask your employer to request an extension of the Notification Letter | Before the letter expires |
| A delivery address that's wrong | Fix the address and the authorised recipients with HR in EP eService | Before the courier SMS goes out |

S Pass and Work Permit holders run a parallel path with MOM, often through Hall C and sometimes on a 1-week registration clock rather than two. Read your own letter and the apply page for your own pass type. Copying an EP friend's timeline is how people end up missing a deadline that was never theirs in the first place.

## What to bring, and what to push HR on

- Your passport and the IPA PDF on your phone, plus a printout, because some counters still prefer paper
- A Singapore residential address that meets the housing rules. Serviced apartment letters usually work, illegal short-lets usually don't ([first housing bridge](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment))
- A local mobile number that's already live. Prepaid is fine.
- Names and NRIC or FIN for up to 3 authorised recipients who can sign for the card
- Confirmation that the S$225 issuance fee actually cleared, and that the Notification Letter landed in your inbox and not only in HR's

If you're stuck further upstream, whether that's an appeal or an IPA medical that has stalled, that belongs on [EP rejection, appeal, and IPA issuance](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance). Losing the plastic after you've already held a card is a different problem with its own fee table: [replace work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card).
