**Short answer:** An **IPA is not permission to work**. Your employer must **issue the pass** while you are in Singapore. Then MOM emails a **Notification Letter** (EP: valid **1 month**) that lets you **start work and travel** while the plastic card prints. If the letter requires it, finish **EPSC** fingerprints/photo within **2 weeks**, set up **SGWorkPass**, and expect card delivery in about **5 working days**.

Week-one Slack threads still confuse three different PDFs: IPA, Notification Letter, and the card. Treat them as a sequence, not synonyms.

## IPA vs issued pass vs card

| Document | What it proves | What it does **not** prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IPA** | Pre-approved entry + **6 months** to get the pass issued; may require a medical | That you can start work or that the card exists |
| **Notification Letter** | Pass is **issued**; you may work/travel for the letter’s validity while waiting for the card | That biometrics are done (check the letter) |
| **Physical / digital work pass** | Ongoing status for everyday checks and many bank/telco counters | That issuance was optional — you still needed the steps above |

<Callout title="Do not fly the family on ‘application submitted’">
MOM’s apply page is explicit: bring candidates only when the pass is **approved**. Visit-pass extensions are not a safety net for premature arrivals. See [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats) for the landing declaration, then push HR on **issuance**, not more IPA screenshots.
</Callout>

## Numbered sequence after you land

1. **Medical (if IPA requires it)** — Complete the Singapore exam on the **IPA/HR form**, not a random Work Permit PDF; keep results ready for issuance uploads. Deep guide: [EP medical for issuance](/guides/ep-medical-examination-issuance).  
2. **Employer issues the pass** — Candidate must be **in Singapore**. Employer/EA uses EP eService, pays fees (**S$225** per EP + **S$30** MJV if applicable), and uploads arrival stamp page, medical/declaration forms, and a **housing-compliant** residential address.  
3. **Read the Notification Letter the same day** — Note validity end date, whether EPSC registration is required, and the card delivery address / authorised recipients. Ask HR to **extend** the letter if EPSC slots blow past the one-month window.  
4. **Book EPSC if required** — Within **2 weeks** of issuance for EP. Bring original passport, appointment letter, Notification Letter, and a phone with a **+65** number plus Singpass + SGWorkPass installed.  
5. **Digital pass** — After Singpass registration at EPSC (or when HR says you can skip the centre), open **SGWorkPass** with Singpass and confirm status/expiry.  
6. **Collect or receive the card** — Delivery target **5 working days** after registration/verification. Failed delivery twice → collect at EPSC after **3 working days**, no appointment.

Bridge the rest of life admin with the [SIM → Singpass → bank map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank) — issuance unlocks FIN recognition for many of those steps.

## Decision table: if your letter says…

| If the Notification Letter says… | Do this | Clock (verify live on MOM) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration of fingerprints/photo **required** | Book EPSC; do not wait for the plastic card to “feel official” | Within **2 weeks** of issuance (EP) |
| Registration **not** required | Set up SGWorkPass when HR confirms; wait for delivery after doc check | Card ~**5 working days** after verification |
| Validity ending before your EPSC slot | Ask employer to **request an extension** of the Notification Letter | Before the letter expires |
| Wrong delivery address | Fix authorised recipients / address with HR in EP eService | Before the courier SMS |

S Pass / Work Permit holders follow a parallel MOM path (often **Hall C**, sometimes a **1-week** registration clock). Read **your** letter and the pass-type apply page — do not copy an EP friend’s timeline blindly.

## What to bring / ask HR (candidate checklist)

- Passport + IPA PDF on phone **and** printed if counters prefer paper  
- Singapore **residential** address that meets housing rules — serviced apartment letters often work; illegal short-lets often do not ([first housing bridge](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment))  
- Local mobile number already live (prepaid is fine)  
- Names/NRIC or FIN of up to **3 authorised recipients** for card delivery  
- Confirmation the **S$225** issuance fee cleared and the Notification Letter hit **your** inbox, not only HR’s  

Appeal / IPA medical stalls belong on [EP rejection, appeal, and IPA issuance](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance). Lost plastic **after** you already held a card: [replace work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card).
