Tech.Pass is EDB’s **personalised tech-talent** visa: apply without a Singapore employer, then operate across employee, founder, investor, consultant, and mentor roles. Reddit threads comparing it with PEP often miss two facts — the **company-scale experience test**, and that **new and renewal applications close from 28 January 2027** as MOM’s ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track takes over.

## Eligibility (verify live on EDB)

You must meet **both**:

1. Last-drawn fixed monthly salary of at least **S$22,500** (or foreign-currency equivalent) in the last year — other income / non-cash components can be assessed case-by-case  
2. At least **5 cumulative years** (within the past **10 years**) in a **leading role** at either:
   - a tech company with valuation / market cap ≥ **US$500m** **or** ≥ **US$30m** funding raised, **or**
   - a tech VC with ≥ **US$500m** AUM  

No Singapore job offer is required to apply. Existing EP / PEP / EntrePass holders who meet the criteria may convert if they also clear their current pass rules.

## What it is for (and not for)

| Use case | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Job-hunt / multi-company consulting while based in SG | Strong — pass is not single-employer |
| Founding / investing / mentoring in the tech ecosystem | Designed for this |
| Ordinary mid-level engineer without leading-role + large-company proof | Weak — stay on [EP + COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) |
| General high earner outside tech company tests | Look at [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) or [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) |

## Renewal reality (while applications still open)

EDB’s published renewal paths include (verify live):

- ≥ **S$270,000** assessable income on the latest IRAS Notice of Assessment, **or**
- ≥ **S$100,000** annual business spending **and** hire thresholds (local PME / LQS counts), **or**
- Singapore-incorporated digital/tech core product with > **US$10m** cumulative investment funding in 36 months **and** programme-recognised investor participation  

Founders generally need meaningful shareholding (commonly **≥ 30%** on ACRA profiles in programme materials).

## The 2027 sunset — plan the handoff

From **28 January 2027**, EDB stops accepting **new and renewal** Tech.Pass applications. Existing holders can ride the pass to expiry. MOM’s March 2026 factsheet introduces **ONE Pass (AI and Tech)** from January 2027 as the replacement track — including a path to combine fixed cash salary (**≥ S$22.5k**) with vested non-cash components toward a **S$30k**/month composite, plus company-scale and experience tests. Details will continue to land on MOM’s live pages — treat press-release annexes as directional until the eligibility page updates.
