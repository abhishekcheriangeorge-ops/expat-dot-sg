Tech.Pass is EDB's personalised visa for tech talent, and the thing that makes it unusual is that you apply without a Singapore employer at all. Once it's issued you can operate as an employee, a founder, an investor, a consultant and a mentor, in whatever mix your year happens to take. The Reddit threads comparing it with PEP tend to miss two things. The first is the company-scale experience test, which is the real gate. The second is that **EDB stops accepting new and renewal applications from 28 January 2027**, as MOM's ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track takes over.

## Eligibility (verify live on EDB)

You have to clear both halves of this, not one.

1. A last-drawn fixed monthly salary of at least S$22,500, or the foreign-currency equivalent, within the last year. Other income and non-cash components can be assessed case by case.
2. At least 5 cumulative years, within the past 10 years, in a leading role at either a tech company with valuation or market cap of ≥ US$500m or ≥ US$30m in funding raised, or a tech VC with ≥ US$500m AUM.

No Singapore job offer is needed to apply, which is the genuinely liberating part. If you already hold an EP, PEP or EntrePass and meet the criteria, you may be able to convert, provided you also clear the rules attached to the pass you're leaving.

## What it's for, and what it isn't for

| Use case | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Job-hunting or consulting across several companies while based in SG | Strong. The pass isn't tied to one employer |
| Founding, investing or mentoring in the tech ecosystem | Exactly what it was designed for |
| Ordinary mid-level engineer without leading-role and large-company proof | Weak. Stay on [EP and COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) |
| High earner outside the tech-company tests | Look at [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) or [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) |

The salary number is the part everyone quotes, and it's the part that misleads. Clearing S$22,500 gets you halfway. The company-scale and leading-role evidence is what actually decides it, and no amount of salary substitutes for it.

## Renewal, while applications are still open

EDB's published renewal paths (verify live) come down to three. You can show ≥ S$270,000 assessable income on your latest IRAS Notice of Assessment. Or ≥ S$100,000 of annual business spending together with the hire thresholds, where local PME and LQS counts matter. Or a Singapore-incorporated digital or tech core product with more than US$10m of cumulative investment funding in 36 months, plus participation from a programme-recognised investor.

Founders should note that meaningful shareholding is generally expected here, commonly ≥ 30% on the ACRA profile in the programme materials. That's the sort of detail that's easy to leave until renewal year and painful to fix then.

## The 2027 sunset, and planning the handoff

From 28 January 2027, EDB stops accepting new and renewal Tech.Pass applications. If you already hold one, you can ride it to expiry — nobody is cutting it short. But there is no automatic conversion switch waiting for you at the end, and no courtesy letter telling you it's time.

MOM's March 2026 factsheet sets out ONE Pass (AI and Tech) from January 2027 as the replacement direction. It includes a path that combines fixed cash salary of ≥ S$22.5k with vested non-cash components toward a S$30k per month composite, alongside company-scale and experience tests. More detail will keep landing on MOM's live pages, so treat the press-release annexes as directional until the eligibility page itself catches up. **Start your next pass while the Tech.Pass is still valid**, because a lapsed pass turns a planning exercise into an emergency.

## The alternatives people mix this up with

| Option | When it is the better tool |
| --- | --- |
| [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) | Broader top-talent and outstanding-achievements route; the post-2027 AI and Tech track |
| [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) | High-earner flexibility without the tech-company leading-role proof, though it's generally one-shot |
| [5-year tech EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/experienced-tech-professionals-with-skills-in-shortage) | Employer-sponsored shortage occupations, with COMPASS diversity points and a higher salary band |
| [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) | Venture-backed or innovative founder path when the Tech.Pass company tests don't fit you |
