The S Pass sits between Work Permit and Employment Pass: mid-skilled roles with a regulated salary floor, **employer quota**, and a **monthly levy**. Day-to-day life can look like an EP. The economics for the company — and often the honesty of the offer narrative — do not.

Forum heat (r/askSingapore) clusters on one trap: HR saying they “have no EP quota” and must put you on S Pass. **Employment Pass has no foreign-worker quota.** S Pass does. If someone cites “EP quota,” ask whether they mean COMPASS risk, salary budget, or a mis-stated S Pass Dependency Ratio Ceiling.

## S Pass vs EP (employee lens)

| Topic | S Pass | Employment Pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it is for | Mid-skilled / associate professional & technician band | Professionals / managers / executives |
| Employer quota (DRC) | Yes — sector caps | No |
| Monthly levy | Yes (harmonised rate — verify live MOM table) | No |
| COMPASS | No | Yes (unless exempt) |
| Family (DP) | Possible from **S$6,000** fixed monthly salary | Same S$6,000 DP threshold for eligible EP holders |
| Typical Reddit flag | “Company claims no EP quota” | Offer below updated qualifying salary |

## Salary and levy (2025 step)

MOM’s COS 2025 third-step increase applies to **new S Pass applications from 1 September 2025** (renewals for passes expiring from 1 September 2026). Orientation figures:

- **All sectors except financial services:** from about **S$3,300** at the youngest band, rising with age (mid-40s band toward ~S$4,800)
- **Financial services:** higher floor (from about **S$3,800**, rising with age)
- **Levy:** Tier 1 harmonised toward **S$650/month** from 1 Sep 2025 (verify the current MOM levy page)

Further steps are already published for later years on the eligibility page. Treat age banding as real — a 40-something candidate needs a higher fixed monthly salary than a 23-year-old in the same role title.

## Quota: why the employer cares

S Pass headcount is capped by sector Dependency Ratio Ceiling (services often the tightest among common office sectors). Quota is computed from local CPF-declared headcount. Late CPF filings can shrink apparent quota and push workers onto higher levy tiers. That is an **employer** problem that still lands on you as “we can only do S Pass” or “renewal is uncertain.”

Ask in writing:

1. Is this role budgeted as S Pass or EP?
2. Does the company currently have S Pass quota headroom?
3. What happens at renewal if quota or salary bands move?

## Family packaging

S Pass holders with **fixed monthly salary ≥ S$6,000** can generally sponsor a legally married spouse and unmarried children under 21 for Dependant’s Passes — same headline DP salary gate as EP. Do **not** assume every LTVP / LOC / trailing-partner story from EP forums transfers automatically; verify the live DP and LTVP pages before relocating a household.

<OfficialSource>
[Dependant’s Pass eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility) is salary- and relationship-gated — not “S Pass equals no family.”
</OfficialSource>

## Practical advice before you accept

- If pay clears EP qualifying salary and the role is professional-grade, push back on “no EP quota” with MOM’s own EP key facts
- Keep certified diplomas and employment history; S Pass document scrutiny is real
- Conversion to EP later is a **new application**, not an automatic upgrade
