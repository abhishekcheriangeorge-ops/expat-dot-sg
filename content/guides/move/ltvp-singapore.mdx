The Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) covers family relationships that do not sit neatly on an Employment Pass holder’s Dependant’s Pass — **parents of high-earning EP/S Pass holders**, foreign spouses of Singapore citizens, some parents/grandparents of Student’s Pass children, and other listed categories. r/askSingapore threads on “bring my mother on LTVP” and “S$12k salary?” are high-intent because the thresholds are easy to misread.

## Common expat / cross-cultural scenarios

| Scenario | Typical pass logic |
| --- | --- |
| EP holder’s legally married spouse / kids | Usually **Dependant’s Pass**, not LTVP |
| EP/S Pass holder’s **parents** | **MOM LTVP** if fixed monthly salary ≥ **S$12,000** (individual, not household) |
| EP/S Pass common-law spouse / step-child / unmarried handicapped child 21+ | MOM LTVP if main pass salary ≥ **S$6,000** |
| Singapore citizen marrying a foreigner | **ICA LTVP** sponsored by the SC (see PMLA) |
| Unmarried partner of an EP holder | Often **no** DP; MOM **common-law spouse LTVP** possible if salary ≥ **S$6,000** and documents clear (below) |
| Parent accompanying a child on Student’s Pass | ICA LTVP possible with strict sponsor rules (often **one** parent/grandparent applicant) |

Treat chat anecdotes as unverified. MOM and ICA categories win.

## Common-law spouse paperwork (MOM)

Reddit’s “we share a flat and bank account — is that enough?” threads miss MOM’s evidence list. For a common-law spouse LTVP, [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/documents-required) ask for **any one of**:

1. Copy of a **common-law marriage certificate**, **or**  
2. Affidavit by both partners declaring a common-law relationship **recognised under the laws of their home country/region**, **notarised in that home country/region**, **or**  
3. A letter from the home country/region’s **embassy** acknowledging the relationship under that country’s law  

Also upload passport biodata pages. Non-English docs need **original + English translation as one file**. Price embassy translators in Singapore before paying overseas character-based quotes — see [notary / apostille](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore).

LTVP alone is still **not work rights**. Remote work for an overseas employer has a separate MOM FAQ path covered in [side income rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass); local Singapore clients still need a proper pass / LOC.

## EP/S Pass parents: the S$12k rule

[MOM LTVP eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/eligibility) is explicit:

- Hold an **Employment Pass or S Pass**  
- Earn a minimum fixed monthly salary of **S$6,000** for most LTVP family categories — based on **your** salary, not combined household income  
- Sponsored by an established Singapore-registered company (usually your employer)  
- **Parents** — only if you earn a fixed monthly salary of at least **S$12,000**  

Reddit’s “combined income under 10k rejected” stories usually confuse **ICA SC/PR parent sponsorship** with **MOM work-pass parent LTVP**, or assume spouse salary counts toward the S$12k floor. It does not.

Employer (or EA) submits for EP/S Pass dependants; published fees and timelines live on [MOM apply](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/long-term-visit-pass/apply-for-a-pass) (verify live — application and issuance fees apply per pass). Bring parents only after IPA/approval sequencing; MOM will not extend a short visit pass to bridge gaps.

## Healthcare and insurance shock

LTVP parents are **not** on citizen/PR subsidy tracks. Forum ICU bill horror stories are the cautionary tale: price private medical insurance early, expect pre-existing exclusions, and budget helper care if needed — see [expat health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat). PR for elderly parents with no Singapore contribution history is generally unrealistic.

## SC spouse path: PMLA → LTVP → (optional) PLOC

ICA’s Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (**PMLA**) is free and, when completed before marriage, shortens LTVP processing for SC foreign spouses to about **6 weeks** versus up to **6 months** without it. The SC sponsors via Singpass on ICA e-Service.

Fees ICA publishes (verify live): **S$45** application, **S$60** issuance, plus **S$30** Multiple Journey Visa if applicable. Renew at least **3 months** before expiry — remaining validity does not roll forward.

<OfficialSource>
Start from [ICA’s LTVP pages](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP) for eligibility tables, PMLA, and fees.
</OfficialSource>

## Work rights: PLOC is not automatic

An LTVP alone does **not** grant employment. Eligible spouses (and unmarried children under 21) of **Singapore citizens or PRs** may obtain a **Pre-approved Letter of Consent (PLOC)** from MOM — often applied together with the LTVP. PLOC lets eligible holders work without finding a job first; it is tied to LTVP validity.

Parents on LTVP (MOM or ICA) generally **cannot** assume PLOC. EP-family trailing partners on DP have a different (post-2021) work story — see [trailing partner career](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

<OfficialSource>
Confirm [MOM PLOC eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/pre-approved-letter-of-consent/eligibility) before promising a dual-income budget.
</OfficialSource>

## What LTVP does not solve

- PR is discretionary — not an LTVP side effect  
- School fees, healthcare subsidies, and HDB eligibility still follow citizenship/PR/pass rules  
- Switching from EP/S Pass to LTVP after marrying an SC/PR needs HR + ICA sequencing so you do not create a work gap
