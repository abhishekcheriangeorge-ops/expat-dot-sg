A Dependant’s Pass (DP) lets eligible spouses and unmarried children under 21 live in Singapore on the back of a qualifying main pass — commonly an Employment Pass or S Pass (subject to MOM’s family-pass salary rules).

## Typical eligibility pattern

The main pass holder usually needs to meet MOM’s salary and pass-type criteria for dependants. Exact thresholds change; verify against MOM before you sign school contracts overseas.

<OfficialSource>
Start from [MOM’s work passes overview](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits) and the family section of the [Employment Pass pages](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass). Thresholds and forms change.
</OfficialSource>

## Work for DP holders (read this carefully)

A DP is **not** automatically a work pass.

From **1 May 2021**, DP holders who want to work for an employer generally need their **own** EP, S Pass, or Work Permit. The old pattern of “get an LOC and take any job” no longer applies for ordinary employment.

A **Letter of Consent** remains relevant mainly for DP holders who are **eligible business owners** (ACRA-registered sole proprietor, partner, or director with ≥30% shareholding / equivalent). First-time LOCs are time-boxed (often up to one year or DP expiry, whichever is shorter). **Renewals** require employing at least one Singaporean/PR who earns the prevailing **Local Qualifying Salary** and has received **CPF contributions for three consecutive months** before you submit — that is the Reddit failure mode when a solo consultancy cannot justify a local hire.

If LOC renewal fails and the spouse still needs Singapore clients, the realistic forks are: pause local invoicing, hire to the published standard, or migrate onto an **employer-sponsored / self-sponsored EP** via a properly capitalised Pte Ltd ([incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore)) — not “keep trading on an expired LOC.” Overseas-only remote work rules: [side income / freelance](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

**ONE Pass exception peers keep citing:** spouses of **ONE Pass** holders can work for a Singapore employer with an LOC without needing their own EP first — that is a different MOM product from ordinary EP DP employment. See [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore). Do not paste that advice onto a normal EP household.

Deep dive and job-search realism: [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

## When the main EP is cancelled

DP status is **tied to the principal**. When the main EP is cancelled or expires, dependants generally lose the DP and need a lawful stay bridge — typically the employer-requested **STVP** path on cancel (up to **90 days** if HR asks; default asks are often shorter). STVP is **not** work authorisation and is usually treated as single-entry in forum experience — leaving Singapore can end the bridge.

Ask HR **in writing**, before cancel day, to request the longest STVP for the **whole household**. Full between-jobs playbook (IR21, graduate LTVP adjacency): [between jobs on EP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). Principal cancel rules: [MOM — Cancel an Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass).

## Schools and housing

International school waitlists and condo viewings often run in parallel with IPA timing. Use provisional holds carefully; understand cancellation penalties if the main pass is delayed. See [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).

- Marriage and birth certificates (official translations if required)  
- Passports with adequate validity

4. School enrolment fees  

## Babies born in Singapore

A newborn does **not** automatically inherit the parent’s pass. Register the birth via LifeSG, secure a passport, and have the employer lodge a DP within MOM’s published windows while the ICA Special Pass covers the baby’s stay. Full clock: [newborn pass & birth registration](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore).

Do not assume the trailing partner’s income in month one. Build the household budget as if it is a single-income posting until a second pass exists.
