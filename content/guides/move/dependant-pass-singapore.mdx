**Short answer:** A Dependant's Pass lets eligible spouses and unmarried children under 21 live here on the back of a qualifying main pass. It is **not** a work pass. An ordinary job needs its own EP, S Pass, or Work Permit, and the Letter of Consent now mainly covers eligible business owners. If the main EP goes, the DPs usually go with it, so plan an STVP bridge for the whole household.

A Dependant's Pass (DP) lets your spouse and unmarried children under 21 live in Singapore on the back of your main pass, usually an Employment Pass or S Pass. MOM applies its own family-pass salary rules on top, so the main pass existing is not by itself enough.

## Who qualifies

The main pass holder has to clear MOM's salary and pass-type bar for dependants. Those thresholds move, and they have moved more than once in recent years, so check the current number before you sign anything overseas, especially a school contract.

## Working on a DP: read this bit carefully

This is where most families get caught out, so it is worth being blunt: a DP is not a work pass.

Since 1 May 2021, a DP holder who wants an ordinary job generally needs their own EP, S Pass, or Work Permit. The old advice you will still find on forums, that your employer can just sort out a Letter of Consent, stopped being true years ago.

The Letter of Consent still exists, but it now mainly covers DP holders who are eligible business owners: an ACRA-registered sole proprietor, partner, or director holding at least 30% of the shares or the equivalent. A first LOC is time-boxed, often to a year or to DP expiry, whichever comes first.

Renewals are where solo consultancies come unstuck. To renew, you need to employ at least one Singaporean or PR earning the prevailing Local Qualifying Salary, with **three consecutive months of CPF contributions already made** before you submit. A one-person business has nothing to show against that bar, which is exactly the failure people post about on Reddit after the fact.

If a renewal does fail and your spouse still needs Singapore clients, there are three honest options: pause the local invoicing, hire to the published standard, or move onto an employer-sponsored or self-sponsored EP through a properly capitalised Pte Ltd ([incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore)). Trading on an expired LOC is not on the list. If the work is genuinely for overseas clients only, the rules are different: see [side income and freelancing](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

For the job-hunting side of this, including what the market actually looks like for a trailing partner, see [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

## When the main EP is cancelled

DP status is tied to the principal pass. When the main EP is cancelled or expires, dependants generally lose the DP too and need a lawful way to stay while things get sorted. That bridge is usually an employer-requested Short-Term Visit Pass, which can run up to 90 days if HR asks for it, though the default request is often shorter. An STVP is not work authorisation, and in practice people report it being treated as single-entry, so leaving Singapore can end the bridge.

So ask HR **in writing, before cancel day**, for the longest STVP they can request, and make sure the request covers the whole household rather than just the pass holder. The full sequence, including IR21 and the graduate LTVP adjacency, is in [between jobs on an EP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore). The principal-side rules are on [MOM's cancel an Employment Pass page](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass).

## Schools and housing

School waitlists and condo viewings tend to run in parallel with your IPA timing, which is uncomfortable but normal. Use provisional holds where you can, and read the cancellation terms properly before you pay anything large, because a delayed main pass is the scenario those penalties are written for. More on this in [school waitlist realism](/guides/school-waitlist-realism).

## What to get ready early

- Marriage and birth certificates, with official translations if yours are not in English
- Passports with enough validity left
- The main pass IPA or issued-pass details

## A sensible order to do things in

1. Main pass clarity, with the salary fixed for MOM
2. DP applications
3. Housing deposit, taking a flexible first lease if schools are still waitlisted
4. School enrolment fees

## Babies born in Singapore

A newborn does not inherit your pass automatically. Register the birth through LifeSG, get a passport sorted, and have the employer lodge a DP inside MOM's published window while an ICA Special Pass covers the baby's stay in the meantime. The full set of clocks is in [newborn pass and birth registration](/guides/newborn-pass-birth-registration-singapore).

## Budgeting

Do not assume the trailing partner earns anything in month one. Build the household budget as a single-income posting until a second pass actually exists, and treat any earlier income as a bonus.
