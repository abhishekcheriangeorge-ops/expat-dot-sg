The Personalised Employment Pass is MOM's high-earner pass that isn't tied to an employer, and that one difference is the whole appeal. You can change jobs without re-running a sponsorship each time, and if you're laid off you don't immediately lose your right to be here. The part people skip past is the other half: it's generally a one-time, non-renewable runway. When it ends you're applying for a fresh EP, or PR, or another personalised pass. Nothing about it rolls over.

## Who it's for, and the live floors

These come from MOM's eligibility page. Verify them there before you apply rather than trusting this page, or anyone's screenshot.

- Fixed monthly salary of at least S$22,500, benchmarked to top EP earners
- Applying from overseas: your last-drawn salary generally has to be within 6 months of the application
- To keep the pass once you have it, fixed salary of at least **S$270,000 per calendar year**, regardless of how many months you were employed

That third one deserves a second read. It's a calendar-year figure and it isn't pro-rated, so a long unpaid stretch doesn't lower the bar, it just makes the bar harder to clear. If you applied before 1 Sep 2023 you follow an older annual floor, so work out which cohort you're in before you plan anything around the number.

## What it actually gives you

The pass isn't attached to one employer, so when you change jobs you notify MOM rather than putting the new company through a fresh EP filing. That matters more than it sounds. It means a hiring manager doesn't have to want you enough to sponsor you, only enough to hire you.

Unemployment is allowed up to 6 months, and past that you cancel. Where PEP earns its keep is when COMPASS scoring or a company's sponsorship politics are the thing blocking an ordinary EP move, rather than anything about your own profile.

## What it doesn't give you

| Myth | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| "I'll just renew it forever" | PEP is structured as a finite personalised pass. Plan the exit to EP or PR early |
| "I can freelance or found a company on it" | MOM lists freelancers, sole props and shareholder-directors as ineligible. Entrepreneurial work points to [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) or [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) |
| "A rejection kills my EP" | The PEP application is a separate filing, but don't cancel your EP until the new pass is ready to issue |
| "Salary dips are fine if I take a break" | The annual S$270k keep-rule is unforgiving of long unpaid gaps |

## What the forums get right

The consensus is sound, and it's more conservative than you'd expect. If your EP renewals look easy and your situation is stable, stay on the EP. PEP is worth spending when retrenchment risk is genuinely high and you want a job-search buffer that doesn't end in an immediate exit, when you expect several employer changes in a short window, or when you're confident PR will land inside the PEP validity.

Outside those cases you can burn the personalised slot and still find yourself needing employer sponsorship afterwards, which is the worst of both arrangements.

## Timing it from overseas

The recurring r/askSingapore question is whether to get the PEP first or find the job first, and the answers converge. If PEP is your plan, apply while you can still prove the S$22,500 last-drawn salary within the window, because waiting until after a gap can quietly end your eligibility.

Using the IPA as a job-hunting buffer inside Singapore is fine, but only once you've understood the 6-month unemployment cancel rule and the annual S$270k keep-floor together, since those two interact badly. And if you're on an internal transfer that already clears an ordinary EP, you probably don't need PEP at all.

## The exit you have to model early

| Exit path | When it works |
| --- | --- |
| A fresh employer EP | Standard COMPASS and age-banded floors; FCF advertising still applies for many sponsors |
| PR | If ICA timing happens to land inside your PEP validity, which is not something you can promise yourself |
| [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) or [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore) | Only if you clear those separate bars while they're still accepting applications |
| Leaving Singapore | Cancel the PEP, with an STVP bridge if you're staying on briefly |

## Cancelling, and the bridge afterwards

If you're leaving for good or you've hit the unemployment limit, cancel through [MOM's PEP cancellation page](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/cancel-a-pass). If you're staying in Singapore after cancellation you still need a valid STVP to be here lawfully, which is its own small process. We've written that up in [between jobs and STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).
