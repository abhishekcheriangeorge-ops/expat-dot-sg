The Personalised Employment Pass is MOM’s **employer-agnostic** high-earner pass. Reddit likes it for job-hop flexibility and layoff buffers. The same threads warn: it is generally a **one-time, non-renewable** runway — after it ends you usually need a fresh EP (or PR / another personalised pass), not an automatic extension.

## Who it is for (live floors)

Per MOM eligibility (verify before you apply):

- Fixed monthly salary of at least **S$22,500** (benchmarked to top EP earners)
- Overseas applicants: last-drawn salary generally within **6 months** of application
- To **keep** the PEP: fixed salary of at least **S$270,000 per calendar year**, regardless of months employed (holders who applied before 1 Sep 2023 follow an older annual floor — check your cohort)

Canonical rules: [PEP eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/eligibility). Numbers move — screenshots in Facebook groups are not law.
</OfficialSource>

## What PEP gives you

- Pass is **not tied to one employer** — notify MOM on job changes rather than re-running a full EP each hop  
- Unemployment is allowed **up to 6 months**; beyond that you must cancel  
- Useful when COMPASS / company sponsorship politics block ordinary EP moves

## What PEP does not give you

| Myth | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| “I’ll renew forever” | PEP is structured as a **finite** personalised pass — plan the exit to EP/PR early |
| “I can freelance / found a company on PEP” | MOM lists freelancers, sole props, and shareholder-directors as **ineligible**; entrepreneurial work points to [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) or [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) |
| “Rejection kills my EP” | Applying for PEP is separate — still do not cancel your EP until the new pass is ready to issue |
| “Salary dips are fine if I take a break” | Annual **S$270k** keep-rule is unforgiving of long unpaid gaps |

## Reddit consensus (useful, not official)

Stay on a renewable **EP** if renewals look easy and you are stable. Consider PEP if:

- Retrenchment risk is high and you want a job-search buffer without immediate exit  
- You expect multiple employer changes in a short window  
- You are highly confident of **PR** inside the PEP validity  

Otherwise you may burn the personalised slot and still need employer sponsorship later.

## Cancellation and STVP

If you leave Singapore permanently or hit the unemployment limit, cancel per [MOM PEP cancel](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/cancel-a-pass). Holders remaining in Singapore still need a valid **STVP** bridge — see [between jobs / STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).
