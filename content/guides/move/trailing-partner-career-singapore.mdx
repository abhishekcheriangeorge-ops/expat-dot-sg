Trailing partners often relocate without a job — then discover Singapore’s work-pass system does **not** treat “spouse of EP holder” as an open labour-market ticket. Facebook expat groups and r/askSingapore threads are blunt: assume the non-sponsored spouse may be out of the market for months, especially in retail, hospitality, or roles employers can fill locally.

## Clarify legal status first (rules changed)

### Dependant’s Pass (DP) of an EP / EntrePass / PEP holder

From **1 May 2021**, MOM aligned DP work rules with the wider work-pass framework: DP holders who want to **work for an employer** generally need their **own** Employment Pass, S Pass, or Work Permit — not a general Letter of Consent for employment.

The remaining LOC path for DP holders is mainly for **eligible business owners** of an ACRA-registered business (sole proprietor, partner, or company director with at least 30% shareholding / equivalent CLG membership). Renewals typically require hiring at least one Singaporean/PR at the prevailing Local Qualifying Salary with CPF contributions for three consecutive months.

Read MOM’s [work passes overview](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits), the [DP business-owner LOC eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/loc-for-dependants-pass-business-owners/eligibility), and the [Budget 2021 DP work factsheet](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/budget2021/factsheet-work-arrangements-dp-holders.pdf). ### LTVP / LTVP+ tied to Singapore citizens or PRs

Spouses/children of citizens or PRs on ICA-issued LTVP/LTVP+ sit under a **different** LOC / pre-approved LOC regime. Do not mix that advice with EP-sponsored DP advice.

## Job-market realism (what peers report)

- Hiring managers often prefer candidates who already have **independent pass eligibility** — your DP is residency, not a hiring shortcut  
- Employers must still clear **COMPASS / salary / quota** constraints to sponsor an EP or S Pass for you  
- Sectors with deep local talent pools (general retail, many junior admin roles) are especially hard for new DP holders  
- Networks, niche skills, and flexible employers matter more than a polished LinkedIn alone  
- Volunteer work and informal gigs can still raise compliance issues — “unpaid” is not automatically safe  

## Remote work for an overseas employer (DP / stay pass)

MOM’s published FAQ for Dependant’s Pass holders draws a line many HR teams still argue about:

- Services to a **Singapore** (ACRA) organisation or client for gain → need a work pass / eligible LOC  
- Services to an **overseas-based** organisation or client → **no** work pass required for that overseas engagement  

Get the FAQ link in writing for nervous US/EU employers, then separately confirm **IRAS** treatment — permission to work is not a tax holiday. Full decision tree: [side income and freelancing on work passes](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## Entrepreneurship is not a loophole

Running a business on DP LOC requires meeting MOM’s owner criteria **before** you operate. First-time LOC duration is typically the shorter of one year from issue or DP expiry; processing is commonly within about four weeks for straightforward cases. Budget setup costs, local hire obligations for renewal, and the fact that the LOC dies if the DP is cancelled. When renewal fails because you cannot justify a Local Qualifying Salary hire, do not keep invoicing Singapore clients — pause, hire to the published standard, or migrate to an EP path ([Dependant’s Pass deep dive](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore), [incorporate](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore)).

## Negotiate upstream — before the main offer is signed

Ask the sponsored partner’s employer for:

1. Clarity on **DP vs LTVP** strategy for the household  
2. **Career coaching** or recruitment introductions in-country  
3. Temporary housing long enough for a proper job search (not a two-week hotel)  
4. Realistic dual-career timelines in the household plan — underemployment is a wellbeing risk, not a soft skill  

See [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding) for how to price these asks against housing and school support.

## Practical 90-day plan for the trailing partner

1. Confirm pass type and work rights in writing with the immigration counsel / HR that filed the main pass  
2. If seeking employment: target employers who routinely sponsor EP/S Pass; lead with scarce skills  
3. If founding: validate ACRA structure and LOC eligibility **before** signing clients  
4. Rebuild network via chambers, professional associations, and [belonging guides](/guides/find-my-people-singapore) — cold applications alone underperform  
5. Revisit the household budget monthly; do not assume dual income in month two  

Underemployment is a first-class relocation risk. Treat it like housing and schooling — plan it before wheels-up.
