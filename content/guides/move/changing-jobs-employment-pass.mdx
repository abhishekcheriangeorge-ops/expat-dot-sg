An Employment Pass belongs to your employer, not to you. That is the detail everything else follows from. Changing jobs is not a transfer stamp on a pass you already hold; it is a fresh EP application filed by the next company, and then cancellation of the old pass once the new one is issued. Which is why the advice that keeps surfacing on r/askSingapore is worth repeating: **don't resign until you hold the new IPA**.

## The sequence MOM actually expects

MOM's FAQ tells you to have the new employer apply for a new EP, and confirms you don't need to cancel your existing pass before that application goes in. Useful, but it doesn't make approval a formality. The new application is assessed on its merits under whatever guidelines are current at the time: salary floors, COMPASS, FCF advertising unless the role is exempt. Holding a pass at Company A buys you nothing at Company B.

| Stage | Who owns it | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer accepted | New HR | Hand over your FIN, documents and medical if asked. It's the same stack as a first EP |
| FCF / MCF ad (if required) | New employer | There are no shortcuts here, and inventing one hurts the filing. See [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) |
| Application to IPA | New employer | This is the point, and not before, at which you tender your resignation |
| Notice and IR21 | Old employer | Budget for [tax clearance withholding](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) even though you're staying in Singapore |
| Cancel old, issue new | Both employers | Surrender the old card on your last day; the new employer issues the new pass |
| FIN | MOM | Usually unchanged from one EP to the next for the same person |

## Moving to a sister company

A move to a branch, subsidiary or affiliate is still a new EP application, filed by the related company. The one concession is that MOM doesn't require MyCareersFuture advertising for related-company moves. Everything else holds. Your current employer cancels the existing EP when your employment there ends, and that cancellation has to complete before the related company can get the new pass issued, so the two sides need to talk to each other rather than to you separately. Mergers and entity changes run down a different notify path entirely. Don't try to DIY it with a personal transfer letter.

## When Company B appears and Company A is still pending

You can't park two live EP outcomes and pick the nicer one later. An EP is tied to a single employer and the whole process is built on that assumption, which is also what the forum consensus lands on.

If you're switching allegiance before any IPA has issued, ask Company A to withdraw promptly, and confirm that with MOM or HR before Company B's filing goes ahead. Once A's IPA is out, dual filings get considerably messier, and the sensible move is to pick a lane and tell both HR teams, rather than hoping MOM quietly picks the better offer on your behalf. If A gets rejected instead, remember the appeal window belongs to the employer and is time-boxed. You cannot file it yourself, which is covered in [EP rejection, appeal and IPA](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance).

## The money side Reddit tends to under-model

IR21 is the one that surprises people. Tax clearance fires when you leave a Singapore employer even if you never leave Singapore and your next IPA is already in hand. The old employer withholds monies due until IRAS has cleared you, so final salary, leave encashment and any bonus can sit frozen for weeks while rent still goes out on the first of the month. Plan the cash flow for it rather than discovering it.

Group medical usually stops on your last day, not on your new start date, which leaves a gap nobody mentions in the exit meeting. If you have elective care booked, price a personal bridge before you tender ([health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat)). Training bonds and clawbacks are a contract argument with your old employer rather than an MOM clock, and a shiny new IPA does nothing to freeze them ([training bonds](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep)). And if the new IPA fails after the old EP has already been cancelled, you are in [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) territory, which is a much weaker position to negotiate from than the one you just left.

## Before you tender

1. Save the new IPA PDF offline, and get HR's confirmation in writing somewhere you can find it again.
2. Check your notice length against the IPA validity and the issuance appointment, not against your own preferred start date.
3. Hold a cash buffer for the IR21 freeze, and ask the new employer for a written start-date letter. It helps with GIRO continuity.
4. Sort out the family. Dependant's Passes hang off your main pass, so sequence the household STVP asks around the cancel date. The full re-sponsor calendar is in [DP re-sponsor when the principal changes EP](/guides/dp-resubmission-when-principal-changes-ep-singapore).
5. Once the new pass is issued, update your address and passport details — [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport).

If you're still weighing the offer itself, the maths is in [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding), and [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore) covers the exits that don't end in another EP.

For the fiddlier IR21 withholding edges on a hop that never leaves the city: [EP job-change IR21 withhold](/guides/ep-job-change-ir21-withhold-edges-singapore).
