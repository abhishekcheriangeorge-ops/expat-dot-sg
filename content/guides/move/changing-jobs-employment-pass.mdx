An Employment Pass is **employer-tied**. Changing jobs is not a transfer stamp — it is a **new EP application** by the next company, then cancellation of the old pass when the new one is issued. r/askSingapore’s highest-signal advice is still: **do not resign until you hold the new IPA**.

## Official sequence (already on EP)

MOM’s FAQ: ask the **new** employer to apply for a new EP. You do **not** need to cancel the existing EP before that application is filed. Approval is still on the merits under prevailing guidelines (salary floors, COMPASS, FCF advertising unless exempt).

| Stage | Who owns it | Pass-holder move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer accepted | New HR | Share FIN, docs, medical if required — same stack as a first EP |
| FCF / MCF ad (if required) | New employer | Do not invent shortcuts; see [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep) |
| Application → IPA | New employer | **Only now** tender resignation at the old firm |
| Notice + IR21 | Old employer | Budget [tax clearance withholding](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving) even though you stay in Singapore |
| Cancel old / issue new | Both employers | Surrender old card on last day; new employer issues the new pass |
| FIN | MOM | Usually **unchanged** across EP issuances for the same person |

## Related-company transfers

Moving to a **branch / subsidiary / affiliate** is still a new EP application by the related company. MOM notes that related-company moves do **not** need MyCareersFuture advertising. The current employer cancels the existing EP when employment ends — cancellation must complete before the related company can get the new pass **issued**. Mergers and entity changes use a different notify path; do not DIY with a personal “transfer letter.”

## Still pending / IPA with Company A when B appears

You generally cannot park two live EP outcomes as a shopping cart. Forum consensus that matches MOM’s one-employer model:

1. Prefer Company A **withdraw** before Company B files if you are switching allegiance pre-IPA  
2. After A’s IPA is out, dual filings get messier — pick a lane with both HRs rather than hoping MOM “picks the better offer”  
3. Rejected A → appeal window is **employer-only** and time-boxed — [EP rejection / appeal / IPA](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance)

## Cash and benefits Reddit under-models

- **IR21** fires on leaving the old Singapore employer even if your next IPA is already in hand — final pay can sit for weeks  
- Group medical often **ends on last day** — price a personal bridge before elective care ([health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat))  
- Training bonds / clawbacks are **contract** fights, not MOM clocks — [training bonds](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep)  
- If the new IPA fails and the old EP is already cancelled, you are in [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore) territory  

## Checklist before you tender

1. New IPA PDF saved offline + HR WhatsApp confirmation  
2. Notice length vs IPA validity / issuance appointment  
3. IR21 cash buffer + written start-date letter for the new employer (helps GIRO continuity)  
4. Dependants: DPs are tied to the main pass — sequence household STVP asks on cancel. Deep re-sponsor calendar: [DP re-sponsor when principal changes EP](/guides/dp-resubmission-when-principal-changes-ep-singapore)  
5. Update address / passport hygiene on the new pass once issued — [notify MOM](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport)
