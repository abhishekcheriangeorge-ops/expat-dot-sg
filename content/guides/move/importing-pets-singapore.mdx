**Short answer:** AVS (NParks) runs pet imports, and it's your animal's export-country schedule, the rabies serology waits and AQC slot scarcity that set the move date — far more often than your job start does. Get housing to accept the pet in writing first, then work through vaccines, titres, licence, quarantine booking and the Changi inspection in that order. Treat the airline's "pet cargo" checklist as incomplete, because it is.

Pet imports here are administered by NParks Animal & Veterinary Service. The single mistake families make is treating the whole thing as airline paperwork, when the airline is really the last link in a chain that starts months earlier on a vet's table in the country you're leaving.

The r/askSingapore relocation threads, whether they're coming from the US, Europe or elsewhere in Asia, converge on the same three things: read AVS first, book AQC early if you're Schedule III, and never sign a lease on a verbal "pets OK".

## Schedules decide your timeline

| Origin schedule (AVS) | Typical post-arrival quarantine theme | Planning implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule I (lower rabies risk) | Often no facility quarantine if conditions met | Still need licences, vaccines, inspection |
| Schedule II | May need ~10-day home or AQC quarantine depending on conditions | Home quarantine needs prior AVS approval |
| Schedule III | **≥30 days at Animal Quarantine Centre (AQC)** + arrival rabies jab | Serology + QMS booking can add months |

Country lists and veterinary conditions do change, so match your pet against the live AVS schedule table rather than a forum post from last year. Match on your pet's export country, not your passport nationality. It's the country the animal physically leaves from that counts, which catches out families who have been posted somewhere other than home.

## The critical path most families under-budget

1. Confirm the species and breed are allowed, and that your housing will accept the animal **in writing**.
2. Microchip and rabies vaccination inside the AVS windows. For higher-risk origins, add rabies serology with its mandatory wait before travel, which is the step that quietly eats your calendar.
3. The PALS pet licence, course and licence both, on the Singapore side where your situation calls for it.
4. Reserve AQC space through the Quarantine Management System (QMS) if quarantine applies. The months-ahead booking panic on Reddit matches AVS's own "book early" guidance, so treat it as real rather than as forum drama.
5. Apply for the AVS import licence on GoBusiness. If you don't have a FIN yet, you'll be doing that through a Singpass Foreign-user Account.
6. Book airline pet acceptance and the Changi animal inspection appointment. Cabin and cargo rules are airline-specific, so don't assume yours works like the one your colleague used.

AVS allows either the owner or an AVS-recognised pet agent to file the personal-pet import and export applications. A good agent earns their fee on export-country paperwork and the Singapore filing, and the published list is a reference rather than an endorsement of anyone on it. Either way, if a window gets missed, you're the one who missed it.

## Housing kills more pet moves than quarantine does

Plenty of condos restrict dog size, breed or number. HDB has its own pet rules. Serviced apartments and short stays often ban animals outright. So don't sign an LOI or TA until the pet clause is in the document, because a verbal assurance from an agent is worth nothing on move-in day, and by then your freight and quarantine deposits are well past refundable.

While you're reading the lease, check three more things: who pays for deep cleaning and flooring deductions at exit, what the lift and common-area rules and MCST by-laws say about animals, and whether your helper or a dog-walker can actually get into the unit when you're not there.

Once your pet is through, [pets daily life](/guides/pets-daily-life-singapore) picks up the story. If crates are travelling with your household shipment, Customs GST rules still apply to those: [shipping household](/guides/shipping-household-singapore).

## What this actually costs

Budget thousands of SGD all-in, once you stack export vet work, serology, flights and cargo, the import licence, quarantine fees and transport, and Singapore licensing. That's before a stick of furniture moves. Schedule III with an agent on both ends is the expensive end of it, and Schedule I done yourself is still not free.
