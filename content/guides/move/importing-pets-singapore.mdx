Singapore’s pet import rules are administered by NParks Animal & Veterinary Service (AVS). The single mistake families make is treating this like airline pet cargo paperwork — **country schedule, serology wait, and quarantine-slot scarcity** set your move date more often than your job start date.

r/askSingapore relocation threads (US/EU/Asia origins) converge on the same advice: read AVS first, book AQC early for Schedule III, and never sign a lease on verbal “pets OK.”

## Schedules decide your timeline

| Origin schedule (AVS) | Typical post-arrival quarantine theme | Planning implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule I (lower rabies risk) | Often no facility quarantine if conditions met | Still need licences, vaccines, inspection |
| Schedule II | May need **~10-day** home or AQC quarantine depending on conditions | Home quarantine needs prior AVS approval |
| Schedule III | **≥30 days at Animal Quarantine Centre (AQC)** + arrival rabies jab | Serology + QMS booking can add **months** |

Exact country lists and veterinary conditions change. Always match your pet’s **export country** (not your passport nationality) to the live AVS schedule table.

Follow [AVS importing dogs and cats](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/pets/importing-exporting-a-pet/import/dogs-and-cats/) end-to-end — schedules, microchip, vaccines, and licence steps.

## The critical path most families under-budget

1. Confirm species/breed is allowed and housing will accept the animal **in writing**  
2. Microchip + rabies vaccination within AVS windows; for higher-risk origins, **rabies serology** with the mandatory wait before travel  
3. **PALS** pet licence (course + licence) on the Singapore side when required for your situation  
4. Reserve **AQC space via Quarantine Management System (QMS)** when quarantine applies — Reddit reports of months-ahead booking pressure match AVS’s “book early” guidance  
5. Apply for the AVS **import licence** on GoBusiness (foreigners without FIN use a **Singpass Foreign-user Account**)  
6. Book airline pet acceptance + Changi animal inspection appointment; cargo vs cabin rules are airline-specific  

Agents help on the **export** country’s paperwork; many owners handle Singapore-side AVS once they have a human contact path. Either way, you remain responsible for missed windows.

<OfficialSource>
Quarantine logistics and fees live on the [Animal Quarantine Centre](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/about-us/our-centres/animal-quarantine-centre/) page; licence filing is via [GoBusiness AVS licence](https://licensing.gobusiness.gov.sg/licence-directory/avs/licence-to-importexporttransship-non-food-animals-birds-eggs-and-biologics).
</OfficialSource>

## Housing kills more pet moves than quarantine

Many condos restrict dog size, breed, or number. HDB has its own pet rules. Serviced apartments and short stays may ban pets entirely. **Never** sign a LOI/TA until pet clauses are written — verbal agent assurances are worthless at move-in.

Also check:

- Who pays deep-clean / flooring deductions at exit  
- Lift / common-area rules and MCST by-laws  
- Whether your helper or dog-walker can access the unit  

## Cost realism (order-of-magnitude)

Expect **thousands of SGD** all-in once you stack export vet work, serology, flights/cargo, import licence, quarantine fees/transport, and Singapore licensing — before furniture. Schedule III + agent on both ends is the expensive path; Schedule I DIY is still not free.

<OfficialSource>
Customs has a separate note on [importing personal pets](https://www.customs.gov.sg/businesses/importing-goods/import-procedures/importing-of-personal-pets/) — do not skip it when AVS clearance is done.
