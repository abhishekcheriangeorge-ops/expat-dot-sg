r/askSingapore keeps asking the same compliance question in different costumes: content creation on an EP, wedding photography on a visit pass, “remote US job while on LTVP,” tutoring on the side. The governing split is simple — **who is the client**, and **what does your pass authorise** — not how small the invoice looks.

## Work pass holders (EP, S Pass, Work Permit, most training passes)

MOM’s general FAQ is blunt:

> All work pass holders must only work for their designated employer. They must not take on additional jobs or engage in activities to earn additional income in Singapore.

That covers weekend Grab, paid Instagram campaigns for Singapore brands, coaching locals for cash, and “just one wedding.” “Unpaid” volunteering can still be messy if it looks like labour for a Singapore entity — ask before you assume goodwill equals clearance. Registering a Pte Ltd or sitting as director does not create a side-hustle loophole — see [incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore) and MOM’s secondary-directorship LOC rules.

<OfficialSource>
Read [MOM — Can a work pass holder work in multiple jobs?](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/can-a-work-pass-holder-work-in-multiple-jobs). Penalties for illegal employment sit under the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act — do not treat forum “nobody gets caught” as a plan.
</OfficialSource>

## Dependant’s Pass / stay-pass holders: overseas vs Singapore clients

MOM’s Dependant’s Pass FAQ draws a bright line:

| Activity while physically in Singapore | Work pass needed? |
| --- | --- |
| Working for / providing services to a **Singapore-based** organisation (ACRA-registered) or client for gain | **Yes** — EP, S Pass, DP-WP, or eligible [business-owner LOC](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/loc-for-dependants-pass-business-owners/eligibility) |
| Working for / providing services to an **overseas-based** organisation or client | **No** work pass for that overseas engagement (per MOM FAQ) |

Reddit employers who panic after “MOM said OK” usually need the FAQ link in writing plus an IRAS conversation — tax residency and “income received in Singapore” are separate from work-pass permission. See [foreign income and tax](/guides/foreign-income-and-tax).

Canonical wording: [Do I need a work pass if I am self-employed or working for an overseas-based employer?](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/dependants-pass/do-i-need-a-work-pass-if-i-am-self-employed-or-working-for-an-overseas-based-employer) (updated March 2024). DP employment paths: [Working in Singapore](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/working-in-singapore).

## Personalised passes are not “open freelance”

| Pass | Side-income reality |
| --- | --- |
| [PEP](/guides/personalised-employment-pass-pep) | Employer-agnostic **employment** — freelancers / sole props / shareholder-directors are generally **ineligible** for the pass itself |
| [Tech.Pass](/guides/tech-pass-singapore) | Explicitly multi-activity (employee, founder, investor, consultant, mentor) **within programme rules** — still not a tourist side-hustle loophole |
| [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) | Top-talent flexibility with published family LOC paths — verify activities against MOM pages |
| [EntrePass](/guides/entrepass-singapore) | For the venture you founded under EntrePass rules — not random gig work |

## Practical decision tree

1. Are you on an **employer-tied** work pass (EP/S Pass/WP)? → Assume **no Singapore side income**.  
2. Are you on **DP/LTVP** earning from a **Singapore** client or ACRA entity? → Get the right pass / LOC **before** you invoice. Remember LOC **renewals** need a local hire at LQS + 3 months CPF — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore).  
3. Are you on **DP/LTVP** earning only from a clearly **overseas** employer/client with no Singapore entity connection? → MOM FAQ says no work pass for that work — still sort **tax**, banking, and contract structure.  
4. Want to sell services broadly in Singapore? → Build the correct pass path ([trailing partner career](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore)), do not invent a YouTube exception.

## What Reddit gets wrong

- “Influencer income is not a job” — if you are paid for activity in Singapore for a Singapore client, treat it as work.  
- “LTVP means I can freelance locally” — LTVP alone does not unlock local freelancing; SC/PR spouses may have PLOC, EP-family common-law LTVP usually does not.  
- “Student Pass part-time is fine for everything” — student work exemptions are narrow; do not generalise them to EP households.

When the dollar amount is material, get regulated advice — [immigration counsel category](/entities/intact-immigration) for pass structure, tax adviser for IRAS, not a Discord poll.
