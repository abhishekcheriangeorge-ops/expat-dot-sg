r/askSingapore internship threads keep inventing a third option: “I’ll just work on my visit pass.” That is illegal. The two published short-stint rails foreigners actually use are the **Work Holiday Pass** family and the **Training Employment Pass (TEP)**. They solve different problems — and neither is a backdoor to PR.

## Side-by-side

| | **Work Holiday Programme (WHP)** | **Work & Holiday (AU / NZ)** | **Training Employment Pass** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who applies | Candidate (self) | Candidate (self) | **Employer** |
| Typical age | 18–25 | 18–30 (citizens of AU / NZ) | No tourist-style age band — student / trainee criteria |
| Stay / work | Up to **6 months** | Up to **12 months** | Up to **3 months**, **not renewable** |
| University gate | Undergrad / grad of listed countries’ recognised unis | Degree or ≥2 years undergrad study | Student: training must be part of course + acceptable institution **or** ≥ S$3,000 fixed monthly; trainee from overseas office: ≥ S$3,000 |
| Levy / quota | None | None | None |
| Family passes | No | No | No |
| After it ends | Leave, or employer sponsors EP / S Pass if you qualify | Same; AU/NZ also cannot work for same employer **>6 months** on this pass | Leave or new pass type — TEP itself does not renew |

## Work Holiday — what Reddit gets wrong

- Many SG hiring managers have **never heard of WHP**. Bring the MOM link; emphasise no levy and no quota.  
- Apply for WHP and roles **in parallel** — employers often want proof you can legally work before they issue an offer.  
- WHP is **not extendable** as WHP. Conversion means a real EP/S Pass story with COMPASS / quota rules — [EP](/guides/employment-pass-singapore), [S Pass](/guides/s-pass-singapore), [FCF](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep).  
- Regulated professions (medicine, law, etc.) still need local registration even on WHP.

Listed WHP source countries/regions (verify live): Australia, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Japan, Netherlands, New Zealand, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States — with the university recognition rules on MOM’s page.

## Training Employment Pass — the internship rail

TEP is for a **training attachment**, not a full-time hire masquerading as training. Employer files; fees and steps are on MOM’s apply page (application + issuance fees; most cases within about three weeks — verify live). MOM’s sample list of institutions is indicative, not a guarantee your school clears — contact MOM early if your uni is off-list.

If TEP fails, do not “start anyway” on a Short-Term Visit Pass. Options are WHP (if eligible), a proper work pass, or training in another jurisdiction.

## Student’s Pass vs these

Full-time study sits on ICA’s Student’s Pass — [guide](/guides/student-pass-singapore). Part-time work rules for Student’s Pass holders are a different MOM exemption track. Mixing “I’m on holiday” with paid work without WHP/TEP/EP is how people get immigration problems, not LinkedIn stories.

## Practical checklist

1. Map nationality + age + student status to WHP vs AU/NZ vs TEP before emailing HR  
2. Never accept “just come on tourist visa and we’ll sort it”  
3. Budget housing for a short lease — [serviced / short-stay](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental)  
4. If converting to EP after WHP, start COMPASS / FCF reality early  
5. Tuition Grant bonds are unrelated to WHP — do not confuse rails ([TG bond](/guides/moe-tuition-grant-bond))
