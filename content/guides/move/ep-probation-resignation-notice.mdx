Probation feels casual. **Pass cancellation is not.** Treat resignation during the first six months with the same immigration seriousness as a later job change.

## Three clocks people mix up

1. **Contract notice / probation termination** — when you stop work for that employer.
2. **MOM pass cancellation** — when the EP is cancelled in MOM systems.
3. **Household dependant status** — when DP/LOC rights fall with the principal.

## Practical resignation checklist

- Read probation and notice clauses before you accept the offer.
- For a new role, aim for [new IPA before resigning](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass).
- Ask HR for the MOM cancel date and whether any short-term visit arrangement will be requested.
- Inventory DP/LOC holders, lease diplomatic clauses, and school deposits.
- Do not surrender the pass card at the airport without HR’s written cancel plan.

Work-pass issuance drama lives on [issuance / EPSC](/guides/work-pass-issuance-epsc-notification); this page is about **exit while employed**.
