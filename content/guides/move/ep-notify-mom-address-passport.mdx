**Short answer:** Update **residential address or mobile within 5 days** in **EP eService** (pass holder or employer). Private-condo updates often need the landlord’s **FWTES** “allow pass holder” tick first. Renewed passports must be updated in eService **before** you rely on automated gates.

r/askSingapore keeps rediscovering the same three MOM clocks: **5 days** to update residential address or mobile, **FWTES landlord consent** blocking private-condo declarations, and **passport renewals** that break automated immigration until EP eService catches up. This is the pass-holder admin guide — not landlord law, not bank KYC.

## Residential address or mobile — 5-day clock

MOM’s [notify-of-changes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) page is blunt: the **pass holder or employer** must update residential address or mobile number within **5 days** of the change. Miss it and the EP holder may be **penalised**.

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm the new address meets housing rules (lawful stay, occupancy caps) |
| 2 | Log in to [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and update address / mobile |
| 3 | Update is **immediate** — print the acknowledgement and keep it with the pass card |

Hotel / illegal short-let “addresses” are how people start the five-day clock with nowhere legal to declare. Prefer a [serviced apartment](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment) or a proper lease you can actually register — see [short-term vs long-term](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental).

## FWTES — when the landlord holds the key

Private residential property (PRP) owners who rent for the first time must complete a one-time declaration in **Foreign Worker Tenant Enquiry Service (FWTES)** so the unit is available for foreign-employee address registration. Until they tick “allow pass holder declarations,” EP eService / OFWAS can refuse your update even though **you** are within the five-day window.

Landlord script that works in practice (no mystery links):

1. Go to [mom.gov.sg → FWTES](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/tes)  
2. Log in with **Singpass**  
3. Select the property → declare it available for foreign worker address registration → submit  

If the owner cannot log in, MOM’s feedback forms cover **owner consent assistance** and **blocked address** cases (agents can sometimes submit on behalf). Escalating with TA + property-tax proof beats arguing in WhatsApp.

| Failure mode | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Owner “thinks FWTES is a scam” | Walk them to the official URL in person / Zoom; agent LOA if needed |
| Occupancy / ex-tenants still registered | Owner removes stale WPHs in FWTES, then you retry |
| Grey short-let | Move to a declare-able address before the composition fine conversation |

## Passport number / expiry — auto-gates need 4 working days

Renewed your passport overseas and assumed ICA would “just know”? EP holders update **passport number and expiry instantly** in EP eService (written requests are no longer accepted). MOM states you can use **automated gates** at local checkpoints only **4 working days** after the update. Manual lanes still work — but do not leave the update until the airport queue.

Other particulars (name, nationality, marital status, DOB, etc.) use the online personal-particulars request with supporting docs — MOM emails outcomes within about **7 working days**.
