**Short answer:** You have **5 days** to update a change of residential address or mobile number in EP eService, and either you or your employer can do it. If you're renting a private condo, the update often won't go through until your landlord has made the "allow pass holder" declaration in FWTES. And when you renew your passport, update it in eService before you rely on the automated immigration gates.

r/askSingapore rediscovers the same three MOM clocks every few months: the 5 days you get for an address or mobile change, the FWTES landlord consent that quietly blocks private-condo declarations, and the passport renewal that breaks automated immigration until EP eService catches up. This is the pass-holder admin guide. It isn't landlord law, and it isn't bank KYC.

## The 5-day clock on address and mobile

MOM's [notify-of-changes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) page is blunt about it. The pass holder or the employer has to update the residential address or mobile number within **5 days** of the change, and an EP holder who misses it can be penalised. It's a small piece of admin with a real consequence attached, which is an unusual combination and the reason it catches people.

| Step | What you're doing |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Check the new address actually meets the housing rules, including lawful stay and occupancy caps |
| 2 | Log in to [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and update the address or mobile |
| 3 | The update takes effect immediately, so print the acknowledgement and keep it with your pass card |

Hotels and illegal short-lets are how people end up with the five-day clock running and nowhere legal to declare. A [serviced apartment](/guides/first-housing-serviced-apartment) or a proper lease you can actually register is the way out of that, and [short-term vs long-term](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental) covers the trade-offs.

## FWTES, where your landlord holds the key

Owners of private residential property who are renting out for the first time have to complete a one-time declaration in the Foreign Worker Tenant Enquiry Service, so that the unit becomes available for foreign-employee address registration. Until they tick "allow pass holder declarations," EP eService and OFWAS can refuse your update even though you're well inside your five-day window. That's the frustrating part: you can be doing everything right and still be blocked by someone who has never heard of the system.

So give your landlord the actual steps rather than a link they'll treat as suspicious.

1. Go to [mom.gov.sg and open FWTES](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/tes)
2. Log in with Singpass
3. Select the property, declare it available for foreign worker address registration, and submit

If the owner genuinely can't log in, MOM's feedback forms cover owner consent assistance and blocked address cases, and agents can sometimes submit on their behalf. Escalating with your tenancy agreement and property-tax proof gets further than another round of WhatsApp messages.

| Where it goes wrong | What fixes it |
| --- | --- |
| The owner is convinced FWTES is a scam | Walk them to the official URL in person or over Zoom, and use an agent LOA if that's what it takes |
| Ex-tenants are still registered against the unit | The owner removes the stale WPHs in FWTES, then you retry your update |
| You're in a grey short-let | Move to an address you can declare, before anyone starts a composition fine conversation |

## Passport renewals, and the 4 working days before the gates work

If you renewed your passport overseas and assumed ICA would simply know, this is the section for you. EP holders update the passport number and expiry themselves in EP eService, and it takes effect instantly. Written requests aren't accepted any more. MOM's position is that you can use the automated gates at local checkpoints only **4 working days** after that update. The manual lanes still work in the meantime, which is your safety net, but it's a poor way to discover the rule at the head of a departure queue.

Other particulars, meaning your name, nationality, marital status, date of birth and the like, go through the online personal-particulars request with supporting documents. MOM emails the outcome within about 7 working days.
