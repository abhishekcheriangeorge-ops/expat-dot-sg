**Short answer:** Treat relocation as a **dependency project**, not a shopping list. Days **1–7** unlock a Singapore number and pass formalities; days **8–30** unlock payroll banking and housing paperwork; days **31–90** lock routines. Use the interactive [arriving checklists](/journeys/arriving) to tick items — this page is the narrative map.

## How to use this checklist

1. Skim the [SIM → Singpass → bank order](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank) so you do not invent circular blockers.  
2. File the [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats) inside ICA’s 3-day window.  
3. Work the phases below; open the matching journey board for checkboxes.  

## First 7 days

- Submit [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats) before landing; keep IPA / pass letter ready  
- Complete pass formalities and collect / activate your pass card; install **SGWorkPass** the same day  
- Get a local prepaid SIM and stable connectivity — [first-week map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank)  
- Open or activate banking where possible (credit cards wait for payslips — see [credit cards](/guides/credit-cards-foreigners-singapore))  
- Confirm temporary housing check-in and inventory (chute seals, aircon unit counts, mould photos)  
- Register critical HR contacts and emergency clinic / dental locations; if you flew with controlled meds, keep the HSA approval letter with your passport  
- If eligible, begin Singpass setup  

Interactive board: [first 7 days](/journeys/arriving/day-7).

## First 30 days

- Stamp the tenancy agreement (lease duty) and save the Stamp Certificate — [renting process](/guides/renting-process-loi-ta-deposits)  
- Transfer chronic meds to a local GP/specialist before the import supply ends — [HSA medication rules](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa)  
- Long-term housing search in earnest (if still in serviced apartment) — negotiate [diplomatic clause](/guides/security-deposit-diplomatic-clause) in the LOI  
- School applications / waitlist follow-ups; queue [translations / apostilles](/guides/document-apostille-notary-singapore) early if ICA/MOM needs them  
- GP registration and insurance cards understood (dental carve-outs included) — [healthcare triage](/guides/healthcare-gp-hospital)  
- Utilities / telecom / PayNow / GIRO basics  
- Commute dry-runs at rush hour  
- Update address with bank, employer, and relevant agencies as required  
- Trailing partner: confirm work rights in writing — overseas remote vs Singapore clients differ ([side income rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass))  
- Understand equity tax points if RSUs vest in year one — [RSU / ESOP tax](/guides/rsu-esop-tax-singapore)  

Interactive board: [first 30 days](/journeys/arriving/day-30).

## First 90 days

- Settle utilities, fibre, OEM plan choice, and recurring payments  
- Build a basic community foothold (club, hash, interest group, or neighbour network)  
- Review budget vs actual spend (include unsubsidised healthcare / dental)  
- Apply for a local credit card only after salary credit + payslips — [credit cards for foreigners](/guides/credit-cards-foreigners-singapore)  
- Note pass expiry and PR long-game only after you understand living reality  

Interactive board: [first 90 days](/journeys/arriving/day-90).

## Frequently asked questions

### What must happen in the first week?

Number, pass path, cash bridge, temporary keys. Singpass and a perfect condo can wait until FIN and address proof exist — see the [dependency map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank).

### When do I switch from Arriving to Living?

When renewals, belonging, and neighbourhood fit matter more than issuance. Use the [Living hub](/living) once the 90-day board feels routine rather than emergency.
