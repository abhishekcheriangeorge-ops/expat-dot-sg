**Short answer:** As a residing foreigner you can usually drive on a valid foreign licence for about **12 months**, then you need a Singapore licence. Conversion = **BTT + Traffic Police appointment** (not an online form). The **JB weekend licence** shortcut does **not** work. Skip conversion entirely if Grab/MRT covers your life.

Driving in Singapore is optional for many central lifestyles and expensive when it is not (COE, parking, ERP, insurance). Licence conversion, however, has a **hard clock** many EP holders miss.

## The 12-month resident rule

If you are a foreigner **residing** in Singapore, Traffic Police guidance allows driving on a valid foreign licence (with IDP / English translation rules as applicable) for a limited period — commonly discussed as **up to 12 months**. After that, you generally need a Singapore licence to keep driving.

New citizens / PRs have separate short windows and conversion obligations — read the live SPF page for your exact status.

| Scenario | Typical implication |
| --- | --- |
| Tourist / short visit | Foreign licence + IDP/translation rules; insurance must name you |
| EP/DP resident under ~12 months | May drive on foreign licence subject to SPF rules |
| Resident past the allowed period | Convert or stop driving |
| “I’ll just get a Malaysia licence this weekend and convert” | **Does not work** — TP looks for genuine residency where the licence was issued |

## Conversion path (recognised foreign licences)

High-level steps peers and TP materials describe:

1. Pass the **Basic Theory Test (BTT)** in Singapore  
2. Gather originals + photocopies (passport/FIN/pass, foreign licence, photo, translations if needed, residency evidence as required)  
3. Attend a **Traffic Police counter appointment** — online conversion is not the path  
4. Receive Singapore QDL subject to approval  

Document checklists differ if your foreign licence was obtained **after** you already held a Singapore pass/PR — TP may demand proof you truly resided in the issuing country (not a fly-in test weekend).

## If your country is not on the easy-conversion path

You enrol with a driving centre (e.g. BBDC and peers), complete theory/practical requirements like any other learner, and budget **time + lesson costs** that often land in the low thousands of SGD if you are starting cold. EP holders are eligible; foreigner licences may show different validity patterns than SC/PR licences — confirm at issuance.

## Do you need to drive at all?

| Lifestyle | Car necessity |
| --- | --- |
| CBD / East Coast / well-served MRT corridor | Often optional (MRT + Grab) |
| Bukit Timah landed / school-run heavy | Car or reliable private hire becomes lifestyle infrastructure |
| Shift work off MRT hours | Car or disciplined Grab budget |

## Practical timeline

- Month 1–3: decide if you will drive in months 6–12  
- If yes: book BTT early; appointment slots and document chasing eat weekends  
- Align insurance: being “named” on a relative’s policy is not automatic  

Convert a licence because you will drive — not because the relocation checklist printed it in bold.
