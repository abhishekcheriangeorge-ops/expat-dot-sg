r/askSingapore’s highest-intent pass confusion is still: “HR says no EP quota, so S Pass.” Use this table before you resign overseas or accept a “temporary” S Pass that locks family and levy economics.

## Decision table

| Axis | Employment Pass | S Pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target band | Professionals / managers / executives | Mid-skilled / associate professional & technician |
| Employer **quota** (DRC) | **No** | **Yes** — sector Dependency Ratio Ceiling |
| Monthly **levy** | No | Yes (verify live MOM tier) |
| Stage 2 | [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) (≥40 pts unless exempt) | No COMPASS |
| Qualifying salary (orientation) | From **S$5,600** (non-FS) / **S$6,200** (FS), age-banded — see below | Lower floors (~**S$3,300+** non-FS from Sep 2025 steps) — age-banded |
| Family DP (headline) | Fixed monthly ≥ **S$6,000** for eligible spouse/kids | Same **S$6,000** fixed monthly gate |
| Typical Reddit red flag | Offer below updated age-band floor | “No EP quota” narrative |

## EP salary floors that keep breaking offers (2025–2028 calendar)

MOM publishes **new application** dates and **renewal** dates on different clocks. Orientation snapshot from the EP eligibility page (always re-check):

| Sector | Current new-application floor (youngest band) | Next published step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All except financial services | **S$5,600**/mo (rises with age to **S$10,700** at 45+) | **S$6,000** for new apps from **1 Jan 2027**; renewals of passes expiring from **1 Jan 2028** |
| Financial services | **S$6,200**/mo (to **S$11,800** at 45+) | **S$6,600** / **S$12,700** on the same 2027/2028 clocks |

Forum heat: candidates who signed at **S$5,000** after the **1 Jan 2025** new-application step, or who confuse **qualifying salary** with COMPASS **C1** percentiles. Fixed monthly salary is what counts — allowance packaging games fail Stage 1 before COMPASS points matter. Deep dive: [Employment Pass guide](/guides/employment-pass-singapore).

## When “S Pass first” is honest vs lazy

**Honest employer reasons**

- Role is genuinely mid-skilled and pay sits in S Pass bands  
- Firm is at EP COMPASS risk (diversity / local employment / credentials) even if cash clears the EP floor  
- Candidate wants to start while documents for EP are incomplete — with a written conversion plan  

**Lazy / incorrect reasons**

- “We have no EP quota” — EP has **no** foreign-worker quota  
- “S Pass is easier for Indians / PRC / whoever” — nationality concentration is a COMPASS **C3/C5** issue, not an S Pass entitlement  
- “Everyone starts on S Pass” — conversion later is a **new** EP application, not an auto-upgrade  

## Family and household math

Both passes can sponsor Dependant’s Passes once fixed monthly salary clears **S$6,000** and relationship rules are met — verify [DP eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility). S Pass levy + quota uncertainty still changes the employer’s willingness to renew; that risk transfers to your housing and school deposits. Trailing partners: [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) and [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore).

## Candidate checklist before you accept

1. Ask HR which pass will be filed and for the **fixed monthly salary** that will appear on the application  
2. Run / demand a screenshot of MOM’s **Self-Assessment Tool** outcome  
3. If they say EP is impossible, ask whether the blocker is **salary**, **COMPASS**, **FCF advertising**, or **quota** — only one of those words belongs to S Pass  
4. Price levy and renewal risk into the household model in [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding)
