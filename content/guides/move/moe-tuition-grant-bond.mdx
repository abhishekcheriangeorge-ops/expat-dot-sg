International students who take MOE’s **Tuition Grant (TG)** trade lower fees for a **3-year full-time employment bond** in a Singapore entity after graduation. r/askSingapore is full of people who only model the fee discount — then discover liquidated damages, sureties, and EP sponsorship friction in the same month.

## Who is bonded

Singapore Citizens are not under the same 3-year employment bond. **Singapore PRs and international students** who receive TG for eligible diploma/undergraduate courses at Institutes of Higher Learning generally are. Read the Tuition Grant Agreement you (and your sureties) signed — Reddit summaries are not your contract.

Scheme hub: [MOE Tuition Grant](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/tuition-grant-scheme). Bond overview: [bond matters](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/tuition-grant-scheme/bond-matters).
</OfficialSource>

## What counts as serving the bond

| Rule | MOE’s published framing |
| --- | --- |
| Length | **3 years** cumulative full-time employment after graduation |
| Start | Begin serving **immediately** after graduation; update employment status on the TG&S portal within **3 months** |
| Employers | Singapore entity employment; you may change companies if cumulative time still adds up |
| Overseas postings | Narrow — typically Singapore-entity payroll / majority-owned subsidiary / secondment paths, often with **prior MOE approval** |
| Part-time / gig | Does **not** count toward the bond |

MOE’s employment page also nudges international graduates to attach S Pass / EP eligibility letters from their institution when job-hunting — useful when recruiters fear COMPASS theatre.

## Liquidated damages — the buy-out and the failure modes

You become liable for liquidated damages if you withdraw/are terminated from the course without continuing TG, change course without taking TG on the new course, **buy out** the bond, or **fail to fulfil** bond requirements after graduation.

Published calculation shape:

- Total TG received  
- Any GST subsidy  
- **10% interest per year**, compounded at the end of each academic year  

Sureties are jointly liable under the agreement language graduates quote on Reddit. Always run MOE’s live LD figures — cohort fees differ.

## Job-loss and resignation windows (forum panic)

Agreement language commonly discussed on Reddit requires securing Singapore employment within about **one year** of graduation, and re-employment within about **six months** after leaving a job, unless MOE grants relief. Treat those clocks as **agreement-specific** — confirm on TG&S / with MOE before you resign into a gap.

Practical pairing with immigration:

- Graduate **LTVP** (seeking employment) can buy time to stay — [ICA graduate LTVP](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/apply/graduate-from-an-institute-of-higher-learning-seeking-employment-in-singapore)  
- EP cancellation still yields MOM STVP rules — [between-jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore)  
- Deferment (further study / approved overseas work) is a formal TG&S path, often with Banker’s Guarantee requirements — not a silent WhatsApp to HR  

## Decide before you sign Year 1

1. Model **full fees vs TG + 3 years locked to Singapore labour market** with today’s EP floors and FCF advertising norms — [FCF / MyCareersFuture](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep)  
2. Tell sureties the LD math in writing  
3. Start career-centre / internship pipelines early; do not wait until final exams  
4. Keep TG&S employment updates current — silent gaps create LD letters  

The Tuition Grant is a real subsidy. It is also a **labour-market bet** on Singapore still wanting to sponsor you three years later. Price that bet honestly.
