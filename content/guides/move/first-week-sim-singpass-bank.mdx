**Short answer:** You do **not** need Singpass on day one to get online or start banking. Land with a prepaid **+65** SIM, finish pass issuance / biometrics with HR, register **Singpass** once your FIN is recognised, then open the payroll account and PayNow. Treat Singpass as an accelerator — not the first lock in the chain.

Forum threads keep inventing circular dependencies (“bank needs Singpass, Singpass needs SIM, SIM needs bank”). The dependency graph is simpler if you separate **bridge** tools from **permanent** tools.

## Decision tree (if X, then Y)

| If you only have… | Do this next | Do not wait for… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport + flight | [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats) within 3 days before landing | Singpass |
| Passport at Changi | Prepaid / tourist **+65** SIM or eSIM | Postpaid contract |
| IPA, no physical card yet | Biometrics / issuance with HR; keep prepaid topped up; ask HR which bank payroll uses | Perfect long-term housing |
| Notification Letter / issued pass + FIN | Singpass registration (app + face verify when offered) | Opening every government account first |
| Local number + pass/IPA + address proof | Bank account (Singpass optional on many paths) | Credit cards |
| Local salary account | PayNow on **FIN**, then GIRO for utilities | Multi-currency “optimisation” |

## Numbered sequence that usually works

1. **Before wheels-down** — Submit the free [SG Arrival Card](/guides/sg-arrival-card-expats). Pack IPA / pass letter, passport, and any medical docs HR flagged.  
2. **Day 0–1** — Buy a prepaid SIM on passport. Save HR, relocation, and building security contacts. Load SimplyGo / contactless transit.  
3. **Issuance week** — Attend EPSC / biometrics when scheduled. MOM’s Notification Letter (after issuance) lets you work while the card prints — confirm the live window on [MOM’s EP apply page](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass/).  
4. **Singpass** — Register once FIN + Singapore mobile are ready. Prefer face verification in the app. Never share OTP or QR with “HR WhatsApp helpers” — see [scams](/guides/scams-singpass-phishing-expats).  
5. **Bank** — Open the payroll account with passport + IPA/pass + address proof. Singpass/MyInfo is the easy path; non-Singpass upload paths exist (example: [DBS foreigner docs](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/support/bank-account-opening-documents-required.html)).  
6. **PayNow + utilities** — Register PayNow on FIN, then SP / fibre once keys are real — [utilities & telecom](/guides/utilities-telecom-setup).  

Bridge money while steps 3–5 clear: foreign debit/credit, employer cheque, or a licensed remittance wallet. Do not empty foreign cards until payroll lands.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need Singpass before I can open a Singapore bank account?

**No.** Singpass speeds MyInfo autofill, but banks commonly accept passport + IPA or issued pass + proof of address without it. If a branch insists on Singpass you do not have yet, ask for the non-Singpass checklist or try the payroll bank HR already uses.

### Can I get a SIM with only IPA or passport?

**Yes for prepaid.** Postpaid staff often want a physical pass, FIN, or Singpass. That refusal is normal — keep the prepaid number alive and port later.

### What if payroll hits before my account opens?

Tell HR on day one. Options peers use: delay first credit, temporary cheque, or open at the partner bank with an HR introduction letter. Escalating through payroll beats arguing alone in a branch queue.

### Should I register PayNow to my mobile number or FIN?

Prefer **FIN** once you have it. Prepaid numbers get recycled; a FIN-linked PayNow survives SIM changes. Details: [PayNow for foreigners](/guides/paynow-setup-foreigners-singapore).
