The Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, ONE Pass to anyone who actually uses it, is MOM's personalised top-talent pass across business, arts, sports, academia and research. Two things get muddled on forums. It isn't a fancier PEP, and it doesn't hand your spouse work rights automatically. What it does do, and this is the part that genuinely changes household plans, is reopen a proper Letter of Consent route for spouses — the route ordinary EP Dependant's Pass holders largely lost after 2021.

## What it takes to qualify, and why you check the live page

MOM assesses these case by case, so read the following as the shape of the thing rather than a form you can score yourself against. The common salary track looks like this:

- A fixed monthly salary of at least S$30,000, or the foreign-currency equivalent, for 12 consecutive months before you apply, or prospective Singapore employment at that level
- An employer or company that meets the "established" tests, in the region of US$500m market cap or valuation, or around US$200m annual revenue, with combined global figures sometimes considered
- If you're applying from overseas on past employment, generally 12 months at an established overseas company

Outstanding achievements in sports, arts and culture, or academia and research can waive the salary track altogether, via a review involving MOM's partner agencies. That is a real route, not a consolation line in the guidance.

There's also an AI and Tech track with blended cash and equity rules, published to open from 28 January 2027. If that track is your whole thesis, read the live eligibility page rather than planning around what people say the draft rules will be.

## It renews, which is the real gap with PEP

ONE Pass is renewable, and it's commonly discussed in 5-year terms. That single fact is what separates it from PEP, which is a finite runway you spend once. The renewal paths include averaging S$30,000 a month over your past five years in Singapore, or running a Singapore company that employs enough locals at EP-qualifying wages. Neither is a formality, so confirm the live renewal bullets before you start treating the pass as something permanent.

## The family part people actually care about

| Family member | Typical pass | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legally married spouse | Dependant's Pass | May work / run a business with an LOC |
| Common-law spouse | LTVP | LOC path also published for ONE Pass common-law spouses |
| Unmarried children under 21 | Dependant's Pass | Other dependants generally need their own work pass |
| Parents / certain step / handicapped children | LTVP | Not a general work authorisation |

A spouse can hold **one LOC at a time**: one employer, or their own company acting as the employer. So an employed spouse who then wants to run something of their own is making a swap, not adding a second permission. The DP itself usually needs at least 3 months of validity left before an LOC filing, which catches people who leave it until the card is nearly done. Renew the DP first, file the LOC after.

And the line worth repeating, because it gets copied wrongly more than anything else on this page: ordinary EP and S Pass DP holders who want employment still need their own EP or S Pass. Advice written for a ONE Pass household doesn't transfer to yours ([Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore), [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore)).

## Moving across from an EP

**Don't cancel your EP in order to apply.** MOM's FAQ is direct about it: keep the EP until the ONE Pass is approved and you're ready to issue. The reason is that you can't work in the gap between EP cancellation and ONE Pass issuance, so cancelling early out of tidiness hands you a window in which you're legally unable to do your job.

## What the dual-career threads are really asking

When r/askSingapore dual-career threads start nominating ONE Pass, it's rarely because the poster is confident of clearing S$30k. It's because the trailing partner's work rights are the thing blocking the whole move, and ONE Pass is the one product that answers that cleanly. Understandable. Just stress-test the salary, company and outstanding-achievements tracks honestly before you build a relocation plan on the back of spouse LOC. Common-law partners sit on an LTVP with a published LOC path under ONE Pass, which is still a long way from automatic work rights.
