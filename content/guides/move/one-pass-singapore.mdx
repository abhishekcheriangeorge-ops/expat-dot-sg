ONE Pass (Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass) is MOM’s **personalised top-talent** pass across business, arts, sports, academia, and research. Forum threads often confuse it with PEP or assume “my spouse automatically gets work rights.” Neither is true — but ONE Pass **does** reopen a proper **Letter of Consent** path for spouses that ordinary EP Dependant’s Pass holders largely lost after 2021.

## Eligibility themes (verify live)

MOM assesses case-by-case. Common salary track:

- Fixed monthly salary of at least **S$30,000** (or foreign-currency equivalent) for **12 consecutive months** before application, **or** prospective Singapore employment at that level  
- Employer / company must meet “established” tests (e.g. ~**US$500m** market cap / valuation **or** ~**US$200m** annual revenue — combined global figures may be considered)  
- Overseas candidates generally need 12 months at an established overseas company when applying on past employment  

**Outstanding achievements** in sports, arts & culture, or academia/research can waive the salary track (agency review with MOM partners).

An **AI and Tech** track with blended cash / equity rules is published to open from **28 January 2027** — if that is your thesis, read the live eligibility page rather than planning on drafts.

<OfficialSource>
Full criteria: [ONE Pass eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility). Do not average two employers’ salaries to hit S$30k — MOM’s own table says the qualifying amount must come from **one** employer.
</OfficialSource>

## Renewal (unlike PEP)

ONE Pass is **renewable** (commonly discussed as **5-year** terms). Renewal paths include averaging **S$30,000**/month over the past five years in Singapore, **or** running a Singapore company that employs enough locals at EP-qualifying wages — confirm the live renewal bullets before you treat the pass as permanent.

## Family: the LOC difference peers care about

| Family member | Typical pass | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legally married spouse | Dependant’s Pass | May work / run a business with **LOC** |
| Common-law spouse | LTVP | LOC path also published for ONE Pass common-law spouses |
| Unmarried children under 21 | Dependant’s Pass | Other dependants generally need their **own work pass** |
| Parents / certain step / handicapped children | LTVP | Not a general work authorisation |

See [passes for families](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families) and [spouse working with LOC](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore). Ordinary EP DP employment LOC ended in 2021 for most jobs — do not copy ONE Pass spouse advice onto a normal EP household. Context: [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore).
</OfficialSource>

A spouse may hold **one LOC at a time** (one employer, or their own company as the “employer”). DP must usually have **≥3 months** validity before an LOC filing; renew first if the card is short. Ordinary EP/S Pass DP holders who want employer work still need their **own** EP/S Pass — do not copy ONE Pass spouse advice onto a normal EP household ([Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore), [trailing partner](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore)).

## Converting from EP

Do **not** cancel your EP to apply. MOM’s FAQ: keep the EP until the ONE Pass is approved and you are ready to issue; you cannot work in the gap between EP cancellation and ONE Pass issuance.

## Reddit dual-career heat (useful, not official)

r/askSingapore dual-career threads keep nominating ONE Pass when the **trailing partner’s work rights** are the household blocker — not because every senior engineer clears S$30k. Stress-test the salary / company / outstanding-achievements track honestly before you treat spouse LOC as a relocation plan. Common-law partners sit on **LTVP** with a published LOC path under ONE Pass — still not automatic work rights.
