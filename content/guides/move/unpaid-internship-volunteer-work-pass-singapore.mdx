**Short answer:** In Singapore, **unpaid ≠ unregulated**. Internships, trainee seats, and “just volunteering in the office” still sit on MOM’s work-pass map. A Short-Term Visit Pass is for visiting — not for quietly joining a standup. Pick the published product (**Training Employment Pass**, **Work Holiday Pass**, or another MOM path) before you accept the offer.

Reddit and Telegram are full of the same failure mode: a founder offers an “unpaid internship for experience,” the visitor treats STVP as a loophole, and HR or immigration later asks which pass authorised the work. Salary is not the only trigger — **productive work for a Singapore entity** is.

## Visit-pass myths that keep failing

| Folklore | Reality check |
| --- | --- |
| “It’s unpaid so MOM does not care” | Pass rules cover work and training, not only payroll |
| “I’m only shadowing for two weeks” | Shadowing that looks like a role still needs the right product |
| “Volunteer = automatic exemption” | Charity / event cases can still need a Misc. Work Pass — verify live |
| “DP spouse can intern for free” | Local work generally needs a separate work pass or eligible LOC |

If the host cannot name the MOM pass on the offer email, pause.

## Common lawful pathways (verify live)

1. **Training Employment Pass (TEP)** — employer-sponsored training for eligible trainees; not a tourist stamp.
2. **Work Holiday Pass (WHP)** — nationality/age/programme limits; still a work product, not STVP cosplay.
3. **Student’s Pass vacation work** — only where MOM/ICA and the school’s published rules allow; not an open internship market.
4. **Miscellaneous Work Pass** — narrow activities (including some performing / event / religious worker patterns). Do not stretch it into a three-month product internship.
5. **Own EP / S Pass / WP** — the honest path when the seat is really a job.

Deep product comparison: [Work Holiday & Training EP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass). Visit-pass clocks: [STVP extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension).

## Dependant’s Pass and “experience internships”

After **1 May 2021**, DP holders who want ordinary Singapore employment generally need their **own** work pass. Relabeling the seat as unpaid training does not restore the old LOC-for-any-job pattern. Career options for trailing partners: [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) and [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore).

## EP holders: second desks and “learning attachments”

Your EP is for the **designated employer**. A partner-company internship, customer secondment, or startup “trial week” that looks like work for another Singapore entity is not free because you already hold an EP. Get MOM/HR process — see also [side income / freelance rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass).

## Host / HR checklist before you fly

1. Name the **exact MOM pass** on the offer.
2. Confirm who files (employer vs programme operator) and the IPA/issuance timeline.
3. Do not schedule office start dates on STVP “while we sort the pass.”
4. Keep written scope: training plan vs productive headcount cover.
5. If the answer is “everyone does unpaid on tourist,” walk away.

**Decision rule:** If a Singapore entity is directing your weekday labour, you need a **work or training product** — not optimism and a return ticket.
