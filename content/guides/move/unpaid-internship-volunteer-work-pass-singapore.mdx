**Short answer:** unpaid doesn't mean unregulated here. Internships, trainee seats and "just volunteering in the office" all sit somewhere on MOM's work-pass map. A Short-Term Visit Pass is for visiting, not for quietly joining a standup. Work out which published product covers the seat — Training Employment Pass, Work Holiday Pass, or another MOM path — before you accept the offer.

Reddit and Telegram are full of the same story. A founder offers an "unpaid internship for experience", the visitor decides an STVP is close enough, and later on HR or an immigration officer asks which pass authorised the work. Salary isn't the trigger. Productive work for a Singapore entity is, whether or not anyone got paid for it.

## Visit-pass myths that keep failing

| Folklore | Reality check |
| --- | --- |
| "It's unpaid so MOM does not care" | Pass rules cover work and training, not only payroll |
| "I'm only shadowing for two weeks" | Shadowing that looks like a role still needs the right product |
| "Volunteer = automatic exemption" | Charity and event cases can still need a Misc. Work Pass, so verify live |
| "DP spouse can intern for free" | Local work generally needs a separate work pass or an eligible LOC |

If the host can't name the MOM pass in the offer email, pause there. That's much cheaper than pausing after you've booked the flight.

## The lawful pathways, in plain terms

1. The Training Employment Pass (TEP) is employer-sponsored training for eligible trainees. Somebody has to file it. It isn't a tourist stamp with a better name.
2. The Work Holiday Pass (WHP) comes with nationality, age and programme limits, and it's still a work product rather than STVP cosplay.
3. Student's Pass vacation work only exists where MOM, ICA and the school's own published rules allow it. It is not an open internship market.
4. The Miscellaneous Work Pass covers a narrow set of activities, including some performing, event and religious worker patterns. Don't stretch it over a three-month product internship.
5. Your own EP, S Pass or Work Permit is the honest answer when the seat is really a job, which it usually is if a paid hire would otherwise be doing it.

For a proper comparison of the first two, see [Work Holiday and Training EP](/guides/work-holiday-training-employment-pass). Visit-pass clocks are in [STVP extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension).

## Dependant's Pass and "experience internships"

Since 1 May 2021, a DP holder who wants ordinary employment in Singapore generally needs their own work pass. Relabelling the seat as unpaid training doesn't bring back the old pattern where an LOC covered any job, because what matters is the work being done rather than what the arrangement is called or whether money changes hands. If you're a trailing partner sizing up the local market, we've written up [trailing partner careers](/guides/trailing-partner-career-singapore) and the wider [Dependant's Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore) picture.

## EP holders: second desks and learning attachments

Your EP ties you to one designated employer. A partner-company internship, a customer secondment, or a startup "trial week" that looks like work for a different Singapore entity isn't free just because you already hold a pass. Take it through MOM and HR process properly, and read [side income and freelance rules](/guides/side-income-freelance-work-pass) for the adjacent cases.

## What to settle with the host before you fly

1. Get the exact MOM pass named on the offer, in writing.
2. Confirm who files it, the employer or the programme operator, and what the IPA and issuance timeline looks like.
3. **Don't agree to an office start date on an STVP** while everyone "sorts the pass out". That is the sentence the whole problem hides behind.
4. Keep the scope in writing. A training plan reads very differently from productive headcount cover if anyone asks about it later.
5. If the answer you get is "everyone does unpaid on tourist", walk away. That's not reassurance, it's a warning.

The decision rule is simple enough. If a Singapore entity is directing your weekday labour, you need a work or training product. Optimism and a return ticket aren't one.
