**Short answer:** Read your own IPA. If it requires a medical after arrival, get it done in Singapore and have the results ready for HR to upload at EP eService issuance. Don't assume the generic Work Permit `medical_form.pdf` from a web search applies to you, because it usually doesn't. And if the labs are slow, HR has to request the STVP extension **7–14 days before expiry** with a doctor's memo, not after you've already overstayed.

r/askSingapore keeps folding three different things into one: IPA-required EP medicals, Work Permit medical PDFs, and employer pre-employment screens done for insurance or fitness. Only the first of those unlocks issuance, and only your IPA tells you whether it applies to you.

## When the IPA says medical

MOM's apply page puts it on the IPA: your letter states whether you need a medical examination after arrival. When the employer goes to issue the pass, the uploads often include a completed medical examination form or a medical declaration form, along with the IPA declaration signed by authorised HR.

| Document | Role |
| --- | --- |
| IPA | Says whether a medical is required, and gives you 6 months to enter and get the pass issued |
| EP medical or declaration form | The evidence HR uploads when issuing the pass in EP eService |
| Employer pre-employment screen | A separate HR or insurance product, and not automatically a MOM form |
| WP medical PDF off a web search | Usually the wrong pass type entirely. Discard it unless HR explicitly confirms otherwise |

<Callout title="Form mismatch is the #1 delay">
If HR emails a PDF that looks like a Work Permit medical and your IPA never listed it, push back in writing. Wrong forms burn clinic fees and STVP calendar days.
</Callout>

## The clinic run, from the candidate's side

1. Confirm the exact form first, from the IPA attachment or HR's pack, and from nowhere else.
2. Book a clinic that can do the chest X-ray and HIV panel on site, same day if possible. SATA, the large GP chains, and employer panel clinics are the usual peer shortlist, but call before you go rather than turning up hopefully.
3. Bring your passport, your IPA, and a local mobile number. Results often need a separate pickup day, and someone will need to reach you.
4. Ask who pays before you tap your card. Plenty of employers reimburse and some pay the panel clinic directly, but you want that answer in writing beforehand, not in an expenses argument later.
5. Hand the results or sealed forms to HR the same day they're ready, so issuance can move while your STVP is still valid.

Personal medicines you're bringing through Customs are a different guide altogether: [HSA personal medication](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa).

## Privacy and pre-existing conditions

Forum heat on this topic tends to mix the TB chest X-ray up with a much broader worry, which is whether MOM or HR ends up seeing your entire medical history. Worth pulling those apart.

The screens are oriented to fitness and public health — TB, HIV, and the clinical exam systems listed on the form. They aren't an underwriting dump. If you have a known remission and you'd rather have context on file, the usual approach is to bring an English specialist letter on letterhead, not to go quiet on the form's questions. And employer group insurance underwriting is a separate exercise from the MOM issuance medical, which we cover in the [health insurance audit](/guides/health-insurance-expat).

## STVP about to expire while the labs grind

MOM's apply page lets employers request an STVP extension 7–14 days before expiry when a candidate is waiting on medical results, uploading a doctor's memo with the collection date. MOM's own worked example: if your STVP expires 17 Feb, the request window is roughly 4–10 Feb. Late requests risk overstay fines, and that window is narrow enough that "HR will get to it" isn't a plan.

## Quick decision table

| If… | Then… |
| --- | --- |
| Your IPA says a medical isn't required | Follow HR's declaration path, and don't invent a WP medical |
| Your IPA says a medical is required | Book a Singapore clinic now, not in week five of a six-month IPA |
| Results are running past your STVP | HR files the MOM STVP extension inside the 7–14 day window |
| HR sends you a WP medical PDF | Ask for the EP-specific form, or confirmation from the IPA attachment |
| You're appealing, or the IPA never issued | The medical is irrelevant until there's an approval: [EP rejection / appeal](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance) |
