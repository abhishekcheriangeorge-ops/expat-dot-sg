**Short answer:** Read **your IPA**. If it requires a medical after arrival, complete it in Singapore, keep results ready for EP eService issuance, and do **not** assume the generic Work Permit `medical_form.pdf` from a web search applies. If labs are slow, HR must extend the STVP **7–14 days before expiry** with a doctor’s memo — not after you overstay.

r/askSingapore keeps conflating three different things: IPA-required EP medicals, Work Permit medical PDFs, and employer pre-employment screens for insurance/fitness. Only the first unlocks issuance.

## IPA says medical — what that actually means

MOM’s apply page: the IPA states whether the candidate needs a **medical examination after arrival**. Issuance uploads often include a **completed medical examination form** or **medical declaration form** plus the IPA declaration signed by authorised HR.

| Document | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **IPA** | Says medical required or not; gives **6 months** to enter and get the pass issued |
| **EP medical / declaration** | Evidence HR uploads when issuing the pass in EP eService |
| **Employer pre-employment screen** | Separate HR/insurance product — not automatically a MOM form |
| **WP medical PDF from Google** | Frequently the **wrong** pass type — discard unless HR explicitly confirms |

<Callout title="Form mismatch is the #1 delay">
If HR emails a PDF that looks like a Work Permit medical and your IPA never listed it, push back in writing. Wrong forms burn clinic fees and STVP calendar days.
</Callout>

## Practical clinic sequence (candidate)

1. **Confirm the exact form** — IPA attachment or HR pack only.  
2. **Book a clinic with chest X-ray + HIV panel on site** the same day when possible (SATA / large GP chains / employer panel clinics are the usual peer shortlist — call first).  
3. **Bring passport + IPA + local mobile** — results often need a pickup day.  
4. **Ask who pays** — many employers reimburse; some pay the panel clinic directly. Get the answer before you tap your card.  
5. **Hand results / sealed forms to HR the same day they are ready** so issuance can proceed while you are still on a valid STVP.

Personal meds you need to bring through Customs are a different guide: [HSA personal medication](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa).

## Privacy and pre-existing conditions

Forum heat often mixes TB chest X-rays with “will MOM/HR see my entire history?” Reality check:

- Screens are typically **fitness / public-health** oriented (TB, HIV, clinical exam systems listed on the form).  
- Unrelated remissions are usually handled by bringing an **English specialist letter** on letterhead if you want context ready — not by hiding from the form’s questions.  
- Employer **group insurance** underwriting is separate from MOM issuance medicals — see [health insurance audit](/guides/health-insurance-expat).

## STVP about to expire while labs process

MOM’s apply page allows employers to request an **STVP extension 7–14 days before expiry** when candidates are waiting for medical results, uploading a **doctor’s memo** with the collection date. Example from MOM: if STVP expires 17 Feb, the request window is roughly **4–10 Feb**. Late requests risk overstay fines.
