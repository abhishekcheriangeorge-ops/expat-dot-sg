**Short answer:** Book a **licensed serviced apartment** (or hotel) for the first **2–8 weeks** when pass timing, school, or neighbourhood fit is still soft. It is one of the few **URA-legal** short-stay paths and usually gives you a usable address for SIM, banking, and pass issuance — unlike illegal condo Airbnb-style lets.

Serviced apartments buy you time: furnished, utilities often included, flexible tenancy — at a premium versus an annual condo lease.

## When they are worth it

- IPA timing uncertain or role still soft  
- School decision open (waitlist / campus choice)  
- You want to walk neighbourhoods at night before committing  
- Employer covers a 2–6 week landing package  
- Pets, helpers, or furniture are still mid-transit  

## Cost vs lease (planning heuristic)

| Path | Cash pattern | Risk you buy down |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serviced 2–8 weeks | High nightly / monthly rate; low exit friction | Wrong neighbourhood, bad school-run, illegal short-let fines |
| Rushed 24-month condo | Lower monthly; high break cost | Diplomatic-clause fights, deposit drama |
| Hybrid | Serviced bridge → proper LOI/TA | Most families’ sweet spot |

Use the serviced stay to run a real search: agent shortlist, night-time noise, school-run timing, condo pet/renovation rules, and utilities ownership. Do not sign a 24-month lease jet-lagged on day three.

## Address, banks, and MOM

A licensed serviced residence usually gives you a **usable Singapore address** for SIM, bank onboarding, and pass admin while you hunt. Illegal Airbnb-style condo stays often fail those checks and create FWTES / landlord-consent dead ends when you later try to update MOM.

Keep:

- Booking confirmation with full unit address  
- A plan for the day you move into the long-term lease (meter photos, address update, fibre)

## Transition checklist

1. Shortlist 2–3 neighbourhoods that match school + commute (see [choosing a neighbourhood](/guides/choosing-neighbourhood-expat))  
2. Engage an agent; understand LOI / Option / TA deposit stack  
3. Verify diplomatic clause and pet clauses **in writing**  
4. Book movers for one clean cutover; avoid overlapping rents longer than one week if cash is tight  
5. Update MOM address promptly once the long-term unit is live
