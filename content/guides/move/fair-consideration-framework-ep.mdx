If you're hunting a Singapore EP from overseas, LinkedIn will sooner or later dump you onto MyCareersFuture (MCF), and someone local will tell you that half the ads on it are "for MOM." The Fair Consideration Framework behind those ads is real policy rather than a meme, and misreading it wastes months of false hope.

## What FCF actually requires

Before most Employment Pass or S Pass applications, the employer has to do five things. It's worth knowing all five, because each one has a failure mode that lands on you rather than on them.

1. Advertise the vacancy on MyCareersFuture.
2. Keep the ad open for at least 14 consecutive days.
3. Fairly consider the candidates who apply, with no nationality, race, age or sex filter doing the shortlisting.
4. Not make a job offer during that mandatory advertising window.
5. Make sure the work-pass application that follows matches the ad on employer, occupation, salary and vacancy count, and quotes the Job Advertisement ID.

## When the ad gets skipped (fair hiring still applies)

Advertising on MCF generally isn't required when one of these applies. MOM's live list is the one that counts, not this table.

| Exemption | What it means for you as a candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed monthly salary of S$22,500 or above | Senior and specialist roles often skip the public ad entirely |
| Employer has fewer than 10 employees | Small firms may never appear on MCF for the EP path at all |
| Role of 1 month or less | Short cover, not your relocation |
| Eligible intra-company transferee (ICT) | An internal-move track with its own EP constraints |

Raising the salary exemption to S$22,500 (from 1 Sep 2023) was explicitly benchmarked to roughly the top decile of EP holders, which tells you how narrow it's meant to be. If your package is nowhere near that and HR still says no ad is needed, it's fair to ask which exemption they're relying on. The awkward edges are picked apart in [FCF advertising exemption edges](/guides/fcf-advertising-exemption-edges-ep-singapore).

## How to read MCF as a foreign candidate

A handful of signals come up over and over, and each has a safer reading than the one people jump to.

A recruiter who points you from LinkedIn to "apply on MyCareersFuture" may simply be running the mandatory FCF clock. It isn't a polite way of saying locals only, forever.

The Singpass-gated apply flow is portal design, not a verdict. The login favours citizens and PRs, which is why many foreigners end up applying through the employer's own ATS instead.

Treat the salary range on the ad as a negotiation floor rather than folklore. For an EP, the declared pay should track the advertised role, so that band is one of the few numbers you can genuinely hold an employer to later.

A role that's visibly heading to a named internal transferee from overseas may be ICT-exempt, in which case the MCF ad you're looking at is HR caution rather than open competition.

Being ghosted after applying as a foreigner is common, and it tells you almost nothing. FCF doesn't entitle you to an interview. It obliges the employer toward a fair process, which is a different thing.

The r/askSingapore line that every MCF post is fake describes a compliance risk MOM polices, not permission for you to assume the job is closed. Sham advertising and discriminatory shortlists can get a firm debarred from hiring foreigners at all, which is precisely why serious HR still runs real screens.

## What this means for your EP timeline

1. Even with a verbal offer in hand, expect at least 14 days of advertising before the EP goes in, unless an exemption applies. The clock itself is broken down in [the FCF MyCareersFuture 14-day clock](/guides/fcf-mycareersfuture-14-day-clock-ep-singapore).
2. COMPASS still applies after FCF, so clearing the ad window doesn't mean you're through. Start with [COMPASS explained](/guides/compass-framework-explained) and [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore).
3. Moving jobs mid-contract means the new employer repeats all of it, FCF and COMPASS both. That's in [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).
4. If the salary on the IPA is mysteriously below the MCF band you screenshotted, ask HR to reconcile it **before you resign overseas**. Afterwards you have no leverage and a plane ticket.

## A hunt sequence that works

1. Target employers who already hire your specialty on EP, rather than any MCF listing with a matching title.
2. Apply through company careers pages and LinkedIn, and treat MCF as intelligence on budget and title.
3. Ask recruiters the blunt version of the question: has the FCF advertising period completed, and is the role EP-sponsorable under current COMPASS?
4. Keep a written salary range that matches what will actually be declared to MOM, which is what [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding) is for.

FCF is a labour-market transparency tool for Singapore. It isn't a foreigner job board, and it isn't proof that sponsorship is impossible. Read the ad, then go and check the sponsor's COMPASS math.
