If you are overseas hunting a Singapore EP, LinkedIn will eventually dump you onto **MyCareersFuture (MCF)**. Locals joke that half the ads are “for MOM.” The Fair Consideration Framework (FCF) is real policy — not a meme — and misunderstanding it wastes months of false hope.

## What FCF actually requires

Before most **Employment Pass** or **S Pass** applications, employers must:

1. Advertise the vacancy on **MyCareersFuture**  
2. Keep the ad open at least **14 consecutive days**  
3. **Fairly consider** candidates (no nationality / race / age / sex discrimination as a hiring filter)  
4. **Not** make a job offer during that mandatory advertising window  
5. Ensure the later work-pass application **matches** the ad (employer, occupation, salary, vacancy count) and quote the Job Advertisement ID

## Common exemptions (still “fair hiring” required)

Advertising on MCF is generally **not** required when any of these apply (verify live MOM list):

| Exemption | Practical reading for candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed monthly salary ≥ **S$22,500** | Senior / specialist roles often skip the public ad theatre |
| Employer has **&lt; 10** employees | Small firms may not appear on MCF for the EP path |
| Role ≤ **1 month** | Short cover — not your relocation |
| Eligible **intra-company transferee (ICT)** | Internal move track with its own EP constraints |

Raising the salary exemption to S$22,500 (from 1 Sep 2023) was explicitly benchmarked to roughly the top decile of EP holders.

## How foreign candidates should read MCF

| Signal | Safer interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| “Apply on MyCareersFuture” from LinkedIn | Employer may be running the **mandatory** FCF clock — not necessarily “locals only forever” |
| Singpass-gated apply flow | Portal UX favours SC/PR login; many foreigners apply via the employer’s **own** ATS instead |
| Salary range on the ad | For EP, declared pay should track the advertised role — use it as a **negotiation floor**, not folklore |
| Role filled by a named overseas internal transfer | May be ICT-exempt; MCF ad might be HR caution, not open competition |
| Ghosted after applying as a foreigner | Common; FCF does not entitle you to an interview — it obliges the **employer** toward fair process |

r/askSingapore anecdotes that “every MCF post is fake” describe a **compliance risk MOM polices**, not a licence for you to assume the job is closed. Sham advertising and discriminatory shortlists can get firms debarred from hiring foreigners — which is why serious HR still runs real screens.

## What this means for your EP timeline

1. Even with a verbal offer, expect **≥14 days** of advertising before EP submission (unless exempt).  
2. COMPASS still applies after FCF — see [COMPASS explained](/guides/compass-framework-explained) and [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore).  
3. Job-hopping mid-contract means the **new** employer repeats FCF + COMPASS — [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore).  
4. If salary on the IPA is mysteriously below the MCF band you screenshotted, ask HR to reconcile before you resign overseas.

## Practical hunt sequence

1. Target employers who already hire your specialty on EP (not “any MCF listing”).  
2. Apply on **company careers pages** + LinkedIn; treat MCF as intelligence on **budget and title**.  
3. Ask recruiters explicitly: “Has the FCF advertising period completed, and is the role EP-sponsorable under current COMPASS?”  
4. Keep a written salary range that matches what will be declared to MOM — [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding).

FCF is a labour-market transparency tool for Singapore. It is not a foreigner job board — and it is not proof that sponsorship is impossible. Read the ad, then verify the **sponsor’s** COMPASS math.
