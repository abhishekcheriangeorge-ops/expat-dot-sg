**Short answer:** The **SG Arrival Card (SGAC)** is ICA’s free electronic arrival + health declaration. Submit it **within three days before you land** (including arrival day). It is **not a visa**. IPA holders use the visitor/IPA path; already-issued work/Dependant/Student/LTVP holders use the resident path — and land-checkpoint residents have a narrow exemption.

Stressed movers lose time at Changi because a paid “arrival card agent” DM’d them on Instagram, or because they filed two weeks early and assumed it was still valid. ICA’s rules are short; follow those, not the reseller.

## Who must submit

Per [ICA’s SG Arrival Card page](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card):

| Traveller | SGAC required? |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign visitor (tourism, business visit, etc.) | **Yes** before immigration clearance |
| IPA letter holder entering to take up a pass | **Yes** (visitor / IPA submission option) |
| Work Pass / DP / Student Pass / LTVP holder arriving by **air or sea** | **Yes** (resident health-declaration path) |
| SC / PR / long-term pass holder via **Woodlands or Tuas** | **Exempt** |
| Pure airside transit — no immigration clearance | **Exempt** |

Long-term passes in ICA’s wording include Student’s Pass, Dependant’s Pass, Long-Term Visit Pass, and Work Pass.

## When to submit (the 3-day clock)

1. Count **three calendar days including arrival day**.  
2. Example from ICA: arrive **30 Jun** → earliest submission **28 Jun**.  
3. Submit via the official **SGAC e-Service** or **MyICA** app.  
4. Keep the acknowledgement email; present your travel document at the counter.  

ICA does **not** endorse paid third-party submission services. If someone charges you for “priority SGAC,” walk away.

## IPA vs issued pass — pick the right door

| Status at boarding | What to select on SGAC | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPA only | Foreign visitor / IPA holder flow | You are still entering to activate a pass |
| Issued work / Dependant / Student / LTVP | Resident / long-term pass holder flow | Health declaration still applies for air/sea |
| SC or PR | Resident flow (unless land-checkpoint exempt) | Same disease-control declaration |

If your flight changes after submission, use ICA’s **Update SGAC** feature. False health declarations can be prosecuted under the Infectious Diseases Act — update rather than “hope nobody notices.”

## What SGAC does *not* replace

- **Visa** (if your nationality needs one)  
- **IPA / work pass issuance** with MOM  
- **CBNI** cash declaration if you carry large amounts — [CBNI guide](/guides/cbni-cash-declaration-singapore)  
- **HSA** approval for controlled personal medication — [bringing medication](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa)  

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the SG Arrival Card?

ICA’s electronic replacement for the old paper white disembarkation card, bundled with an electronic health declaration for disease control.

### Do tourists and new EP holders both need it?

**Yes**, before immigration clearance (with the transit and land-checkpoint exceptions above). New EP movers on IPA still file — usually under the visitor/IPA option.

### Can one person file for the whole family?

ICA’s **Group Submission** is for people travelling together on **one trip**. Each traveller’s particulars still need to be correct; do not invent a group just to reuse one email.

### What if my health status changes after I submit?

Update via **Update SGAC** on the official e-Service. Do not board on a stale false declaration.

### What should I do immediately after clearing immigration?

Get a +65 SIM, message HR about issuance/biometrics, and follow the [first-week SIM → Singpass → bank map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank). Tick the interactive [day-7 checklist](/journeys/arriving/day-7).

## What happens next

SGAC gets you through the hall. The rest of arriving is dependency work: pass card, Singpass, payroll bank, temporary housing letter. Stay on the [Arriving hub](/arriving) until those four stop blocking each other.
