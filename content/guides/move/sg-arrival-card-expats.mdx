**Short answer:** The SG Arrival Card (SGAC) is ICA's free electronic arrival and health declaration. Submit it within three days before you land, counting arrival day itself. It is **not a visa**. IPA holders use the visitor/IPA path, already-issued work, Dependant, Student and LTVP holders use the resident path, and residents crossing at the land checkpoints have a narrow exemption.

People lose time at Changi over this for two reasons, and both are avoidable. Either a paid "arrival card agent" found them on Instagram and sold them something that is free, or they filed two weeks early and assumed it would still be good on the day. ICA's rules here are genuinely short. Follow those and ignore the reseller.

## Who has to submit one

Straight from [ICA's SG Arrival Card page](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card):

| Traveller | Do you submit? |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign visitor, whether tourism or a business trip | Yes, before immigration clearance |
| IPA letter holder arriving to take up a pass | Yes, using the visitor / IPA submission option |
| Work Pass, DP, Student Pass or LTVP holder arriving by air or sea | Yes, on the resident health-declaration path |
| SC or PR or long-term pass holder coming through Woodlands or Tuas | Exempt |
| Pure airside transit with no immigration clearance | Exempt |

Long-term passes, in ICA's wording, take in the Student's Pass, the Dependant's Pass, the Long-Term Visit Pass and the Work Pass. So if you're wondering whether yours counts, it almost certainly does.

## The three-day clock

Count three calendar days including arrival day. ICA's own worked example: you arrive 30 Jun, and the earliest you can submit is 28 Jun. That window is tighter than most people assume, which is exactly why the keen fortnight-ahead filing doesn't help anyone. Put a reminder in your phone instead of trusting yourself to remember it in the middle of a move.

Submit through the official SGAC e-Service or the MyICA app, keep the acknowledgement email, and have your travel document ready at the counter. ICA doesn't endorse paid third-party submission services. If someone is charging you for "priority SGAC," they are charging you for nothing.

## IPA or issued pass: pick the right door

| Where you stand at boarding | What to select on SGAC | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPA only | The foreign visitor / IPA holder flow | You're still entering in order to activate a pass |
| Issued work, Dependant, Student or LTVP | The resident / long-term pass holder flow | The health declaration still applies for air and sea |
| SC or PR | The resident flow, unless the land-checkpoint exemption covers you | Same disease-control declaration either way |

If your flight changes after you've submitted, use ICA's Update SGAC feature rather than leaving a stale form sitting in the system. A false health declaration can be prosecuted under the Infectious Diseases Act, which makes hoping nobody notices a poor plan for a five-minute fix.

## What SGAC doesn't replace

It's an arrival declaration and nothing more. It isn't a visa if your nationality needs one, and it has no bearing on MOM issuing your IPA or your work pass. It also doesn't cover the two declarations new arrivals most often forget: the [CBNI cash declaration](/guides/cbni-cash-declaration-singapore) if you're carrying large amounts, and [HSA approval](/guides/bringing-medication-singapore-hsa) for controlled personal medication.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the SG Arrival Card?

ICA's electronic replacement for the old paper white disembarkation card, with an electronic health declaration bundled in for disease control.

### Do tourists and new EP holders both need it?

Yes, both, before immigration clearance, with the transit and land-checkpoint exceptions above. If you're moving here on an IPA you still file, usually under the visitor/IPA option.

### Can one person file for the whole family?

ICA's Group Submission is built for people travelling together on one trip, so a family flying in together is fine. Each traveller's particulars still have to be correct, and it isn't a way to invent a group so that everyone can reuse one email address.

### What if my health status changes after I submit?

Update it through Update SGAC on the official e-Service. Don't board on a stale declaration that you already know is wrong.

### What should I do immediately after clearing immigration?

Get a +65 SIM, message HR about issuance and biometrics, and work down the [first-week SIM, Singpass and bank map](/guides/first-week-sim-singpass-bank). The interactive [day-7 checklist](/journeys/arriving/day-7) keeps the order straight so you don't have to hold it in your head.

## What happens next

SGAC gets you through the arrivals hall, and that is the easy part. Everything after it is dependency work: the pass card, Singpass, a payroll bank account, a letter proving where you're temporarily living. Each of them tends to want one of the others first.
