Singapore’s Employment Pass (EP) is the primary work pass for foreign professionals, managers, and executives. It is issued by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM), tied to a specific employer, and sits at the centre of most corporate relocations.

## Who the EP is for

The EP is aimed at mid-to-senior talent. MOM assesses both the individual (qualifications, experience, salary) and the employer’s hiring context through the Complementary Assessment Framework — commonly called COMPASS.

You typically need:

- A job offer from a Singapore-registered company that can sponsor the pass
- Fixed monthly salary at or above the prevailing EP minimum for your **age band and sector**
- Relevant qualifications and/or professional experience

## Qualifying salary (Stage 1) — the number Reddit gets wrong

Stage 1 is an absolute floor. Fail it and COMPASS points do not save you. Orientation from MOM’s eligibility tables (re-check before you sign):

| Sector | Youngest-band floor (current new apps) | Age 45+ band | Next published step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All except financial services | **S$5,600**/mo | **S$10,700**/mo | **S$6,000** / **S$11,500** — new apps from **1 Jan 2027**; renewals of passes expiring from **1 Jan 2028** |
| Financial services | **S$6,200**/mo | **S$11,800**/mo | **S$6,600** / **S$12,700** on the same clocks |

Salary rises **progressively with age from 23**. A signed S$5,000 offer after the **1 Jan 2025** new-application step is a common r/askSingapore failure mode — especially when HR’s draft still “passes” an outdated SAT screenshot. **New applications** and **renewals** move on different dates; do not assume your renewal still sits on last year’s floor.

Fixed monthly salary is what counts for the floor. Variable bonuses and many allowances do not patch a shortfall. Side-by-side with S Pass quota/levy: [EP vs S Pass comparison](/guides/ep-vs-s-pass-comparison).

## COMPASS in one paragraph

COMPASS scores candidates on individual factors (salary relative to local peers, qualifications) and firm-related factors (diversity, local employment support). Points below the passing score generally mean rejection unless a clear exemption applies. Treat COMPASS as part of offer design — not an afterthought once you have already resigned overseas.

Before most EP submissions, employers must also run the **Fair Consideration Framework** clock — typically advertising on MyCareersFuture for **≥14 days** unless an exemption (e.g. ≥ S$22,500 fixed monthly salary, small firm, ICT) applies. How candidates should read those ads: [FCF & MyCareersFuture](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep).

## Application flow

1. Employer (or appointed employment agent) submits via EP Online
2. MOM may request clarification or additional documents
3. In-principle approval (IPA) letter is issued if successful
4. You complete formalities in Singapore (card issuance) after arrival or when eligible

Processing times vary. Build contingency into resignation and shipping dates; do not assume a fixed calendar week.

Rejected, stuck on appeal, or confused by IPA medical / card clocks? Read [EP rejection, appeal & IPA issuance](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-ipa-issuance) — only the employer can appeal, and you have a **3-month** window from rejection.

## After you land

Once your pass is issued:

- Carry your pass card (or digital equivalent when accepted) as required
- Update banking, telco, and landlord records with your FIN
- Register for Singpass once eligible — it unlocks most government and bank digital services (FIN first; IPA alone often fails registration)
- Keep residential address / mobile / passport particulars current — **5-day** address/mobile clock and passport auto-gate lag are covered in [notify MOM of EP changes](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport)
- Note the pass expiry early; renewals are a living-mode problem, not a last-week scramble

## Changing employers (or switching while an EP is still pending)

Deep playbook: [changing jobs on an Employment Pass](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass). Short version:

- Already on an EP: the **new** employer applies for a new pass; **resign after IPA**; cancel the old pass when the new one is issued — see MOM’s change-jobs FAQ and [career pivots](/guides/career-pivots-singapore)
- Still pending / IPA with Company A when Company B appears: you generally cannot park two live EP outcomes indefinitely — ask A to **withdraw** before B files, or you risk messy dual filings
- Job change still triggers **IR21 tax clearance** with the leaving employer even if you stay in Singapore — budget withheld final pay ([tax clearance](/guides/tax-clearance-when-leaving))

## Leaving or between jobs (cancel → STVP)

When employment ends, the **employer** cancels the EP (within a week after last day; advance cancel up to 14 days is allowed). If you remain in Singapore, MOM’s cancel page expects an employer-requested **Short-Term Visit Pass** for valid stay — **up to 90 days** from the application date. Ask HR **in writing** before cancel day; default HR behaviour is often a shorter window. STVP is **not** work authorisation. Dependants cascade off the main pass — [Dependant’s Pass](/guides/dependant-pass-singapore). Full ops (IR21, travel, graduate LTVP): [between jobs on EP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## Common friction points

- **Package structure:** Allowances that are not “fixed monthly salary” may not count toward the EP floor
- **Age band vs offer:** mid-30s / mid-40s candidates need higher floors than the youngest-band headline
- **Job title vs reality:** MOM looks at substance; inflated titles without matching duties invite scrutiny
- **Dependent timing:** Family passes usually follow the main pass — sequence housing and school deposits accordingly
