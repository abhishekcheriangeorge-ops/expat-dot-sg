r/askSingapore fills with “HR said MOM rejected my EP — what are my chances if we appeal?” and “offer retracted after non-rectifiable rejection.” The hard rule: **you are not a party of record**. Only the employer or authorised agent who filed the application can enquire or appeal. Your job is to feed them evidence, not to email MOM yourself and expect a personal caseworker.

## Rejection is usually an advisory, not a mystery novel

MOM’s published appeal page tells employers to read the **rejection advisory** in EP eService (or the rejection letter for manual filings). Appeal only if you can **address those issues with new information**. No new facts → no outcome change.

Common rectifiable buckets that match forum heat and MOM’s own “supporting docs not needed if already fixed” list:

| Pattern | What usually needs fixing |
| --- | --- |
| Salary / COMPASS miss | Raise fixed monthly salary; re-check SAT; fix firm diversity / support factors |
| Qualification mismatch | Align degree campus / verification proof with what was keyed |
| FCF advertising gap | Complete ≥14 days on MyCareersFuture unless an exemption applies |
| IRAS / employer tax hygiene | Employer clears outstanding tax admin flagged in the advisory |
| Job / title vs substance | Rewrite role description to match actual PME duties |

Non-rectifiable outcomes (fraud, prior immigration bans, undisclosed history) are why some HR emails say MOM “cannot disclose.” Treat that as a closed door for *this* application — not a cue to invent a second identity.

## Before you resign on an “appeal pending” vibe

Candidate-side checklist (advisory → appeal vs refile → do not resign early): [EP rejection appeal playbook](/guides/ep-rejection-appeal-candidate-playbook-singapore).

1. Ask HR for a **screenshot of the SAT** at the offered salary and the rejection advisory text — not a paraphrase.  
2. Confirm FCF was run correctly — see [Fair Consideration Framework](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep).  
3. Re-read [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) with the exact job code and firm factors, not LinkedIn optimism.  
4. Keep housing / school deposits refundable until IPA is in hand — see [decoding your offer](/guides/decoding-job-offer-singapore).

A company that will not share the advisory is not ready to sponsor an appeal. Walk.

## IPA is not the plastic card

When approval lands, the **in-principle approval (IPA)** letter is a **pre-approved single-entry visa** giving the candidate **6 months** to enter Singapore and **get the pass issued**. It also states whether a **medical examination** is required after arrival — clinic forms, WP-PDF myths, and STVP extensions while labs process live on [EP medical for issuance](/guides/ep-medical-examination-issuance).

Do **not** fly the family on “application submitted.” MOM’s apply page is explicit: bring candidates only when the pass is approved — visit-pass extensions are not a safety net for premature arrivals.

### IPA reassessment / “postpone your arrival”

r/askSingapore threads describe employers suddenly emailing: MOM asked to **postpone arrival** because the work-pass application needs **reassessment**. That usually means MOM is reviewing again after new information — it is **not** automatic rejection, but the IPA **can still be revoked**.

Candidate playbook:

1. Ask HR for the **exact MOM wording** (email/screenshot), not a paraphrase.  
2. **Do not fly**; freeze non-refundable housing/school deposits where you can.  
3. Only the employer/agent can chase MOM — you cannot casework the reassessment yourself.  
4. Treat “HR says MOM is delaying” with healthy scepticism if the company also goes quiet on start dates — insist on written status.  

Until reassessment clears in writing, treat the IPA as **frozen**, not travel-ready.

## Issue the pass → notification letter → EPSC → card

Missed biometrics slots and letter extensions: [EPSC missed slot / Notification Letter extension](/guides/epsc-missed-slot-notification-letter-extension-singapore).

| Step | Clock (verify live) | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get pass issued in EP eService | Within **6 months** of IPA; candidate must be **in Singapore** | Employer / EA |
| Fees at issuance | **S$225** per pass (+ **S$30** MJV if applicable) | Employer pays |
| Notification letter | Valid **1 month** — start work / travel while waiting for card | Email to both sides |
| Fingerprints / photo at EPSC | Within **2 weeks** if the letter requires it | Candidate appointment |
| Physical card delivery | ~**5 working days** after registration / doc check | Authorised recipients |

Issuance uploads often include passport arrival stamp page, medical form or medical declaration, and the IPA declaration signed by authorised HR. Address must meet housing requirements — FWTES / landlord blockers belong in the [short-term vs long-term rental](/guides/short-term-vs-long-term-rental) guide, not as “MOM will understand.” Candidate-facing clocks for the Notification Letter, EPSC bag list, and card delivery live on [work pass issuance → EPSC](/guides/work-pass-issuance-epsc-notification).

### Medical results stuck / STVP about to expire

If issuance is waiting on lab results, employers can request an **STVP extension 7–14 days before expiry** with a **doctor’s memo** stating the results collection date. Late requests risk overstay fines. That MOM path is not the same as ICA tourist visit-pass extensions — see [visit-pass extension](/guides/short-term-visit-pass-extension) and [between jobs STVP](/guides/between-jobs-stvp-singapore).

## What candidates should push HR to do

- Run / re-run the [Self-Assessment Tool](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) before refiling  
- File appeal **inside 3 months** with only the documents the advisory asks for (extra noise slows processing)  
- Keep written proof the appeal was actually submitted — Reddit “HR said they uploaded” is not status  

Lost card *after* issuance is a different playbook: [replace work pass card](/guides/lost-replace-work-pass-card).
