COMPASS is MOM’s points-based Stage 2 for most Employment Pass applications. Stage 1 is still the **EP qualifying salary**. Fail Stage 1 and COMPASS points do not matter.

Reddit heat (r/askSingapore) clusters on: “Is my offer realistic?”, “Does my university campus count for 20 points?”, “How long is pending after COMPASS?”, and “HR said no EP quota” (see [EP vs S Pass](/guides/ep-vs-s-pass-comparison) — EP has no quota).

## Pass mark

You generally need **40 points** across individual attributes, firm attributes, and optional bonuses.

| Criterion | What it measures | Points (orientation) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C1 Salary** | Fixed monthly salary vs local PMET benchmarks **by sector and age** | 20 / 10 / 0 at ≥90th / 65–&lt;90th / &lt;65th percentile |
| **C2 Qualifications** | Degree-equivalent credibility | 20 top-tier list / 10 other recognised degree-equivalent / 0 none |
| **C3 Diversity** | Your nationality’s share of the firm’s PMETs | 20 / 10 / 0 at &lt;5% / 5–&lt;25% / ≥25% |
| **C4 Local employment** | Firm’s local PMET share vs sector peers | 20 / 10 / 0 at ≥50th / 20–&lt;50th / &lt;20th percentile |
| **C5 Skills bonus (SOL)** | Job on Shortage Occupation List | +20 or +10 (reduced if nationality share ≥ 1/3 of PMETs) |
| **C6 SEP bonus** | Firm on Strategic Economic Priorities pathways | +10 |

Small firms (&lt;25 PMETs) often score a default **10** on C3 and C4 — useful context when joining a tiny startup vs a large bank.

## C1 is not the qualifying salary

Two different salary concepts trip candidates:

1. **EP qualifying salary** — absolute floor (age-banded; e.g. non-FS from **S$5,600** at the youngest band up to **S$10,700** at 45+, with financial services higher — see [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore))
2. **C1 COMPASS salary** — where your fixed pay sits versus **sector percentile benchmarks** (updated annually; e.g. C1 tables released for new apps in a calendar year and renewals on a staggered window)

Clearing the floor with 0 C1 points is common. You then need points from qualifications, firm diversity/local mix, and/or SOL/SEP bonuses. Candidates earning at least **S$22,500** fixed monthly salary are generally **exempt from COMPASS** — verify the live eligibility page.

### New application vs renewal clocks

MOM often announces floor and C1 updates **months ahead**, then applies them first to **new** applications and later to **renewals**. Forum advice that “my friend renewed at last year’s number” can be true **and** useless for your new hire IPA. Always ask HR which table their SAT used.

## C2 university list myths

MOM’s top-tier list is institution-based (QS top 100, selected Asian institutions, Singapore Autonomous Universities, agency-endorsed specialist schools). Forum speculation about “same brand, different campus” is risky — awarding body wording on the certificate matters. When in doubt, HR should confirm against MOM’s published list / SAT outcome, not Reddit lore.

## What candidates can control

- Accurate degree attestation and early document packs
- Job scope and **fixed** monthly salary aligned with market (variable bonuses may not count the way you hope — verify MOM definitions with HR)
- Honesty about nationality concentration if the firm already employs many peers from your country (C3 / C5 reductions)

You cannot personally “fix” a firm’s C4 score overnight. Employers should run Workforce Insights / SAT **before** verbal offers.

## How employers should prepare

Run COMPASS self-assessment before candidates resign. Handshake IPAs are how relocation horror stories start. Fair Consideration Framework advertising still applies for many new EP applications — see [FCF & MyCareersFuture](/guides/fair-consideration-framework-ep).
