EntrePass is MOM’s pathway for foreign entrepreneurs building **venture-backed or innovative-technology** businesses in Singapore. It is not a generic “ACRA registration = pass” product. Incorporation mills that sell “guaranteed EntrePass” are selling hope against published eligibility. For the ACRA/CSP/resident-director mechanics alone, see [incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore).

## Who should consider it

- Serial founders, innovators, or investors who will be **operationally present**
- Teams that can show funding, incubator backing, IP, research collaboration, or a prior tech exit
- People whose business is **not** on MOM’s ineligible list (coffee shops, bars, massage, employment agencies, TCM, geomancy, etc.)

If you already have a Singapore employer ready to sponsor a normal role, an [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) is usually cleaner. If you are top-talent salary / spouse-LOC motivated, compare [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore).

## Eligibility gates (meet company + at least one innovator test)

You generally need a **private limited** company (ACRA) where you hold at least **30%**, that is venture-backed or owns innovative technologies — **and** at least one of:

| Path | Orientation bar (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Funding raised | ≥ **S$100,000** from a single round from eligible investors (SEEDS, Vertex, SSGE partners, reputable VCs/angels, etc.) |
| Incubator / accelerator | Government-recognised or internationally renowned (e.g. SSGA/SSGF partners, Y Combinator-class names) |
| Founded & sold a tech / venture-backed business | Docs proving you founded, innovation/venture nature, and sale under your management |
| Registered IP | IP with significant competitive advantage, registered with an approved national IP office |
| Singapore research collaboration | Ongoing collab with eligible IHL / RI (A*STAR, CREATE, ICA-listed IHLs, etc.) with your involvement |

Companies **older than 12 months** at application are assessed against **renewal** criteria — do not assume a quiet shelf company unlocks a founder pass.

## Businesses that will not work

MOM explicitly excludes ordinary F&B courts, nightlife, massage/reflexology, TCM/herbal dispensing, employment agencies, and geomancy. “Innovative kopi” narratives rarely survive that list.

## After approval

- Banking KYC is heavier than employee EP accounts — see [opening a bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat)  
- CPF obligations appear when you hire locals  
- Family passes have their own salary / tenure gates on renewals — do not relocate a household on IPA vibes alone  
- Many founders later transition to employer-sponsored **EP** once the company can hire them under standard criteria  

## Ecosystem, not just immigration

Startup SG and the chamber / founder networks matter for fundraising reality checks more than for form-filling. Browse [Startup SG](https://www.startupsg.gov.sg/) and [chambers](/guides/chambers-of-commerce-singapore) once the pass thesis is credible.
