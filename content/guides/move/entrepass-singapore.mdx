EntrePass is MOM’s route for foreign founders building venture-backed or innovative-technology businesses here. What it isn’t is a “register a company, collect a pass” product. If an incorporation mill is selling you a guaranteed EntrePass alongside your ACRA package, they’re selling hope against published eligibility criteria that MOM and Enterprise Singapore both assess. If you only need the ACRA, corporate secretary and resident-director mechanics, we cover those separately in [incorporating as a foreigner](/guides/incorporate-company-foreigner-singapore).

## Who this actually fits

It fits serial founders, innovators and investors who will be operationally present, running the thing from Singapore rather than holding shares from somewhere else, and who can point to something concrete: money raised, an incubator behind you, registered IP, a live research collaboration, or a technology business you founded and sold. And it only works if your business isn’t on MOM’s ineligible list, which rules out coffee shops, bars, massage, employment agencies, TCM and geomancy, among others.

If you already have a Singapore employer lined up to sponsor an ordinary role, an [Employment Pass](/guides/employment-pass-singapore) is usually the cleaner path and you should take it. If what you’re really chasing is a top-talent salary profile and spouse work rights, compare [ONE Pass](/guides/one-pass-singapore) before you commit to the founder story. And if you’re still working out whether you need a pass at all or just a resident director, that comparison is here: [EntrePass vs resident director](/guides/entrepass-resident-director-vs-pass-singapore).

## The two gates: company test, then one innovator test

You generally need a private limited company registered with ACRA in which you hold at least 30%, and that company has to be venture-backed or own innovative technologies. That’s the company gate. On top of it you need to clear at least one innovator test, and MOM wants documents rather than narrative:

| Path | Orientation bar (verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| Funding raised | ≥ S$100,000 from a single round from eligible investors (SEEDS, Vertex, SSGE partners, reputable VCs/angels, etc.) |
| Incubator / accelerator | Government-recognised or internationally renowned (e.g. SSGA/SSGF partners, Y Combinator-class names) |
| Founded & sold a tech / venture-backed business | Docs proving you founded, innovation/venture nature, and sale under your management |
| Registered IP | IP with significant competitive advantage, registered with an approved national IP office |
| Singapore research collaboration | Ongoing collab with eligible IHL / RI (A*STAR, CREATE, ICA-listed IHLs, etc.) with your involvement |

One trap worth naming: if your company is **older than 12 months** when you apply, it gets assessed against the renewal criteria rather than the new-application ones. So the quiet shelf company someone suggested you park for a year doesn’t smooth the path. It moves you straight to the harder test.

## Businesses that won’t get through

MOM explicitly excludes ordinary food courts, coffee shops and hawker businesses, bars and nightlife, massage and reflexology, TCM and herbal dispensing, employment agencies, and geomancy. Founders do try to reframe these, and “innovative kopi” narratives rarely survive that list. It’s an exclusion list, not a scoring rubric.

## The renewal ladder, and why you model it first

EntrePass renews, but the Total Business Spending and Local Workforce bars ramp with each cycle. In other words the pass gets harder to hold, not easier, and the year you’re least able to spend is the year the bar first appears. Apply up to 3 months before expiry. At each renewal you generally still need at least 30% shareholding, a Startup SG Network profile, ongoing venture or innovative activity, and your latest financials.

| Renewal cycle | Annual TBS (orient) | Local workforce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | — | — |
| 2nd | S$100,000 | 1 |
| 3rd | S$200,000 | 2 |
| 4th+ | Keeps climbing on MOM’s table | Keeps climbing |

Local workforce can mix PMEs, meaning employees on a basic monthly salary at or above the published PME floor for that cycle, with Local Qualifying Salary employees. The people you count have to show **CPF contributions for ≥3 months on the company’s direct payroll**. A nominee director or a friend added on paper won’t clear that check, because CPF records are exactly what the audit looks at.

## What changes once you’re approved

Banking KYC is heavier for a founder than it is for an employee arriving on an EP, so give account opening more runway than you think it needs. We walk through what banks ask for in [opening a bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-expat). CPF obligations start the moment you hire locals, and since those hires are what the renewal ladder demands, that cost isn’t really optional — it’s part of keeping the pass.

Family passes carry their own salary and tenure gates at renewal, which is why moving a whole household on the strength of an IPA alone is a gamble worth naming out loud. And plenty of founders eventually step across to an employer-sponsored EP once the company is substantial enough to hire them under the ordinary criteria.
