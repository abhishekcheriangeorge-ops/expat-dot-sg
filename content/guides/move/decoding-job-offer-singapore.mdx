A Singapore offer usually reads better than it behaves. The headline number blends things that work very differently once you're here: your fixed monthly salary, which is the figure MOM actually looks at, and then allowances, benefits and one-off relocation candy, which mostly don't count for your pass and don't survive contact with a rent cheque either.

## Read the compensation stack

Fixed monthly salary is the line that decides everything else. It's what EP and S Pass thresholds are measured against, and it's what feeds the COMPASS salary factor, so check it against the floor for your age band rather than the floor you read about last year ([EP floors](/guides/employment-pass-singapore)).

Housing allowance comes in two shapes and they aren't interchangeable. Cash in your account and a company lease signed by your employer differ on tax and on what happens when you move. School fees support is where you want to be pedantic: what's the cap, which currency, and which children it covers. Flights and shipment are often one-way or capped, so check whether dependants are in the cap at all.

Tax equalisation or protection turns up in some multinational packages and almost never in local ones. If it's in yours, read how it works before assuming it makes you whole. Bonus and RSU lines need three questions: when they pay, what the leaver provisions say, and what FX does between grant and landing. The medical schedule matters more than people expect, because group cover ends the day the job does — that's the gap [health insurance](/guides/health-insurance-expat) exists to explain.

Before you celebrate the headline, ask which pass HR intends to file. "We have no EP quota, so it'll be an S Pass" is usually a mis-statement rather than a policy, and it's worth pushing back on. Compare [EP vs S Pass](/guides/ep-vs-s-pass-comparison) first.

## The clauses nobody reads until it's too late

- Notice period and garden leave
- Probation length, which bites harder when your household is mid-relocation
- Repayment of relocation costs if you leave early
- Who sponsors the pass if the role changes
- Training bonds, sign-on clawbacks and "remaining salary" penalties. These are the expensive ones, and they often turn up only in the contract email rather than the offer letter. Read [bonds and liquidated damages](/guides/training-bonds-clawbacks-ep) **before** you sign an EP-tied retention clause.
