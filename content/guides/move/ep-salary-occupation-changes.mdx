**Short answer:** Address and mobile updates run on a 5-day clock and they're yours to file. Salary and occupation updates are mostly your employer's clocks, and a salary cut can **pull the family's DP or LTVP down with it**. A quiet payroll change is not invisible to MOM.

This guide sits beside [address / passport / mobile notify](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport), which covers the clocks you own personally. Job hops to an unrelated employer are a different playbook: [changing jobs on EP](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass).

## Lowering salary is the high-risk path

This is the one that hurts families, because a pay cut arrives as a payroll conversation and lands as an immigration event. MOM's notify page sets it out like this:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who notifies | Employer |
| Timing | Submit at least 1 month before the intended lower-salary date |
| Qualifying test | Revised salary must still qualify for the current pass, so run [SAT](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) |
| If it fails | Cancel the pass and apply for a new pass the person can actually hold |
| Family cascade | If the revised salary fails DP/LTVP privilege criteria, MOM revokes existing DP/LTVP tied to the pass |

Read that last row twice. Your spouse's and children's right to be here hangs off a number in your employment contract, and nobody in payroll is thinking about it that way when they propose a trim.

The salary-against-NOA mismatches that surface at renewal are a different theatre, and Next-owned. The rule while the pass is live is simpler: the salary declared to MOM is a floor your employer has to honour, and a reduction needs the formal notify. Not a handshake, not a quiet adjustment next quarter.

## Raising salary

A raise on its own doesn't need immediate notification. You indicate the higher figure at EP renewal and that's enough. There's one exception worth remembering: if you're going to apply for dependant privileges after a salary increase, notify MOM before you apply, not after.

How fixed monthly salary gets defined, and which parts of a package never count towards it, sits on [decoding the offer](/guides/decoding-job-offer-singapore) and [salary package decoding](/guides/salary-package-decoding).

## Occupation change

A title change with no salary movement still runs through your employer. Their path:

1. Submit the occupation-update request in MOM's channel.
2. Pick the closest match from MOM's A–Z standard occupations. Closest, not most flattering.
3. Upload professional-body support letters where the occupation requires them.
4. Expect the outcome by email within about 3 weeks.

If the original EP leaned on Shortage Occupation List (C5) COMPASS points, MOM may contact the employer before any fresh application is re-assessed. So don't let HR silently refile on the assumption the points travel with the new title.

## Related-company transfer vs a true job change

| Scenario | What MOM expects |
| --- | --- |
| Branch / subsidiary / affiliate | The related company files a new EP, with no MyCareersFuture ad. Your current employer cancels before the new pass can be issued. |
| Unrelated new employer | The full [job-change](/guides/changing-jobs-employment-pass) playbook. Resign after IPA. |
| Merger / entity change / new UEN | A different "change in business entity" notify. Not a personal transfer letter. |

The subsidiary case catches people out because it feels internal, like moving desks. It isn't. There's a cancel-before-issue order to it, and that sequence is where the risk sits.

## What to ask HR in writing

1. Will this payroll change be filed as a MOM salary notify, and on what date?
2. Does the revised fixed monthly salary still clear SAT at my age and sector band?
3. Are there DP or LTVP holders on my pass, and have you checked the privilege criteria for them?
4. Is this an occupation rename, a COMPASS-sensitive C5 role, or a related-company move?
5. Will IRAS and payroll documents (IR8A, payslips) stay consistent with what MOM sees?

While all this is going on, keep your own [address and passport clocks](/guides/ep-notify-mom-address-passport) clean, and treat [COMPASS](/guides/compass-framework-explained) as living rules if HR is re-benchmarking salary for renewal year. Mid-term cuts that collide with a renewal NOA request are covered on [EP renewal NOA packaging](/guides/ep-renewal-noa-packaging-depth-singapore).
